### Description

Injector Seal Replacer Kit

### Purpose

The injector seal replacer kit, Part Number 3164197 is used to replace the oil seal on the injector for Signature, ISX and QSX15 Engines.

![[22c00181.png]]

| Table 1, Injector Seal Replacer Kit, Part Number 3164197 |  |  |  |
|---|---|---|---|
| Item Number | Part Number | Description | Quantity |
| 1 | 3164198 | Injector holding fixture | 1 |
| 2 | 3164206 | Bracket | 1 |
| 3 | 3164821 | Flat head socket cap screw-M6x1x12 | 2 |
| 4 | 3398495 | 3/32 pin punch | 1 |
| 5 | 3164199 | Seal installer | 1 |

To replace the injector oil seal, refer to the Troubleshooting and Repair Manual, Signature, ISX and QSX15 Engines, Bulletin [[3666239 — Signature™, ISX, and QSX15 Service Manual\|3666239]].

Injector holding fixture, Part Number 3164198, is used to hold the injector while replacing the seal.

![[22c00182.png]]

Use a 3/32 inch pin punch, Part Number 3398495, to remove the oil seal.

![[22c00189.png]]

Seal installer, Part Number 3164199, is used to install the new seal.

![[22c00193.png]]
