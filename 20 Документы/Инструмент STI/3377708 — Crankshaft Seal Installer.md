### Description

Crankshaft Seal Installer

### Purpose

This document provides information for crankshaft seal installer, Part Number 4919705. The installer is used to align the rear cover plate and install the rear crankshaft oil seal (with or without a wear sleeve) on NH, NT, and N14 engines.

Use the following procedure for additional information on the use of the crankshaft seal installer, Part Number 4919705, in the N14 Troubleshooting and Repair Manual, Bulletin 3666142. Refer to Procedure 001-024 in Section 1.
