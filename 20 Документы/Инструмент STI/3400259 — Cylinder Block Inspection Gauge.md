### Description

Cylinder Block Inspection Gauge

### Purpose

This document provides information for Cylinder Block Inspection Gauge, Part Number 4918968. An engineering design change to the QSK45 and QSK60 engines altered the design of the connecting rod from angle split to a straight split design. Due to this change, the cylinder blocks of engines already in service require internal machining to accept the new connecting rod. The gauge is to be used **only** for the side wall clearance of each cylinder location of the cylinder block, to determine if the required machining has been completed to the required specification.

For additional information, refer to Service Bulletin 4022147 on the QSK45, QSK60, QSK45G, QSK60G Cylinder Block Machining Procedure To Upgrade To Straight Split Connecting Rod.
