### Description

Charge Air Cooler Leak Test Kit

### Purpose

This document provides information for charge air cooler leak test kit, Part Number 4919085. The kit is used to determine if the charge air cooler (CAC) needs to be repaired or replaced for all engines.

Disconnect all tubes and hoses from the CAC inlet and outlet.

Install the appropriate flexible hoses, included in kit, Part Number 4919085, onto the CAC.

Install the appropriate spring hose clamps included in kit, Part Number 4919085 and tighten.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 8 n•m [71 in-lb]

![[10c00501.png]]

Install the appropriate leak test caps into the hoses.

Install the appropriate spring hose clamps included in kit, Part Number 4919085 and tighten.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 8 n•m [71 in-lb]

![[10c00502.png]]

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Equipment damage and personal injury can result from unrestrained leak test caps blowing out of the hoses. Be certain the plugs are properly restrained.

Install the leak test cap retaining chains in front of the CAC in such a way as to limit the movement of each cap, in case the cap blows out of the hose when air pressure is applied. The chains **must** limit the caps to the shortest possible movement.

![[10c00503.png]]

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Excessive air pressure can blow a leak test cap out of a hose, resulting in equipment damage or personal injury. Do NOT use more than 241 kPa \[35 psi\] air pressure.

Connect the flexible hose to the quick connect fitting on the leak test cap.

Connect the pressure gauge assembly to the flexible hose.

The safety relief valve on the leak test cap will relieve pressure at 276 kPa \[40 psi\]. If escaping air is heard, it is an indication that the maximum test air pressure has been exceeded.

![[10c00504.png]]

Use shop air to pressurize the CAC while watching the pressure gauge readout. Do **not** exceed 241 kPa \[35 psi\].

When the pressure drops to 207 kPa \[30 psi\], begin timing with a stop watch.

If the pressure drop is greater than 49 kPa \[7 psi\] in 15 seconds, the CAC **must** be replaced.

![[oi100sn.png]]

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Once the test is completed, relieve the pressure in the CAC by disconnecting the flexible hose from the quick connect fitting on the leak test cap.

Disconnect the gauge assembly and flexible hose from the leak test cap.

Remove the leak test components from the CAC.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Periodically check the relief valve on the leak test caps to ensure that they start passing air at 276 Pa \[40 psi\].
