### Description

Rear Power Takeoff Replacer Kit

### Purpose

This service tool instruction provides information for using the rear power takeoff (REPTO) replacer kit, Part Number 3164761. The REPTO replacer kit is used to remove and install the QSX15 REPTO components.

\* The existing replacer plate, Part Number 3162995, included in the rear crankshaft seal kits, Part Number 3164153 and Part Number 3162994, can be modified by drilling three additional 17-mm \[21/32-inch\] holes. The six 17-mm \[21/32-inch\] holes are equally spaced on a 138-mm \[5.433-in\] bolt circle. The additional three holes update rear crankshaft seal replacer, Part Number 3162995, to the rear crankshaft seal replacer, Part Number 3164763 (included in kit, Part Number 3164780).

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> For additional information on servicing the REPTO components, refer to the Troubleshooting and Repair Manual, Signature, ISX, and QSX15 Engines, Bulletin [[3666239 — Signature™, ISX, and QSX15 Service Manual\|3666239]].

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> This assembly weighs 23 kg \[50 lb\] or more. To reduce the possibility of personal injury, use a hoist or get assistance to lift this component.

The two guide pins (3), Part Number 3164764, are used to guide the flywheel housing during removal and installation.

The two eye bolts (4), Part Number 3164767, are used to lift the flywheel housing during removal and installation.

![[22c00212.png]]

The replacer plate (6), Part Number 3164762, handle (5), Part Number 3164837, and capscrews, Part Number [[3925883]], are used to install the idler shaft into the flywheel housing.

A slide hammer (similar to Snap-On Part Number CJ114) and the replacer plate (6), Part Number 3164762, are used to remove the idler shaft.

![[22c00213.png]]

The three guide pins (2), Part Number 3164765, are used to support the REPTO crankshaft gear for both the removal and installation processes.

The three spacers (1), Part Number 3164766, are placed on three flywheel mounting capscrews and the capscrews are installed into the three open holes in the REPTO crankshaft gear. Tighten the capscrews to press the crankshaft gear onto the crankshaft.

![[22c00214.png]]

The crankshaft seal is removed and installed using the rear crankshaft seal replacer kit, Part Number 3164780. The flywheel mounting capscrews (without the spacers) are used to press the crankshaft oil seal into location.

![[22c00215.png]]
