Cylinder Head Leak Test Kit

### Purpose

This document provides instructions for using the cylinder head leak test kit, Part Number 3164341. This kit can be used with QST30, K19, K38, K50, QSK19, QSK19G, QSK23, QSK45, QSK45G, QSK60, QSK60G, and QSK78 Series engines.

Preparatory

Assemble the wing nut (8), clamp, and plug (13), as necessary.

![[22a00130.png]]

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The QST30 cylinder head as shown uses five clamp and plug assemblies; other models use **only** the four longer assemblies.

Install the clamp and plug assembly into the cylinder head, blocking the coolant passages.

Insert the appropriate capscrew through the valve guide side of the cylinder head, and thread it into the clamp.

Tighten the capscrew.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 20 n•m [177 in-lb]

![[22a00131.png]]

Install the sealing washer (10) and pipe nipple (9) into the coolant vent port of the cylinder head.

![[22a00132.png]]

Install the o-ring (4) into the o-ring groove of the test adapter (5).

Thread the adapter elbow (6) into adapter (5).

![[22a00133.png]]

Place the test adapter assembly into the cylinder head.

Install the clamp (7) over the test adapter assembly and secure it with the appropriate capscrew and washer.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 54 n•m [40 ft-lb]

![[22a00134.png]]

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Wear appropriate eye and face protection when using compressed air. Flying debris and dirt can cause personal injury.

Connect the pressure regulator valve kit to the pipe nipple (9).

Connect the pressure regulator valve kit to shop air and adjust air pressure.

| Measurements |  |  |
|---|---|---|
|  | kpa | psi |
| Air Pressure: | 345 | 50 |

![[22a00135.png]]

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> This component weighs 23 kg \[50 lb\] or more. To reduce the possibility of personal injury, use a hoist or get assistance to lift this component.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The QST30 Series engine cylinder head is shown.

Attach an eyebolt to the cylinder head.

Use a suitable lifting device to lift the cylinder head.

Immerse the cylinder head into a tank of water.

Check for bubbles. Bubbles indicate an air leak.

If the cylinder head leaks, it **must** be repaired or replaced.
