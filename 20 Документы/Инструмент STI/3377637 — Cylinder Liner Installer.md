### Description

Cylinder Liner Installer

### Purpose

The cylinder liner installer is used to press the cylinder liner into the cylinder block. Cylinder liner protrusion is checked while the liner is held down by the installer.

This instruction combines the instructions for the Cummins L10, M11, ISM, NH/NT, N14, QST30, Signature, QSK45, QSK60, K19, QSK19, K38, K50, and QSK78 cylinder liner installers. Slight differences can exist among the various installers because of the different cylinder liner sizes. The function and procedures are the same for all of the installers listed. Refer to the appropriate table to determine the correct installer.

Position the cylinder liner installer plate (3) across the top of the cylinder liner with the step in the cylinder liner bore.

Tap the top of the cylinder liner installer plate (3) with a soft hammer to square up, and start the cylinder liner into the press-fit cylinder block bore.

![[ck1plaa.png]]

The installer screw threads need to be lubricated and the installer screw swivel foot brass wear pad should be coated with anti-seize compound.

Place the cylinder liner installer fixture (1) across the cylinder liner, and install two cylinder head capscrews.

Tighten the capscrews.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 68 n•m [50 ft-lb]

Position the cylinder liner installer plate so that the cylinder liner protrusion measurements can be taken.

![[ck1tohh.png]]

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Do not use an impact wrench to tighten the installer screw (2). Doing so could result in damage to the tool and cylinder block.

Tighten the installer screw (2).

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 136 n•m [100 ft-lb]

Using the depth gauge assembly, Part Number 3164438, check cylinder liner protrusion. Refer to the appropriate troubleshooting and repair manual for the correct procedure.

![[ck1touo.png]]

Loosen the installer screw (2). Remove the two cylinder head capscrews and the cylinder liner installer fixture (1).

Remove the cylinder liner installer plate (3).

Repeat the above procedures to install the remaining cylinder liners.
