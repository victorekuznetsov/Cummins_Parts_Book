### Description

Temperature Indicator Marker

### Purpose

This document provides information for using the temperature indicator markers. The markers are used to indicate the exhaust port temperature on the QSK19, QSK23, QSK45, QSK60, and QSK78 Series Engines.

QSK19, refer to Procedure

006-026

in the Troubleshooting and Repair Manual QSK19 Series Engines, Bulletin 3666098.

QSK23, refer to Procedure

006-026

in the Troubleshooting and Repair Manual QSK23 Series Engines, Bulletin [[4021375 — QSK23 Troubleshooting and Repair Manual\|4021375]].

QSK45 and QSK60, refer to Procedure

006-026

in the Troubleshooting and Repair Manual QSK45 and QSK60 Series Engines, Bulletin 3666261.

QSK78, refer to Procedure

006-026

in the CENSE™ System QSK78 Engine Series Troubleshooting and Repair Manual, Bulletin 3666753.
