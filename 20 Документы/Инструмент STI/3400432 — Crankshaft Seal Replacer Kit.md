### Description

Crankshaft Seal Replacer Kit

### Purpose

This document provides information for rear crankshaft seal replacer kit, Part Number 5298540. The kit is used to remove and install the QSK23 rear crankshaft oil seal (with or without wear sleeve).

- Use the following procedure for additional information on the seal replacer in the QSK23 Troubleshooting and Repair Manual, Bulletin [[4021375 — QSK23 Troubleshooting and Repair Manual\|4021375]]. [[89-001-024 — Crankshaft Seal, Rear|Refer to Procedure 001-024 in Section 1.]]
