### Description

Connecting Rod Guide Pin

### Purpose

This document provides information for connecting rod guide pin, Part Number 3375098, and other similar guide pins listed in Table 2. The connecting rod guide pins are used to guide the connecting rod and piston assembly during removal and installation.

All connecting rod guide pins are purchased as a quantity of one. Two or more may be required during removal and installation of the connecting rod and piston assembly. Check the respective service manual for details.
