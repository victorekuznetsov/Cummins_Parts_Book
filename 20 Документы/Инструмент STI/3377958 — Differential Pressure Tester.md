### Description

Differential Pressure Tester

### Purpose

This document provides information for the Differential Pressure Tester, Part Number 3824223. The tester is used with the appropriate leak test adapter to check individual cylinders to help locate sources of leaks or excessive blowby. The tester measures the leakdown rate of the cylinder by comparing the inlet pressure, (left hand gauge), with the pressure in the cylinder, (right hand gauge). The results from each cylinder are compared with each other to determine the relative blowby.
