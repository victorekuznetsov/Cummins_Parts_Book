Safety Guidelines:

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> This engine-lifting fixture must be used for lifting the specified engines only. Improper use can result in equipment damage, severe personal injury, or death.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Rigging and lifting must be done by trained and experienced personnel only . Improper use can result in equipment damage, severe personal injury, or death.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Personnel must stay clear of the engine and engine-lifting fixture while the engine is lifted. Failure to do so can result in severe personal injury or death.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> The adjusting screw must not be adjusted while the engine-lifting fixture is loaded. Doing so can result in equipment damage, severe personal injury, or death.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> The engine must not swing while it is lifted with the engine-lifting fixture. Doing so can result in equipment damage, severe personal injury, or death.

The following safety instructions are in accordance with the Occupational Safety and Health Administration's (OSHA) regulations for rigging equipment for handling material, Standard Number 1926.251.

#### General

- The engine-lifting fixture (1) and **all** of its components will be inspected prior to each use and as necessary during its use. Damaged or defective equipment **must** be removed from service.
- Store the engine-lifting fixture and its components in a safe place and away from the immediate work area when **not** in use.
- Hooks, links, makeshift fasteners (formed from bolts, rods, and so forth), or other such attachments **must not** be used.
- A thorough inspection **must** be made on a regular basis, based on the frequency of sling use, severity of service conditions, nature of the lifts being made, and experience gained on the service life of slings used in similar circumstances. Inspection intervals **must not** be longer than 12 months.
- The shop should maintain a record of the most recent month in which each sling assembly was thoroughly inspected and make the record available for inspection.
- Operators **must** be trained and experienced in hoisting objects.

#### Slings:

- Inspection of the slings for the following conditions **must** be done before each use.
- Cuts to the sling cover exposing red-striped white-core yarns
- Holes, tears, snags, or abrasions exposing red-striped white-core yarns
- End fittings that are pitted, corroded, cracked, distorted, or broken
- The sling shows signs of melting, charring, or chemical damage
- Capacity tag is illegible or missing
- Other visible damage that causes doubt as to the strength of the sling.

If any of the above conditions are observed, the sling **must** be removed from service.

It is recommended to store slings in a safe place when **not** in use.

- Nylon and polyester are seriously degraded at temperatures above 93°C \[199°F\].
- Many chemicals have an adverse effect on nylon and polyester. See Table 2, General Guide for Chemical Effects on Nylon and Polyester.
