Beginning with the EPA 2013 engine launches, federal regulation requires changes to some of the methods in which Cummins Inc. delivers service information, including INSITE™ electronic service tool and technical training for automotive engines. These changes are due to Service Information Regulation (SIR) and as a result, service information **must** be made available to **all** interested parties in the United States of America (U.S.A.) and Canada.

This regulation impacts all U.S. Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) certified on-highway automotive Cummins® engines built since 2007.

The purpose of Service Information Regulation is to make sure that engine manufacturers make information that would enable repairs, service, and maintenance of On-Board Diagnostic (OBD) certified engines, systems, and other emission related engine components available to the service and repair industry.

In essence, Cummins can **not** provide service information to distributors/dealers and exclude other interested parties.

**Any** interested party is defined as:

- **Any** person or entity engaged in the business of service or repair of heavy duty motor vehicles, engines, or transmissions
- **Any** individual or business engaged in the diagnosis, service, and repair of a heavy duty engine, who is **not** directly affiliated with Cummins Inc. authorized network
- **Any** licensed or registered person or entity engaged in the business of service or repair of passenger cars, light-duty trucks, or medium-duty motor vehicles, engines, or transmissions
- **Any** commercial business or government entity that repairs or services its own vehicle fleet(s)
- Tool and equipment companies.

The Service Information Regulation mandates the sale of information be made available at a fair, reasonable, and non-discriminatory price. As such, the published suggested list pricing to the interested parties for access to the website, service information, tools, and training has been established to be fair and reasonable to meet the Service Information Regulation requirements.

QuickServe® Online (QSOL) is the primary method for providing service information. Additionally, QuickServe® Store has been made available for the online purchase of service information, INSITE™ electronic service tool, and Cummins® Virtual College. Distributors that provide training will be required to allow **any** interested party to attend classroom training for impacted automotive on-highway engines.

## INSITE™ Electronic Service Tool:

INSITE™ Basic, INSITE™ MR-HD Automotive LITE or INSITE™ MR-HD Automotive Pro annual subscriptions are available for sale to **any** interested party requesting the tool within the U.S.A. and Canada.

Two versions of the INSITE™ electronic service tool have been developed to be offered to any interested party:

- MR-HD Automotive Lite – limited access version that provides access to engines covered under SIR but does **not** allow Engine Control Module (ECM) calibration download capabilities.
- MR-HD Automotive Pro – full access version that provides access to engines covered under SIR, including ECM calibration download.

Interested parties, that are **not** Distributors, Dealers or National Accounts/Fleets, shall be allowed to purchase only the MR-HD Automotive versions of INSITE™ electronic service tool. Other versions, such as INSITE™ MR-HD Plus electronic service tool and INSITE™ Service Plus electronic service tool, exist but provide access to engines not covered under SIR regulations and therefore should not be made available to interested parties.

## INSITE™ Electronic Service Tool Terms of Use:

- INSITE™ electronic service tool is a very powerful tool that, amongst other functionalities, possesses the ability to either update an existing ECM calibration to the latest available ECM calibration, or to change the existing ECM calibration to a different ECM calibration.
- Terminology:
- It is critical to understand the difference between updating an ECM calibration and changing an ECM calibration.
- ECM Calibration Update - going from ECM calibration ab12345.01 to ECM calibration ab12345.02. Specifically, the change is seen above where the base code stays the same and the revision number changes. This does not require a Fleet Count. Examples for updating an ECM calibration are below.
- ECM Calibration Change - going from ECM calibration ab12345.01 to ECM calibration ba54321.01. Specifically, the change is seen above where the base code is changed from how it was originally presented. The revision is **not** a factor when the base code changes. This action requires a Fleet Count. Examples for updating an ECM calibration are below.
- A repair required ECM calibration change can only be performed by interested parties when a Service Publication (i.e. Service Bulletin, Installation Instruction or Technical Service Bulletin) directs the need for a Fleet Count to be utilized.
- At the time of request, the interested party will be required to provide the Service Publication number to the distributor before the sale of the Fleet Count is completed.
- Fleet Counts are provided by Distributors, when a requirement exists, to allow the changing of an ECM calibration or re-establishing an ECM calibration in the event the ECM has been ROM-booted.
- A Fleet Count is a single use item, once a Fleet Count has been utilized a new Fleet Count will need to be obtained.
- Zap-Its are tools that are used to remove passwords placed on the ECM to prevent unauthorized or damaging changes from taking place.
- Where a Fleet Count or Zap-It is required, an individual must have a Promotion ID, taken the necessary training, have the INSITE™ Pro license and obtain the approval of their local distributor.
- Training requirements will differ according to functionality or password levels (Basic, Lite, and Pro) desired.
- The QuickServe ® Store will provide for sale INSITE™ electronic service tool software CD bundled with INSITE™ electronic service tool Cummins Virtual College. Once the customer has purchased the bundle online, INSITE™ electronic service tool software CD and INSITE™ electronic service tool Cummins® Virtual College will be shipped to the customer with instructions regarding how to register and obtain the passwords for the software.

Each distributor **must** provide for sale INSITE™ Basic, MR-HD Automotive Lite, or Pro, and Cummins® Virtual College to any interested party who wants to purchase it. See the definition of "Interested Party". The INSITE™ electronic service tool registration process will remain the same as it is presently. The annual subscription will be registered and paid at the distributor.

## FAQ for Service Information Regulation (SIR):

| **What is Service Information Regulation (SIR)?** |  |
|---|---|
| **Q1:** | **What is Service Information Regulation (SIR)?** |
| A1: | Service Information Regulation, also known as “SIR”, is a government requirement that mandates engine and vehicle manufacturers to provide service information to **any** interested party for the successful repair, service, and maintenance of engines equipped with diagnostic systems and other emission-related components. |
| **Q2:** | **What does the term "Interested Party" mean to Cummins Inc.?** |
| A2: | An Interested Party or Covered Persons includes **any** person or entity engaged in the service or repair of vehicle, **any** business or entity that repairs its own vehicles, tool manufacturers, and **any** person or entity engaged in the manufacture or remanufacture of parts. In short, very few entities are excluded. |
| **Q3:** | **Who are the government agencies that regulate SIR?** |
| A3: | The Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) and California Air Resource Board (CARB) both have regulations concerning the availability of service information, tools, and training. |
| **Q4:** | **What fines may be imposed for not complying with Service Information Regulation (SIR)?** |
| A4: | Both EPA and CARB can issue significant financial penalties for violation of the regulation. |
| **Q5:** | **What regions are affected by Service Information Regulation (SIR)?** |
| A5: | United States of America and Canada. |
| **Q6:** | **For what products do Service Information Regulation (SIR) regulations apply?** |
| A6: | Products that SIR Regulations apply to: Year 1994 and later passenger cars, light duty trucks, medium duty engines, and vehicles equipped with OBD II systems. Year 2007 and later heavy duty products equipped with EMD+ or HD OBD systems. |
| **Q7:** | **What are the key components of Service Information Regulation (SIR)?** |
| A7: | Service Information Regulation (SIR) includes publications, product training, website access, and tools. |
