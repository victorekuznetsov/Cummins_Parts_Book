## Aftertreatment Diesel Exhaust Fluid (DEF) Dosing and Selective Catalytic Reduction (SCR) System Reuse Guidelines

This document provides reuse guidelines for the aftertreatment diesel exhaust fluid (DEF) dosing and selective catalytic reduction (SCR) systems, in conjunction with the information provided in the corresponding Service Manual.

**Product Affected**

- ISB4.5 CM2350 B104
- ISB6.7 CM2250
- ISB6.7 CM2350 B101
- ISB6.7 CM2350 B103
- ISC8.3 CM2250
- ISL9 CM2250
- ISF2.8 CM2220
- ISF2.8 CM2220 AN
- ISF2.8 CM2220 IAN
- ISF3.8 CM2220 AN
- ISF3.8 CM2220 IAN
- ISL CM2350 L101
- ISX12 CM2350 X102
- ISX12/ISX11.9 CM2250
- ISX15 CM2250
- ISX15 CM2350 X101
- QSB6.7 CM2350 B105
- QSF3.8 CM2350 F107
- QSK19 CM2450 K155G
- QSL9 CM2350 L102

The reuse guidelines contained in this document are **only** Cummins Inc. approved inspection methods and pass/fail criteria with regards to inspecting an aftertreatment SCR catalyst.

The aftertreatment SCR catalyst housing **must** be free of dents. Mounting flanges **must** be free of dents, cracks, or gouges to seal properly with gaskets.

Replace any oil, fuel, or coolant-soaked aftertreatment SCR catalyst.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The aftertreatment DEF dosing unit filter is a non-reusable maintenance item. The information below is provided for use in determining if contaminated DEF has passed through the dosing system.
