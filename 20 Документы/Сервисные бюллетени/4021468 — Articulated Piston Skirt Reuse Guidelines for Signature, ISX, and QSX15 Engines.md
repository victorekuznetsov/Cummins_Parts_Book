## Articulated Piston Skirt Reuse Guidelines for Signature, ISX, and QSX15 Engines

## General Information

This bulletin applies inspection criteria for piston skirts on Signature, ISX, and QSX15 engines. Cummins Distributors and Dealers are encouraged to consult these guidelines when seeking to determine if used articulated piston skirts are acceptable for reuse.

Piston skirts for articulated pistons are now available independent of the piston crowns. Crown reuse guidelines are outlined in Procedure [[10-001-043 — Piston|Refer to Procedure 001-043]] of the Troubleshooting and Repair Manual, Signature, ISX and QSX15 Engines, Bulletin [[3666239 — Signature™, ISX, and QSX15 Service Manual\|3666239]].

Cylinder Liner Inspection - Inspect, and replace as necessary, all cylinder liners that do **not** meet the reuse guidelines as detailed in the Parts Reuse Guidelines manual, Bulletin 3810303.
