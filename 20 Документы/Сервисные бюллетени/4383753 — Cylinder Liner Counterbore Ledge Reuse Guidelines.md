## Cylinder Liner Counterbore Ledge Reuse Guidelines

This document provides reuse guidelines for cylinder liner counterbore ledge surfaces, supplementing the information provided in the Service Manuals listed in Table 1 below. These guidelines should be consulted when determining if cylinder liner counterbore ledge surfaces are acceptable for reuse or machining.
