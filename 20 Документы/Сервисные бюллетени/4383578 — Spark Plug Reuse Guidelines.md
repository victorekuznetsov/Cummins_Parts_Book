## Руководство по повторному использованию Spark Plug

В этом документе содержатся руководящие принципы замены и повторного использования свечей зажигания. Используйте эту информацию в сочетании с контентом, представленным в соответствующем руководстве по обслуживанию.

| Меры | Сравните с свечами зажигания от других цилиндров, чтобы определить, является ли проблема изолированной для одного цилиндра или для всех цилиндров. Исследовать возможные причины внутренних утечек масла и жалобы на потребление масла. Замените все свечи зажигания с появлением вышеперечисленных отложений. |

> ## Spark Plug Reuse Guidelines
>
> This document provides guidelines for replacement and reuse of spark plugs. Use this information in conjunction with the content provided in the corresponding service manual.

> | Appearance | Clean, without any signs of use. |
>
> | Spark Plug - Good Used Plug |  |

> | Appearance | Clean, without any defects; minor signs of use. |
> | Action | Reuse |
>
> | Spark Plug - Acceptable Deposits |  |
> |---|---|
> | Appearance | Slightly discolored. |
> | Cause | Deposits from normal combustion. |
> | Action | Reuse |
>
> | Spark Plug - Oil Deposits |  |
> |---|---|
> | Appearance | Coated with black, oily sludge. |
> | Cause | Oil Deposits, leaking valve stem seals, piston malfunction, piston ring malfunction. |
> | Action | Compare with spark plugs from the other cylinders to determine if the issue is isolated to one cylinder or across all cylinders. Investigate possible causes of internal oil leaks and complaints of oil consumption. Replace all spark plugs with the appearance of the above deposits. |
>
> | Spark Plug - Unacceptable Deposits |  |
> |---|---|
> | Appearance | Exceedingly discolored, or coated with ash. |
> | Cause | Deposits from oil consumption, coolant consumption, or poor fuel quality. |
> | Action | Check for excessive coolant consumption. Check for excessive oil consumption. Compare with spark plugs from the other cylinders to determine if the issue is isolated to one cylinder or across all cylinders. Replace all spark plugs with the appearance of the above deposits. |
>
> | Spark Plug - Dry Fouling |  |
> |---|---|
> | Appearance | Dry, black deposits on electrodes and core nose. |
> | Cause | Running rich for a prolonged period of time. |
> | Action | Compare with spark plugs from the other cylinders to determine if the issue is isolated to one cylinder or across all cylinders. Investigate possible causes of rich running condition. Replace all spark plugs with the appearance of the above deposits. |
>
> | Spark Plug - Wet Fouling |  |
> |---|---|
> | Appearance | Wet, black deposits on electrodes and core nose. |
> | Cause | Prolonged misfire. |
> | Action | Compare with spark plugs from the other cylinders to determine if the issue is isolated to one cylinder or across all cylinders. Investigate possible causes of misfire condition. Replace all spark plugs with the appearance of the above deposits. |
