## Idler Gear Bushing Inspection Procedure

**Product Affected**

- 38 Liter - All engines
- 50 Liter - All engines

**Issue**

Excessive idler gear bushing wear.

**Symptom**

- High copper levels in lubricating oil samples. See table 1 below.
- Idler gear busing wear causing gear train damage.

**Root Cause**

Corrosion and pitting on idler gear bushing caused by degraded oil.

**Resolution:**

It is recommended that for sites experiencing excessive idler gear bushing wear, a 250 fixed-hour oil change be implemented unless extended oil analysis is performed according to Service Bulletin Number 4022060, Oil Analysis Techniques for High Horsepower Diesel Engines.

Refer to 5411406 Fluids for Cummins Products Service Manual for guidance on oil practice to avoid oil becoming corrosive.

For sites experiencing excessive idler gear bushing wear, see Table 2 below for revised copper limits and total base number limits.

| Table 1, Copper Content and Acidity Limits for Lubricating Oil |  |  |
|---|---|---|
|  | Copper (Cu) (ppm) | Total Base Number (mg KOH/g) |
| Caution | 30ppm | 9 |
| Critical | 50ppm | 7 |

**Bushing Inspection at Rebuild**

Gears that meet reuse criteria **must** have bushings visually inspected in addition to meeting required tolerances. See corresonding Service Manual. Reference Service Procedure 001-063.

Figures 2, 3, and 4 represent examples of compromised idler gear bushings at different levels of severity, which are **not** suitable for reuse. If any of these examples are observed, idler gear bushing **must** be replaced or a new idler gear and idler gear bushing assembly installed.

Re-bushing Criteria

![[01r00241.png]]

Figure 5, Genuine Cummins® Approved Stamped Idler Gear Bushings.

- Idler gear bushing **must** have a Cummins stamp. If stamp is not present, it has **not** been sourced from an approved supplier and **mustnot** be used.
- Idler gear bushing must also have TZZX stamp referencing supplier.
- After idler gear bushing has been replaced, it **must** be machined to meet design specification to remove tin flash.
- Once tin flash has been removed and bushing ID is within specification, idler gear **must** be installed immediately to avoid corrosion from environmental exposure.
