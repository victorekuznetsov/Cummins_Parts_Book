## Parts Reuse Guidelines

This document serves as a placeholder to point to various reuse guidelines.

The legacy information that used to be in Service Bulletin 3810303, titled Parts Reuse Guidelines, has been moved to Service Bulletin 5411359. The title has been changed from “Parts Reuse Guidelines” to “Bearing, Counterbore, Cylinder Liner, and Piston Reuse Guidelines for NH/NT and L10 Engines”

The service bulletins listed below are supplemental to the Service Manual and/or Troubleshooting and Repair Manual.

The Service Bulletins listed below do **not** apply to all engines. To check for applicability, do the following:

1. Read the service bulletin title. If the component or system is **not** on the engine, then it does **not** apply.
2. Look for a list of engines or other qualifiers in the title or introduction section of the document.
