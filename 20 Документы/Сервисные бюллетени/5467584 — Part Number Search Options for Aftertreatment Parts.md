## Part Number Search Options for Aftertreatment Parts

**Products Affected:**

- B6.7 CM2350 B121B
- L9 CM2350 L116B
- X15 CM2350 X114B
- X15 CM2350 X116B

**Introduction**

The aftertreatment assembly part numbers and serial numbers can be found and used in many different ways. This document explains the different locations and tools that are available to find aftertreatment assembly part numbers and serial numbers.

**Table of Contents**

1. Features and Parameters Tab in INSITE™ Electronic Service Tool
2. Visual Inspection of Aftertreatment Assembly and Serial Numbers
3. QuickServe Online™ (QSOL) Reference
4. QSOL Assembly Part Number Lookup

**I. Features and Parameters Tab in INSITE™ Electronic Service Tool**

One way to identify the aftertreatment assembly part number and serial number is to use INSITE™ electronic service tool. This information is read by the engine control module (ECM) and saved to the ECM. The part and serial numbers are broadcast to the user when INSITE™ electronic service tool is connected to the ECM. They can be found in the Aftertreatment Information section of the Features and Parameters tab. The aftertreatment assembly part number shown is the Cummins Emission Solutions™ (CES) part number.

**II. Visual Inspection of Aftertreatment Assembly and Serial Numbers**

Aftertreatment assembly component part numbers and serial numbers are located on the aftertreatment system.

![[00l00027.png]]

Figure 2, Aftertreatment Identification Location.

The aftertreatment assembly has information laser-etched into the canister. There are several important identification locations on the aftertreatment system.

1. Aftertreatment diesel oxidation catalyst (DOC) part identification
2. Aftertreatment diesel particulate filter (DPF) part identification
3. Aftertreatment decomposition tube part identification
4. Aftertreatment assembly part identification
5. Aftertreatment selective catalytic recirculation (SCR) part identification

![[00000043.png]]

Figure 3, Aftertreatment Assembly Identification Information.

Aftertreatment System Assembly Part Identification Numbers

- The Cummins Emission Solutions™ part number (A123A123) (also known as the E PN)
- The Cummins® part number (1234567) (also known as the C PN)
- The aftertreatment assembly serial number (1234567891011)

More information about part number identification and location can be found in the corresponding service manual.

- See B6.7 CM2350 B121B Service Manual, Bulletin 5411223. Refer to Procedure 100-001 in Section E.
- See L9 CM2350 L116B Service Manual, Bulletin 5411330. Refer to Procedure 100-001 in Section E.
- See X15 CM2350 X114B Service Manual, Bulletin 5411181. Refer to Procedure 100-001 in Section E.
- See X15 CM2350 X116B Service Manual, Bulletin 5411186. Refer to Procedure 100-001 in Section E.

**III. QuickServe Online (QSOL) Reference**

A Cummins® part number **must** be used when ordering parts. Use the CES part number or serial number and quickserve.cummins.com to find the Cummins® part information. First select the Parts tab and then select the Emissions Catalog tab. Enter the CES part number or serial number into the text box and then select the Search button.

**IV. QSOL Assembly Part Number Lookup**

There are two options to view the bill of materials for the assembly part number. See Figure 6 below.

1. Select blue hyperlink under Cummins® Part Number section. This will lead directly to parts list for aftertreatment system.
2. If hyperlink is **not** active, enter aftertreatment assembly Cummins® Part Number into “Search All Engines for:” box and select Search. There should be **only** one item found in Search Results. Select link in Search Results to open parts list for aftertreatment system.

![[11r00307.png]]

Figure 6, Search Results of an Aftertreatment Assembly Part Number.

All replaceable items are listed on bill of materials for assembly. Service kits and child parts are sometimes **not** listed. See Figure 7 below.
