## Catalyst Muffler for Service Use Only

### Purpose

This Service Bulletin introduces a new service design for the diesel catalyst in the B and C Series diesel engines.

Diesel oxidation catalysts are being used on B Series and C Series automotive engines to reduce the particulate emissions and meet EPA emissions standards. Customers have reported a loss of engine power due to the plugging of the catalyst.

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Do not idle engine for long periods of time. Long periods of idling (more than 10 minutes) can damage an engine by dropping combustion chamber temperatures so low the fuel will not burn completely. This will cause carbon to clog the injector spray holes and piston rings, and can cause the valves to stick. It can also plug the catalyst muffler, leading to a low power complaint. If the engine coolant temperature becomes too low (60°C \[140°F\]), raw fuel will wash the lubricating oil off the cylinder walls and dilute the crankcase oil. Moving parts will no longer receive the correct amount of lubrication.

This condition is typically reported on engines with:

- A light load operation (frequent and/or extended idle) and/or
- Low ambient temperature.

The new service design was developed to resist plugging, particularly for engines operated in northern climates with a light-duty cycle condition.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Periodic operation at high engine loads will help prevent this failure by burning off accumulated deposits.

To determine if a catalyst muffler is the root cause of the low power or no response complaint:

1. Connect a pressure gauge (measuring in Hg) to the pressure tap on the inlet end of the catalyst muffler.
2. Measure the exhaust restriction on the engine under rated rpm and load.

a. If pressure is above 6 in Hg, replace the catalyst muffler with the service use

only

catalyst muffler (see instructions).

b. If pressure is less than 6 in-Hg, the catalyst muffler is

not

the root cause of the low power or no response complaint. Please reference the appropriate Troubleshooting and Repair Manuals for low power complaints in the “Engine Power Output Low” symptom tree.

For instructions on taking measurements, look at the following publications:

- Troubleshooting and Repair Manual, B Series Engines 1991 and 1994 Certification Levels, Bulletin 3666087. Refer to Procedure.
- Troubleshooting and Repair Manual, C Series Engines 1991 and 1994 Certification Levels, Bulletin 3666003. Refer to Procedure.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The above test procedure **must** be run at the engine rated rpm and load for proper verification of plugged catalyst.

Replacement catalyst mufflers are available through authorized OEM dealers or direct from suppliers such as:

- Donaldson Company, Inc. (800) 374-1374 or
- Nelson Industries, Inc. (800) 356-6606.

Cummins Inc. does **not** supply the replacement catalyst muffler. The following are the catalyst element numbers that are used to make sure that the correct service use **only** catalyst element is inside the muffler. The service use **only** catalyst replacement mufflers require at least a 10-inch diameter muffler can.

| Engine Family | Production Catalyst Identification | Service Catalyst Identification |
|---|---|---|
| B5.9 | 26233 | 32434 |
| C8.3 | 26265 | 32434 |

The service use **only** catalyst muffler does **not** supersede or obsolete the original part number for the catalyst muffler. It is **not** to be used to fix a low power and/or no response complaint when the exhaust restriction is below 6 in Hg and the catalyst muffler is **not** plugged. The same service catalyst substrate will be used for the B5.9 and C8.3 engine families.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The service use **only** catalysts have been certified by EPA for all B and C Series engine configurations.

The following configurations are covered by field fix number 226 since they are certified products that are no longer in production. The dataplate **must** be stamped with field fix number 226 (FF226) in the box that reads “Reference Number” (1) whenever a service use **only** catalyst muffler (Part Number 32434) is installed on an engine.
