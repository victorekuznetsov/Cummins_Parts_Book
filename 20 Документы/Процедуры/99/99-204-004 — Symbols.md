### General Information

The symbols are used **only** in printed manuals to help communicate the intent of the instructions. Symbols will **not** appear in the online version of this procedure. When one of the symbols appears in the printed manual, the symbol conveys the meaning defined below.

Four symbols are possible for each text and graphic combination.
