### Maintenance Check

Marine and Industrial Applications

Inspect the cooling system hoses and hose connections for leaks or deterioration. Particles of deteriorated hose can be carried through the cooling system and restrict or clog small passages, especially the heat exchanger and lubricating oil cooler, and partially stop circulation. Replace as necessary.
