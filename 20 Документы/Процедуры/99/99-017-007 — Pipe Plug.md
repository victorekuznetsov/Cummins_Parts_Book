### Remove

Remove the pipe plug.

![[17200012.png]]

### Clean and Inspect for Reuse

Use spray cleaner, Part Number 3823717 or equivalent, to clean the threads of the pipe plugs and threaded bores.

![[ck2pxea.png]]

Inspect the threads of the pipe plugs for mutilation or damage.

Inspect the threaded bores for damage.

Repair the bores, if necessary. Refer to the L10 Alternative Repair Manual, Bulletin Number 3810310.

![[ck2pxsa.png]]

### Install

Apply a film of pipe plug sealant, Part Number 3375066 or equivalent, to the threads.

Install and tighten the pipe plug to the appropriate torque. [[99-018-012 — Pipe Plug Torque Values|Refer to Procedure 018-012 in Section V.]]

![[bp2ppha.png]]

**Never** loosen a tapered pipe plug thread fitting to achieve recommended torque or rotations. If loosening is necessary, completely remove, clean, and inspect for reuse prior to installing.

Overtightening can cause damage to components and pipe plug fittings.
