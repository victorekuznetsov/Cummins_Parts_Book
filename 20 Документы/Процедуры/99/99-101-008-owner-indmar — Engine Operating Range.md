### General Information

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Do not operate the engine at full throttle below peak torque rpm (refer to engine dataplate for peak torque rpm) for more than 30 seconds. Operating the engine at full throttle below peak torque will shorten engine life to overhaul, can cause serious engine damage, and is considered engine abuse.

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Do not operate the engine beyond the maximum engine speed. Operating the engine beyond the maximum engine speed can cause severe engine damage. Use proper operating techniques for the vehicle, vessel, or equipment to prevent engine overspeed. The maximum engine speed specification is listed in Maintenance Specifications (Section V).

These engines are designed to operate at rated rpm at Wide Open Throttle (WOT) and should not be operated at peak torque.

![[oi204vf.png]]

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Never replace a fuse with a higher amperage fuse. This will result in damage at another point in the electrical system or possibly cause a fire.

The engine is equipped with spade type fuses and is located in central electrical unit behind the cover. Remove the central electrical unit cover mounting capscrews.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> If a fuse fails after replacing it a short time later, the engine electrical system must be checked by an Authorized Cummins Repair Facility.

![[13800078.png]]

Determine the failed fuse and replace it with the proper amperage fuse.

1. Accessory - 5 Amps
2. Main Fuse - 30 Amps
3. Accessory - 5 Amps
4. Actuator - 15 Amps
5. Accessory - 5 Amps
6. Oil Extraction Pump - 5 Amps

![[13800079.png]]

Replace the fuse from Volkswagon fuses available from CMD parts distribution center.

#### Color Code for Fuses

- Orange is for 5 Amps
- Blue is for 15 Amps
- Green is for 30 Amps

Secure the cover of the central electric unit using the mounting capscrews.

![[13800078.png]]

Two additional fuses are located in the individual instrumentation control panel. Remove the mounting capscrews and pull the panel in a forward direction. Determine the failed fuse and replace it with the proper amperage fuse.

1. Ignition on - 2 Amps
2. Permanent positive - 2 Amps

Replace the instrumentation control panel and tighten the mounting capscrews.
