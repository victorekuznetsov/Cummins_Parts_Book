# PT/CT Board
**Плата трансформаторов напряжения и тока (PT/CT)**

### Remove

The PT/CT board is located in the controls box. Installation can vary. Some generator sets will have the PT/CT board mounted behind the bus PT module.

Disconnect the 12-pin AMP connector 09 on the AC harness from the PT/CT board.

![[19802840.png]]

Disconnect the 23-pin AMP connector 08 on the generator control harness (or the paralleling harness if applicable) from the PT/CT board.

Remove the four capscrews that hold the PT/CT board to the controls box.

![[19802825.png]]

### Install

Install the new PT/CT board in the controls box. Tighten the four capscrews.

Connect the 23-pin AMP connector 08 on the generator control harness to the PT/CT board.

If the paralleling harness is being used, then connector 08 from this harness **must** be used

![[19802825.png]]

Connect the 12-pin AMP connector 09 on the AC harness to the PT/CT board.

![[19802840.png]]
