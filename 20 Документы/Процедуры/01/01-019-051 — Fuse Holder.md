# Fuse Holder
**Держатель предохранителя**

> [!abstract] Процедура · `01-019-051`
> **Двигатели:** [[37292556 — QST30 CM552 CPL 1244|37292556]], [[37295879 — QST30 CM552 CPL 2139|37295879]]
> **Семейство:** QST30
> **Входит в руководства:** [[4021419 — QSX15, QSK23, QSK45, QSK60, QSK78, and QST30 Power Generation Electronic Control Syst|4021419]]
> **Секции:** Section 19 - Electronic Engine Controls - Group 19
> **Даты:** изменён 2003-11-10
> **Источник:** [QuickServe](https://quickserve.cummins.com/qs3/pubsys2/xml/en/procedures/01/01-019-051.html) · [PDF-оригинал](https://github.com/victorekuznetsov/Cummins_Parts_Book/raw/claude/cummins-parts-knowledge-base-qa0n50/bulletins/procedures/01-019-051.pdf)

### Remove

Cut and remove the plastic hold-down wire tie. Cut away and remove the connector from the harness.

![[19400442.png]]

Remove 6 mm \[1/4 inch\] of insulation from the supply wire.

![[19400280.png]]

### Install

Install the replacement fuse holder, Part Number 3823758, onto the bare wires.

![[ee8cnhj.png]]

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Only use wire crimping pliers, Part Number 3822930.

Crimp the repair wire onto the bare wire.

Use a heat gun, Part Number 3823860, to heat the shrink tubing. The tube will shrink and make the connections waterproof.

![[19400444.png]]
