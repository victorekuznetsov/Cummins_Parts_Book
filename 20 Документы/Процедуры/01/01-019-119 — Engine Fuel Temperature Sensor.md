# Engine Fuel Temperature Sensor
**Датчик температуры топлива**

> [!abstract] Процедура · `01-019-119`
> **Двигатели:** [[37292556 — QST30 CM552 CPL 1244|37292556]], [[37295879 — QST30 CM552 CPL 2139|37295879]]
> **Семейство:** QST30
> **Входит в руководства:** [[4021419 — QSX15, QSK23, QSK45, QSK60, QSK78, and QST30 Power Generation Electronic Control Syst|4021419]]
> **Секции:** Section 19 - Electronic Engine Controls - Group 19
> **Даты:** изменён 2002-12-05
> **Источник:** [QuickServe](https://quickserve.cummins.com/qs3/pubsys2/xml/en/procedures/01/01-019-119.html) · [PDF-оригинал](https://github.com/victorekuznetsov/Cummins_Parts_Book/raw/claude/cummins-parts-knowledge-base-qa0n50/bulletins/procedures/01-019-119.pdf)

### Remove

Clean the area around the sensor.

Lift up on the locking tab and pull the electrical connectors apart.

![[19801033.png]]

Remove the sensor using a deep well socket, Part No. 3823843.

![[19801034.png]]

### Install

Check that the new sensor has an o-ring installed.

Lubricate the o-ring with clean engine oil.

![[19801035.png]]

Install the new sensor into the control valve body.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 14 n•m [124 in-lb]

![[19801034.png]]

Use quick-dry electrical contact cleaner, Part No. 3824510, to remove all dirt and moisture from harness connector.

![[19801036.png]]

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Use only Cummins-recommended lubricant DS-ES, Part No. 3822934. Other lubricants, such as lubricating oil or grease, in the connectors can cause ECM damage, poor engine performance, or premature connector wear.

Apply a thin coating of lubricant to the harness connector.

![[19801037.png]]

Push the connectors together until they lock.

Operate the engine to check for leaks.
