# Danfoss (Circular DIN Series) Connector

### Pin Replacement

The 19-pin Deutsch connector is used as the Inline C connector on the engine harness.

![[19400422.png]]

Use the Deutsch extraction tool(s), Part Number 3822760 and Part Number 3824816, over the wire to remove a pin from the connector.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Replace one wire at a time. If more than one wire needs replacement, attach a lettered tag to each wire removed. Refer to the wiring diagram in Section of this manual.

![[ee8comm.png]]

Push the tool into the connector about 25 mm \[1 in\] until it bottoms on the terminal flange.

![[ee8comn.png]]

Hold the tool on the terminal flange and pull the wire and the connection pin out of the connector. Note and record the hole from which the pin is removed.

![[ee8como.png]]

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The repair wire is 127 mm \[5 in\] long.

The repair wire for the male terminal is Part Number 3822920 (16 gauge) or Part Number 3824807 (12 gauge).

The repair wire for the female terminal is Part Number 3822921 (16 gauge) or Part Number 3824808 (12 gauge).

Use wire crimp tool, Part Number 3822930, to cut 127 mm \[5 in\] off the wire and pin.

Use the wire crimp tool to remove 6 mm \[¼ in\] of insulation from the wire.

![[cel37.png]]

Install the correct repair wire on the bare wire.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Make sure the bare wire extends into the splice connector.

![[ee8coaj.png]]

Use the wire crimp tool to crimp the repair wire onto the bare wire.

![[ee8cohv.png]]

Use a heat gun, Part Number 3822860, to heat the shrink tubing around the wire. The tubing will shrink and make the connection waterproof.

![[ee8coal.png]]

Insert the pin into the correct hole of the connector.

The pin **must** click into place and hold the wire in the connector.

Pull the wire gently to make sure it is sealed in the connector.

![[ee8coam.png]]

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Use only Cummins recommended lubricant DS-ES, Part Number 3822934. Other lubricants, such as lubricating oil or grease, in the connectors can cause ECM damage, poor engine performance, or premature connector wear.

Apply a small amount of lubricant to the connector terminals. Do **not** fill the entire connector cavity with lubricant.

![[cel35.png]]

### Connector Replacement

The 19-inch Deutsch connector is used as the Inline C connector on the engine harness.

![[19400422.png]]

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Replace one wire at a time. Attach a lettered tag to each wire removed. Refer to the wiring diagram in Section E of this manual.

To replace the connector, use Deutsch extraction tool, Part Number 3822760 (16 gauge) or 3824816 (12 gauge), over each wire to remove all pins from the connector.

![[ee8comm.png]]

Push the tool into the connector about 25 mm \[1 in\] until it bottoms on the terminal flange.

![[ee8comn.png]]

Hold the tool on the terminal flange and pull the wire and the connecting pin out of the connector. Note and record the hole from which the pin is removed.

![[ee8como.png]]

Replace the connector plug or the connector receptacle.

![[19400423.png]]

Insert the pin into the correct holes of the replacement connector.

Each pin **must** click into place and hold the wires in the connector.

Pull the wires gently to make sure they are seated in the connector.

![[ee8coam.png]]

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Use only Cummins recommended lubricant DS-ES, Part Number 3822934. Other lubricants, such as lubricating oil or grease, in the connectors can cause ECM damage, poor engine performance, or premature connector wear.

Apply a small amount of lubricant to the connector terminals. Do **not** fill the entire connector cavity with lubricant.

![[cel35.png]]
