1. Be sure that the fuel rate is corrected for temperature, see table below.

| Fuel Temperature | Correction for Flow Rate |
|---|---|
| Less than 7°C \[45°F\] | Flow meter is **not** accurate |
| 7 to 13°C \[45 to 55° F\] | Subtract 2 percent from flow rate reading |
| 13 to 20°C \[55 to 68°F\] | Subtract 1 percent from flow rate reading |
| 20 to 29°C \[68 to 85°F\] | No Correction |
| 29 to 42°C \[85 to 108°F\] | Add 1 percent to flow rate reading |
| 42 to 56°C \[108 to 132°F\] | Add 2 percent to flow rate reading |
| 56°C \[132°F\] above | Flowmeter **not** accurate |
