### General Information

The basic heavy-duty electrical system consists of:

- Batteries (1)

- (Usually two or four connected in parallel)

- Starting motor (2)
- Alternator (3)
- Magnetic switch (4)
- Ignition switch (5)
- Necessary wiring

All components **must** be carefully matched.
