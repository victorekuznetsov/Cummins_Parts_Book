# Fuel Drain Line Restriction
**Сопротивление магистрали слива топлива**

> [!abstract] Процедура · `20-006-012`
> **Двигатели:** [[41349633 — QSK19 CM2150 MCRS CPL 3666|41349633]]

> **Секции:** Section 6 - Injectors and Fuel Lines - Group 06
> **Даты:** изменён 2017-08-28
> **Источник:** [QuickServe](https://quickserve.cummins.com/qs3/pubsys2/xml/en/procedures/20/20-006-012.html) · [PDF-оригинал](https://github.com/victorekuznetsov/Cummins_Parts_Book/raw/claude/cummins-parts-knowledge-base-qa0n50/bulletins/procedures/20-006-012.pdf)

### Measure

with Mechanically Actuated Injector

Remove the fuel drain hose.

Install the adapter, Part Number ST-434-2, and pressure gauge, Part Number ST-1274-1, between the fuel drain hose and the engine fuel drain fitting.

![[06400051.png]]

If the engine has a drain side check valve, install the adapter between the fuel drain hose and the check valve.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Engines with electronically actuated injectors do **not** use a drain line check valve.

![[06400052.png]]

Start and operate the engine at high idle, no load.

- With Check Valve: 0-21 kPa \[0-3 psi\]
- With Check Valve Removed: 14-34 kPa \[2-5 psi\]

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Engines with mechanically actuated injectors are built with a check valve.

![[06400053.png]]

If the fuel drain line restriction is above specifications, inspect the fuel drain line. [[20-006-013-tr — Fuel Drain Lines|Refer to Procedure 006-013 in Section 6.]]

![[fp8hoca.png]]

Remove the gauge. Install the fuel drain line.

Tighten the fuel line.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 75 n•m [55 ft-lb]

![[06400039.png]]

with Electronically Actuated Injector

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Depending on the circumstance, diesel fuel is flammable. When inspecting or performing service or repairs on the fuel system, to reduce the possibility of fire and resulting severe personal injury, death, or property damage, never smoke or allow sparks or flames (such as pilot lights, electrical switches, or welding equipment) in the work area.

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Do not spill or drain fuel into the bilge area when disconnecting or removing fuel lines, replacing filters, and priming the fuel system. Do not drop or throw filter elements into the bilge area. The fuel and fuel filters must be disposed of in accordance with local environmental regulations.

With the fuel supply valve closed, remove the M14 STOR plug from the fuel drain manifold and install a Compuchek™ fitting, Part Number 3824844, or equivalent.

![[06k00049.png]]

Connect the vacuum gauge and adapter, Part Number 3164491, or equivalent, and digital multimeter, Part Number 3164488 or 3164489, or equivalent, to the Compuchek™ fitting.

![[06k00050.png]]

Open the fuel supply valve, and start and operate the engine at high idle and no load.

Check for the fuel drain line restriction.

Fuel Drain Line Restriction:

| With check valve |  |  |
|---|---|---|
| mm-hg |  | in-hg |
| 127 | MAX | 5 |

| Without check valve |  |  |
|---|---|---|
| mm-hg |  | in-hg |
| 254 | MAX | 10 |

![[06400053.png]]

If the fuel drain line restriction is above specifications, inspect the fuel drain line. [[20-006-013-tr — Fuel Drain Lines|Refer to Procedure 006-013 in Section 6.]]

![[fp8hoca.png]]

Remove the vacuum gauge and adapter from the fuel inlet manifold block.

![[06k00050.png]]

Remove the Compuchek™ fitting and install the M14 STOR plug into the port in the fuel inlet manifold block.

Tighten the plug.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 29 n•m [21 ft-lb]
