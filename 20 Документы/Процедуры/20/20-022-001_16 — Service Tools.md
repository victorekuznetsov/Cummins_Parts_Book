> [!quote]- Original (English) · английский оригинал
> ### Mounting Adaptations
>
> | **Tool Number** 3163720 | **Dowel Pin Extractor** Remove dowel pins. |  |
> |---|---|---|
> | **Tool Number** ST-1232 | **Drill Ream Fixture** Machine dowel hole to install oversize dowels in cylinder block and flywheel housing. Use with a drill, reamer, and the appropriate drill/ream bushing set. |  |
> | **Tool Number** 3375272 | **Engine Support Bracket** Attaches to each side of the cylinder block and supports the front of the engine. |  |
> | **Tool Number** 3162871 | **Engine Lifting Fixture** Used to remove and install the engine. Designed to lift 2721 kg \[6000 lb\]. |  |
> | **Tool Number** 3822512 | **Engine Lifting Fixture** Used to remove and install the engine. Designed to lift 3175 kg \[7000 lb\]. |  |
> | **Tool Number** 5395137 | **Engine Lifting Hooks** Used to lift engine. Used on the lifting lugs between cylinder heads, two are required. Used with Part Number 5395137. Designed to lift 1134 kg \[2500 lb\]. |  |
