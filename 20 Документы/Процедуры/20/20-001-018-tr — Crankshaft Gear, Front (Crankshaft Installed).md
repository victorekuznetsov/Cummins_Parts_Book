### Preparatory Steps

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Coolant is toxic. Keep away from children and pets. If not reused, dispose of in accordance with local environmental regulations.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Do not remove the pressure cap from a hot engine. Wait until the coolant temperature is below 50°C \[120°F\] before removing the pressure cap. Heated coolant spray or steam can cause personal injury.

Prepare the engine to remove the front crankshaft gear.

- Drain the coolant. [[20-008-018-tr — Cooling System|Refer to Procedure 008-018 in Section 8.]]
- Bar the engine to top dead center compression stroke. [[20-001-012-tr — Camshaft Gear (Camshaft Installed)|Refer to Procedure 001-012 in Section 1.]]
- Remove the front gear cover and all related components. [[20-001-031-tr — Gear Cover, Front|Refer to Procedure 001-031 in Section 1.]]
- Remove the water pump. [[20-008-062-tr — Water Pump|Refer to Procedure 008-062 in Section 8.]]
- Remove the camshaft idler gear. [[20-001-036-tr — Idler Gear, Camshaft|Refer to Procedure 001-036 in Section 1.]]
- Remove the water pump idler gear. [[20-001-040-tr — Idler Gear, Water Pump|Refer to Procedure 001-040 in Section 1.]]
- Remove the hydraulic pump idler gear. [[20-001-039-tr — Idler Gear, Hydraulic Pump|Refer to Procedure 001-039 in Section 1.]]
- Remove the front gear cover spacer plate. [[20-001-029-tr — Gear Cover Spacer Plate|Refer to Procedure 001-029 in Section 1.]]

![[ck800wa.png]]

### Remove

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Only remove the gear when the crankshaft or the gear is damaged.

Use a puller jaw, Part Number 3375835, and bridge assembly, Part Number 3375834. Install the puller jaw and bridge assembly on the gear.

![[ks8gema.png]]

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Heat can be used to aid the removal of the gear. Do not use a cutting torch. The high temperature of a torch will damage the teeth of the gear.

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Do not exceed 475 N•m \[350 ft-lb\] of torque when turning the jackscrew or damage can occur.

Lubricate the puller jackscrew with engine oil. Hold the pry bar and turn the jackscrew to remove the gear.

![[ks8gemb.png]]

Remove the key.

Use a hone stone and polish the shaft outside diameter Remove all of the small scratches, burrs, and small grooves.

![[ks8kema.png]]

### Inspect for Reuse

Clean and inspect the crankshaft for reuse. Measure the outside diameter

| Crankshaft outside diameter (Gear Location) |  |  |
|---|---|---|
| mm |  | in |
| 111.25 | MIN | 4.380 |
| 111.28 | MAX | 4.381 |

![[ks800ta.png]]

### Install

Install the key.

![[ks8keha.png]]

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Wear protective clothing to prevent personal injury from burns.

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Do not exceed the specified time or the temperature. Damage to the gear and the gear teeth will result.

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Do not attempt to install the gear without using heat.

Use an oven and adjust the heat to 235°C \[455°F\]. Heat the gear for a **minimum** of 1 hour, but **no** more than a maximum of 6 hours. The inside diameter of the gear will become larger and will simplify the installation.

![[dp8gewa.png]]

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Wear protective clothing to prevent personal injury from burns.

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Allow the gear to air cool slowly. Do not use water or oil to reduce the cooling time. This will cause the gear to crack.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The timing mark on the crankshaft gear **must** be visible from the front of the gear after it is installed on the crankshaft.

Lubricate the outside diameter of the crankshaft with Lubriplate® Number 105 multi-purpose lubricant, or equivalent. Remove the gear from the oven and install it on the crankshaft. The keyway in the gear **must** be aligned with the key in the crankshaft.

![[ks8geha.png]]

### Finishing Steps

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Coolant is toxic. Keep away from children and pets. If not reused, dispose of in accordance with local environmental regulations.

Prepare the engine for normal operation.

- Install the front gear cover spacer plate. [[20-001-031-tr — Gear Cover, Front|Refer to Procedure 001-031 in Section 1.]]
- Install the water pump. [[20-008-062-tr — Water Pump|Refer to Procedure 008-062 in Section 8.]]
- Install the hydraulic pump idler gear. [[20-001-039-tr — Idler Gear, Hydraulic Pump|Refer to Procedure 001-039 in Section 1.]]
- Install the water pump idler gear. [[20-001-040-tr — Idler Gear, Water Pump|Refer to Procedure 001-040 in Section 1.]]
- Install the camshaft idler gear. [[20-001-036-tr — Idler Gear, Camshaft|Refer to Procedure 001-036 in Section 1.]]
- Install the front gear cover. [[20-001-031-tr — Gear Cover, Front|Refer to Procedure 001-031 in Section 1.]]
- Fill the engine with coolant. [[20-008-018-tr — Cooling System|Refer to Procedure 008-018 in Section 8.]]

Start the engine and check for leaks.
