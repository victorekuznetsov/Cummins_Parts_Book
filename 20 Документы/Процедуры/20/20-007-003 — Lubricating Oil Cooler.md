### Pressure Differential Test

Use a differential pressure gauge or one pressure gauge with two oil hoses and two valves to eliminate gauge error. Use a gauge with a minimum pressure capacity of 517 kPa \[75 psi\].

![[lf4etka.png]]

Install each hose into the \[1 1/16-12 UNF inch\] plugged hole at each end of the oil cooler housing.

![[lc4hsla.png]]

Operate the engine at rated rpm until the oil temperature is at least 65°C \[150°F\].

![[oi400la.png]]

**Close** valve Number. 1 and **open** valve Number. 2. Read the oil pressure after the oil has passed through the oil coolers.

![[lc4pcla.png]]

**Open** valve Number. 1 and **close** valve Number. 2. Read the pressure of the oil being supplied to the oil coolers.

Compare the difference between the two pressures. If the difference is more than 138 kPa \[20 psi\], replace the oil cooler elements. [[20-007-007-tr — Lubricating Oil Cooler Element|Refer to Procedure 007-007 in Section 7.]]

Max Lubricating Oil Cooler Differential: 138 kPa \[20 psi\]
