### Disassemble

Remove the locking nut and adjusting screw.

![[03400030.png]]

Remove the socket from the ball on the rocker lever shaft.

![[03400039.png]]

Place the ball in a vice as shown.

Wiggle the rocker lever until the ball swivels.

Use a mallet to tap the rocker lever until the ball comes out.

![[03400040.png]]

### Magnetic Crack Inspect

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The rocker lever **must** be disassembled to do this inspection.

Use the residual method. Apply coil shot amperage.

Amperage (Ampere Turns)

1200 D.C. or rectified A.C

2000 D.C. or rectified A.C

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Ampere turn is an electrical current of one ampere flowing through the coil, multiplied by the number of turns in the coil.

The lever **must** be replaced if there is a crack in any location.

Demagnetize the lever.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> When using solvents, acids or alkaline materials of cleaning, follow the manufacturer's recommendations for use. Wear goggles and protective clothing to reduce the possibility of personal injury.

Use solvent to clean the rocker lever.

![[03400037.png]]

### Assemble

Install the adjusting screw and the lock nut in each lever.

Do **not** completely tighten the lock nut until the assembly is installed on the engine.

![[03400030.png]]

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Do not overstress the press fit.

Use a press to seat the new ball into the cavity in the rocker lever.

The lip on the ball **must** be flush with the surface on the rocker lever.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Use an old push tube socket end to help hold and align for proper seating.

![[03400041.png]]

Install the new socket on the ball.

![[03400042.png]]
