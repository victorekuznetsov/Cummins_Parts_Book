Loosen the hose clamp at the breather vent tube.

Remove the tube support bracket capscrew and the bracket.

Remove the vent tube and the hose from the engine.

If the vent tube is blocked with an obstruction or sludge, it **must** be cleaned to prevent excess crankcase pressure buildup.

### Clean and Inspect for Reuse

with Mechanically Actuated Injector

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> When using solvents, acids, or alkaline materials for cleaning, follow the manufacturer's recommendations for use. Wear goggles and protective clothing to reduce the possibility of personal injury.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Some solvents are flammable and toxic. Read the manufacturer's instructions before using.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Wear appropriate eye and face protection when using compressed air. Flying debris and dirt can cause personal injury.

Use solvent to clean the inside of the crankcase breather tube. Dry with compressed air.

Use air pressure to blow through the vent tube.

Replace the vent tube if it is clogged or dented.

![[br1tbea.png]]

Replace the mesh if it is torn, broken down, or deteriorating.

Clean the vent tube and screens in an approved cleaning solvent. Dry with compressed air.

Clean the following parts:

1. Wing Nut
2. Washer
3. Breather Cap
4. Screen Mesh
5. Element.

Wipe out the breather housing (6).

![[br8etha.png]]

with Electronically Actuated Injector

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> When using solvents, acids or alkaline materials for cleaning, follow the manufacturer's recommendations for use. Wear goggles and protective clothing to reduce the possibility of personal injury.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Wear appropriate eye and face protection when using compressed air. Flying debris and dirt can cause personal injury.

Use solvent to clean the rocker cover and breather baffle.

Dry with compressed air.

![[03400173.png]]

Inspect all parts for cracks, stripped threads, and any other damage.

Replace any parts that are damaged.
