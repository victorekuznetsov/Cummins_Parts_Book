### Preparatory Steps

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Do not remove the pressure cap from a hot engine. Wait until the coolant temperature is below 50°C \[120°F\] before removing the pressure cap. Heated coolant spray or steam can cause personal injury.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Coolant is toxic. Keep away from children and pets. If not reused, dispose of in accordance with local environmental regulations.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Batteries can emit explosive gases. To reduce the possibility of personal injury, always ventilate the compartment before servicing the batteries. To reduce the possibility of arcing, remove the negative (-) battery cable first and attach the negative (-) battery cable last.

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Use caution when draining coolant that the coolant is not spilled or drained into the bilge area. Do not pump coolant overboard. If the coolant is not reused, it must be disposed of in accordance with local environmental regulations.

- Disconnect the battery cables. Refer to Procedure [[99-013-009 — Battery Cables and Connections|013-009]].
- Drain the coolant. Refer to Procedure [[20-008-018-tr — Cooling System|008-018]].
- Remove the water pump. Refer to Procedure [[20-008-062-tr — Water Pump|008-062]].
- Remove the starter if it is located on the right side of the engine. Refer to Procedure [[20-013-020-tr — Starting Motor|013-020]].
- Remove the oil cooler cover, Procedure [[20-007-045-tr — Lubricating Oil Cooler Cover|007-045]], or the torque converter cooler, Procedure [[20-008-065-tr — Torque Converter Cooler|008-065]], and the marine gear oil cooler Procedure [[20-008-041 — Marine Gear Oil Cooler|008-041]].

### Remove

Remove the capscrews and oil cooler housing.

Remove and discard the gaskets.

![[lc4hsma.png]]

### Clean and Inspect for Reuse

Clean and inspect the housing. Check the cast iron housings for corrosion.

![[lc4hsea.png]]

### Install

Install two guide studs. Put the gasket and housing into position.

Install, but do **not** tighten, capscrews.

![[lc4hsma.png]]

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> The two capscrew holes marked "X" align with cylinder head capscrews. The cylinder head capscrews will be damaged if the cooler housing capscrews are too long.

Do **not** tighten the capscrews until the oil cooler cover or torque converter cooler cover is installed.

Install the capscrews as illustrated in the graphic using the table below.

1. 31.750 mm \[1.250 in\]
2. 44.450 mm \[1.750 in\]
3. 41.275 mm \[1.625 in\]
4. 36.830 mm \[1.450 in\]
5. 41.275 mm \[1.625 in\]

![[lc4hsha.png]]

### Finishing Steps

- Install the Oil Cooler Cover, Procedure [[20-007-045-tr — Lubricating Oil Cooler Cover|007-045]], or the Torque Converter Cooler, Procedure [[20-008-065-tr — Torque Converter Cooler|008-065]] or the Marine Gear Cooler, Procedure [[20-008-041 — Marine Gear Oil Cooler|008-041]].
- Install the starter if removed. Refer to Procedure [[20-013-020-tr — Starting Motor|013-020]]
- Install the water pump. Refer to Procedure [[20-008-062-tr — Water Pump|008-062]].
- Fill with coolant. Refer to Procedure [[20-008-018-tr — Cooling System|008-018]]
- Connect the battery. Refer to Procedure [[99-013-009 — Battery Cables and Connections|013-009]]
- Operate the engine and check for leaks.
