### Preparatory Steps

with Mechanically Actuated Injector

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Depending on the circumstance, fuel is flammable. When performing any or all of the following procedures to remove fuel supply lines and related components, keep all cigarettes, flames, pilot lights, arcing equipment, and switches out of the work area and areas sharing ventilation to reduce the possibility of severe personal injury or death when working on a fuel system.

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Do not spill or drain fuel into the bilge area when disconnecting or removing fuel lines, replacing filters, and priming the fuel system. Do not drop or throw filter elements into the bilge area. The fuel and fuel filters must be disposed of in accordance with local environmental regulations.

- Remove as much of the engine wire harness and brackets as necessary to gain access to the fuel tubes. Refer to Procedures 019-043 in the Troubleshooting and Repair Manual, Electronic Control System QSK19, QSK23, QSK45, QSK60 and QSK78 Engines, Bulletin 3666113 or 019-043 in the Troubleshooting and Repair Manual, Electronic Control System Series QSK19 CM850 Modular Common Rail System Engine, Bulletin 4021493.
- On engines with an air compressor, remove the air inlet connection. Discard the connection hose and the gasket. Refer to Procedure [[20-012-014-tr — Air Compressor|012-014]].

![[ck800wa.png]]

with Electronically Actuated Injector

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Wear safety glasses or a face shield, as well as protective clothing. Hot steam can cause serious personal injury.

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Very small amounts of dirt and debris can be very harmful to the injectors and the cone seats on the injectors. Extra care is required to keep the fuel connections clean during removal and installation. The fuel supply line(s) must be placed in a plastic bag or connections be covered immediately to keep them clean when removed from the engine. Dirt and debris can cause fuel system damage.

- Thoroughly clean debris from all fittings and components before removal. Make sure that the debris, water steam, or cleaning solution does **not** get inside the fuel system.

### Remove

with Mechanically Actuated Injector

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Depending on the circumstance, fuel is flammable. When performing any or all of the following procedures to remove fuel supply lines and related components, keep all cigarettes, flames, pilot lights, arcing equipment, and switches out of the work area and areas sharing ventilation to reduce the possibility of severe personal injury or death when working on a fuel system.

Use two wrenches to remove the fuel tubes and hoses.

Support the mating fittings with a wrench. Loosen the fuel tube nuts with the other wrench.

Loosen the fuel tubing nuts at the fuel junction block on the fuel manifold.

Remove the fuel tube clamps.

Remove the fuel tubing mounting clamp and the fuel tubes.

Remove the fuel control supply hose.

Discard the o-ring.

![[06400048.png]]

Remove the hose between the fuel filter head outlet and the fuel pump inlet fitting.

Discard the o-rings.

Remove the fuel inlet line from the fuel tank to the fuel filter.

### Clean and Inspect for Reuse

with Mechanically Actuated Injector

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> When using solvents, acids, or alkaline materials for cleaning, follow the manufacturer's recommendations for use. Wear goggles and protective clothing to reduce the possibility of personal injury.

Remove and discard the ferrule seals.

Use solvent to flush the tubing and fuel block connectors.

![[ft800sa.png]]

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Wear appropriate eye and face protection when using compressed air. Flying debris and dirt can cause personal injury.

Use compressed air to dry the lines and flush any loose dirt particles from the lines.

![[06400040.png]]

Inspect the inside of the hose that connects the fuel tank to the fuel filter and the fuel inlet hose.

- The inner lining of the hose can separate from the center hose section.
- A separation or flap can cause a restriction in the fuel flow.

Inspect for any pinches in the hose that would obstruct the flow.

Make sure the hose does **not** have pinches or loops that would obstruct the flow.

![[fp8hoca.png]]

Inspect the fuel line for cracks that can cause a loss of pressure.

Inspect the fuel line for sharp bends that can cause a restriction in pressure.

![[fp8tbcc.png]]

Inspect the fuel control supply hose for pinched areas that prevent the proper flow, or frayed mesh. The hose **must** be replaced, if damaged.

![[06400047.png]]

with Electronically Actuated Injector

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> When using solvents, acids, or alkaline materials for cleaning, follow the manufacturer's recommendations for use. Wear goggles and protective clothing to reduce the possibility of personal injury.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Some solvents are flammable and toxic. Read the manufacturer's instructions before using.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Wear appropriate eye and face protection when using compressed air. Flying debris and dirt can cause personal injury.

Use solvent to clean the fuel supply lines, tee fittings, and unions.

Dry with compressed air.

![[06400338.png]]

Inspect the fuel supply lines for restrictions that could possibly prevent fuel from flowing freely. Replace the fuel supply lines if kinks, damage, or leakage is found.

Inspect the tee fittings and unions for damage. Replace if damaged, cracked, or stripped threads are found.

### Install

with Mechanically Actuated Injector

Install new o-rings on the control valve body inlet fitting and fuel pump outlet fitting. Make sure the o-rings are installed properly.

![[06400031.png]]

Install and tighten the fuel control valve supply hose.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 61 n•m [45 ft-lb]

![[06400048.png]]

Install new o-rings on the fuel pump inlet line fitting and fuel filter head outlet fitting. Make sure the o-rings are installed properly.

![[06400031.png]]

Install the hose at the fuel pump inlet and fuel filter head outlet fitting.

Tighten the hose.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 88 n•m [65 ft-lb]

![[06400046.png]]

Install and tighten the fuel inlet line that connects the fuel tank to the fuel filter.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value

Support the fitting. Use a crowfoot wrench and a torque wrench to tighten the fuel tube nut.

> 27 n•m [20 ft-lb]

### Finishing Steps

with Mechanically Actuated Injector

- On engines equipped with an air compressor, install the air compressor air inlet connection with a new hose and gasket. Refer to Procedure [[20-012-014-tr — Air Compressor|012-014]].
- Install the engine harness and the brackets. Refer to Procedures 019-043 in the Troubleshooting and Repair Manual, Electronic Control System QSK19, QSK23, QSK45, QSK60 and QSK78 Engines, Bulletin 3666113 or 019-043 in the Troubleshooting and Repair Manual, Electronic Control System Series QSK19 CM850 Modular Common Rail System Engine, Bulletin 4021493.
- Operate the engine and check for leaks.

![[ck800wa.png]]

with Electronically Actuated Injector

- Turn the keyswitch to the ON position for 30 seconds and allow the electric lift pump to prime the fuel system.
- Operate the engine and check for leaks.
