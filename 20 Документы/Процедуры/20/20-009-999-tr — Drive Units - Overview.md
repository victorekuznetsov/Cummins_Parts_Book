### General Information

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Installing an air compressor on a fuel pump drive housing will result in failure due to the lack of lubrication.

The accessory drive housing is manufactured out from aluminum. The aluminum housing does **not** contain a bushing, and does **not** require thrust washers.

The housing is available in two designs, **with** and **without** provisions for mounting an air compressor. Those **without** air compressors do **not** have provisions for an air compressor oil supply and drain-back. The fuel pump drive housing has provisions for a Lovejoy™ coupling. The air compressor drive has provisions for a splined sleeve-type coupling. The procedures are identical for both designs.

The fuel pump/air compressor drive gear has stamped marks. The stamped marks **must** be aligned properly with the camshaft idler gear so that the valve and injector adjustment marks on the accessory drive pulley are oriented correctly.

The retainer capscrew on the air compressor drive (splined half-coupling) is special. It has a drilling in it that provides lubrication of the splined coupling.

The accessory drive located on the back side of the accessory housing (1) drives the fuel pump (2). The fuel pump engages the accessory drive with splines cut on the end of the fuel pump shaft engaging mating splines in the back of the accessory drive.

The accessory drive also has an oil passage (3) to transfer engine oil to the fuel pump for lubrication.

A mesh (4) is installed in the accessory drive on the mating face to the fuel pump. This mesh prevents any debris from the fuel pump from entering into the engine oil sump and causing damage to the engine.
