### Rotation Check

with Mechanically Actuated Injector

If the gear pump can **not** be primed, then check the rotation of the gear pump.

Because the gear pump inlet fitting is located on the side of the gear pump, the fitting can **not** be removed to check the rotation of the gear pump.

![[05400009.png]]

Remove the fuel pump. Refer to Procedure [[20-005-016-tr — Fuel Pump|005-016]].

![[05400320.png]]

Remove the pulsation damper. Refer to Procedure [[20-005-031 — Fuel Pump Pulsation Damper|005-031]].

![[gr8dpha.png]]

Rotate the fuel pump by hand and check for gear pump rotation.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> If fuel pump gears do **not** rotate, replace or repair fuel pump. Refer to Procedure [[20-005-016-tr — Fuel Pump|005-016]].

![[05400028.png]]

Use a remote starter, Part Number 3376506. Crank the engine and make sure the accessory drive rotates **counterclockwise**.
