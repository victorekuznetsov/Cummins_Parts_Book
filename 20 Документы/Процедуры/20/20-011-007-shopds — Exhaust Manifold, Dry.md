# Exhaust Manifold, Dry

### Remove

All Applications Except Marine

Use a drift and hammer to bend the lock plate tabs away from the capscrew heads, if equipped.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Some engines do **not** use this type of lock plate. Some engines use a lock plate that snaps over the capscrew head. Some engines use locking capscrews that do **not** require lockplates.

![[313.png]]

Remove two capscrews as shown.

Install two 11.112 mm x 127 mm \[0.4375 in x 5 in\] guide studs (12), as shown.

![[xm400mr.png]]

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> This component or assembly weighs greater than 23 kg \[50 lb\]. To prevent serious personal injury, be sure to have assistance or use appropriate lifting equipment to lift this component or assembly.

Remove the remaining capscrews.

Remove the exhaust manifold and the gaskets.

Discard the gaskets.

![[xm400mq.png]]

Marine Applications

Remove the six mounting capscrews and the turbocharger bracket cover plate.

![[11400124.png]]

Remove the exhaust manifold heat shield. [[20-011-015-tr — Exhaust Manifold Heat Shield|Refer to Procedure 011-015 in Section 11.]]

Remove the exhaust manifold heat shield bracket and capscrews from the rocker box.

![[11400123.png]]

Remove the turbocharger heat shield support bracket.

![[11400122.png]]

Remove capscrews 1, 2, and 3, and spacers.

Remove capscrew 4 and washer.

![[11400118.png]]

Use a drift and hammer to bend the lock plate tabs away from the capscrew heads, if equipped.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Some engines do **not** use this type of lock plate. Some engines use a lock plate that snaps over the capscrew head. Some engines use locking capscrews that do **not** require lockplates.

![[313.png]]

Remove the lower capscrews and install alignment rods.

Remove the 12 upper exhaust manifold mounting capscrews and spacers.

![[11400117.png]]

Remove the exhaust manifold, with gaskets, from the cylinder heads.

![[11400116.png]]
