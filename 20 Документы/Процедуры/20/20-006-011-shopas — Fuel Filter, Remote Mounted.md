### Install

Rail Applications

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Fuel is flammable. Keep all cigarettes, flames, pilot lights, arcing equipment, and switches out of the work area and areas sharing ventilation to reduce the possibility of severe personal injury or death when working on the fuel system.

Install the bracket and four capscrews on the lubricating oil pan. Tighten the capscrews.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 75 n•m [55 ft-lb]

![[17400024.png]]

Install the fuel filter head and four capscrews on the bracket. Tighten the capscrews.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 25 n•m [221 in-lb]

![[17400023.png]]

Install the two hoses and o-rings on the fuel filter head.

Tighten the fittings.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> Fitting - Fuel tank to fuel filter 115 n•m [85 ft-lb]

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> Fitting - Fuel filter to fuel pump 90 n•m [66 ft-lb]

Operate the engine and check for leaks.
