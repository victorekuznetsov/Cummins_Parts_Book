### Preparatory Steps

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> To reduce the possibility of personal injury, avoid direct contact of hot oil with your skin.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Some state and federal agencies have determined that used engine oil can be carcinogenic and cause reproductive toxicity. Avoid inhalation of vapors, ingestion, and prolonged contact with used engine oil. If not reused, dispose of in accordance with local environmental regulations.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> This component or assembly weighs greater than 23 kg \[50 lb\]. To prevent serious personal injury, be sure to have assistance or use appropriate lifting equipment to lift this component or assembly.

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Use caution when draining oil or replacing filters that the oil is not spilled or drained into the bilge area. The oil and oil filters must be disposed of in accordance with local environmental regulations.

- Drain the oil. [[20-007-037 — Lubricating Oil System|Refer to Procedure 007-037 in Section 7.]]
- Remove the oil pan. [[20-007-025-tr — Lubricating Oil Pan|Refer to Procedure 007-025 in Section 7.]]
- Remove the oil pan adapter cover. [[20-007-026-tr — Lubricating Oil Pan Adapter Cover Plate|Refer to Procedure 007-026 in Section 7.]]
- Remove the turbocharger oil drain hoses. [[20-010-045 — Turbocharger Oil Drain Line|Refer to Procedure 010-045 in Section 10.]]
- Remove the front engine support bracket. [[20-016-002-tr — Engine Support Bracket, Front|Refer to Procedure 016-002 in Section 16.]]
- Remove the oil filters. [[20-007-013-tr — Lubricating Oil Filter (Spin-On)|Refer to Procedure 007-013 in Section 7.]]
- Remove the lubricating oil suction tube. Refer to Procedure 007-035 in Section 7.
- Remove the three capscrews from the suction tube.
- Remove the oil pan suction tube.
- Remove and discard the gasket.
- Remove the dipstick and dipstick tube.

### Remove

Disconnect the breather vent from the hand hole cover.

![[07400015.png]]

Remove the lubricating oil pan adapter

Remove the two 7/16-14 capscrews and the five 3/8-16 capscrews from the flywheel housing.

![[op4adma.png]]

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> This component or assembly weighs greater than 23 kg \[50 lb\]. To prevent serious personal injury, be sure to have assistance or use appropriate lifting equipment to lift this component or assembly.

Remove all the capscrews and the pan adapter.

Remove and discard the gasket (40) and the four bolt seals (41) from the adapter.

![[00r01168.png]]

### Clean and Inspect for Reuse

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> When using solvents, acids, or alkaline materials for cleaning, follow the manufacturer's recommendations for use. Wear goggles and protective clothing to reduce the possibility of personal injury.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Wear appropriate eye and face protection when using compressed air. Flying debris and dirt can cause personal injury.

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Use a solvent that will not harm aluminum.

Use an aluminum-safe solvent to clean the oil pan adapter.

![[07400033.png]]

Inspect the adapter for cracks, fretting, and other damage.

Check the holes for damage.

If damage is found, the oil pan adapter **must** be repaired or replaced.

If the oil pan adapter is damaged, the sealing bead of the gasket will **not** be able to seal and a leak may occur.

![[07k00019.png]]

### Install

Preferred Method

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The engine **must** be completely rolled over to perform this procedure with the Preferred Method. If a complete engine roll over is **not** possible, reference the Optional Method.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Make sure the entire block and oil pan adapter surfaces are clean and dry of oil and debris for optimal gasket sealing and silicon adhesion.

Apply contact adhesive such as 3M™ Spray 77™ or 3M™ 4693 to hold the capscrew seals in position. Do **not** use the 3M™ Spray 77™ or 3M™ 4693 adhesive on the rectangular seal areas.

Install the four new capscrew seals on the oil pan adapter.

![[07k00017.png]]

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Make sure the gear cover, gear cover spacer plate, and related gaskets are flush with the bottom of the block. They should **not** protrude more than 0.05 mm \[0.002 inch\] from the bottom of the block surface. If out of specification, the gear cover spacer plate alignment should be corrected to make sure the oil pan adapter gasket will conform and seal in the T-joint. [[20-001-029-tr — Gear Cover Spacer Plate|Refer to Procedure 001-029]] in Section 1 for gear cover spacer plate.

Apply Cummins® RTV, Part Number 4918882 or 3164070, or equivalent, to the bottom of the cylinder block in the following locations, as illustrated.

![[07k00048.png]]

Apply RTV sealant, Part Number 4918882 or 3164070, or equivalent to the outside edges of the block pan rail.

Apply the new oil pan adapter gasket on the block face oil pan adapter.

Apply RTV sealant, Part Number 4918882 or 3164070, or equivalent to outside edges of oil pan adapter gasket.

Place the new oil pan adapter gasket on the block face.

Be sure the gasket holes align with the holes in the oil pan adapter.

![[00r01169.png]]

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> This component or assembly weighs greater than 23 kg \[50 lb\]. To prevent serious personal injury, be sure to have assistance or use appropriate lifting equipment to lift this component or assembly.

Position the oil pan adapter against the cylinder block and then move it toward the rear of the engine until it contacts the flywheel housing.

![[07k00024.png]]

Lubricate the capscrew threads with clean engine oil.

Install the four washers and capscrews as shown. Push the adapter towards the block and flywheel housing while hand-tightening the capscrews.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> If the two block capscrews can **not** be started, it may be necessary to hand-tighten the two flywheel housing capscrews first so the capscrew holes in the oil pan adapter line up with the block capscrew holes.

The four capscrews shown **must** be tightened alternately and evenly using the following torque steps and sequence to be sure the adapter is seated into the corner of the block and flywheel housing simultaneously.

Install the three capscrews located in the center of the flywheel housing and tighten them in the sequence shown. Use the steps and torque values below when tightening the capscrews.

Torque Value:

![[01400280.png]]

Lubricate the capscrew threads with clean engine oil.

Install the two 7/16-inch washers and capscrews shown.

Tighten the capscrews.

> 65 n•m [48 ft-lb]

![[op4adhh.png]]

Lubricate the capscrew threads with clean engine oil.

Install all capscrews hand-tight before applying torque.

Install capscrews 24 through 28.

These capscrews thread into the front gear cover.

Install the remaining 3/8-inch washers and capscrews (1 through 23).

Do **not** use the 3M™ Spray 77™ or 3M™ 4693 adhesive on the rectangular seal areas.

Install the four new capscrew seals on the oil pan adapter.

![[07k00017.png]]

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Make sure the gear cover, gear cover spacer plate, and related gaskets are flush with the bottom of the block. They should **not** protrude more than 0.05 mm \[0.002 inch\] from the bottom of the block surface. If out of specification, the gear cover spacer plate alignment should be corrected to make sure the oil pan adapter gasket will conform and seal in the T-joint. See Procedure 001-029 in Section 1 for gear cover spacer plate.

Apply Cummins® RTV, Part Number 4918882 or 3164070, or equivalent, to the bottom of the cylinder block in the following locations as shown:

- the joint where the cylinder block meets the front gear housing
- the joint where the cylinder block meets the flywheel housing.

![[07k00048.png]]

Install the new oil pan adapter gasket onto the oil pan adapter.

To ease in assembly, plastic wire ties should be looped though a capscrew hole, toward the outside of the oil pan adapter, to hold the gasket in place.

![[00r01170.png]]

Install wire ties in a minimum of four places on the outside of the oil pan adapter as shown.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Wire ties improperly looped toward the inside of the oil pan adapter can damage the seal bead when the ties are removed and result in a leak.

![[00r01171.png]]

Install a 3/8" guide stud into the flywheel housing bolt hole to assist in the installation of the oil pan adapter.

![[07k00020.png]]

### Finishing Steps

- Install the dipstick tube and dipstick. [[20-007-011-tr — Lubricating Oil Dipstick Tube|Refer to Procedure 007-011 in Section 7.]]
- Install the lubricating oil suction tube. Refer to Procedure 007-035 in Section 7.
- Install new oil filters. [[20-007-013-tr — Lubricating Oil Filter (Spin-On)|Refer to Procedure 007-013 in Section 7.]]
- Install the front engine support bracket. [[20-016-002-tr — Engine Support Bracket, Front|Refer to Procedure 016-002 in Section 16.]]
- Install the turbocharger oil drain hoses. [[20-010-045 — Turbocharger Oil Drain Line|Refer to Procedure 010-045 in Section 10.]]
- Install the oil pan adapter cover. [[20-007-026-tr — Lubricating Oil Pan Adapter Cover Plate|Refer to Procedure 007-026 in Section 7.]]
- Install the oil pan. [[20-007-025-tr — Lubricating Oil Pan|Refer to Procedure 007-025 in Section 7.]]
- Fill the engine with clean 15W-40 oil. [[20-007-037 — Lubricating Oil System|Refer to Procedure 007-037 in Section 7.]]
- Operate the engine to normal operating temperature and check for leaks.
