### Measure

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> This check **must** be performed after the gear cover is removed. If the cover is **not** already removed, refer to Procedure No. 001-031-002.

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> The adjacent (engaging) gear must not turn as the gear being measured is turned.

Position a dial indicator so the tip is contacting the surface of the gear tooth as shown. Do **not** allow the mating gear to turn.

Rotate the gear being measured **clockwise**. Position the indicator to "0". Now rotate the gear **counterclockwise** and read the indicator.

| mm |  | in |
|---|---|---|
| 0.07 | MIN | 0.003 |
| 0.30 | MAX | 0.012 |
