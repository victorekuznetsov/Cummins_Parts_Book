# Fan Drive Idler Pulley Assembly
**Натяжной ролик привода вентилятора в сборе**

> [!abstract] Процедура · `20-008-030-tr`
> **Двигатели:** [[41349633 — QSK19 CM2150 MCRS CPL 3666|41349633]]
> **Семейство:** QSK19
> **Входит в руководства:** [[4021592 — QSK19, QSK19 CM850 MCRS, and QSK19 CM2150 MCRS Service Manual|4021592]]
> **Секции:** Section 8 - Cooling System - Group 08
> **Даты:** изменён 2013-11-05
> **Источник:** [QuickServe](https://quickserve.cummins.com/qs3/pubsys2/xml/en/procedures/20/20-008-030-tr.html) · [PDF-оригинал](https://github.com/victorekuznetsov/Cummins_Parts_Book/raw/claude/cummins-parts-knowledge-base-qa0n50/bulletins/procedures/20-008-030-tr.pdf)

### Preparatory Steps

- Remove the belt guards, if equipped. [[20-008-001 — Belt Guard|Refer to Procedure 008-001 in Section 8.]]
- Remove the fan belt. [[20-008-002-tr — Drive Belt, Cooling Fan|Refer to Procedure 008-002 in Section 8.]]
- Remove the fan drive idler arm assembly. [[20-008-029-tr — Fan Drive Idler Arm Assembly|Refer to Procedure 008-029 in Section 8.]]

![[ck800wa.png]]

### Disassemble

Remove the expansion plug from the pulley.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Some pulleys have a plate and gasket instead of an expansion plug.

Remove the plate and gasket.

![[08a00178.png]]

The locknut has a left hand thread. To loosen the nut turn to the right.

Remove the locknut (1). Lift the pulley (2) from the shaft.

![[08a00179.png]]

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The bearings and spacers are part of a kit. All parts **must** be replaced, if any part is worn or damaged.

Remove the seal (5).

Remove the bearings (3) and (6). Mark them for location.

Remove the inner bearing spacer (4).

![[08a00180.png]]

### Clean and Inspect for Reuse

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Do not allow the force of the compressed air to turn the bearing when cleaning. Bearing damage will result due to lack of lubrication.

Clean and check the parts.

![[08a00181.png]]

Use a hammer and a drift to remove the bearing races (1).

Remove the retaining ring (2).

The thickness of the retaining ring has an affect on the bearing end clearance. The bearing kit contains a new retaining ring.

![[08400443.png]]

Measure the inside diameter.

| Pulley Bore Inside Diameter |  |  |
|---|---|---|
| mm |  | in |
| 45.936 | MIN | 1.8085 |
| 45.961 | MAX | 1.8095 |

![[08400444.png]]

Check the shaft for wear at the seal location.

Measure the outside diameter.

| Shaft Outside Diameter |  |  |
|---|---|---|
| mm |  | in |
| 21.961 | MIN | 0.8646 |
| 21.974 | MAX | 0.8651 |

If the shaft shows any wear at the seal surface, the shaft assembly **must** be replaced.

![[08a00182.png]]

Remove the shaft locknut and the lockwasher.

Be sure the shaft is tight in the bracket. If the shaft is loose, replace both pieces.

Install the parts.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 150 n•m [110 ft-lb]

![[08a00183.png]]

Install the retaining ring.

Use a mandrel (3) and an arbor press to install the bearing races (1). They **must** be in tight contact with the retaining ring.

![[08400445.png]]

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Do not lubricate the seal or the seal surface of the shaft. The seal and seal surface must be clean and dry.

Use water pump type grease to lubricate the rear bearings (11) and the bearing race.

Place the bearing on the race.

Use a mandrel (7) to install the seal (10). The seal **must not** be more than 0.51 mm \[0.020 in\] below the top surface.

![[fa600aa.png]]

### Assemble

Install the pulley on the shaft.

The bearing **must** slide easily over the shaft. If it does **not**, check for chips or burrs.

![[08a00185.png]]

Install the inner bearing spacer (4).

Use water pump type grease to lubricate the bearing and the bearing race.

Install the bearing (3).

![[08a00186.png]]

The locknut has a left hand thread. To tighten the locknut, turn to the left.

Install the locknut.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 150 n•m [110 ft-lb]

![[08a00187.png]]

Use a dial indicator to measure the clearance.

| Bearing End Clearance |  |  |
|---|---|---|
| mm |  | in |
| 0.08 | MIN | 0.003 |
| 0.25 | MAX | 0.010 |

If the clearance is **not** within specifications, be sure the spacer has been installed correctly.

Remove the locknut and inspect the assembly.

The bearings **must** touch the spacer. The bearing races **must** touch the retaining ring.

![[08a00184.png]]

Fill the front bearing cavity 2/3-full with a water pump type grease.

![[08a00188.png]]

Install the sealing plug or plate.

![[08a00189.png]]

### Inspect

Every 6000 hours or 2 years, inspect the fan drive idler pulley assembly. Rebuild or replace the idler pulley as necessary. Contact a Cummins® Authorized Repair Location.

![[08400041.png]]

### Finishing Steps

- Install the fan drive idler arm assembly. [[20-008-029-tr — Fan Drive Idler Arm Assembly|Refer to Procedure 008-029 in Section 8.]]
- Install the fan belt. [[20-008-002-tr — Drive Belt, Cooling Fan|Refer to Procedure 008-002 in Section 8.]]
- Install the fan guards, if equipped. Refer to Procedure 008-001 in Section 8.
- Operate the engine and check for noise.
