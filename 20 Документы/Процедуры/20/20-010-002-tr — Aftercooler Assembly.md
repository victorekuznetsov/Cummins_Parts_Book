# Aftercooler Assembly
**Охладитель наддувочного воздуха в сборе**

### Preparatory Steps

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Do not remove the pressure cap from a hot engine. Wait until the coolant temperature is below 50°C \[120°F\] before removing the pressure cap. Heated coolant spray or steam can cause personal injury.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Coolant is toxic. Keep away from children and pets. If not reused, dispose of in accordance with local environmental regulations.

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Use caution when draining coolant that the coolant is not spilled or drained into the bilge area. Do not pump coolant overboard. If the coolant is not reused, it must be disposed of in accordance with local environmental regulations.

- Drain the cooling system. Refer to Procedure [[20-008-018-tr — Cooling System|008-018]].
- Remove the air crossover pipe. Refer to Procedure [[20-010-019-tr — Air Crossover|010-019]].
- Remove the aftercooler vent line. Refer to Procedure [[20-008-017 — Coolant Vent Lines|008-017]].
- Remove the aftercooler coolant inlet and outlet hoses. Refer to Procedure [[20-010-006 — Aftercooler Coolant Tube|010-006]].
- Remove the fuel tubing mounting clamp and fuel tubes. Refer to Procedures [[20-006-013-tr — Fuel Drain Lines|006-013]] or [[20-006-024-tr — Fuel Supply Lines|006-024]].
- Remove the wiring harness. Refer to Procedure 019-043 in the Troubleshooting and Repair Manual Electronic Control System QSK19 CM850, Modular Common Rail System Series Engine, Bulletin 4021493.
- Remove the intake manifold pressure sensors. Refer to Procedure 019-061 in the Troubleshooting and Repair Manual, Electronic Control System, QSK19 CM850, Modular Common Rail, Bulletin 4021493.
- Remove the intake manifold temperature sensors. Refer to Procedure 019-059 in the Troubleshooting and Repair Manual, Electronic Control System, QSK19 CM850, Modular Common Rail, Bulletin 4021493.

### Remove

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> This component or assembly weighs greater than 23 kg \[50 lb\]. To prevent serious personal injury, be sure to have assistance or use appropriate lifting equipment to lift this component or assembly.

Remove two mounting capscrews. Install two 9.525 mm \[0.375 in\] guide studs.

Use a hoist, two tee handles, and a lifting sling. Attach the tee handles, sling, and hoist. Raise the hoist until there is tension on the sling.

Remove the remaining capscrews and the aftercooler assembly.

Remove and discard the gaskets.

![[06400100.png]]

Remove and discard the aftercooler bolt seals from the aftercooler housing.

![[08400060.png]]

Rail Applications

Remove the 24 capscrews and intake manifold.

![[17400065.png]]

### Disassemble

Remove the capscrews, seal retainer (3), gaskets (4), and seals (5). Discard the seals and gaskets.

![[08400079.png]]

Remove the aftercooler cover capscrews and cover.

![[08400078.png]]

Remove the element and gaskets.

Inspect the cover and intake manifold gasket surfaces for damage.

Discard the gaskets.

![[at4etha.png]]

### Inspect for Reuse

Clean and inspect the aftercooler for reuse. Look into the cylinder head ports and into the aftercooler for indications of coolant leakage. Refer to Procedure [[20-008-018-tr — Cooling System|008-018]].

Replace the aftercooler core if leaks are found.

![[08400059.png]]

Use a hose plugged with a pipe plug. Plug one water tube as shown.

Attach an air line and gauge to the remaining tube.

Pressure check the element.

Apply air pressure and check for leaks.

| kpa |  | psi |
|---|---|---|
| 415 | MAX | 60 |

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Heating the tank to 50°C \[120°F\] will improve the test results.

![[at4etsa.png]]

### Assemble

Install the element and two new gaskets.

![[at4etha.png]]

Install the aftercooler cover and capscrews.

Do **not** tighten the capscrews until after the seals are installed.

![[08400078.png]]

Use clean engine oil to lubricate the seals.

Install the seal on the retainer.

Use clean engine oil to lubricate the outside dimension of the water bosses on the aftercooler element.

Slide the gasket on the water bosses. Slide the seal and retainer on the tube.

Install the capscrews and tighten.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 35 n•m [25 ft-lb]

![[08400079.png]]

Install the 24 capscrews.

Tighten using the following steps and values.

| **Torque Value:** | Step 1 | 35 n.m \[25 ft-lb\] |
|---|---|---|

|  | Step 2 | 50 n.m \[35 ft-lb\] |
|---|---|---|

### Install

Install the bolt seals in the aftercooler housing.

![[08400060.png]]

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Use guide studs long enough to protrude beyond the installed part.

Install 12 guide studs in the upper row of capscrew holes in the cylinder heads.

Install the gaskets on the studs with the raised bead on the gasket toward the cylinder head.

![[06400101.png]]

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> This component or assembly weighs greater than 23 kg \[50 lb\]. To prevent serious personal injury, be sure to have assistance or use appropriate lifting equipment to lift this component or assembly.

Install the aftercooler and the bottom row of capscrews. Tighten the capscrews **only** enough to hold the part.

Remove the guide studs and install the top row of capscrews.

### Finishing Steps

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Coolant is toxic. Keep away from children and pets. If not reused, dispose of in accordance with local environmental regulations.

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Use caution when filling the cooling system that the coolant is not spilled into the bilge area. Do not pump coolant overboard. If the coolant is spilled, it must be disposed of in accordance with local environmental regulations

- Install the intake manifold pressure sensors. Refer to Procedure 019-061 in the Troubleshooting and Repair Manual, Electronic Control System, QSK19 CM850 Modular Common Rail, Bulletin 4021493.
- Install the intake manifold temperature sensors. Refer to Procedure 019-059 in the Troubleshooting and Repair Manual, Electronic Control System, QSK19 CM850 Modular Common Rail, Bulletin 4021493.
- Install the wiring harness. Refer to Procedure 019-043 in the Troubleshooting and Repair Manual, Electronic Control System, QSK19 CM850, Modular Common Rail, Bulletin 4021493
- Install the fuel tubing mounting clamp and fuel tubes. Refer to Procedures [[20-006-013-tr — Fuel Drain Lines|006-013]] or [[20-006-024-tr — Fuel Supply Lines|006-024]].
- Install the aftercooler coolant inlet and outlet hoses. Refer to Procedure [[20-010-006 — Aftercooler Coolant Tube|010-006]].
- Install the aftercooler vent line. Refer to Procedure [[20-008-017 — Coolant Vent Lines|008-017]].
- Install the crossover pipe. Refer to Procedure [[20-010-019-tr — Air Crossover|010-019]].
- Fill the cooling system and check for leaks.
