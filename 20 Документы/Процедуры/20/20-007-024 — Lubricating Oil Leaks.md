### Inspect

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> When using a steam cleaner, wear safety glasses or a face shield, as well as protective clothing. Hot steam can cause serious personal injury.

Use a steam cleaner or a high pressure washer to clean the engine.

![[ew400ec.png]]

Operate the engine until the coolant temperature reaches 80°C \[176°F\]. Inspect the exterior of the engine for leaking gaskets, seals, o-rings, pipe plugs, or fittings.

Before replacing any gaskets, check that the capscrews are tightened to the correct torque values. Refer to Procedure [[99-018-009 — Capscrew Markings and Torque Values|018-009]] for the capscrew torque listings.

![[14400006.png]]

Check for a loose, broken, or missing oil dipstick tube, dipstick, or oil filler cap.

![[07400006.png]]

Check the engine crankcase breather elements and breather hose for restriction.

If **not** restricted, check blowby. Refer to Procedure [[20-014-005 — Engine Testing (Engine Dynamometer)|014-005]]. High blowby can cause gasket and seal leakage.

![[07400005.png]]

### Fluorescent Dye Tracer

Add one unit of Part Number 3376891 Fluorescent Tracer to each 38 liters \[10 U.S. gallons\] of engine lubricating oil. Refer to Procedure [[20-007-025-tr — Lubricating Oil Pan|007-025]] for oil pan capacity.

Operate the engine at low idle for 10 minutes.

![[ew400kc.png]]

Shut the engine OFF.

![[oi400sb.png]]

Use a high intensity black light, Part Number 3163337 or equivalent, to inspect for oil leakage. Oil will glow a dark blue color.

![[oi400sc.png]]

If an oil leak into the coolant is suspected, check the coolant.

![[oi400vr.png]]
