### Remove

Marine Applications

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Do not remove insulation from inside of turbocharger heat shield. Insulation is permanently affixed to the heat shield.

Remove the turbocharger heat shield mounting capscrews.

Remove the turbocharger heat shield.

![[10400065.png]]

### Clean

Marine Applications

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> When using solvents, acids, or alkaline materials for cleaning, follow the manufacturer's recommendations for use. Wear goggles and protective clothing to reduce the possibility of personal injury.

Use solvent to clean the turbocharger heat shield.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Take care **not** to damage the turbocharger heat shield insulation.

![[10400066.png]]

### Inspect for Reuse

Marine Applications

Inspect the turbocharger heat shield for cracks, holes, and damaged bolt holes.

![[10400067.png]]

### Install

Marine Applications

Install the turbocharger heat shield.

Install and tighten the turbocharger heat shield mounting capscrews.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 35 n•m [25 ft-lb]
