### General Information

The capscrews shown will be referred thoughout this procedure as:

1. Outboard Horizontal.
2. Inboard Horizontal.
3. Inboard Vertical.

### Preparatory Steps

Rail Applications

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Do not remove the pressure cap from a hot engine. Wait until the coolant temperature is below 50°C \[120°F\] before removing the pressure cap. Heated coolant spray or steam can cause personal injury.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Coolant is toxic. Keep away form children and pets. If not rescued, dispose of in accordance with local environmental regulations.

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Use caution when draining oil or replacing filters that the oil is not spilled or drained into the bilge area. The oil and oil filters must be disposed of in accordance with local environmental regulations.

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Use caution when draining coolant that the coolant is not spilled or drained into the bilge area. Do not pump coolant overboard. If the coolant is not reused, it must be disposed of in accordance with local environmental regulations.

- Remove the intake and exhaust pipes from the turbocharger.
- Remove the turbocharger. [[20-010-033-tr — Turbocharger|Refer to Procedure 010-033 (Turbocharger) in Section 10]]

### Clean and Inspect for Reuse

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> When using solvents, acids, or alkaline materials for cleaning, follow the manufacturer's recommendations for use. Wear goggles and protective clothing to reduce the possibility of personal injury.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Wear appropriate eye and face protection when using compressed air. Flying debris and dirt can cause personal injury.

Use a soda blast process to remove paint from the bracket. Do **not** sand blast or bead blast parts, as this will cause damage.

Once the paint is removed, clean the bracket with solvent.

Dry with compressed air.

![[10400399.png]]

Use a magnetic particle inspection to inspect the bracket for cracks or other damage.

Inspection **must** be performed by an operator holding at least a level 2 ASNT or PCN certification.

![[10400470.png]]

Inspect the welds in the highlighted areas closely.

Replace the turbocharger support bracket if any cracks or other damage is found.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Do **not** weld or repair the turbocharger support bracket, as this will cause further damage to the bracket.

![[10400471.png]]

Inspect the connection brace (3) for cracks and wear damage. Replace if damage is found.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The connection brace (3) **must** be replaced at engine overhaul.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The vertical capscrews (1) **must** be replaced anytime the capscrews are removed from the engine.

![[10400472.png]]

Inspect the capscrews for cracks and wear damage. Replace if damage is found.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> All capscrews **must** be replaced at engine overhaul.

![[10400381.png]]

> Each time the turbocharger support bracket is installed, the following parts **must** be replaced:

- Clamping plate
- Two inboard vertical capscrews.

> At engine overhaul the following parts **must** be replaced:

- All fasteners, washers, and lockplates
- Connection brace
- Clamping plate.

Thread the stud into the turbocharger inlet exhaust connection with the stud pilot to the outside in all four locations. Position the gasket (2) on the studs and mount the turbocharger. Install the 10 mm \[0.4 in\] nuts (3) on the studs and tighten finger tight to hold the turbocharger in place.

Tighten the nuts diagonally. Check thread protrusion. There should be 2 to 3 threads showing.

### Finishing Steps

Rail Applications

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Coolant is toxic. Keep away form children and pets. If not reused, dispose of in accordance with local environmental regulations.

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Use caution when filling the cooling system that the coolant is not spilled into the bilge area. Do not pump coolant overboard. If the coolant is spilled, it must be disposed of in accordance with local environmental regulations

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Use caution when filling the lubricating system that the oil is not spilled into the bilge area. Do not pump oil overboard. If the oil is spilled, it must be disposed of in accordance with local environmental regulations

- Install the intake and exhaust pipes to the turbocharger.
- Paint the turbocharger bracket assembly with a Cummins Inc. approved paint, such as service tool, Part Number 3824515.
- Operate the engine at 71°C \[160°F\] coolant temperature and check for leaks.
- Check the V-band clamp torque 8.5 N•m \[75 in-lb\] after operating the engine.
