# Rock Shields
**Защита от камней**

> [!abstract] Процедура · `20-016-011`
> **Двигатели:** [[41349633 — QSK19 CM2150 MCRS CPL 3666|41349633]]
> **Семейство:** QSK19
> **Входит в руководства:** [[4021592 — QSK19, QSK19 CM850 MCRS, and QSK19 CM2150 MCRS Service Manual|4021592]]
> **Секции:** Section 16 - Mounting Adaptations - Group 16
> **Даты:** изменён 2006-06-30
> **Источник:** [QuickServe](https://quickserve.cummins.com/qs3/pubsys2/xml/en/procedures/20/20-016-011.html) · [PDF-оригинал](https://github.com/victorekuznetsov/Cummins_Parts_Book/raw/claude/cummins-parts-knowledge-base-qa0n50/bulletins/procedures/20-016-011.pdf)

### Remove

Rail Applications

Remove the twelve capscrews, washers, and two rock shield panels from the rock shield bracket assembly.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The removal of the rock shield to this level may provide required access to the intake side of the engine for service. The following procedure will provide additional access to the engine.

![[17400096.png]]

Remove the twelve capscrews and “T” brackets from the rock shield mounting brackets located on the flywheel housing, front gear cover, and lubricating oil pan adapter.

![[17400100.png]]

Remove the six capscrews and two rock shield brackets from the flywheel housing.

Remove the two capscrews, washers, and rock shield bracket from the front gear cover.

Remove the three capscrews, washers, and rock shield bracket from the lubricating oil pan adapter.

![[17400095.png]]

### Install

Rail Applications

Install the six capscrews and two rock shield brackets on the flywheel housing.

Install the two capscrews, washers, and rock shield bracket on the front gear cover.

Install the three capscrews, washers, and rock shield bracket on the lubricating oil pan adapter.

| 1/2 - 13 x 1.5” | 75 n.m | \[55 ft-lb\] |
|---|---|---|

| 5/8 - 11 x 1.5” | 150 n.m | \[110 ft-lb\] |
|---|---|---|

| 5/8 - 11 x 1.75” | 150 n.m | \[ 110 ft-lb\] |
|---|---|---|

![[17400095.png]]

Install the twelve capscrews and the two “T” brackets on the rock shield mounting brackets located on the flywheel housing, front gear cover, and lubricating oil pan adapter. Tighten the capscrews.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 75 n•m [55 ft-lb]

![[17400100.png]]

Install the twelve capscrews, washers, and two rock shield panels on the rock shield bracket assembly. Tighten the capscrews.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 8 n•m [72 in-lb]
