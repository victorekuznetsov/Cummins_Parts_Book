### General Information

A used oil analysis can help diagnose internal damage and determine if it was caused by one of the following:

- Oil diluted with coolant
- Oil diluted with fuel.

![[lt801ka.png]]

### Pressure Test

Coolant-Diluted Lubricating Oil

For lubricating oil that is diluted with coolant, Perform the cooling system pressure test. Refer to Procedure [[56-008-018-tr — Cooling System|008-018]].

![[cy8cysb.png]]

### Fluorescent Dye Tracer

Lubricating Oil Dilution

Perform this test on oil that is diluted with fuel.

This test is **not** effective on a cold engine, less than 21°C \[70°F\] coolant temperature, or with a loose overhead setting.

Install an isolated fuel supply tank to the inlet and drain lines. Add fluorescent tracer, Part Number 3376891, to the fuel supply tank.

![[07600328.png]]

Remove the valve cover mounting capscrews, but do **not** remove the valve covers.

![[07400023.png]]

Start the engine and operate it at high idle for 30 seconds.

Run the engine at low idle.

Complete the next step within 5 minutes.

![[ew400va.png]]

While the engine is operating at low idle, remove the valve covers and gaskets. Refer to Procedure [[56-003-011-tr — Rocker Lever Cover|003-011]]

![[07600344.png]]

Injectors normally have a small amount of fuel leakage. Fuel will be a yellow color.

Use a black light to find fuel leaks from inside or around the injector.

![[07400024.png]]

If there is excess leakage around the outside of the injector, remove the injector and replace all four o-rings. Refer to Procedure [[56-006-026-tr — Injector|006-026]].

![[07400025.png]]

If there is excess leakage from inside the injector, replace the injector. Refer to Procedure [[56-006-026-tr — Injector|006-026]].

![[07400027.png]]

Install the rocker lever covers. Refer to Procedure [[56-003-011-tr — Rocker Lever Cover|003-011]].

![[07400023.png]]

QSK60 Marine Applications

Perform this test on oil that is diluted with fuel.

This test is **not** effective on a cold engine, less than 21°C \[70°F\] coolant temperature, or with a loose overhead setting.

Install an isolated fuel supply tank to the inlet and drain lines. Add fluorescent tracer, Part Number 3376891, to the fuel supply tank.

![[07600328.png]]

Remove the aftercooler heat shields. Refer to Procedure [[56-010-129-tr — Aftercooler Heat Shield|010-129]].

Remove the valve cover mounting capscrews, but do **not** remove the valve covers.

![[07400023.png]]

Start the engine and operate it at high idle for 30 seconds.

Run the engine at low idle.

Complete the next step within 5 minutes.

![[ew400va.png]]

While the engine is operating at low idle, remove the valve covers and gaskets. Refer to Procedure [[56-003-011-tr — Rocker Lever Cover|003-011]]

![[07600344.png]]

Injectors normally have a small amount of fuel leakage. Fuel will be a yellow color.

Use a black light to find fuel leaks from inside or around the injector.

![[07400024.png]]

If there is excess leakage around the outside of the injector, remove the injector and replace all four o-rings. Refer to Procedure [[56-006-026-tr — Injector|006-026]].

![[07400025.png]]

If there is excess leakage from inside the injector, replace the injector. Refer to Procedure [[56-006-026-tr — Injector|006-026]].

![[07400027.png]]

Install the rocker lever covers. Refer to Procedure [[56-003-011-tr — Rocker Lever Cover|003-011]].

Install the aftercooler heat shields. Refer to Procedure [[56-010-129-tr — Aftercooler Heat Shield|010-129]].
