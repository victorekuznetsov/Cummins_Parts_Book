# Intercooler Assembly

> [!abstract] Процедура · `56-010-083-shopds`
> **Двигатели:** [[33210083 — QSK60 CM500 CPL 2699|33210083]], [[33219033 — QSK60 CM500 CPL 2848|33219033]], [[33224343 — QSK60 CM500 CPL 2849|33224343]]

> **Даты:** изменён 2019-06-13

### Remove

It is a good practice to mark the location of all lines during removal.

Disconnect the coolant lines from the thermostat housing.

Position coolant lines aside.

![[08600242.png]]

Remove the 12 capscrews from each of the four intercooler intake crossovers and the two intercoolers.

Remove and discard the gasket.

![[10600104.png]]

Loosen the hose clamps on each end of the four air connection hoses for the four intercooler intake crossovers and the four low-pressure turbochargers.

![[10600103.png]]

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The left rear high-pressure turbocharger connection is shown. The other high-pressure turbocharger connections are similar.

Loosen the two intake hose clamps on the four high-pressure turbochargers and the two intercoolers.

Disconnect the hose clamp at the intercooler.

![[10600097.png]]

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The front intercooler water supply and return connections are shown. The rear intercooler connections are similar.

Disconnect the water supply and return piping from the intercoolers.

![[10600118.png]]

Remove the capscrews from each of the four intercooler supports.

![[10600119.png]]

Remove the lifting eye from the bracket between the intercooler and the two-stage frame.

![[10600120.png]]

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> The QSV81G and QSV91G engines can weigh as much as 11,000 kg \[24,200 lbs\]. Use a properly rated hoist and engine lifting fixture, Part Number 3163264, to lift the engine. Rigging and lifting must be done by trained, experienced personnel.

Install a M12 x 1.75 threaded eye bolt in the top of the intercoolers.

Remove the intercoolers.

![[10600121.png]]
