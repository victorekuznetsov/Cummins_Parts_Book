### General Information

QSK45 Engines

The QSK45 engine exhaust manifold is designed so that all six cylinders on each bank are connected to a single common outlet located near the center, between number 3 and number 4 cylinders.

![[10600099.png]]

QSK60 Single Stage Industrial and Power Generation

The QSK60 engine exhaust manifold is designed as a pulse-style with twin high-pressure turbocharging on each bank. Each turbocharger serves four cylinders. The turbocharger exhaust outlet feeds into a main exhaust outlet for each bank.

![[11600073.png]]

QSK60 Marine Applications

The marine exhaust system used on the QSK60 marine engine is designed with unique flanges.

The marine exhaust manifold side profile is shown in the illustration.

![[11600086.png]]

The marine exhaust manifold bellows has a different design than the industrial exhaust manifold bellows.

The marine exhaust manifold bellows is shown in the illustration.

![[11600088.png]]

The exhaust manifold bellows gasket is located in the end of the bellows.

The bellows will **not** seal without the gasket in place.

![[11600089.png]]

The illustration shows the marine exhaust manifold bellows with the gasket fitted in place.

### Remove

Industrial and Power Generation

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> The exhaust manifolds must be disassembled to remove them from the engine. Failure to disassemble the exhaust manifolds will result in exhaust manifold bellows damage.

When removing the exhaust manifolds, remove one section of the exhaust manifold at a time, beginning from either end of the manifold. Note the location and orientation of each section during removal.

Remove the exhaust manifold mounting capscrew lock tabs. Discard the lock tabs.

Remove the exhaust manifold mounting capscrews and spacers.

![[02600011.png]]

Note the direction of the bellows. They **must** be installed correctly for proper engine performance. If a directional arrow is **not** visible, mark the bellows. The open end of the sleeve inside the bellows **always** points in the direction of exhaust flow (toward the turbine inlet).

Remove the V-band clamp from either end of the bellows.

Remove the end section of the exhaust manifold.

Discard the manifold gaskets.

![[11600054.png]]

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Never attempt to compress the exhaust manifold bellows. Bellows compression can result in damage to the bellows and an exhaust leak.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> It is **not always** necessary to remove all exhaust manifold sections and bellows. Remove **only** those components required.

Remove the V-band clamp and the bellows section from the next section of the exhaust manifold.

Repeat the steps above until the required sections of the exhaust manifold and bellows have been removed.

![[11600055.png]]

### Clean and Inspect for Reuse

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> When using a steam cleaner, wear safety glasses or a face shield, as well as protective clothing. Hot steam can cause personal injury.

Use a steam cleaner to clean the exhaust manifold and components.

Inspect the exhaust manifold components for cracks, warpage, capscrew marks in the manifold flange holes, and leaks at the joints.

Inspect the bellows for cracks or other damage.

If any of the sections leak or are damaged, the manifold section **must** be replaced.

If capscrew thread marks are visible in the manifold flange holes, rework the holes and slots to new dimensions.

![[11600307.png]]

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> In old manifolds, the flange holes have a 13.5 mm \[0.531 in\] diameter and the slots are 13.5 x 10.25 mm \[0.531 x 0.4035 in\]. In new manifolds, the flange holes have been increased to 14 mm \[0.551 in\] diameter and the slots to 14 x 10.5 mm \[0.551 x 0.4133 in\]. The holes and slots center axes remain in the same positions as in the previous designs. If there is capscrew damage or capscrew replacement, the holes and slots in the old manifolds **must** be machined to the dimensions of the new manifolds. The increased clearance between the capscrews and the holes and slots in manifolds will reduce the capscrew load due to thermal expansion of the manifold during engine operation.

Old Exhaust Manifolds

1. 13.5 \[0.4035 in\]

2. 10.25 mm \[0.4035 in\]

3. 13.5 mm \[0.531 in\]

New Exhaust Manifolds

4. 14 mm \[0.551 in\]

5. 10.5 mm \[0.4133 in\]

6. 14 mm \[0.551 in\]

![[11600301.png]]

Place a straight rule, or equivalent, over the contact surface of each the exhaust manifolds as shown in the illustration.

Use feeler gauges to measure if there is any gap between the straight edge and the contact surface of the manifold. If the gap is greater than 0.2 mm \[0.0078 in\], it is **not** to be reused.

### Install

A manifold-centering dowel tool should be fabricated locally to assist in the installation of the gaskets and the exhaust manifold sections.

![[11600302.png]]

Use the dimensioned illustration to fabricate four manifold centering dowels from a length of 14 mm \[0.5511 in\] diameter standard steel bar. The tolerance for dimensions in the illustration is ±0.1 mm \[±0.0039 in\].

This tool is intended for manual use.

![[11600303.png]]

Industrial and Power Generation

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The exhaust manifold for the QSK60 is shown. The procedures for the QSK45 are similar.

The exhaust manifold **must** be assembled as it is being installed.

Do **not** tighten the bellows clamps until all the exhaust manifolds are installed.

Apply anti-sieze compound, Part Number 3824879, to the exhaust mounting capscrews.

The exhaust manifold bellows are directional. Make sure they are installed in the proper manner when installing the exhaust manifold.

![[11600057.png]]

Ensure that the threads in the cylinder heads are free from any debris. If needed, the threads may be cleaned by tapping out the thread.

All exhaust manifold gaskets **must** be installed with the bead toward the manifold.

Two manifold centering dowels **must** be used to position each exhaust manifold gasket onto each exhaust port.

Hand tighten the centering dowels into the lower holes of right bank cylinder heads number 5 and 6, or left bank cylinder heads number 3 and 4.

Install the exhaust manifold gaskets onto the dowels. Make sure the gasket side with the embossed bead (marked "OUT") is facing away from the cylinder head exhaust port. Make sure the gasket is properly aligned.

![[11600304.png]]

Place the exhaust manifold section onto the centering dowels.

Note there are two different capscrews available for use; a stainless steel capscrew and a steel capscrew. The stainless steel capscrew can be identified as it will have the part number on the capscrew head. The stainless steel capscrew **must** also be fitted with the new spacers, these can be identified as they have two grooves around the circumference.

For the stainless steel capscrew, lubricate the threads and the capscrew underhead with Loctite® LB8009 anti-seize lubricant. Do **not** use copper grease to lubricate the stainless steel capscrew.

For the steel capscrew, lubricate the threads and capscrew underhead with copper grease.

Install capscrews and spacers into the upper holes and center the manifold section.

Tighten the upper capscrews.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 10 n•m [89 in-lb]

Remove the centering dowels from the lower holes and install the capscrews and spacers. Hand tighten the capscrews.

Tighten all eight capscrews in the exhaust manifold section.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 47 n•m [35 ft-lb]
