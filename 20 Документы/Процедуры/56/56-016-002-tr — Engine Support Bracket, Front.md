# Engine Support Bracket, Front
**Передний кронштейн опоры двигателя**

### Preparatory Steps

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Batteries can emit explosive gases. To reduce the possibility of personal injury, always ventilate the compartment before servicing the batteries. To avoid arcing, remove the negative (-) battery cable first, and attach the negative (-) battery cable last.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> This component or assembly weighs greater than 23 kg \[50 lb\]. To prevent serious personal injury, be sure to have assistance or use appropriate lifting equipment to lift this component or assembly.

- Disconnect the batteries. See equipment manufacturer service information.
- Remove the fan drive belt, if applicable. [[56-008-002-tr — Drive Belt, Cooling Fan|Refer to Procedure 008-002 in Section 8]].
- Remove the vibration damper. [[56-001-052-tr — Vibration Damper, Viscous|Refer to Procedure 001-052 in Section 1]].
- Remove the crankshaft adapter. [[56-001-017-tr — Crankshaft Adapter|Refer to Procedure 001-017 in Section 1]].

![[ck800wa.png]]

### Remove

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> This component or assembly weighs greater than 23 kg \[50 lb\]. To prevent serious personal injury, be sure to have assistance or use appropriate lifting equipment to lift this component or assembly.

Install the engine lifting fixture (1), Part Number 3163264, and lifting straps (2).

![[22600034.png]]

Disconnect the lubrication tube from the top of the support bracket.

Remove the capscrews that attach the front engine support to the equipment frame.

Lift the engine off the front support bracket.

![[16600004.png]]

Remove the front engine support.

![[16600005.png]]

### Inspect for Reuse

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> This component or assembly weighs greater than 23 kg \[50 lb\]. To prevent serious personal injury, be sure to have assistance or use appropriate lifting equipment to lift this component or assembly.

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Damaged engine mounts and brackets can cause the engine to move out of alignment, damage the driveline components in the equipment, and result in vibration complaints.

Inspect the front engine mount for cracks or damage.

Inspect all mounting brackets for cracks or damaged bolt holes.

Replace the bracket if cracked or damaged.

![[16600006.png]]

Measure the front support bore.

| Front Support Bore |  |  |  |
|---|---|---|---|
|  | mm |  | in |
| Previous Engine Support Bracket | 279.45 | MIN | 11.00 |
|  | 279.95 | MAX | 11.02 |
| New Engine Support Bracket | 294.00 | MIN | 11.57 |
|  | 294.132 | MAX | 11.58 |

If the front support bore is **not** within specifications, the front engine support bracket **must** be replaced.

![[16600007.png]]

### Install

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> This component or assembly weighs greater than 23 kg \[50 lb\]. To prevent serious personal injury, be sure to have assistance or use appropriate lifting equipment to lift this component or assembly.

Lubricate the inside diameter of the front support and the outside diameter of the front gear cover wear ring with industrial grease.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Make sure the offset on the mounting pads is facing away from the front of the engine.

Install the front engine support over the wear ring.

![[16600005.png]]

Lower the engine until the front engine support is in position.

Install and tighten the mounting capscrews.

Connect the lubrication tube from the top of the support bracket.

See equipment manufacturer service information for torque specifications.

Remove engine lifting fixture.

![[16600004.png]]

Use high performance, industrial-type grease (NLGI Grade Number 2) fortified with extreme pressure (EP) additives, such as BP Energrease™ FPG or Castrol Spheerol™ EPL 2 grease, or its equivalent.

If the above greases are **not** available locally, contact a Cummins® Distributor for advice on other Cummins® approved equivalents.

Lubricate the support until grease appears on the outside of the support.

![[07600260.png]]

### Finishing Steps

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> This component or assembly weighs greater than 23 kg \[50 lb\]. To prevent serious personal injury, be sure to have assistance or use appropriate lifting equipment to lift this component or assembly.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Batteries can emit explosive gases. To reduce the possibility of personal injury, always ventilate the compartment before servicing the batteries. To avoid arcing, remove the negative (-) battery cable first, and attach the negative (-) battery cable last.

- Install the crankshaft adapter. [[56-001-017-tr — Crankshaft Adapter|Refer to Procedure 001-017]].
- Install the vibration dampers. [[56-001-052-tr — Vibration Damper, Viscous|Refer to Procedure 001-052]].
- Install fan drive belt, if applicable. [[56-008-002-tr — Drive Belt, Cooling Fan|Refer to Procedure 008-002]].
- Connect the batteries. See equipment manufacturer service information.
