### General Information

All Applications

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> This procedure applies to Stage 1 filters on engines with electronically actuated injectors **only**.

Use the following procedure for engines with mechanically actuated injectors. [[56-006-015-tr — Fuel Filter (Spin-On Type)|Refer to Procedure 006-015 in Section 6.]]

Use the following procedure for Stage 2 fuel filters. Refer to Procedure 006-076 in Section 6.

The Stage 1 filters are mounted off the engine and use seven micron filtration media. The fuel priming pump is mounted with the Stage 1 filter assembly. The Stage 1 filters are mounted at the original equipment manufacturer (OEM) discretion, provided Cummins Inc. application guidelines are met.

The FH234 series is on the left of the illustration and the FH239 series is on the right. The filter consists of the following components:

1. Stage 1 filter
2. Water in fuel sensor
3. Drain valve
4. Lift pump.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The following steps show the FH234 series, but the FH239 series steps are very similar.

![[06s00030.png]]

Marine Applications

Marine applications use either an FH234 Sea Pro™ spin-on style filter or an FH240 Sea Pro™ cartridge style filter.

Marine applications use a filter head that utilizes valves to allow for changing of the filters while the engine is in operation.

The valves are located between the inlet and outlet fuel manifolds on the filter assembly. They consist of a lever type valve with an ON or OFF position.

FH234 Stage 1 filters are mounted off the engine and use seven micron filtration media. The fuel priming pump is mounted with the Stage 1 filter assembly. The Stage 1 filters are mounted at the OEM discretion, provided Cummins Inc. application guidelines are met.

![[06600374.png]]

FH240 Stage 1 filters are mounted off engine and use five micron filtration media. The fuel priming pump is mounted on-engine. The Stage 1 filters are mounted at OEM discretion, provided Cummins Inc. application guidelines are met.

The Sea Pro™ FH240 Classed filter consists of the following:

1. Nut
2. Nut Seal
3. Service Cap
4. Service Cap Seal
5. Spring
6. Stage 1 Filter Element and Seals (Includes 2, 4, and 7)
7. Stand Pipe Gasket
8. Filter Housing
9. Fuel Out Manifold
10. Fuel In Manifold
11. Handle
12. Drain Valve
13. Water in fuel Sensor
14. Mounting Bracket.

![[06f00020.png]]

#### Valve Operation - Cartridge Filters

- For dual unit flow, the valve handle should be facing upward.
- For single unit flow, rotate the valve handle either left or right 90 degrees depending on which unit is to be used.
- To shut off flow, rotate the valve handle 180 degrees from the dual flow position.

### Preparatory Steps

Industrial Applications

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Depending on the circumstance, diesel fuel is flammable. When inspecting or performing service or repairs on the fuel system, to reduce the possibility of fire and resulting severe personal injury, death or property damage, never smoke or allow sparks or flames (such as pilot lights, electrical switches, or welding equipment) in the work area.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно

### Remove

Industrial Applications

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Depending on the circumstance, fuel is flammable. Keep all cigarettes, flames, pilot lights, arcing equipment, and switches out of the work area and areas sharing ventilation to reduce the possibility of severe personal injury or death when working on the fuel system.

Stage 1 Filters:

Place a suitable container under the fuel filter to be replaced.

Use a collar/vent cap wrench, Part Number 3944451 S, to open the vent cap to break the air lock in the filter.

![[06600363.png]]

Open the fuel drain valve and allow the fuel level to drain to a point below the collar. Close the drain valve.

![[06600364.png]]

Use the collar/vent cap wrench, Part Number 4944451 S, to loosen the collar.

![[06600365.png]]

Remove the clear cover, filter spring, fuel filter element, and o-ring.

Discard the o-ring.

Remove the sealing grommet from the filter stud and discard.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The FH239 series has two filter elements that are stacked together. Make sure both elements are removed and replaced at the same time.

![[06600370.png]]

### Clean

All Applications

> The Industrial application filter is shown. Although different in appearance, the procedure remains the same for Industrial and Marine applications.

Check the sealing surface of the filter body. Wipe the surface with a clean, lint-free cloth to remove any debris or dirt.

### Install

Industrial Applications

Stage 1 Filters:

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Verify the used o-ring has been removed and discarded before installing the new o-ring.

Install a new o-ring, filter element (supplied with a sealing grommet inserted into the filter element), filter spring, and clear cover.

![[06600370.png]]

The FH239 Series has two filter elements that are stacked together. The bottom element has a key that **must** match the keyway (1) in the filter head.

![[06s00031.png]]

Install the vent cap and collar onto the clear cover and hand tighten **only**.

Do **not** use tools to tighten the collar.

![[06600371.png]]

Marine Applications

Stage 1 Spin-On Filters:

Install the Stage 1 filter on the filter head. Turn the filter until the gasket touches the surface of the filter head.

Tighten the filter an additional ½ to ¾ turn.

![[06600379.png]]

Move the valve handle to the ON position.

Raise the engine rpm for 1 minute to purge air from the system.

Repeat the procedure for the remaining filter replacements.

![[06600397.png]]

Stage 1 Cartridge Filters:

Install a new pre-lubricated o-ring on the filter element. Insert filter element into the filter housing. The FH240 series element has a key that **must** match the keyway (1) in the filter head.

![[06s00029.png]]

Place service cap on top of filter housing with replacement service cap seal, making sure cap is centered on housing. Install knob and hand-tighten.

![[06f00027.png]]

### Prime

Industrial Applications

Stage 1 Filters:

Check that the drain valve is closed at the base of the filter.

Close the fuel supply valve.

![[06600383.png]]

Use the collar/vent cap wrench, Part Number 3944451 S, to remove the vent cap from the top of the clear cover.

![[06600366.png]]

Fill the clear cover with enough clean diesel fuel to cover the bottom half of the fuel filter.

Install the vent cap.

Tighten the vent cap by hand **only**.

Open the fuel shutoff valve.

![[06600367.png]]

Remove the air bleed line from the drain manifold block and place it into a collection container.

![[06600634.png]]

Turn the keyswitch ON and observe fuel exiting the air bleed line.

When a steady stream of fuel is observed, turn the keyswitch OFF.

If the priming pump stops operating before a steady stream of fuel is observed, turn the keyswitch OFF, wait 30 seconds, and turn the keyswitch back ON. Repeat as necessary to observe a steady stream of fuel.

![[06600635.png]]

Install an air bleed line to drain the manifold block.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> Air bleed line 45 n•m [33 ft-lb]

![[06600636.png]]

Start the engine.

When the lubrication system reaches its normal operating pressure, increase the engine speed to high idle for 1 to 2 minutes.

Once all the air is purged, loosen the vent cap until the fuel level lowers to just above the collar.

Tighten the vent cap by hand **only**.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The clear filter cover will **not** fill completely during engine operation. It will fill over time and the fuel level will rise as the filter element traps contaminants.

![[06600368.png]]

Tighten the collar a second time while the engine is operating.

Do **not** use any tools to tighten the collar or damage can possibly result.

![[06600369.png]]

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно

### Drain

Industrial Applications

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Depending on the circumstance, fuel is flammable. Keep all cigarettes, flames, pilot lights, arcing equipment, and switches out of the work area and areas sharing ventilation to reduce the possibility of severe personal injury or death when working on the fuel system.

Stage 1 Filters:

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The accumulated water **must** be drained at least weekly. For fuel that has high water content, more frequent draining will be necessary.

To drain the accumulated water, shut the engine down.

Place a suitable container under the fuel filter.

With the fuel supply valve closed, open the vent cap to break the air lock in the fuel filter.

![[06600363.png]]

Open the drain valve. If there is accumulated water in the system, the water will flow out first.

When fuel begins to flow out of the drain, close the drain valve.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Dispose of the drained fluids in accordance with local environmental regulations.

![[06600364.png]]

Close the vent cap.

Start the engine, raising the rpm for 1 minute to purge the air from the system.

If necessary, repeat the procedure for the remaining filters to remove the excess air.

![[06600372.png]]

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
