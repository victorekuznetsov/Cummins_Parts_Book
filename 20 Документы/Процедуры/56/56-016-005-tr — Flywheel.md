### Remove

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> This component or assembly weighs greater than 23 kg \[50 lb\]. To prevent serious personal injury, be sure to have assistance or use appropriate lifting equipment to lift this component or assembly.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> For the QSK45 excavator applications that use the 12-point head capscrews, remove and discard the flywheel, capscrews, and spacer at end of every life rebuild.

Remove two capscrews and install two guide studs.

Install two T-handles.

Install the lifting straps.

Remove the remaining capscrews.

A mallet can be used to tap the flywheel, if necessary.

Remove the flywheel.

Remove and discard the roll pin in the rear face of the crankshaft.

![[fw400ma.png]]

### Inspect for Reuse

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> When using a steam cleaner, wear safety glasses or a face shield, as well as protective clothing. Hot steam can cause personal injury.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> When using solvents, acids, or alkaline materials for cleaning, follow the manufacturer's recommendations for use. Wear goggles and protective clothing to reduce the possibility of personal injury.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Wear appropriate eye and face protection when using compressed air. Flying debris and dirt can cause personal injury.

Use solvent or steam to clean the flywheel.

Use a wire brush to clean the crankshaft pilot bore.

Dry with compressed air.

![[fw1brea.png]]

Inspect the teeth of the ring gear for wear or damage.

If the teeth of the ring gear are worn or damaged, the ring gear **must** be replaced. [[56-016-008-tr — Flywheel Ring Gear|Refer to Procedure 016-008 in Section 16.]]

![[fw1rgsa.png]]

Use Cummins® dye penetrant kit, Part Number 3375432, to check the area of the mounting capscrew holes for cracks. There **must not** be any cracks in the flywheel mounting area.

Inspect the pilot bearing mounting hole for damage. If the pilot hole is damaged, the flywheel **must** be replaced.

![[fw1brsa.png]]

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Do not use a cracked flywheel. A cracked flywheel can break and cause personal injury or property damage.

Inspect the face of the flywheel for cracks or other damage.

The flywheel **must** be replaced if cracked or otherwise damaged.

![[fw1bdsa.png]]

### Install

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> There are two types of flywheel mounting capscrews used on the QSK45 and QSK60 engines.

The capscrews can be identified by having either a 6-point head or a 12-point head. Engines using the 12-point head capscrews also have a clamping ring installed.

1. 6-point head capscrew
2. 12-point head capscrew.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The 12-point head capscrew is **only** used on the QSK45 engine in certain excavator applications.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> For the QSK45 excavator applications that use the 12-point head capscrews (2), remove and discard the flywheel, capscrews, and spacer at end of every life rebuild.

![[16600062.png]]

Lubricate the flywheel mounting capscrew threads and flange head surfaces with clean engine oil.

![[16600063.png]]

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> This component or assembly weighs greater than 23 kg \[50 lb\]. To prevent serious personal injury, be sure to have assistance or use appropriate lifting equipment to lift this component or assembly.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
>

Install a new roll pin into the dowel hole in the rear face of the crankshaft to achieve the correct protrusion, 8.7mm +/- 1.0mm.

Install two guide studs to prevent the flywheel from rotating.

Install the flywheel and line up the dowel hole in the flywheel with the roll pin in the crankshaft.

Install the flywheel mounting capscrews.

Remove the two guide studs and install the last two flywheel mounting capscrews.

Remove the two T-handles.

> Perform this step **only** for QSK45 applications that use the 12-point head capscrews and clamping ring.

Insert the clamping ring against the flywheel. The clamping ring can be installed with either side toward the flywheel.

Install the 12-point head flywheel mounting capscrews.
