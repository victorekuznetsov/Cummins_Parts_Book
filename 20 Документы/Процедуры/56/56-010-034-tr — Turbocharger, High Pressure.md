### Preparatory Steps

Industrial and Power Generation

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Do not remove the pressure cap from a hot engine. Wait until the coolant temperature is below 50°C \[120°F\] before removing the pressure cap. Heated coolant spray or steam can cause personal injury.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Coolant is toxic. Keep away from children and pets. If not reused, dispose of in accordance with local environmental regulations.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> The turbocharger and turbocharger components can remain hot after the engine has been shut down or secured. To reduce the possibility of fire, property damage, burns, or other serious personal injury, allow the turbocharger system to cool down before beginning this procedure or repair and make sure that no combustible materials are located where it might come in contact with a hot turbocharger or turbocharger components.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Batteries can emit explosive gases. To reduce the possibility of personal injury, always ventilate the compartment before servicing the batteries. To reduce the possibility of arcing, remove the negative (-) battery cable first and attach the negative (-) battery cable last.

- Disconnect the batteries. See equipment manufacturer service information.
- Drain the cooling system to a point below the turbochargers. [[56-008-018-tr — Cooling System|Refer to Procedure 008-018 in Section 8.]]
- Disconnect the turbocharger coolant supply and return lines. [[56-010-041 — Turbocharger Coolant Hoses|Refer to Procedure 010-041 in Section 10.]]
- Disconnect the turbocharger oil supply lines. [[56-010-046-tr — Turbocharger Oil Supply Line|Refer to Procedure 010-046 in Section 10.]]
- Disconnect the turbocharger oil drain lines. [[56-010-045-tr — Turbocharger Oil Drain Line|Refer to Procedure 010-045 in Section 10.]]
- Remove the required exhaust connection pipework. [[56-011-001-tr — Exhaust Connection Pipe|Refer to Procedure 011-001 in Section 11.]] [[56-011-002 — Exhaust Connection Pipe (Turbocharger to Turbocharger)|Refer to Procedure 011-002 in Section 11.]]

High-pressure turbochargers are used on all QSK45 and QSK60 engines and are mounted directly to the exhaust manifold. The QSK60 two-stage engine also has four low-pressure turbochargers.

Removal of a high-pressure turbocharger on a QSK60 two-stage engine often requires the removal of low-pressure turbochargers and/or other components.

Determine which of the four high-pressure turbochargers to remove.

### Install

Check for Correct Component

Compare the assembly number (1) on the turbocharger dataplate with the turbocharger specified in the Control Parts List (CPL), Bulletin 3379133 (2).

The CPL number for each engine is listed on the engine's dataplate (3).

![[tb1plga.png]]

Holset® turbochargers are used on the QSK45 and QSK60 engines. Use the data tag on the turbocharger to determine the model.

![[10400034.png]]

The HE850 model name is unable to be retroactively applied to early design releases due to regulatory compliance constraints. These HE850 turbochargers will still have HE800 as the model name listed on the turbocharger dataplate. For differentiation from the standard HE800 turbocharger, the HE850 model name will be referenced throughout this procedure.

The procedure and specifications will be different based on the type of turbocharger. Identify the type of turbocharger equipped:

- The HE800 turbocharger (1) can be identified by the V-band joint between the compressor and bearing housing.
- The HE850 turbocharger (2) can be identified by the bolted joint between the compressor and bearing housing.

![[10a00312.png]]

One turbocharger is mounted directly on the exhaust manifold.

Both the HE800 turbocharger and HE850 turbocharger are cooled using coolant from the high-temperature circuit.

Each HE800 turbocharger weighs approximately 57 kg \[ 126 lb \]. Each HE850 turbocharger weighs approximately 65 kg \[ 143 lb \]. Both will require proper lifting equipment.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> All turbocharger oil and water fittings are the flat-face o-ring types of fittings. Make sure the o-ring is in place before attaching the hose(s) to the fittings.

The Holset® turbocharger uses a 9/16-18 UNF, straight-thread, o-ring type of oil supply fitting.

> Do not reuse old capscrews (3) or flange nuts (4). Discard these components. Reusing these components can contribute to exhaust leaks.

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Check to be sure the correct turbocharger assembly is installed. The turbocharger assembly number is on the dataplate that is mounted on the turbocharger. Compare the assembly number with the number in the Control Parts List (CPL) manual with the corresponding CPL for the engine being serviced. Many turbochargers appear the same physically, but contain different parts. If the wrong turbocharger assembly is installed, the performance of the engine will be less than specification, or damage can result to the engine due to excessive cylinder pressure and temperature.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Replacement turbochargers are often built with a standard turbine housing and compressor housing orientation. These housing orientations on the replacement turbocharger may not match the housing orientations of the turbocharger that is being replaced. Make sure that the turbocharger is correctly aligned during installation to prevent damage to the turbocharger bellows.

Install the gasket.

Do **not** install the turbocharger without spacers.

Tighten the flange nuts (4).
