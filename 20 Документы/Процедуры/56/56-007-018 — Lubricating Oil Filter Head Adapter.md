# Lubricating Oil Filter Head Adapter
**Переходник головки масляного фильтра**

> [!abstract] Процедура · `56-007-018`
> **Двигатели:** [[33210083 — QSK60 CM500 CPL 2699|33210083]], [[33219033 — QSK60 CM500 CPL 2848|33219033]], [[33224343 — QSK60 CM500 CPL 2849|33224343]]
> **Семейство:** QSK60
> **Входит в руководства:** [[4021530 — QSK45 and QSK60 Service Manual|4021530]]
> **Секции:** Section 7 - Lubricating Oil System - Group 07
> **Даты:** изменён 2005-07-20
> **Источник:** [QuickServe](https://quickserve.cummins.com/qs3/pubsys2/xml/en/procedures/56/56-007-018.html) · [PDF-оригинал](https://github.com/victorekuznetsov/Cummins_Parts_Book/raw/main/bulletins/procedures/56-007-018.pdf)

### Preparatory Steps

The QSK45 engine has three combination oil filters and the QSK60 engine has four combination oil filters.

Oil filter heads that use the combination oil filter have an adapter for the filter.

Remove the oil filters. Refer to Procedure [[56-007-013-tr — Lubricating Oil Filter (Spin-On)|007-013]].

![[07600230.png]]

### Remove

Remove the four capscrews (1).

Remove the filter head adapter (2) from the oil filter head (3).

Remove and discard the o-ring (4).

![[07600296.png]]

### Clean and Inspect for Reuse

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> When using solvents, acids, alkaline materials for cleaning, follow the manufacturer's recommendations for use. Wear goggles and protective clothing to reduce the possibility of personal injury.

Use solvent to clean the adapter.

Inspect the adapter for cracks or other damage.

![[07400003.png]]

### Install

The QSK45 engine has three combination oil filters, and the QSK60 engine has four combination oil filters.

Oil filter heads that use the combination oil filter have an adapter for the filter.

Lubricate the o-ring (4), and install in the filter head (3) before the adapter (2).

Install the adapter (2). Install the capscrews (1). Tighten the capscrews.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 23 n•m [17 ft-lb]

![[07600296.png]]

### Finishing Steps

**Only** use Fleetguard filters, Part Number 3919823, or Cummins filters, Part Number 4016413. Do **not** use Fleetguard LF9000 or LF9001 filters.

Install new combination oil filters. Refer to Procedure [[56-007-013-tr — Lubricating Oil Filter (Spin-On)|007-013]].

![[07600230.png]]

Operate the engine to normal operating temperature and check for leaks.

![[07600232.png]]
