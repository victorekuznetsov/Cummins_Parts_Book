# Sea Water Pump
**Насос забортной воды**

> **Двигатели:** [[33210083 — QSK60 CM500 CPL 2699|33210083]], [[33219033 — QSK60 CM500 CPL 2848|33219033]], [[33224343 — QSK60 CM500 CPL 2849|33224343]]

### Remove

with Mechanically Actuated Injector

Close the sea water inlet valve.

![[08600044.png]]

Disconnect the sea water inlet tube.

Disconnect the sea water outlet tube.

Remove the clamping devices that secure the sea water inlet and outlet tubes to the mounting brackets.

![[08600591.png]]

Disconnect the sea water pump priming hose.

![[08600592.png]]

Remove the four sea water pump-to-front accessory drive adapter capscrews.

![[08600593.png]]

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> This component or assembly weighs greater than 23 kg \[50 lb\]. To prevent serious personal injury, be sure to have assistance or use appropriate lifting equipment to lift this component or assembly.

Remove the pump from the accessory drive adapter.

![[08600594.png]]

with Electronically Actuated Injector

Close the sea water inlet valve.

![[08600044.png]]

Disconnect the sea water pump priming hose. If the priming hose does **not** connect to the sea water inlet tube, **only** disconnect the hose end that is attached to the sea water pump.

![[08600956.png]]

Disconnect the sea water inlet tube (1).

Discard wear sleeve and o-ring.

Remove the sea water connection (2).

![[08600957.png]]

Remove the sea water pump bracket.

![[08600958.png]]

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> This component or assembly weighs greater than 23 kg \[50 lb\]. To prevent serious personal injury, be sure to have assistance or use appropriate lifting equipment to lift this component or assembly.

Remove the four capscrews from the sea water pump to front accessory drive adapter.

Remove the pump from the accessory drive adapter.

Discard the gasket.

![[08600959.png]]

### Inspect for Reuse

Inspect the sea water pump housings for damage.

Inspect the sea water pump for signs of oil or water leaks.

If the sea water pump housings are damaged or show signs of leaks, the sea water pump **must** be replaced.

![[08600595.png]]

Inspect the sea water pump shaft splines for any damage.

If the sea water pump shaft splines are damaged, the sea water pump **must** be replaced.

![[08600596.png]]

### Install

with Mechanically Actuated Injector

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> This component or assembly weighs greater than 23 kg \[50 lb\]. To prevent serious personal injury, be sure to have assistance or use appropriate lifting equipment to lift this component or assembly.

Carefully install the sea water pump into the front accessory drive adapter.

![[08600594.png]]

Install the four sea water pump to front accessory drive adaptor capscrews.

Tighten the capscrews.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 80 n•m [59 ft-lb]

![[08600593.png]]

Connect the sea water pump priming hose.

![[08600592.png]]

Connect the sea water inlet tube.

Connect the sea water outlet tube.

Install the clamping devices that secure the sea water inlet and outlet tubes to the mounting brackets.

Tighten the capscrews.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 45 n•m [33 ft-lb]

![[08600591.png]]

Open the sea water inlet valve.

![[08600044.png]]

with Electronically Actuated Injector

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> This component or assembly weighs greater than 23 kg \[50 lb\]. To prevent serious personal injury, be sure to have assistance or use appropriate lifting equipment to lift this component or assembly.

Install the new water pump gasket.

Carefully install the sea water pump to the accessory drive adapter and tighten the four capscrews.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 80 n•m [59 ft-lb]

![[08600959.png]]

Install the sea water pump bracket.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 80 n•m [59 ft-lb]

![[08600958.png]]

Install new wear sleeve and o-rings to the sea water inlet tube and sea water connection.

Install the sea water connection (2).

Connect the sea water inlet tube (1).

Tighten the capscrews.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 45 n•m [33 ft-lb]

![[08600957.png]]

Connect the sea water pump priming hose.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 45 n•m [33 ft-lb]

![[08600956.png]]

Open the sea water inlet valve.

![[08600044.png]]
