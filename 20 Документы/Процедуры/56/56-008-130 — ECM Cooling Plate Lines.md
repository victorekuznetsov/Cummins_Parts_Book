# ECM Cooling Plate Lines

> **Двигатели:** [[33210083 — QSK60 CM500 CPL 2699|33210083]], [[33219033 — QSK60 CM500 CPL 2848|33219033]], [[33224343 — QSK60 CM500 CPL 2849|33224343]]

### Preparatory Steps

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Coolant is toxic. Keep away from children and pets. If not reused, dispose of in accordance with local environmental regulations.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Do not remove the pressure cap from a hot engine. Wait until the coolant temperature is below 50°C \[120°F\] before removing the pressure cap. Heated coolant spray or steam can cause serious personal injury.

- Drain the cooling system. Refer to Procedure [[56-008-018-tr — Cooling System|008-018]].

![[ck800wa.png]]

### Remove

Remove the capscrews securing the clips which hold the electronic control module (ECM) cooling plate lines to the cam follower covers and the air compressor housing.

![[08600732.png]]

Remove the electronic control module (ECM) cooling plate supply and return lines.

![[08600721.png]]

Remove the brackets from the coolant lines.

![[08600722.png]]

Remove the male connectors and adapters from the Low Temperature Aftercooler lines. Discard the o-rings.

![[08600723.png]]

### Inspect for Reuse

Inspect the coolant lines for cracks, damage, age wear, or leakage.

Inspect the fittings on both ends of the coolant lines for damage and stripped fittings. Replace if damage is found.

![[06400222.png]]

Inspect the connectors and adapters for cracks, damaged and stripped threads. Replace if damage is found.

![[08600715.png]]

### Install

If the adapters are being reused, apply sealant, Cummins® Part Number 3375066, or equivalent, to the threads of the adapter. If installing new adapters, sealant is already applied to the threads.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 15 n•m [133 in-lb]

![[08600730.png]]

If the male connectors are being reused, apply sealant, Cummins® Part Number 3375066, or equivalent, to the threads of the connectors. If installing new male connectors, sealant is already applied to the threads.

Install the male connectors into each of the adapters.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 15 n•m [133 in-lb]

![[08600725.png]]

Install the electronic control module (ECM) coolant plate supply and return lines.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 15 n•m [133 in-lb]

![[08600721.png]]

Install the electronic control module (ECM) coolant line brackets.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 45 n•m [33 ft-lb]

![[08600722.png]]

Attach the bracket to the cam follower cover and air compressor housing.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 45 n•m [33 ft-lb]

![[08600732.png]]

### Finishing Steps

- Fill the cooling system. Refer to Procedure [[56-008-018-tr — Cooling System|008-018]].
- Operate the engine and check for leaks.
