### Remove

with Mechanically Actuated Injector

Remove the cam follower cover capscrews and the cam follower cover.

Remove and inspect the cam follower gasket.

Discard the gasket **only** if damaged.

![[04600002.png]]

with Electronically Actuated Injector

Remove the capscrews securing the cam follower covers to the engine.

Remove the cam follower cover.

Note the location of the different lengths of the capscrews.

![[04600019.png]]

Remove the gasket from the cam follower cover.

Save the gasket for possible reuse.

![[04600020.png]]
