### Preparatory Steps

Industrial and Power Generation

- Remove the rocker lever cover. Refer to Procedure [[56-003-011-tr — Rocker Lever Cover|003-011]].
- Remove the rocker lever assembly. Refer to Procedure [[56-003-009-tr — Rocker Lever Assembly|003-009]].
- Remove the push rods. Refer to Procedure [[56-004-014-tr — Push Rods or Tubes|004-014]].
- Remove the crossheads. Refer to Procedure [[56-002-001-tr — Crosshead|002-001]].
- Remove the crankcase breather. Refer to Procedure [[56-003-001-tr — Crankcase Breather (External)|003-001]].
- Remove the wiring harness. Refer to 019-043.
- Remove the cam follower cover. Refer to Procedure [[56-004-002-tr — Cam Follower Cover|004-002]].

QSK60 Marine Applications

- Remove the aftercooler heat shields. Refer to Procedure [[56-010-129-tr — Aftercooler Heat Shield|010-129]].
- Remove the rocker lever cover. Refer to Procedure [[56-003-011-tr — Rocker Lever Cover|003-011]].
- Mark the location of the rocker lever assemblies as they are removed, so they can be installed in the original location.
- Remove the rocker lever assembly. Refer to Procedure [[56-003-009-tr — Rocker Lever Assembly|003-009]].
- Mark the push rods so they can be reinstalled in their original position.
- Remove the push rods. Refer to Procedure [[56-004-014-tr — Push Rods or Tubes|004-014]].
- Remove the crossheads.
- When removing the cam follower assemblies located on the left bank of the engine, the crankcase breather **must** be removed. Refer to Procedure [[56-003-001-tr — Crankcase Breather (External)|003-001]] for procedures to remove the crankcase breather.
- When removing the cam follower assemblies located on the left bank of the engine, the wiring harness and wiring harness bracket **must** be removed. Refer to [[19-019-043 — Engine Wiring Harness|019-043]] in the Troubleshooting and Repair Manual, QSK Fuel Systems, QSK19, QSK23, QSK45, QSK60, and QSK78 Series Engines, Bulletin [[3666113 — QSK19, QSK23, QSK45, QSK60, and QSK78 Electronic Control System Troubleshooting and R\|3666113]] for procedures to remove the wiring harness.
- Remove the cam follower cover. Refer to Procedure [[56-004-002-tr — Cam Follower Cover|004-002]].

- Remove the left bank and right bank wiring harness. Refer to 019-043.
- Remove the crankcase breather. Refer to Procedure [[56-003-001-tr — Crankcase Breather (External)|003-001]].
- Remove the fuel return manifold. Refer to Procedure [[56-006-021-tr — Fuel Manifold (Drain)|006-021]].
- Remove the fuel pump. Refer to Procedure [[56-005-016-tr — Fuel Pump|005-016]].
- Remove the cam follower cover. Refer to Procedure [[56-004-002-tr — Cam Follower Cover|004-002]].

### Remove

with Mechanically Actuated Injector

Mark the location of the cam follower assemblies as they are removed, so they can be installed in the original location.

Remove the cam follower mounting capscrews.

Pull the cam follower assembly straight out until the assembly is off the ring dowels.

![[kf400ha.png]]

with Electronically Actuated Injector

Mark the location of the cam follower assemblies as they are removed, so they can be installed in the original location.

Remove the two capscrews and washers securing the cam follower assembly.

Pull the cam follower assembly straight out until the assembly is off the ring dowels.

### Disassemble

with Mechanically Actuated Injector

If the engine has excessive wear because of air cleaner or air inlet tube failure, the cam followers **must** be replaced.

The shaft **must** rotate easily. If resistance is apparent, disassemble and check for burrs on the shaft.

![[kf6shca.png]]

Remove the 12 point capscrews.

Remove the retaining ring and pull the shaft out.

Mark and tag the parts for future installation.

### Install

with Mechanically Actuated Injector

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Do not touch the machined surfaces of the camshaft follower roller with bare hands. This will cause rust to form; before handling, lubricate the camshaft follower roller with clean engine oil.

Use clean engine oil to lubricate the camshaft and cam followers.

Install the cam follower assembly. The shaft **must** fit on both ring dowels.

Install and tighten the mounting capscrews.

| **Torque Value:** | Step 1 | 35 n.m \[26 ft-lb\] |
|---|---|---|

|  | Step 2 | 140 n.m \[103 ft-lb\] |
|---|---|---|

|  | Step 3 | 280 n.m \[207 ft-lb\] |
|---|---|---|

![[kf400ha.png]]

with Electronically Actuated Injector

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Service replacement cam followers are coated with a heavy preservative to prevent rust. This preservative **must** be removed completely with solvent before the parts are installed on the engine. Use clean engine oil to lubricate the cam follower assembly and camshaft.

Lubricate the cam follower assembly.

![[04600031.png]]

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Do not touch the machined surfaces of the camshaft follower roller with bare hands. This will cause rust to form; before handling, lubricate the camshaft follower roller with clean engine oil.

Install the cam follower assembly. The shaft **must** fit on both ring dowels.

Lubricate the threads and install and tighten the mounting capscrews.

|---|---|---|
