### Preparatory Steps

Box Type

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Fuel is flammable. Keep all cigarettes, flames, pilot lights, arcing equipment, and switches out of the work area and areas sharing ventilation to reduce the possibility of severe injury or death when working on a fuel system.

- Remove the fuel control valve body assembly. [[56-006-049-tr — Fuel Control Valve Body Assembly|Refer to Procedure 006-049]].
- Disconnect the blowby pressure sensor connector. [[19-019-043 — Engine Wiring Harness|Refer to Procedure 019-043]] in the Troubleshooting and Repair Manual, Electronic Control System QSK19, QSK23, QSK45, QSK60 and QSK78 Series Engines, Bulletin [[3666113 — QSK19, QSK23, QSK45, QSK60, and QSK78 Electronic Control System Troubleshooting and R\|3666113]].
- Remove the wiring harness cover.
- Remove the hand hole covers as necessary. [[56-001-035-tr — Hand Hole Cover|Refer to Procedure 001-035]].

![[ck800wa.png]]

Purse Type

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Some state and federal agencies have determined that used engine oil can be carcinogenic and cause reproductive toxicity. Avoid inhalation of vapors, ingestion, and prolonged contact with used engine oil. If not reused, dispose of in accordance with local environmental regulations.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> To reduce the possibility of personal injury, avoid contact of hot oil with your skin.

- Drain the engine oil if the crankcase breather outlet tubes are to be removed. [[56-007-037-tr — Lubricating Oil System|Refer to Procedure 007-037]].
- Disconnect the blowby sensor connector. [[19-019-043 — Engine Wiring Harness|Refer to Procedure 019-043]] in the Troubleshooting and Repair Manual, Electronic Control System QSK19, QSK23, QSK45, QSK60 and QSK78 Series Engines, Bulletin [[3666113 — QSK19, QSK23, QSK45, QSK60, and QSK78 Electronic Control System Troubleshooting and R\|3666113]].
- Remove the lubricating oil filler tube. [[56-007-065-tr — Lubricating Oil Fill Tube|Refer to Procedure 007-065]].
- Remove the extended oil dipstick tube, if necessary. [[56-007-011-tr — Lubricating Oil Dipstick Tube|Refer to Procedure 007-011]].
- Remove the hand hole covers as necessary. [[56-001-035-tr — Hand Hole Cover|Refer to Procedure 001-035]].

### Clean and Inspect for Reuse

Box Type

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> When using solvents, acids, or alkaline materials for cleaning, follow the manufacturer's recommendations for use. Wear goggles and protective clothing to reduce the possibility of personal Injury.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> When using a steam cleaner, wear safety glasses or a face shield, as well as protective clothing. Hot steam can cause serious personal injury.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Wear appropriate eye and face protection when using compressed air. Flying debris and dirt can cause personal injury.

Soak the breather in solvent or clean with steam.

Dry the breather assembly with compressed air.

![[03600005.png]]

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> When using solvents, acids, or alkaline materials for cleaning, follow the manufacturer's recommendations for use. Wear goggles and protective clothing to reduce the possibility of personal Injury.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Wear appropriate eye and face protection when using compressed air. Flying debris and dirt can cause personal injury.

Use solvent to clean the inside of the crankcase breather and vent hoses.

Dry the crankcase breather and vent hoses with compressed air.

The air **must** flow freely through the breather. If the breather is blocked or restricted, it **must** be replaced.

![[br1tbea.png]]

Replace the hoses if they have obstructions or sludge buildup.

![[03600005.png]]

Inspect the hoses for cracks or other damage. Replace if necessary.

![[03600010.png]]

Inspect mounting brackets and crankcase breather box for cracks or damage. Replace if necessary.

![[03600078.png]]

with Electronically Actuated Injector

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> When using solvents, acids, or alkaline materials for cleaning, follow the manufacturer's recommendations for use. Wear goggles and protective clothing to reduce the possibility of personal Injury.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Wear appropriate eye and face protection when using compressed air. Flying debris and dirt can cause personal injury.

Use solvent to clean all of the parts removed. Dry with compressed air.

![[03600135.png]]

Inspect all crankcase breather components for cracks, rust, or damage. Replace damaged components.

### Finishing Steps

Box Type

- Install the hand hole covers as necessary. [[56-001-035-tr — Hand Hole Cover|Refer to Procedure 001-035]].
- Install the wiring harness cover.
- Connect the blowby sensor connector. [[19-019-043 — Engine Wiring Harness|Refer to Procedure 019-043]] in the Troubleshooting and Repair Manual, Electronic Control System QSK19, QSK23, QSK45, QSK60 and QSK78 Series Engines, [[3666113 — QSK19, QSK23, QSK45, QSK60, and QSK78 Electronic Control System Troubleshooting and R\|3666113]].
- Install the fuel control valve body assembly. Refer Procedure [[56-006-049-tr — Fuel Control Valve Body Assembly|Refer to Procedure 006-049]].

![[ck800wa.png]]

Purse Type

- Install the hand hole covers as necessary. [[56-001-035-tr — Hand Hole Cover|Refer to Procedure 001-035]].
- Install the lubricating oil filler tube. [[56-007-065-tr — Lubricating Oil Fill Tube|Refer to Procedure 007-065]].
- Install the extended oil dipstick tube, if necessary. [[56-007-011-tr — Lubricating Oil Dipstick Tube|Refer to Procedure 007-011]].
- Connect the blowby sensor connector. [[19-019-043 — Engine Wiring Harness|Refer to Procedure 019-043]] in the Troubleshooting and Repair Manual, Electronic Control System QSK19, QSK23, QSK45, QSK60 and QSK78 Series Engines, Bulletin [[3666113 — QSK19, QSK23, QSK45, QSK60, and QSK78 Electronic Control System Troubleshooting and R\|3666113]].
- Fill the engine with clean engine oil, if necessary. [[56-007-037-tr — Lubricating Oil System|Refer to Procedure 007-037]].
