### Measure

The gauge adapter (or fitting) **must** be installed at a 90-degree angle to the airflow in a straight section of pipe. The adapter location **must** be at least one pipe diameter before the turbocharger.

The adapter **must not** extend through the wall of the air intake tubing.

If the adapter **must** be located in a curved section of tubing, locate it on the flat side of the curve, **not** in the radius.

Install the gauge adapter.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> For a containerized generator set application, air intake restriction measurement **must** be taken relative to the ambient pressure outside the container.

![[ci8gaha.png]]

Install a manometer in the intake piping between the turbocharger and the air filter. The gauge **must** have a capacity of 93 mm H 2 O \[25 in H 2 O\].

![[10400011.png]]

Stall speed is **not** full power. Operate the engine at rated rpm and load.

Operate the engine at full throttle and rated rpm with maximum load. Record the reading of the gauge or manometer.

Reference the Intake Air System in Section V of the Service Manual for the Intake Air Restriction Specification.

![[eg800kb.png]]

If the restriction exceeds specifications, replace or clean the air filter element. Refer to the equipment manufacturer service information.

![[10600136.png]]

Inspect the intake air piping for damage; refer to the equipment manufacturer service information.

If damage is **not** visible, check the size and routing of the intake air piping; refer to the Installation Recommendations.

![[ci400sa.png]]

Remove the test equipment.
