### Install

The QSK45 engine has three oil cooler covers, and the QSK60 single- and two-stage engines have four oil cooler covers. The rear two-stage frame support bracket **must** be installed after the rear cover is installed. **Only** the QSK60 two-stage engine has frame support brackets welded on the two center covers.

Install one edge-molded gasket (6) on each of the oil cooler element mounting studs (2). Use Loctite® 271, Part Number 3375068, sparingly on the oil cooler stud threads.

Install a new gasket (5) and the oil cooler cover (4). Position twenty capscrews (3) to secure the cover in position, but do **not** tighten the capscrews.

Install the header plate gaskets with the part number on the gasket facing upwards over the guide studs and making sure all mounting holes line up. Position the twenty capscrews (3) to secure the cover in position, but do **not** tighten the capscrews.

Tighten the oil cooler cover capscrews in the sequence shown.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 45 n•m [33 ft-lb]

Tighten the self-locking nuts (21 and 22).

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> Initial Torque Value 45 n•m [33 ft-lb]

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> Final Torque Value 95 n•m [70 ft-lb]

For industrial engines, tighten the two-stage frame support bracket capscrews in the sequence shown.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 45 n•m [33 ft-lb]

For four turbocharger engines, tighten the exhaust collector support bracket capscrews in the sequence shown.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
