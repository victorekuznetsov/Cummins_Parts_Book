# Installation Procedure
**Порядок установки**

### Remove

Disconnect the wiring from the back of the local analog panel (inside of the customer interface box) as follows:

- Note and record the position of each connector for proper installation of the local analog display. Note that it is possible to put connectors in the wrong mates, as some are the same size but **not** uniquely keyed.
- Loosen the two attaching screws on each of the eight green connectors. Note that some of the mating connectors permanently attached to the local analog display do **not** have mating internal threads. These screws are **not** required to be loosened:

Four on the left side below the black reset button

Three on the top center and right

One on the bottom to the right.

Pull the eight green connectors from their mates on the back of the local analog display. Pull on the connector body rather than the wires, it might be possible to pull wires out of the connector (in which case it will need to be connected again via attaching screws the side of the connector).

- Pull the ground strap off the spade terminal on the right side back of the local analog display.

![[15600116.png]]

Remove the four black plastic mounting clamps holding the local analog display to the door as follows:

- Press down on the top of one of the clamps (toward the door) until a click is felt. This compresses the spring until it latches.
- Remove the clamp from the local analog display by sliding it away from the local analog display along the surface of the door, so that it clears the slot in the side of the local analog display. It is **not** attached to the door. The clamp can be used for the replacement local analog display.
- Repeat steps 1 and 2 from the other three clamps, making sure to hold the local analog display in place from the front of the door prior to removing the last clamp.

![[15600091.png]]

### Install

![[15600092.png]]

1. Base of clamp
2. Slots in local analog display
3. Clamp hooks to engine in local analog display
4. Customer interface box door goes between clamp base and local analog display flange

Slide the new local analog display into the opening in the customer interface box or engine room panel.

Clamp it in place with the four black plastic clamps.

Hold the local analog display against the door from the front, hold the base of the clamp against the back side of the door and slide it into the two slots nearest a top or bottom corner of the local analog display, and up firmly against the side of the local analog display.

Engage the two wide hooks that are on either side of the clamp into the slots. It is preferable to use the hooks that stand higher off the back side of the customer interface box door.

Using thumb and index fingers, squeeze the upper corners of the plastic clamp toward each other until a bang is heard, meaning the spring loaded clamp has released and pressed against the back side of the door, engaging the hooks on the side into the local analog display slot.

Repeat steps 1, 2, and 3 above for three more clamps on the remaining top and bottom corners of the local analog display.

Connect the wiring.

Insert each of the eight connectors into the mating connector on the back of the local analog display. The connectors are labeled (J7, J12, etc.) on both the back of the local analog display and on the mating connector for reference.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> It is possible to put a connector in the wrong mate on the local analog display, as several are the same size and are **not** uniquely keyed.

Tighten the attaching screws snugly.

Connect the ground strap by sliding it onto the spade lug on the right side of the rear of the local analog display.
