### Specifications

Minimum recommended battery capacity:

| Engine Model | System Voltage | Temperature Range | Cold Cranking Amperes | Reserve Capacity | Maximum Allowable Starting Circuit Resistance |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| QSK45 | 24 | -18 to 0°C \[0 to 32°F\] | 1800 | 640 A | 0.0020 ohms |
| QSK60 | 24 | Above 0°C \[32°F\] | 1800 | 640 A | 0.0020 ohms |
| QSK60 Two-Stage | 24 | All | 1800 | 640 A | 0.0020 ohms |

#### Battery Cable Sizes - American Wire Gauge (maximum length in cranking motor circuit):

#### 24 to 32 VDC

| Number 00 | 6.1 m \[20 ft\] |
|---|---|

| Number 000 | 8.2 m \[27 ft\] |
|---|---|

| Number 0000 or two Number 0 (see note) | 6.1 m \[20 ft\] |
|---|---|

| Two Number 00 | 13.7 m \[45 ft\] |
|---|---|

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Two strands of number 0 cable can be used instead of one number 0000 cable, provided all connections are carefully made to maintain equal current flow in each parallel cable.

| Minimum Cranking Speed Without Starting Aid | 150 rpm |
|---|---|

Starting aids, such as block heaters, lubricating oil pan heaters, and so forth, are available to aid in cold weather starting.

The number of plates within a given battery size determines reserve capacity. Reserve capacity is the length of time sustained cranking can occur.

CCA ratings are based on two 12 VDC batteries in series.
