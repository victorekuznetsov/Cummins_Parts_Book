### Maintenance Check

Remove the fan belt. [[56-008-002-om-rep — Drive Belt, Cooling Fan|Refer to Procedure 008-002 in Section A.]]

![[08600162.png]]

Rotate the fan hub pulley to check for rough or damaged bearings.

Inspect the pulley grooves for excessive wear.

Inspect the fan hub for grease leakage and fan clutch for oil leakage.

Use a dial indicator to check the bearing end clearance.

| Fan Hub End Clearance |  |  |
|---|---|---|
| mm |  | in |
| 0.03 | MIN | 0.001 |
| 0.15 | MAX | 0.006 |

![[08400041.png]]

If the inspections result in a faulty condition, replace the fan hub with a new or rebuilt unit as necessary. Contact a Cummins® Authorized Repair Location.

![[08600164.png]]

Install the fan belt. [[56-008-002-om-rep — Drive Belt, Cooling Fan|Refer to Procedure 008-002 in Section A.]]
