### General Information

The QSK45 and QSK60 utilize the outer base circle overhead set procedure.

The outer base circle procedure requires the use of an accurate, dial-type, inch-pound torque wrench with a range from 0 to 35 N•m \[0 to 300 in-lb\]. Use torque wrench, Part Number 3164795 or equivalent. The torque wrench needs to be checked on a routine basis for proper calibration. Using a clicker-type torque wrench is **not** recommended.

![[03400018.png]]

The QSK45 and QSK60 valve rocker levers incorporate a ball-and-socket to minimize crosshead wear and reduce tipping of the valves.

![[03400007.png]]

with Mechanically Actuated Injector

The QSK45 and QSK60 valve rocker lever assembly consists of a exhaust rocker lever (1), injector rocker lever (2), intake rocker lever (3), and a rocker lever shaft (4).

![[03600120.png]]

with Electronically Actuated Injector

The QSK45 and QSK60 valve rocker lever assembly consists of a rocker lever shaft (1), intake rocker lever (4), rocker lever spacer (3), and a exhaust rocker lever (2).
