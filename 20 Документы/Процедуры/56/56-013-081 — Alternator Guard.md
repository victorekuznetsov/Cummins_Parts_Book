# Alternator Guard

> [!abstract] Процедура · `56-013-081`
> **Двигатели:** [[33210083 — QSK60 CM500 CPL 2699|33210083]], [[33219033 — QSK60 CM500 CPL 2848|33219033]], [[33224343 — QSK60 CM500 CPL 2849|33224343]]
> **Семейство:** QSK60
> **Входит в руководства:** [[4021530 — QSK45 and QSK60 Service Manual|4021530]]
> **Секции:** Section 13 - Electrical Equipment - Group 13
> **Даты:** изменён 2022-04-05
> **Источник:** [QuickServe](https://quickserve.cummins.com/qs3/pubsys2/xml/en/procedures/56/56-013-081.html) · [PDF-оригинал](https://github.com/victorekuznetsov/Cummins_Parts_Book/raw/main/bulletins/procedures/56-013-081.pdf)

### Preparatory Steps

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Batteries can emit explosive gases. To reduce the possibility of personal injury, always ventilate the compartment before servicing the batteries. To reduce the possibility of arcing, remove the negative (-) battery cable first and attach the negative (-) battery cable last.

- Disconnect the batteries. [[56-013-001-tr — Charging System Alternator|Refer to Procedure 013-001 in Section 13.]]

### Remove

Remove the rear cover plate.

![[13c00134.png]]

Remove alternator guard. Verify mounting brackets remain fixed to the alternator guard.

![[13c00135.png]]

Remove upper and lower mounting brackets from the front cover of the engine. Verify the three front gear cover capscrews (1) are installed.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 80 n•m [59 ft-lb]

![[13c00136.png]]

### Disassemble

Remove lock nuts (1), washers (2), vibration isolators (3) and alternator guard brackets (4) from all studs on alternator guard.

![[13c00137.png]]

### Clean and Inspect for Reuse

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> When using a steam cleaner, wear safety glasses or a face shield, as well as protective clothing. Hot steam can cause serious personal injury.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> When using solvents, acids, or alkaline materials for cleaning, follow the manufacturer's recommendations for use. Wear goggles and protective clothing to reduce the possibility of personal injury.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Wear appropriate eye and face protection when using compressed air. Flying dirt and debris can cause personal injury.

Clean alternator guard. Use solvent or steam. Dry with compressed air.

![[13c00129.png]]

Inspect alternator guard.

Replace alternator guard if:

- Cracked
- Threads corroded
- Otherwise damaged.

![[13c00130.png]]

Inspect alternator guard mounting bracket.

Replace alternator guard mounting bracket if:

- Cracked
- Threads corroded
- Otherwise damaged.

![[13c00131.png]]

Inspect vibration isolators.

Replace vibration isolator if:

- Cracked
- Worn
- Otherwise damaged.

![[13j00033.png]]

Inspect alternator guard brackets.

Replace bracket if:

- Cracked
- Captive capscrew missing or damaged
- Otherwise damaged.

![[13c00132.png]]

### Assemble

Install vibration isolators (3), alternator guard brackets (4), washers (2), and lock nuts (1) onto studs on alternator guard, as shown.

![[13c00137.png]]

### Install

Install upper and lower support brackets. Verify the brackets are installed level.

Install and tighten the long capscrews (1).

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 80 n•m [59 ft-lb]

Install and tighten the short capscrew.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 45 n•m [33 ft-lb]

![[13c00136.png]]

Position the alternator guard in line with the upper and lower support brackets. Secure the guard with capscrews.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 45 n•m [33 ft-lb]

![[13c00135.png]]

Install the rear cover to the alternator guard using 4 capscrews.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> Alternator guard rear cover 45 n•m [33 ft-lb]

![[13c00134.png]]

### Finishing Steps

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Batteries can emit explosive gases. To reduce the possibility of personal injury, always ventilate the compartment before servicing the batteries. To reduce the possibility of arcing, remove the negative (-) battery cable first and attach the negative (-) battery cable last.

- Connect the batteries. [[56-013-001-tr — Charging System Alternator|Refer to Procedure 013-001 in Section 13.]]
