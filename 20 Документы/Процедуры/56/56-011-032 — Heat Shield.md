### Maintenance Check

Check for clearance between heat shields and engine fluid lines such as fuel supply lines, coolant pipes, oil lines.

If there is **not** a minimum of 2mm clearance the heat shields **must** be adjusted to prevent contact.

Inspect for missing or loose capscrews. Any missing capscrews **must** be replaced.

Ensure heat shield capscrews are torqued correctly.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 54 n•m [40 ft-lb]

![[11k00242.png]]

Right Bank Front Fuel line:

Check for clearance between right bank front fuel line supplying cylinder 2-3 and heat shield.

If clearance is less than 2mm, heat shield **must** be adjusted to increase clearance.

Check capscrew torques of critical fastening locations as shown in diagram and replace any capscrews if missing.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 54 n•m [40 ft-lb]

Complete visual inspection of fuel line to ensure no rubbing or scoring marks are present. If any such wear is witnessed, replace damaged hard section of fuel line.

![[11k00243.png]]

Left Bank Front LTA Pipes:

Remove cover part as shown in the diagram.

Complete visual inspection and check clearance between the heat shield LTA coolant pipes as shown in the diagram.

Check surrounding capscrew torques and replace any missing capscrews.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 54 n•m [40 ft-lb]

If any contact is seen between the coolant pipe and heat shield, heat shield **must** be adjusted to increase clearance. Check pipe for wear or damage.

![[11k00244.png]]

Complete visual inspection of bracket as shown in diagram for cracks or damage If any cracks are observed, bracket **must** be replaced.

Right Bank Front LTA Pipe:

Complete visual inspection on right bank front LTA pipe ensuring clearance can be witnessed between heat shield and LTA pipe.

If there is no clearance, heat shields **must** be adjusted to increase clearance.

Check capscrew torques.
