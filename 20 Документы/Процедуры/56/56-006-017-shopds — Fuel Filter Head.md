### Remove

with Mechanically Actuated Injector

Remove the fuel filter supply and return hoses.

Remove the fuel filter head mounting capscrews and the fuel filter head.

![[07600256.png]]

with Electronically Actuated Injector

Remove the six fuel filter head mounting capscrews and the fuel filter head.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> For marine applications, the location of the fuel filter head can vary.

![[05600282.png]]

**For Generator Model DQKAN with Low NOx Emissions Capability**

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Some graphics in this procedure are generic and do **not** depict the correct number of fuel filters.

Remove fuel filter head mounting capscrews and the fuel filter head.
