### Remove

The procedures for removing the lubricating oil pump on the QSK45 and QSK60 engines are similar. The oil pump for the QSK60 is shown.

It is recommended that the oil pump to crankshaft gear backlash be measured before removing the pump to check for a worn gear.

| Lubricating Oil Pump Idler Gear Backlash |  |  |
|---|---|---|
| mm |  | in |
| 0.21 | MIN | 0.008 |
| 0.39 | MAX | 0.015 |

![[07600377.png]]

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> This component or assembly weighs greater than 23 kg \[50 lb\]. To prevent serious personal injury, be sure to have assistance or use appropriate lifting equipment to lift this component or assembly.

Loosen all of the mounting capscrews (1).

Support the lubricating oil pump.

Remove the mounting capscrews (1).

Gently lower the lubricating oil pump (2) away from the engine (3). Note the amount and location of any shims (4).
