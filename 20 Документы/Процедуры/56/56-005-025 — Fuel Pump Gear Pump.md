### Rotation Check

with Mechanically Actuated Injector

Because the gear pump inlet fitting is located on the side of the gear pump, the fitting can **not** be removed to check the rotation of the gear pump.

![[05400028.png]]

Remove the fuel pump. Refer to Procedure [[56-005-016-tr — Fuel Pump|005-016]].

![[05600074.png]]

Rotate the fuel pump by hand and check for gear pump rotation.

If the fuel pump gears do **not** rotate, replace or repair the fuel pump. Refer to Procedure [[56-005-016-tr — Fuel Pump|005-016]].

![[05400028.png]]

Using a remote starter, Part Number 3376506, crank the engine, and make sure the accessory drive rotates **counterclockwise**.
