### Leak Test

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Wear safety glasses or a face shield, as well as protective clothing, to prevent personal injury when using a steam cleaner or high-pressure water.

Use steam or high-pressure water. Clean the exterior of the engine.

![[00600029.png]]

Operate the engine at low idle.

Check for oil or coolant leaks between the cylinder head and the engine block.

![[07600232.png]]

Listen for a chirping sound between the cylinder head and engine block.

A chirping sound, usually during engine acceleration, indicates a combustion leak.

![[05600063.png]]

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> This assembly weighs 23 kg \[50 lb\] or more. To avoid personal injury, use a hoist or get assistance to lift this assembly.

If a leak is found, remove the cylinder head. Refer to Procedure [[56-002-004-tr — Cylinder Head|002-004]].

![[02400005.png]]

Pressure check the cooling system to make sure the leak was at the head gasket and **not** at the liner counterbore. Refer to Procedure [[56-008-018-tr — Cooling System|008-018]] for coolant leaks at the counterbore.

![[ew400sc.png]]

Install the cylinder head and all related components. Refer to Procedure [[56-002-004-tr — Cylinder Head|002-004]].

![[02400002.png]]
