Pre-Oil Filter Pressure Sensor Circuit

### Circuit Description

The pre-oil filter pressure is the engine oil pressure before the oil has passed through the oil filter. The pre-oil filter pressure sensor is located on the oil inlet side of the lubricating oil system.

The post-oil filter pressure is the engine oil pressure after the oil has passed through the oil filter.
