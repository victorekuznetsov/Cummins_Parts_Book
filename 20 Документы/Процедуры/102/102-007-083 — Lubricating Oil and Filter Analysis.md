### Inspect

Lubricating Oil

An analysis of used oil can help diagnose internal engine damage and determine if it was caused by one of the following:

- Intake air filter malfunction
- Coolant leaks
- Oil diluted with fuel
- Ceramic or metallic particles causing wear.

![[oi801kj.png]]

For additional oil analysis information, see Fluids for Cummins® Products Service Manual, Bulletin 5411406.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Do **not** disassemble an engine for repair based **only** on the results of an oil analysis. The oil filter **must** also be inspected. If the oil filter shows evidence of internal engine damage, find the source of the problem and repair the damage. Use the appropriate procedure(s) based on the following oil filter inspection.

![[lt800gq.png]]

Lubricating Oil Filter

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Carefully cut open the upper section of the combination filter. The filter element spring is under compression and can cause personal injury.

Use filter cutter, Part Number 3376579, to open the upper section of the combination filter.

![[lf1spbb.png]]

Remove the filter element from the can for inspection. Check for evidence of moisture or foreign particles.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> For more accurate inspection results of the filter element, excess oil **must** be pressed out of the filter media with a shop vise or similar.

| Element | Probable Source |
|---|---|
| Copper | Bearings and bushings |
| Chromium | Piston rings |
| Iron | Cylinder liners |
| Lead | Bearing overlay material |
| Aluminum | Piston wear or scuffing |

![[lf1tosb.png]]

#### Fleetguard™ Centrifuge Cartridge

- Use a screwdriver or chisel to remove the brass bushing out of the top of the rotor.

![[07100285.png]]

Cut the top flange off of the cartridge.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Place the cartridge horizontally to stop contamination getting inside.

![[07100286.png]]

Use a screwdriver or a punch to remove the center tube out of the cartridge.

![[07100287.png]]

Use a hammer to strike the cartridge around the spin weld in the center of the cartridge.

![[07100288.png]]

Carefully pull the two parts apart revealing the core and the sludge cake.
