### Remove

Industrial and Power Generation

Loosen the wing nuts (4) on the air cleaner housing (5) to remove the precleaner panel with the dust bin (6). To remove the precleaner panel (2) equipped with an exhaust aspirator, loosen the U-bolt clamp securing the precleaner to the aspirator tubing.

Remove the dirty cartridge (3) by inserting fingers into the cartridge opening (loosen all four corners of the cartridge one at a time) and pull it straight out.

With the larger cartridge, break the seal along the edges. After the seal has been broken, pull the cartridge straight out and slightly up so the cartridge will clear the sealing frame and edges of the air cleaner housing.

![[ac4etgd.png]]

Marine Applications

Remove the air inlet capscrews (1) to remove the air inlet hood (2) and moisture separator (3).

Remove the sealing angle mounting capscrews (6) and sealing angle (5).

Remove the air cleaner element (4).

![[10600290.png]]

### Clean

Industrial and Power Generation

Cummins Inc. does **not** recommend cleaning and reusing the cartridge. When returned to service, the life expectancy of a cleaned cartridge will be **only** a fraction of the original service life.

The precleaner dust bin (6) is self-cleaning.

Clean the precleaner openings (2). Remove all soot, oil film, and any other objects that can become lodged in the opening.

Remove any dust or dirt in the lower portion of the precleaner and aspirator tubing.

Remove all foreign material from the inside of the air cleaner housing.

![[ac4etgd.png]]

Marine Applications

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> It is not recommended to clean and reuse the cartridge. When returned to service the life expectancy of a cleaned cartridge will be only a fraction of the original service life.

The moisture separator can be sprayed with light water pressure and brushed with a soft bristle brush.

Remove all foreign material from the inside of the air cleaner housing.

![[10600292.png]]

### Inspect

Industrial and Power Generation

Excessive oil mist shortens the life of any dry-type cartridge. Troubleshooting at this point can appreciably lengthen new cartridge life.

Inspect the dirty cartridge for soot or oil. If there is soot inside the tubes, check for leaks in the engine exhaust system, and exhaust blow-back into the air intake and exhaust from other equipment.

If the cartridge appears oily, check for fumes escaping from the crankcase breather.

Inspect clamps and flexible hose or tubing to make sure all fittings are airtight on the cleaners with exhaust aspirators.

![[ac4etgd.png]]

Marine Applications

Excessive oil mist shortens the life expectancy of any dry-type cartridge. Troubleshooting at this point can appreciably lengthen new cartridge life.

Inspect the dirty cartridge and moisture separator for soot or oil. If there is soot inside the pleats, check for leaks in the engine exhaust system, and exhaust blow-back into the air intake system from exhaust of other equipment.

If the cartridge or moisture separator appears oily, check for fumes escaping from the crankcase breather.

Inspect clamps and flexible hoses or tubing to make sure all fittings are airtight on the cleaners with exhaust aspirators.

![[10600293.png]]

### Install

Industrial and Power Generation

Inspect the new filter cartridge for shipping damage before installing.

Install a new cartridge by inserting fingers in the cartridge opening and pushing the cartridge straight into the housing. Do **not** hit the cartridge tubes against the sealing flange on the edges of the air cleaner housing.

As the cleaner requires no separate gaskets for seals, care **must** be taken when inserting the cartridge to create a proper seat within the cleaner housing.

Firmly press all edges and corners of the cartridge with fingers to effect a positive air seal against the sealing flange of the housing. The cartridge **must not** be pounded or pressed in the center to seal the cartridge.

Care **must** be taken to keep the cleaner's face unobstructed.

Replace the precleaner panel (2), and tighten the wing nut (4) by hand.

Turn the wing nuts 1 to 1-1/2 turns with a small adjustable wrench. Do **not** overtighten.

A precleaner equipped with an exhaust aspirator, assemble the aspirator tube to the precleaner panel, and tighten the U-bolt.

![[ac4etgd.png]]

Marine Applications

Install a new cartridge (4) into the rear housing (7).

Install the sealing angle (5) and tighten the capscrews (6).
