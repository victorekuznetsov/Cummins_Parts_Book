### Inspect

This type of fan clutch is engaged by air pressure and is disengaged by spring tension.

Air pressure of 620 to 830 kPa \[90 to 120 psi\] is required to override the clutch spring tension to engage the fan.

![[fn8cnkc.png]]

If the fan does **not** operate within the temperature range indicated on the coolant temperature sensor (1), the fan clutch and the controls **must** be checked. Refer to the OEM service manual.
