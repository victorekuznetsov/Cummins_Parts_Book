### Maintenance Check

Industrial Applications

Every 6000 hours or 2 years, measure the drive pulley flange end clearance.

Remove the fan drive belt.

![[08200107.png]]

Inspect the fan hub for:

- Freedom of rotation
- Cracks
- Grease seal leakage.

![[08200109.png]]

Measure the fan hub end clearance.

| Fan Hub End Clearance |  |  |
|---|---|---|
| mm |  | in |
| 0.08 | MIN | 0.003 |
| 0.41 | MAX | 0.016 |

Replace or rebuild the fan hub if the end clearance does **not** meet these specifications, or contact a Cummins® Authorized Repair Location for rebuilding the fan hub.
