# Fan Hub, Belt Driven
**Ступица вентилятора с ремённым приводом**

> **Двигатели:** [[35354607 — QSM11 CM570 CPL 8608|35354607]], [[35373113 — QSM11 CM570 CPL 8471|35373113]], [[71156161 — QSM11 CM570 CPL 8543|71156161]]

> **Даты:** изменён 2016-02-23
> **Источник:** [QuickServe](https://quickserve.cummins.com/qs3/pubsys2/xml/en/procedures/35/35-008-036-tr.html) · [PDF-оригинал](https://github.com/victorekuznetsov/Cummins_Parts_Book/raw/main/bulletins/procedures/35-008-036-tr.pdf)

### Initial Check

Remove the fan drive belt. [[35-008-002-tr — Drive Belt, Cooling Fan|Refer to Procedure 008-002 in Section 8.]]

Inspect the fan hub for:

- Freedom of rotation
- Cracks
- Grease seal leakage.

![[fa2beka.png]]

Measure the fan hub end clearance.

| Fan Hub End Clearance |  |  |
|---|---|---|
| mm |  | in |
| 0.08 | MIN | 0.003 |
| 0.41 | MAX | 0.016 |

Replace or rebuild the fan hub if the end clearance does **not** meet these specifications, or contact a Cummins® Authorized Repair Location for rebuilding the fan hub.

![[fa2bdia.png]]

### Preparatory Steps

- Remove the fan and clutch assembly. [[35-008-040-tr — Fan, Cooling|Refer to Procedure 008-040 in Section 8.]]

![[ck800wa.png]]

### Remove

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> This component or assembly weighs greater than 23 kg \[50 lb\]. To prevent serious personal injury, be sure to have assistance or use appropriate lifting equipment to lift this component or assembly.

Remove the fan drive pulley.

Remove the four capscrews and the fan hub.

![[fa2puhb.png]]

### Install

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> This component or assembly weighs greater than 23 kg \[50 lb\]. To prevent serious personal injury, be sure to have assistance or use appropriate lifting equipment to lift this component or assembly.

Install the fan hub and four capscrews.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 47 n•m [35 ft-lb]

![[fa2bdhd.png]]

### Finishing Steps

- Install the fan drive pulley and the fan clutch and assembly. [[35-008-040-tr — Fan, Cooling|Refer to Procedure 008-040 in Section 8.]]
- Install, adjust, and tighten the fan drive belt. [[35-008-002-tr — Drive Belt, Cooling Fan|Refer to Procedure 008-002 in Section 8]]

![[ck800wa.png]]
