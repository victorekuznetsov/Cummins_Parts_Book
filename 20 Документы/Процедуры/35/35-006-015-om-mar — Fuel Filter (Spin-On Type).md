# Fuel Filter (Spin-On Type)
**Топливный фильтр (навинчиваемый)**

> [!abstract] Процедура · `35-006-015-om-mar`
> **Двигатели:** [[35354607 — QSM11 CM570 CPL 8608|35354607]], [[35373113 — QSM11 CM570 CPL 8471|35373113]], [[71156161 — QSM11 CM570 CPL 8543|71156161]]
> **Семейство:** QSM11
> **Входит в руководства:** [[4021940 — QSM11 Marine Operation and Maintenance Manual|4021940]]
> **Секции:** Section 4 - Maintenance Procedures at 250 Hours or 6 Months
> **Даты:** изменён 2011-10-07
> **Источник:** [QuickServe](https://quickserve.cummins.com/qs3/pubsys2/xml/en/procedures/35/35-006-015-om-mar.html) · [PDF-оригинал](https://github.com/victorekuznetsov/Cummins_Parts_Book/raw/main/bulletins/procedures/35-006-015-om-mar.pdf)

### Remove

Clean the area around the fuel filter and the fuel filter head.

Remove the fuel filter with a filter wrench.

![[ff8etma.png]]

Remove the thread adapter sealing ring (1).

Use a clean, lint-free towel to clean the gasket surface on the fuel filter head.

![[ff2hdva.png]]

### Install

Use the correct filter(s) for your engine. [[35-018-024-om-mar — Filter Specifications|Refer to Procedure 018-024 in Section V.]]

Clean the area around the fuel filter and the fuel filter head.

Lubricate the fuel filter gasket with clean engine oil.

Fill the new fuel filter with clean diesel fuel.

![[ff8bdka.png]]

Install the new thread adapter sealing ring (1) supplied with the new filter.

![[06200008.png]]

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> If the filter canister is damaged in any way, do not use it. Dents or scrapes can lead to a rupture or premature failure of the filter.

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Mechanical overtightening of the filter can distort the threads and damage the fuel filter seal.

Install the filter on the filter head.

Turn the filter until the gasket contacts the filter head surface.

![[07200039.png]]

Tighten the filter, by hand, an additional ½ to ¾ of a turn after the gasket contacts the filter head surface, or as specified by the filter manufacturer.

![[07200040.png]]
