### Specifications

Automotive Applications

| Maximum Intake Restriction (Clean Air Filter Element) | 254 mm H 2 O \[10.0 in H 2 O\] |
|---|---|

| Maximum Intake Restriction (Dirty Air Filter Element) | 635 mm H 2 O \[25.0 in H 2 O\] |
|---|---|

#### Maximum Temperature Rise Between Ambient Air and Engine Inlet Air

| With EGR - Ambient (Above 0° \[32°F\] | 11.1°C \[20°F\] |
|---|---|

| Without EGR - Ambient (Above 0° \[32°F\] | 17°C \[30°F\] |
|---|---|

| Maximum Allowable Pressure Drop From Turbocharger to Intake Manifold | 152 mm Hg \[6 in Hg\] |
|---|---|

| Maximum Allowable Pressure Drop Across Charge Air Cooler | 76 mm Hg \[3 in Hg\] |
|---|---|

Marine Applications

| Maximum Intake Restriction (Clean Element) | 254 mm H 2 O \[10 in H 2 O\] |
|---|---|

| Maximum Intake Restriction (Dirty Element) | 507 mm H 2 O \[20 in H 2 O\] |
|---|---|

| Maximum Intake Manifold Temperature | 49°C \[120°F\] |
|---|---|

#### Maximum Engine Room Temperature:

| Intake air Versus Ambient Air at Rated Condition | 35°C \[63°F\] difference |
|---|---|

| Maximum Intake Restriction (Clean Element) | 381 mm H 2 O \[15 in H 2 O\] |

| Maximum Intake Restriction (Dirty Element) | 635 mm H 2 O \[25 in H 2 O\] |
|---|---|

#### Maximum Temperature Rise Between Ambient Air and Engine Inlet Air:

|---|---|
