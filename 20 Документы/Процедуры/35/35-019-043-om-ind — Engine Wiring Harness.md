### Maintenance Check

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Never touch any wiring connection when the keyswitch is in the ON position. An electrical shock or personal injury can result.

Inspect all wiring connections and wiring harnesses for damage. Damage to the wiring can cause improper engine operation and poor performance.
