### General Information

Industrial Applications

Perform maintenance at whichever interval occurs first. At each scheduled maintenance interval, perform all previous maintenance checks that are due for scheduled maintenance.

#### Maintenance Procedures at Daily Interval (Section 3)

- Drive Belts - Check
- Crankcase Breather Tube - Check
- Fuel-Water Separator - Drain
- Lubricating Oil Level - Check
- Fan, Cooling - Check
- Coolant Level - Check
- Charge Air Piping - Check
- Air Cleaner Restriction - Check
- Air Tanks and Reservoirs - Drain

#### Maintenance Procedures at 250 Hours, or 6 Months 1 (Section 4)

- Fuel Filter (Spin-On Type) - Change
- Lubricating Oil and Filters 2 - Change
- Coolant Filter 3 - Change
- Supplemental Coolant Additive (SCA) and Antifreeze Concentration - Check
- Engine Wiring Harness - Check

#### Maintenance Procedures at 1500 Hours, or 1 Year 1 (Section 5)

- Engine Steam Cleaning - Clean
- Overhead Set - Adjust
- Radiator Shutter Assembly - Check
- Water Pump - Check
- Cooling System Hoses - Check
- Air Cleaner Assembly (Engine-Mounted) - Check
- Air Leaks, Air Intake and Exhaust Systems - Check
- Turbocharger - Check
- Air Cleaner Restriction - Check
- Batteries - Check
- Engine Mounts - Inspect for Reuse

#### Maintenance Procedures at 6000 Hours, or 2 Years 1 (Section 6)

- Vibration Damper, Viscous - Check
- Cooling System - Flush
- Fan Drive Idler Pulley Assembly - Check
- Fan Hub, Belt Driven - Check
- Turbocharger - Check
- Air Compressor Discharge Lines - Check

Reference the appropriate sections for complete inspection and maintenance procedures.

1. These maintenance intervals **must** be performed at hours or months, whichever interval occurs first. At each scheduled maintenance interval, perform all previous maintenance procedures that are due for scheduled maintenance.
2. Fuel burn is the most accurate method for determining oil change intervals. Oil change intervals can be required more frequently, depending on the quality of the oil available. Reference the Oil Drain Interval table for marine and industrial engines in this procedure for additional oil change intervals.
3. Do **not** change the coolant filter if the SCA concentration level is over three units.
