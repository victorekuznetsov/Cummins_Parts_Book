### Fluorescent Dye Tracer

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> This test is **not** effective on a cold engine (less than 21°C \[70°F\] coolant temperature), or with a loose overhead setting.

Install an isolated fuel supply tank to the inlet and drain lines. Add fluorescent tracer, Part Number 3376891, to the fuel supply tank.

![[fs1tbpa.png]]

Remove the valve cover mounting hardware, but do **not** remove the valve cover.

![[vc200me.png]]

Start the engine, and operate it at high idle for 30 seconds. Let the engine go to low idle.

Complete the next step within 5 minutes.

![[oi203vf.png]]

While the engine is operating at low idle, remove the valve cover. [[35-003-011-tr — Rocker Lever Cover|Refer to Procedure 003-011 in Section 3.]]

![[03200047.png]]

Use black light, Part Number 3377253 or 3377394, to find fuel leaks from inside or around the injector. Refer to the black light manufacturer's operating instructions.

Injectors normally have a small amount of fuel leakage.

![[oi8towa.png]]

If there is excess leakage around the outside of the injector, check the top o-ring for damage.

Remove the injector and replace all three o-rings. [[35-006-026-tr — Injector|Refer to Procedure 006-026 in Section 6.]]

![[fi8orsa.png]]

If there is excess leakage from inside the injector, replace the injector.

[[35-006-026-tr — Injector|Refer to Procedure 006-026 in Section 6.]]

![[fi800sa.png]]

### Fuel Pressure Test

Remove the fuel drain line from the fuel drain tee fitting.

![[07200057.png]]

Install a cap on the tee fitting.

![[07200056.png]]

Remove the fuel drain line from the gear pump check valve. Install a plug in the fuel line.

![[fp8tbab.png]]

Remove the fuel supply line from the fuel supply pump shutoff valve.

Install a regulated air supply to the fuel supply line.

![[fp8ftmc.png]]

Apply air pressure to the cylinder head. The maximum air pressure is 552 kPa \[80 psi\].

![[07200058.png]]

Check for fuel leaks at each injector.

A little engine oil is helpful for exposing fuel leaks. Some leaks can be heard.

![[fi800sb.png]]

Replace the leaking injector, or replace all three outer o-rings.

[[35-006-026-tr — Injector|Refer to Procedure 006-026 in Section 6.]]

Check the injector for normal leakage.

![[fi200hm.png]]

Remove the air line, and install the fuel supply line.

![[fp8ftma.png]]

Remove the plug from the gear pump drain line.

Install the drain line on the gear pump check valve.

![[fp8tbab.png]]

Remove the cap from the fuel drain line fitting.

![[07200056.png]]

Install the fuel drain line.

![[07200057.png]]
