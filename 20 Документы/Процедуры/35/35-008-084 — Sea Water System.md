### Flush

Marine Applications

Remove the hose from the sea water pump outlet.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Place the hose into a container to catch the backflushed water.

![[08200162.png]]

Remove the sea water discharge line from the gear cooler.

![[08200163.png]]

Using hose reducers, install a clean, fresh water supply to the outlet of the gear oil cooler.

Flush the sea water system.

![[08200164.png]]

Remove the fresh water supply.

![[08200164.png]]

Install the sea water discharge line to the gear cooler.

![[08200163.png]]

Install the sea water pump outlet line onto the sea water pump.

![[08200162.png]]
