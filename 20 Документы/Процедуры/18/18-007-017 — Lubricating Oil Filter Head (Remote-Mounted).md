# Lubricating Oil Filter Head (Remote-Mounted)
**Головка масляного фильтра (выносная)**

> [!abstract] Процедура · `18-007-017`
> **Двигатели:** [[37269910 — K19 CPL 1253|37269910]], [[37280605 — K19 CPL 447|37280605]]
> **Семейство:** K19
> **Входит в руководства:** [[4021499 — K19 Service Manual|4021499]]
> **Секции:** Section 7 - Lubricating Oil System - Group 07
> **Даты:** изменён 2017-11-10
> **Источник:** [QuickServe](https://quickserve.cummins.com/qs3/pubsys2/xml/en/procedures/18/18-007-017.html) · [PDF-оригинал](https://github.com/victorekuznetsov/Cummins_Parts_Book/raw/main/bulletins/procedures/18-007-017.pdf)

### Preparatory Steps

- Remove lubricating oil filters. Refer to Procedure 007-013 in Section 7.

### Remove

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> A remote oil filter is used as an example in this procedure. The filter can be mounted in a different location depending on the equipment manufacturer.

Disconnect the inlet and outlet hoses from the lubricating oil filter head.

![[17400027.png]]

Disconnect the bypass oil hose from the lubricating oil filter head.

![[17400028.png]]

Remove capscrews and lubricating oil filter head from the bracket.

![[17400029.png]]

Remove the capscrews and lubricating oil filter head bracket.

![[17400030.png]]

### Inspect for Reuse

Check lines and hoses for cracks.

If the lines or hoses are cracked, they **must** be replaced.

![[oi8hosa.png]]

Inspect the lubricating oil filter head bracket for cracks or damage.

If the lubricating oil filter head bracket is cracked or damaged, it **must** be repaired or replaced.

![[17400098.png]]

### Install

Install the lubricating oil filter head bracket and capscrews.

Tighten the capscrews.

Use the following procedure for the correct torque values. [[99-018-009 — Capscrew Markings and Torque Values|Refer to Procedure 018-009 in Section V.]]

![[17400030.png]]

Install the lubricating oil filter head and capscrews.

Tighten the capscrews.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 25 n•m [20 ft-lb]

![[17400029.png]]

Connect the bypass oil hose to the lubricating oil filter head.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 35 n•m [25 ft-lb]

![[17400028.png]]

Connect the inlet and outlet hoses to the lubricating oil filter head.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 270 n•m [200 ft-lb]

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Make sure the port labeled OUT on the block connects to the inlet port on the filter head and the outlet port on the filter head connects to the inlet port on the block.

![[17400027.png]]

### Finishing Steps

- Install the lubricating oil filters. Refer to Procedure 007-013 in Section 7.
