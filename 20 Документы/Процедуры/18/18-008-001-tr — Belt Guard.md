# Belt Guard
**Защитный кожух ремня**

> [!abstract] Процедура · `18-008-001-tr`
> **Двигатели:** [[37269910 — K19 CPL 1253|37269910]], [[37280605 — K19 CPL 447|37280605]]
> **Семейство:** K19
> **Входит в руководства:** [[4021499 — K19 Service Manual|4021499]]
> **Секции:** Section 8 - Cooling System - Group 08
> **Даты:** изменён 2011-01-06
> **Источник:** [QuickServe](https://quickserve.cummins.com/qs3/pubsys2/xml/en/procedures/18/18-008-001-tr.html) · [PDF-оригинал](https://github.com/victorekuznetsov/Cummins_Parts_Book/raw/main/bulletins/procedures/18-008-001-tr.pdf)

### Remove

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The alternator guard may appear different than illustrated depending on the alternator option. Although different in apperance, the procedure remains the same.

Remove the alternator belt guard assembly.

If equipped, use **only** the captive-washer capscrews.

![[08i00001.png]]

Remove the four capscrews and washers from the accessory drive pulley and the belt guard, if installed. Remove the capscrew, isolator, and spacer.

Remove the belt guard.

![[08400510.png]]

Remove the two mounting capscrews and the belt guard bracket.

![[08400512.png]]

Remove the three capscrews, isolators, and spacer from the upper bracket. Remove the belt guard.

![[08400511.png]]

### Inspect for Reuse

Inspect the belt guard and bracket for cracks or other damage. If damaged, they **must** be replaced.

Inspect the isolators for damage. If the isolators are hard and brittle, they **must** be replaced.

If damaged or bent, replace the belt guard studs.

![[08400513.png]]

### Install

Install the belt guard and mounting bracket. Tighten the two capscrews

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 40 n•m [30 ft-lb]

![[08400512.png]]

Install the belt guard.

Install the three capscrews, isolators, and spacer into the upper bracket.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 25 n•m [221 in-lb]

![[08400511.png]]

Install the accessory drive pulley and belt guard, if installed.

Install the four capscrews and washers.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 15 n•m [133 in-lb]

Install the one capscrew, isolator, and spacer.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 25 n•m [221 in-lb]

![[08400510.png]]

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The alternator guard may appear different than illustrated depending on the alternator option. Although different in apperance, the procedure remains the same.

Install the alternator belt guard assembly.

Tighten the captive-washer capscrews.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 3/8 inch Capscrew 40 n•m [30 ft-lb]

Install the two captive-washer capscrews.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 1/4 inch Capscrew 10 n•m [89 in-lb]
