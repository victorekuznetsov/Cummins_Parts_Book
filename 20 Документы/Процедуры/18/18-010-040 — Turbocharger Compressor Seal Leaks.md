# Turbocharger Compressor Seal Leaks

### Inspect

Remove the air intake piping from the turbocharger.

![[tb4ilna.png]]

Remove the hose and two clamps between the air crossover and the turbocharger.

Remove the clamp between the air crossover and the aftercoolers or intercoolers.

Remove the air crossover.

![[10400076.png]]

Examine the compressor discharge and air crossover piping for oil.

![[10400026.png]]

If oil is in the compressor discharge or crossover piping, check the axial and radial clearance of the turbocharger. [[18-010-033-tr — Turbocharger|Refer to Procedure 010-033 in Section 10.]]

![[tb1shia.png]]

Check for intake restriction. Refer to Procedure 010-031 in Section 10.

If the restriction exceeds specifications, replace or clean the air filter element. Refer to the OEM service manual.

![[10600136.png]]

Remove the capscrews and disconnect the oil drain line from the turbocharger.

Remove and discard the gasket.

Disconnect the oil drain tube from the union and remove the oil drain line.

Remove the adapter elbow from the cylinder block.

Discard the hose if it is cracked, frayed, or will **not** bend without cracking.

![[tb4hoha.png]]

Check the angle of the drain tube.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The angle of the tube **must** be within 35 degrees of vertical. Adjust the turbocharger, if necessary. [[18-010-033-tr — Turbocharger|Refer to Procedure 010-033 in Section 10.]]

![[tb400ua.png]]

If the drain tube is free of restrictions and at the correct angle, check the crankcase breathers and tubes to make sure they are **not** plugged or restricted.

![[br1tbea.png]]

If these checks do **not** reveal the problem, measure the crankcase pressure (blowby). Refer to Procedure 014-005 in Section 14.

![[10400028.png]]

If these checks do **not** reveal the problem, measure the crankcase pressure (blowby).
