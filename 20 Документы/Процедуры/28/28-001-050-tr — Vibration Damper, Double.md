### Preparatory Steps

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> This component or assembly weighs greater than 23 kg \[50 lb\]. To prevent serious personal injury, be sure to have assistance or use appropriate lifting equipment to lift this component or assembly.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Batteries can emit explosive gases. To reduce the possibility of personal injury, always ventilate the compartment before servicing the batteries. To reduce the possibility of arcing, remove the negative (-) battery cable first and attach the negative (-) battery cable last.

- Disconnect the batteries. [[99-013-009 — Battery Cables and Connections|Refer to Procedure 013-009 in Section 13.]]
- Remove the fan belt, if equipped. [[28-008-002-tr — Drive Belt, Cooling Fan|Refer to Procedure 008-002 in Section 8.]]

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Engines that do **not** have a belt-driven fan hub do **not** have a pulley.

- Remove the crankshaft pulley, if equipped, from the vibration damper. Refer to Procedure 001-022 in Section 1.

![[ck800wa.png]]

### Remove

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> This component or assembly weighs greater than 23 kg \[50 lb\]. To prevent serious personal injury, be sure to have assistance or use appropriate lifting equipment to lift this component or assembly.

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Do not pry on the vibration damper with tools that are sharp or pointed; damage to the damper can result.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The roll pin (1) does **not** have to be removed unless it is damaged.

The use of a guide bolt aids the disassembly procedure.

Remove the SAE Grade 8 capscrews (5), the hardened washers (6), the outer damper (4), the pulley (3), and the inner damper (2).

![[da600hb.png]]

### Clean and Inspect for Reuse

Clean and inspect the dampers for reuse.

[[28-001-052-tr — Vibration Damper, Viscous|Refer to Procedure 001-052 in Section 1.]]

![[da100ec.png]]

### Install

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> This component or assembly weighs greater than 23 kg \[50 lb\]. To prevent serious personal injury, be sure to have assistance or use appropriate lifting equipment to lift this component or assembly.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> The capscrews must be SAE Grade 8. The washers must be hardened washers. If the wrong parts are installed, the capscrews can fail, allowing the damper to become loose and cause personal injury.

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Dampers are available with standard and high viscosity fluid inside the damper. If the damper has been replaced, check to be sure the correct dampers are installed.

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> The first vibration damper (2) must be aligned correctly with the crankshaft adapter, or the valve and injector adjustment marks on the vibration damper will be in the wrong position.

The damper nearest to the front gear housing **must** contain the valve and injector adjustment marks.

If removed, install the roll pin (1) in the crankshaft adapter.

The use of a guide bolt aids the assembly procedure.

Check the mating surfaces of the adapter, both dampers, and the pulley to be sure they are clean, dry, and free of nicks or burrs.

Align the hole for the pin in the damper with the roll pin. Install the first damper (2) on the adapter. Install the pulley (3).

Check to be sure the pilots on the crankshaft adapter and the pulley are aligned.

Install the second damper (4).

Use clean engine oil to lubricate the capscrews and the washers.

Install the washers and capscrews.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 190 n•m [140 ft-lb]

### Finishing Steps

- Install

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> This component or assembly weighs greater than 23 kg \[50 lb\]. To prevent serious personal injury, be sure to have assistance or use appropriate lifting equipment to lift this component or assembly.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Batteries can emit explosive gases. To reduce the possibility of personal injury, always ventilate the compartment before servicing the batteries. To reduce the possibility of arcing, remove the negative (-) battery cable first and attach the negative (-) battery cable last.

- ll the crankshaft pulley to the vibration damper, if equipped. Refer to Procedure 001-022 in Section 1.
- Measure the eccentricity and wobble. [[28-001-052-tr — Vibration Damper, Viscous|Refer to Procedure 001-052 in Section 1.]]
- Install the fan belt, if equipped. [[28-008-002-tr — Drive Belt, Cooling Fan|Refer to Procedure 008-002 in Section 8.]]
- Connect the batteries. [[99-013-009 — Battery Cables and Connections|Refer to Procedure 013-009 in Section 13.]]
- Operate the engine and check for proper operation.
