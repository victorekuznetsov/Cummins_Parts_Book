### Specifications

with Electronically Actuated Injector

#### Maximum fuel supply restriction at fuel pump inlet with dirty fuel filter element(s) at maximum fuel flow:

| Industrial Applications | 34 kPa \[10 in-Hg\] |
|---|---|

| Marine Applications | 34 kPa \[10 in-Hg\] |
|---|---|

#### Maximum fuel drain restriction before (or without) check valve:

| Industrial Applications | 34 kPa \[10 in-Hg\] |
|---|---|

| Marine Applications | 34 kPa \[10 in-Hg\] |
|---|---|

| Maximum fuel inlet temperature: | 71°C \[160°F\] |
|---|---|

| Minimum fuel tank venting rate: | 1700 liters/hr \[60 cubic ft/hr\] |
|---|---|

with Mechanically Actuated Injector

| Engine Idle Speed | 650 to 725 rpm |
|---|---|

#### Maximum Fuel Inlet Restriction (at rated power)

| Clean Fuel Filter | 14 kPa \[4 in-Hg\] |
|---|---|

#### Maximum Fuel Inlet Restriction (at rated power)

| Dirty Fuel Filter | 27 kPa \[8 in-Hg\] |
|---|---|

#### Maximum Fuel Drain Line Restriction (at high idle, no load)

| No Check Valves | 8 kPa \[2.5 in-Hg\] |
|---|---|

| Check Valves | 22 kPa \[6.5 in-Hg\] |
|---|---|

#### Fuel Check Valve between Fuel Filter and Fuel Pump

| Minimum Opening Pressure | 2.1 kPa \[0.6 in-Hg\] |
|---|---|

#### Fuel Check Valve between Fuel Pump and Cylinder Head

| Opening Pressure | 21 to 55 kPa \[6.20 to 16.25 in-Hg\] |
|---|---|

#### Fuel Check Valve in Fuel Drain Line

| Opening Pressure | 1.7 to 3.3 kPa \[0.5 to 1 in-Hg\] |
|---|---|

| Minimum Engine Cranking Speed | 150 rpm |
|---|---|

#### Minimum Fuel Tank Air Venting Capability

| With 8.5 kPa \[2.5 in-Hg\] or less fuel drain restriction | 425 liter/hr \[15 cubic ft/hr\] |
|---|---|

| Derate Engine Fuel Rate for High Altitude | 4 percent per 300 m \[1000 ft\] above 3600 m \[12,000 ft\] |
|---|---|

| Derate Engine Fuel Rate for Hot Weather | 2 percent per 11°C above 38°C \[1 percent per 10°F above 100°F\] |
|---|---|

#### Shutoff Valve Solenoid Coil Resistance

| 12 VDC | 6 to 10 ohms |
|---|---|

| 24 VDC | 26 to 40 ohms |
|---|---|
