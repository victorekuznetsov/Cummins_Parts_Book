# Marine Gear Oil Cooler Plumbing
**Трубопроводы маслоохладителя судового реверс-редуктора**

### Preparatory Steps

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Batteries can emit explosive gases. To reduce the possibility of personal injury, always ventilate the compartment before servicing the batteries. To reduce the possibility of arcing, remove the negative (-) battery cable first and attach the negative (-) battery cable last.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Do not remove the pressure cap from a hot engine. Wait until the coolant temperature is below 50°C \[120°F\] before removing the pressure cap. Heated coolant spray or steam can cause personal injury.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Coolant is toxic. Keep away from children and pets. If not reused, dispose of in accordance with local environmental regulations.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> To reduce the possibility of personal injury, avoid direct contact of hot oil with your skin.

- Disconnect the batteries or air supply line to the air starter to prevent accidental engine starting. Refer to the OEM service manual.
- Drain the cooling system. [[28-008-018-tr — Cooling System|Refer to Procedure 008-018 in Section 8.]]

![[ck800wa.png]]

### Remove

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> **Only** remove the elbows from the marine gear oil cooler if necessary.

Remove the bolt securing the p-clips (1) together.

Remove the coolant line connectors (2) from the elbows on the marine gear oil cooler.

Remove the coolant line connectors (3) from the water transfer tube.

![[08e00173.png]]

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> **Only** remove the fittings from the LTA water pump if necessary.

Remove the bolt securing the p-clips (1) together.

Remove the coolant line connectors (2) from the fittings on the LTA water pump.

Remove the coolant line connectors (3) from the water transfer tube.

![[08e00174.png]]

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> There are 3 brackets on the QSK38 engine and 4 brackets on the QSK50 engine.

Remove the bolts (1), p-clips, and water transfer tubes.

Discard the o-rings.

![[08e00175.png]]

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> If the lubricating oil pan is being removed, mark the location of the brackets to make sure they are installed in the same location.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> There are 3 brackets on the QSK38 engine and 4 brackets on the QSK50 engine.

Remove the bolts, washers, and brackets from the oil pan.

![[08e00176.png]]

### Inspect for Reuse

Inspect the coolant lines and water transfer tubes for cracks, fretting, or other damage. If damage is found, the part **must** be replaced.

![[08e00177.png]]

Inspect the brackets and p-clips for cracks and other damage. Check that the coating on the water transfer tube clips has **not** been worn away.

Inspect the capscrews for damaged threads.

If damage is found, the part **must** be replaced.

![[08e00178.png]]

### Install

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> There are 3 brackets on the QSK38 engine and 4 brackets on the QSK50 engine.

Install the bolts, washers, and brackets into the lubricating oil pan.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 45 n•m [33 ft-lb]

![[08e00176.png]]

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Do **not** torque any fittings or bolts until all components have been installed to make sure of proper alignment.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> There are 3 brackets on the QSK38 engine and 4 brackets on the QSK50 engine.

Install the bolts (1), p-clips, and water transfer tubes, using new o-rings.

![[08e00175.png]]

If removed, install the elbows with new o-rings onto the engine and marine gear oil cooler.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 135 n•m [100 ft-lb]

Connect the coolant line connectors (2) to the fittings on the LTA water pump.

Connect the coolant line connectors (3) to the water transfer tubes.

Install the bolt securing the p-clips (1) together.

![[08e00174.png]]

Connect the coolant line connectors (2) to the elbows on the marine gear cooler.

Connect the coolant line connectors (3) to the water transfer tubes.

Install the bolt securing the p-clips (1) together.

Tighten the hose fittings and clips.

Hose Fittings

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 100 n•m [74 ft-lb]

Clips

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 25 n•m [221 in-lb]

![[08e00173.png]]

### Finishing Steps

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Batteries can emit explosive gases. To reduce the possibility of personal injury, always ventilate the compartment before servicing the batteries. To reduce the possibility of arcing, remove the negative (-) battery cable first and attach the negative (-) battery cable last.

- Connect the batteries or air starter air supply line. Refer to the OEM service manual.
- Fill the cooling system. [[28-008-018-tr — Cooling System|Refer to Procedure 008-018 in Section 8.]]
- Operate the engine and check for leaks
