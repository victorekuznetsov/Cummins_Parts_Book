This graphic illustrates the cylinder numbering sequence for the KTA38 and QSK38 engines.

- RB = Right-bank of cylinders
- LB = Left-bank of cylinders.

To determine the right and left bank on the KTA38 and QSK38 engines, stand at the rear of the engine and face the front.

When referencing system electronics, a standard cylinder numbering system has been adopted. This is only used for the electronic system components on engines with electronically actuated injectors.
