### Drain

Operate the engine until the water temperature reaches 60°C \[140°F\].

Shut down the engine.

![[07600232.png]]

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Some state and federal agencies have determined that used engine oil can be carcinogenic and cause reproductive toxicity. Avoid inhalation of vapors, ingestion, and prolonged contact with used engine oil. If not reused, dispose of in accordance with local environmental regulations.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> To reduce the possibility of personal injury, avoid direct contact of hot oil with your skin.

Remove all oil drain plugs from the oil pan. Drain the oil immediately to make sure all of the suspended contaminants are removed from the engine.

![[07600257.png]]

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Some state and federal agencies have determined that used engine oil can be carcinogenic and cause reproductive toxicity. Avoid inhalation of vapors, ingestion, and prolonged contact with used engine oil. If not reused, dispose of in accordance with local environmental regulations.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> To reduce the possibility of personal injury, avoid direct contact of hot oil with your skin.

For engines with the Eliminator™ filter system, also drain the oil from the Eliminator™ by removing the vent plug (1) and the oil drain plug (2).

![[07600595.png]]

### Remove

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Some state and federal agencies have determined that used engine oil can be carcinogenic and cause reproductive toxicity. Avoid inhalation of vapors, ingestion, and prolonged contact with used engine oil. If not reused, dispose of in accordance with local environmental regulations.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> To reduce the possibility of personal injury, avoid direct contact of hot oil with your skin.

Use an oil filter wrench, Part Number 3375049, or equivalent, to remove the full flow and bypass oil filters.

Discard the filters if **not** required for failure analysis.

![[07600230.png]]

### Install

Clean the oil filter head sealing surface with a lint free cloth.

![[07600259.png]]

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Some state and federal agencies have determined that used engine oil can be carcinogenic and cause reproductive toxicity. Avoid inhalation of vapors, ingestion, and prolonged contact with used engine oil. If not reused, dispose of in accordance with local environmental regulations.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> To reduce the possibility of personal injury, avoid direct contact of hot oil with your skin.

Use engine oil to lubricate the oil filter's rubber seal.

Fill the oil filters with clean engine oil.

![[lf8etwb.png]]

Install the oil filters as specified by the oil filter manufacturer.

Cummins Inc. recommends the use of high quality oil filters such as Fleetguard™.

![[07600230.png]]

### Fill

Check and clean the oil drain plug threads and the sealing surface. Install and tighten the oil drain plugs.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 45 n•m [33 ft-lb]

![[07600258.png]]

For engines with the Eliminator™ filter system, install the vent plug (1) and the oil drain plug (2).

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 20 n•m [177 in-lb]

![[07600595.png]]

Cummins Inc. recommends the use of a high quality 15W-40 diesel engine oil such as Valvoline™ Premium Blue™ or Premium Blue™ Extreme Life.

Choose the correct oil for the operating climate as outlined. [[102-018-003 — Lubricating Oil Recommendations and Specifications|Refer to Procedure Procedure 018-003 in Section V.]]

![[pl800gb.png]]

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Some state and federal agencies have determined that used engine oil can be carcinogenic and cause reproductive toxicity. Avoid inhalation of vapors, ingestion, and prolonged contact with used engine oil. If not reused, dispose of in accordance with local environmental regulations.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> To reduce the possibility of personal injury, avoid direct contact of hot oil with your skin.

Fill the engine to the correct level with clean engine oil. Refer to Procedure 018-017 in Section V for engine oil capacity.

Check the oil level on the dipstick. The level **must** be to the high (H) mark on the dipstick. Do **not** overfill. Drain off excess oil in a pan.

To properly check the oil level:

- Remove the dipstick and wipe clean.
- Insert dipstick fully and remove to observe the reading.
- Repeat to make sure consistent readings.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The o-ring seal on the dipstick can cause a vacuum during fill and falsely create a high reading. Vent the dipstick during fill to prevent a vacuum or false reading.

![[oi801vw.png]]

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Before starting the engine, complete the steps given in Starting Procedure After Extended Shutdown or Oil Change in Section 1 to make sure the engine receives adequate lubrication. Shortage of lubrication can damage the engine.

Operate the engine at low idle speed to inspect for leaks at the oil filters and drain plug(s).

Verify oil is flowing through the reservoir tank. The tank **must** warm as lubricating engine oil circulates through.

![[07600232.png]]

Turn keyswitch OFF.

Wait approximately 5 minutes to allow the oil to drain from the upper parts of the engine.

Check the oil level.

Add oil as necessary to bring the oil level to the high (H) mark on the dipstick.

![[oi801vw.png]]
