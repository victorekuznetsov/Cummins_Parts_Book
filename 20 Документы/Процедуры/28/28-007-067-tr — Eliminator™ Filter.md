### Remove

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> This component or assembly weighs greater than 23kg \[50lb\]. To prevent serious personal injury, be sure to have assistance or use appropriate lifting equipment to lift this component or assembly.

Remove the mounting capscrews.

Install the guide studs and remove the eight remaining capscrews.

Remove the two support capscrews.

Remove the filter over the guide studs while removing the connection tube from the hand hole cover.

Remove the gaskets over the guide studs.

Remove the four guide studs from the engine block.

### Install

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> The Eliminator™ filter can be installed on either side of the engine. Check to be sure the lubricating oil transfer tube has been installed correctly. The filter must be on the opposite bank from the oil transfer tube. If the Eliminator™ filter is installed on the wrong bank, the engine will be severely damaged because of the lack of lubrication.

Install the Eliminator™ filter hand hole cover with a new gasket.

Tighten the capscrews.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 47 n•m [35 ft-lb]

![[07600184.png]]

Lubricate the new o-ring seals (1) with vegetable oil and install on both ends of the connection (2) tube.

Install the connection tube into the filter housing.

![[07b00266.png]]

Install six guide studs to the engine in the locations shown.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Do **not** use threaded guide studs. This will aid in guide stud removal once the Eliminator™ filter is installed.

Install the gasket over the guide studs.

Make sure the six mounting holes are visible through each gasket.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The same gasket is installed in both locations and in the same orientation. To aid in correct installation the part number is printed in the top right hand corner of the gasket. The part number **must** face away from the cylinder block.

![[07400462.png]]

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> This component or assembly weighs greater than 23kg \[50lb\]. To reduce the possibility of personal injury, be sure to have assistance or use appropriate lifting equipment to lift this component or assembly.

Install the filter over the guide studs while guiding the connection tube into the hand hole cover.

Install six capscrews and washers and finger tighten.

Remove the guide studs and install the remaining capscrews and washers, and finger tighten.

![[07600061.png]]

Tighten the capscrews in sequence shown.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 47 n•m [35 ft-lb]

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Due to the difficulty in accessing the lower capscrews, the use of a 3/8 drive socket and torque wrench is recommended to gain access to achieve the correct torque value on these capscrews.

Tighten the capscrews.

### Pressure Test

This test will indicate oil pressure before and after the oil flows through the Eliminator™.

Use a differential pressure gauge or one pressure gauge with two oil hoses and two valves (1 and 2) to eliminate gauge error. Use a gauge with a minimum pressure capacity of 1400 kPa \[200 psi\].

Be sure the engine is switched OFF.

![[07e00184.png]]

Remove the two plugs from the filter housing.

Install end of the valve assembly in the port of the filter nearest to the centrifuge from where the plug has been removed.

Install the end of the valve assembly in the port of the filter furthest away from the centrifuge from where the other plug has been removed.

![[07600238.png]]

Operate the engine at rated rpm until the engine oil reaches its normal operating temperature. Continue running the engine at rated rpm for the duration of the pressure test.

![[oi400la.png]]

Close valve (1), open valve (2), and record the pressure value before the Eliminator™.

Close valve (2), open valve (1), and record the pressure value after the Eliminator™.

If the difference in pressure is more than the maximum allowable value, inspect and clean the Eliminator™ screens before checking the oil pressure differential again.

| Measurements |  |  |
|---|---|---|
|  | kpa | psi |
| Maximum Oil Pressure Differential | 60 | 9 |

![[07e00184.png]]

Switch the engine OFF.

Remove the valve assembly from the Eliminator™ and install the two plugs.

Tighten the plugs.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 10 n•m [89 in-lb]
