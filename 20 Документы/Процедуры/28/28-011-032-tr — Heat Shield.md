### Preparatory Steps

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Batteries can emit explosive gases. To reduce the possibility of personal injury, always ventilate the compartment before servicing the batteries. To reduce the possibility of arcing, remove the negative (-) battery cable first and attach the negative (-) battery cable last.

- Disconnect the batteries. See equipment manufacturer service information.

### Install

K38

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Rear out and front out configurations for heat shields are similar. The process below is for rear out, which can be used as a guideline for front out configurations.

Ensure 5 mm to 10 mm clearance between heat shields and turbocharger oil drain hoses and coolant pipes.

Install the left bank, side section of heat shield to the exhaust manifold.

Install the right bank, side sections of heat shield to the exhaust manifold.

Install the front section of heat shielding. Tighten the two capscrews.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 23 n•m [204 in-lb]

![[11500353.png]]

Install the support brackets to the cylinder heads and rocker housings.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> Capscrews to Rocker Cover 27 n•m [239 in-lb]

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> Capscrews to Rocker Housing 45 n•m [33 ft-lb]

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> Capscrews to Heat Shield 38 n•m [28 ft-lb]

![[11500352.png]]

Install the front exhaust manifold cover.

Hand tighten the 13 capscrews.

![[11500351.png]]

Install the rear exhaust manifold cover using two capscrews on either side.

Hand tighten the two capscrews.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Do **not** install the top two capscrews.

Install the rear section of the exhaust manifold heat shielding through the rear and side shields with nine capscrews.

Hand tighten the capscrews.

![[11500350.png]]

Install the first section of heat shielding around the turbochargers.

Hand tighten the capscrews.

![[11500349.png]]

Install the heat shield covers under the turbine outlets.

Hand tighten the capscrews.

Install the outboard section of heat shielding around the right bank turbocharger.

Hand tighten the capscrews.

Install the remaining section of heat shielding from the compressor side of the left bank turbocharger.

Hand tighten the capscrews.

![[11500348.png]]

Install the center section of heat shielding on the compressor side of the turbochargers.

Hand tighten the capscrews.

![[11500347.png]]

Install the three sections of heat shielding on the compressor side of the turbochargers.

Hand tighten the capscrews.

![[11500346.png]]

Install the heat shield section from the top of the turbochargers using the nine capscrews around the shielding.

Hand tighten the capscrews.

Tighten all capscrews to the following torque unless otherwise stated above.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 27 n•m [239 in-lb]

![[11500345.png]]

Single Stage

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> For installation, reverse the removal procedure above. Hand tighten the capscrews until completely assembled unless otherwise stated.

Ensure 5 mm to 10 mm clearance between heat shields and turbocharger oil drain hoses and coolant pipes.

Install the lower exhaust manifold side shields to the engine.

![[11500345.png]]

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value

![[11500342.png]]

Install the brackets and capscrews between the right bank rocker lever housings and exhaust manifold side shields.

![[11500340.png]]

Install the heat shielding and capscrews around the bottom of the turbochargers.

![[11500339.png]]

Install the top sections of the heat shields over the turbochargers and tighten the capscrews.

Tighten the remaining capscrews.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 27 n•m [239 in-lb]

### Finishing Steps

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Batteries can emit explosive gases. To reduce the possibility of personal injury, always ventilate the compartment before servicing the batteries. To reduce the possibility of arcing, remove the negative (-) battery cable first and attach the negative (-) battery cable last.

- Connect the batteries. See equipment manufacturer service information.
- Start the engine and check for proper operation.
