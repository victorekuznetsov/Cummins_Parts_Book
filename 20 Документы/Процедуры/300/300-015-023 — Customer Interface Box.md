# Customer Interface Box
**Интерфейсная коробка заказчика**

> [!abstract] Процедура · `300-015-023`
> **Двигатели:** [[35354607 — QSM11 CM570 CPL 8608|35354607]], [[35373113 — QSM11 CM570 CPL 8471|35373113]], [[71156161 — QSM11 CM570 CPL 8543|71156161]]
> **Семейство:** QSM11
> **Входит в руководства:** [[4332828 — Marine C Command HD Elite™ Panel System Master Repair Manual|4332828]]
> **Секции:** Section 15 - Instruments and Controls - Group 15
> **Даты:** изменён 2019-07-17
> **Источник:** [QuickServe](https://quickserve.cummins.com/qs3/pubsys2/xml/en/procedures/300/300-015-023.html) · [PDF-оригинал](https://github.com/victorekuznetsov/Cummins_Parts_Book/raw/main/bulletins/procedures/300-015-023.pdf)

### Remove

Customer Interface Box (C.I.B.)

Disconnect the C1 and C2 connectors at the bottom of the C.I.B. panel. The data link has a protective cap and does **not** need to be removed unless the data link is in use.

![[15900066.png]]

Loosen the mounting bolts (1) on the C.I.B. panel mounting brackets.

![[15400059.png]]

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> This component or assembly weighs greater than 23 kg \[50 lb\]. To reduce the possibility of serious personal injury, make sure to have assistance or use appropriate lifting equipment to lift this component or assembly.

Support the C.I.B. panel.

Remove the four mounting bolts (1).

![[15400058.png]]

Diesel Control Unit

Make sure all wire identification is attached to the wires before removing the terminal strip plug connectors from the circuit board.

Disconnect the wires from the diesel control unit by unplugging the terminal strips (1) from the circuit board.

![[15400060.png]]

Disconnect the Ethernet Modbus™ connector (2) from the diesel control unit.

![[15400061.png]]

Remove the diesel control unit mounting screws (1) from the front plate on the outside of the C.I.B.

Slide the diesel control unit out of the front of the C.I.B. panel.

![[15400062.png]]

Loosen the spring clamps that hold the circuit board to aid in removal.

Use a flat head screwdriver to loosen the screw in the clamp.

![[15400063.png]]

Remove the diesel control unit circuit board from the C.I.B. and rail by pressing down and lifting outward at the bottom of the circuit board.

![[15400064.png]]

Shutdown Unit

Make sure all wire identification is attached to the wires before removing the terminal strip plug connectors from the circuit board.

Disconnect the wires from the shutdown unit by unplugging the terminal strips (1) from the circuit board.

![[15400060.png]]

Disconnect the Com 3 configuration Ethernet connector (2) from the shutdown unit.

Disconnect the Com 4 USB connector (3) from the shutdown unit.

![[15400065.png]]

Loosen the spring clamps that hold the circuit board to aid in removal.

Use a flat head screwdriver to loosen the screw in the clamp.

![[15400063.png]]

Remove the shutdown unit circuit board from the C.I.B. and rail by pressing down and lifting outward on the bottom of the circuit board.

![[15400066.png]]

Engine Stop Button

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> To reduce the possibility of personal injury and equipment damage, shipboard tag out and lock out procedures must be followed.

Make a note of the connection points of all wires.

Loosen the wire set screws and remove the wires from the switch.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Panels for the offshore drill rig application will have a third contact with corresponding wires in this location.

![[15600154.png]]

Loosen the bottom set screw.

Lift the upper lever.

Pull the rear switch assembly off the button.

![[15600155.png]]

Slide the engine stop button out of the panel door.

![[15600156.png]]

Power Switch

Make a note of the connection points of all wires.

Loosen the wire set screws and remove the wires from the switch.

![[15600157.png]]

Remove the switch assembly by turning the switch assembly **counterclockwise** and pulling outward.

![[15600158.png]]

Turn the button mounting nut **counterclockwise** and remove the nut.

Slide the button out of the panel.

![[15600159.png]]

Ethernet switch, if equipped.

Disconnect the Ethernet cable from the Ethernet switch.

![[15600160.png]]

Remove the cable tray cover.

Disconnect the Ethernet switch power connector.

![[15600161.png]]

Loosen the terminal strip securing the mechanism set screw.

Slide the terminal strip securing the mechanism to the right as much as possible.

Place a flat head screwdriver in the bottom tab of the Ethernet switch.

To remove the Ethernet switch, apply downward pressure on the screwdriver handle while lifting up on the Ethernet switch.

![[15600162.png]]

Terminal Strip

Remove the cable tray cover.

Remove the Ethernet switch.

![[15600161.png]]

Make a note of the connection points of all wires.

Place a flat head screwdriver in the slot below the wire to be removed.

Apply downward pressure on the screwdriver handle and remove the wire(s).

![[15600164.png]]

Place a flat head screwdriver in the upper slot of the connector.

To remove the terminal strip connector, apply downward pressure on the screwdriver handle while lifting up on the connector.

![[15600165.png]]

Circuit Breaker

Make a note of the location of the wires on the circuit breaker.

Loosen the set screws and remove the wires.

![[17800123.png]]

Place a flat head screwdriver in the lower release rectangle and move the handle upward to release the circuit breaker from the track.

Remove the circuit breaker.

![[17800124.png]]

Conductor

Remove the associated cable tray covers (1).

![[17800127.png]]

Note the location of the wire to be removed.

Place the screwdriver in the square slot on the terminal strip.

Move the handle in a downward motion and remove the wire.

![[17800149.png]]

Push Style Terminal Strip.

Note the location of the wire to be removed.

Place the screwdriver on the release button.

Push the screwdriver onto the release button to release the wire.

Remove the wire while holding down on the release button.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Do **not** use a sharp object that could pierce the release button.

![[19c91606.png]]

Remove the wire from the cable tray.

Remove the wire from the other connection point.

![[17800150.png]]

Logic Relays

- Solid state relay
- Mounted in a block.

Remove the cable tray cover.

![[15600166.png]]

Make a note of the connection points of all wires.

Loosen the relay terminal screws.

![[15600167.png]]

Place a flat head screwdriver in the tab on the relay block.

Apply downward pressure on the screwdriver handle while pushing up on the relay block and remove the block and relay assembly.

![[15600168.png]]

### Install

Customer Interface Box

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> This component or assembly weighs greater than 23 kg \[50 lb\]. To reduce the possibility of serious personal injury, make sure to have assistance or use appropriate lifting equipment to lift this component or assembly.

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> An isolator must be used when mounting a C.I.B. panel. Vibration can damage the panel and internal components.

Support the C.I.B. panel.

Install the four mounting bolts (1) through the panel and into the mounting brackets.

Tighten the mounting bolts.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 64 n•m [47 ft-lb]

![[15400058.png]]

Connect the C1 and C2 connectors at the bottom of the C.I.B. panel. Connect the data link if it was disconnected.

![[15900066.png]]

Diesel Control Unit

Install the diesel control unit through the front of the C.I.B. door and install the four mounting screws (1).

Tighten the screws.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 0.4 n•m [3.6 in-lb]

![[15400062.png]]

Connect the Ethernet Modbus™ connector (2) to the diesel control unit.

![[15400061.png]]

Make sure all wire identification on the terminal strip connector matches the diesel control unit.

Connect the terminal strip connector (1) to the circuit board.

![[15400060.png]]

Shutdown Unit

Install the shutdown unit circuit board by latching the unit on the top of the support rail and pressing the unit firmly into place.

The unit will make an audible noise when it seats into position.

![[15400066.png]]

If loosened for removal, tighten the spring clamps with a flat head screwdriver.

![[15400063.png]]

Connect the Com 3 configuration Ethernet connector (2) to the shutdown unit.

Connect the Com 4 USB connector (3) to the shutdown unit.

![[15400065.png]]

Make sure all wire identification matches the circuit board.

Connect the the terminal strip connector (1) to the circuit board.

![[15400060.png]]

Engine Stop Button

Slide the engine stop button into the panel door.

![[15600156.png]]

Lift the lever on the switch assembly and place onto the button.

Tighten the bottom set screw.

![[15600155.png]]

Install the wires into the noted locations.

Tighten the set screws.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Panels for the offshore drill rig application will have a third contact with corresponding wires in this location.

![[15600154.png]]

Power Switch

Slide the button into the panel.

Install the button nut and tighten.

![[15600159.png]]

Place the switch assembly onto the button assembly and push it until it snaps onto the button assembly.

Install the wires into the noted locations and tighten the set screws.

![[15600157.png]]

Ethernet Switch, if equipped.

Place the Ethernet switch on the support rail connector with the top of the switch connecting the assembly on the top of the support rail.

Push the bottom of the switch downward until it snaps onto the track.

Slide the terminal strip securing mechanism to the left and tighten the set screw.

![[15600169.png]]

Connect the Ethernet switch power connector.

Install the cable tray cover.

![[15600161.png]]

Connect the Ethernet cable to the Ethernet switch.

![[15600160.png]]

Terminal Strip

Position the upper leg of the terminal strip onto the track.

Press the lower portion of the terminal strip until it snaps onto the track.

![[15600170.png]]

Place a flat head screwdriver in the slot below the wire to be installed.

Apply downward pressure on the screwdriver handle and install the wire(s).

Install the cable tray cover.

![[15600164.png]]

Logic Relays

- Solid state relay
- Mounted in a block.

Place the lower leg of the relay block on the track.

Press the upper portion of the block until it snaps into place.

![[15600171.png]]

Install the wires into the relay block.

Tighten the set screws.

Install the cable tray cover.

![[15600167.png]]

Circuit Breaker

Place the upper connecting tab on the track.

Push the lower portion of the circuit breaker until it locks onto the track.

![[17800134.png]]

Non-Push Style Terminal Strip

Install the wires into the noted locations and tighten the set screws.

![[17800123.png]]

Push Style Terminal Strip.

Install the wires into the noted locations and lightly pull on wire to ensure connection.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> May need to press release button on terminal strip to allow wire to connect to the terminal strip.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Do **not** use a sharp object that could pierce the release button.

![[19c91606.png]]

Conductor

Route the wire through the cable tray to the associated connector.

![[17800150.png]]

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> To reduce the possibility of personal injury and equipment damage, the wire must have the correct ferrule installed with the stripping and crimp tool.

Strip the wire with the stripping and crimp tool, Part Number 4918722.

Crimp the new ferrule onto the end of the wire.

![[17800139.png]]

Place the screwdriver in the square slot on the terminal strip.

Move the handle in a downward motion and insert the wire.

![[17800149.png]]

Install the cable tray covers (1).

![[17800127.png]]
