### Fault Code: 777

### Turbocharger Air Inlet Temperature - Engine Protection

Printable Version

### Overview

| Codes | Reason | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Fault Code: 777 PID(P): SPN: FMI: Lamp: Yellow SRT: | The turbocharger inlet air temperature has exceeded the standard ambient air temperature limit. | The engine will go into a derate mode until the turbocharger inlet air temperature drops to a normal level. |
