### Overview

| Codes | Reason | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Fault Code: 384 PID(P): S237 SPN: 626 FMI: 11 Lamp: Yellow SRT: 00-365 | The ether injection solenoid circuit is open or shorted at pin 2 of the engine harness. | Ether injection feature is disabled. |

![[19400679.png]]

Ether Injection Solenoid Circuit

### Circuit Description

The ether injection solenoid is a device used by the ECM to control the ether injection system during cold starts.

### Component Location

The ether injection solenoid location is dependent on the OEM. Refer to the OEM manual.

Refer to Troubleshooting Fault Code t05-384
