### General Information

QSK-equipped engines use three separate wiring harnesses to control the engine and some of the vehicle operations.

1. Engine harness.
2. Original equipment manufacturer (OEM) interface harness.
3. OEM harness.

![[19400385.png]]

Replace a harness if there is an open or short circuit found under the protective covering of the harness body.

![[19400386.png]]

### Remove

QSK19 Engines

Disconnect the engine harness from the coolant pressure sensor.

Remove the engine harness clamps.

![[19801065.png]]

Cut the ties on the alternator bracket and front engine cover bracket.

![[19801066.png]]

![[19400388.png]]

![[19801067.png]]

Disconnect the intake manifold temperature sensor and intake manifold pressure sensor.

![[19400390.png]]

Disconnect fuel pump pressure sensor (1) and fuel pump actuator (2).

![[19400779.png]]

Cut the tie on the engine data link connector bracket.

Remove the bracket.

![[19801068.png]]

Disconnect the data link connector from the harness support bracket by removing the four screws.

![[19801069.png]]

Disconnect the 2- and 6-pin Deutsch™ connectors.

![[19801070.png]]

Disconnect the engine harness from the following:

1. Oil pressure sensor.
2. Timing actuator.
3. Rail actuator.
4. Ambient air pressure sensor.
5. Rail pressure sensor.
6. Timing pressure sensor.
7. Fuel temperature sensor.

![[19400392.png]]

Disconnect the fuel shutoff control wires to the fuel shutoff solenoid.

![[05400020.png]]

![[19400242.png]]

### Install

QSK19 Engines

Remove the engine harness from the clamps and bracket.

![[19801062.png]]

Connect the engine harness connector to the ECM.

Use an inch-pound torque wrench, Part Number 3376592, with 4 mm \[5/32 in\] hex head adapter to tighten the connector jackscrew.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 2.8 n•m [25 in-lb]

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Do **not** over-torque as connector damage can occur.

![[19400242.png]]

Fasten the harness to the harness support brackets using wire ties.

![[19801072.png]]

Connect the engine harness to the following:

1. Oil pressure sensor.
2. Timing actuator.
3. Rail actuator
4. Ambient air (barometric) pressure sensor.
5. Rail pressure sensor.
6. Timing pressure sensor.
7. Fuel temperature sensor.

![[19400392.png]]

Connect the fuel shutoff control wires and ground wires to the fuel shutoff solenoid.

Tighten the retaining nuts.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 3 n•m [27 in-lb]

![[05400020.png]]

Connect the engine block ground to the engine block.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 20 n•m [177 in-lb]

![[19400393.png]]

Connect the 2- pin and 6-pin Deutsch™ connectors.

![[19801073.png]]

Connect the fuel pump pressure sensor (1) and actuator (2).

![[19400779.png]]

Connect the data link connector to the harness support bracket.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 3 n•m [27 in-lb]

![[19400389.png]]

Install the data link connector support bracket.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 4 n•m [35 in-lb]

Fasten the harness for the intake manifold temperature sensor and intake manifold pressure sensor to the data link support bracket.

![[19801075.png]]

Connect the wiring harness to the intake manifold temperature sensor and intake manifold pressure sensor.

![[19400390.png]]

Connect the coolant temperature sensor and engine position sensor.

Fasten the harness to the fuel tube, aftercooler tube, and thermostat housing.

Fasten the harness to the front engine cover bracket and alternator bracket.

> 20 n•m [177 in-lb]

Connect the 9-pin data link connector to the engine harness support bracket.

- Slide the 9-pin connector into the engine harness support bracket connector slot.
- Secure the 9-pin connector with four screws.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 1 n•m [9 in-lb]

![[19400769.png]]

Connect the oil level sensor to the engine harness.

![[19400768.png]]

Connect the engine harness ECM connector to the ECM.

Use an inch-pound torque wrench, Part Number 3376592, with 4 mm \[5/32 in\] hex head adapter to tighten the connector jackscrew.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 2.8 n•m [25 in-lb]

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Do **not** over-torque as connector damage can occur.

![[19400242.png]]

Connect the engine harness to the following:

- Timing pressure sensor (1).
- Ambient air pressure sensor (2).
- Fuel temperature sensor (3).
- Rail pressure sensor (4).

![[19400767.png]]

Connect the fuel shutoff control wires and ground wires to the fuel shutoff solenoid.

Tighten the retaining nuts.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 3 n•m [27 in-lb]
