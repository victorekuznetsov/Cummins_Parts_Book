The number of plates within a given battery size determines the reserve capacity. Reserve capacity is the length of time in which sustained cranking can occur.

Cold cranking amperes are based on two 12-VDC batteries in series.

Listed below are battery cable sizes in American wire gauge (maximum length in cranking motor circuit).

#### 24 to 32 VDC

|---|---|

\*Two strands of number 0 cable can be used in place of one number 0000 cable providing all connections are carefully made so that equal current flow is in each parallel cable.
