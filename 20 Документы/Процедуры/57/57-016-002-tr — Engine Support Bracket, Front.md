# Engine Support Bracket, Front
**Передний кронштейн опоры двигателя**

> [!abstract] Процедура · `57-016-002-tr`
> **Двигатели:** [[37292556 — QST30 CM552 CPL 1244|37292556]], [[37295879 — QST30 CM552 CPL 2139|37295879]]
> **Семейство:** QST30
> **Входит в руководства:** [[4021539 — QST30 Service Manual|4021539]]
> **Секции:** Section 16 - Mounting Adaptations - Group 16
> **Даты:** изменён 2021-02-02
> **Источник:** [QuickServe](https://quickserve.cummins.com/qs3/pubsys2/xml/en/procedures/57/57-016-002-tr.html) · [PDF-оригинал](https://github.com/victorekuznetsov/Cummins_Parts_Book/raw/claude/cummins-parts-knowledge-base-qa0n50/bulletins/procedures/57-016-002-tr.pdf)

### Remove

Remove the engine support bracket.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The QST30 Series engine has a trunnion mount that slips over the gear cover nose and is lubricated with grease.

Remove the trunnion mount.

![[16a00013.png]]

### Clean and Inspect for Reuse

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> When using solvents, acids, or alkaline materials for cleaning, follow the manufacturer's recommendations for use. Wear goggles and protective clothing to reduce the possibility of personal injury.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Wear appropriate eye and face protection when using compressed air. Flying debris and dirt can cause personal injury.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> When using a steam cleaner, wear safety glasses or a face shield, as well as protective clothing. Hot steam can cause serious personal injury.

Use solvent or steam to clean the engine support bracket.

Dry with compressed air.

![[16a00037.png]]

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> This component or assembly weighs greater than 23 kg \[50 lb\]. To prevent serious personal injury, be sure to have assistance or use appropriate lifting equipment to lift this component or assembly.

Inspect the support for cracks or damage.

If the support is cracked, it **must** be replaced.

![[16a00012.png]]

### Install

Install the front engine support trunnion.

Use Lubriplate™, or equivalent, to lubricate the bore of the mount and trunnion sleeve on the front cover gear.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Verify that the grease fitting is installed and tightened.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 7 n•m [62 in-lb]

Install the trunnion mount.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The trunnion mount is installed with the flat (machined) side toward the engine (part number facing out).

![[01a00203.png]]

Use high performance, industrial-type grease (NLGI Grade Number 2) fortified with extreme pressure (EP) additives, such as BP Energrease™ FPG or Castrol Spheerol™ EPL 2 grease, or its equivalent.

If the above greases are **not** available locally, contact a Cummins® Distributor for advice on other Cummins® approved equivalents.

Lubricate the support until grease appears at the outside of the support.

![[01a00204.png]]

Measure the clearance between trunnion mount and front cover.

Use gap gauge or equivalent for checking trunnion clearance.

If the clearance is **not** within specifications, replace the trunnion or front cover.

| Trunnion Clearance |  |  |
|---|---|---|
| mm |  | in |
| 4.50 | MIN | 0.177 |
| 5.00 | MAX | 0.197 |
