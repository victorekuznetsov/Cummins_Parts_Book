### Measure

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Extreme care must be used when prying against the viscous damper. Sharp pry bars can damage the damper casing, resulting in a leak of the viscous fluid and failure of the damper.

Measure the end clearance by attaching an indicator to rest against the damper or pulley, while prying against the front cover and inner part of the pulley or damper.

End clearance **must** be present with the engine mounted in the unit and assembled to the transmission or converter.

Use a dial indicator to measure the crankshaft end clearance.

| Crankshaft End Clearance |  |  |
|---|---|---|
| mm |  | in |
| 0.140 | MIN | 0.0055 |
| 0.320 | MAX | 0.0126 |

Repair limit: 0.50 mm \[0.0197 in\].

If the clearance is **not** within specifications, contact a Cummins® Authorized Repair Location.
