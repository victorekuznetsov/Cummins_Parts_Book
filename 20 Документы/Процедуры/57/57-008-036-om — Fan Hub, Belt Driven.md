# Fan Hub, Belt Driven
**Ступица вентилятора с ремённым приводом**

> [!abstract] Процедура · `57-008-036-om`
> **Двигатели:** [[37292556 — QST30 CM552 CPL 1244|37292556]], [[37295879 — QST30 CM552 CPL 2139|37295879]]
> **Семейство:** QST30
> **Входит в руководства:** [[3666134 — QST30 Operation and Maintenance Manual|3666134]]
> **Секции:** Section 6 - Maintenance Procedures at 6000 Hours or 1 Year · Section 6 - Maintenance Procedures at 6000 Hours or 2 Years
> **Даты:** изменён 2025-11-12
> **Источник:** [QuickServe](https://quickserve.cummins.com/qs3/pubsys2/xml/en/procedures/57/57-008-036-om.html) · [PDF-оригинал](https://github.com/victorekuznetsov/Cummins_Parts_Book/raw/claude/cummins-parts-knowledge-base-qa0n50/bulletins/procedures/57-008-036-om.pdf)

### Remove

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> This component or assembly weighs greater than 23 kg \[50 lb\]. To prevent serious personal injury, be sure to have assistance or use appropriate lifting equipment to lift this component or assembly.

Remove the four capscrews, washers, and the fan hub assembly from the fan support.

Remove the fan hub assembly (1).

![[fa6puha.png]]

### Install

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> This component weighs greater than 23 kg \[50 lb\]. To prevent serious personal injury, be sure to have assistance or use appropriate lifting equipment to lift this component or assembly..

Install the fan hub assembly (1), and the four capscews and washers, to the fan support.

Tighten the fan hub capscrews.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 180 n•m [133 ft-lb]

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Go to the fan option for fan hub centerlines and mounting locations.
