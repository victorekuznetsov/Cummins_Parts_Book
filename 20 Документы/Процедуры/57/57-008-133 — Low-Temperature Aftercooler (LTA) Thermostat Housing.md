# Low-Temperature Aftercooler (LTA) Thermostat Housing
**Корпус термостата контура LTA**

> [!abstract] Процедура · `57-008-133`
> **Двигатели:** [[37292556 — QST30 CM552 CPL 1244|37292556]], [[37295879 — QST30 CM552 CPL 2139|37295879]]
> **Семейство:** QST30
> **Входит в руководства:** [[4021539 — QST30 Service Manual|4021539]]
> **Секции:** Section 8 - Cooling System - Group 08
> **Даты:** изменён 2022-10-13
> **Источник:** [QuickServe](https://quickserve.cummins.com/qs3/pubsys2/xml/en/procedures/57/57-008-133.html) · [PDF-оригинал](https://github.com/victorekuznetsov/Cummins_Parts_Book/raw/claude/cummins-parts-knowledge-base-qa0n50/bulletins/procedures/57-008-133.pdf)

### Select Service Tools

#### Recommended Cummins® Service Tools

- No recommended Cummins® service tools.

#### Additional Service Items

- Gasket scraper.

### Preparatory Steps

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Batteries can emit explosive gases. To reduce the possibility of personal injury, always ventilate the compartment before servicing the batteries. To reduce the possibility of arcing, remove the negative (-) battery cable first and attach the negative (-) battery cable last.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Do not remove the pressure cap from a hot engine. Wait until the coolant temperature is below 50°C \[ 120°F \] before removing the pressure cap. Heated coolant spray or steam can cause personal injury.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Coolant is toxic. Keep away from children and pets. If not reused, dispose of in accordance with local environmental regulations.

- Disconnect the batteries. See equipment manufacturer service information.
- Drain the cooling system. [[57-008-018 — Cooling System|Refer to Procedure 008-018 in Section 8]].
- Disconnect the radiator hoses from the low-temperature aftercooler (LTA) water outlet connection. See equipment manufacturer service information
- Disconnect the aftercooler lines from the LTA thermostat housing. Refer to Procedure 010-006 in Section 10.
- Remove the LTA water outlet connection and cover plate. [[57-008-110 — Low-Temperature Aftercooler (LTA) Water Outlet Connection|Refer to Procedure 008-110 in Section 8]].

### Remove

Remove the remaining two capscrews, LTA thermostat housing, and gasket.

Discard the gasket.

![[08n00198.png]]

Remove the LTA thermostat housing support capscrews from the front and rear brackets.

Remove the LTA thermostat housing support (1).

![[08n00199.png]]

### Disassemble

Remove the LTA thermostats and thermostat seals. [[57-008-131 — Low-Temperature Aftercooler (LTA) Thermostat|Refer to Procedure 008-131 in Section 8.]]

![[08n00200.png]]

Remove the draincock (1) from the thermostat housing.

Remove the hose coupling (2), male union (3), and o-rings from the LTA thermostat housing support.

Discard the o-rings.

![[08n00201.png]]

### Clean and Inspect for Reuse

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> When using solvents, acids, or alkaline materials for cleaning, follow the manufacturer's recommendations for use. Wear goggles and protective clothing to reduce the possibility of personal injury.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Wear appropriate eye and face protection when using compressed air. Flying debris and dirt can cause personal injury.

Remove any gasket material from the LTA thermostat housing and support. Use a gasket scraper.

Clean the components with solvent.

Dry with compressed air.

![[08n00202.png]]

Inspect the thermostat housing for the following:

- Cracks
- Thread damage
- Damage to sealing surfaces
- Pitting and corrosion.

Replace any damaged components.

![[08n00203.png]]

Check the thermostat for wear or damage.

If the barrel of the thermostat is worn or fretted, it **must** be replaced. Refer to Procedure 008-013 in Section 8.

![[ec800sc.png]]

### Assemble

Install the draincock (1) into the thermostat housing.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 47 n•m [35 ft-lb]

Install new o-rings on the hose coupling and male union.

Apply vegetable oil to the o-rings.

Install the hose coupling (2) and male union (3) into the thermostat housing support.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 68 n•m [50 ft-lb]

![[08n00204.png]]

Install the LTA thermostats seals. [[57-008-165 — Low Temperature Aftercooler (LTA) Thermostat Seal|Refer to Procedure 008-165 in Section 8.]]

Install the LTA thermostats. [[57-008-131 — Low-Temperature Aftercooler (LTA) Thermostat|Refer to Procedure 008-131 in Section 8.]]

![[08n00200.png]]

### Install

Install the LTA thermostat housing support (1) between the mounting brackets.

Install the capscrews.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 65 n•m [48 ft-lb]

![[08n00199.png]]

Install the LTA thermostat housing onto the thermostat housing support. Use a new gasket.

Install the two mounting capscrews on the right bank side of the LTA thermostat housing. Hand tighten **only**.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The LTA thermostat housing capscrews **must** be tightened with the LTA thermostat housing and LTA water outlet connection capscrews.

![[08n00198.png]]

Install the LTA water outlet connection and cover plate. [[57-008-110 — Low-Temperature Aftercooler (LTA) Water Outlet Connection|Refer to Procedure 008-110 in Section 8.]]

Tighten the 10 mounting capscrews to secure the LTA thermostat housing.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 65 n•m [48 ft-lb]

![[08n00194.png]]

### Finishing Steps

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Coolant is toxic. Keep away from children and pets. If not reused, dispose of in accordance with local environmental regulations.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Batteries can emit explosive gases. To reduce the possibility of personal injury, always ventilate the compartment before servicing the batteries. To reduce the possibility of arcing, remove the negative (-) battery cable first and attach the negative (-) battery cable last.

- Connect the aftercooler lines to the LTA thermostat housing. Refer to Procedure 010-006 in Section 10.
- Connect the radiator hose to the LTA water outlet connection. See equipment manufacturer service information.
- Fill the cooling system. [[57-008-018 — Cooling System|Refer to Procedure 008-018 in Section 8.]]
- Connect the batteries. See equipment manufacturer service information.
- Operate the engine to 85°C \[ 185°F \] coolant temperature. Check for leaks.
