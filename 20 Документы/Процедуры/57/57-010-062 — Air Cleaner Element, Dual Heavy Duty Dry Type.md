### Maintenance Service

Heavy duty air cleaners combine centrifugal cleaning with element filtering before air enters the engines.

Before disassembly, wipe dirt from the cover and the upper portion of the air cleaner.

Loosen the wing nut (3), remove the band clamp (1) securing the dust pan (2).

Loosen the wing nut (3). Remove the dust shield (4) from the dust pan (2). Clean the dust pan and shield.

Remove the wing nut (5) that secures the air cleaner primary element (6) in the air cleaner housing. Inspect the rubber sealing washer on the wing nut.

Remove the dirty primary cleaner element (6). If the inner safety element (8) is being replaced based on high intake restriction, remove the wing nut (7) and replace the inner safety element.

Install the inner safety element (8) and secure with the wing nut (7). Check the seals.

Install the dust plan (2) and band clamp (1). Operate the engine at rated speed and power and record the intake restriction.
