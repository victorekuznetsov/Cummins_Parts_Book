### Remove

Low Pressure Fuel Line(s)

For generator and generator drive engines with mechanical lift pumps, the following steps **must** be completed:

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Thoroughly clean the area around the fuel lines before removal. Sketch or photograph the fuel line arrangement before removal. Tag the lines to identify them for assembly purposes.

Remove the tank fuel supply line (1) and the return to tank line (2).

![[05a00043.png]]

For generator and generator drive engines with mechanical lift pumps, the following steps **must** be completed:

Remove the lift pump outlet line (1).

Remove the lift pump inlet line (2).

Remove the injection pump inlet line (3).

Remove the injection pump overflow line (4).

[[57-006-013 — Fuel Drain Lines|Refer to Procedure 006-013 in Section 6]] to inspect low pressure and fuel drain lines for reuse.

![[06a00119.png]]

For industrial engines with mechanical lift pumps and an EHAB (fuel shutoff valve) device, the following steps **must** be completed:

Remove the EHAB supply line (1).

Remove the EHAB outlet line (2).

Remove the EHAB injection pump inlet line (3).

Remove the EHAB injection pump overflow line (4).

![[06a00106.png]]

For industrial engines with mechanical lift pumps and an EHAB (fuel shutoff valve) device, the following steps **must** be completed:

Disconnect the low-pressure lines from the fuel manifold blocks.

Remove the mechanical lift pump supply line (1).

Remove the injection pump overflow line (2).

Remove the fuel supply to EHAB line (3).

Remove the lift pump outlet line (7).

Remove the fuel injection pump inlet line (8).

![[06a00056.png]]

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Thoroughly clean the area around the fuel lines before removal. Sketch or photograph the fuel line arrangement before removal. Tag the lines to identify them for assembly purposes.

For industrial engines with mechanical lift pumps, remove the low-pressure fuel lines from the mechanical fuel lift pump and the fuel filter head.

![[06a00047.png]]

For industrial engines and generator/generator drive engines with electric lift pumps, the following steps **must** be followed to remove the low-pressure fuel lines from the system.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Thoroughly clean the area around the fuel lines before removal. Sketch or photograph the fuel line arrangement before removal. Tag the lines to identify them for assembly purposes.

Remove the lift pump fuel supply line (1).

Remove the fuel line (2) from the filter to the fuel manifold.

Remove the injection pump inlet line (3).

Remove the injection pump overflow line (4).

![[06l00001.png]]

Remove the fuel crossover lines from the left bank fuel connection block, or manifold, and the right bank fuel connection block, or manifold.

[[57-006-021-tr — Fuel Manifold (Drain)|Refer to Procedure 006-021 in Section 6]] for removal and installation of fuel drain manifold on high pressure fuel pump and fuel drain lines from rocker lever housings.

![[06a00040.png]]

High Pressure Fuel Line(s)

Remove the aftercooler assembly. Refer to Procedure 010-002 in Section 10.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The aftercooler assembly **must** be removed if high- pressure injector supply lines are to be removed.

![[08a00063.png]]

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> The high pressure fuel supply lines are shaped to provide fuel to the injectors at the proper point in the cycle. Changes to the shape or dimensions of these lines will affect timing. Do not bend the high pressure fuel supply lines. Engine performance can be impaired or damage can occur.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Thoroughly clean the area around the fuel lines before removal.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Mark or photograph all fuel tubing, mounting pads, and brackets for identification purposes, to aid in reassembly.

Disconnect the high-pressure injector fuel supply lines (3).

Loosen the locknut (2) and remove the connector (1).

![[06a00033.png]]

### Clean and Inspect for Reuse

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The procedure that follows is for rubber or braided hoses.

Inspect the inside of the hose from the fuel tank to the fuel filter for any separation of the liner.

Inspect for any pinches or loops in the hose that could obstruct the flow.

If damage is found, the part **must** be replaced.

![[fp8hoca.png]]

For the braided fuel supply lines, inspect the hoses for frayed mesh, and pinched areas that prevent the proper fuel flow. The hose **must** be replaced if it is damaged.

![[06400047.png]]

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Wear appropriate eye and face protection when using compressed air. Flying debris and dirt can cause personal injury.

For rigid steel fuel supply lines, use compressed air to flush the lines and remove any loose dirt particles.

![[06400040.png]]

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> The high-pressure fuel supply lines are shaped to provide fuel to the injectors at the proper point in the cycle. Changes to the shape or dimensions of these lines will affect timing. Do not bend the high-pressure fuel supply lines. Engine performance can be impaired or damage can occur.

Inspect the fuel lines for cracks that can cause a loss of pressure.

Inspect the fuel lines for sharp bends that can cause a restriction in pressure.

The part **must** be replaced if damaged is found.

![[06a00111.png]]

### Install

High Pressure Fuel Line(s)

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> When installing flexible hoses, make sure that all the original clips are fitted and that the hoses do not contact any other hoses or engine components. Interference between hoses and other engine components can lead to leakages and engine damage.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Make sure there is a minimum of 15 mm (5/8 in) between flexible hoses and hard lines.

Install the left bank high-pressure fuel lines.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 23 n•m [204 in-lb]

![[06a00052.png]]

Install the right bank high-pressure fuel lines.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 23 n•m [204 in-lb]

![[06a00053.png]]

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> High-pressure fuel line clamps must be installed as shown. If the clamps are not installed, the lines can break causing a fuel leak.

Install the tube clamp brackets onto the right bank of the engine.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The tube clamp brackets are attached on the right bank between cylinders number 2 and number 3 and between cylinders number 4 and number 5, and by the number 4 cam follower cover.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 65 n•m [48 ft-lb]

Install the tube clamps to the tubes.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The right bank uses five clamps.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 9 n•m [80 in-lb]

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The tube clamp with the spacer **always** goes with the turbocharger oil drain tube.

![[06a00054.png]]

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> High-pressure fuel line clamps must be installed as shown. If the clamps are not installed, the lines can break, causing a fuel leak.

Install the tube clamp brackets onto the left bank of the engine.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 65 n•m [48 ft-lb]

Install the tube clamps to the tubes.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 9 n•m [80 in-lb]

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Left bank tube clamp brackets are attached between cylinders number 2 and number 3 and between cylinders number 3 and number 4.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The left bank uses seven clamps.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Some of the tube brackets are installed on top of the turbocharger oil drain tube flanges.

![[06a00055.png]]

Install the aftercooler assembly. Refer to Procedure 010-002 in Section 10.

Install the low-pressure fuel crossover tubes to the left bank fuel connection block and to the right bank fuel connection block.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> All fuel lines and tubes have two sealing washers per banjo capscrew.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 27 n•m [239 in-lb]

Install the tube clamps.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 9 n•m [80 in-lb]

![[06a00040.png]]

For industrial engines with mechanical lift pumps and EHAB devices, the following low pressure fuel lines **must** be installed.

- EHAB supply (3)
- Lift pump supply (1)
- Fuel filter supply (7)
- EHAB supply filtered (8)
- Overflow (2).

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 27 n•m [239 in-lb]

![[06a00056.png]]

For industrial and generator/generator drive engines with electric left pumps, the following low-pressure fuel lines **must** be installed:

- Lift pump supply (1)
- Fuel manifold supply, filtered (2)
- Fuel injection pump supply (3)
- Overflow (4).

Hand tighten all connections.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Use two sealing washers with each banjo connector around the fuel injection pump and the fuel manifold. Two different sizes of banjo connectors are used. Each requires a different torque value.

![[06l00001.png]]

For industrial and generator/generator drive engines with electric lift pumps, apply the final torque to each banjo and line as indicated.

Use a 22 mm socket to tighten the banjo connectors at location (1).

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 30 n•m [22 ft-lb]

Use a 19 mm socket to tighten the banjo connectors at location (2).

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 20 n•m [177 in-lb]

![[06l00002.png]]

- Install the lift pump supply line.
- Install the fuel filter supply line.
- Install the injection pump supply line.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value

### Vent

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> This section applies **only** to engines with mechanical lift pumps.

Controlled venting is provided at the injection pump through the fuel drain manifold. Small amounts of air introduced by changing the fuel filters or fuel injection pump supply line will be vented automatically if the fuel filter is changed in accordance with the instructions.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> QST30 engines with electric lift pumps can purge the system of air by energizing the electric lift pumps. Refer to Procedure 005-045 in Section 5. Refer to Procedure 006-003 in Section 6.

![[06a00019.png]]

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Manual venting for engines with mechanical lift pumps is required if any of the following is true.

- Fuel filter is **not** filled prior to installation.
- Fuel injection pump is replaced.
- High-pressure fuel line connections are loosened or fuel lines replaced.
- Initially starting the engine or starting up the engine after an extended period of time without engine operation.
- Vehicle fuel tank has been run until empty.

![[06a00019.png]]

Prior to venting the generator drive engine, connect a 24-VDC wire from the positive terminal of the starter to the fuel shutoff solenoid post.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Remove this wire before starting the engine.

![[05a00026.png]]

Prior to venting the industrial engine with mechanical lift pumps, place the keyswitch in the ON position, but do **not** crank the engine.

![[01a00099.png]]

Loosen the relief valve (1) on the fuel filter head.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> On industrial engines with mechanical lift pumps, loosen the plug in the outlet port on the front of the fuel filter head.

![[06a00002.png]]

Operate the plunger on the mechanical lift pump until the fuel flowing from the relief valve (1) (outlet fitting) is free of air.

Tighten the relief valve (1) (outlet fitting) on the fuel filters.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 11 n•m [97 in-lb]

![[06a00003.png]]

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> For generator/generator drive engines, the overflow valve is mounted on the face of the P8500 injection fuel pump. For industrial engines, the overflow line is on the EHAB. [[57-005-066 — Fuel Pump Overflow Valve|Refer to Procedure 005-066 in Section 5.]]

Loosen the overflow/pressure regulator fitting.

Operate the plunger on the mechanical fuel lift pump until the fuel flowing from the overflow/pressure regulator valve is free of air.

Tighten the overflow/pressure regulator valve. [[57-005-066 — Fuel Pump Overflow Valve|Refer to Procedure 005-066 in Section 5.]]
