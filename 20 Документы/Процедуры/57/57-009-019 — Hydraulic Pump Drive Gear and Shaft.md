# Hydraulic Pump Drive Gear and Shaft
**Шестерня и вал привода гидронасоса**

> [!abstract] Процедура · `57-009-019`
> **Двигатели:** [[37292556 — QST30 CM552 CPL 1244|37292556]], [[37295879 — QST30 CM552 CPL 2139|37295879]]
> **Семейство:** QST30
> **Входит в руководства:** [[4021539 — QST30 Service Manual|4021539]]
> **Секции:** Section 9 - Drive Units - Group 09
> **Даты:** изменён 2015-08-07
> **Источник:** [QuickServe](https://quickserve.cummins.com/qs3/pubsys2/xml/en/procedures/57/57-009-019.html) · [PDF-оригинал](https://github.com/victorekuznetsov/Cummins_Parts_Book/raw/claude/cummins-parts-knowledge-base-qa0n50/bulletins/procedures/57-009-019.pdf)

### Preparatory Steps

- Remove the hydraulic pump drive. [[57-009-016-tr — Hydraulic Pump Drive|Refer to Procedure 009-016 in Section 9.]]

![[ck800wa.png]]

### Disassemble

Remove the drive cover, if applicable.

![[09f00036.png]]

Use a two-jaw puller to carefully remove gear from shaft.

![[09a00088.png]]

Remove shaft (7), two thrust bearings (2, 6), and two bushing (3, 4), from the housing (4).

![[09a00101.png]]

### Clean and Inspect for Reuse

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> When using solvents, acids, or alkaline materials for cleaning, follow the manufacturer's recommendations for use. Wear goggles and protective clothing to reduce the possibility of personal injury.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Wear appropriate eye and face protection when using compressed air. Flying debris and dirt can cause personal injury.

Clean the shaft and gear with solvent.

Dry with compressed air.

![[09600107.png]]

Check the gear teeth and gear bore for damage.

Replace the gear if damaged.

![[09f00040.png]]

Measure the inside diameter of the gear if it is to be reused.

| Hydraulic Pump Drive Gear Inside Diameter |  |  |
|---|---|---|
| mm |  | in |
| 41.000 | MIN | 1.614 |
| 41.025 | MAX | 1.615 |

![[hd8geta.png]]

Inspect the shaft in the gear fit area for fretting, burrs, or other damage.

Clean burrs with a piece of emery cloth.

![[09600059.png]]

Measure the shaft diameters.

| Hydraulic Pump Drive Shaft Diameters |  |  |  |
|---|---|---|---|
|  | mm |  | in |
| A | 47.931 | MIN | 1.887 |
|  | 47.950 | MAX | 1.888 |
| B | 41.070 | MIN | 1.617 |
|  | 41.095 | MAX | 1.618 |

If the diameters are **not** within specification, the shaft **must** be replaced.

![[09f00043.png]]

### Assemble

Coat two bushings with engine oil and insert them into housing at a recessed length of 0.991 mm \[0.039 in\] from each end.

![[09a00074.png]]

Coat both thrust bearings in engine oil and place one over shaft. Place shaft through housing. Place second thrust bearing over shaft.

![[09a00061.png]]

Press the gear onto the shaft. A spacer may be needed to support the bottom of the shaft.

![[09f00046.png]]

Replace the drive cover, if applicable.

![[09f00036.png]]

### Finishing Steps

- Install the hydraulic pump drive. [[57-009-016-tr — Hydraulic Pump Drive|Refer to Procedure 009-016 in Section 9.]]
- Operate the engine and check for leaks and proper operation.
