### Clean and Inspect for Reuse

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Wear appropriate eye and face protection when using compressed air. Flying debris and dirt can cause personal injury.

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Use a cleaning solution that won't hurt aluminum.

Use a brush and solvent around the areas where connections where the fuel pump were removed to make sure no dirt remains on the fuel injection pump.

Dry with compressed air.

![[05l00001.png]]

Inspect the fuel injection pump for the following damage:

- Mounting surfaces and capscrew holes for cracks and raised metal
- Damage to threads
- Damaged cable or harness connectors.

![[05a00137.png]]

Inspect the fuel pump shaft and threads for damage.

![[05a00079.png]]

Inspect the spline adapter for damage or missing splines.

![[05a00087.png]]

Inspect the nut threads and flats for damage.

![[05a00080.png]]

### Install

Remove the fuel injection pump timing pin cap (2). Verify the position of the timing pin. The fuel pump **must** be locked. Reverse the position of the timing pin (1) to lock the pump as shown.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 30 n•m [22 ft-lb]

![[05a00144.png]]

Seal Replacement

1. O-ring seal (flange drive housing)
2. O-ring seal (flange drive oil supply)
3. Lift pump gasket
4. O-ring seals - EHAB to injection pump
5. Sealing washer (banjo connectors).

![[05a00090.png]]

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Failure to prelubricate the fuel injection pump can cause pump damage.

Prelubricate the fuel pump oil sump by adding clean engine oil to the fuel pump oil drain opening until oil comes out of the drain.

![[05a00084.png]]

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Check to make sure the injection pump timing lock pin is removed before continuing. Severe damage to the fuel injection pump will occur unless the fuel injection pump is unlocked.

Prior to installation, the tapered fuel pump shaft and spline adaptor should be cleaned with non-petroleum based cleaner, (3824510) or equivalent, and wiped with a lint free rag. Contact with tapered shaft and inside of the spline adapter with bare hands should be avoided as it contaminates the mating surfaces.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> For an Open-Drive (Base-Mount) fuel pump, a Woodruff key is not required for installation of the spline adapter.

Slide the adapter onto the fuel pump shaft.
