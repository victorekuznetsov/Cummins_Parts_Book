# Engine Dataplate

### Remove

Remove the rivets that secure the dataplate to the gear housing.

![[gh900sb.png]]

### Install

Drive the rivets in until they contact the dataplate.

![[gh9plhb.png]]

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> If the rivets are driven in too far, they will cut through the dataplates.

![[gh9plva.png]]

If the dataplate is loose or has been damaged, drill new holes, and attach with new rivets. Mark the drill bit at 6.0 mm \[0.236 in\] to avoid drilling too deeply into the gear housing.

![[gh9tona.png]]

Drill the dataplate, taking care **not** to destroy any data printed on the dataplate.

![[gh9plxa.png]]

Drive the rivets in until they contact the dataplate.

![[gh9plhb.png]]
