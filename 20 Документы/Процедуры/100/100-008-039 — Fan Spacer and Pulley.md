### Preparatory Steps

- Remove the drive belt.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Loosen the capscrews before removing the belt, and tighten the capscrews after the belt is installed.

![[17900012.png]]

### Remove

Remove the four capscrews, fan, and spacer.

![[08900011.png]]

### Inspect for Reuse

Inspect the fan pulley for cracks near bolt holes.

Check for cracks, loose rivets, and bent or loose blades.

![[08d00079.png]]

### Install

Lift the tensioner, and install the belt.

Service Tip: If difficulty is experienced installing the drive belt (the belt seems too short), position the belt over the grooved pulleys first; then, while holding the tensioner up, slide the belt over the water pump pulley.

![[17900012.png]]

Install the four capscrews, fan, and spacer.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 24 n•m [212 in-lb]

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 43 n•m [32 ft-lb]
