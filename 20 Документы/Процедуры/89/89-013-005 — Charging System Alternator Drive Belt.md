### Remove

Engines with 75A Alternator:

Loosen the adjusting link and the alternator mounting capscrews.

Loosen the adjusting nut to relieve tension on the alternator belt.

Remove the belt.

![[13400062.png]]

### Inspect for Reuse

Poly-Vee Belt

Inspect the belts daily. Check the belt for intersecting cracks. Transverse (across the belt width) cracks are acceptable. Longitudinal (direction of belt length) cracks that intersect with transverse cracks are **not** acceptable. Replace the belt if it is frayed or has pieces of material missing. Refer to Section A for belt adjustment and replacement procedures.

Belt damage can be caused by:

- Incorrect tension
- Incorrect size or length
- Pulley misalignment
- Incorrect installation
- Severe operating environment
- Oil or grease on the side of belts.

![[fa9blsa.png]]

Cogged Belt

Inspect the belts daily. Replace the belts if they are cracked, frayed, or have chunks of material missing. Small cracks are acceptable.

Adjust the belts that have a glazed or shiny surface, which indicates belt slippage. Correctly installed and tensioned belts will show even pulley and belt wear. Refer to Section A for belt adjustment and replacement procedures.

Belt damage can be caused by:

- Incorrect tension
- Incorrect size or length
- Pulley misalignment
- Incorrect installation
- Severe operating environment
- Oil or grease on the belts

![[fa8blsb.png]]

Measure the belt tension in the center span of the pulleys.

An alternate method, deflection method, can be used to check belt tension by applying 98 N \[22 lbf\] force between the pulleys on V-belts. If the deflection is more than one belt thickness per foot of pulley center distance, the belt tension **must** be adjusted.

Refer to Section A for adjustment procedures.

![[fa8blda.png]]

For cogged belts, make sure that the belt tension gauge is positioned so that the center tensioning leg is placed directly over the high point (hump) of a cog. Other positioning will result in incorrect measurement.

![[fa1csvb.png]]

### Install

Do **not** attempt to pry the belt onto the pulley. Turn the adjusting nut **counterclockwise** to shorten the link, if necessary. Install the alternator belt.

![[13400062.png]]

Tighten the jam nuts on the adjusting link.

Tighten the adjusting link and alternator mounting capscrews.

Jam nuts:

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 196 n•m [125 ft-lb]

Alternator mounting capscrew (top):

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 75 n•m [55 ft-lb]

Alternator mounting capscrew (bottom):

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 120 n•m [89 ft-lb]

Alternator adjusting link mounting capscrew:

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 75 n•m [55 ft-lb]

![[13400065.png]]

### Adjust

Loosen the alternator and adjusting link mounting capscrews.

Loosen the jam nut on the adjusting link.

![[13400065.png]]

Turn the adjusting screw **clockwise** to tighten the belt tension.

![[13400064.png]]

Tighten the jam nuts on the adjusting screw.

Tighten the adjusting link and alternator mounting capscrews.

Jam nuts:

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 196 n•m [125 ft-lb]

Alternator mounting capscrew (top):

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 75 n•m [55 ft-lb]

Alternator mounting capscrew (bottom):

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 120 n•m [89 ft-lb]

Alternator adjusting link mounting capscrew:

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 75 n•m [55 ft-lb]

![[13400065.png]]

Check the belt tension again to be sure it is correct.

The belt tension **must** be:

| Measurements |  |  |
|---|---|---|
|  | n | lbf |
| Poly-Vee Belt | 670 | 150 |

#### Cogged Belt

- Use belt deflection method to set belt tension.

![[13400064.png]]

Engines with 140A Alternator:

Fit the v-ribbed belt on the alternator and accessory drive pulley.

Install a torque wrench on the belt tensioner at the location shown below. Move it away from the alternator.

Mount the v-ribbed belt on the alternator pulley and accessory drive pulley.

Ensure the belt is seated correctly on the grooves of the alternator pulley and accessory drive pulley.

Remove the torque wrench from the belt tensioner to bring the belt tensioner back to its original position.
