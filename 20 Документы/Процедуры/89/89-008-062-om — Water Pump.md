### Inspect

Inspect the water pump for leakage. Inspect the water pump and bypass tube for coolant or oil leakage. Check the water pump weep hole for excessive leakage. A minor chemical buildup or streaking at the water pump weep hole is normal. Do **not** repair or replace the water pump unless an actual leak is confirmed. If a leak is confirmed, contact a Cummins® Authorized Repair Facility.
