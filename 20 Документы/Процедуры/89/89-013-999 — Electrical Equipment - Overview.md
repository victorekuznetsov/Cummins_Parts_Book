### General Information

The basic heavy-duty electrical system consists of:

- Batteries (1)(Usually two or four connected in parallel)
- Starting motor (2)
- Alternator (3)
- Magnetic switch (4)
- Ignition switch (5)
- Necessary wiring (**not** shown)

All components **must** be carefully matched.
