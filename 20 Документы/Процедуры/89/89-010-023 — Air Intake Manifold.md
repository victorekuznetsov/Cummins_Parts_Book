### Preparatory Steps

- Remove the fuel drain lines. [[89-006-013 — Fuel Drain Lines|Refer to Procedure 006-013]] in Section 6.
- Remove the fuel supply lines. [[89-006-024 — Fuel Supply Lines|Refer to Procedure 006-024]] in Section 6.
- Remove the fuel filter assembly. [[89-006-015 — Fuel Filter (Spin-On Type)|Refer to Procedure 006-015]] in Section 6.
- Drain the cooling system. Refer to Procedure 008-018 in Section 8.
- Remove the coolant filter assembly. Refer to Procedure 008-060 in Section 8.
- Remove the intake piping. See equipment manufacturer service information.

![[ck800wa.png]]

### Remove

Generator-Drive

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> This component or assembly weighs greater than 23 kg \[50 lb\]. To prevent serious personal injury, be sure to have assistance or use appropriate lifting equipment to lift this component or assembly.

Remove the 42 capscrews and the intake manifold.

![[10400169.png]]

Remove the three fuel drilling plugs from the front and rear of the intake manifold.

1. Fuel rail supply drilling (1/8 inch pipe thread)
2. Fuel timing supply drilling (1/4 inch pipe thread)
3. Fuel return drilling (3/8 inch pipe thread)

![[10400174.png]]

Industrial Applications

Remove the air crossover connections.

![[10h00001.png]]

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> This component or assembly weighs greater than 23 kg \[50 lb\]. To prevent serious personal injury, be sure to have assistance or use appropriate lifting equipment to lift this component or assembly.

Remove the 21 capscrews from each intake manifold and remove each manifold.

![[10400187.png]]

Remove the three fuel drilling plugs from the rear of the intake manifolds.

1. Fuel rail supply drilling (1/8 inch pipe thread)
2. Fuel timing supply drilling (1/4 inch pipe thread)
3. Fuel return drilling (3/8 inch pipe thread)

![[10400174.png]]

### Inspect for Reuse

Inspect the fuel manifold for cracks in the fuel passages.

The manifold **must** be replaced if damaged.

![[10400170.png]]

### Finishing Steps

- Install the intake piping. See equipment manufacturer service information.
- Install the coolant filter and lines. Refer to Procedure 008-060 in Section 8.
- Fill the cooling system. Refer to Procedure 008-018 in Section 8.
- Install the fuel filter assembly. [[89-006-015 — Fuel Filter (Spin-On Type)|Refer to Procedure 006-015]] in Section 6.
- Install the fuel supply lines. [[89-006-024 — Fuel Supply Lines|Refer to Procedure 006-024]] in Section 6.
- Install the fuel drain lines. [[89-006-013 — Fuel Drain Lines|Refer to Procedure 006-013]] in Section 6.
