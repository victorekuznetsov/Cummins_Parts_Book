### General Information

The cooling system **must** be designed to allow the air to escape while filling the radiator.

![[ra800vb.png]]

An engine vent line is routed from the cylinder heads to the radiator top tank expansion space above the water level.

![[08400349.png]]

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Do not route the fill or vent line in a manner that will allow air to be trapped in the system or damage to the cooling system can occur.

Route the vent line away from the makeup or fill line.

The vent line **must** have a continuous rise to prevent air lock and inadequate venting.
