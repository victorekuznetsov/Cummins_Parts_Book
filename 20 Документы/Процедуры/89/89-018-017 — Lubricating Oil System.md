### Specifications

#### Oil Pressure (with 15W-40 oil at 107°C \[225°F\]):

| At Idle (minimum allowable) | 70 kPa \[10 psi\] |
|---|---|

| At Rated Speed (minimum allowable) | 210 kPa \[30 psi\] |
|---|---|

#### Oil Temperature:

| Maximum | 120°C \[250°F\] |
|---|---|

#### Oil Filter Capacity:

| Combination Filter (two LF9325 Fleetguard®) | 5.3 liters \[1.4 gal\] |
|---|---|
