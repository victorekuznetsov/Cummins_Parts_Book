# Block Stiffener Plate
**Пластина жёсткости блока**

> **Даты:** изменён 2003-07-28

### Preparatory Steps

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> To reduce the possibility of personal injury, avoid direct contact of hot oil with your skin.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Some state and federal agencies have determined that used engine oil can be carcinogenic and cause reproductive toxicity. Avoid inhalation of vapors, ingestion, and prolonged contact with used engine oil. If not reused, dispose of in accordance with local environmental regulations.

- Drain the lubricating oil and remove the oil pan. Refer to Procedure [[89-007-025 — Lubricating Oil Pan|007-025]].

![[ck800wa.png]]

### Remove

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> This component weighs 23 kg \[50 lb\] or more. To reduce the possibility of personal injury, use a hoist or get assistance to lift this component.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> The block stiffener plate is attached by the suction tube capscrews. Removing these screws prior to securing the stiffener plate can cause the plate to drop and cause personal injury.

Install the four oil pan capscrews in the stiffener plate at the location in the illustration.

Remove the oil suction tube brackets and oil suction tube. Refer to Procedure 007-035 for clean and inspect of the lubricating oil suction tube.

![[01400592.png]]

Remove the stiffener plate capscrews and remove the stiffener plate.

![[01400520.png]]

### Clean

Clean the gasket sealant from the block stiffener plate, oil pan, and block.

![[01400521.png]]

### Install

Place a bead of gasket sealer 2 to 3 mm \[0.08 to 0.12 in\] on the stiffener plate.

![[01400522.png]]

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> This component weighs 23 kg \[50 lb\] or more. To reduce the possibility of personal injury, use a hoist or get assistance to lift this component.

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> The lubricating oil pan must be installed within 30 minutes of gasket sealant application. Improper sealing and engine damage can result.

Place the stiffener plate on the block and install the four capscrews.

![[01400593.png]]

Place an o-ring on the suction tube.

Install the suction tube.

Tighten the capscrews.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 66 n•m [48 ft-lb]

Remove the temporary mounting capscrews.

![[01400520.png]]

Place a bead of gasket sealer 2 to 3 mm \[0.08 to 0.12 in\] wide on the oil pan.

![[01400523.png]]

Install the oil pan. Refer to Procedure [[89-007-025 — Lubricating Oil Pan|007-025]] for capscrew torque sequence.

![[01400524.png]]

### Finishing Steps

- Fill the oil pan with clean 15W-40 lubricating oil. Refer to Procedure [[89-007-037 — Lubricating Oil System|007-037]].
- Operate the engine until the engine is at 70°C \[160°F\] coolant temperature and check for leaks.

![[ck800wa.png]]
