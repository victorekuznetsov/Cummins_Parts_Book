### General Information

Rocker Lever Covers

The QSK23 rocker lever covers are made of cast aluminum and utilize re-usable o-ring type valve cover gaskets.

![[07400246.png]]

Injector Levers

The QSK23 injector levers are made from forged steel.

Rocker Levers

Each QSK23 rocker lever is a single piece, solid design with a precision-ground nose radius.

Rocker Lever Shaft

The QSK23 uses a large 45 mm \[1.77 in\] rocker lever shaft diameter due to high injector train loads.

![[00600094.png]]

The valves and injectors are adjusted using the outer base circle (OBC) feeler gauge, or screwdriver method.

![[03400112.png]]
