### Initial Check

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The illustrations shown will be of the SS model single-cylinder air compressor. Differences in procedures for other models of Cummins® air compressors will be shown where necessary.

Shut the engine OFF.

Open the draincock on the wet tank to release compressed air from the system.

![[ca800wa.png]]

Remove the air inlet and outlet connections from the air compressor.

![[12200003.png]]

Measure the total carbon deposit thickness inside the air discharge line as shown.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The carbon deposit thickness **must not** exceed 1.6 mm \[0.06 in\].

![[12a00002.png]]

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> The air discharge line must be capable of withstanding extreme heat and pressure to reduce the possibility of personal injury and property damage. Refer to the manufacturer's specifications.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> If the total carbon deposit thickness exceeds specification, remove and clean, or replace the air discharge line. See equipment manufacturer service information.

![[12200004.png]]

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> The air discharge line must be capable of withstanding extreme heat and pressure to reduce the possibility of personal injury and property damage. Refer to the manufacturer's specifications.

Continue to check for carbon buildup in the air discharge line connections up to the first connection or wet tank.

Clean or replace any lines and fittings with carbon deposits greater than 1.6 mm \[0.06 in\]. See equipment manufacturer service information for cleaning or replacement instructions.

![[cp8tbtb.png]]

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> When using solvents, acids, or alkaline materials for cleaning, follow the manufacturer's recommendations for use. Wear goggles and protective clothing to reduce the possibility of personal injury.

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Do not use a sharp object to remove carbon. The sealing surfaces can be damaged.

Remove the air compressor head and valve assembly. [[89-012-013 — Air Compressor Unloader and Valve Assembly|Refer to Procedure 012-013 in Section 12.]]

Clean the compressor head and valve assembly components with solvent and a nonmetallic brush to remove carbon.

Inspect the valve assembly components for reuse.

![[cp8hdea.png]]

Assemble the air compressor using new gaskets and o-rings. [[89-012-013 — Air Compressor Unloader and Valve Assembly|Refer to Procedure 012-013 in Section 12.]]

Install and tighten the air inlet and outlet connections.

![[cp8hdaa.png]]

Close the wet tank drain cock.

Start and operate the engine and check for air leaks.

![[ca200da.png]]
