### Maintenance Check

To rotate the engine crankshaft **clockwise**, push in on the engine barring device and rotate **counterclockwise**.

Rotate the crankshaft through two complete revolutions.

If the engine does **not** turn freely, the equipment can have a malfunction. Refer to the manufacturer's instructions. The engine can have internal problems.

![[03400111.png]]

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Extreme care must be used in prying against the viscous damper. Sharp pry bars can damage the damper casing, resulting in a leak of the viscous fluid and ultimate failure of the damper.

Measure the crankshaft end clearance with a dial indicator.

| Crankshaft End Clearance |  |  |
|---|---|---|
| New Minimum | New Maximum | Worn Limit |
| 0.14 mm | 0.32 mm | 0.60 mm |
| \[0.006 in\] | \[0.013 in\] | \[0.024 in\] |

The check can be made by attaching a dial indicator resting against the damper or pulley while prying against the front cover and inner part of the pulley or damper. End clearance **must** be in specification with the engine mounted in the unit and assembled to the transmission or converter.

If the clearance is **not** within specifications, contact a local Cummins® Authorized Repair Location.
