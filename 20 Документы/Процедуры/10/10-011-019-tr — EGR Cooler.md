### Test

Automotive with CM870

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> The exhaust and exhaust components can remain hot after the engine has been shut down or secured. To avoid the risk of fire, property damage, burns or other serious personal injury, allow the exhaust system to cool before beginning this procedure or repair and make sure that no combustible materials are located where they might come in contact with hot exhaust or exhaust components.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Test is performed with exhaust gas recirculation (EGR) cooler installed on the engine with Fleetguard® Coolant Test Strip CC2602B.

#### Coolant Test Strip - EGR Cooler Exhaust Outlet

- Test Fleetguard® 3-Way Coolant Test strip, following instructions on the package, with distilled water to create a baseline for analysis.
- Remove rear EGR connection tube. [[10-011-025-tr — EGR Connection Tubes|Refer to Procedure 011-025 in Section 11.]]

![[11c00576.png]]

- Remove a small sample of soot from the EGR cooler exhaust outlet.
- Place soot sample into a small clean container.
- Mix soot sample with 25 ml (0.8 oz) of distilled water.

![[11a00098.png]]

- Test soot and water solution with a Fleetguard® 3-Way Coolant Test strip by following the instructions on the package.
- EGR cooler is reusable if all the pads on the test strip keep their baseline color.
- Install exhaust outlet tube and return to published troubleshooting.
- A change in any of the three test pads indicates coolant is in the exhaust outlet side of the cooler. Proceed to inlet side test.
- **Note:** It may be necessary to test raw coolant from the cooling system to make sure the Fleetguard® 3-Way Coolant Test strips will react if the type of coolant is unknown.

![[08800006.png]]

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Test is performed with the EGR cooler installed on the engine with Fleetgard® Coolant Test Strip CC2602B.

#### Coolant Test Strip - EGR Cooler Exhaust Inlet

- Test Fleetguard® 3-Way Coolant Test Strip, following instructions on the pakage, with distilled water to create a baseline for analysis.
- Remove front EGR cooler connection. Refer to Procedure 011-024 in Section 10.

![[11c00431.png]]

- Remove a small sample of soot from the EGR cooler exhaust inlet.
- Place soot sample into a small clean container.
- Mix soot sample with 25 ml (0.8 oz) of distilled water.

![[11a00099.png]]

- Test soot and water solution with a Fleetguard® 3-Way Coolant Test strip by following the instructions on the package.
- A change in any of the three test pads indicates coolant is in the exhaust inlet side of the cooler. Proceed to Preparatory Steps.
- EGR cooler is failed if coolant is in the outlet side and **not** in the inlet side of the EGR cooler.

![[08800006.png]]

|---|---|---|
| Coolant in Outlet | Coolant in Inlet | Outcome |
| Yes | Yes | Pressure test EGR cooler. |
| No | N/A | EGR cooler is **not** failed. Return to published troubleshooting. |
| Yes | No | EGR cooler is failed. Replace cooler. |
| Yes | Yes | Pressure test EGR cooler. |

- Inspect turbocharger turbine housing and rear EGR connection tube for any signs of coolant deposits if the EGR cooler is **not** reusable.

- If coolant deposits are found in the turbocharger turbine housing, inspect the turbocharger, EGR valve, EGR mass measurement flow assembly, exhaust transfer connection elbow, EGR crossover tube, and the crankcase pressure sensor for progressive damage.
- Clean the aftertreatment fuel injector, regardless if coolant deposits are present or **not**. [[101-011-042-tr — Aftertreatment Fuel Injector|Refer to Procedure 011-042 in Section 11.]]

### Preparatory Steps

Automotive with CM870

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Do not remove the pressure cap from a hot engine. Wait until the coolant temperature is below 50°C \[120°F\] before removing the pressure cap. Heated coolant spray or steam can cause personal injury.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Coolant is toxic. Keep away from children and pets. If not reused, dispose of in accordance with local environmental regulations.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> If the EGR cooler is being replaced due to an internal coolant leak, the EGR differential pressure tubes **must** be replaced. Refer to Procedure 011-026 in Section 11.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Brush away all loose dirt from around the area of the air-handling connections to reduce the possibility of contamination of the interior of the engine.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Use protective caps from the ISX Clean Care Kit, Part Number 4919403, and the original equipment manufacturer (OEM) Clean Care Kit, Part Number 4919425, to cover open connection points.

> If the EGR cooler is being replaced due to a malfunction that caused an internal coolant leak and Fault Code 555 and/or 556 is present, the crankcase breather system must be inspected. Maintenance-free crankcase breather inserts must be cleaned and inspected for reuse. Crankcase breather elements with filtration media must be replaced. [[10-003-019 — Crankcase Breather Element|Refer to Procedure 003-019 in Section 3.]]

- Remove the turbocharger. [[10-010-033-tr — Turbocharger|Refer to Procedure 010-033 in Section 10.]]
- Remove the exhaust gas pressure sensor tube. Refer to Procedure 011-027 in Section 11.

Remove EGR hose.

![[11c00019.png]]

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> The straps on the EGR cooler are not serviceable. Adjusting or removing the straps can damage the EGR cooler.

Remove the four nuts that attach the EGR cooler to the lubricating oil cooler assembly.

Remove EGR cooler assembly from the engine.

Remove coolant supply and vent jumper tubes if they remain in the block after the cooler is removed.

### Pressure Test

Automotive with CM870

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> This test is performed with the EGR cooler removed from the engine.

Clean EGR cooler using the steps found in the Clean and Inspect for Reuse section of this procedure, if **not** already completed.

Plug large coolant supply port of the EGR cooler with cap, Part Number 3164738, and collar, Part Number 3164986, from the EGR Cooler Leak Test Kit, Part Number 3164225.

Secure hose clamp, Part Number 3043487.

![[11o00002.png]]

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Plain coupling, Part Number 3683068, and o-ring, Part Number [[3049221]], from EGR cooler, are used in this step.

Insert plain coupling, Part Number 3683068, in the EGR cooler coolant return port.

Install cap, Part Number 3164737, and collar, Part Number 3164736, from the EGR Cooler Leak Test Kit, Part Number 3164225, over the plain coupling, Part Number 3683068.

Secure with hose clamp, Part Number [[3056787]].

![[22c00175.png]]

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Vent tube, Part Number 3682540, and the o-ring, Part Number 3682076, from the EGR cooler, are used in this step.

Insert vent tube, Part Number 3682540, from the EGR Cooler Leak Test Kit, Part Number 3164225, in the small EGR cooler coolant supply port.

Install the cap, Part Number 3164739, and collar, Part Number 3164985, from the EGR Cooler Leak Test Kit, Part Number 3164225, over the vent tube, Part Number 3682540.

Secure with hose clamp, Part Number 209773.

![[11c00815.png]]

Install threaded plug, Part Number [[3678923]], from the EGR Cooler Leak Test Kit, Part Number 3164225, in the EGR cooler coolant vent port.

![[11o00003.png]]

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Wear appropriate eye and face protection when using compressed air. Flying debris and dirt can cause personal injury.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The EGR cooler **must** be properly cleaned of soot accumulation and other debris before submerging it in the test tank.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Air Pressure Regulator Kit, Part Number 3164231, or equivalent, is suitable for use during this step.

Install air pressure regulator, Part Number 3164231, or equivalent, onto the plug, Part Number 3164737, in the EGR cooler coolant return port.

Make sure the air pressure regulator is closed and connect the compressed air to the pressure regulator.

Completely submerge the EGR cooler in room temperature water. Make sure to submerge the cooler vertically, with the outlet end down, so air can **not** be left in the open (gas) side of the cooler.

Apply 206 kPa \[30psi\] of air pressure to the cooler.

![[11o00004.png]]

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Wear appropriate eye and face protection when using compressed air. Flying debris and dirt can cause personal injury.

Inspect for bubbles escaping from uncapped gas ports of the EGR cooler or from the exterior shell of the cooler.

Verify that bubbles are **not** a result of loose fittings or trapped air.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Bubbles coming from a damaged EGR cooler can be very small and/or **not** in a steady stream.

If bubbles are **not** observed, the EGR cooler is usable.

Make sure the pressure regulator is closed.

Disconnect the air pressure from the EGR cooler assembly.

Carefully vent the trapped air from the EGR cooler assembly.

Remove the parts supplied in the EGR Cooler Leak Test Kit, Part Number 3164225.

![[11200136.png]]

If EGR cooler is **not** usable, inspect the turbocharger turbine housing and rear EGR connection tube for any signs of coolant deposits. Coolant deposits can be identified as dried white deposits coating the inside of the turbine housing or EGR connection tube.

If coolant deposits are found in the turbocharger turbine housing, inspect the turbocharger for progressive damage. [[10-010-033-tr — Turbocharger|Refer to Procedure 010-033 in Section 10.]]

If coolant deposits are found in the EGR connection tube, inspect the EGR connection tube(s) and EGR mixer for reuse. [[10-011-025-tr — EGR Connection Tubes|Refer to Procedure 011-025 in Section 11.]]

If the EGR cooler is being replaced due to damage that caused an internal coolant leak:

- Replace EGR differential pressure sensor tubes. [[10-011-026-tr — EGR Differential Pressure Sensor Tubes|Refer to Procedure 011-026 in Section 11.]]
- Check for EGR differential pressure sensor fault codes. If fault codes are found, follow the appropriate troubleshooting tree, and replace the EGR differential pressure sensor, if required. Use the following procedure in the Signature and ISX CM870 Electronic Control System Troubleshooting and Repair Manual, Bulletin 4021334. Refer to Procedure 019-370 in Section 19.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> If damage resulted in a hydraulic lock and the coolant level reached the intake manifold, it may be necessary to clean the charge air cooler. See equipment manufacturer service information.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> If damage resulted in a hydraulic lock and the coolant level reached the intake manifold, inspect the air compressor. [[10-012-014-tr — Air Compressor|Refer to Procedure 012-014 in Section 12.]]

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> If damage resulted in a hydraulic lock, it may be necessary to remove the injectors and evacuate coolant from the cylinder(s). [[10-006-026-tr — Injector|Refer to Procedure 006-026 in Section 6.]]

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Air pressure regulator kit, Part Number 3164231, or equivalent, is suitable for use during this step.

Install air pressure regulator, Part Number 3164231, or equivalent, on the plug, Part Number 4918689, in the EGR cooler coolant supply port.

Install a ball-type shutoff valve onto the supply side of the air pressure regulator, to allow the air supply to be shut off during testing.

![[11a00029.png]]

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Wear appropriate eye and face protection when using compressed air. Flying debris and dirt can cause personal injury.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The test **must** be performed when the EGR cooler has reached ambient temperature. Do **not** apply air pressure to a hot EGR cooler.

Make sure air pressure regulator and ball-type shutoff valve are closed and connect a compressed air supply to the pressure regulator.

Apply 620 kPa \[90 psi\] of air pressure to the EGR cooler.

Inspect for air escaping from the EGR cooler or pressure test assembly.

Check for leaks by spraying the exterior of the EGR cooler and all lines and fittings with a mixture of mild soap and water. Repair leaking fittings or lines.

Replace EGR cooler if external leaks, caused by cracks or other damage, are found on EGR cooler.

![[11a00030.png]]

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Wear appropriate eye and face protection when using compressed air. Flying debris and dirt can cause personal injury.

Shut air supply off to the air pressure regulator, Part Number 3164231, using the ball valve.

Disconnect compressed air supply from the air pressure regulator.

Record time the compressed air supply was shut off and removed from the air pressure regulator.

Record pressure shown on the air pressure regulator gauge.

![[11a00031.png]]

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Wear appropriate eye and face protection when using compressed air. Flying debris and dirt can cause personal injury.

Place EGR cooler in a vertical position with the exhaust gas inlet bellows pointing upward.

Spray exhaust inlet bulkhead and gas tubes with a mixture of mild soap and water.

![[11a00032.png]]

Gently flex the exhaust gas inlet bellows so that the inside corners of the bulkhead can be sprayed as well.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> It is important to wet the entire bulkhead with the mixture of mild soap and water. The highest percentage of leaks occur in the inside corners of the EGR cooler.

![[11a00033.png]]

Allow EGR cooler to remain pressurized for 15 minutes.

Gently flex bellows and inspect inside corners of bulkhead and gas tubes for continuous formation of bubbles.

Measure and record air pressure.

If the measured pressure loss in the EGR cooler is more than 103 kPa \[15 psi\] in 15 minutes, or if there is a continuous formation of bubbles near the gas tubes and bulkhead, the EGR cooler is **not** usable

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The pressure test is meant to identify large leaks and the inspection with mild soap and water is meant to identify small leaks. Do **not** use small losses observed during the pressure test **only** to evaluate the EGR cooler for reuse, as they are often associated with issues with the leak test equipment.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Stationary bubbles that form on the soot deposits or in the gas tubes are **not** an indication of a leak.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The continuous formation of bubbles is an indication that the EGR cooler is **not** usable.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> If EGR cooler is **not** usable based on a loss of pressure, carefully inspect the air pressure regulator, air lines, ball valves, o-rings, and other components for leaks. A mixture of mild soap and water may be sprayed on the assembly to aid in leak detection.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> If EGR cooler is **not** usable based on a loss of pressure, it can be necessary to evaluate the EGR cooler pressure testing assembly for leaks before replacing the EGR cooler. Assorted fittings or a known good EGR cooler can be used for this test.

![[11a00034.png]]

Make sure pressure regulator is closed.

Disconnect air pressure from the EGR cooler assembly.

Carefully vent trapped air from EGR cooler assembly.

Remove components installed from EGR Cooler Leak Test Kit, Part Number 4918655 or 2892437.

![[ck800wa.png]]

If EGR cooler is usable, inspect the EGR cooler coolant lines, couplings, o-rings, and grommets for cracks or other damage.

Replace necessary components if cracks or other damage is found. Refer to Procedure 011-031 in Section 11.

![[11c00211.png]]

If EGR cooler is **not** usable, inspect the turbocharger turbine housing and rear EGR connection tube for any signs of coolant deposits.

Coolant deposits can be identified as dried white deposits coating the inside of the turbine housing.

If coolant deposits are found in the turbocharger turbine housing, check for turbocharger related fault codes.

If turbocharger related fault codes are found, inspect the turbocharger. [[10-010-033-tr — Turbocharger|Refer to Procedure 010-033 in Section 10.]]

If coolant deposits are found in the rear EGR connection tube, inspect the EGR valve for progressive damage. [[10-011-022-tr — EGR Valve|Refer to Procedure 011-022 in Section 11.]]

If coolant deposits are found in the EGR valve, inspect the EGR venturi and front EGR connection tubes for progressive damage. [[10-011-025-tr — EGR Connection Tubes|Refer to Procedure 011-025 in Section 11.]]

If EGR cooler is being replaced due to damage that caused an internal coolant leak:

- Clean aftertreatment fuel injector, regardless if coolant deposits are present or **not**. [[101-011-042-tr — Aftertreatment Fuel Injector|Refer to Procedure 011-042 in Section 11.]]
- If Fault Code 555 and/or 556 is present, replace the crankcase breather element. [[101-003-019-tr — Crankcase Breather Element|Refer to Procedure 003-019 in Section 3.]]
- Inspect crankcase pressure sensor. Use the following procedure in the ISX CM871 and ISM CM876 Electronic Control System Troubleshooting Manual, Bulletin 4021560. Refer to Procedure 019-445 in Section 19.
- Inspect EGR differential pressure sensor tubes for coolant. If coolant is found, replace the EGR differential pressure sensor tubes. [[10-011-026-tr — EGR Differential Pressure Sensor Tubes|Refer to Procedure 011-026 in Section 11.]]
- Check for EGR differential pressure sensor fault codes. If fault codes are found, follow the appropriate troubleshooting tree, and replace the EGR differential pressure sensor if, required. Use the following procedure in the ISX CM871 and ISM CM876 Electronic Control System Troubleshooting and Repair Manual, Bulletin 4021560. Refer to Procedure 019-370 in Section 19.
- If coolant entered exhaust system, aftertreatment system can be recovered. [[101-014-013-tr — Aftertreatment Testing|Refer to Procedure 014-013 in Section 14.]]

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> If damage resulted in a hydraulic lock and the coolant level reached the intake manifold, it may be necessary to clean the charge air cooler. See equipment manufacturer service information.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> If damage resulted in a hydraulic lock and/or the coolant level reached the intake manifold, inspect the air compressor air inlet and outlet tubes for signs of coolant. If coolant is found, clean and inspect the air compressor head. [[101-012-007-tr — Air Compressor Cylinder Head, Single Cylinder|Refer to Procedure 012-007 in Section 12.]]

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> If damage resulted in a hydraulic lock, it may be necessary to remove the injectors and evacuate coolant from the cylinder(s). [[10-006-026-tr — Injector|Refer to Procedure 006-026 in Section 6.]]
