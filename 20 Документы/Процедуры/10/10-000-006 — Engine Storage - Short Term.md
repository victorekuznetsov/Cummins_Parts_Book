### General Information

This procedure describes the proper method to prepare an engine for short term storage (1 to 6 months).

Operate the engine at high idle speed until the coolant temperature indicator reaches 70°C \[160°F\].

Shut OFF engine.

![[oi803vs.png]]

Remove the fuel lines to the fuel pump and the injector return line. Place the ends into a container.

Install a new fuel filter. [[10-006-015-tr — Fuel Filter (Spin-On Type)|Refer to Procedure 006-015 in Section 6.]]

Fill the container with straight Number 1 or Number 2 diesel fuel (no biodiesel content).

Run the engine for 10 minutes to make sure the straight diesel fuel has filled all of the fuel system components.

![[00c00146.png]]

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> To reduce the possibility of personal injury, avoid direct contact of hot oil with your skin.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Some state and federal agencies have determined that used engine oil can be carcinogenic and cause reproductive toxicity. Avoid inhalation of vapors, ingestion, and prolonged contact with used engine oil. If not reused, dispose of in accordance with local environmental regulations.

Drain the lubricating oil pan, the lubricating oil filter(s), and the fuel filter.

Install the lubricating oil pan drain plug in the oil pan.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 47 n•m [35 ft-lb]

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The oil pan can remain empty until the engine is ready to use.

![[00c00150.png]]

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Coolant is toxic. Keep away from children and pets. If not reused, dispose of in accordance with local environmental regulations.

Drain the cooling system. [[10-008-018-tr — Cooling System|Refer to Procedure 008-018 in Section 8.]]

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> It is **not** necessary to drain the coolant if it is a permanent type antifreeze with a rust inhibitor. Do **not** drain the coolant if the engine is installed in a vehicle.

![[oi206vd.png]]

Cover all openings with tape to prevent dirt and moisture from entering the engine.

Install a warning tag on the engine. The tag **must** indicate that the engine does **not** contain oil and **must not** be operated.

![[00c00148.png]]

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Do not pull or pry on the fan to manually rotate the engine. To do so can damage the fan blades. Damaged fan blades can cause premature fan failures which can result in serious personal injury or property damage.

Rotate the crankshaft two or three revolutions every three to four weeks using the compressor drive or barring device to rotate the crankshaft. [[10-001-016-tr — Crankshaft|Refer to Procedure 001-016 in Section 1.]]

![[01c00147.png]]

Remove the engine from short term storage.

Remove the tape from the openings.

Remove the warning tag.

- Replace the lubricating oil filter. [[10-007-013-tr — Lubricating Oil Filter (Spin-On)|Refer to Procedure 007-013 in Section 7.]]
- Replace the coolant filter. Refer to Procedure 008-006 in Section 8.
- Replace the fuel filter. [[10-006-015-tr — Fuel Filter (Spin-On Type)|Refer to Procedure 006-015 in Section 6.]]

Prime the lubricating oil system. [[10-007-037-tr — Lubricating Oil System|Refer to Procedure 007-037 in Section 7.]]

Use clean diesel fuel to flush the diesel fuel that was stored in the engine.

![[00c00146.png]]

- Fill the cooling system, if necessary. [[10-008-018-tr — Cooling System|Refer to Procedure 008-018 in Section 8.]]
- Adjust the valve clearance. [[10-003-004-tr — Overhead Set|Refer to Procedure 003-004 in Section 3.]]
- Adjust the belt tension. Refer to Procedure 013-021 in Section 13.

![[00c00151.png]]
