# Lubricating Oil Fill Tube

> [!abstract] Процедура · `10-007-065`
> **Двигатели:** [[80141463 — QSX15 CM570 CPL 3088|80141463]], [[80248213 — QSX15 CM570 CPL 8760|80248213]]
> **Семейство:** QSX15
> **Входит в руководства:** [[3666251 — Signature and ISX Operation and Maintenance Manual|3666251]]
> **Секции:** Section A - Adjustment, Repair, and Replacement
> **Даты:** изменён 2002-08-28
> **Источник:** [QuickServe](https://quickserve.cummins.com/qs3/pubsys2/xml/en/procedures/10/10-007-065.html) · [PDF-оригинал](https://github.com/victorekuznetsov/Cummins_Parts_Book/raw/main/bulletins/procedures/10-007-065.pdf)

### Remove

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Removal of the oil fill tube without loosening the tube brace capscrew is likely to cause fill tube thread breakage.

Loosen the tube brace capscrew (1).

Remove the tube brace capscrew from the gear cover (2).

![[07c00255.png]]

Remove the oil fill tube (1) and the tube brace (2) from the oil fill tube connection (3).

![[07c00256.png]]

Rotate the oil fill tube **counterclockwise** to remove. A smooth, rounded, end bar can be used for leverage to rotate the oil fill tube connection.

![[07c00257.png]]

Remove the ring seals from the oil fill connection, the oil fill tube, and the oil fill tube cap.

Discard the ring seals.

Ring seals:

1. Oil fill connection ring seal
2. Oil fill tube ring seal
3. Oil fill cap ring seal.

![[07c00258.png]]

### Clean

Clean the oil fill connection and gear cover seal surface.

![[07c00259.png]]

### Inspect for Reuse

Inspect the oil fill tube and fill tube threads for cracks or other damage.

Inspect the oil fill tube sealing surfaces for cracks or other damage.

![[07c00261.png]]

### Install

Install a new oil fill connection ring seal.

Use clean engine oil to lubricate the oil fill connection ring seal.

![[07c00262.png]]

Install the oil fill connection to the lower gear cover.

![[07c00263.png]]

Install the new ring seals on the bottom of the oil fill tube (1) and on the oil fill tube cap (2).

![[07c00264.png]]

Install the oil fill tube cap and the oil fill tube.

![[07c00265.png]]

Install and tighten the tube brace gear cover capscrew (2).

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 28 n•m [21 ft-lb]

Tighten the tube brace capscrew (1).

![[07c00266.png]]
