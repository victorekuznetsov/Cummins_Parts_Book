### General Information

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Do not pull or pry on the fan to manually rotate the engine. To do so can damage the fan blades. Damaged fan blades can cause premature fan failures which can result in serious personal injury or property damage.

This procedure instructs how to rotate the engine for service events.

The following options may be available to bar the engine over. Associated instructions are provided in this procedure.

- With Air Compressor or Hydraulic Pump
- Without Air Compressor or Hydraulic Pump

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The illustrations in this procedure are generic and may **not** represent the hardware on all engines.

![[nobox.png]]

### Preparatory Steps

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Batteries can emit explosive gases. To reduce the possibility of personal injury, always ventilate the compartment before servicing the batteries. To reduce the possibility of arcing, remove the negative (-) battery cable first and attach the negative (-) battery cable last.

- Disconnect the batteries. See equipment manufacturer service information.

![[ck800wa.png]]

### Remove

With Air Compressor or Hydraulic Pump Drive

Remove the lubricating oil fill connector from the lower gear case cover, if equipped.

Remove the gear cover access cover, if applicable.

![[nobox.png]]

Without Air Compressor or Hydraulic Pump Drive

Loosen the cover plate capscrews. Rotate the cover plate to gain access.

Remove the lubricating oil fill tube, if equipped.

![[nobox.png]]

### Rotate

With Air Compressor or Hydraulic Pump Drive

Insert a ¾ inch drive ratchet and extension into the air compressor drive. Bar the engine over.

Rotate the engine **clockwise** when viewed from the front of the engine.

![[00900645.png]]

Without Air Compressor or Hydraulic Pump Drive

Insert a 1 1/2 inch socket. Engage the barring gear into the gear mesh. Bar the engine over.

Rotate the engine **clockwise** when viewed from the front of the engine.

![[00c00061.png]]

### Install

With Air Compressor or Hydraulic Pump Drive

Remove all tools.

Install the lubricating oil fill connector, if equipped.

Install the gear cover access cover, if applicable.

![[nobox.png]]

Without Air Compressor or Hydraulic Pump Drive

Rock the barring device back and forth until the gear disengages.

Remove all tools.

Rotate the gear cover back in place, if applicable.

Install the lubricating oil fill tube, if equipped.

![[nobox.png]]

### Finishing Steps

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Batteries can emit explosive gases. To reduce the possibility of personal injury, always ventilate the compartment before servicing the batteries. To reduce the possibility of arcing, remove the negative (-) battery cable first and attach the negative (-) battery cable last.

- Connect the batteries. See equipment manufacturer service information.

![[ck800wa.png]]
