# Oil Pressure Switch
**Датчик-реле давления масла**

> **Даты:** изменён 2007-04-16

### Remove

High Speed

Remove the brass cap and turn the valve 1/4-turn in the **clockwise** direction.

Loosen the high speed oil pressure switch connector screw.

Remove the high speed oil pressure switch connector.

![[19601007.png]]

Loosen the high speed oil pressure switch mounting screws with an allen wrench.

Remove the high speed oil pressure switch.

![[19601008.png]]

Low Speed

Remove the brass cap and turn the valve 1/4-turn in the **clockwise** direction.

Loosen the low speed oil pressure switch connector screw.

Remove the low speed oil pressure switch connector.

![[19601011.png]]

Loosen the low speed oil pressure switch mounting screws with an allen wrench.

Remove the low speed oil pressure switch.

![[19601012.png]]

### Install

High Speed

Place the high speed oil pressure switch onto the block.

Install the allen head mounting screws and tighten.

![[19601008.png]]

Connect the harness connector to the high speed oil pressure switch connector.

Tighten the connector screw.

Turn the valve 1/4-turn in the **counterclockwise** direction.

Install the brass cap.

![[19601007.png]]

Low Speed

Place the low speed oil pressure switch onto the block.

Install the allen head mounting screws and tighten.

![[19601012.png]]

Connect the harness connector to the low speed oil pressure switch connector.

Tighten the connector screw.

Turn the valve 1/4-turn in the **counterclockwise** direction.

Install the brass cap.

![[19601011.png]]
