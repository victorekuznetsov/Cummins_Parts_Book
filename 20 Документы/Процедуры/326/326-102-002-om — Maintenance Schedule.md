### General Information

Cummins Inc. recommends that the system be maintained according to the Maintenance Schedule in this section. Some of these maintenance procedures must be completed by qualified personnel. Contact a local Cummins® Authorized Repair Location for detailed information. If the system is equipped with a component or accessory not manufactured or supplied by Cummins Inc., component manufacturer's maintenance recommendations. Original equipment manufacturer (OEM)-supplied equipment and components can impact the performance and reliability of the Cummins® electronic throttle and shift and Cummins® inboard joystick systems if they are not correctly maintained. Use the chart provided in this section as a convenient way to record maintenance performed.

Follow the manufacturers' recommended maintenance procedures for OEM supplied thruster systems.

Perform maintenance at whichever interval occurs first.

At each scheduled maintenance interval, perform all previous maintenance checks that are due for scheduled maintenance.

#### Maintenance Procedures at Daily Interval (Section 3)

- Batteries - Check 1

#### Maintenance Procedures at 125 Hours or 3 Months (Section 4)

- Battery Cables and Connections - Check 1
- Electrical Connections - Check
- Propeller - Check

#### Maintenance Procedures at 250 Hours or 6 Months (Section 5)

- Sacrificial Zinc Anode - Check
- Direct Current Motor - Check 2

#### Maintenance Procedures at 500 Hours or 1 Year (Section 6)

- Sacrificial Zinc Anode - Replace
- Drive Leg - Check
- Propeller - Clean
- System Enable Switch - Check

1 Follow the manufacturers' recommended maintenance procedures for the battery cables and connections.

2 For high use applications, such as hire vessels, the brushes should be checked for wear every 3 months.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> **The hours and months outlined above are aligned with Cummins® engine maintenance intervals.The Cummins® electronic throttle and shift and Cummins® inboard joystick system maintenance should be completed at the time of engine maintenance.**

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> **The batteries for the inboard joystick system are an OEM component. Follow the manufacturer's recommended maintenance schedule to maximize battery performance and life. Weak performing batteries will directly impact the performance of the inboard joystick system.**
