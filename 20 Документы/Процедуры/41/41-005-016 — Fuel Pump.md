### Preparatory Steps

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> As far as possible the pump should be dismounted while the engine timing pin is engaged and piston number 1 is at the TDC position.

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> If space does not permit to do the same, pump can be taken out in any position of engine crank.

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Pump should be fitted in locked condition (pump timing pin engaged in cam lock mechanism on governor rotor) and engine piston No.1 position at TDC. (Engine timing pin engaged).

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Immediately after dismounting pump, bar the engine to achieve piston No.1 position at TDC and engine timing pin engaged. Do not forget to disengage the timing pin. Trying to bar the engine while timing pin engaged will break away timing pin tip.

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Protect the fuel pump openings immediately by protective caps available with Service Engineer.

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Cleanliness during the operation is most important. Dirt particles will damage feed pump suction valve and dirt in fuel gallery will damage pump elements seriously.

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Do not use cotton waste.

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Always perform operation with proper tools and spanner.Do not use inch spanners at all.

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> In absense of gear pullers do not hammer pump shaft while taking out pump assembly from place. This will seriously damage threading on pump camshaft.

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> If you are replacing a pump store all protective caps and caution tags with you.

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Do not lift the pump by holding feed pump. Support it from below governor housing and front flange.
