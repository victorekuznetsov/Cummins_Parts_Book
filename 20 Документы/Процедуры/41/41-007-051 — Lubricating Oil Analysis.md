### General Information

An analysis of used oil can help diagnose internal engine damage and determine if it was caused by one of the following:

- Intake air filter malfunction
- Coolant leaks
- Oil diluted with fuel
- Metal particles causing wear.

![[oi801ke.png]]

For additional oil analysis information, refer to Cummins Engine Oil Recommendations, Bulletin No. [[3810340 — Cummins® Engine Oil and Oil Analysis Recommendations|3810340]].

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Do **not** disassemble an engine for repair based **only** on the results of an oil analysis. Inspect the oil filters also. If the oil filter, shows evidence of internal damage, find the source of the problem and repair the damage. Refer to the appropriate procedure(s).
