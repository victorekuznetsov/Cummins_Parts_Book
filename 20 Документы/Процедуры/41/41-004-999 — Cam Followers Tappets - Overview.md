### General Information

**Valve Tappet**

The valve tappets are mushroom-shaped. The offset position of the tappet against camshaft lobe causes the tappet to rotate as it lifts the push rod.

![[cg900sa.png]]

The ball end of the push rod fits into the ball socket in the tappet. The other end of the push rod has a ball socket in which the ball end of the rocker lever adjusting screw operates.

![[pr900sa.png]]

A loose rocker lever and the need to reset the valve clearance frequently can indicate camshaft lobe or tappet wear. If an inspection of the levers, valve stems, and push rods does **not** show wear, then tappet and/or camshaft lobe wear can be suspected.
