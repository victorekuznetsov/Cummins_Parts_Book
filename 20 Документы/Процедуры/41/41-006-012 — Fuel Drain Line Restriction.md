### Test

Remove the fuel drain hose.

Install the adapter, Part Number ST-434-2, and pressure gauge, Part Number ST-1273, between the fuel drain hose and the engine fuel drain fitting.

![[06400051.png]]

Start and operate the engine at high idle, no load.

| mm-hg |  | in-hg |
|---|---|---|
| 508 | MAX | 20.0 |

![[06400053.png]]

If the fuel drain line restriction is above specifications, inspect the fuel drain line. Refer to Procedure [[41-006-013 — Fuel Drain Lines|006-013]].

![[fp8hoca.png]]

Remove the gauge.

Install the fuel drain line.

Tighten the fuel drain line.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 23 n•m [204 in-lb]
