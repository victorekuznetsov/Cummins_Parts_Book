### Adjust

RSV Governor Solenoid

Idle speed adjustment for engines with RSV governors requires the setting of both the low-idle speed screw (1) and the bumper spring screw (2).

![[ip9csua.png]]

Loosen the locknut and back out the bumper spring screw until there is no change in engine speed.

![[ip9nuua.png]]

Loosen the locknut and adjust the idle speed screw to 40 to 50 rpm less than the desired speed.

Turn the idle speed screw:

Counterclockwise

to decrease rpm

Clockwise

to increase rpm.

Tighten the locknut.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 8 n•m [72 in-lb]

![[ip9nuub.png]]

Turn the bumper spring screw **clockwise** until the desired idle speed is obtained.

Tighten the locknut.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 8 n•m [72 in-lb]

![[ip9csut.png]]

RQVK Governor Solenoid

Idle speed adjustment on fuel injection pumps with RQV or RQVK governors requires setting **only** the stop screw.

Turn the idle stop screw:

Counterclockwise

to raise the rpm

Clockwise

to decrease the idle speed.

![[fp9csua.png]]

Loosen the locknut and turn the idle speed screw to the desired idle speed.

Tighten the locknut.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 8 n•m [72 in-lb]
