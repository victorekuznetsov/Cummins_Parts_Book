# Coolant Pressure Sensor
**Датчик давления охлаждающей жидкости**

> [!abstract] Процедура · `00-019-016`

### Remove

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> QSK45/60/78 **only**

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Do not remove the pressure cap from a hot engine. Wait until the coolant temperature is below 50°C \[120°F\] before removing the pressure cap or the coolant temperature sensor. Heated coolant spray or steam can cause personal injury.

Drain the cooling system. Refer to the troubleshooting and repair manual for the specific engine platform.

![[ra800qa.png]]

Lift up on the locking tab and pull the electrical connectors apart.

![[19400387.png]]

Remove the sensor using a deep well socket, Part Number 3823843.

![[19801029.png]]

### Install

Verify that the new sensor has an o-ring installed.

Lubricate the o-ring with clean engine oil.

![[19801030.png]]

Install the new sensor into the engine block. Tighten the sensor using deep well socket, Part Number 3823843.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 14 n•m [124 in-lb]

![[19801029.png]]

Use quick-dry electrical contact cleaner, Part Number 3824510, to remove all dirt and moisture from the harness connector.

![[19801031.png]]

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Use only Cummins-recommended lubricant DS-ES, Part Number 3822934. Other lubricants, such as oil or grease, in the connectors can cause ECM damage, poor engine performance, or premature connector pin wear.

Apply a thin coating of lubricant to the harness connector.

![[19801032.png]]

Push the connectors together until they lock.

Fill the cooling system and operate the engine to check for leaks.

![[19801028.png]]
