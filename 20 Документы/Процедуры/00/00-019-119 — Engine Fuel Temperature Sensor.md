### Remove

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The art shown in the following procedure is based on the QSK19 engine configuration. The fuel temperature sensor on the QSK45, QSK60, and QSK78 engines is on the opposite side of the ECVA.

Clean the area around the sensor.

Lift up on the locking tab and pull the electrical connectors apart.

![[19801033.png]]

Remove the sensor using a deep well socket, Part Number 3823843.

![[19801034.png]]

### Install

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The art shown in the following procedure is based on the QSK19 engine configuration. The fuel temperature sensor on the QSK45, QSK60, and QSK78 engines is on the opposite side of the ECVA.

Check that the new sensor has an o-ring installed.

Lubricate the o-ring with clean engine oil.

![[19801035.png]]

Install the new sensor into the control valve body.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 14 n•m [124 in-lb]

![[19801034.png]]

Use quick-dry electrical contact cleaner, Part Number 3824510, to remove all dirt and moisture from harness connector.

![[19801036.png]]

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Use only Cummins-recommended lubricant DS-ES, Part Number 3822934. Other lubricants, such as oil or grease, in the connectors can cause ECM damage, poor engine performance, or premature connector pin wear.

Apply a thin coating of lubricant to the harness connector.

![[19801037.png]]

Push the connectors together until they lock.

Operate the engine to check for leaks.

![[19801038.png]]
