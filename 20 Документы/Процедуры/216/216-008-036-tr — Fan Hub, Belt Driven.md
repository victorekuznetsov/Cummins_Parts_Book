# Fan Hub, Belt Driven
**Ступица вентилятора с ремённым приводом**

> [!abstract] Процедура · `216-008-036-tr`
> **Двигатели:** [[82099327 — QSB6.7 CM2150 B109 CPL 4375|82099327]]
> **Семейство:** QSB6.7
> **Входит в руководства:** [[4326168 — QSB6.7 CM2150 B109 Service Manual|4326168]]
> **Секции:** Section 8 - Cooling System - Group 08
> **Даты:** изменён 2016-09-14
> **Источник:** [QuickServe](https://quickserve.cummins.com/qs3/pubsys2/xml/en/procedures/216/216-008-036-tr.html) · [PDF-оригинал](https://github.com/victorekuznetsov/Cummins_Parts_Book/raw/main/bulletins/procedures/216-008-036-tr.pdf)

### General Information

Several different fan hub mounting locations and fan hub options are available. The illustrations in this procedure are generic and may **not** represent the hardware on all engines.

### Preparatory Steps

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Batteries can emit explosive gases. To reduce the possibility of personal injury, always ventilate the compartment before servicing the batteries. To reduce the possibility of arcing, remove the negative (-) battery cable first and attach the negative (-) battery cable last.

- Disconnect the batteries. See equipment manufacturer service information.
- Remove the accessory drive pulley belt, if equipped. See equipment manufacturer service information.
- Remove the cooling fan drive belt. Refer to Procedure 008-002 in Section 8.
- Remove the electric fan clutch. Refer to Procedure 008-026 in Section 8 or the equipment manufacturer service information.
- Remove the cooling fan, if equipped. See equipment manufacturer service information.
- Remove the accessory drive pulley, if equipped. Refer to Procedure 009-004 in Section 9.
- Remove the fan spacer and pulley. Refer to Procedure 008-039 in Section 8.

### Remove

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> There are many available fan hub configurations. Be sure to note the location, orientation, and mounting pattern of the hub prior to removal from the engine.

Remove the fan hub mounting capscrews.

Remove the fan hub.

![[08v00040.png]]

### Initial Check

Inspect the fan hub for indication of oil seal leakage.

Inspect the fan hub for cracks and other damage.

![[fa900sa.png]]

Rotate the fan hub by hand to check for freedom of rotation.

The fan hub **must** rotate without any wobble or excessive end clearance.

![[fa900sb.png]]

Measure the fan hub end clearance.

| Fan Hub Shaft End Clearance |  |  |
|---|---|---|
| mm |  | in |
| 0.15 | MAX | 0.006 |

If damaged parts are found, the shaft does **not** rotate freely, or the shaft end clearance is **not** within specifications, the fan hub **must** be rebuilt or replaced.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Some fan hub assemblies can be rebuilt with new bearings and related components. Check for part availability before disassembling the fan hub.

![[fa900na.png]]

### Disassemble

Secure the fan hub.

Remove the center bolt and retainer.

![[fa900fa.png]]

Install a bearing splitter as illustrated.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Do **not** overtighten the bearing splitter as it may damage the shaft when the bearing is pressed out.

![[fa9toha.png]]

Support the fan hub housing/bearing splitter and press out the shaft.

![[fa9shma.png]]

Support the fan hub housing and press out the bearing.

Remove the snap rings.

![[fa9bema.png]]

### Inspect for Reuse

Inspect the fan hub for damage to the:

- Snap rings
- Housing
- Shaft
- Bolt.

If damage is found, the fan hub can **not** be rebuilt and **must** be replaced as an assembly.

### Assemble

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Do not press the bearing by the inner portion of the bearing. Damage to the bearing can result.

Press a new bearing into the fan hub housing until it is even with the front of the housing.

| Fan Hub Bearing Installation Force |  |  |
|---|---|---|
| n |  | lbf |
| 9000 | MIN | 2023 |

Replace the fan hub if the installation force is less than specified.

Install the snap rings.

![[fa9beha.png]]

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Verify that the inner race is adequately supported to avoid damage to the bearing. Do not press on the fan hub.

Support the bottom of the inner race. Press the shaft into the bearing until it is seated.

Measure the assembled height. If the height is incorrect, disassemble and assemble with a new bearing.

| Fan Hub Assembled Height |  |  |
|---|---|---|
| mm |  | in |
| 124.85 | MIN | 4.915 |
| 125.65 | MAX | 4.946 |

![[fa9shha.png]]

Secure the fan hub assembly.

Install the retainer and center bolt.

Tighten the center bolt.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 77 n•m [57 ft-lb]

![[fa900fa.png]]

### Install

Install the fan hub and mounting capscrews.

Tighten the capscrews.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 32 n•m [24 ft-lb]

![[08v00040.png]]

### Finishing Steps

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Batteries can emit explosive gases. To reduce the possibility of personal injury, always ventilate the compartment before servicing the batteries. To reduce the possibility of arcing, remove the negative (-) battery cable first and attach the negative (-) battery cable last.

- Install the fan spacer and pulley. Refer to Procedure 008-039 in Section 8.
- Install the accessory drive pulley, if equipped. Refer to Procedure 009-004 in Section 9.
- Install the electric fan clutch. Refer to Procedure 008-026 in Section 8 or the equipment manufacturer service information.
- Install the cooling fan, if equipped. See equipment manufacturer service information.
- Install the cooling fan drive belt. Refer to Procedure 008-002 in Section 8.
- Install the accessory drive pulley belt, if equipped. See equipment manufacturer service information.
- Connect the batteries. See equipment manufacturer service information.
- Operate the engine and check for proper operation.
