### Specifications

#### Oil Pressure

| Low idle (minimum allowed) | 124 kPa \[ 18 psi \] |
|---|---|

| At rated (minimum allowed) | 207 kPa \[ 30 psi \] |
|---|---|

| High-pressure dump valve opening pressure range | 900 kPa \[ 131 psi \] to 1100 kPa \[ 160 psi \] |
|---|---|

| Main rifle regulating valve opening pressure range | 220 kPa \[ 32 psi \] to 250 kPa \[ 36 psi \] |
|---|---|

| Oil filter differential pressure to open bypass | 689 kPa \[ 100 psi \] |
|---|---|

#### Oil Capacity of Standard Engine (50 Liter Lubricating Oil Pan)

| Pan only | 50 liters \[ 13.2 U.S. gal \] to high |
|---|---|

| High to low (on dipstick) | 15 liters \[ 4.0 U.S. gal \] |
|---|---|

| Lubricating oil filter capacity | 2 liters \[ 0.5 U.S. gal \] |
|---|---|

| Total system | 53 liters \[ 14.0 U.S. gal \] to high |
|---|---|

#### Oil Capacity of Standard Engine (44 Liter Lubricating Oil Pan)

| Pan only | 44 liters \[ 11.6 U.S. gal \] to high |
|---|---|

| High to low (on dipstick) | 9 liters \[ 2.4 U.S. gal \] |
|---|---|

| Lubricating oil filter capacity | 2 liters \[ 0.5 U.S. gal \] |
|---|---|

| Total system | 49 liters \[ 12.9 U.S. gal \] to high |
|---|---|

#### Oil Capacity of Standard Engine (37 Liter Lubricating Oil Pan)

| Pan only | 37 liters \[ 9.8 U.S. gal \] to high |
|---|---|

| High to low (on dipstick) | 10 liters \[ 2.6 U.S. gal \] |
|---|---|

| Lubricating oil filter capacity | 2 liters \[ 0.5 U.S. gal \] |
|---|---|

| Total system | 42 liters \[ 11.1 U.S. gal \] to high |
|---|---|

#### Maximum Oil Temperature

| Maximum oil temperature | 135°C \[ 275°F \] |
|---|---|

The note 50L, 44L, or 37L for the lubricating oil pan capacity can be found on the lubricating oil dipstick.
