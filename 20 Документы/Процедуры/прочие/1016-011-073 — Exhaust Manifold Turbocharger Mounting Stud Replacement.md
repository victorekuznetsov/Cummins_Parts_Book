# Exhaust Manifold Turbocharger Mounting Stud Replacement

### Select Service Tools

#### Recommended Cummins® Service Tools

- Penetrating oil, Part Number 2892116, or equivalent
- Anti-seize compound, Part Number 3824879, or equivalent

#### Additional Service Items

- Standard stud extractor.

### Remove

Apply penetrating oil, Part Number 2892116, or equivalent, to the base of the turbocharger mounting stud to be removed.

Multiple applications of the penetrating oil may be necessary to free the turbocharger mounting stud. See equipment manufacturer service information.

![[11r00388.png]]

Use a standard stud extractor to remove the turbocharger mounting stud(s) from the exhaust manifold.

Discard the mounting studs.

![[11r00389.png]]

### Inspect for Reuse

Inspect for damaged threads in the turbocharger mounting stud holes.

Inspect the manifold sections for cracks or other damage.

![[11r00390.png]]

### Install

Install new mounting studs.

Install the shorter threaded end of the turbocharger mounting stud in the exhaust manifold flange.

Apply a coat of anti-seize compound, Part Number 3824879, or equivalent, to the threads.

Use two mounting nuts locked together to tighten the studs.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 20 n•m [177 in-lb]
