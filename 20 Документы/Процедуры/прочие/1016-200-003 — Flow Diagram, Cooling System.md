> ### Flow Diagram
>
> Flow Through Engine
>
> 1. Coolant from radiator
> 2. Coolant flow to water pump
> 3. Water pump
> 4. Coolant flow from water pump to oil cooler module
> 5. Oil cooler element
> 6. Coolant flow around oil cooler element
> 7. Coolant flow from oil cooler module to cylinder block
> 8. Coolant flow from oil cooler module to coolant manifold
> 9. Coolant manifold
> 10. Coolant flow from coolant manifold to cylinder block
> 11. Coolant flow around cylinders
> 12. Coolant flow from cylinder block to lower cylinder head
> 13. Coolant flow to upper cylinder head
> 14. Coolant flow from cylinder head to thermostat housing.
>
> Flow Through Coolant Thermostat - With Retarder, Parallel
>
> 1. Coolant flow from cylinder head
> 2. Thermostat closed
> 3. Coolant flow through bypass tube to coolant inlet connection
> 4. Coolant inlet connection
> 5. Thermostat open
> 6. Coolant flow to radiator
> 7. Coolant flow return from water tank (optional)
> 8. Coolant flow to cabin heater
> 9. Coolant flow return from cabin heater
> 10. Coolant from lubricating oil cooler to Exhaust Gas Recirculation (EGR) cooler
> 11. Coolant from evaporator (Liquefied Natural Gas (LNG)) or regulator (Compressed Natural Gas (CNG))
> 12. Coolant flow return from EGR cooler
> 13. Coolant from lubricating oil cooler to turbocharger bearing housing
> 14. Coolant flow return from turbocharger bearing housing
> 15. Coolant flow to retarder plumbing
> 16. Coolant flow return from retarder plumbing
> 17. Coolant flow from radiator
> 18. Coolant flow to evaporator (LNG) or regulator (CNG).
>
> Flow Through Coolant Thermostat - With Retarder, Series
>
> 1. Coolant flow from cylinder head
> 2. Thermostat closed
> 3. Coolant flow through bypass tube to coolant inlet connection
> 4. Coolant inlet connection
> 5. Thermostat open
> 6. Coolant flow to radiator
> 7. Coolant flow return from water tank (optional)
> 8. Coolant flow to EGR cooler
> 9. Coolant flow through EGR cooler to retarder plumbing
> 10. Coolant flow through retarder plumbing to retarder
> 11. Coolant flow through retarder plumbing to evaporator (LNG) or regulator (CNG)
> 12. Coolant flow from evaporator (LNG) or regulator (CNG)
> 13. Coolant flow from retarder
> 14. Coolant flow from radiator
> 15. Coolant from thermostat housing to turbocharger bearing housing
> 16. Coolant flow return from turbocharger bearing housing.
>
> Flow Through Coolant Thermostat - Without Retarder
>
> 1. Coolant flow from cylinder head
> 2. Thermostat closed
> 3. Coolant flow through bypass tube to coolant inlet connection
> 4. Coolant inlet connection
> 5. Thermostat open
> 6. Coolant flow to radiator
> 7. Coolant flow return from water tank (optional)
> 8. Coolant flow to cabin heater
> 9. Coolant flow return from cabin heater
> 10. Coolant flow return from water tank (optional)
> 11. Coolant from lubricating oil cooler to EGR cooler
> 12. Coolant flow return from EGR cooler
> 13. Coolant from lubricating oil cooler to turbocharger bearing housing
> 14. Coolant flow return from turbocharger bearing housing
> 15. Coolant flow to evaporator (LNG) or regulator (CNG)
> 16. Coolant from evaporator (LNG) or regulator (CNG)
> 17. Coolant flow from air compressor return line to water inlet connection
> 18. Coolant flow from radiator.
>
> Flow Through Air Compressor, Without EGR Water Jacket
>
> 1. Coolant flow from cylinder block to air compressor
> 2. Coolant inlet port
> 3. Air compressor
> 4. Coolant outlet port
> 5. Coolant flow from air compressor return line to water inlet connection.
>
> Flow Through EGR/Air Intake System, With Water Jacket
>
> 1. Coolant flow from cylinder block to EGR mixer
> 2. Coolant flow from cylinder block to air compressor
> 3. EGR mixer
> 4. EGR valve
> 5. Coolant flow from EGR mixer to venturi tube
> 6. Venturi tube
> 7. Coolant flow from venturi tube to air intake connection
> 8. Air intake connection
> 9. Coolant flow from air intake connection to water inlet connection
> 10. Air compressor
> 11. Coolant flow from air compressor to water inlet connection.
>
> Flow Through Turbocharger
>
> 1. Coolant from lubricating oil cooler to turbocharger bearing housing
> 2. Turbocharger bearing housing
> 3. Coolant from turbocharger bearing housing to coolant thermostat housing.
>
> Flow Through EGR Cooler
>
> 1. Coolant from lubricating oil cooler to EGR cooler
> 2. EGR cooler
> 3. Coolant from evaporator (LNG) or regulator (CNG)
> 4. Coolant from EGR cooler to water inlet connection.
>
> Flow Through Regulators (CNG)
>
> 1. Coolant to fuel pressure regulator (CNG)
> 2. Fuel pressure regulator (CNG)
> 3. Coolant from fuel pressure regulator (CNG).
