### General Information

For the engines with engine-mounted fuel filter, the fuel supply lines include fuel line (1) from fuel supply line adapter to fuel filter, fuel line (2) from fuel filter to fuel metering valve, and fuel line (3) from fuel metering valve to air/fuel mixer.

![[05s00067.png]]

For the engines with remote-mounted fuel filter, the fuel supply lines include fuel supply line adapter (1) from fuel filter to fuel line (2), fuel line (2) from fuel supply line adapter (1) to fuel metering valve, and fuel line (3) from fuel metering valve to air/fuel mixer.

The graphics shown are for illustration purposes **only**. Actual hardware can vary.

![[05s00085.png]]

### Initial Check

Clean all connectors before disassembly. Dirt or contaminants can damage the fuel system.

Inspect all fuel supply line connectors and lines. Look for cracks in the lines or leaks in the connectors.

![[05s00046.png]]

### Preparatory Steps

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Batteries can emit explosive gases. To reduce the possibility of personal injury, always ventilate the compartment before servicing the batteries. To reduce the possibility of arcing, remove the negative (-) battery cable first and attach the negative (-) battery cable last.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Natural gas is explosive and flammable. Always be sure to maintain adequate ventilation in the work area. Keep all cigarettes, flames, pilot lights, arcing equipment, and switches out of the work area and areas with shared ventilation to reduce the possibility of severe personal injury or death when working on a natural gas system.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> If natural gas leaks are present, do not store the equipment inside or in any area that is covered. Severe personal injury can result from asphyxiation or explosion.

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Clean any external surfaces of fuel metering valve before removal. Dirt or contaminants can damage the fuel system.

- Turn off the vehicle's main fuel supply valve.
- Disconnect the batteries. See equipment manufacturer service information.

### Remove

Disconnect the fuel supply line from the air/fuel mixer and fuel metering valve, using a second wrench to support the fuel supply fittings in the air/fuel mixer and fuel metering valve. Remove the fuel supply line.

Disconnect the fuel supply line from fuel metering valve and fuel filter or the fuel supply line adapter that is engine mounted, using a second wrench to support the fuel supply fittings in the fuel metering valve and fuel filter or the fuel supply line adapter that is engine mounted. Remove the fuel supply line.

Disconnect the fuel supply line from fuel filter and fuel supply line adapter that is engine mounted, using a second wrench to support the fuel supply fittings in the fuel filter and fuel supply line adapter that is engine mounted. Remove the fuel supply line.

![[05s00068.png]]

Remove the connectors from fuel supply line adapter or air/fuel mixer, if required.

Remove the fuel supply line adapter to flywheel housing.

![[05s00069.png]]

### Clean and Inspect for Reuse

Inspect the inlet and outlet of fuel supply line, adapter, and connectors for damage. Replace if damage is found.

![[05s00070.png]]

### Install

Install the fuel supply line adapter to flywheel housing.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 24 n•m [212 in-lb]

Install the connectors to fuel supply line adapter, if removed.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 47 n•m [35 ft-lb]

Install the connector to air/fuel mixer, if removed.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 120 n•m [89 ft-lb]

![[05s00069.png]]

Connect the fuel supply lines to the fuel supply line adapter, fuel filter, fuel metering valve, and air/fuel mixer. The fuel supply fitting **must** be held in place with a wrench when tightening the fitting connectors to prevent overtightening during the installation process.

Tighten the fitting connectors.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> Fitting connectors on the fuel metering valve and fuel supply line adapter 71 n•m [52 ft-lb]

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> Fitting connectors on the fuel filter and air/fuel mixer 117 n•m [86 ft-lb]

![[05s00071.png]]

### Finishing Steps

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Batteries can emit explosive gases. To reduce the possibility of personal injury, always ventilate the compartment before servicing the batteries. To reduce the possibility of arcing, remove the negative (-) battery cable first and attach the negative (-) battery cable last.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Natural gas is explosive and flammable. Always be sure to maintain adequate ventilation in the work area. Keep all cigarettes, flames, pilot lights, arcing equipment, and switches out of the work area and areas with shared ventilation to reduce the possibility of severe personal injury or death when working on a natural gas system.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> If natural gas leaks are present, do not store the equipment inside or in any area that is covered. Severe personal injury can result from asphyxiation or explosion.

- Connect the batteries. See equipment manufacturer service information.
- Slowly open the vehicle's main fuel supply valve.
- Operate the engine and check for leaks.
