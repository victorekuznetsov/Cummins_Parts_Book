### Remove

Aftermarket

Turn the keyswitch to the OFF position.

Loosen and remove any nylon wire ties used to secure the ICON™ idle control module connector cables to the vehicle frame or other wiring.

Disconnect the ICON™ idle control module A and B connectors from the ICON™ engine harness.

![[19c00917.png]]

Remove the two mounting capscrews securing the ICON™ idle control module to the vehicle's firewall.

Carefully remove the ICON™ idle control module.

![[15800033.png]]

### Install

Aftermarket

Install the ICON™ idle control module to the vehicle's firewall using two mounting capscrews.

![[15800033.png]]

Connect the ICON™ idle control module A and B connectors to the ICON™ engine harness.

Secure the ICON™ idle control module connector cables to the vehicle's frame or other wiring using nylon wire ties.

![[19c00917.png]]

### Convert

To determine if the ICON™ system needs to be converted from unswitched battery to keyswitch for the ignition bus relay circuit, perform the following test.

Turn the keyswitch OFF.

Turn the blower switch ON.

If the blowers are operating with the keyswitch OFF then the cab wiring harness **must** be converted as follows.

![[nobox.png]]

Remove the ignition bus power 1 (9) and ignition bus power 2 (10) from the unswitched battery post of the keyswitch assembly.

![[15800069.png]]

Install the ignition bus power 1 (7) and ignition bus power 2 (8) to the ignition post of the keyswitch assembly.

![[19803847.png]]
