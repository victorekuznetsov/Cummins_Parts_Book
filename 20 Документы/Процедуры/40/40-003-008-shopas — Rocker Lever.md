### Install

B4.5 RGT Engines

Install the rocker lever assemblies and pedestals in their original position.

Install the pedestal mounting capscrews.

![[02d00042.png]]

Tighten the pedestal mounting capscrews.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 36 n•m [27 ft-lb]

![[03900040.png]]

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Rotate the adjusting screws until the adjusting screw contacts the socket of the push rod. This will ensure the push rod stays in contact with the tappet and adjusting screw when the engine is rotated to set valve lash. If not completed, the push rods may be bent or damaged.

![[pr900sa.png]]

B3.9, B5.9, and B4.5 Engines

Because the rocker lever assemblies shares a mounting capscrew with the cylinder head, the initial rocker lever installation is a part of the cylinder head installation procedure. Refer to [[40-002-004-tr — Cylinder Head|Procedure 002-004]].

Tighten the remaining 8-mm rocker lever assembly pedestal capscrews.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 24 n•m [18 ft-lb]

![[rh9csnc.png]]

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Rotate the adjusting screws until the adjusting screw contacts the socket of the push rod. This will ensure the push rod stays in contact with the tappet and adjusting screw when the engine is rotated to set valve lash. If not completed, the push rods may be bent or damaged.

![[pr900sa.png]]
