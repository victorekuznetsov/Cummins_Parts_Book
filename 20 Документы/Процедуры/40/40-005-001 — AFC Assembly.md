### General Information

Air-Fuel Control (AFC) Malfunctions

The RQVK governors are equipped with an air-fuel control (AFC) device to help control emissions. The AFC regulates the fuel-to-air mixture by sensing manifold pressure. A malfunction of the AFC can cause low power or excessive exhaust smoke under load. Leaks at the fittings or a restriction in the tube from the intake manifold to the AFC can also cause low power.
