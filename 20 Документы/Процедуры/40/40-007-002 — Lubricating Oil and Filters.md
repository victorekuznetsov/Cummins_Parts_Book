### Drain

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Some state and federal agencies have determined that used engine oil can be carcinogenic and cause reproductive toxicity. Avoid inhalation of vapors, ingestion, and prolonged contact with used engine oil. If not reused, dispose of in accordance with local environmental regulations.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> To reduce the possibility of personal injury, avoid direct contact of hot oil with your skin.

Change the lubricating oil and filter(s) at the specified oil change interval. Refer to Procedure [[40-102-002-om-mar — Maintenance Schedule|102-002]] (Maintenance Schedule) in Section 2 to find the correct change interval for your application.

![[07200008.png]]

Operate the engine until the water temperature reaches 60°C \[140°F\]. Shut off the engine.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Use a container that can hold at least 26 liters \[27 qt\] of lubricating oil.

Remove the oil drain plug from the bottom of the lubricating oil pan.

![[op9pgma.png]]

### Remove

Clean the area around the lubricating oil filter head.

Remove the oil filter.

Clean the gasket surface of the filter head.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The o-ring can stick on the filter head. Make sure it is removed before installing the new filter.

![[07900018.png]]

### Install

Use the correct oil filter.

Refer to Procedure [[00-018-004 — Coolant Recommendations and Specifications|018-004]] (Cummins/Fleetguard® Filter Specifications) in Section V for oil filter part number(s).

![[07900016.png]]

Fill the filter with clean lubricating oil before installation.

Apply a light film of lubricating oil to the gasket sealing surface before installing the filter.

![[07d00054.png]]

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Mechanical overtightening of filter can distort the threads or damage the filter element seal.

Install the filter as specified by the filter manufacturer.

![[lf8etha.png]]

Clean the sealing surface.

Check the oil drain plug threads.

Install the drain plug.

| Steel Pan | 60 n.m | \[44 ft-lb\] |
|---|---|---|

| Aluminum Pan | 30 n.m | \[22 ft-lb\] |
|---|---|---|

![[lf9pgsa.png]]

### Fill

Use a high-quality 15W-40 multiviscosity lubricating oil, such as Valvoline® Premium Blue® or its equivalent, in Cummins engines. Choose the correct lubricating oil for your operating climate as outlined in Procedure [[40-018-003 — Lubricating Oil Recommendations and Specifications|018-003]] (Lubricating Oil Recommendations and Specifications) in Section V.

![[pl800gc.png]]

Fill the engine with clean lubricating oil to the proper level. Refer to Procedure [[40-018-017-om-mar — Lubricating Oil System|018-017]] (Lubricating Oil System) in Section V for lubricating oil capacity.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Total system capacity assumes lubricating oil pan plus lubricating oil filter.

Some applications use a slightly different lubricating oil pan capacity. All lubricating oil quantities **must** be adjusted accordingly. Contact a local Cummins Distributor if you have any questions.

![[oi900va.png]]

Operate the engine and check for leaks at the filters and oil drain plug.

![[07900232.png]]
