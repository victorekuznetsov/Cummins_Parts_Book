### General Information

For your convenience, listed below are the section numbers that contain specific instructions for performing the maintenance checks.

Perform maintenance at whichever interval occurs first. At each scheduled maintenance interval, perform all previous maintenance checks that are due for scheduled maintenance.

#### Maintenance Procedures at Daily Interval (Section 3)

- Engine Lubricating Oil Level - Check
- Engine Coolant Level - Check
- Cooling Fan - Inspect
- Drive Belts - Inspect
- Fuel-Water Separator - Drain

#### Maintenance Procedures at 10,000 Kilometers \[6000 Miles\], 250 Hours, or 3 Months 1, 4 (Section 4)

- Lubricating Oil - Change 1
- Lubricating Oil Filters - Change
- Air Cleaner Restriction - Check
- Air Intake Piping - Check
- Charge-Air Cooler - Inspect 4
- Air Compressor - Check
- Batteries - Inspect
- Radiator Pressure Cap - Check

#### Maintenance Procedures at 19,000 Kilometers \[12,000 Miles\], 500 Hours, or 6 Months 3, 5 (Section 5)

- Fuel Filter (Spin-On Type) - Change 5
- Antifreeze - Check 3
- Cooling Fan Belt Tensioner - Inspect

#### Maintenance Procedures at 38,000 Kilometers \[24,000 Miles\], 1000 Hours, or 1 Year 2 (Section 6)

- Overhead Set - Adjust 2
- Drive Belts - Test

#### Maintenance Procedures at 77,000 Kilometers \[48,000 Miles\], 2000 Hours, or 2 Years 3 (Section 7)

- Air Compressor Discharge Lines - Check
- Cooling System - Flush 3, 6
- Vibration Damper, Rubber - Inspect
- Vibration Damper, Viscous - Inspect

1. Refer to the oil change interval tables provided in this section to find the specific oil change interval for individual applications.
2. Initial valve lash clearance adjustment, subsequent adjustments to be performed at 77,000 Kilometers \[48,000 Miles\] intervals or every 8th oil change for automotive engines.
3. Heavy-duty year around antifreeze that meets the critical composition of ASTM D6210 **must** be used. The change interval is 2 years or 320,000 Kilometers \[200,000 Miles\], whichever occurs first. Antifreeze is essential for freeze, overheat, and corrosion protection.
4. Service interval is 2 years or 320,000 Kilometers \[200,000 Miles\], whichever comes first.
5. Service interval is every other oil change or 19,000 Kilometers \[12,000 Miles\], 500 hours, or 6 months, whichever occurs first.
6. The cooling system requirement to Flush at this scheduled maintenance includes: Drain, Flush. and Fill.

- Is the vehicle an on-highway application?

- Is the vehicle used in a construction, mining, or logging operation?

If Yes -

- Refer to Table 2.

If No -

- Is the vehicle in an agricultural or stationary power application?

If Yes -

If No -

- Use the following oil change interval, 10,000 kilometers \[6000 miles\], 250 hours, or 3 months, whichever occurs first.
