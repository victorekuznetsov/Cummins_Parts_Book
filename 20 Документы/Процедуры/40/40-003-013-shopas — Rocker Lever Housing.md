### Install

Rear Gear Train

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Care must be taken when installing the gasket. Do not twist or stretch it. Failure to do so can result in an oil leak.

Install the rocker housing and a new rocker housing gasket, if removed.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 24 n•m [212 in-lb]
