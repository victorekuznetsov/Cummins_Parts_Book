# Fan Hub, Belt Driven
**Ступица вентилятора с ремённым приводом**

### Preparatory Steps

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Batteries can emit explosive gases. To reduce the possibility of personal injury, always ventilate the compartment before servicing the batteries. To reduce the possibility of arcing, remove the negative (-) battery cable first and attach the negative (-) battery cable last.

- Disconnect the batteries. [[40-013-007 — Batteries|Refer to Procedure 013-007 (Batteries) in Section 13.]]
- Remove the drive belt. [[40-008-002-tr — Drive Belt, Cooling Fan|Refer to Procedure 008-002 (Drive Belt, Cooling Fan) in Section 8.]]

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> [[40-008-026-tr — Fan Clutch, Electric|If equipped with Cummins® Electric Fan Clutch option, refer to Procedure 008-026 (Fan Clutch, Electric) in Section 8.]]

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Some applications do **not** have a cooling fan or the cooling fan is located elsewhere on the application.

- If required, remove the cooling fan. Refer to OEM service manual for instructions
- Remove the fan pulley and spacer. [[40-008-039-tr — Fan Spacer and Pulley|Refer to Procedure 008-039 (Fan Spacer and Pulley) in Section 8.]]

![[ck800wa.png]]

### Remove

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> There are many available fan hub configurations. Be sure to note the location, orientation, and mounting pattern of the hub prior to removal from the engine.

Remove the four capscrews and the fan hub.

![[fa900mc.png]]

### Clean and Inspect for Reuse

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> When using solvents, acids, or alkaline materials for cleaning, follow the manufacturer's recommendations for use. Wear goggles and protective clothing to reduce the possibility of personal injury.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Wear appropriate eye and face protection when using compressed air. Flying debris and dirt can cause personal injury.

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Do not expose the cooling fan drive belt to solvents, acids, or alkaline materials for cleaning. Belt damage can result.

Clean the fan hub and fan pulley with solvent.

Dry with compressed air.

![[08d00196.png]]

Inspect the fan hub for indications of oil seal leakage.

Inspect the fan hub for cracks or other damage.

Inspect the fan hub chamfer and fan pulley mating surface for damage.

Replace the fan hub if damage is found.

![[fa900sa.png]]

Turn the fan hub by hand to check for freedom of rotation.

The fan hub **must** spin freely without any wobble or excessive end clearance.

![[fa900sb.png]]

Inspect the fan hub bearing for wear.

The bearing **must** have a minimal amount of side-to-side or end-play movement.

Replace the fan hub if there is more than 0.15 mm \[0.006 in\] of end play in the fan hub.

| mm |  | in |
|---|---|---|
| 0.15 | MAX | 0.006 |

![[fa900na.png]]

### Install

Install the fan hub and four capscrews.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 24 n•m [212 in-lb]

![[fa900mc.png]]

### Finishing Steps

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Batteries can emit explosive gases. To reduce the possibility of personal injury, always ventilate the compartment before servicing the batteries. To reduce the possibility of arcing, remove the negative (-) battery cable first and attach the negative (-) battery cable last.

- Install the fan pulley and spacer. [[40-008-039-tr — Fan Spacer and Pulley|Refer to Procedure 008-039 (Fan Spacer and Pulley) in Section 8.]]

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Some applications do **not** have a cooling fan or the cooling fan is located elsewhere on the application.

- If removed, install the cooling fan. Refer to OEM service manual for instructions
- Install the drive belt. [[40-008-002-tr — Drive Belt, Cooling Fan|Refer to Procedure 008-002 (Drive Belt, Cooling Fan) in Section 8.]]

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> [[40-008-026-tr — Fan Clutch, Electric|If equipped with Cummins® Electric Fan Clutch option, refer to Procedure 008-026 (Fan Clutch, Electric) in Section 8.]]

- Connect the batteries. [[40-013-007 — Batteries|Refer to Procedure 013-007 (Batteries) in Section 13.]]
- Operate the engine and check for proper operation.
