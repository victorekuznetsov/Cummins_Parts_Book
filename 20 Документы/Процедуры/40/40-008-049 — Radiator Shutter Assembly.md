### General Information

Shutters are designed to control airflow across the radiator. If the shutters fail to open when needed, the engine can run hot. Failure of the shutters to close can result in too much airflow and the engine running cold.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Make sure the air temperature sensor is functioning correctly. Check the air-operated shutter controls. Check for air leaks. Refer to the equipment manufacturer's service manual.

Winterfronts can be used on a charge air cooled engine, but **must** be designed to cover the frontal area of the cooling system partially. A minimum of charge air cooled frontal area **must** be left open to air flow.
