### Install

Front Gear Train

For front gear train engines, the front crankshaft seal is installed with the front gear cover removed. The front crankshaft seal is installed on the engine with the front gear cover.

![[gc9sehg.png]]

Rear Gear Train

Use tool, Part Number 3164659, to install the oil seal into the front gear cover.

![[22d00056.png]]

Mount the replacer screw assembly (1) onto the crankshaft nose.

Install the two M12 x 1.25 x 60 mm socket head capscrews (3).

![[22d00051.png]]

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Do not use an impact wrench or air tools. Doing so can damage the tool.

Place the new front crankshaft seal over the crankshaft nose and slide it by hand toward the front gear cover as far as possible.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Make sure the seal is positioned squarely with the crankshaft.

While holding the replacer screw, install the crankshaft seal replacer (2) onto the replacer screw assembly.

Advance the crankshaft seal replacer toward the seal by rotating it **clockwise** until it is positioned against the seal.

![[22d00052.png]]

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Do not overtighten the replacer screw assembly after the crankshaft seal replacer contacts the front cover. Doing so can damage the tool.

While holding the crankshaft seal replacer, rotate the replacer screw **counterclockwise** until the crankshaft seal replacer contacts the front gear cover.

Remove the service tools.

![[22d00056.png]]
