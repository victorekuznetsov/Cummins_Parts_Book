### Preparatory Steps

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Batteries can emit explosive gases. To reduce the possibility of personal injury, always ventilate the compartment before servicing the batteries. To reduce the possibility of arcing, remove the negative (-) battery cable first and attach the negative (-) battery cable last.

- Disconnect the batteries.

![[13900050.png]]

- Remove the rocker lever cover. Refer to [[40-003-011-tr — Rocker Lever Cover|Procedure 003-011]].

![[ck800wa.png]]

### Remove

Rear Gear Train

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Check the gasket while it is installed in the rocker housing. Once the gasket is removed it **must** be replaced.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Check for cracks in the rocker housing bridge area before removing the mounting capscrews.

Remove the rocker housing, capscrews, and gasket.

![[03d00061.png]]

### Clean and Inspect for Reuse

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> When using solvents, acids, or alkaline materials for cleaning, follow the manufacturer's recommendations for use. Wear goggles and protective clothing to reduce the possibility of personal injury.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Wear appropriate eye and face protection when using compressed air. Flying debris and dirt can cause personal injury.

Clean the rocker housing with solvent.

Dry the rocker housing with compressed air.

![[03d00047.png]]

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Check the gasket while it is installed in the rocker housing. Once it is removed it **must** be replaced.

Inspect the rocker housing for cracks or any other damage, especially on the cylinder head mounting surface.

Check the gasket for cracks or is twisted in the groove.

Replace the gasket if it is torn, if cracks are present or it is twisted in the groove.

![[03d00048.png]]

### Install

Rear Gear Train

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Care must be taken when installing the gasket. Do not twist or stretch it. Failure to do so can result in an oil leak.

Install the rocker housing and a new rocker housing gasket, if removed.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 24 n•m [212 in-lb]

![[03d00061.png]]

### Finishing Steps

- Install the rocker lever cover and gasket. Refer to [[40-003-011-tr — Rocker Lever Cover|Procedure 003-011]].

![[ck800wa.png]]

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Batteries can emit explosive gases. To reduce the possibility of personal injury, always ventilate the compartment before servicing the batteries. To reduce the possibility of arcing, remove the negative (-) battery cable first and attach the negative (-) battery cable last.

- Connect the batteries
- Operate the engine and checks for leaks.

![[13900050.png]]
