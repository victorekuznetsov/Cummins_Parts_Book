### Install

Saddle Jet Piston Cooling Nozzle

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Do not use a hammer to install the piston cooling nozzles. Use hand pressure only . Using a hammer can cause component damage.

Push the piston cooling nozzle into place by hand.

Use a flat punch to push the nozzle into the recess.

![[pn900hb.png]]

J-Jet Piston Cooling Nozzle

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The crankshaft **must** be installed before the J-jet piston cooling nozzle can be installed.

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Slight bending of the piston cooling nozzles can result in severe engine damage. Replace piston cooling nozzle if it is bent or damaged during disassembly or assembly.

Install the piston cooling nozzle one cylinder at a time rotating the crankshaft as necessary for access.

Use a long extension to guide the capscrew and/or piston cooling nozzle into place.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The locator pin on the J-jet piston cooling nozzle **must** engage the locating hole in the block for proper alignment.

Tighten the capscrew.
