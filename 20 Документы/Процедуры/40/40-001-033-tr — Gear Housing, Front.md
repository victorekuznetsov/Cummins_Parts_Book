### Preparatory Steps

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Batteries can emit explosive gases. To reduce the possibility of personal injury, always ventilate the compartment before servicing the batteries. To reduce the possibility of arcing, remove the negative (-) battery cable first and attach the negative (-) battery cable last.

Disconnect the batteries.

![[13900050.png]]

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Fuel is flammable. Keep all cigarettes, flames, pilot lights, arcing equipment, and switches out of the work area and areas sharing ventilation to reduce the possibility of severe personal injury or death when working on the fuel system.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The minimum clearance required from the front gear housing face to any obstruction in the camshaft area for camshaft removal is 81.28 cm \[32 in\]. If the required amount of clearance is **not** available, the engine **must** be removed from the vehicle. Refer to Procedure [[40-000-001 — Engine Removal|000-001]].

- Remove the engine water pump (cooling fan) drive belt. Refer to Procedure [[40-008-002-tr — Drive Belt, Cooling Fan|008-002]]
- If required, remove the fan hub. Refer to Procedure [[40-008-039-tr — Fan Spacer and Pulley|008-039]]
- Remove the vibration damper. If equipped with a viscous damper, refer to [[40-001-052-tr — Vibration Damper, Viscous|Procedure 001-052]]. If equipped with a rubber damper, refer to [[40-001-051-tr — Vibration Damper, Rubber|Procedure 001-051]]
- Remove the front gear cover. Refer to Procedure [[40-001-031-tr — Gear Cover, Front|001-031]]
- Remove the rocker lever cover. Refer to Procedure [[40-003-011-tr — Rocker Lever Cover|003-011]]
- Remove the rocker levers. Refer to Procedure [[40-003-008-tr — Rocker Lever|003-008]]
- Remove the push tubes. Refer to Procedure [[40-004-014-tr — Push Rods or Tubes|004-014]]
- Remove the fuel transfer pump. Refer to Procedure [[40-005-045-tr — Fuel Lift Pump|005-045]]
- Use tappet replacement kit, Part Number 3822513, to raise the tappets. Refer to Procedure [[40-004-015-tr — Tappet|004-015]]
- Remove the camshaft. Refer to Procedure [[40-001-008-tr — Camshaft|001-008]]
- Remove the fuel injection pump. For rotary fuel injection pumps, refer to Procedure [[40-005-014-tr — Fuel Injection Pump, Rotary|005-014]]. For inline fuel injection pumps, refer to Procedure [[40-005-012-tr — Fuel Injection Pumps, In-Line|005-012]]
- Remove or disconnect driven accessories (i.e., hydraulic pump).

### Remove

Remove the four front oil pan capscrews.

![[op9csmb.png]]

Remove the gear housing capscrews.

Note the location of the gear housing capscrews as they are removed. Some of the capscrews are an internal torx fastener and **must** be installed in the same location as removed to ensure proper clearance.

![[gh9csmc.png]]

Using a feeler gauge, separate the lubricating oil pan gasket from the gear housing.

![[op9gkwd.png]]

Use a plastic hammer to loosen the front gear housing.

Remove the gear housing.

![[gh900mc.png]]

### Clean and Inspect for Reuse

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> When using solvents, acids, or alkaline materials for cleaning, follow the manufacturer's recommendations for use. Wear goggles and protective clothing to avoid personal injury.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Compressed air used for cleaning should not exceed 207 kPa \[30 psi\]. Wear protective clothing and goggles to avoid personal injury.

Use solvent to clean the gear housing.

Dry the housing with compressed air.

![[01d00071.png]]

Clean all gasket material from the cylinder block.

![[ck9gkea.png]]

Inspect the gear housing for cracks or damage.

If any damage is found, replace the gear housing.

![[01d00072.png]]

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> If the front gear housing is cracked at the accessory drive hole or fuel injection pump mounting location, double check the mounting brackets, fasteners and component installation practices.

![[gh900sa.png]]

Inspect the timing pin housing and pin for damage.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Do **not** remove the timing pin assembly unless it is damaged or leaking, or the gear housing is being replaced. Refer to Procedure [[40-001-049 — Timing Pin Housing|001-049]].

![[it900vd.png]]

If the fuel injection pump studs are damaged or being installed in a new housing, coat the threads with Loctite 242. Use two nuts locked together to remove and install the studs.

![[gh900wa.png]]

If the gear housing is being replaced, remove the engine dataplate and install it on the new gear housing; refer to Procedure [[40-001-057 — Engine Dataplate|001-057]].

![[gh900sb.png]]

### Install

For engines equipped with a paper gasket style oil pan, inspect the oil pan gasket for damage.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> If the engine is equipped with a suspended oil pan with a rubber oil pan gasket and the gasket is found to be damaged, the oil pan **must** be removed and the entire oil pan gasket replaced. Refer to [[40-007-025-tr — Lubricating Oil Pan|Procedure 007-025]]. If the engine used sealant only between the gear housing and the oil pan, remove the old sealant and apply new sealant, Part Number 3164070, to the oil pan mounting flange.

If the pan gasket is torn, it can be repaired.

Cut the torn gasket off even with the front of the cylinder block.

![[op9gkwf.png]]

Using the old gasket as a pattern, cut the front section of a new gasket to the same size.

![[op9gkwg.png]]

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The gear housing must be installed within 10 minutes of applying the sealant.

Clean the sealing surfaces.

Coat the new gasket on both sides with sealant, Part Number 3164067.

Be sure there is a bead of sealant at the intersecting joint of the cylinder block, oil pan, and gear housing.

![[01d00340.png]]

Use common thread or very fine wire to hold the new gasket splice in position as illustrated.

![[op9gkwi.png]]

Check the fit of the new gear housing to cylinder block gasket. It may be necessary to trim the bottom edges of the gasket before installation.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> When properly trimmed, the gear housing gasket **must** be even with the lubricating oil pan gasket when installed.

![[op9gkwj.png]]

Position the gasket on the alignment dowels.

Use guide pins, M8 x 1.25 x 50, to assist in aligning the gasket and gear housing. Make sure to remove the guide pins after alignment.

Apply a bead of sealant, Part Number 3164067, at the intersecting joint of the cylinder block, oil pan, and gear housing.

![[op9gkha.png]]

Carefully install the gear housing.

Make sure the gasket is still in place.

Install and tighten the mounting capscrews.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 24 n•m [18 ft-lb]

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> If a new front gear housing and/or timing pin assembly is installed, the timing pin assembly **must** be accurately located. Refer to Procedure [[40-001-049 — Timing Pin Housing|001-049]].

![[gh900hc.png]]

Start the oil pan capscrews into the holes **not** being used to tie the gasket in place.

![[op9csmc.png]]

Remove the thread or wire holding the gasket in place.

![[op9gkwl.png]]

Install the remaining two capscrews.

Tighten the capscrews.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 24 n•m [18 ft-lb]

![[op9csha.png]]

### Finishing Steps

- Install the camshaft. Refer to Procedure [[40-001-008-tr — Camshaft|001-008]]
- Release the tappets. Refer to Procedure 004-015
- Install the push rods. Refer to [[40-004-014-tr — Push Rods or Tubes|Procedure 004-014]]
- Install the rocker levers. Refer to [[40-003-008-tr — Rocker Lever|Procedure 003-008]]
- Adjust the valve lash. Refer to [[40-003-004-tr — Overhead Set|Procedure 003-004]]
- Install the rocker lever cover. Refer to [[40-003-011-tr — Rocker Lever Cover|Procedure 003-011]]
- Install the fuel transfer pump. Refer to Procedure [[40-005-045-tr — Fuel Lift Pump|005-045]]
- Install the fuel injection pump. For rotary fuel injection pumps, refer to Procedure [[40-005-014-tr — Fuel Injection Pump, Rotary|005-014]]. For inline fuel injection pumps, refer to Procedure [[40-005-012-tr — Fuel Injection Pumps, In-Line|005-012]].
- Install or connect driven accessories (i.e., hydraulic pump)
- Install the front gear cover. Refer to Procedure [[40-001-031-tr — Gear Cover, Front|001-031]]
- Install the vibration damper. If equipped with a viscous damper, refer to [[40-001-052-tr — Vibration Damper, Viscous|Procedure 001-052]]. If equipped with a rubber damper, refer to [[40-001-051-tr — Vibration Damper, Rubber|Procedure 001-051]]
- If removed, install the fan hub. Refer to Procedure [[40-008-039-tr — Fan Spacer and Pulley|008-039]]
- Install the engine water pump drive belt. Refer to Procedure [[40-008-002-tr — Drive Belt, Cooling Fan|008-002]].

![[ck800wa.png]]

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Batteries can emit explosive gases. To reduce the possibility of personal injury, always ventilate the compartment before servicing the batteries. To reduce the possibility of arcing, remove the negative (-) battery cable first and attach the negative (-) battery cable last.

Connect the batteries.

Operate the engine at idle for 5 to 10 minutes and check for leaks or loose parts.
