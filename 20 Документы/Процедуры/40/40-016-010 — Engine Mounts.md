### Inspect for Reuse

Inspect all rubber-cushioned mounts for cracks or damage.

Inspect all mounting brackets for cracks or damaged bolt holes.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Damaged engine mounts and brackets can cause engine misalignment and driveline component damage, and result in vibration complaints.
