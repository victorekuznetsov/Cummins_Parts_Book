# Engine Run-in (Chassis Dynamometer)

### Test

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Refer to [[100-014-002 — Engine Testing (Chassis Dynamometer)|Procedure 014-002]] before operating the engine to reduce the possibility of internal component damage.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Refer to [[40-014-005-tr — Engine Testing (Engine Dynamometer)|Procedure 014-005]] for general operating procedures and safety precautions.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Operate the vehicle in a gear that produces a road speed of 90 to 95 km/h \[56 to 59 mph\].

![[oi902vk.png]]

Operate the engine at 1200 rpm and 25 percent of torque peak load until the water temperature reaches 70°C \[158°F\].

![[14900042.png]]

Operate the engine at 1200 rpm and 40 percent of torque peak load for two minutes. Check the gauges, and record the readings.

![[oi902vm.png]]

Operate the engine at 1600 rpm and 65 percent of torque peak load for five minutes. Check the gauges, and record the readings.

![[14900041.png]]

Operate the engine at torque peak rpm and full load for 4 minutes. Check the gauges and record the readings.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Refer to the engine data sheet for the torque peak rpm of the engine model being tested.

![[oi902vo.png]]

Operate the engine at rated speed (rpm) and full load for four minutes. Check the gauges, and record the readings. Compare the readings to those published on the appropriate engine data sheet.

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Do not shut off the engine immediately after the run-in is completed. Allow the engine to cool by operating it at low idle for a minimum of three minutes to reduce the possibility of internal component damage.

![[oi902vp.png]]

Make sure all instrumentation is removed before removing the vehicle from the dynamometer.
