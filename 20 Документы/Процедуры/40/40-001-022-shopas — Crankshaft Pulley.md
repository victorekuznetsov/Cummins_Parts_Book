### Install

Install the six capscrews that hold the crankshaft pulley to the nose of the crankshaft.

Tighten crankshaft pulley capscrews.

| **Torque Value:** | Step 1 | 50 n.m \[37 ft-lb\] |
|---|---|---|

|  | Step 2 | Rotate 90 degrees |
|---|---|---|
