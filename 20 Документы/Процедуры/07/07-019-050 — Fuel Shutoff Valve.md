# Fuel Shutoff Valve
**Клапан отсечки топлива**

### General Information

The fuel shutoff valve is mounted on top of the fuel filter.

Check the valve to make sure it has the correct voltage rating.

The voltage rating and part number are stamped on the case of the valve.

![[19901362.png]]

### Initial Check

Check the resistance of the fuel shutoff valve coil.

Disconnect the voltage supply wire (long post).

Select the resistance function on the multimeter.

Touch one of the multimeter leads to the fuel shutoff valve terminal.

Touch the other multimeter lead to a good, clean surface on the engine block.

Measure the resistance.

The table below shows the resistance specifications for the solenoid.

If the resistance is **not** correct, replace the valve.

The resistance of the solenoid increases if it is hot.

The resistance provided below is for a cold solenoid:

| Voltage Rating | Resistance |
|---|---|
| 12 VDC | 6 to 10 ohms |
| 24 VDC | 26 to 40 ohms |

![[19901366.png]]

### Remove

1. Disconnect the fuel inlet tubing from the fuel shutoff valve.
2. Disconnect the voltage supply wire and the ground wire from the fuel shutoff solenoid.

![[19901363.png]]

Remove the two capscrews, lock washers, and washers securing the fuel shutoff valve to the fuel filter.

Remove the fuel shutoff valve.

![[19901364.png]]

### Inspect for Reuse

Make sure all wire connection nuts are tight, whether a wire is attached or **not**.

![[19901417.png]]

Use a wire brush to clean corroded terminal posts.

![[19901418.png]]

Make sure the shutoff valve coil is the correct voltage.

The coil voltage and part number are cast into the terminal connection end of the coil.

![[19901362.png]]

Remove the four mounting capscrews securing the fuel shutoff valve solenoid to the fuel shutoff valve.

Remove the solenoid housing, fuel shield, spring washer, valve disc, actuator disc, and actuator housing.

Discard the o-rings.

![[19901365.png]]

Use mineral spirits to clean all parts **except** the solenoid.

Do **not** get solution on the solenoid. Clean the solenoid with a dry cloth. Use 200-grit emery cloth on a flat surface to polish the solenoid surface.

Check the fuel shield, spring washer, valve disc, actuator disc, and actuator housing for dirt, bonding separation, corrosion, cracks, or wear.

Inspect the disc and fuel shutoff valve for excessive wear.

Replace any parts if necessary.

![[gr8vaka.png]]

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Clean all components before assembly. Dirty components can cause the engine to not shut down.

Assemble the shutoff valve as follows:

Install a new o-ring (6) between the spacer (7) and the fuel shutoff valve body (8).

Install the spacer (7) with the o-ring groove toward the coil.

Install the actuator disc (5) with the cup side toward the solenoid.

Install the spring washer (3) with the cup side toward the solenoid.

Align the actuator disc (5), spacer (7), and valve disc (4) on the shutoff valve body (8).

Install a new o-ring (6) between the spacer (7) and the actuator disc (5).

Put the spring washer (3) on the valve disc (4), with the cavity side positioned upward, in a position around the valve locator.

The solenoid **must** be orientated with the electrical supply connection post on the bottom.

Install the fuel shield (2) and solenoid (1) on the shutoff valve body (8).

Install new capscrew o-rings and tighten the capscrews.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 8 n•m [71 in-lb]

![[19800921.png]]

### Install

Install the fuel shutoff valve and tighten the two capscrews.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 14 n•m [124 in-lb]

![[19901364.png]]

Connect the fuel tubing to the fuel shutoff valve.

Connect the voltage supply and ground wires to the fuel shutoff solenoid.

![[19901363.png]]
