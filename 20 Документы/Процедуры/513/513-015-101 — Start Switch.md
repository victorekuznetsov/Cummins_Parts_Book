- Electrical test lead, Part Number 3823993.

#### Additional Service Items

- No additional service items required.

### General Information

The start switch is typically located on the helm panel.

PS102

The switch is pressed and held to start the engine. A single quick press of the start switch will engage the starter but will **not** start the engine.

PS103

A single quick press up (2) of the start / stop switch (1) will engage the starter, and start the engine.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Unless the engine is equipped with starter lock out capability, activating the start switch while the engine is running will engage the starter and may result in engine damage.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The switch and adapter harness is considered a switch assembly, therefore the Cummins® part number is **only** on the switch's adapter wiring harness label. If the label does **not** have a Cummins® part number, refer to the original equipment manufacturer (OEM) service manual for removal and installation information.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The OEM may choose to **not** use the mounting bezel or gasket provided with the switch.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The Cummins®-supplied switch is shown for PS102 and PS103 systems.

### Preparatory Steps

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Batteries can emit explosive gases. To reduce the possibility of personal injury, always ventilate the compartment before servicing the batteries. To reduce the possibility of arcing, remove the negative (-) battery cable first and attach the negative (-) battery cable last.

- Disconnect the batteries. See equipment manufacturer service information.

### Remove

Disconnect the helm wiring harness from the switch adapter wiring harness.

Note the harness routing and location of harness mounting points for installation.

![[15e00118.png]]

Remove the switch and adapter wiring harness from the vessel station panel.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The switch should **not** be disconnected from adapter wiring harness. If removed, reference the harness label naming and symbol on the switch face to be sure the switch is connected to the proper adapter wiring harness.

![[15e00119.png]]

### Clean and Inspect for Reuse

Inspect the switch, harness, mounting bezel, and gasket.

Replace if damaged.

The OEM may choose to **not** use the mounting bezel or gasket provided with the switch.

![[15e00120.png]]

### Install

If equipped, install the bezel and gasket to the switch.

Install switch and adapter wiring harness onto the vessel station panel. Be sure the orientation of the switch is correct.

![[15e00119.png]]

Connect the helm wiring harness to the switch adapter wiring harness.

![[15e00118.png]]

### Finishing Steps

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Batteries can emit explosive gases. To reduce the possibility of personal injury, always ventilate the compartment before servicing the batteries. To reduce the possibility of arcing, remove the negative (-) battery cable first and attach the negative (-) battery cable last.

- Connect the batteries. See equipment manufacturer service information.
- Perform system test to verify proper function. [[513-015-047 — Final Verification|Refer to Procedure 015-047 in Section 15.]]
