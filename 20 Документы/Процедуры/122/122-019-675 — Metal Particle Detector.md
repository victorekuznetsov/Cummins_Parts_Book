### General Information

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The following steps for the removal, inspection, maintenance, and installation of the metal particle detector switch are for engines with an metal particle detector switch installed in the front gear housing and/or fuel pump.

This procedure is to provide removal, inspection, cleaning, reuse criteria, and installation procedure for this warning system.

![[19r99842.png]]

### Remove

Gear train:

Disconnect the harness from the gear train metal particle detector switch located on the bottom of the gear housing.

![[19e02118.png]]

Remove the switch from the gear housing.

![[19e02119.png]]

Fuel pump accessory drive:

Disconnect the harness from the fuel pump metal particle detector switch located on the bottom of the fuel pump accessory drive.

Remove the switch from the accessory drive.

![[19r99843.png]]

### Inspect for Reuse

Inspect the metal particle detector switch for debris and damage.

If there is debris on the switch, clean the switch.

If the sensor is chipped, cracked, extruded, or otherwise damaged, replace the sensor with a new sensor.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> It is recommended the metal particle detector switch be removed and inspected at 500 hour maintenance intervals.

![[19e02120.png]]

### Install

Gear train:

Install the gear train metal particle detector switch into the mounting hole at the bottom of the gear housing.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 88 n•m [65 ft-lb]

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Verify the o-ring in the metal particle detector switch is lubricated with clean engine oil before assembly. Also, verify the plastic sleeve at the harness connection point is removed before assembly.

![[19e02119.png]]

Connect both gear train metal particle detector switch harnesses to each bank of the engine.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 45 n•m [33 ft-lb]

![[19e02118.png]]

Fuel Pump accessory drive:

Install the fuel pump accessory drive metal particle detector switch into the mounting hole at the bottom of the accessory drive.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 88 n•m [65 ft-lb]

Connect metal particle detector switch harness.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 45 n•m [33 ft-lb]

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Verify the o-ring in the metal particle detector switch is lubricated with clean engine oil before assembly. Also, verify the plastic sleeve at the harness connection point is removed before assembly.

![[19r99843.png]]
