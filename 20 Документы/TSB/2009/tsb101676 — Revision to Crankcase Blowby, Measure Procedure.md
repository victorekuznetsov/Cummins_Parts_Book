## Revision to Crankcase Blowby, Measure Procedure

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Service/Parts Topic updates common Procedure 014-010 Crankcase Blowby, Measure. Updates were made to the Crankcase Blowby, Measure chart in the General Information that will be used on all Midrange diesel engines.

Blowby specifications have been added for new engines, rebuilt engines, and for troubleshooting.
