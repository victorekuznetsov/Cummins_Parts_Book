## New Fuel Filter Procedures

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Service/Parts Topic revises procedure Fuel Filter (Spin-On Type), dividing the information into three separate procedures:

- Procedure 006-015 (Fuel Filter (Spin-On Type)) is for the fuel filter on engines with mechanically actuated injectors.
- Procedure 006-075 (Fuel Filter Stage 1) is for the stage 1 filter on engines with electronically actuated injectors.
- Procedure 006-076 (Fuel Filter Stage 2) is for the stage 2 filter on engines with electronically actuated injectors.
