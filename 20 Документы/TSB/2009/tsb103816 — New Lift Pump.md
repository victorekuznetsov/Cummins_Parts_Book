## New Lift Pump

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Service/Parts Topic introduces a new lift pump for the QST30 engine and supersedes Service/Parts Topic 09T5-4.

The new lift pump has a hardened gerotor with an improved tolerance for debris. The new lift pump, Part Number 4975617, went into production with engine serial number (ESN) First 37240363.

| Old Part Number | New Part Number | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 4975518 | 4975617 | PUMP, FUEL TRANSFER |

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> This is a product improvement and is **not** subject to campaign.
