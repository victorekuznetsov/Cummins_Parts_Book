## Revision, Symptom Tree t105, Lubricating Oil Pressure Low

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Service/Parts Topic revises symptom tree T105 (Lubricating Oil Pressure Low) to include additional steps for the instant loss of lubricating engine oil. The additional steps provide a list of components that **must** be checked when this condition occurs.
