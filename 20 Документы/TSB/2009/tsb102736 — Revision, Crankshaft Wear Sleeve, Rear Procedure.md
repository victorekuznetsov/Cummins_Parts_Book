## Revision, Crankshaft Wear Sleeve, Rear Procedure

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Service/Parts topic revises the torque value for the rear seal carrier mounting bolts in Procedure 001-067 (Crankshaft Wear Sleeve, Rear) in Section 1. The revised torque reduces the likelihood of rear seal carrier oil leaks.

This change went into effect at Cummins Consolidated Diesel in July of 2003. The ESN first was 46773606. This change went into effect at Darlington in February of 2003. The ESN first was 21552187.
