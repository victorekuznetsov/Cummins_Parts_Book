## Maintenance Schedule Revision

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Service/Parts Topic revises Procedure 102-002 (Maintenance Schedule) in Section 2 of the above listed manuals, to add the Radiator Pressure Cap maintenance check. The manuals are being revised to make certain the radiator pressure cap is functioning correctly. The system **must** maintain pressure to make sure of proper engine performance.
