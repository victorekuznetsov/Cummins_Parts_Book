## Engine Wiring Harness Routing

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

**Product Affected**

- QSK23 CM500

**Issue**

Engine wiring harness interference with the ECM cover plate.

Symptom:

- Engine wiring harness rubs through the ECM cover plate.

Root Cause:

- Engine wiring harness routing not robust.

**Verification/Confirmation**

Wiring harness interferes with the ECM cover plate.

**Resolution**

Add wire tie to hold the harness away from ECM cover plate to prevent interference. There should be a gap of at least 25 mm (1 in) between the cover plate and the wiring harness.

![[19h00106.png]]

Figure 1, Wiring Harness Installed with Wire Ties

**Service Instructions**

Wire tie the harness to prevent interference with the ECM cover plate.
