**Issue**

The supplier was changed for the lifting eye and rocker levers. During this change the flange thickness was changed from 5 mm to 2.4 mm. This has led to the flange yielding during torquing. The bolts, [[3089194]] and [[3089193]], should only be used if they have the 5 mm flange. The 2.4 mm flange **must** be quarantined and direction followed for the parts alert mentioned below.

**Verification/Confirmation**

The good bolts can be seen visually as shown below or they can be measured using suitable measuring equipment.

![[17r00937.png]]

Figure 1, Good and Bad Bolt

**Resolution**

Bolts with the correct flange size have been received in Daventry. They are currently being sent to the distribution facilities Following the parts alert all, further parts received should be of the 5 mm flange.
