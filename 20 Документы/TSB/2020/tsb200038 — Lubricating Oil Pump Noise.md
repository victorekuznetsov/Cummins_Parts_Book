**Issue**

A new heavy-duty lubricating oil pump, Part Number [[5532492]], is experiencing unusual noise characteristics.

This issue may be experienced on engines equipped with part option LP1745.

Symptom:

- A buzzing noise originating from the front of the lubricating oil pan.
- Buzzing noise from the front of the lubricating oil pan is usually present at idle with cold lubricating oil but can also be present at higher engine rpm and at warmer lubricating oil temperatures in some cases.

Root Cause:

- Buzzing noise from the front of the lubricating oil pan is caused by a pulsation frequency from the lubricating oil pump.

**Verification**

- Operate the engine at idle:

**Resolution**

- No action is to be taken.
- This buzzing noise will **not** harm the engine.
- A solution is being developed and is **not** available yet. This document will be updated when a solution is found.
