**Description of Change**

This TSB Replaces TSB120148 and does **not** apply to K19, KTA19, QSK19 horizontal rail engines, as these engines use a different cylinder head. New valve spring part number and cylinder head part numbers have been released for ReCon® and Service. The new spring will be made available as an individual component or included as part of a full cylinder head assembly.

**Reason for Change**

New valve spring part number and cylinder head part numbers have been released for ReCon® and Service.

**Customer Communication**

- Follow existing QuickServe® Online (QSOL) procedures for changing valve springs or cylinder heads.
- New valve springs contain damping coils making them visibly different in form to the old valve spring.
- New valve springs have a yellow orientation instruction and arrow printed on the spring itself.

**Service Instructions**

- Follow existing QSOL procedures for changing valve springs or cylinder heads.

**Part Compatibility**

Full compatibility, the new valve spring will fit the existing part number cylinder heads.

**Part Identification**

- New valves springs are identified with yellow orientation arrow and instruction ‘TOP'. This is printed onto the valve spring.
- New valve springs are visibly different to the existing part number valve spring due to the addition of damping coils which are to be orientated cylinder head side of the assembly.
- Complete functionality of the valve spring will be lost if **not** orientated correctly.
