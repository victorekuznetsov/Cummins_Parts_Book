## New Pre-Lube Pressure Switch

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

**Product Affected**

- QST30 CM552
- QST30 CM2350 T101
- QST30 CM2350 T103C

**Issue Summary**

Symptom:

- Engine will **not** start
- Lubricating oil leaking through the pre-lube pressure switch

Root Cause:

- Engine **not** starting- soldering the wires to the internal electrical contacts of the pre lube pressure switch.
- Oil leaks- O-ring and diaphragm hardening because of unsuitable materials.

**Verification**

- Visually inspect the pre-lube pressure switch to identify the lubricating oil leak through the vent hole on the switch.
- Use multimeter to measure resistance of pre-lube switch when pressure is applied. Variable resistance or infinite resistance indicates poor wire connection.

**Resolution**

- If customer is experiencing multiple pre-lube pressure switch malfunctions, replace malfunctioning switch with new pre-lube pressure switch.

**Service Instructions**

- For pre lube pressure switch removal and installation instructions, see corresponding Service Manual. Reference Procedure 007-086 in Section 7.
- Before connecting the pre-lube switch harness to the engine harness, use quick-dry electrical contact cleaner, Part Number 3824510, to remove all dirt and moisture from the pre-lube switch connector and the engine wiring harness connector.
