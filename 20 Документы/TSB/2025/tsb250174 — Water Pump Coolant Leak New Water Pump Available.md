## Water Pump Coolant Leak: New Water Pump Available

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

**Product Affected**

- QSK23 CM2250 K109
- QSK23 CM2350 K127
- QSK23 CM500

**Issue Summary**

Symptom:

- External coolant leak from water pump weep hole

Root Cause:

- Contaminants in coolant cause excessive wear on water pump seal creating leak path
- Impeller design creating excessive back pressure on water pump seal bellows creating leak path

**Verification**

If an engine experiences an external coolant leak from the water pump weep hole and is using the existing water pump part numbers listed in Table 1, reference the resolution section below.

**Resolution**

- A new water pump assembly is available which uses improved sealing elements and impeller design.
- If an affected water pump part number is found leaking, install the new water pump listed in Table 1. See the corresponding Service Manual. Reference Procedure 008-062 in Section 8.
- **Never** rebuild the existing water pump part number using the new components. The entire water pump assembly **must** be replaced.
