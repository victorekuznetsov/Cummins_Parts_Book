### Contents

**Product Affected**

- K50
- QSK50 CM850 MCRS
- QSK50 CM2150 MCRS
- QSK50 CM2350 K108
- QSK50 CM2350 K130

**Issue Summary**

Symptom:

- Crankshaft adapter capscrews have been found broken upon inspection and during engine rebuilds.

**Resolution**

- **Always** replace crankshaft adapter capscrews during rebuilds and mid-life events.
