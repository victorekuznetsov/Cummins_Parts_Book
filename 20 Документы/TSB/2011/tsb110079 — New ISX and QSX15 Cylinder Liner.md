## New ISX and QSX15 Cylinder Liner

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This document introduces a new ISX and QSX15 cylinder liner. This new liner has the thickness of the top press fit ledge reduced. This reduced press fit ledge allows for better cooling of the top ring reversal area, which improves the liner's resistance to wear in this area.

![[01a00283.png]]

Old Cylinder Liner, Part Number 4101507 and New Cylinder Liner, Part Number 2882764

The new liner is completely backwards compatible and can be mixed and matched in an engine.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> This change does **not** affect the groove top cylinder liner, Part Numbers 3682025 and 4025192. Groove top liners will still be available for service repair.
