## Cylinder Block Service Kits

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This document was originally released between 1994 and 2001. It has been added to QSOL for informational purposes

This document identifies the correct service cylinder bloc kits required to replace a C series cylinder block. The cylinder block used in **pre-1991** engines, **part number 3802265**, has been obsoleted and replaced by a cylinder block with thick walled cylinder liner bores, **part number 3802549**. Use the **part number 3802559** cylinder block as a replacement for engines produced during **1991** and after.

**Pre-1991 Engines**

Pre-1991 engines were built with single piston cooling nozzles per cylinder and a lower capacity lubrication system. The cylinder block now in production has dual piston cooling nozzles per cylinder and a higher capacity lubrication system. This difference requires the use of the cylinder liner kit and service cylinder block kit listed below when replacing the cylinder block on engines built prior to **engine serial number (ESN) 44487830 (CDC), ESN 21083000 (DEP), and ESN 30417102 (BZL).**

| **Description** | **Part Number** | **Number Required** |
|---|---|---|
| Cylinder Liner Kit | 3802407 | 6 |
| Cylinder Block Kit | 3802549 | 1 |

3802549 includes:

| **Description** | **Part Number** | **Quantity** |
|---|---|---|
| Main/thrust Bearing | 3802210 | 1 |
| Cylinder Block and Hardware | 3802559 | 1 |
| Cylinder Head Capscrew | 3917728 | 14 |
| Cylinder Head Capscrew | [[3917729]] | 12 |
| Lube Oil Pressure Regulator | 3918532 | 1 |
| Lubricating Oil Pump | 3926201 | 1 |

**1991 Engines with Thin-Wall Cylinder Block**

Engines with the following ESN's were built with dual piston cooling nozzles per cylinder, high capacity lubrication system and thin-wall cylinder block.

**ESN 44487830 to 44706125 (CDC)ESN 21083000 to 21123435 (DEPESN 30417102 to 30417119 (BZL)**

Use the following cylinder liner kit and cylinder block kit when replacing the cylinder block.

| **Description** | **Part Number** | **Number Required** |
|---|---|---|
| Cylinder Liner Kit | 3802407 | 6 |
| Cylinder Block Kit | 3802559 | 1 |

3802559 includes:

| **Description** | Part Number | Quantity |
|---|---|---|
| Hardware Kit | 3802367 | 1 |
| Cylinder Block | 3926145 | 1 |

**1991 Engines With Thick-Wall Cylinder Block**

Use the following cylinder block kit when replacing the cylinder block. The cylinder liners **can** be reused **if** they meet the reuse guidelines.

| **Description** | **Part Number** | **Number Required** |
|---|---|---|
| Cylinder Block Kit | 3802559 | 1 |

3802559 includes:

| **Description** | **Part Number** | **Quantity** |
|---|---|---|
| Hardware Kit | 3802367 | 1 |
| Cylinder Block | 3926145 | 1 |
