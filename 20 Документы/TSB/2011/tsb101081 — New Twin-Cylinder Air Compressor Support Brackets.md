## New Twin-Cylinder Air Compressor Support Brackets

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This document introduces new air compressor support brackets for K19 and QSK19 engines that are equipped with the twin-cylinder air compressor, Part Number 3103409.

The new brackets, Part Numbers 2888623 and 2888626, obsolete and supersede the previous versions, Part Numbers 4081295 and 4081296. These brackets are being introduced as a product improvement. The new design is a two-piece, rigid-mounted bracket design. This design eliminates the wear that was seen on the previous brackets caused by movement between the two bracket pieces. The difference between the two bracket designs are shown in Figure 1, Old Design, and Figure 2, New Design.

The parts being removed from options CP4073 and CP4076 are listed in Table 1. Listed in Table 2 are the parts being added to options CP4073 and CP4076. These are also the parts required to upfit an engine from the previous brackets to the improved bracket design.

The new bracket design utilizes the same mounting holes as the fuel filter options that were mounted directly below the old air compressor support bracket. These options are listed in Table 3. In order to install the new air compressor support bracket design, these fuel filter options **must** be moved. The fuel filter option may be remote mounted or moved to the hand hole cover, as shown in Figure 3. It may be necessary to rotate the fuel filter housing 180 degrees to allow proper routing of the fuel pump inlet hose. Table 4 lists fuel line part numbers and lengths that can be ordered for the new fuel filter mounting locations.

This change took effect in production on October 21, 2010. Any engines built after this date will have been built with the new compressor support bracket design.
