## New Water Pump O-ring Adapter

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This document announces the release of a new o-ring adapter for QSK38/50/60 MCRS water pumps. The new o-ring adapter obsoletes the previous part number. A dimensional change was made in the o-ring groove to improve the sealing properties of the joint at low temperatures.

This change was made to all dual impellor water pumps fitted to QSK38/50/60 MCRS engines, water pump options WP 6704, WP 6705, WP 6707, WP 6708, WP 6713, WP 6714, and WP 6718. The ESN first for this change was 33183776, built on November 8, 2010.
