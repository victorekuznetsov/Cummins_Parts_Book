## STC, HVT Tappet Assemblies and Rebuild Kits (T381)

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This document was originally released between 1994 and 2001. It has been added to QSOL for informational purposes

This document contains all current information on tappet assembly part numbers for the new "mushroom" design. This new STC tappet design was implemented for increased durability. Eleven different STC/HVT tappet assemblies that have been produced by Cummins and ReCon® have been superseded to 2 current part numbers (See Table 1).

All thin socket assemblies/kits have a lower durability and can **not** be rebuilt with the new mushroom kit, Part No. [[3804700]]. All thin socket kits and assemblies are obsoleted.

The fiel has the option of purchasing ReCon® rebuilt mushroom tappets to replace the thin design as shown above.

Thick socket rebuild kits are obsoleted, but they can be rebuilt with the new mushroom kit, Part No. [[3804700]].

All current production engine models with STC, HVT tappet assemblies have the current "mushroom" tappet design.
