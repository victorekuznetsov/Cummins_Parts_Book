## MidRange Engine Block Serial Number Location

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

The purpose of this document is to describe the location of the engine block stamped serial number. The location of the engine serial number may vary, depending upon the engine plant at which the engine was manufactured. This document covers all of the Cummins® B, C, and L series MidRange engine models and the associated production plants.
