## Calibration Issue on October 2011 INCAL™ DVD

### Core Issue

Calibration L21750.06, on the October 2011 INCAL™ DVD, used in GROVE™ truck mounted cranes, has been found to cause an engine surge when the unit is operated from the cab on the superstructure.

### Confirmation

The engine will surge when operated from the crane operator's controls in the cab on the superstructure. The surge will be seen when operating the engine at 1800 RPM or higher and will result in an engine speed oscillation of 75 to 150 rpm.

### Resolution

Calibrate the electronic control module (ECM) with calibration L21750.07 or newer from QuickServe™ Online, or the December 2011 INCAL™ DVD, when it becomes available.
