## Injector Set Frequency

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Remove topic 93T6-18 and **replace** with this topic. The Fixed Time(s) in Table 1 have been corrected.

This topic is being issued to highlight the recent change in the recommended VALVE AND INJECTOR SET AND RESET INTERVALS for all K engines. This recommendation became effective July 1991 when issued in the K19 Operation and Maintenance Manual, Bulletin [[3666013 — K19 Industrial and Marine Operation and Maintenance Manual\|3666013]]-00, and the K38 and K50 Operation and Maintenance Manual, Bulletin No. [[3810497 — K38, K50, QSK38 and QSK50 Operation and Maintenance Manual\|3810497]]-01. These recommendations continue in the July and August 1993 update of these Operation and Maintenance Manuals (K19, Bulletin No. [[3666013 — K19 Industrial and Marine Operation and Maintenance Manual\|3666013]]-01; K38 and K50, Bulletin No. 3810491-02). These manuals state:

Cummins® K engines in most applications will **not** experience significant valve and injector train wear after an initial adjustment is made at 1,500 hours. (Reference Table 1 for the injector set methods and values per injector.) After this adjustment, Cummin®s recommends the valves and injectors **not** be adjusted again until the 6,000 hours or 2 year injector calibration interval. Because injector train hardware is typically mixed between cylinders during injector replacement, Cummins® recommends to adjust valves and injectors at 1,500 hours after all injector replacements.

If the valve or injector adjustment is checked during troubleshooting or before the recommended maintenance interval, adjustment is **not** required if measurements are within the recheck limits. (Reference Table 1.)

#### Simplified Injector Setting Recommendations

1. Install the injectors, then adjust the crossheads, valves and injectors.
2. Operate the engine 1,500 hours and re-adjust the valves and injectors.
3. Operate the engine an additional 4,500 hours (6,000 hours on injectors), then clean and calibrate or replace the injectors. Start with Step 1 again.

Fixed time engine injectors are set by adjusting the travel using a dial indicator. This is the **only** acceptable method for adjusting these injectors.

STV and HVT injectors **must** be adjusted using the Outer Base Circle (OBC) method. Use this method rather than previous methods because:

1. It is much easier
2. There is less chance of errors
3. STC injectors do **not** have to be removed to set the total travel.

The injector set methods and values for the different injectors are specified below.

| Table 1: Crosshead, Valve and Injector Settings and Recheck Limits |  |  |
|---|---|---|
| Crosshead, Valve, Injector Type | Normal Setting | Reset Limits |
| Fixed Time (K38 and K50) | 7.811 mm \[0.3075 in\] Travel | 7.75 to 7.87 mm \[ 0.305 to 0.310 in\] Travel |
| Fixed Time (K19) | 7.72 mm \[0.304 in\] Travel | 7.67 to 7.77 mm \[0.302 to 0.306 in\] Travel |
| STC and HVT (OBC) | 10 N•m \[89 in-lb\] | 10 N•m \[89 in-lb\] |

Injector adjustment sequence charts for the OBC adjustment method are included in this topic. These charts can also be found in the Operation and Maintenance Manuals listed above, Service Parts Topic 92T6-2, and in Bulletin 3666049 (a plasticized card).

If problems of injector or valve train wear occur, the previous recommendation of setting valves and injectors every 1,500 hours is to be used.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Two full engine rotations are required to adjust the valves and injectors on K19, K38, and K50 engines.

**Determine the Cylinder in Position for Valve Set**

The crosshead and valves can be adjusted on a cylinder that has all of the valves closed. Reference the tables on the following pages.

The same procedure can be used to determine the cylinder in position for valve setting for both methods (travel method or OBC method). However, different injectors will be adjusted. If the rocker lever assemblies have been removed, use this step to determine the cylinder to set.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> All adjusting screws **must** be loose on all cylinders, and the push rod **must** remain in alignment.

Perform this step on both cylinders to be checked.

![[rh400ub.png]]

Hold both rocker levers against the crossheads. Turn the adjusting screws until they touch the push rods. Turn the locknuts until they touch the levers.

Compare the height of the adjusting screws above the locknut. The cylinder with the adjusting screws that are nearest to the same height has both valves closed.

The push rods will be close to the same height above the top of the rocker lever housing on the cylinder that has both valves closed.

![[rh800sa.png]]

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> One engine mode, KTTA19-G/GSGC2, CPL1170, contains a unique camshaft that creates a noticeable difference in the height of the valve adjusting screws. When the valves are properly adjusted on CPL1170 **only**, the exhaust valve adjusting screw will have approximately one thread visible **above** the top of the locknut. The intake valve adjusting screw will have approximately 3 threads visible **above** the top of the adjusting screw.

If the rocker levers have **not** been removed, wiggle the valve rocker levers on the two cylinders in question. The crossheads and valves on the cylinder where both levers feel loose are ready to adjust.

Use the chart to determine the injector that is ready to adjust.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Adjustment can begin on any valve set mark.

**OUTER BASE CIRCLE (OBC) INJECTOR AND VALVE SETTING PROCEDURE FOR K SERIES ENGINES**

These charts **must** be used when using the OBC Injector Set Method on K engines that have STC and HVT injectors. Details of the OBC set procedure are specified in SPT 92T6-2. The setting specifications are:

| **Injectors:** | 10 N•m \[89 in-lb\] |
|---|---|
| **Intake Valve Lash:** | 0.036 mm \[0.014 in\] |
| **Exhaust Valve Lash:** | 0.069 mm \[0.027 in\] |

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> When setting the injectors, the rocker lever adjusting screw **must** to be torqued to 11 N•m \[97 in-lb\], loosen the adjusting screw, then torque the adjusting screw **again** to the 10 N•m \[89 in-lb\] specification.

PT (Type D) fixed time injectors **must not** to be adjusted using the OBC method. Fixed time PT (Type D) injectors **must** be adjusted using travel method.
