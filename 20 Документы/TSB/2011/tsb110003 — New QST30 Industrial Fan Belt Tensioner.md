## New QST30 Industrial Fan Belt Tensioner

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This document announces changes to the industrial fan drive options for the QST30 engine. These engine options were originally introduced in Technical Service Bulletin 100650.

The previous design used a torsion spring to provide system tension and a control rod to prevent idler pulley kick back during hard decelerations of the engine. This option change reintroduces a linear spring design in order to increase overall system tension and reduce the possibility of belt walking. A similar design was used on Industrial engines prior to November 2007. The new design removes the torsion spring; however, it maintains the more robust pivot bushing assembly. This pivot assembly has a higher capability than the pivot design used prior to November 2007.

The options affected by this change are listed below. The engine serial number (ESN) first for these options is 37248030, effective 11 February 2011.

- FA5079
- FA5085
- FA5086
- FA5087
- FA5088
- FA5090
- FA5091
- FA5092

The spacers and washers included in each option as described in Tables 3 through 9 are used to control the system's belt tension. The washers and spacers should be installed as shown in Figure 1 and the top nut tghten until the spacer bottoms out on the bracket. This will make sure the belt tension remains in the desired range.
