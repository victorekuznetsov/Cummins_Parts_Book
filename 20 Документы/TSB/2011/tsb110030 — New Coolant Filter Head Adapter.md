## New Coolant Filter Head Adapter

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This document introduces a new coolant filter head adapter for filters on all QST30, K/QSK38, QSK45, K/QSK50, QSK60, QSK60G, and QSK78 engines. The new head adapter is 3 mm \[0.12 in\] longer than the previous part to allow for increased thread engagement. This part was introduced to reduce the possiblity of coolant filters becoming loose on the QSK78 engine. As this is a common part, it is being updated across all engines.

| New Part Number | Old Part Number |
|---|---|
| [[3643997]] | 3175768 |

The ESN first for the new coolant filter head adapter is 33183243 for QSK60G engines and 66301530 for QSK78 engines.

All engines produced after 4 October 2010 are equipped with the longer coolant filter head adapter.
