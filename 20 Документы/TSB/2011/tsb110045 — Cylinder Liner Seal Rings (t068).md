## Cylinder Liner Seal Rings (t068)

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Service Parts Topic is to announce the release of new brass seal rings for the K Series cylinder liners. The new seal rings have three locating tabs on the inside diameter. Refer to Figure 1. The tabs will hold the seal ring in the correct position on the cylinder liner during installation of the liner in the cylinder block thus preventing misassembly that can lead to coolant leaks at the liner counterbore.

The tabs are manufactured to have an interference fit to the Lower Press Fit (LPF) diameter of the cylinder liner so the abs will be positioned in the small radius immediately under the liner flange holding the seal ring in position. Due to the inetrference fit, during installation of the seal ring it is necessary to keep the seal ring straight on the liner and push with your fingers on the seal ring near the tabs to work it down over the LPF diameter. Do **not** push on the seal rings too hard or the tabs can be deformed to the point the seal ring will **not** fit squarely on the bottom of the liner flange. The thickest seal rings can deform to a very slight angle during installation on the liner. Some deformation is acceptable as long as the tabs will still engage the radius under the liner flange. No sealant or special tools are necessary to install the new seal rings.

Which seal ring to use depends upon which cylinder liner is to be used.

- Table 1 explains the seal rings to be used on K19 cylinder liners that have a **standard** LPF outside diameter.
- Table 2 explains the seal rings to be used on K19 cylinder liners that have an 0.51 mm \[0.020 in\] **oversize** LPF outside diameter used in a K19 cylinder block that has been salvaged per Service Parts Topic 92T1-18.
- Table 3 explains the seal rings to be used on K38 and K50 cylinder liners whether used in a K38 or K50 cylinder block or a K19 cylinder block that has been salvaged per Service Parts Topic 92T1-18.

The seal rings with tabs are to be used with the cylinder liners given in Tables 1, 2 and 3 regardless of the sytle of the cylinder block.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Seal rings **without** tabs, that are to be used to depletion, **must** be used per the instructions within Service Parts Topic 92T1-18.

All three tabs on all the seal rings except those used on cylinder liners with the standaard K19 LPF outside diameter have notches to aid in identification. The notches show the inside diameter, **not** thickness. The seal rings with tabs are **not** marked to show thickness. It will be necessary to use outside micrometers to determine thickness. The thickness specifications in Table 1, 2, and 3 are the nominal thickness. Thickness tolerance on the 0.52 mm \[ 0.0205 in\] thick seal rings is ± 0.020 mm \[0.0004 in\]. Thickness tolerance on all other seal rings is 0.013 mm \[0.0005 in\].

> [!note] Note · Примечание
