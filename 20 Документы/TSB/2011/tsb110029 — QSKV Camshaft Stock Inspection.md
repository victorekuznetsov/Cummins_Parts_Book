## QSKV Camshaft Stock Inspection

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This document instructs customers with stock camshafts in stock for the QSK45, QSK60, QSK60 Gas, and QSK78 engines, to perform a date code inspection on the camshaft.

To support an ongoing investigation, customers with the following camshaft part numbers in stock are requested to check the date code.

#### Suspect Dates:

- Parts produced prior to 10-November-2010.

#### Description of Problem:

- Some QSKV45/60/78 camshafts produced prior to 10-November-2010 are potentially at risk from a known quality issue
- Atlas-produced camshafts are **not** affected.

As per the instructions below, all QSKV45/60/78 camshafts in stock need to be checked. If the camshaft was produced after the 10-November-2010 or was manufactured by Atlas (USA) the camshaft is known to be good and can used. Below are details on identifying the camshaft manufacturer and date codes

![[01k00005.png]]

If the camshaft is stamped with the initials ATS and USA, no further action is required.

![[01k00006.png]]

If the camshaft has been stamped with the initials EPCGE it is at risk and will need inspection.

![[01k00007.png]]

If the date code on an EPCGE camshaft is prior 10L0\#\#, the camshaft was built prior to 10-November-2010, then contact the point of purchase and arrange for a return of the camshaft via a RGA (return goods authorization). The parts distribution center (PDC) will return parts to the supplier. The above illustration outlines the details on how the part date code is constructed and read.

![[01k00008.png]]

The illustration above is an example of a camshaft that was produced on 03M9 = 3-December-2009.

Produced before 10-November-2010 and therefore classified as "At Risk".
