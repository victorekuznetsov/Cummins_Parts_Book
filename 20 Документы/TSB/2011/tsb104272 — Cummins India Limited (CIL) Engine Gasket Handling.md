## Cummins India Limited (CIL) Engine Gasket Handling

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

Subject:

The following engines families manufactured at Cummins India Limited from 1988-1998: 4B, 6B, 6C, NTC495, NTC743, NT855, K19, V28, K38, and K50, if the engine was initially delivered for sale inside of India, but subsequently exported.

#### Effective:

- Immediately

#### Purpose:

- Engine Repair Guidelines

#### Background:

- It has come to the attention of Cummins Inc. that some of the gaskets originally supplied in the engines listed may have contained some percentage of asbestos when originally manufactured. Cummins Inc. believes that the encapsulated chrysotile asbestos material present in these gaskets does **not** release asbestos fibers that can be inhaled, and therefore does **not** present a safety risk, unless it is handled in such a manner as to abrade, or disturb the integrity of the gasket surface, which could occur during some servicing. We do **not** believe that normal servicing of the engine will cause this to occur. Cummins Inc. has conducted tests that confirmed the belief that these gaskets can be safely handled and removed. If you have need to service one of these engines, and if that service event involves a gasketed joint, and if you further have reason to believe that the gasketed joint has never been opened, and if you have any questions regarding the proper procedures to employ regarding the removal and replacement of these gaskets, please contact Cummins Inc. at 1-800-CUMMINS™ or 1-812-377-3000. Also, the normal operation of these units does **not** require you to take any additional precautions.
- Any used gasket(s) as described above from these units should be disposed of by following all local rules and regulations regarding their disposal.

#### Actions:

- The specific models affected by this document are as listed above.
- The manufactured date can be determined from the engine serial number located on the unit's dataplate.

#### Product Repair Instructions:

- You may proceed in your normal practice with any repairs involving these engines. To ensure safe handling when performing any repairs involving a gasketed joint, you should wet down the gasket before removal, and also use any available local exhaust and ventilation systems.
- Be advised that some countries may have regulations that apply to the removal, handling, and disposal of asbestos. If you have any questions regarding such regulatory matters, contact Cummins Inc. as listed below for assistance.

#### Questions:

- For any questions regarding this action, make contact in the following order.

#### Dealers:

#### Distributors:

3. The Technical Support line 1-812-377-6517.
