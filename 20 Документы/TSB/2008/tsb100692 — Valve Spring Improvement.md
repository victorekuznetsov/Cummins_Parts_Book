## Valve Spring Improvement

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Technical Service Bulletin identifies improvements made to valve spring, Part Number 4936076, in the supplier process.

The process change eliminates abrasions on the spring coil surface.

The valve spring is used on the following engines:

- 6C, C8.3 Bosch™ P Pumps
- 6C, C8.3 Bosch™ A Pumps
- 6C, C8.3 Bosch™ MW Pumps
- 6C, C8.3 Denso or Nippondenso
- ISC/QSC8.3 CM554
- ISC/QSC8.3 CM850
- ISC CM2150
- ISL/QSL9 CM554
- ISL/QSL9 CM850
- ISL CM2150
- C8.3 Natural Gas
- C Gas Plus
- L Gas Plus
- ISL G.

Cylinder head assemblies begin on 6-March-2008, with engine serial number (ESN) first 46871080 at CDC, and at DEP with ESN first 21825615.
