## New Connecting Rod

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Technical Service Bulletin announces the release of a new connecting rod to be used on all 8.3 liter engines.

![[01d00409.png]]

Old Connecting Rod Bushing (left) and New Connecting Rod Bushing (right)

The new connecting rod has a newly designed bushing oil groove. Connecting rods with the old and new style bushings can be mixed within the same engine.

The table below identifies the part number and engine serial number (ESN) first for the old and new connecting rods.

| Old Part Number | New Part Number | Engine Serial Number (ESN) First | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3971394 | 4947898 | 46889300 | Rod, engine connecting |
