### Warranty Statement

### Contents

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> This service parts topic supersedes Topic 06T8-17; replace 06T8-17 with Topic 08T8-12A.

This Service Parts/Topic announces the introduction of a reintroduced Caltherm thermostat and supersedes the old Standard Thompson thermostat.

The C and L Series diesel and natural gas engines now make use of a reintroduced thermostat part number.

Thermostat, Part Number 4930594, supersedes thermostat, Part Number 4936026 for the C and L Series diesel and natural gas engines, excluding ISC and ISL CM2150 engines.

The ISC and ISL CM2150 engines have a higher coolant flow requirement due to the addition of EGR cooling, and **ONLY** thermostat, Part Number 4936026, can be used by the field as a replacement thermostat in ISC and ISL CM2150 engines.

This thermostat part number supersedes the current part number. This change has no effect on the structure of service kits. The current thermostat part number, superseding thermostat part number, and approval date are listed below in table 1.

| Part Number | Part Number |  |
