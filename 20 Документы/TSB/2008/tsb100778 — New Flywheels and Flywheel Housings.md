## New Flywheels and Flywheel Housings

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Technical Service Bulletin replaces 07T16-2. Two additional new flywheel housings have been added to Table 1, Part Numbers 4942185 and 4942186. This Technical Service Bulletin announces a standardization of flywheels which will eliminate the need for starter motor spacers. Several new flywheels and flywheel housings have been released.

Presently the spacers are used between the starter and the flywheel housing to achieve correct spacing.

![[16d00114.png]]

Figure 1, Starter Spacer Application (1 - Starter Spacer, 2 - Starter, 3 - Flywheel Housing, 4 - Flywheel

The new flywheel and flywheel housing hold a constant spacing between the starter mounting plate on the flywheel housing and the flywheel.

![[16d00115.png]]

![[16d00116.png]]

Figure 2, Old Flywheel Housing - 106 mm \[4.2 in\] with Step-down to 92.05 m \[3.6 in\] Starter Bore

Figure 3, New Flywheel Housing - 92.05 m \[3.6 in\] Starter Bore

The new flywheel and flywheel housing part numbers will obsolete and supersede the present part numbers. See Table 1, Flywheel Housing Part Number Changes for direct supersession information. See Table 2, Flywheel Part Number Changes, for direct part number supersessions and for the compatibility information/conditions existing when using a new flywheel with an old flywheel housing.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> If a new flywheel is used with a new flywheel housing, no spacer is used.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> A new flywheel housing can **not** be used with an old flywheel.
