## New J-seal Compatible Exhaust Manifold Sections for All Three Piece Exhaust Manifolds

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

**Product Affected**

- ISX15 (All Versions)
- QSX15 (All Versions)

**Description of Change**

This document announces new J-seal compatible exhaust manifold sections for all three piece exhaust manifolds.

**Reason for Change**

The new exhaust manifolds have been introduced to commonize the J-seal slip joint technology for all three piece exhaust manifolds and improve robustness of the slip joint to exhaust leaks.

**Part Compatibility**

- The new exhaust manifolds pieces are **not** compatible with existing exhaust manifold pieces and can **not** be intermixed.
- If replacement of one existing exhaust manifold section is required and a like part is **not** available, all exhaust manifold pieces **must** be replaced with the new design.

**Part Inventory Action**

Use existing part number inventory before using the new part number.
