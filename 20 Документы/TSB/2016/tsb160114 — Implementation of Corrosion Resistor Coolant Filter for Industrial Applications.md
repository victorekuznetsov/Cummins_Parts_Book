## Implementation of Corrosion Resistor Coolant Filter for Industrial Applications.

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

**Product Affected**

- NTA-855-C (Industrial applications **only**.)

**Description of Change**

This document announces the implementation of Corrosion Resistor Coolant Filter.

**Reason for Change**

The new corrosion resistor coolant filter will:

- Filter out debris.
- Prevent pitting, corrosion, rust, and coolant deposits on engine components.
- Help maintain correct coolant concentration by releasing supplemental coolant additives (SCA).

**Service Instructions**

- Corrosion resistor coolant filter, Part number [[4058965]], **must** be installed at next scheduled maintenance interval for coolant filter and all subsequent intervals.
- Corrosion resistor coolant filter, Part Number [[4058965]], **must** be replaced every 300 hours or 6 months. See NTA-855-C Operation and Maintenance Manual. Reference Maintenance Procedure for Every 300 Hours or 6 Months in Section 5.
- The coolant shutoff valves **must** be in the open position during engine operation. See Figure 1 below.
- For removal and installation information, reference Procedure 008-006. See link below. **Note:** https://quickserve.cummins.com/qs3/pubsys2/xml/en/procedures/106/106-008-006-tr-csa.html?q=106-008-006-tr
