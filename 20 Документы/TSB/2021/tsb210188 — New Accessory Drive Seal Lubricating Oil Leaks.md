**Description of Change**

A new accessory drive seal has been released which utilizes a press fit in to the gear cover rather than a three-bolt design.

**Reason for Change**

There has been reports of lubricating oil leaks from the accessory drive seals.

**Service Instructions**

- The new accessory drive seal utilizes a press fit design. A new seal installation tool kit **must** be purchased. See Table 1 below for part numbers.
- If an accessory drive seal is being replaced and changing from the three-bolt design to the press fit design, a new pulley **must** also be purchased. The existing pulleys have an extra step in the casting which damages the new accessory drive seal during installation.

**Part Compatibility**

Existing pulleys fitted to engines are **not** compatible with new design accessory drive seal. If a new accessory drive seal is purchased a new pully **must** also be purchased.
