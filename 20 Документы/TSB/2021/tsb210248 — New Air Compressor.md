## New Air Compressor

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

**Product Affected**

- KTA19 (All Versions)
- KTAA19 (All Versions)

**Description of Change**

- Current air compressor will be replaced with a pre-existing air compressor.
- This change will result in a slight change in tube routing because of differing placement of coolant ports.
- New option includes addition of inlet water tube.
