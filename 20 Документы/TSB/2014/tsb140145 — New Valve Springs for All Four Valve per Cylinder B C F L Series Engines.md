## New Valve Springs for All Four Valve per Cylinder B/C/F/L Series Engines

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This document announces new valve springs for all four valve per cylinder B/C/F/L series engines.

The valve springs are backward compatible and transparent to assembly. Service parts became available on 02 September 2014. In stock parts are affected. See Parts Alert MDC-040-14 for details. A paint color visual check is available to distinguish the new valve springs from the obsolete valve springs.

When changing valve springs, both valve springs under the same crosshead for any midrange engine should be changed. See TSB130158 for reference.
