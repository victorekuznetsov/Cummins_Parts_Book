## ISX15 and QSX15 Cylinder Liner Shim Supersession

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This document announces the supersession of cylinder liner shims used to regain cylinder liner protrusion height after using counterbore cutter kit, Part Number 2892436, to perform a cylinder liner counterbore machining operation on ISX15 and QSX15 engines.

Cylinder liner shim, thickness 0.508 mm \[ 0.020 in \], Part Number 2892402, has been obsoleted and superseded by Part Number 5298564.

Cylinder liner shim, thickness 0.813 mm \[ 0.032 in \], Part Number 2892403, has been obsoleted and superseded by Part Number 5298565.
