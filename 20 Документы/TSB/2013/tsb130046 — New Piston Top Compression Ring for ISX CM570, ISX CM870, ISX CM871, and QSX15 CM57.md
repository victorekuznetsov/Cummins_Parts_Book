## New Piston Top Compression Ring for ISX CM570, ISX CM870, ISX CM871, and QSX15 CM570

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This document introduces the new ISX15 / QSX15 top compression ring with thicker chrome plating. This change applies to all ISX15 and QSX15 engines that previously used piston top compression ring, Part Number 3684010. The new ring is backward compatible with the previous piston top compression ring, Part Number 3684010. The new ring has the same geometric dimensions, but integrates thicker chrome plating on the bottom face and liner mating face. This increased chrome plating will decrease ring wear and improve ring life. The piston ring kit part number has changed and is referenced in Table 1 and Table 2 below.

The thicker chrome ring started in production for these engines on January 17th, 2013 with engine serial number (ESN) first 79635888.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> ISX15 CM2250, QSX15 CM2250, and ISX15 CM2350 X101 engines were originally introduced with the thicker chrome piston top compression ring. There will be no change to ring kits for these engine families.
