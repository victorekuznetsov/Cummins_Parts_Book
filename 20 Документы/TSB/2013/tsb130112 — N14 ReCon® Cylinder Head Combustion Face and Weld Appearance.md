## N14 ReCon® Cylinder Head Combustion Face and Weld Appearance

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This document outlines the allowable appearance of ReCon® N14 cylinder heads.

1. The present machining process leaves marks on the cylinder head that do **not** look up to standard. These marks are cosmetic and the combustion face meets all surface finish and flatness specifications.

![[02900199.png]]

1. Machining marks.

The salvage process for cylinder heads allows for the welding and re-machining of certain areas of the cylinder head. The photograph below shows examples of welded and re-machined areas, and their resulting appearance. Cylinder heads that have been welded and re-machined will be marked with a “W”.

![[02900200.png]]

1. Welding marks
2. “W” Indicates cylinder head was welded
3. Welded material line (this is **not** a fracture line)
4. Welding marks

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> All the above machining and welding marks are **not** regarded as unacceptable and are approved by Cummins Inc. Welding and re-machining of cylinder heads is **only** allowed to be performed by Cummins® ReCon®.
