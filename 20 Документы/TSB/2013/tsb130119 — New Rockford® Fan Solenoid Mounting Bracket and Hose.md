## New Rockford® Fan Solenoid Mounting Bracket and Hose

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This document announces the release of a new Rockford® Fan solenoid mounting bracket and hose for QSK45, QSK60, and QSK78 engines. The part number for the existing mounting bracket is being obsoleted and replaced with a modified spacer that has a new part number. The new hose is a different length to comply with the change in position of the solenoid on the new spacer.

Table 1 lists the obsoleted part number for the mounting bracket.

Table 1: Obsoleted Part Number for the Mounting Bracket
