## New Exhaust Gas Temperature Sensor for QSK38 and QSK50 Tier 2 Engines

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This document announces the release of a revised exhaust gas temperature sensor for use in the QSK38 and QSK50 Tier 2 engines.

The revised exhaust gas temperature sensor was released to improve durability of the sensor and its wiring system. Changes to the sensor include a longer sensor body housing.

This part is released for service only. An accouncement will be released when these parts are implemented into production.

Parts will be available from the 30 Sept 2013 to order from Cummins® parts distribution centers.
