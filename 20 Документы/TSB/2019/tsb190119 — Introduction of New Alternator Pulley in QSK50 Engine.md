## Introduction of New Alternator Pulley in QSK50 Engine

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

**Product Affected**

- QSK50 CM2150 MCRS **(Industrial Only)Description of Change**

­

This document announces an introduction of new alternator pulley in QSK50 CM2150 MCRS engine model.

**Reason for Change**

­

It was observed that the pulley was press fitted on the shaft and affected the key movement, which caused charging alternator failure.

**Service Parts Availability**

­

It was observed that the pulley was press fitted on the shaft and affected the key movement, which caused charging alternator failure.
