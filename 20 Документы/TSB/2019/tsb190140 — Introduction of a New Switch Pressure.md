## Introduction of a New Switch Pressure

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

**Product Affected**

- KTTA-19-C **(Industrial Only)Description of Change**

­

This document announces an introduction of new stainless steel fuel switch pressure for KTTA-19-C engine model. Hence, a new stainless steel fuel switch pressure is introduced, which has higher yield strength, higher tensile strength and lower elongation than brass material so to withstand pressure hammering effect better than brass material.

**Reason for Change**

­

It was observed that there were fuel leakages from the electrical connector through breather hole. These fuel leakages may led to excessive fuel delivery to piston and consequentially heavy white smoke was observed.
