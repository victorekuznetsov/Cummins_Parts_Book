### Contents

**Product Affected**

- QSK50 CM2150 MCRS
- QSK50 CM2350 K108
- QSK50 CM2350 K130
- QSK50 CM850 MCRS

**Description of Change**

A new improved dual impeller water pump has been released including:

- Additional lubricating oil drain orifices in water pump bearing housing
- New spring loaded rubber lubricating oil seal

**Reason for Change**

New dual impeller water pump provides more robust lubricating oil and coolant sealing by:

- Reducing the level of lubricating oil in water pump bearing housing.
- Including a more robust lubricating oil seal.
