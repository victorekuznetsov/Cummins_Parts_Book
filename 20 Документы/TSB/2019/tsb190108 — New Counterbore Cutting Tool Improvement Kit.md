## New Counterbore Cutting Tool Improvement Kit

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

- ISX (All Versions)

- QSX15 (All Versions)

**Description of Change**

A new counterbore cutting improvement kit is available. The existing counterbore cutting tool design has been changed to reduce the stress and strain required to perform the counterbore cutting operation.

![[17r00324.png]]

Figure 1, Counterbore Cutting Improvement Kit, Part Number 5395552.

1. Clip, wire retaining
2. Pin, clevis
3. Adapter, drive
4. Driver, counterbore cutter
5. Bearing, needle
6. Race, outer bearing
7. Spring, compression
8. Collar, stop

**Reason for Change**

- Is used to aid and improve the ergonomics of cylinder block counter bore cutting operations with the engine installed in the vehicle.
- Can be used with all engine-specific counter bore cutting kits that use counterbore ledge cutter drive unit, Part Number 3163785, as the drive unit for the cutter plate.

**Service Instructions**

- The stop collar, Part Number 5395558, has a recessed surface and ledge that reduces the collar's tendency to back away from the drive unit during rotation. The stop collar can be used as a direct replacement for the collar that comes with the drive unit. The recessed surface on the stop collar **must** face the drive unit body.
