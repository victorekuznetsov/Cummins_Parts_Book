## Incorrect Diagnoses of Exhaust Manifold Lubricating Oil Carryover

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

**Product Affected**

- ISX (All 15 Liter Versions)
- QSX (All 15 Liter Versions)
- X15 (All Versions)

**Issue**

Symptom:

- Exhaust manifold gasket, exhaust manifold J seal, or exhaust manifold turbocharger flange gasket fluid leaks.

Root Cause:

- Extended engine operation at low idle creates condensate and fluid buildup in the exhaust system.

Once the exhaust manifold is removed, the exhaust manifold may show what looks like high exhaust manifold oil carryover.

**Verification**

- Verify unit is not consuming lubricating engine oil. Use the Oil Consumption Evaluator found on the Service Tools Tab in QSOL to determine if troubleshooting for oil consumption is needed.
- If no excessive lubricating oil consumption symptoms are present then there is no issue with the product. Please follow the step described in the resolution section.

**Resolution**

- If the engine will experience extended idle time, engine idle protection should be turned on or the idle speed needs to be increased to 1000 rpm to eliminate condensation buildup.
