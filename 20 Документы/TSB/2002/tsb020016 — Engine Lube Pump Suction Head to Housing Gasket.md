## Engine Lube Pump Suction Head to Housing Gasket

### Core Issue

It has been found that on some QSK45, QSK60, and QSK78 engines the torque on the capscrews that hold the engine lube pump suction head to the housing do **not** meet specifications. This early warning instructs the technicians to check the torque on these bolts, if the oil pan is being removed for another reason.

### Confirmation

All QSK45, QSK60, and QSK78 engines.

Low or erratic oil pressure.

The capscrews that hold the engine lube pump suction head to the housing do **not** meet the 47 N•m \[35 ft-lb\] specification.

The gasket between the engine lube pump suction head and housing is crushing, and the capscrews are loosing some of their torque.

If the torque is complete lost, the engine lube pump could loose suction, causing a loss of engine oil pressure.

### Resolution

![[07600502.png]]

Torque the engine lube pump suction head to housing capscrews to specification.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 47 n•m [35 ft-lb]
