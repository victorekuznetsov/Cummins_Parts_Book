## Oil Volumes for Isolated Oil Gasket

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

Certain options and kits require the addition of extra oil when performing an oil change. Both the isolation oil pan gasket option and the bedplate (block stiffener) option each require additional oil to be added during the oil change. The dipstick tube does **not** need to be changed.

Engines with the following part numbers for the isolation gasket or the bedplate **must** add extra oil.
