## New Alternator and Refrigerant Compressor Belt Guard

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This service/parts announcement introduces a new alternator and refrigerant compressor belt guard for the QST30 industrial engine. Reference Figure 1 below for the configuration of the new guard on the engine.

![[17a00004.png]]

Figure 1, Belt Guard Installed

The affected applications are Komatsu, HD785 and 330M haul trucks **only**. This change is an improvement in durability over the previous guard. The new style guard includes vibration isolators at the four mounting tabs. Reference Figure 2 for the configuration of the mounting tabs.

![[17a00005.png]]

Figure 2, Belt Guard Mounting Tab Locations

Approximate engine serial number first for the new alternator and refrigerant compressor belt guard is 37181167, built 16DEC97.

The new alternator and refrigerant compressor belt guard supersedes guard Part Number 3094661.

The new belt guard, Part Number 4067854 (Figure 2), is content of option number SG5019. The part numbers listed below complete the content of option number SG5019:

| Part Number | Description | Torque Value | Quantity |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3034771 | Spacer, mounting |  | 1 |
| [[3093744]] | Capscrew, hexagon head | 62 N•m \[48 ft-lb\] | 3 |
| 3349409 | Isolators, rubber vibration |  | 8 |
| [[3410273]] | Spacer, insert |  | 4 |
| [[3896408]] | Capscrew, flanged hexagon head | 62 N•m \[48 ft-lb\] | 1 |

The alternator and refrigerant compressor belt guard is **not** sold as an aftermarket kit. The mounting hardware should be ordered separately.

The belt guard is mounted to the front of the engine using spacers and vibration isolators, as illustrated in Figure 3.

![[17a00006.png]]

Figure 3, Spacer and Vibration Isolator Installation

> This is a product improvement and is **not** subject to campaign.
