## ISX15 and QSX15 Wide Valve Camshaft Lobe and Valve Rocker Lever Roller Release

### Core Issue

To improve the robustness of the ISX15 and QSX15 valve camshaft, the camshaft lobe and rocker lever rollers were widened. Increasing the width of these two components reduces the contact stress between the camshaft and the rocker lever rollers.

### Confirmation

A compatibility matrix **must** be used to determine whether non-malfunctioning parts **must** be replaced. This matrix is shown in the camshaft and rocker lever Clean and Inspect for Reuse sections of the engine service manuals. The matrix is shown below.

### Resolution

These new parts are **not** fully backward compatible to the previous versions of the valve camshaft and valve rocker lever roller.
