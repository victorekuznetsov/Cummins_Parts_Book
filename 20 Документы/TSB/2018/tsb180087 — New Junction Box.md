## New Junction Box

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

**Product Affected**

- NH, NT / PT Fixed Time
- NTA855

**Reason for Change**

Wire insulation can malfunction because of contact with junction box front cover plate. This can lead to electrical system shorting and other component damage.

**Description of Change**

- Width increased by 30mm \[ 1.18 in \] for clearance between harness and front cover plate.
- Addition of 12mm +/- 1 mm \[ 0.5 in \] thick plain foam (Firend TB 070) on inside of front cover plate, with self-adhesive backing.
