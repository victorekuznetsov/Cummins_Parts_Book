## Water Pump Coolant Seal, Material Change

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Technical Service Bulletin is to inform the field of a new and improved water pump coolant seal, Part Number [[4096426]]. The new water pump coolant seal incorporates a higher temperature rubber material and is designed to improve the water pump sealing durability. The new water pump coolant seal replaces the old water pump coolant seal, Part Number 3092348. Production use began with engine serial number (ESN) first 37223634.

The new water pump coolant seal is included in the following water pumps and water pump repair kits:

| 4090033 | Water pump repair (major) |
