## Revised Exhaust Manifold Part Numbers

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Technical Service Bulletin lists which exhaust manifold part numbers are required for each manifold configuration on QST30 engines. The manifold part numbers are categorized by exhaust manifold option (TBXXXX) and by front out or rear out exhaust configurations.
