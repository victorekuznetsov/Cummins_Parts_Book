## New Upper Idler Gear Idler Shaft

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

**Product Affected**

- ISX CM570
- ISX CM870
- ISX CM871
- ISX CM871 E
- QSX15 CM570

**Description of Change**

A new upper idler gear idler shaft has been released with a revised orientation indicator.

**Reason for Change**

A new idler shaft was released with reduced radial growth material properties because radial growth can prevent proper lash setting.

**Service Instructions**

See corresponding Service Manual. Refer to Procedure 001-088 in Section 1 for installing the idler shaft.
