## Premium Lubricating Oil Pan Gasket

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This document announces a premium lubricating oil pan gasket to reduce oil pan leak occurrences.

**Product Affected**

- K50 (All Versions)
- QSK50 (All Versions)

**Description of Change**

The existing lubricating oil pan gasket has been updated to an improved edge molded gasket.

**Reason for Change**

To reduce lubricating oil pan leak occurrences.

**Customer Communication**

Upgrade to the premium lubricating oil pan gasket is recommended if the existing lubricating oil pan gasket is leaking. Upgrade to the premium lubricating oil pan gasket is at the customers discretion.

**Service Parts Availability**

Service parts are available. See Table 1 for part numbers.

| Table1, Lubricating Oil Pan Gasket Part Numbers |  |  |
|---|---|---|
| Part Description | Existing Part Number | Premium Part Number |
| Lubricating Oil Pan Gasket | 3000630 or 3043213 | [[3641727]] |

**Part Structure**

The premium part is backward compatible and transparent to assembly.

Part number 3000630 has been obsoleted and superseded by 3043213.

Part number 3043213 will remain available.

**Part Inventory Action**

None.

**Production Status**

None.

**Associated Publications**

Use the following procedure in the K38, K50, QSK38, and QSK50 Service Manual, Bulletin 4021528. Refer to Procedure 007-025 in Section 7.
