## New Cylinder Liner Crevice Seal and Associated Cylinder Liner Part Numbers

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

**Product Affected** ­

- G19
- K19
- KTA19GC CM558/CM2358 SLB
- QSK19 CM500

- QSK19 CM2150 MCRS
- QSK19 CM2350 K105
- QSK19 G CM558/CM2358
- QSK19 G CM558/CM2358 and CM700/CM2358

**Description of Change**

The new cylinder liner crevice seal groove now sits further down in the lower press fit area. The groove acts as a positive locator for the upper crevice seal, which minimizes vertical movement of the seal during installation. The new upper crevice seal is now a D-shape design. The seal material remains unchanged. See Figure 1 below. The previous cylinder liner crevice seal groove sat higher in the lower press fit area and did **not** have a positive locator, which could allow the seal to move vertically during installation. The previous upper crevice seal was a rectangular design. See Figure 2 below.

![[01r00070.png]]

Figure 1, New Cylinder Liner and Upper Crevice Seal Installed in Cylinder Block

![[01r00071.png]]

Figure 2, Existing Cylinder Liner and Upper Crevice Seal Installed in Cylinder Block

­ **Reason for Change**

The new cylinder liner and upper crevice seal is more robust and helps to reduce seal pinching between the cylinder liner flange and brass sealing ring during cylinder liner installation. This can help reduce coolant leaks from the cylinder head gasket area.

**Part Compatibility** ­

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> New crevice seals are compatible with new cylinder liners only. Existing crevice seals are compatible with existing cylinder liners only.

Existing and new cylinder liners can be mixed in the same engine. The new cylinder liners are compatible with all existing power cylinder components. ­

**Part Inventory Action**

Use obsolete part number inventory, if available, before using the new part number.
