## Valve Guide Wear Caused by Oil and Carbon Buildup from Valve Stem Seal: Black Smoke, Engine Noise, or Lower Power

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

**Product Affected**

- QST30 CM552
- QST30 PG CM850

**Issue Summary**

Symptoms:

- Excessive black smoke
- Abnormal engine noise
- Low power

Root Cause:

- Existing Tier 2 valve stem seal is **not** intended for the engine operating exhaust port pressures and hence, does **not** seal the exhaust gases or meter oil per requirement.

**Verification**

- Upon inspection would oil/soot mixture found packed in the cylinder head near valve stems and springs and valve stem seals.
- Upon removal of the valve stem guides and valves severe amount of corrosion, carbon deposits and material loss from valve guides is observed.

**Resolution**

Replace Tier 2 Valve Stem Seal with Tier 4 Valve Stem Seal and/or Replace entire cylinder head assembly with new part number.

1. Remove Suspect Cylinder Head from Engine

- See corresponding Service Manual. Reference Procedure 002-004 in Section 2.

2. Suspect Cylinder Head Removal Preparation

- Purchase a full set of QST30 cylinder heads. See Parts list in Appendix A1.1.
- Purchase all new parts as specified in parts list and reference reuse list for reworking used good cylinder heads in Appendix A1.2

3. Disassemble Suspect Cylinder Head

- Disassemble cylinder head following Procedure 002-004 and 002-020 in Section 2
- Skip removing exhaust and intake valve guides unless reworking good used cylinder head.
- Remove tier 2 valve stem oil seals.
- Skip Clean, Inspect, Leak and Pressure Test sections in 002-004 in Section 2 unless reusing good used cylinder head continue following all procedures.

4. Installing Replacement Cylinder Head

- Install replacement cylinder head. See corresponding Service Manual. Reference Procedure 002-004 in Section 2

5. Reworking Suspect Tier 2 Cylinder Head

Assembly Preparation

Gather all supplies and tools needed for performing assembly.

Cummins® Tools Needed Prior to Assembly:

- Valve guide driver, Part Number 6304260
- Valve seal driver, Part Number 3163429
- Valve spring compressor, Part Number ST-448 or Part Number 3163639, or equivalent
- Valve spring stand, Part Number ST-1022
- Compressor plate, Part Number 3375043

Assembly:

- First few cylinder heads assembled, measure all valve guide lengths, and record the value and compare with guide overall length dimensions located in Appendix B1.
- Install exhaust and intake valve guides using tool when reworking good used cylinder head
- Verify guide installs properly to 21.5 mm by measuring guide exposed difference (see B1.2).
- Install Tier 4 valve stem seals on all exhaust and intake valve guides by using tool, Part Number 3163429.
- Continue assembling cylinder head. See corresponding Service Manual. Reference Procedure 002-004 and 002-020 in Section 2.

**Reason for Change**

Cylinder head assembly was changed to counter recurring valve stem seal malfunctions in the field.
