## Rocker Lever Ball Fracture and Replacement: Low Power and Engine Smoking

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document authorizes specific changes to the repair practice for failures covered under product warranty coverages.

**Issue**

Symptom:

- Low power
- White smoke
- Black smoke
- Abnormal engine noise

Root Cause:

- Fracture of rocker lever ball at interface of shank and flange on rocker lever

**Verification**

Verify rocker lever ball has fractured at interface of shank and flange on rocker lever. See Figure 1 below.

![[03r00056.png]]

Figure 1: Rocker Lever Ball Malfunction.

**Resolution**

- If fracture of rocker lever ball is observed on any rocker lever, all six rocker lever assemblies (containing all twelve individual intake and exhaust rocker levers) **must** be replaced.
- Rocker lever ball malfunction is related to a material issue for a limited rocker lever build period.

**Service Parts Availability**

Rocker levers are to be replaced with same part number. See Table 2 for part numbers.
