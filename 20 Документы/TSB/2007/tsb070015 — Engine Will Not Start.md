## Engine Will Not Start

### Core Issue

This early field notification informs the field of customer complaints concerning Case New Holland of an initial no-start concern on QSX15 engines when the keyswitch is moved immediately from the “OFF” to “CRANK” position.

### Confirmation

This early field notification applies to all QSX15 engines used in both Case STX and New Holland TJ 4WD tractors.

The unit can come into the repair facility with a crank but no start concern.

Verify if the operator is **not** allowing sufficient time for the dash to be illuminated before attempting to crank the engine. The operator should be pausing for 2 to 3 seconds in the “RUN” position before trying to crank the engine.

Upon investigation, passing through the “RUN” position on the keyswitch to quickly will allow cranking before the ECM can command fueling. When this occurs the engine will crank, but will **not** start.

None.

### Resolution

Make sure the operator of the unit is pausing for 2 to 3 seconds in the “RUN” position before trying to crank the engine in the event of a crank no start concern. The information in this bulletin is intended to inform the field how to educate the customer in the proper starting procedure of the engine, and to avoid any field service calls related to this complaint. Please distribute this bulletin to all industrial service managers, service writers, and field service technicians.
