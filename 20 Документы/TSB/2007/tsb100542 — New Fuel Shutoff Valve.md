## New Fuel Shutoff Valve

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Technical Service Bulletin introduces a new fuel shutoff valve and all the associated parts that are now available for the QST30 engine (all applications).

The new fuel shutoff valve features the following product improvements:

- modified wear plate designed to conform with other high horsepower engine families
- modified fuel manifold counter bore depth to adjust for wear plate
- new actuator disc for improved flow characteristics.

The engine serial number (ESN) first for the new fuel shutoff valve is 37226082 and occurred on November 1, 2006.

The new fuel manifold assembly obsoletes and supersedes the old fuel manifold assembly.
