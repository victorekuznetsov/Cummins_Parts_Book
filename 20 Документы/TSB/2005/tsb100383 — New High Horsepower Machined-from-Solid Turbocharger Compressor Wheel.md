## New High Horsepower Machined-from-Solid Turbocharger Compressor Wheel

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Technical Service Bulletin announces a new machined-from-solid turbocharger compressor wheel for QST30 engines operating in specific applications. The new turbocharger was released to resolve low-cycle fatigue issues.

The previous compressor wheel is cast with the outer blade contour machined to achieve the desired profile. The sides of the cast wheel blades are left in the 'as cast' condition.

The new compressor wheel is machined from a solid billet of aluminum to reduce the number of inclusions in the wheel. All surfaces of the machined-from-solid wheel are machined.

This product improvement is **not** covered by warranty.

**Only** certain, high-duty cycle applications require the use of turbochargers with machined-from-solid turbocharger compressor wheels.

Consult QuickServe® Online to determine if a given engine and application combination is eligible to receive this product improvement.
