## New Turbochargers and Related Service Kits

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Technical Service Bulletin announces new turbochargers and related service kits for use on QSK19, QSK38, QSK45, QSK50 series diesel engines due to the change in turbocharger part numbers.

The new service kits contain turbochargers with new impeller designs in order to further improve product durability.

The installation procedure remains unchanged.
