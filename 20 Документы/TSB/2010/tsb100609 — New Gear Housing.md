## New Gear Housing

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Technical Service Bulletin introduces a new gear housing on all ISM and ISMe engines. The new housing went into effect for Part Number 4906948 on May 21, 2007 with an engine serial number first of 35197969, and for Part Number 4906947 on May 24, 2007 with and engine serial number first of 35198730.

The new gear housing contains a radius in the accessory drive support web area to eliminate the stress riser and eliminate cracking of the accessory drive web support.
