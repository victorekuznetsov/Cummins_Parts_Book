## New Turbocharger Wastegate Mounting

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Technical Service Bulletin is to announce a new turbocharger kit, Part Number 4090043, released for the ISX Family 3 CM570 engine. This change affects ISX Family 3 engines with any of the following control parts lists (CPL): 2442, 2630, 2633, 2801, 2802, 2834, 2835, 8246 and 8247.

Engines built prior to engine serial number (ESN) 79146928 (November 29, 2005) were built with turbine housings utilizing a 4-bolt mounting system for the wastegate actuator.

Engines with ESN 79146928 and higher (November 29, 2005) were built with a new turbine housing that has been modified by removing one of the wastegate mounting bosses.

When turbocharger kit, Part Number 4090043, replaces turbocharger kit, Part Number 4089760, on one of the affected CPLs, the engine dataplate **must** be stamped with Field Fix Number 371. This number is required by law to retain certification of the engine.

For correct turbocharger installation, refer to Procedure [[10-010-033-tr — Turbocharger|010-033]] in the Signature, ISX and QSX15 Service Manual, Bulletin [[3666239 — Signature™, ISX, and QSX15 Service Manual\|3666239]].

Turbocharger kit, Part Number 4090043, is also used on some present production ISX Family 3 CM570 CPLs. The present productions CPLs do **not** require the Field Fix number to be stamped on the dataplate.
