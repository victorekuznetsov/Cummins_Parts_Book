## Fuel Pump Rebuild

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Technical Service Bulletin provides procedures to rebuild fuel pumps for the QSK45 and QSK60 Series engine. A new style fuel of pump has been introduced that has a modified shaft diameter to ease rebuild of the pump. Other than this change, the rebuild procedure for old- and new-style pumps is the same. Tables containing lists of common parts used to rebuild the fuel pumps are shown below.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> This is a product improvement and is **not** subject to campaign.
