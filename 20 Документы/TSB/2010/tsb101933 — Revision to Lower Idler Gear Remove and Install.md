## Revision to Lower Idler Gear Remove and Install

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Service Parts Topic identifies the process error for removing and installing the lower idler gear. The use of a guide stud is specified for removal and installation of the gear in Procedure 001-088 of the service manual. The gear needs to be lifted over the crankshaft tone wheel to be removed and installed. The use of a guide stud will **not** allow the gear to be removed or installed.
