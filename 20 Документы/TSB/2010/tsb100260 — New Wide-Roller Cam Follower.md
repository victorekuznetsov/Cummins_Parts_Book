## New Wide-Roller Cam Follower

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Technical Service Bulletin is a parts announcement which details changes to injector cam followers used on K19 Engines and describes the following:

- Application considerations
- Differences between the existing cam follower and the wide-roller cam follower
- Repair practices for the replacement of the existing cam followers
- Part numbers for the existing and replacement cam followers.

Special consideration **must** be given to K19 engines that run in continuous duty applications, operating with light loads for long periods of time.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> This is a product improvement **not** subject to campaign.

![[04400043.png]]

Figure 1, Cam Follower Forging Part Number

Both cam followers have the same forging, Part Number 206231, illustrated in Figure 1. Do **not** use the forging part number as a guide.

![[04400045.png]]

Figure 2, Cam Follower Roller Differences

In Figure 2, the existing standard cam follower is on the left hand side of the illustration and the wide-roller cam follower is on the right hand side. The measurements in the illustration are for reference **only**.

![[04400044.png]]

Figure 3, Cam Follower Pin Orientation

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> To reduce the possibility of damaging the camshaft do not mix wide-roller cam followers with standard roller cam followers.

The standard roller cam follower, in Figure 3, located on the left hand side of the illustration, has a groove in the lever for pin and roller orientation. The wide-roller cam follower on the right hand side of the illustration, has pin and roller orientation but does **not** have a groove in the lever.

When installing the wide-roller cam follower the entire lever **must** be replaced because the roller will **not** fit into the standard roller lever casting.

The wide-roller cam follower, Part Number 3081413, includes the socket, roller, lever, and pin and replaces the standard cam follower, Part Number 3039163.

The wide roller is **only** in the wide-roller cam follower.

The parts package is in service and production.
