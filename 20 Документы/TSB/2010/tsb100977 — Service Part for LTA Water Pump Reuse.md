## Service Part for LTA Water Pump Reuse

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This document announces the service part number used when replacing the o-ring installed inside the low-temperature aftercooler (LTA) water pump for QSK45 and QSK60 engines. Every time the LTA water pump, Part Number 3638083, is disassembled for inspection or repair, the internal o-ring, Part Number 4919909, **must** be replaced. The o-ring, Part Number 4919909, is labeled as (3) in the Figure 1 below.
