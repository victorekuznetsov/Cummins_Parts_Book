## Lubricating Oil Cooler Leak Test

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Service/Parts Topic modifies Procedure 007-003 of the Signature, ISX, QSX15 Troubleshooting and Repair Manual, Bulletin [[3666239 — Signature™, ISX, and QSX15 Service Manual\|3666239]], to include additional instructions for leak testing the lubricating oil cooler assembly. Leak testing the oil cooler assembly can be useful in troubleshooting coolant in lubricating oil conditions, testing the element to cooler housing gaskets and testing the elements.

This Service/Parts Topic also introduces a new service tool, lubricating oil cooler leak test kit, Part Number 4918200. This kit is necessary to leak test the oil cooler as an assembly.
