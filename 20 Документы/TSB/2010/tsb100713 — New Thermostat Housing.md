## New Thermostat Housing

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Technical Service Bulletin introduces a new thermostat housing, Part Number 4924516, for the QSK60 Series Engines. The new thermostat housing improves accessibility to the thermostats and increased coolant flow.

The new thermostat housing mounts in the same manner as the old thermostat housing. Engine Serial Number (ESN) first for the new thermostat housing is 33157500. See Figure 1 for the new thermostat housing.

![[08600765.png]]

Figure 1, New Thermostat Housing.

When upgrading from the old thermostat housing to the new thermostat housing, Part Number 4924516, additional hardware is required due to the increased water transfer tube size.
