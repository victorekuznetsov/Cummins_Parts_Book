## New LTA Water Pump

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> This Technical Service Bulletin supersedes Service/Parts Topic 06T8-22. Replace 06T8-22 with this Service/Parts Topic.

This Technical Service Bulletin introduces a revised LTA water pump, Part Number 3640492, and a new LTA water pump service kit, Part Number 4955807. The improvements include a change from a greased bearing to oil supplied bearings, which has increased lubrication. The oil feed is taken from the side of the gear cover.

Additional changes include improved water and oil seals, increased pump body strength, the introduction of a discharge cavitation plate, and drillings at the 6 o'clock and the 3 o'clock positions on the pump body for optional connection of the oil feed hose.

The older style and revised LTA water pumps look similar in appearance, due to the primary improvements occurring internally. Reference Figure 1.

![[08600820.png]]

Figure 1, New oil feed.

The strength of the water pump body has been improved by adding a stiffening rib, shown in the diagram below, and the introduction of a one-piece water pump body. Reference Figure 2.

![[08600821.png]]

Figure 2, Stiffening Rib (A).

The revised LTA water pump part numbers are as follows:

| Part Number | Description |
|---|---|
| 3636850 | Old (original) LTA water pump |
| 3638083 | Single oil supply LTA water pump (Production only) |
| 3640492 | Optional oil supply LTA water pump (Service and Production) |

The oil supplied bearing LTA water pump, Part Number 3638083, has been installed in production since August 2006. The engine serial number (ESN) first is 33165335.

If a new LTA water pump, Part Number 3640492, is to be installed in place of an old greased bearing water pump, Part Number 3636850, the following parts will be required:

| Part Number | Quantity | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 3638095 | 1 | Flexible Hose |
| [[3963982]] | 2 | Sealing Washer |
| 68152 | 2 | Clip |
| [[3682701]] | 1 | Male Union Elbow |
| 3638094 | 1 | Banjo Connection Screw |

A service kit, Part Number 4955807, has been released which contains LTA water pump, Part Number 3640492, and all the above components.

- For installation and removal information on QSK60G engines, refer to Procedure 008-109 (Low-Temperature Aftercooler (LTA) Water Pump) in the Troubleshooting and Repair Manual, Industrial QSK45G and QSK60G Series Engines, Bulletin 4021383.
