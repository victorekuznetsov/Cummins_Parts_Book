## Service Tools Procedure, New Cylinder Block Protective Plugs

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Service/Parts Topic adds Protective Plug, Part Number 4918874 and Protective Plug, Part Number 4918875, to Procedure 022-001 (Service Tools) in Section 1 of the Signature™, ISX, and QSX15 Service Manual, Bulletin [[3666239 — Signature™, ISX, and QSX15 Service Manual\|3666239]].

These new protective plugs are used in the front and rear oil passages of the cylinder block during repairs. The protective plugs protect the cylinder block from debris that can enter the oil passages. These protective plugs can **only** be used on cylinder blocks from engines built after August 2006. These new protective plugs were released because the oil passages were slightly modified and the available plugs no longer fit in the oil passages.
