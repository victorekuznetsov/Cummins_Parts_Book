## Step Addition, Symptom Tree t047 (Engine Noise Excessive) and Symptom Tree t048 (Engine Noise Excessive - Combustion Knocks)

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Service/Parts Topic adds a step calling for the removal of the engine vibration damper, and for the engine to be operated in the RPM range where the noise or knock occurs, to symptom trees t047 and t048 in the Service Manual, Signature, ISX, and QSX15, Bulletin [[3666239 — Signature™, ISX, and QSX15 Service Manual\|3666239]].
