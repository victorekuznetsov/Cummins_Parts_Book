## Cylinder Head Gasket Change

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> This Technical Service Bulletin supersedes 01T2-9 and 02T2-2; replace 01T2-9 and 02T2-2 with this topic.

This Technical Service Bulletin introduces a change made to the QSK45 and QSK60 series engines cylinder head gasket.

The cylinder head gasket now has an improved coating made of aluminium that helps to prevent degradation/corrosion of the steel carrier. The new gasket, Part Number [[4086225]], also incorporates previous improvements made to the component, which includes a change in material used for the cooling grommets in order to improve temperature capability, and an increase in the thickness of the seven head bolt hole grommets to improve sealing. It is visibly identifiable from the previous versions by its aluminium surface finish and colour.

The new cylinder head gasket went into production at the Daventry Engine Plant in July 2002, with engine serial number 33152354.
