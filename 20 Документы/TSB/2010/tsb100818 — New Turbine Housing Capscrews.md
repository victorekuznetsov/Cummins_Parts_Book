## New Turbine Housing Capscrews

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Technical Service Bulletin supercedes topic 07T10-19. Replace 07T10-19 with this topic. This topic introduces the improved turbine housing capscrew used on the QSK19, QSK45, QSK60, and QSK78 industrial engines.

This Technical Service Bulletin announces new A286 stainless steel turbine housing capscrews that are now available for HE-851 and HX-83 turbochargers in industrial applications.

The improved capscrew can be used as a service part or during rebuilds of QSK19/45/60/78 engines.

They can be used on Tier I and Tier II applications.

The A286 stainless steelcapscrew is presently in production on QSK60 single stage industrial engines.

The engine serial number first is 33168646 and the turbocharger serial number first is H070413527. QSK60 single stage service turbochargers built prior to turbocharger serial number H070413527 will be kitted to include the new capscrews in the box and should be fitted to the turbocharger prior to installation.

The kit part number is 4032182 and includes 8 capscrews and 4 lockplates.

The torque value and installation procedure remain the same.
