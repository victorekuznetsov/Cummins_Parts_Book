## New Oil Fill Tube and Cap Assemblies for C, ISC, and ISL CM554, CM850, CM2150, and CM2250

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This document announces two new oil fill tube and cap assemblies. The new oil fill tube and cap design will address assembly strains and resonance issues. The new cap incorporates a larger rivet to address leak issues at the cap. Part Number 3936062, oil fill tube, is obsoleted and superseded by oil fill tube and cap assemblies, Part Number 5268123, used with gas applications and Part Number 5266930, used with diesel applications.

| New Part Number | Old Part Number | Application | Option Number | Engine Serial Number (ESN) First |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5268123 | 3936062 | Gas | OP9133, OP9394 | 73119881 |
| 5266930 | 3936062 | Diesel | OP9380 | Not available |
