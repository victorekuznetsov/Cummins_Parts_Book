## New Fuel Lift Pump Gasket

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Technical Service Bulletin alerts the field to an alternate repair for fuel lift pump assemblies on ISC CM554, QSC 8.3 CM554, ISL CM554, QSL9 CM554, Signature, ISX, and QSX15 engines. The gasket was released to repair leaks between the fuel lift pump housing and mounting adapter.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The warrantable repair for a fuel lift pump leak is replacement of the lift pump assembly. The practice described below is also approved as an alternate warranty repair.

Because of the fuel lift pump design differences, two service gaskets have been released. The Part Numbers are 4928511 and 4928512. These gaskets are **not** interchangeable. However, ISC/ISL CM554 engines built after approximately ESN 46319395 and ISX engines built after ESN 79055000 can use fuel lift pump gasket Part Number 4928512.

To identify the correct gasket, inspect the face of the lift pump housing:

- Remove the three bolts on the top of the lift pump mounting adapter.
- Check for a support rib (see the new-style lift pump gasket illustration).

The correct gasket can also be determined by the lift pump serial number located on the side of the lift pump housing or on the lift pump mounting adapter.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The lift pump may need to be removed to read the serial number.

![[05d00948.png]]

ISX Fuel Lift Pump Serial Number

The ISX lift pump serial number is interpreted as follows:

1. Last four characters of the supplier part number
2. Year
3. Month
4. Day
5. Shift

For example, 374SO6B29A is an ISX lift pump built on February 29, 2006 during the first shift. This lift pump will use gasket Part Number 4928512 because it is after the dates listed in the table below.

The early-style lift pump service gasket, Part Number 4928511, can **only** be used on lift pump, Part Number 4076857, and lift pump part numbers built before the dates in the table below.

The new-style lift pump service gasket, Part Number 4928512, can **only** be used on lift pump part numbers built on or after the dates listed in the table below.

ISC/ISL Fuel Lift Pump Serial Number

The ISC/ISL lift pump serial number is interpreted as follows:

1. Last four digits of the Cummins part number
2. Year
3. Month
4. Day
5. Shift

For example, 90850H29A is an ISC lift pump built on August 29, 2000 during the first shift. The lift pump will use gasket, Part Number 4928511, because it is before the dates listed in the table below.

The early-style service gasket, Part Number 4928511, can **only** be used on lift pumps, Part Numbers 3944384, 3944386, 3945502, and lift pump part numbers built before the dates in the table below.
