## New Thermostat Housing Gaskets

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Technical Service Bulletin introduces a new thermostat housing gasket on QSK45/60 engines. The new gasket is of a metal core construction which replaces the fibre type gasket. The new gaskets are designed to provide improved sealing, thus reducing premature leaks at these joints.
