## New Lower Gear Cover

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Technical Service Bulletin introduces a new lower gear cover on all Signature/ISX and QSX15 engines. See the table below for part number status. The new gear cover went into production on 13 March, 2007 with engine serial number (ESN) first 79239101. The new gear cover is backward compatible with all Signature/ISX and QSX15 engines.

The gear cover has been changed in order to eliminate a nominal offset between the crankshaft center line and the center line of the crankshaft seal stud pattern on the gear cover. This offset was found during an investigation into front crankshaft seal oil leaks.
