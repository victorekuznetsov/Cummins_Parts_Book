## Fan Drive Idler Arm Assembly Procedure, Multiple Section Revisions

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Service/Parts Topic introduces a revision to Procedure 008-029 (Fan Drive Idler Arm Assembly) in the QSK45 and QSK60 Service Manual, Bulletin Number [[4021530 — QSK45 and QSK60 Service Manual\|4021530]] and the QSK78 Service Manual, Bulletin 3666727.

- The Clean and Inspect for Reuse section ([[4021530 — QSK45 and QSK60 Service Manual\|4021530]]) and Inspect for Reuse section (3666727) were modified to clarify the dimensions of the different types of shafts used.
- The Assemble section was modified to include details on greasing the bearings on the slide arm type adjuster.
