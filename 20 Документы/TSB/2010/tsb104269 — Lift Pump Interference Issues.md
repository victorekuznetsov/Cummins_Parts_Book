## Lift Pump Interference Issues

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This document addresses interference issues in the lift pumps for B and C Series diesel engines.

BMC pumps are to be used on Samsung machines due to interference issues. When an interference issue results, use the appropriate BMC pump to resolve the issue. Table 1 lists the supersession from AC Rochester to Federal Mogul (Carter) to BMC pumps. These pumps are a direct supersession on all applications **except** Chrysler.

| AC Part Number | FMC Part Number | BMC Pump |
|---|---|---|
| 3925709 | 3932224\* | N/A |
| 3927153 | 3932224 | N/A |
| 3917998 | 3932226 | 3284073 |
| 3917999 | 3932226 | 3284074 |
| 3918000 | 3932227 | 3284014 |
| 3918076 | 3932228 | 3284015 |
| 3926034 | 3932225 | N/A |
| 3282870 | 3932229 | N/A |
| N/A | 3932230 | N/A |

\* For Chrysler applications, use Service Kit 3802837 along with lift pump Part No. 3932224. This kit includes a short hose and two clamps. The only hose in this kit is 10 mm \[0.39 in\] shorter on the fuel heater and strainer side and is only required when changing from the AC Rochester to the Federal Mogul (Carter) lift pump.

The new pump supplier has printed a date code and the last four digits of the Cummins part number on the mounting flange of the pump. The part number is the larger size of the two groups.
