## Oversize Head Gaskets

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This document announces the availability of oversize head gaskets for QSK45 and QSK60 engines. To maintain proper engine operation, oversize head gaskets are needed for blocks which have the top surface or deck machined during overhaul.

The following dimensions **must** be measured to determine the correct oversize head gasket to be used:

- Block deck to crankshaft centerline (1)
- Block deck to crankshaft bore (2).

![[01600454.png]]

For new block deck height, reference Head Deck Surface in the Inspect section in the QSK45/60 Service Manual, Bulletin [[4021530 — QSK45 and QSK60 Service Manual\|4021530]]. [[102-001-026 — Cylinder Block|Refer to Procedure 001-026 in Section 1.]]
