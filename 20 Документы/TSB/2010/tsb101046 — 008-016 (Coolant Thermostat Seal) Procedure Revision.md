## 008-016 (Coolant Thermostat Seal) Procedure Revision

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

Procedure 008-016 (Coolant Thermostat Seal) in Section 8 in the QSK45 and QSK60 Service Manual, Bulletin [[4021530 — QSK45 and QSK60 Service Manual\|4021530]], has been updated to include a new step in the Install section. The new step details how to check for the parallelism of the seal once it has been installed into the counter bore of the thermostat housing.
