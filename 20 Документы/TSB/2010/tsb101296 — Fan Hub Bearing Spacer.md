## Fan Hub Bearing Spacer

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This service/parts topic introduces a new fan hub bearing spacer for the QSK45 and QSK60 engines equipped with a direct drive fan hub. The spacer is located between the two bearings in the fan hub. Its purpose is to maintain preload on the bearings within the fan hub to increase durability.

Previous to the introduction of this spacer, the fan hub for QSK45 and QSK60 engines did **not** contain a spacer. When replacing the bearings or rebuilding the fan hub on engines built prior to the introduction of the fan hub bearing spacer, install the new spacer to the fan hub.

The spacer is denoted by arrow 12 in Figure 1 below.

The new spacer, Part Number 4100760, was introduced in production at Daventry with engine serial number first 33152994 on 19-August-2002 and is compatible with all QSK45 and QSK60 engines fitted with the direct drive fan hub.

This service/parts topic also includes a revision of procedure 008-036 (Fan Hub, Belt Driven) to include the installation of the fan hub bearing spacer.

This service/parts topic supercedes 02T8-4; replace 02T8-4 with this topic.
