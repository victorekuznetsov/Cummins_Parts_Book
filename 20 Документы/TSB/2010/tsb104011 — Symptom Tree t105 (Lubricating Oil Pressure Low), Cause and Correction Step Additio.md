## Symptom Tree t105 (Lubricating Oil Pressure Low), Cause and Correction Step Addition

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Service/Parts Topic revises troubleshooting symptom tree t105 (Lubricating Oil Pressure Low) in Service Manual, QSK45 and QSK60, Bulletin [[4021530 — QSK45 and QSK60 Service Manual\|4021530]]. The order of inspection has been revised to aid in troubleshooting. An additional Cause and Correction step has been added to check malfunctioning sensors in the common sensor voltage supply circuit.
