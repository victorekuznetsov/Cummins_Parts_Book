## Revision, Clean and Inspect for Reuse Steps, Lubricating Oil Cooler and Lubricating Oil Thermostat Procedures

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Service/Parts Topic identifies how to inspect the oil thermostat seat depth in the oil cooler housing in Procedure 007-003 (Lubricating Oil Cooler) and adds a link in Procedure 007-039 (Lubricating Oil Thermostat) in the Signature, ISX, QSX15 Service Manual, Bulletin [[3666239 — Signature™, ISX, and QSX15 Service Manual\|3666239]].

Shallow thermostat seat depth can lead to damaged oil thermostats and problems with oil pressure fluctuation. If the seat depth dimension is below the minimum specification, the oil cooler housing will need to be replaced.

| Distance From Seat to Spot Face at End of Lubricating Oil Cooler Housing |  |  |
|---|---|---|
| 123.47 mm | Minimum Dimension | 4.861 inch |

![[07c00546.png]]

Use this reference dimension for the following lubricating oil cooler housing part numbers: 4952496, 4952497, 4952507, and 4952508.
