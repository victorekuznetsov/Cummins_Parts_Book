## Two Stage Frame Identification

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This document informs the field of the changes made to two stage frames during design changes from initial product introduction to current production frames.

From initial introduction of the two stage frame there has been a total of eight changes in part number allocated to the mounting frame.

This document will give both a visual and a descriptive listing to identify which part number is the allocated frame.

![[10600476.png]]

Figure 1, Frame, Part Number 3411156

Frame, Part Number 3411156:

Initial frame design released for production two stage engines. The original "twin deck" frame with exhaust outlets mounted on plates above the high pressure turbochargers.

![[10600477.png]]

Figure 2, Frame, Part Number 4016774

Frame, Part Number 4016774:

Re-designed to introduce mounting the exhaust outlet pads to the "I" beam section with no cross braces.

A - Exhaust outlet mounting pads attached to center "I" beam only.

B - Central exhaust outlet mount is a two piece component.

C - Capped end, semi-circular load spreaders.

![[10600478.png]]

Figure 3, Frame, Part Number 4100475

Frame, Part Number 4100475:

A - Un-capped box section load spreaders.

B - Late frames have holes added for coolant loop mounting.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Early frames did **not** include holes for the coolant loop tubing. Later frames will have holes included.

![[10600479.png]]

Figure 4, Frame, Part Number 4101183

Frame, Part Number 4101183:

As frame number 4100475, except for:

A - Two tapped holes in center turbocharger mount support gusset

B - Two tapped holes in rear channel cross member.

![[10600480.png]]

Figure 5, Frame, Part Number 3635285

Frame, Part Number 3635285:

As frame number 4101183, except center "I" beam is constructed by two pressed steel sections welded together 'A'.

![[10r00145.png]]

Figure 6, Frame, Part Number 3635903

Frame, Part Number 3635903:

As frame number 3635285 except for modified and added side rail holes positions.

This frame is known as Tier 1 framework.

![[10r00146.png]]

Figure 7, Frame, Part Number 4927515

Frame, Part Number 4927515:

As frame number 3635903 except:

- The addition of side rail break to avoid contact with high pressure turbocharger re-orientation (A)
- The addition of tabs at load spreaders (B)
- Modified side rail holes positions (C)
- Removed pads (D)

This frame is known as initial Tier 2 framework.

![[10r00147.png]]

Figure 8, Frame, Part Number 3642933

Frame, Part Number 3642933:

Existing frame that is known as common framework for Tier 1 and Tier 2.
