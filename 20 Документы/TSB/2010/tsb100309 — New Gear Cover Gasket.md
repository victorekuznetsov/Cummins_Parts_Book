## New Gear Cover Gasket

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This purpose of This Technical Service Bulletin is to inform the field of an improvement made to the gear cover gasket used on QSK45 and QSK60 engines. The new gasket was introduced to improve sealing capability around the oil drilling orifices.

The old gear cover gasket, Part No. [[3332035]] was obsoleted and superseded by new gear cover gasket, Part No. [[4100983]].

The new gear cover gasket was released April 2003, with Engine Serial Number first of 33153813.
