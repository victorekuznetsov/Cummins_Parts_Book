## Revision, Troubleshooting Tree t021, Coolant Loss - Internal

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Service/Parts Topic introduces an additional step in the Coolant Loss - Internal troubleshooting symptom tree for the Signature™, ISX, and QSX15 Service Manual, Bulletin [[3666239 — Signature™, ISX, and QSX15 Service Manual\|3666239]].

An incorrect or malfunctioning radiator cap can have an effect on the cooling system and cooling system components. The second step in the Coolant Loss - Internal symptom tree identifies a cause and correction relating to the radiator cap.
