## Two Designs of Metering and Timing Actuator Kits Now Available from ReCon®

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Technical Service Bulletin introduces a change in the availability of ReCon® Metering and Timing Actuator Kits.

ISX metering and timing actuators have been available in a number of different designs. The two latest designs are virtually identical internally, but have either a stator housing sealed with an expansion plug and o-ring type mounting gasket or a stator housing plug with an o-ring seal and an embossed steel mounting gasket.

Until now, ReCon® **only** offered metering actuator kits (4089985RX) and timing actuator kits (4089986RX) with the stator housing plug using an o-ring seal and an embossed steel housing gasket. ReCon® is now offering both designs for sale as replacement part kits. The designs are interchangeable and either version can be installed without any problems.

Reference the illustrations below for the visual differences between the two designs of metering and timing actuator kits.

The release date for the Metering and Timing Actuator Kits for Service is: July 8, 2009.

This is a Service Part **only**.
