## Oversize Cylinder Liners

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Service/Parts Topic obsoletes and supersedes Topic 10T1-32, Topic 05T1-25, and Topic 06T1-19.

This Service/Parts Topic informs the field of oversize cylinder liners for use on the QSK45 and QSK60 Series engines.

The cylinder liners have been released for use when there is a requirement to machine the upper/lower press fit and or counterbore ledge due to any wear or fretting damage that may have occurred. During engine rebuild, the upper/lower press fit areas and counterbore ledge of the cylinder block should be inspected for any evidence of wear or fretting. Any cylinder bores that show signs of wear and/or fretting should be machined and an appropriate oversize cylinder liner fitted. Engine block upper and lower press fits that show no signs of wear or fretting need **not** have oversize cylinder liners fitted and standard cylinder liners can be used.

Three oversize cylinder liner parts along with oversize cylinder liner shims for the respective oversize cylinder liners have been released.

- 0.25 mm \[0.010 in\]
- 0.51 mm \[0.020 in\]
- 0.76 mm \[0.030 in\]

Standard non oversize cylinder liner shims can be used with both the standard cylinder liner and for the 0.25 mm \[0.010 in\] oversize cylinder liner. A 0.51 mm \[0.020 in\] oversize cylinder liner shim can be used for both the 0.51 mm \[0.020 in\] and 0.76 mm \[0.030 in\] oversize cylinder liners The oversize dimensional features of the cylinder liner apply to the upper press fit diameter, lower press fit diameter, and the upper press fit seating ledge. All other dimensional features of the cylinder liner remain unchanged. For available cylinder liner shims see Table 3.

The upper press fit (1 and 3), lower press fit (2 and 5) and the seating face dimensions (4) all increase by the respective cylinder liner oversize value. The 0.25 mm \[0.010 in\] oversize cylinder liner, has the upper press fit. The lower press fit and the upper press fit seating ledge dimensions all increased by 0.25 mm \[0.010 in\] from their nominal dimension values. See Figure 3. See Table 2 and 3.

Various shim thicknesses are available for both the standard and the 0.51 mm \[0.020 in\] oversize shim to enable the correct cylinder liner protrusion to be obtained upon engine assembly.

If a standard (non oversize) cylinder liner is to be fitted to an engine block, no work needs to be carried out on the engine block. If an oversize cylinder liner is to be fitted, the engine block **must** be machined for the respective oversize cylinder liner. The upper press fit (1), lower press fit (2), and upper press fit seating face features **must** all be machined to enable the correct fit of an oversize cylinder liner. See Figure 1. Any number of cylinder bores can be machined for oversize cylinder liners.
