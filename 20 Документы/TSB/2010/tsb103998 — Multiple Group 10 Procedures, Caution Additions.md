## Multiple Group 10 Procedures, Caution Additions

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Service/Parts Topic adds a Caution statement to Procedure 010-041 (Turbocharger Coolant Hoses), Procedure 010-045 (Turbocharger Oil Drain Line), and Procedure 010-046 (Turbocharger Oil Supply Line). The statement is below:

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> When installing flexible hoses be sure that all the original clips are fitted and that the hoses do not contact any other hoses or engine components. Interference between hoses and other engine components can lead to leakages and engine damage.
