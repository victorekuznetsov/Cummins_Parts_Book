## New Dipstick and Gage Tube Assembly

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Technical Service Bulletin announces a product improvement for a dipstick and gage tube assembly used with some QSX engines.

This change improves reliability of the oil gage tube, Part Number 3681847. This change will result in the obsolescence of both this gage tube and the matching dipstick. Both parts are superseded by an already existing dipstick assembly with the same calibration, and mounting location. Use the below table for supersession and detailed part replacement information.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The individual old parts are **not** compatible with the new parts. When replacing the dipstick or gage tube, the new assembly needs to be ordered. A male union is also required, order separately as shown in the table below.

| Part Description | Old Part Number | New Part Number | Additional Parts Required For Service (Ordered separately) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tube, Oil Gage | 3681847 | 3103339 (New Dipstick and Gage Tube as an Assembly) | [[4926324]] (Union, Male) |
| Dipstick | 3681848 | 3103339 (New Dipstick and Gage Tube as an Assembly) | [[4926324]] (Union, Male) |
