## New Lubricating Oil Filter Head and Oil Cooler Elements

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Technical Service Bulletin announces a new style of lubricating oil filter head and oil cooler element. This new part style will be used on all production engines built after the engine serial number (ESN) firsts listed.

No parts are superseded with this change. All previous lubricating oil filter head and oil cooler elements remain available for service **only**.

83 mm Oil Cooler Element Information:

New oil cooler elements have been released. These oil cooler elements have a height of 83 mm. Previous elements were 63 mm in height. The mounting capscrews have changed and a spacer is now used. A stainless steel oil cooler element will be offered for some engine ratings.

None of the 83 mm oil cooler elements are backwards-compatible with previous lubricating oil filter head housings. They **must** be used with the newly released single cooler cavity lubricating oil filter head.

Single Cavity Lubricating Oil Filter Head Information:

A new lubricating oil filter head has been released with several design changes including:

- a single deeper cooler cavity
- a permanent baffle
- an added OEM coolant port.

This lubricating oil filter head is required when the 83 mm oil cooler elements are used.
