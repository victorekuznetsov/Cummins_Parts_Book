## Main Bearing Capscrew Procedure Revision

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Service/Parts Topic introduces a new torque specification for use in the field during the installation of main bearing capscrews on the ISX engine family. This change will affect **only** older blocks produced prior to engine serial number (ESN) 79012120. The present specification will continue to be used on newer engines.

The procedure change will affect only the torque specifications.

This topic includes the following:

- Engine serial numbers which are affected by this change
- Instructions on determining the older USA block and the present production MEX block
- The new torque procedure specifications.
