## New Piston

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Technical Service Bulletin introduces new Ferrous Cast Ductile (FCD) single-piece, cast iron pistons for the QSK45 and QSK60 engines.

The new FCD piston is being released to improve scuff resistance during cold starts where the ambient temperature is below -4°C \[25°F\]. The new FCD piston has a revised skirt profile that reduces the contact pressure between the piston and liner during cold start conditions. The tables below outline the old part numbers and options affected for the High BMEP (Brake Mean Effective Pressure), Low BMEP, and TA Luft pistons. There is no restriction in using this new FCD piston with the old FCD piston design.

It is still recommended for the cold climates to use preheating instructions put forth in the Early Field Notification Number E07020.

The Engine Serial Number (ESN) first for production is 33171052, built November 5, 2007.
