### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Service/Parts Topic revises Procedure 007-031 (Lubricating Oil Pump) in Bulletin [[3666239 — Signature™, ISX, and QSX15 Service Manual\|3666239]], Signature™, ISX, and QSX15 Service Manual, to include instructions to reuse the external idler gear capscrew.

This capscrew is **not** listed for sale in the lubricating oil pump option. To obtain a replacement the capscrew, the complete lubricating oil pump option **must** be purchased.
