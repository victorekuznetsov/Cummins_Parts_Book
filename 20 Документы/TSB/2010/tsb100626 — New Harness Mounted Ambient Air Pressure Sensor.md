## New Harness Mounted Ambient Air Pressure Sensor

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Technical Service Bulletin announces the release of the harness mounted ambient air pressure sensor on heavy duty engines for production and service.

The engines affected by this change are the ISX CM570, QSX15 CM570, ISM CM570, and the QSM11 CM570.

When replacing the complete main engine harness on heavy duty engines, there will **not** be a connector on the harness that connects to the old style engine mounted ambient air/barometric pressure sensor. The new engine harness will come with a new ambient air/barometric pressure sensor already attached to the new harness. The old style engine mounted sensor will **not** be used and can be removed and discarded.
