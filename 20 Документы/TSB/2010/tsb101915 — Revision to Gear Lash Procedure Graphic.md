## Revision to Gear Lash Procedure Graphic

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Service Parts Topic identifies the graphic change for adjusting back lash when setting base engine timing in the Adjust step of Procedure 001-088, Engine Base Timing. The fourth graphic in the step did show the same location of the shim for both the top and bottom of the idler gear. Gear lash is consistently set on the load side of the teeth. A graphic change has been made to show the load location on the top of the idler gear is opposite of the load location on the bottom of the idler gear.
