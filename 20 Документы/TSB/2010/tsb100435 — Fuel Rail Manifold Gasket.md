## Fuel Rail Manifold Gasket

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Technical Service Bulletin supersedes topic 06T6-4; replace 06T6-4 with this topic.

This Technical Service Bulletin introduces the new fuel rail manifold gaskets for use on the QSK 45/60 engines.

The new gasket (nickel plated), Part Number [[3637870]], replaces the present gasket, Part Number 4086222. Please scrap any of the old gaskets, Part Number 4086222, that remain in distributor stock.

The new gaskets are nickel plated. This will reduce the possibility of any fretting issues seen with the old gasket on the one piece fuel manifold.

Torque for the fuel manifold mounting capscrews is unchanged.

The effective date for the new gasket is 29th November 2005.
