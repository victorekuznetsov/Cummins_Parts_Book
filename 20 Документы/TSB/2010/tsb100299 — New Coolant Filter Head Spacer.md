## New Coolant Filter Head Spacer

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Parts Announcement introduces a new coolant filter head spacer for the QSK45 and QSK60 engines, Part Number 4101315.

The new spacer Part Number [[4101325]] (1), allows the installation of the coolant pressure sensor, Part Number 3408556 (2), into the left bank side of the thermostat housing, with the sensor body and extension connector located between the two coolant filter canisters.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> This is a product improvement and is **not** subject to campaign.

![[08600628.png]]

Figure 1, New Installation Arrangement

The affected applications are all QSK45 and QSK60 engines, as detailed below:

The relocation of the coolant pressure sensor from the cylinder block jacket to the thermostat housing requires connector sub-harness, Part Number 3635690, and a new version of the calibration software. This configuration is now the standard on Industrial engines made on or after February 23, 2004, with serial number first 33155959. The relevant versions of the software are published on the calibration CD dated April, 2004, and later.

The relocation of the coolant pressure sensor from the cylinder block jacket to the thermostat housing requires **only** the new spacer, Part Number [[4101325]], as the Generator harness and software were designed for the sensor relocation. This configuration is now the standard on QSK45 Generator engines made on or after December 2002, with serial number first 33153135 and on QSK60 Generator engines made on or after April 7, 2003, with serial number first 33153788.

Please add the new part numbers to hard copies of the QSK45 and QSK60 Parts Catalogs.
