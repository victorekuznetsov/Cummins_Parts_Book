## Revision to Procedure 020-012, Engine Brake Solenoid Valve

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Service/Parts Topic introduces a change in the troubleshooting and repair Procedure 020-112, Engine Brake Solenoid Valve for the Signature, ISX, and QSX15 Engines Service Manual, Bulletin [[3666239 — Signature™, ISX, and QSX15 Service Manual\|3666239]]. The changes made are in the Inspect for Reuse section, to resistance check values for a 12-volt supply solenoid and to the measurement method.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> This is a product improvement and is **not** subject to campaign.

Resistance Values

The cold engine and hot engine resistance values should be between 5 and 20 ohms for solenoids that require a supply of 12 volts.

The table below indicates the old and new resistance check values for a 12-volt supply:

| Engine State | Old Resistance Value | New Resistance Value |
|---|---|---|
| Cold | 8 to 10 ohms | 5 to 20 ohms |
| Hot | 16 to 20 ohms | 5 to 20 ohms |

Measurement Method

In order to eliminate any measurement error, follow the steps below to check the resistance of the solenoid:

- Disconnect the wiring harness.
- Measure the resistance from the terminal to the body of the solenoid, as indicated in the illustration.
