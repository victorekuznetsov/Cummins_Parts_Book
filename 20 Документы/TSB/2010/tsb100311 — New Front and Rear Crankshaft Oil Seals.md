## New Front and Rear Crankshaft Oil Seals

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Technical Service Bulletin details the introduction of new front and rear crankshaft oil seals on the QSK45, QSK60, and QSK78 series engines. The new seals have been released to improve durability of system components.

The introduced oil seals are backward compatible and can be fitted as direct replacements. Mounting hardware remains unchanged for both front and rear seals.

The engine serial number first for the QSK45/60 was 33157377, built 4th of August 2004, and the engine serial number first for the QSK78 was 66300518, built 9th of August 2004.
