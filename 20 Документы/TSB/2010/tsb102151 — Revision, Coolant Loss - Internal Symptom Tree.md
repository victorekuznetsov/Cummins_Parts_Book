### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Service/Parts Topic revises symptom tree T021, Coolant Loss - Internal in the Signature, ISX, and QSX15 Service Manual, Bulletin [[3666239 — Signature™, ISX, and QSX15 Service Manual\|3666239]]. The revision adds a step to check the air compressor air inlet and air outlet tubes for signs of coolant and moves the step for pressure testing the cooling system.
