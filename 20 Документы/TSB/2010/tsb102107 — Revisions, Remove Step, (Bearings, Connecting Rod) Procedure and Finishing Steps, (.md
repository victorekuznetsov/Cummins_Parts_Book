## Revisions, Remove Step, (Bearings, Connecting Rod) Procedure and Finishing Steps, (Gear Housing, Front) Procedure

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Service/Parts Topic revises the Signature, ISX, and QSX15 Service Manual, Bulletin [[3666239 — Signature™, ISX, and QSX15 Service Manual\|3666239]].

1. The connecting rod guide pin part number has been added to the Remove step of Procedure 001-005 (Bearings, Connecting Rod).
2. A reference to Procedure 019-042 (Engine Speed Sensor (ESS)) has been added to the Finishing Steps of Procedure 001-033 (Gear Housing, Front) for installing the crankshaft position sensor.
