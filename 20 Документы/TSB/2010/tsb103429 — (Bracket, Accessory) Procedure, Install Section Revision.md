### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Service/Parts Topic revises the Install Section of Procedure 001-082 (Bracket Accessory) in Section 1 of the Signature™, ISX, and QSX15 Service Manual, Bulletin [[3666239 — Signature™, ISX, and QSX15 Service Manual\|3666239]]. An instruction to apply a heavy moly grease to the area of contact between the brace and the cylinder head has been added.
