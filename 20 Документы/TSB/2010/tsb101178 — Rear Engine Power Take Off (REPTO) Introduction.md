## Rear Engine Power Take Off (REPTO) Introduction

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This service/parts topic provides procedures for the Rear Engine Power Take Off (REPTO), Procedure 016-007, on QSX15 engines. The REPTO replacer kit, Part Number 31664761, provides all the necessary tools to remove and install the various components from the REPTO application on QSX15 engines equipped with REPTO units.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The replacer plate included in the rear crankshaft seal kit, Part Number 3162995, can be modified by drilling three additional holes. The six 17-mm \[21/32-in\] holes are equally spaced on a 138-mm \[5.433-in\] bolt circle. The additional three holes update the existing rear crankshaft seal tool for the new rear crankshaft seal replacer, Part Number 3164780.
