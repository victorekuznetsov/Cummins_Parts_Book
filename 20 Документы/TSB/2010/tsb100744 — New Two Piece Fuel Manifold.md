## New Two Piece Fuel Manifold

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Technical Service Bulletin introduces design changes to the QSK45 and QSK60 series engine fuel manifold.

The damage mode associated with the present fuel manifold is a fatigue crack that propagates from close to the mounting hole at the bottom edge of the manifold.

![[06600546.png]]

One Piece Fuel Manifold

![[06600547.png]]

Two Piece Fuel Manifold

When a one piece fuel manifold fails, it is recommended that the fuel manifolds on both banks be replaced with the two piece manifold.

The two piece fuel manifold will reduce motion between fuel manifold(s) and gasket(s).

The two piece fuel manifolds are connected by three fuel transfer tubes with non reusable o-rings.

![[06600548.png]]

Two Piece Fuel Manifold Transfer Tubes Detail

![[06600549.png]]

Fuel Transfer Tube o-rings and dust seals

The transfer tubes are protected by a fuel line shield as shown below.

![[06600550.png]]

Fuel Line Shield

The remaining inventories of the one piece fuel manifolds have been scrapped.
