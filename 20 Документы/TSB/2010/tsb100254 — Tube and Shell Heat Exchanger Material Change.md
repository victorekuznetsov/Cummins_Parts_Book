## Tube and Shell Heat Exchanger Material Change

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Technical Service Bulletin introduces an improved tube material for the tube and shell type heat exchangers used on K19 and K38 engines. Marine, Construction, and Power Generation applications use some or all of the part numbers affected.

The material change is from 90/10 copper nickel to 70/30 copper nickel. The 70/30 copper nickel material allows for longer durability in more corrosive environments.

All of the old part numbers for the heat exchanger cores and assemblies will be superseded for service. Refer to Table 1 for new part numbers.
