## New KV Lubrication Oil Filter Head Gasket

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

![[lf6hdha.png]]

This document announces the release of a new lubricating oil filter head gasket for use on K38, K50, QSK38, and QSK50 series engines built with inboard piston cooling nozzles. The gasket material has been changed from steel core to edge molded to increase durability and to improve engine reliability. The new edge molded gasket is used for both the inlet and outlet port connections of the lubricating oil filter head.

Engines built with outboard piston cooling nozzles can **not** use the new edge molded gasket and **must** use the old steel core gaskets for the inlet (1) and outlet (2) connections of the lubricating oil filter head. This is due to the oil supply for the outboard piston cooling nozzles coming through the outlet port of the lubricating oil filter head.

Since engines built with outboard cooling nozzles still require the steel core gaskets, they will continue to be available for service.

Use the following procedures for information on the lubricating filter head and gasket installation in the K38, K50, QSK38, and QSK50 Service Manual, Bulletin 4021528. [[28-007-015-tr — Lubricating Oil Filter Head|Refer to Procedure 007-015 in Section 7.]] [[28-007-067-tr — Eliminator™ Filter|Refer to Procedure 007-067 in Section 7.]]
