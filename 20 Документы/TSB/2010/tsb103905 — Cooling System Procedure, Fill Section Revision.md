## Cooling System Procedure, Fill Section Revision

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Service/Parts Topic revise the Fill section of Procedure 008-018 (Cooling System) in the Signature, ISX and QSX15 Service Manual, Bulletin [[3666239 — Signature™, ISX, and QSX15 Service Manual\|3666239]].

The changes provide new information in the procedure regarding the location and type of manual bleed valve(s) or pipe plug(s) installed on certain chassis.
