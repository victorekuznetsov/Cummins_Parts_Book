## Field Notification of Mislabels QSK60 Phase 2.0 CENSE™ Wiring Diagram, Bulletin 4021412 (Version-02 and -03)

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

The purpose of this document is to inform the field of three sensor and six wire mislabels on the Lube Oil Filter Extension Harness Connector for the QSK60 Phase 2.0 Industrial with CENSE™ Wiring Diagram, Bulletin 4021412. These mislabels exist on two versions of the wiring diagram, Bulletin 4021412-02 and 4021412-03. Reference Table 1 and Table 2 for the intended label information until Bulletin 4021412-04 of the wiring diagram is available.

| Table 1: Sensor Labels from the Lube Oil Filter Extension Harness Connector |  |
|---|---|
| Sensor Mislabels | Intended Sensor Labels |
| Intake Manifold Temperature **\#3 (Right Bank Rear)** Sensor | Intake Manifold Temperature **\#4 (Right Bank Rear)** Sensor |
| Intake Manifold Temperature **\#3 (Right Bank Front)** Sensor | Intake Manifold Temperature **\#2 (Right Bank Front)** Sensor |
| Intake Manifold Temperature **\#2 (Left Bank Rear)** Sensor | Intake Manifold Temperature **\#3 (Left Bank Rear)** Sensor |

| Table 2: Wire Labels from the Lube Oil Filter Extension Harness |  |
|---|---|
| Wire Mislabels | Intended Wire Labels |
| Intake Manifold Temperature **\#3 (Right Bank Rear)** 5 Volt Supply | Intake Manifold Temperature **\#4 (Right Bank Rear)** 5 Volt Supply |
| Intake Manifold Temperature **\#3 (Right Bank Rear)** Signal | Intake Manifold Temperature **\#4 (Right Bank Rear)** Signal |
| Intake Manifold Temperature **\#3 (Right Bank Front)** 5 Volt Supply | Intake Manifold Temperature **\#2 (Right Bank Front)** 5 Volt Supply |
| Intake Manifold Temperature **\#3 (Right Bank Front)** Signal | Intake Manifold Temperature **\#2 (Right Bank Front)** Signal |
| Intake Manifold Temperature **\#2 (Left Bank Rear)** 5 Volt Supply | Intake Manifold Temperature **\#3 (Left Bank Rear)** 5 Volt Supply |
| Intake Manifold Temperature **\#2 (Left Bank Rear)** Signal | Intake Manifold Temperature **\#3 (Left Bank Rear)** Signal |
