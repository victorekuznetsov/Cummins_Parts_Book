## Symptom Tree t021 (Coolant Loss - Internal), Cause Revision

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Service/Parts Topic revises Symptom Tree t021 (Coolant Loss - Internal). The revision changes the procedure reference for inspecting the air compressor air lines. This change improves troubleshooting logic by directing users to Procedure 012-007 (Air Compressor Cylinder Head, Single Cylinder) for an initial check of the air lines instead of Procedure 012-014 (Air Compressor) for air compressor removal.
