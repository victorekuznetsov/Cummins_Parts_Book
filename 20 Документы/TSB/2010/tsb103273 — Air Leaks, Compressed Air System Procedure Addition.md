## Air Leaks, Compressed Air System Procedure Addition

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This purpose of this Service/Parts Topic is to revise Procedure 012-019 (Air Leaks, Compressed Air System) in order to include this procedure in Section 4 of the Operation and Maintenance Manual, Bulletin [[3666260 — QSK45 and QSK60 Operation and Maintenance Manual\|3666260]]. This procedure needs to be performed for all compressed air systems to prevent carbon build-up and possible failure of the compressed air system.
