## Oil Leak at Lower Front Cover

### Core Issue

This Early Field Notification informs the field that a possible cause of an external oil leak from behind the vibration damper is a lower front gear cover manufacturing crack.

### Confirmation

ISX family engines starting with ESN 79372302, build date 08-May-09. This issue was corrected beginning 09-Jun-09 with ESN 79376451.

Symptoms and observations are an external oil leak that initiates behind the vibration damper and runs down the lower front gear cover.

Put dye in the oil and operate the engine to verify that the leak initiates from behind the vibration damper. Remove the vibration damper and inspect for a crack in the gear cover crankshaft seal boss at the 10 o'clock position. The typical crack location is at the bend transition between the flat cover surface and the raised stud boss.

Manufacturing issue at supplier resulting in a crack at this location.

None

### Resolution

Replace the lower gear cover.
