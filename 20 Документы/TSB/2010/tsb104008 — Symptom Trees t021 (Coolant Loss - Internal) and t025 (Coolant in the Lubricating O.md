### Contents

This Service/Parts Topic revises Symptom Trees t021 (Coolant Loss - Internal) and t025 (Coolant in the Lubricating Oil) in Section TS of the Signature™, ISX, and QSX15 Service Manual, Bulletin [[3666239 — Signature™, ISX, and QSX15 Service Manual\|3666239]].

The cause descriptions for injector sleeve is leaking (t021 and t025) and the first investigated instance of coolant in the lubricating oil (t025) have been revised.
