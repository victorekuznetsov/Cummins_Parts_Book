## Connecting Rod Reuse Guidelines at Rebuild or Repair

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Service/Parts Topic supercedes Service/Parts Topic 06T1-34. Replace Service/Parts Topic 06T1-34 with this Topic.

This Service/Parts Topic informs the field that angle split connecting rods installed in QSK45/60 engines in fracturing rigs, excavators, loader, and mechanically driven haul trucks **must not** be reused at rebuild or repair, regardless of hours. Angle split connecting rods installed in QSK45/60 engines in electrically driven haul trucks, power generation, marine or other applications **must only** be reused at rebuild or repair if the connecting rods have less than 10,000 hours and pass the reuse guidelines.

Straight split connecting rods from any application can be reused regardless of the hours, provided they pass the reuse guidelines.

Warranty Impact

Warranty will not cover connecting rod malfunctions in which the connecting rods were not replaced during previous rebuild or repair, as directed by this Service/Parts Topic. Warranty will also follow the directions outlined in this Service/Parts Topic during the adjudication of warranty claims and when giving repair direction. This Service/Parts Topic does not authorize campaign or TRP actions.

Parts Information

The connecting rods should be replaced with new or ReCon® "NX" connecting rods when needed. This is a product improvement and is not subject to campaign.

Repair Procedure

Angle split connecting rods from QSK45/60 engines in fracturing rigs, excavators, loader, and mechanically driven haul trucks should **not** be reused at rebuild or repair, regardless of hours. Angle split connecting rods from QSK45/60 engines in electrically driven haul trucks, power generation, marine or other applications should only be reused at rebuild or repair if the connecting rods have less than 10,000 hours on them. If the connecting rod does **not** pass the reuse guidelines, it **mustnot** be reused, regardless of hours.

Straight split connecting rods from any application can be reused at rebuild or repair, regardless of hours, provided they pass reuse guidelines.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Angle split and straight split connecting rods **can not** be mixed within an engine. All connecting rods must be the same design. Straight split connecting rods can **not** be installed into engines built prior to Engine Serial Number (ESN) 33172060, unless they have had a cylinder block upgrade.
