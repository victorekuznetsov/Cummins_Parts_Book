## Revision, General Information, Overhead Set (OBC) Procedure

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Service/Parts Topic intends to make it clear that QSK45 and QSK60 engines with the Quantum fuel system (mechanically actuated injectors) no longer require a valve and injector set to be performed at 1500 hours. Instead, the valves and injectors will **only** be adjusted when the injector is serviced.

This Service/Parts Topic revises the General Information step of Procedure 003-006 (Overhead Set (OBC)) to include a statement that reflects the guidelines for adjusting the overhead.
