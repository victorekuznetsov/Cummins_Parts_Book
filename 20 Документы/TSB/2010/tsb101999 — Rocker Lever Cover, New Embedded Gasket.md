## Rocker Lever Cover, New Embedded Gasket

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Service/Parts Topic is revising the Rocker Lever Cover, Procedure 003-011. The updates are including an additional remove and install procedure for the new rocker lever cover assembly, Part Number 3104387.

Engines built after September 7, 2006, will have a cover gasket embedded into the new rocker lever cover and will include the following items;

- Rocker lever cover
- Cover gasket
- Rocker lever cover isolators.
