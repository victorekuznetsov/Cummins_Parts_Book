## One Piece Oil Filter Head Introduction

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This service/parts topic obsoletes SPT01T7-12 and introduces a new one-piece oil filter head design for the QSK45 and QSK60 engines. Replace SPT01T7-12 with this one. The old design is a two piece assembly consisting of a back plate which attaches to the cylinder block and a filter head that attaches to the back plate. The new oil filter head is a one-piece design that bolts directly to the block eliminating the joint between the filter head and the back plate. The QSK45 oil filter head mounts three oil filters, the QSK60 oil filter head mounts four oil filters. The old two piece filter head has been obsoleted and replaced with the new one-piece filter head. If you order either of the old part numbers you will automatically receive a complete assembled one piece filter head.

Figure 1 shows the old design and figure 2 shows the new design.

![[07600434.png]]

![[07600437.png]]

Fig. 1, Old Design

Fig. 2, New Design

Other differences in the design include the amount of mounting capscrews; the new design uses eight instead of ten like the old design. The position of the CENSE™ post-oil filter pressure sensor on the QSK60 has moved from the rear of the oil filter head facing vertically downwards. The new position is at the rear of the oil filter head facing horizontally toward the rear of the engine.

The new one piece filter head went into production at the Daventry Engine Plant for the QSK45 with engine serial number first 33151960. The approximate engine serial number first for QSK60 generator set engines is 33144999 and for industrial QSK60 is 33149441.
