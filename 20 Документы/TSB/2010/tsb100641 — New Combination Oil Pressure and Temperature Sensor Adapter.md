## New Combination Oil Pressure and Temperature Sensor Adapter

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Technical Service Bulletin announces the release of a new combination oil pressure and temperature sensor adapter, Part Number 4918864. The combination oil pressure and temperature sensor adapter is intended for use on the ISX with CM570 and the QSX with CM570 engine applications, in conjunction with the combination oil pressure and temperature sensor, Part Number [[4921475]].

Engines built after January 1, 2003 do **not** require the adapter.

When installing the combination oil pressure and temperature sensor adapter, be sure the adapter has the o-ring fitted and torque the adapter to 14 n•m \[124 in-lb\]. Install the combination oil pressure and temperature sensor onto the adapter and torque to 14 n•m \[124 in-lb\].

It is **not** necessary to use the new adapter with the previous version of the combination oil pressure and temperature sensor, Part Number 3417185.
