## Troubleshooting Symptom Trees, Multiple Step Revisions

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Service/Parts Topic announces multiple changes to the Troubleshooting Symptom Trees T101 (Lubricating Oil in the Fuel) and T102 (Lubricating Oil Consumption Excessive) in the Signature™, ISX and QSX15 Service Manual, Bulletin [[3666239 — Signature™, ISX, and QSX15 Service Manual\|3666239]].

Troubleshooting Symptom Tree T101 (Lubricating Oil in the Fuel), has been modified to include steps for performing a dye test before checking for asphaltines in dark fuel, inspecting the cylinder head injector bore, replacing the upper plunger seal, and inspecting the Integrated Fuel System Module (IFSM) mounting gasket before leak testing the fuel passages.

Troubleshooting Symptom Tree T102 (Lubricating Oil Consumption Excessive) has been updated to include the dye test for lubricating oil in the fuel, replacement of the upper injector seals, and replacement of the injectors.
