## Rocker Lever Support Capscrews

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

Remove from your files Service/Parts Topic 96T3-1A and replace it with this topic. This topic corrects procedure 003-009 in the above mentioned manuals. The revised procedure has the correct torque value that **must** be used for both aluminum and cast-iron rocker shaft support assemblies.

This topic applies to M11 and ISM/QSM11 engines.

A new mounting capscrew has been released for the rocker lever supports for all M11 and ISM/QSM11 engines. The new capscrew is made of a higher grade material which does **not** allow a higher assembly torque. The new capscrew, Part Number [[3080722]], is a Class-10.9 capscrew. It replaces capscrew, Part Number 3067382, which is a Class-9.8 capscrew. The new capscrew was introduced in production on 10 April 1995 with engine serial number 34771894.
