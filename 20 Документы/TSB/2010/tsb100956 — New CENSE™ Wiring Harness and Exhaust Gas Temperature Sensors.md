## New CENSE™ Wiring Harness and Exhaust Gas Temperature Sensors

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This document introduces a new CENSE™ wiring harness and exhaust gas temperature sensors for use on QSK45 and QSK60 series engines.

The new CENSE™ wiring harness contains an additional clipping location for the exhaust gas temperature sensor harness. The new exhaust gas temperature sensor kit contains an additional grommet seal.

The new and old style CENSE™ harnesses can be mixed within an engine at repair. The installation procedure requires the harness to have a second grommet seal added to the exhaust gas temperature sensor harness in the additional clipping location.

The modified CENSE™ harness and sensors went into production at the Daventry Engine Plant on April 12, 2010. Engine Serial Number (ESN) first is 33180597.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> There are changes to the installation procedure.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> New and old CENSE™ harnesses can be mixed within an engine.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> This a product improvement, and is **not** subject to TRP and campaign actions.
