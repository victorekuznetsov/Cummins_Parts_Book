## Addition, Guided Steps 1C and 1C-1, Fault Code 433

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Service/Parts Topic adds Steps 1C and 1C-1 in Fault Code 433 in the Signature, ISX, and QSX15 Electronic Control System, Troubleshooting and Repair Manual, Bulletin 3666259, to include voltage checks at the sensor and at the ECM to determine if low voltage from the ECM or high resistance in the harness is the cause of a fault.
