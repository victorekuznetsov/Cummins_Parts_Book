## New Oil Suction Tube and Brackets

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Technical Service Bulletin introduces a new lubricating oil suction tube and brackets for QST30 rear deep sump applications; Option OP 5137 **only**. The new oil suction tube and brackets have been designed to reduce cracking in the oil suction tube, caused by vibration.

The new oil suction tube, Part Number 4975629, obsoletes and supersedes the old oil suction tube, Part Number 3092243. The new bracket, Part Number 4975644, and two clamps, Part Number 4975653, obsolete and supersede the old bracket, Part Number 3092242. Reference the table below for old and new part numbers within Option OP 5137.

The new tube can be identified by the bend in the horizontal portion of the suction tube. Compared to the new tube, the old tube is straighter, as illustrated in Figure 1. The new bracket design has been changed from a side-mount design, as illustrated in Figure 2, to a center-mount design, as illustrated in Figure 3.

The engine serial number (ESN) first for this change is 37244312.
