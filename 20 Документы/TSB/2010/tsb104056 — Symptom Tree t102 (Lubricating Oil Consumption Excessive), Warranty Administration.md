## Symptom Tree t102 (Lubricating Oil Consumption Excessive), Warranty Administration Manual Link Addition

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Service/Parts Topic revises the "Lubricating Oil Consumption Excessive" symptom tree, t102, in the Signature™, ISX, and QSX15 Service Manual, Bulletin [[3666239 — Signature™, ISX, and QSX15 Service Manual\|3666239]]. This tree now includes a link to Procedure 502-016 in the Warranty Administration Manual, Bulletin 4021290. This procedure contains the oil consumption specifications that are needed as the oil consumption rate is verified in the first cause and correction step in t102.
