## Rocker Lever Spacer - Inspect For Reuse

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This service/parts topic describes a wear condition of the injector rocker lever spacer that can result in an interference condition in the overhead. In addition, this topic defines the warranty impact and updates the rocker lever assembly procedure: 003-009.

Engineering stack-up analysis and on-engine measurements have determined that injector rocker lever spacer wear over 1.5 mm \[0.060 in\] can result in interference between the injector rocker lever and the intake valve rocker lever. Applying this wear limit to the spacer thickness results in a minimum acceptable spacer thickness of 11.5 mm \[0.453 in\].

As shops conduct repairs in which overhead parts are removed or adjusted (i.e. head gasket repairs, injector replacements, etc.), they are to visually check parts. Should injector rocker lever spacer wear be identified during the visual check, mechanics should consider the condemnation limit and replace **only** those spacers that have excessive wear as defined by the condemnation limit. Spacers replaced as part of the repair are to be included in the claim and the claim will be paid accordingly. That is, replacement of the 'abnormally worn' spacer(s) will be covered under warranty if the engine is still within the base engine warranty period.

As repair shops conduct repairs in which overhead parts are removed and rocker lever interference is found, the affected levers and injector rocker lever spacers are to be replaced. Also, all other spacers are to be inspected. Spacers **not** meeting the condemnation limit are to be replaced.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Warranty will **not** cover the replacement of an injector rocker lever spacer with a thickness greater than the 11.5 mm \[0.453 in) condemnation limit.
