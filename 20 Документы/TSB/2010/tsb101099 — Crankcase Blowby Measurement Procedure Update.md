## Crankcase Blowby Measurement Procedure Update

### Core Issue

This document announces the addition of specific instructions for measuring blowby on engines equipped with an external coalescent breather filter.

### Confirmation

For engines equipped with external coalescent breather elements (ECMs CM871, CM876, CM2250, CM2150, and engines equipped with Enviroguard filters), connect the appropriate blowby service tool to the crankcase breather tube on the inlet side of the coalescent breather housing.

![[14p00004.png]]

Make sure to install the service tool on the inlet side.

![[14p00005.png]]

### Resolution

By installing the blowby service tool into the inlet side crankcase breather tube, the potential for inaccurate blowby measurements caused by varying filter restrictions is eliminated.

Use the following procedure for measuring crankcase blowby in the following manuals:

- Refer to Procedure 014-010 in Section 14 in Service Manual, ISX11.9 CM2250, Bulletin 2883445.
- [[101-014-010-tr — Crankcase Blowby, Measure|Refer to Procedure 014-010 in Section 14]] in Service Manual, Signature™, ISX, and QSX15, Bulletin [[3666239 — Signature™, ISX, and QSX15 Service Manual\|3666239]].
- [[101-014-010-tr — Crankcase Blowby, Measure|Refer to Procedure 014-010 in Section 14]] in Service Manual, ISM, ISMe, and QSM11, Bulletin [[3666322 — ISM, ISMe, and QSM11 Service Manual\|3666322]].
- Refer to Procedure 014-010 in Section 14 in Service Manual, ISZ13 CM2150, Bulletin 4022133.
- Refer to Procedure 014-010 in Section 14 in Service Manual, ISM CM876 SN, Bulletin 4022196.
- Refer to Procedure 014-010 in Section 14 in Service Manual, ISX15 CM2250, Bulletin 4022250.
