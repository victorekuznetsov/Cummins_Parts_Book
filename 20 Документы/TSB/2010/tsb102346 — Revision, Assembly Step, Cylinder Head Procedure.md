## Revision, Assembly Step, Cylinder Head Procedure

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Service/Parts Topic announcs a new drop gauge, Part Number 4918779. The drop gauge is used to measure valve guide straightness and diameter after pressing the valve guide into the cylinder head during cylinder head rebuild on the QSK45 and QSK60 engines.
