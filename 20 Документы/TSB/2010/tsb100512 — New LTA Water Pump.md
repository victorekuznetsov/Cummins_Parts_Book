## New LTA Water Pump

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Service Parts/Topic announces the introduction of a revised LTA water pump, Part Number 3638083, and the changes that have been included.

The improvements included a change from greased bearings to oil fed bearings, which has increased lubrication. The oil feed is taken from the side of the gear cover.

Additional changes included improved water and oil seals, increased pump body strength, and the introduction of a discharge cavitation plate to prevent cavitation damage to the pump body.

The older style and revised LTA water pumps look similar in appearance, due to the main developments occurring internally.

![[08600820.png]]

New Oil Feed, Part Number 3638095, (with new banjo type connector)

The strength of the pump body has been improved by adding a stiffening rib, as shown in the diagram below, and the introduction of a one-piece pump body.

![[08600821.png]]

The stiffening rib is shown, marked A.

The part number for the revised LTA water pump is as follows:

| Old Water Pump Part Number | Revised Water Pump Part Number |
|---|---|
| 3636850 | 3638083 |

The effective date of production was 7th August 2006, ESN 1st 33165335.

If a new water pump (Part Number 3638083) is to be fitted in place of an old water pump (Part Number 3636850), the following parts will be required:

| Part Number | Quantity | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 3638095 | 1 | Hose, Flexible |
| [[3963982]] | 2 | Washer, Sealing |
| 68152 | 2 | Clip |
| [[3682701]] | 1 | Elbow, Male Union |
| 3638094 | 1 | Screw, Banjo Connection |
