## Lubricating Oil Pump Procedure, Multiple Section Revision

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This service/Parts Topic introduces multiple revisions to Procedure 007-031 (Lubricating Oil Pump) in the QSK45 and QSK60 Service Manual, Bulletin [[4021530 — QSK45 and QSK60 Service Manual\|4021530]].

A Machine section has been added to the procedure. This new section shows a process for reworking the old lubricating oil pumps, Part Numbers 4100710 and 3638986 for the QSK45 engines and Part Numbers 3638987 and 4100711 for the QSK60 engines, to provide a clean oil supply to the lubricating oil pump idler bushing.
