## New Pre-Lube Oil Pressure Shutdown Switch

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Technical Service Bulletin announces the release of a new oil pressure switch for use on the QSK45 and QSK60 pre-lubrication system.

The original pre-lube pressure switch, Part Number 4065465, is replaced with Part Number 4101287. The original switch has a pressure setting of 17.2 kPa \[2.5 psi\] and the new switch has an improved pressure setting of 31.0 kPa \[4.5 psi\].

The new pressure switch went into production at the Daventry Engine Plant on May 13, 2003. The engine serial number first is 33154015.

This is a product improvement and is **not** subject to campaign.
