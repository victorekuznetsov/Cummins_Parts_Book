## Revision to Engine Brake Piston Procedure, Actuator Piston Reuse

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Service/Parts Topic introduces a revision in the Remove and Inspect for Reuse steps in Procedure 020-019, Engine Brake Piston. The change made is in the event of an actuator piston malfunction on a lever. Replace the lever that contains the malfunctioning actuator piston.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> This is a product improvement and is **not** subject to campaign.
