## New Hand Hole Cover

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This service/parts topic introduces a new hand hole cover for the QSK45 and QSK60 series engines. The new hand hole cover is introduced as part of the new crankcase breather system that replaces the conventional head deck and crankcase side mounted breather arrangement.

The new hand hole cover went into production at the Daventry Engine Plant from Engine Serial Number first of 33152044 for the QSK45 and 33153219 for the QSK60.
