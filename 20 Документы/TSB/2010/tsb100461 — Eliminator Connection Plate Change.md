## Eliminator Connection Plate Change

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Technical Service Bulletin informs the field of an Eliminator connection plate change for use on the QSK45/60 Series engines.

The Eliminator connection plate material has been changed from aluminum to steel. Both parts are interchangeable and are exactly the same, apart from material type. The current o-ring (Part Number [[3393437]]) **must** still be used with this plate.

The graphic below shows the component as installed on the engine and as a separate component.
