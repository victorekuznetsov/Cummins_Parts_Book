## Rear Crankshaft Seal Correction – Incorrect Component Used During Engine Assembly

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

**Product Affected**

- QSK19 CM2150 MCRS
- QSK19 CM500
- K19

**Description of Change**

There are two distinct rear crankshaft seal designs: a dry seal, which prevents engine lubricating oil from entering the flywheel housing, and a wet seal, which prevents transmission fluid entering the engine in applications with a wet clutch or fluid sump flywheel housing.

Dry rear crankshaft seals were incorrectly installed during engine assembly for certain wet flywheel housing options, leading to incorrect part numbers showing on parts.cummins.com for these engines. Operation could lead to fluid ingress from flywheel housing to engine oil, possibly resulting in increased engine lubricating oil level and reduced engine lubricating oil quality. It is advised to confirm the appropriate seal is used for any service event that requires replacement of the rear crankshaft seal.

The following flywheel housing options were incorrectly built with dry rear crankshaft seals, listed by build plant. Engines with these options should be operating with a wet rear crankshaft seal (see Service Parts section).

| **Seymour (CIC)** | **India(CIL)** | **China(CQP)** |
|---|---|---|
| FH4025-03 FH4027-05 FH4151-02 FH4151-03 FH4160-04 | FH4151-02 FH4192-02 FH4196-02 | FH4025-02 FH4160-03 |

The option used to build a particular engine can be verified by searching the Engine Serial Number on parts.cummins.com and selecting ‘Parts Catalog – Option View', then searching for the FH option. The two digit number following the hyphen “-“ on an option number is a plant-specific revision designation. **Only** the option revisions listed above are affected.

**Service Instructions**

If changing from a dry to wet rear crankshaft seal, an additional o-ring seal is required during installation. O-ring seal part number is listed in the Service Parts section.

Follow published remove and install procedures for rear crankshaft seal and o-ring seal.
