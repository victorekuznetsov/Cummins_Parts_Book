## Fleetguard® CH44105 & CH44120 Centrifuge Retainer Change

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

A batch of Fleetguard® CH44105 centrifuges has been produced that have out of specification top retainers. The new style retainer (\#2 in Figure 1) was a cost reduction design change to the CH44105 & CH44120 products purchased by Fleetguard®. The new style retainer is out of specification in flatness and parallelism. This causes the edge of the retainer to act as a cutting tool against the bushing material on the top of the internal rotor shaft, causing the bushing to malfunction. Additionally, the smaller outside diameter (OD) of the retainer has an increased load per unit area (\#2 in Figure 2).

This has been corrected by Fleetguard® by reverting all centrifuges in stock back to the older style retainer. In Plant the engine serial number (ESN) first for engines built with known good centrifuges is 33189155, built 12 September 2011.
