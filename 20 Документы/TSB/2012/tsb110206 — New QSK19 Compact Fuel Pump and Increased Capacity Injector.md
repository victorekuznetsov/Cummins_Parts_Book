### Contents

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> This document supersedes topics 99T5-9, 99T6-9, 00T5-1, and 00T6-10; replace 99T5-9, 99T6-9, 00T5-1, and 00T6-10 with this one.

This document corrects part numbers for the P10 fuel pump (found on page 1) and the OEM interface harness (found on page 5). The correct part numbers are as follows: P10 fuel pump, Part Number 3088415, and OEM interface harness, Part Number 3331775.

This document introduces the new compact fuel pump and the increased capacity trapped volume spill injector for the QSK19.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The product improvements will affect **only** some new production engines built after December 1998. The present injectors, Part Number 3867761 and Part Number 3867762, and the P10 fuel pump, Part Number 3088415, will continue to be available for service.

**Increased Capacity Trapped Volume Spill Injector**

The increased capacity trapped volume spill injector, Part Number 4010029, will provide increased fuel-flow capacity for the QSK19 power generation engines and the marine applications that require more power density than the QSK19 industrial applications. This injector will also have common features of the other Quantum™ engine fuel systems, such as the QSK45 and 60. The effect of drain line restriction on start of injection variability, which affects performance and engine durability, will be reduced with the use of the injector. The injector adds robustness to some of the main components with improvements in design and manufacture.

**Compact Fuel Pump**

The compact fuel pump is about 10 cm \[4 in\] shorter than the replaced P10 pump. The reduced length permits the use of a two-cylinder air compressor on the QSK19 engines. The compact pump achieves this reduced length by eliminating most of the fuel-wetted components of the P10 pump, which include governor weights, gears, shafts, plungers, sleeves, springs, and shims. The compact pump provides fuel pressure by a precision three-piece gear pump with a floating-gear design for improved efficiency and durability. In the compact pump, the pressure is electronically controlled by an actuator and pressure sensor; this makes it less sensitive to wear and changes in fuel properties by providing more precise control of the pump pressure.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> This is a product improvement and is **not** subject to campaign.

**Harnesses and Brackets**

The engine harness, OEM harness, ECM, and brackets have been changed as of January 1998. They will continue to be used with the new injector and the compact fuel pump. The part numbers follow:

- Engine harness, Part Number 3331774
- OEM interface harness, Part Number 3331775
- ECM, Part Number 3867743

**Calibrations**

New fuel system and new electronic fault codes have been established. **The new fault codes are:**

- 118 - Fuel Pump Pressure Sensor Circuit Failed High
- 119 - Fuel Pump Pressure Sensor Circuit Failed Low
- 316 - Fuel Pump Actuator Circuit Error
- 318 - Fuel Pump Fueling Flow Mismatched.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> For additional details on fault code descriptions, refer to the Wiring Diagram, Bulletin 3666133-03.

There will be no calibrations for the old injector, Part Number 3867761 and Part Number 3867762, with compact pump or the new injector, Part Number 4010029, with the P10 pump. A new software calibration is required for a new injector with compact pump.

For troubleshooting procedures, refer to the Troubleshooting and Repair Manual, QSK Fuel System, Bulletin 3666113.

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Both of the new fuel system components are not interchangeable with prior fuel system components. This is due to the design of the components and the software calibrations. The engine and OEM wiring harnesses installed before January 1998 also are not compatible with the new parts.
