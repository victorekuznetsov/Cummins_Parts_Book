## New QSK45/60 Tier 1 Exhaust Gas Temperature Sensors and Harnesses

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This document announces the release of new Exhaust Gas Temperature Sensor (EGTS) Harnesses and Sensors for Tier1 QSK45 and QSK60 engines. The following improvements have been applied to the harness and sensor:

- Higher temperature rated EGTS connector
- Improved EGTS connector location
- Improved EGTS wiring harness routing
- Improved EGTS harness rail mounting

The new EGTS hardware is **not** backward compatible with previous versions, therefore previous versions will continue to be available for service to allow repairs to be completed without requiring a complete hardware change.

New and old hardware can be mixed within an engine. For example, on a QSK45 engine, one bank can be new hardware and one bank old hardware.

The new EGTS hardware went into production at the Daventry Engine Plant on 2 April 2012, engine serial number (ESN) first 33192570.
