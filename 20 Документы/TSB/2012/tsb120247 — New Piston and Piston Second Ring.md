## New Piston and Piston Second Ring

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This document announces the release of a new piston and piston second ring for use on QSK60 and QSK45 Tier 1 Diesel engines.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> This piston change does **not** apply to engines that use the low brake mean effective pressure piston. **Only** the ring pack change applies to engines using the low brake mean effective pressure piston.

The new piston and piston second ring have been released to improve engine durability.

The new piston has a cut back top land to prevent carbon buildup in this area.

The new piston second ring has a larger ring gap to reduce pressure buildup between the top and second rings during normal operation.

With the release of the new piston and second ring, a new piston and piston ring kit will be released. Present and new part numbers are shown in Table 1.

Present and new pistons and piston second rings can be mixed in an engine, but Cummins Inc. recommends the new piston and new piston second ring be installed together in the same cylinder.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The present piston, piston second ring, piston kit, and piston ring kits, are still available for service on industrial and marine application engines.

This change went into effect on QSK60 Power Generation engines at the Daventry Engine Plant on 05-Sep-2012, with engine serial number (ESN) First 33194947.

The change was made on the Industrial and Marine engines at the Daventry Engine Plant on 08-Apr-2013.The engine serial number (ESN) First for Industrial engines are 33197388 (QSK60) and 33197652 (QSK45). The ESN First for QSK60 Marine engines is 33197451.
