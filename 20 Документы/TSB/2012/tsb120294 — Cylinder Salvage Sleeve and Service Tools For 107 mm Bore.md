## Cylinder Salvage Sleeve and Service Tools For 107 mm Bore

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This document is announcing the availability of a cylinder salvage sleeve (repair sleeve) for the 107 mm bore for the ISB amd QSB engines.

The following table summarizes what repair sleeve and service tools to use, depending upon engine type.

| Engine Displacement (L) | Nominal Bore (mm) | Machined Bore Diameter For Sleeve Installation (mm) | Salvage Sleeve Part Number | Sleeve Driver Part Number | Use of Torque Plate, Part Number 2892406 (Optional/Required) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5.9 | 102 | 104.500 to 104.515 | 3904166 | 3823230 | Optional |
| 4.5 RGT | 107 | 109.700 to 109.715 | 4919951 | 2892407 | Required |
| 6.7 | 107 | 109.700 to 109.715 | 4919951 | 2892407 | Required |

Refer to the appropriate section in the service manual for the detailed procedure.

Engines can be repaired in any variation of stock, oversized, and sleeved cylinders. Mismatching will **not** affect the rotating balance of the power cylinder components.
