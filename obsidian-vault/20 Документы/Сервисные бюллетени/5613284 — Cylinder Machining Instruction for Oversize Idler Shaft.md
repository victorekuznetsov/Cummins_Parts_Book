## 1.1 Overview

On some cylinder blocks the idler shaft bores were found damaged during service events. In order to be able to reuse the cylinder block, two kits of oversize idler shafts were created for QSK45/QSK60 and QSK78 (all except K126 and K137 series). There is also a QSK78 Accessory Comp oversize Idler Shaft for QSK78 CM2350 K154C engines which have the MCRS Fuel system upgrade. This document provides the information on how to oversize the idler shaft bore and use oversize idler shaft kit. Reference Figure 1, Table 1, and Table 2 below for parts familiarization with the cylinder block and the current idler.

If the oversize of one of the idler shaft bores is required, the four idler shaft bores will be oversized (Dimension D on Figure 4) and the four current idler shafts and screws replaced by the oversize idler shaft kit.

For QSK78 CM2350 K154C engines which have the MCRS Fuel system upgrade, Idler Shaft, Part Number 6315345, and kit, Part Number 5628813, will need to be ordered. The accessory comp idler shaft, Part Number [[5467076]], of kit, Part Number 5628813, will be replaced by part number 6315345. Reference Figure 4 for existing and oversize shaft drawing specifications.

## 1.2 Procedure Selection

Use the service kit with oversize idler shaft (Part Number [[5581413]] for QSK60 and Part Number 5628813 for QSK78). All the idler shafts and screws **must** be replaced.

## 1.1.1 Validate Existing Bore Position

- Clean all idler bores thoroughly, remove debris, and degrease.
- Remove burrs and raised metal from idler bores around damage areas.
- Fit Pins Part A pin to each bore.
- Hammer into place with slide hammer to full depth of finished hole beyond damage.
- Select large digital Vernier scale and measure across pins to establish the radial distances.
- Calculate the actual distance by subtraction of the half pin dimension and record.
- Reference the data shown in Table 3 to confirm the bores are in position.
- If all dimensions are within specification, proceed to step 2. If errors found in measurements, reverify, and calculate adjustment needed to correct bore position before proceeding.

## 1.1.2 Components Set Up and Cut Oversize to Verified Positions

- Clean the cylinder block around the lubricating oil pan face, set onto machine and set up square to machine axis for front face.
- Verify by sweeping the front face of the cylinder block with an indicator clock, adjust until front face is square to axis.
- Move spindle location to in front of idler bores in turn with pins located.
- Use a centricator device in the machine spindle to adjust machine position and find the centre of the Part A pin diameter (see Part A drawing in appendix).
- Lock the machine axis position, move the spindle away laterally from the pin.
- Remove the centricator device from the spindle.
- Remove the Part A pin with a slide hammer.
- Keeping the machine position, fit an appropriate boring bar to spindle. Use machine to cut idler bore to oversize tolerance.
- Validate and adjust final bored size as necessary to achieve oversize.
- Repeat for each bore in turn.

## 1.1.3 Validate Final Bore Size and Position

- Once machined and final bore sizes verified and recorded, reconfirm achieved idler bore positions by use of the following steps.
- Repeat section one measurement checks process, use Part B pin (see Part B drawing in appendix) inserted into the oversize idler bores.
- Complete the records chart (see appendix 1.2.4) in full to confirm calculated positions are in expected tolerance.

## 1.1.4 Identification and Component Marking

- Oversize parts are different part numbers and are marked as oversize. Verify they are identified and kitted according to the correct cylinder block with corresponding bores.
- Mark the cylinder block as oversize “OS 0.5 mm” on the front face, adjacent to each idler location outside of the contact face, in readable 10 mm \[3/8 in\] stamped characters.
- Record in engine rebuild records, the use of the oversize set for future part ordering purposes.

## 1.1.5 Final Check to Confirm Achieved Gear Backlash Clearances are Achieved on Reassembly

- See corresponding Service Manual. Reference Procedure 001-055 in Section 1, as a final check is performed.

## 1.3 Appendix

## 1.2.1 Gaging and Processing Equipment Requirements

- Suitable mill borer in good condition with axis locks, Centricator attachment with ± 0.002 mm increment scale.
- Fine boring bar with micron adjust to achieve final cut size.
- Comparator bore gage to measure bore size with 0.002 mm reading discernment.
- Large Vernier scale with digital reading, discernment ± 0.002 mm.
- Part A and Part B pins.
- Crank centre bar, ST number 3164872.
- Cam centre bar, 112 DIA g6 toleranced plain pin x 100mm long dimensions in the standard. (This cam centre bar is part of the kit ST-1177 / 3163649).

## 1.2.2 Service Tools Required

- Part A Pin x 4, Part B Pin x 4.
- Crank Centre Bar, Part Number 3164872.
- Cam Centre Bar Diameter 111.988mm MAX 111.966mm MIN, x 125 long.
- Boring machine in good condition with ideal horizontal spindle, or appropriate alignment vertical with 90-degree head attachment.
- Gages Required.
