## Self-Calibrating Variable Geometry Turbocharger (VGT) Actuator

**Product Affected**

- X15 CM2350 X114B
- X15 CM2350 X116B
- X15 CM2450 X124B
- X15 CM2450 X134B

**Description of Change**

- This document announces a new VGT actuator that has a self-calibrating capability.
- INSITE™ electronic service tool is **not** required to install/calibrate the new VGT actuator.

**Reason for Change**

New self-calibrating VGT actuator can be installed in place of non self-calibrating VGT actuator. See Table 1.

To facilitate field installation of the turbocharger actuator without the use of INSITE™ electronic service tool.

**Service Instructions**

Use of incorrect procedure will result in electronic service tool status message indicating the procedure was stopped or failed.

Reference the corresponding Service Manual procedure if using a self-calibrating VGT actuator part number, identified by the instructions in the VGT actuator kit box. See Table 2.
