**I. Introduction**

- This document provides reuse guidelines for the aftertreatment selective catalytic reduction (SCR), in conjunction with the information provided within the Service Procedures listed in Table 7 below.
- The reuse guidelines contained in this document are the **only** approved inspection methods and pass/fail criteria for inspecting aftertreatment SCR for reuse.

**II. Reuse Guidelines Summary**

| Aftertreatment Selective Catalytic Reduction (SCR) Reuse Guidelines |  |  |  |  |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Component | Corresponding Table and Figure | Condition | Approach | Additional Remarks |
| SCR Inlet | 1 | New with writing on face | Reuse | See Table 1 below |
| SCR Inlet | 2 | Iron oxide deposits | Reuse | See Table 2 below |
| SCR Inlet | 3 | DEF deposits | Conditional Reuse | See Table 3 below |
| SCR Inlet | 4 | Erosion | Reuse | See Table 4 below |
| SCR Inlet | 5 | Water stains | Conditional Reuse | See Table 5 below |
| SCR Inlet | 6 | Sodium deposits | Conditional Reuse | See Table 6 below |

Aftertreatment SCR Housing Reuse Guidelines:

- The aftertreatment SCR housing **must** be free of dents.
- All sealing flanges **must** be free of dents, cracks, or gouges to seal properly with gaskets.

**V. Inspection**

An aftertreatment SCR can show damage through signs of contamination with coolant, oil, or fuel.

Repair Direction: Any oil, fuel, or coolant-soaked aftertreatment SCR catalyst **must** be replaced.
