## Guide to Finding The Aftertreatment Device From an Engine Serial Number using QuickServe® Online

This guide can be referenced when locating the aftertreatment device part number using QuickServe® Online (QSOL), in instances when the original aftertreatment device is **not** available for inspection.

The best method of obtaining the correct replacement part number for the aftertreatment device fitted to your vehicle is by examining the part number on the component.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> The catalyst substrate contains Vanadium Pentoxide. Vanadium Pentoxide has been determined by the State of California to cause cancer. Always wear protective gloves and eye protection when handling the catalyst assembly. Do not get the catalyst material in your eyes. In case of contact, immediately flood eyes with large amounts of water for a minimum of 15 minutes. Avoid prolonged contact with skin. In case of contact, immediately wash skin with soap and water.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> The exhaust and exhaust components can remain hot after the engine has been shut down or secured. To avoid the risk of fire, property damage, burns or other serious personal injury, allow the exhaust system to cool before beginning this procedure or repair and make sure that no combustible materials are located where they might come in contact with hot exhaust or exhaust components.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> This component or assembly weighs greater than 23 kg \[ 50 lb \]. To prevent serious personal injury, be sure to have assistance or use appropriate lifting equipment to lift this component or assembly.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> To reduce the possibility of personal injury, wear goggles and protective clothing.

The Cummins® part number will be stamped/etched on the casing of the aftertreatment device, reference A in Figure 1. The Cummins® part number you require is identified as the “ASSY NO”.

Figure 1, Location of Cummins® Part Number on Aftertreatment Device.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Figure 1 shows a typical aftertreatment device **only** to demonstrate where the Cummins® part number is located (A). Other aftertreatment devices may vary.

The following steps can be used to assist in finding the aftertreatment device and is considered as a last resort in circumstances where the original aftertreatment device is **not** available for inspection.

1. Insert the engine serial number (ESN), of the engine that requires aftertreatment device identification. In the ESN entry box on QuickServe® Online (QSOL). Select the \> key to the right of the input box. QSOL will now load all the information that is relevant to this ESN.
2. Select the Parts Tab on the QSOL menu bar that appears near the top of the screen beneath the QSOL banner.
3. Under the Parts Information menu near the center of the screen, select the Parts Catalog (Option Part Detail).
4. The screen now displays a listing of options that **only** relate to the ESN that has been entered into QSOL. The options are listed in alphabetical order. Locate the “PE\*\*\*\*” option. This option is the aftertreatment device option that contains the aftertreatment system components. Select the “PE....” option. The following picture will be displayed. See Figure 2 below.

![[11r00010.png]]

Figure 2, PE Option Image

1. The actual aftertreatment device required is **not** listed. PE options can have more than one aftertreatment device. (The catalyst element will be common in all devices within a given option number). Steps 6, 7, and 8 detail how the correct aftertreatment device can be found.
2. Insert the aftertreatment option number (PE....), in the search box in the top right corner of the QSOL screen. Press the \> key just to the right of the search box. QSOL will now list all possible aftertreatment systems connected with the PE option that has been determined by the ESN. (The aftertreatment device part number is found within the aftertreatment system).
3. Decide which aftertreatment device is required. Examine a graphic of each aftertreatment system listed and determining which aftertreatment system is required.
4. Select the first aftertreatment system part number. QSOL will now display a graphic of the aftertreatment device. If the aftertreatment device shown is fitted to the vehicle, order the aftertreatment device parts number that is shown. If the aftertreatment device shown is **not** fitted to the vehicle, examine the next aftertreatment device.
5. Repeat Step 6, with the next aftertreatment system number listed.
6. If the aftertreatment device is **not** able to be be visually identified by using the aftertreatment system graphics listed, contact the Cummins® Customer Assistance Center or local Customer Support contact.
7. Before contacting the Cummins® Customer Assistance Center, make a note of all the aftertreatment device part numbers that are listed within the aftertreatment system part numbers. The aftertreatment device part numbers will need to be provided to the Customer Assistance Center and **not** the aftertreatment system numbers.
8. Once aftertreatment device part number have been provided, the Cummins® Customer Assistance Center will be able to determine key dimensions of the aftertreatment device. The aftertreatment device assembly dimensions will provide overall lengths and inlet/outlet diameters to help in identifying the correct aftertreatment device for the vehicle.
9. Order the aftertreatment device parts needed.
