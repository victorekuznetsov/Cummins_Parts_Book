## Cummins® - Branded Prestolite Starters, Alternators, Service, and Repair

This Service Bulletin covers service intervals, service, repair kits, standard repair times, service tools, and literature to support Cummins® - Branded Prestolite Alternators and Starter motor, for the B,C,L,M,N,K, and Q Series engines.

## Section 1. Service Intervals

It is recommended that the branded Starters and Alternators be maintained at the following service intervals:

- Starters:

20,000 strikes, 120,000 Miles \[192,000 Km\], 10,000 Hours, or 2 Years; whichever occurs first.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Under circumstances where the number of strikes are **not** accumulated at a fast rate (e.g. stand-by gensets), perform service after 20,000 strikes, **not** hours of operation or years.

- Alternators:

120,000 Miles \[192,000 Km\], 10,000 Hours or 2 Years; whichever occurs first.

It is recommended that before starting a Starter motor or Alternator service or repair, reference be made to Section 7 in this service bulletin.

When performing Starter or Alternator service or repairs, general safety precautions **must** be followed to provide personal safety, as outlined in Cummins Inc. service manuals.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Batteries can emit explosive gases. To reduce the possibility of personal injury, always ventilate the compartment before servicing the batteries. To reduce the possibility of arcing, remove the negative (-) battery cable first and attach the negative (-) battery cable last.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Acid is extremely dangerous and can damage the machinery and can also cause serious burns. Always provide a tank of strong soda water as a neutralizing agent when servicing the batteries. Wear goggles and protective clothing to reduce the possibility of serious personal injury.

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> To prevent damage to the Starter, do not engage the starting motor for more than 30 seconds at a time.

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> The Starter is an intermittent duty electric motor that is capable of high outputs for short periods of time. Excessive cranking of the starting motor will lead to a component malfunction.

Wait 2 minutes between each attempt to start (electrical starting motors **only**).

If the engine does **not** start after three attempts, check the fuel supply system.

Absence of blue or white exhaust smoke during cranking indicates that fuel is **not** being delivered. The starter **must not** be used to prime the fuel system. Reference the appropriate base engine Troubleshooting and Repair Manual for the correct engine specific priming procedure.

## Section 4. Warranty Information

The following is for Cummins® Branded Presolite™ Starters and Alternators Service and Warranty.

All Cummins Inc. engine plants phased in the introduction of Branded Prestolite™ Starters and Alternators fitted to engines from September 1997. These Starters and Alternators will have new dataplates which state that this product is built for Cummins Inc. by the accessory supplier.

These products will be covered under New Engine Warranty on all applications except for the U.S./Canada Recreational Vehicle (RV), School Bus, Fire and Crash Truck, where the coverage is limited to 2 years. The appropriate legal description has been revised to reflect this change of coverage. This warranty applies to the Cummins® Branded Starters and Alternators **only**. If a Cummins® branded Starter or Alternator fails during the warranty period due to a defect in material or workmanship, Cummins Inc., at its option, will replace/repair the Starter or Alternator without charge to the owner.

In order to receive warranty reimbursement for Cummins® branded Starters and Alternators, the Cummins Inc. branded dataplates **must** be confirmed.

The use of ReCon® is the standard method for replacing failed components. The warranty tag in the ReCon® box is to be completed, attached to the failed core and returned to Cummins Inc. UKDRC for the UK and MPDC for Europe, Middle East, and Africa. The warranty tag has to match with the submitted warranty claim. Repairs, instead of ReCon® exchange, could possibly be carried out if the cost is less, or if usage of ReCon®, is **not** feasible. For this, Cummins Inc. issued Rotary Electric repair kits have to be used in connection with proper tooling and by trained personnel. The Standard Repair Times for Starters and Alternators have been updated to reflect the use of these products.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> This is the sole and exclusive warranty applicable to these products. There are no other warranties, expressed or implied, including no warranties of fitness for a particular purpose and merchantability. Under no circumstances will Cummins Inc. be liable for incidental or consequential damage.
