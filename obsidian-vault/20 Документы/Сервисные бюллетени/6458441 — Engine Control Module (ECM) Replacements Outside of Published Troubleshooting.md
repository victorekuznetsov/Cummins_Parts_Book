## Engine Control Module (ECM) Replacements Outside of Published Troubleshooting

**Product Affected**

- All engine models equipped with an engine control module (ECM)

**Summary**

ECMs have been found to have a higher than expected rate of analysis results being Trouble Not Found (TNF).

**Issue**

- Repeat malfunctions, fault codes, or symptoms resulting in an incorrect replacement of an ECM.

**Root Cause**

- ECMs are being replaced outside of published troubleshooting.
- Technical escalation process is **not** being followed.

**Resolution**

- The ECM claim may be subject to adjustment or denial if replaced outside of published troubleshooting or escalation process. See warranty memo M2202 and M2206 for more information.
- Watch the video below to understand options prior to an ECM replacement that is not supported by published troubleshooting or diagnostics. **Note:** [http://tsb.cumminsvirtualcollege.com/6458441\_en.aspx](http://tsb.cumminsvirtualcollege.com/6458441_en.aspx)
- If necessary, follow the technical escalation process prior to replacing the ECM
