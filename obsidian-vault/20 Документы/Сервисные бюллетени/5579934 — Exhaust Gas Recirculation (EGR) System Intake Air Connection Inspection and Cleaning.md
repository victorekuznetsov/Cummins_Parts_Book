## Exhaust Gas Recirculation (EGR) System Intake Air Connection Inspection and Cleaning Guidelines

**Table of Contents**

1. Products Affected
2. Introduction
3. Inspection and Cleaning of Intake Air Connection Exhaust Gas Entrance Ports
4. Inspection and Cleaning of Air Intake Connection Differential Pressure Sensor Ports/Cross Drillings

**I. Products Affected**

All midrange engines with exhaust gas recirculation (EGR)

**II. Introduction**

- This document provides guidelines to inspect and clean the intake air connection in the exhaust gas recirculation (EGR) system.
- The intake air connection combines EGR flow and fresh air flow into charge flow. Charge flow then goes through the air intake grid heater prior to reaching the air intake manifold.
- EGR flows through the intake air connection exhaust gas entrance ports prior to entering the fresh air flow stream. A differential pressure is measured across one of the exhaust gas entrance ports. The engine control module (ECM) uses this measurement to estimate EGR flow.
- EGR flow measurement by the ECM is accurate when the intake air connection exhaust gas entrance ports are clean or lightly sooted.
- EGR differential pressure sensor ports/cross drillings need to be maintained in a clean condition to ensure that EGR flow measurement by ECM is accurate.
- Air Intake Connection exhaust gas entrance ports and EGR differential pressure sensor ports/cross drillings may accumulate moderate soot deposits that can change the ECM's EGR flow measurement accuracy.

**III. Inspection and Cleaning of Intake Air Connection Exhaust Gas Entrance Ports**

- Figures 3, 4, and 5 below show multiple examples of soot accumulation in the exhaust gas entrance ports in various Air Intake Connections.
- Verify the engine is off and that the EGR system has been given time to cool down.
- Remove the Air Intake Connection from the engine. See corresponding Service Manual. Reference Procedure 010-080 in Section 10.
- Inspect the exhaust gas entrance ports on the Air Intake Connection for any soot deposits using Figures 3, 4 and 5 below. Figure 2 below is a reference to how a normal Air Intake Connection should look.
- If moderate soot deposits are observed inside the Air Intake Connection exhaust gas entrance ports proceed to thoroughly clean the Air Intake Connection. Use a nylon brush to clean off any loose soot and use a solvent to clean out the rest of the deposits. Verify that EGR differential pressure sensor ports (vertical and horizontal) are also cleaned out. Reference Section IV in this document. See corresponding Service Manual. Reference Procedure 010-080 in Section 10.
- If shellac (hard, shiny that is not able to be cleaned with a nylon brush) is found on the part proceed to replace the Air Intake Connection.
- After cleaning, install the Air Intake Connection onto the engine. Reference Figure 6 below for a visual on how the Air Intake Connection should look like after proper cleaning. See corresponding Service Manual. Reference Procedure 010-080 in Section 10.
- After the Air Intake Connection has been installed on the engine check for leaks on the intake and the exhaust systems. See corresponding Service Manual. Reference Procedure 010-024 in Section 10.

**IV. Inspection and Cleaning of Air Intake Connection Differential Pressure Sensor Ports/Cross Drillings**

- Verify the engine is off and that the EGR system has been given time to cool down.
- Remove the Air Intake Connection from the engine. See corresponding Service Manual. Reference Procedure 010-080 in Section 10.
- Reference Figure 1 for an overview of the Air Intake Connection and to locate the EGR differential pressure sensor ports.
- Inspect the EGR differential pressure sensor ports/cross drillings on the Air Intake Connection for any soot buildup. Use a flashlight and place against each port to inspect. Determine if any visible light can be observed on the other end of each port or if any chunks of soot are present. Reference Figures 7, 8, and 9.
- If soot deposits are observed inside the Air Intake Connection EGR differential pressure sensor ports/cross drillings, proceed to thoroughly clean them. Use a nylon brush and solvent to remove all soot found inside. Reference Figures 10-12 for locations of EGR differential pressure sensor ports/cross drillings. See corresponding Service Manual. Reference Procedure 010-080 in Section 10.
- Inspect the EGR differential pressure sensor ports/cross drillings for any visible signs of soot deposits. Use a flashlight to look inside ports and inspect other side of each port to confirm that some light is visible. Reference Figures 13-15 for a visual example.
- Use Figure 1 above to locate the exhaust gas entrance ports. If EGR exhaust gas entrance ports are found to contain excessive soot deposits, reference Section III in this bulletin for instructions on how to clean both sides.
- After cleaning is completed, install the Air Intake Connection onto the engine. See corresponding Service Manual. Reference Procedure 010-080 in Section 10.
- After the Air Intake Connection has been installed on the engine check for leaks on the intake and the exhaust systems. See corresponding Service Manual. Reference Procedure 010-024 in Section 10.
