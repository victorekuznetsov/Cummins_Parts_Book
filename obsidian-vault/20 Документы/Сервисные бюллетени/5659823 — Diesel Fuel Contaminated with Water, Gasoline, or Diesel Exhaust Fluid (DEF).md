## Diesel Fuel Contaminated with Water, Gasoline, or Diesel Exhaust Fluid (DEF)

This document contains generalized information to cover the full range of Cummins® products. For engine specific fuel system information, see the corresponding Operation and Maintenance manual and Owner's manual.

The appropriate fuel recommendations and specifications for engines can be found in the Fluids for Cummins® Products Service Manual, Bulletin [[5411406 — Fluids for Cummins® Products Service Manual\|5411406]]. Operating the engine using fuels and additives that do **not** meet the specifications may cause symptoms including, but **not** limited to, the following:

- Crank No Start
- Runs Rough
- Low Power
- Exhaust Smoke
- Low Fuel Rail Pressure

Follow the appropriate Service Procedure depending on whether the diesel fuel system has been contaminated with water, gasoline, or DEF.

**Procedure A- Diesel Fuel Contaminated with Water, Engine Started**

1. Remove, drain, and clean the fuel tank to prevent reintroducing the contaminants.
2. Replace all fuel system components.
3. Flush any remaining fuel lines with clean diesel fuel.
4. Install and fill the tank with recommended fuel. See Fluids for Cummins® Products Service Manual, Bulletin [[5411406 — Fluids for Cummins® Products Service Manual\|5411406]].

**Procedure B- Diesel Fuel Contaminated with Water, Engine not Started**

Follow Procedure B **only** if the ignition key has **not** been inserted or turned to the ON position. Turning the key to the RUN position may operate the fuel priming pump and supply the contaminated fuel to the high-pressure fuel system.

1. Verify there is **not** water in the Fuel Water Separator.
2. Remove, drain, and clean the fuel tank.
3. Install and fill the tank with recommended fuel. See Fluids for Cummins® Products Service Manual, Bulletin [[5411406 — Fluids for Cummins® Products Service Manual\|5411406]].

**Procedure C - Diesel Fuel Contaminated with Gasoline, Engine not Started**

Follow Procedure C **only** if the ignition key has **not** been inserted or turned to the ON position. Turning the key to the RUN position may operate the fuel priming pump and supply the contaminated fuel to the high-pressure fuel system.

1. Remove, drain, and clean the fuel tank.
2. Install and fill the tank with recommended fuel. See Fluids for Cummins® Products Service Manual, Bulletin [[5411406 — Fluids for Cummins® Products Service Manual\|5411406]].

**Procedure D - Diesel Fuel Contaminated with Gasoline, Engine Started**

1. Remove, drain, and clean the fuel tank to prevent reintroducing the contaminants.
2. Perform Fuel System Flush.
3. Replace all fuel filters on the vehicle.
4. Install and fill the tank with recommended fuel. See Fluids for Cummins® Products Service Manual, Bulletin [[5411406 — Fluids for Cummins® Products Service Manual\|5411406]].

**Procedure E - Diesel Fuel Contaminated with DEF, Engine Not Started**

Follow Procedure E **only** if the ignition key has **not** been inserted or turned to the ON position. Turning the key to the RUN position may operate the fuel priming pump and supply the contaminated fuel to the high-pressure fuel system.

1. Remove, drain, and clean the fuel tank.
2. Install and fill the tank with recommended fuel. See Fluids for Cummins® Products Service Manual, Bulletin [[5411406 — Fluids for Cummins® Products Service Manual\|5411406]].

**Procedure F - Diesel Fuel Contaminated with DEF, Engine Started**

1. Remove, drain, and clean the fuel tank to prevent reintroducing the contaminants.
2. Replace all fuel system components.
3. Install and fill the tank with recommended fuel. See Fluids for Cummins® Products Service Manual, Bulletin [[5411406 — Fluids for Cummins® Products Service Manual\|5411406]].
