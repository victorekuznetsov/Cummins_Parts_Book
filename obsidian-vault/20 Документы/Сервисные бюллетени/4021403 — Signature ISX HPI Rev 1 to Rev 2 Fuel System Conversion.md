## Signature/ISX HPI Rev 1 to Rev 2 Fuel System Conversion

## Introduction

This publication contains information regarding the correct procedure to convert a 1998/99 Australia (ADR) Certified ISX Family 1 (ISX engines with horsepower rating between 400 and 465 hp) or 1998/99 ISX Family 3/Signature engine (ISX engines with horsepower ratings above 525 hp) from the Revision 1 HPI fuel system to the Revision 2 HPI fuel system. This procedure was developed in order to address the issue of parts availability of the Revision 1 fueling or metering actuators and injectors. In the event that a Revision 1 fueling or timing actuator, or injector is **not** available for service needs, this procedure **must** be followed in order to repair the engine correctly and maintain emissions standards compliance. This procedure will comply with Field Fix Number 316 listed in the Control Parts List, Bulletin 4021326.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Wiring kit, Part Number 3164855, **must** be used during this repair.

Below is a brief summary of the component changes required. Refer to the Troubleshooting and Repair Manual, Signature, ISX and QSX15 Engines, Bulletin [[3666239 — Signature™, ISX, and QSX15 Service Manual\|3666239]], for additional procedures required to convert Australia (ADR) ratings.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Australia (ADR) conversions **must only** be performed during extensive engine repairs or engine rebuild, due to the steps necessary to perform the conversion in these ratings.
