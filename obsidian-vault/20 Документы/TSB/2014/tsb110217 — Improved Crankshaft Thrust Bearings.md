## Improved Crankshaft Thrust Bearings

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This document introduces new thrust bearings for the K19 and QSK19 engines in service and production.

The new standard thrust bearings obsolete and supersede the old bearing. Oversize bearings also obsolete and supersede the old oversize bearings. The new bearings make use of an improved material for increased component durability. The new bearing design does **not** affect the removal or installation procedures.

The new ESN first for the change in plant is 37251000.

New and ReCon® engines built on or after 06-Sep-2011 will have the new thrust bearings.
