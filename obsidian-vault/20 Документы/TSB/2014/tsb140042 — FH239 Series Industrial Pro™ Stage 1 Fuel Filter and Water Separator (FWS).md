## FH239 Series Industrial Pro™ Stage 1 Fuel Filter and Water Separator (FWS)

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This document announces the improved FH239 Series Industrial Pro™ fuel filter and water separator (FWS), featuring NanoNet™ Technology for stage 1 filtration on all Cummins® high horsepower engines equipped with modular common rail fuel systems (MCRS). The new FH239 Series Industrial Pro™ FWS features the following improvements:

- Better filtration performance on all Cummins® MCRS engines (four times better than previous design)
- 500 hour fuel filter service intervals\*\*
- Weight reduction
- Increased water holding capacity (five times more than previous design)
- Reduction in sealing joints
- Optional fuel heater

\*\* Reference the Fuel Filter Change Intervals section of the Maintenance Schedule in the appropriate Operation and Maintenance Manual.

The improved FH239 Series Industrial Pro™ FWS comes in two sizes. The short version is to be used on the QSK 19 MCRS engines and the tall version is to be used on the QSK 38/50/60 MCRS engines. These were implemented in production in December of 2013.

The FH239 Series Industrial Pro™ FWS represents the latest advances in fuel filtration technology. Installation of the FH239 system will provide the highest level of protection for the fuel system through the use of the highest quality Cummins Filtration™ NanoNet™ filter elements.
