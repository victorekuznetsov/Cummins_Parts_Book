## New Rear Gear Camshaft Idler Shaft

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This document introduces a replacement rear gear camshaft idler shaft and associated parts.

**Product Affected**

QSK23 CM500

QSK23 CM2250 K109

QSK23 PG CM850

**Description of Change**

Replacement idler gear with bushing and idler gear retainer released with replacement rear gear camshaft idler shaft. See Figure 1 and 2.

![[01d00870.png]]

Figure 1, Obsolete Design

1. Camshaft idler shaft
2. Idler gear (large)
3. Idler gear with bushing
4. Gear retainer

![[01d00871.png]]

Figure 2, Replacement Design

1. Camshaft idler shaft
2. Idler gear (large)
3. Idler gear with bushing
4. Gear retainer

**Reason for Change**

New design includes improved clamp load for rear gear camshaft idler shaft and improved idler gear bushing lubrication.

**Service Instructions**

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Obsolete rear gear camshaft idler shaft and idler gear with bushing not compatible with replacement parts. Component oil lubrication will be affected, engine damage will result.

Replacement part numbers recommended for use.

**Service Parts Availability**

Service parts are available. See Table 1.

| Table 1, Rear Gear Camshaft Idler Shaft and Gear Part Numbers |  |  |
|---|---|---|
| Part Name | Obsolete Part Number | Replacement Part Number |
| Camshaft Idler Shaft | 4097269 | [[4365225]] |
| Idler Gear with Bushing | 4096764 | 4365226 |
| Gear Retainer | 4096709 | [[4365229]] |

**Part Structure**

Camshaft idler shaft, idler gear with bushing, and gear retainer part numbers have been obsoleted. When replacing any of these parts, all must be replaced.
