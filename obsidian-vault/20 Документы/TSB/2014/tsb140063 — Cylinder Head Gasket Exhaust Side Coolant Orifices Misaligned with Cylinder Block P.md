## Cylinder Head Gasket Exhaust Side Coolant Orifices Misaligned with Cylinder Block Passages

### Core Issue

Heavy duty cylinder head gaskets with Part Number [[4299098]] have 12 orifices on the exhaust side of the engine that are primarily used for venting the cooling system. Some gaskets have the potential for three of these orifices to be misaligned with the coolant passages in the cylinder block.

### Confirmation

With the cylinder head gasket located on the cylinder block, misaligned orifices will be identifiable near cylinders 5 and 6 (reference Figure 1).

![[02r00002.png]]

Figure 1, New Cylinder Head Gasket on Cylinder Block

1. Regular Orifice.
2. Misaligned Orifice.

### Resolution

New cylinder head gaskets identified with misaligned orifices in these locations are fit for service and should be used, **not** scrapped.

Gaskets removed from service are **not** considered failed or defective as a result of misaligned orifices observed in these locations.
