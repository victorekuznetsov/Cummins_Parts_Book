## ISX15 and QSX15 External Coolant or Oil Leak from Lubricating Oil Cooler

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

**Product Affected**

ISX15 and QSX15 engines.

**Description of Change**

Lubricating oil cooler gasket has a thicker bead.

**Reason for Change**

External coolant or oil leak from lubricating oil cooler. The thicker lubricating oil cooler gasket bead improves sealing capability.
