## New Flywheel Housing

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Service Parts Topic is to inform the field of the introduction of a new flywheel housing for NT855R and N14ER horizontal rail engines.

There have been reports of flywheel housing cracking in high mileage, overhauled engines. This has been seen on some rail applications using remote transmissions. Whilst there is no evidence of this problem with the same flywheel housing used in similar applications throughout the world, to minimize the occurrence of cracks developing, a revised design flywheel housing has been released. The new flywheel housing, Part Number 3287698, obsoletes the old flywheel housing, Part Number 3251848.

The new flywheel housing is similar in appearance to the old flywheel housing. However, the changes to the new flywheel housing include, changes in the material specification to Spheroidal Graphite Cast Iron, and an increase in wall thickness.
