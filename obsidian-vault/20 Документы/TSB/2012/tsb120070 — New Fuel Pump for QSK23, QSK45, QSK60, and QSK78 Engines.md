## New Fuel Pump for QSK23, QSK45, QSK60, and QSK78 Engines

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This document introduces a new fuel pump for use on QSK23, QSK45, QSK60, and QSK78 industrial engines.

The new pump is a modified design. It has modified internal pump body geometry that allows installation of the seal retainer to improve pump durability.

The new pump is compatible for installation with the old pump.

The new pumps have been installed on new engines, starting with engine serial number (ESN) first 33192035 for QSK45 and QSK60 and ESN first 66302303 for QSK78, at the Daventry Engine Plant. The old pump has **not** been obsoleted and will continue to be available for service use on power generation engines.
