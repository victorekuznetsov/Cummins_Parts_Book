## K Series Cylinder Head Changes (T206)

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> This is an update of Service Parts Topic 94T2-3. Remove and replace Service Parts Topic (SPT) 94T2-3 with this new topic. This topic releases the following new parts for K Series cylinder heads.

- Exhaust Valves, Part No. [[3088389]] and 3088391, and Intake Valve Part No. 3088393 have chrome plated valve stems for increased durability. In addition, all new K valves will also be chrome plated. See Table VIII, Part No. [[3088389]] replaces Part No. [[3803528]], Part No. 3088391 replaces Part No. 3803529, and Part No. 3088393 replaces Part No. 3803515.
- Cylinder head assemblies are now produced **without** valve crosshead guide. The valve crosshead guide is **not** required with the release of the stemless valve crosshead, Part No. [[3086362]]. The valve crosshead guide hole is still machined in the cylinder head casting, for serving heads with stemed crossheads, but the stemless crosshead can be used on all K Series engines.
- Valve Spring, Part No. 3630628 has been redesigned to stop wear at the first active coil that can progress to a fatigue failure. Part No. 3630628 replaces Part No. 3629007.

**Premium No. 1:** Cylinder head assembly, Part No. 3811985 is for use on high load factor and constant speed applications such as marine, base load or prime power generator drives, some drilling machines, and hydraulic excavators. This cylinder head assembly contains powdered metal valve guides, valve rotators, shorter springs, wear resistant Tribaloy intake valves and valve inserts, and high temperature Inconel exhaust valves. Reference Tables 1, Premium No. 1, for further part number information.

**Premium No. 2:** Cylinder head assembly, Part No. 3811986 is for use on uprate engines that do **not** require the Tribaloy intake valve. This cylinder head is used on all K Series engines with a maximum design rating of 101 horsepower (hp) per cylinder or more that do **not** require the Premium No. 1 cylinder head assembly, Part No. 3811985. The difference between the two cylinder head assemblies is Premium No. 2, Part No. 3811986 does **not** have the Tribaloy valves and valve inserts. Reference Table 1, Premium No. 2, for further part number information.

**Improved Standard Cylinder Head:** Improved standard cylinder head assembly, Part No. 3811987 is for use on all K Series engines with a maximum design rating of 100 hp per cylinder or less but **not** prime power or base load units. This assembly contains standard intake valves, Stellite 6 exhaust valves, and standard valve seat inserts but does **not** contain rotators. Reference Table 1, Standard, for further part number information.

**Standard Cylinder Head:** Cylinder head assembly, Part No. 3811988 is used in standby generator engines **not** prime power or base load units. This assembly contains standard intake valves, Stellite 6 exhaust valves, and standard valve seat inserts but does **not** contain rotators. Reference Table 1, Standard, for further part number information.

**Special Application Cylinder Heads:** Cylinder head assembly, Part No. 3811989 is for use on locomotive applications and was used on some hydraulic excavators. It contains standard valves, flat-top cast iron valve guides **with seals on all four valve guides**, but does **not** contain rotators. These valve seals are used in applications where there is a large percentage of idle time that can cause oil or fuel slobber. Reference Table 1, Special Application, Part No. 3811989, for further part number information.

Cylinder head, Part No. 3630361 is for use on horizontal rail car applications only. It contains specially machined valve guides for improved valve stem lubrication on horizontally mounted K19 engines. This part number replaces Part No. 3811990 that did **not** include a valve stem seal. Reference Table 1, Special Applications, Part No. 3630361 for further part number information.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> When installing a valve guide, Part No. 3235286, the oil hole in the guide **must** be pointed toward the closest crosshead guide or crosshead guide bore so the valve guide and stem will receive adequate lubrication.

**Tribaloy Intake Valve:** Intake valve, Part No. 3052820 is available for the K Series engine. This valve is faced with an extremely wear resistant material called Tribaloy. The Tribaloy intake valves are used on specific applications where valve beat-in has been a problem. This intake valve is very effective at reducing the rate of intake valve beat-in when used with rotators and shorter valve springs. These high load constant speed applications include prime and base load generator sets, marine applications, some drilling machines, and hydraulic excavators.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Do **not** use Tribaloy intake valve, Part No. 3052820, without the Tribaloy intake valve insert, Part No. 3052819 and valve rotator, Part No. 3065936 or 3081081. Also do **not** use the Tribaloy intake valve in the exhaust position.

The valve head thickness of the Tribaloy intake valve, Part No. 3052820, **must** be measured with the valve head thickness tool, Part No. 3823800. The intake valve head thickness tool, No. 3375940, **must not** be used on the Tribaloy intake valve to prevent grinding the Tribaloy facing too thin.

**Valve Spring Rotators:** Valve spring rotators were released to decrease valve guide wear. The rotator allows the valve spring to naturally rotate the valve as the valve opens. The rotator prevents the valve from rotating back to the original position. The rotator fits between the cylinder head and the valve spring in place of the lower valve spring guide, Part No. 3001663. Because the rotator adds approximately 9.525 mm \[3/8 in\] in height to the valve spring assembly, a shorter valve spring **must** be used in conjunction with the valve rotator.

When install a K or KV valve rotator, Part No. 3081081 a rotation check of the rotator in the free state can **not** be done. When turning the rotator by hand, it may turn roughly, appear difficult to turn, or **not** turn at all. Rotators that do not turn freely by hand are **not** "seized". The difficulty in turning the rotator is **not** uncommon. Small dimensional variations in components can make some rotators appear tight, but when the valve spring load is applied, the rotator compresses. This provides more clearance between the collar and the body of the rotator, allowing it to rotate freely.

![[kn6spka.png]]

- The rotator **must** be soaked in clean engine oil for at least 5 minutes prior to installation.
- The rotator **must** be installed over the valve guide with the spring pilot flange face-up as shown in this illustration.

**Valve Crossheads and Guides:** Cummins has released a stemless valve crosshead, Part No. [[3086362]] for the K Series engines. New cylinder head assemblies, Part No. 3811989, Part No. 3811986, Part No. 3811987, Part No. 3811988, Part No. 3811989, and Part No. 3630361 will **not** have the valve crossheads guide Part no. 205169. The valve crosshead guide hole will still be machined in the cylinder head casting. Engines using the stemmed crosshead will require installation of the valve crosshead guide Part No. 205169, but the stemless crosshead can be used with or without the crosshead guide installed and can be mixed with guided crossheads on the same cylinder.

Stemless crossheads can be used on all K19, K38 and K50 Series engines. The first K19 engine built with stemless crossheads was ESN first 37158462 on 14Jul1995. The first K38 and K50 engine built with the stemless crossheads was ESN first 33131466 on 07Aug1995. No adjustment is required on the stemless crosshead and it can be installed on the small pocket on either valve. Adjustment of the rocker lever to crosshead lash remains unchanged.

**Inconel Exhaust Valve:** The Inconel exhaust valve, Part No. [[3088389]] was released to improve durability of high output and high duty cycle engines. Exhaust valve, Part No. [[3088389]] supersedes exhaust valve, Part No. 3034827. The Part No. [[3088389]] exhaust valve has chrome plated stems with the weld moved up into the valve guide. The exhaust valve is very effective at reducing the number of exhaust valve failures in these applications. Uprate engines (more than 101 hp per cylinder) have higher exhaust temperatures and the fatigue strength of the Inconel material increases as the temperature increases. The Inconel exhaust valve, Part No. [[3088389]] is included in the Premium No. 1, Part No. 3811985, Premium No.2, Part No. 3811986 and the Rail Car, Part No. 3630361 cylinder head assemblies.

**Stellite 6 Exhaust Valve:** The standard exhaust valve, Part No. 3088391, is faced with Stellite 6 material. Exhaust valve Part No. 3088391 supersedes exhaust valve Part No. 3035110. The Part No. 3088391 exhaust valve has chrome plated stems with the weld moved up into the valve guide. The Stellite 6 material is more heat resistant than the previous Super-Eatonite material. The standard exhaust valve materials can be identified by the cast-in letter code on the valve head. Reference Table VIII, Valve Identification, in this bulletin.

**Cylinder Head Castings:** The cylinder head castings, Part No. 3009088, 3176619, 3025042, and [[3081064]], are acceptable for rebuild and reuse. For production only purposes:

- Cylinder head casting, Part No. 3176619 supersedes casting, Part No. 3009088.
- Cylinder head casting, Part No. [[3081064]] supersedes casting, Part No. 3176619.

When replacing cylinder head castings, Part No. 3009088 or 3176619 with casting, Part No. [[3081064]], on engines built to CPL-0801, add Field Fix No. 183 to the dataplate.

Cylinder Head castings, Part Nos. 205010, 205015, and 205016, **must** have the injector bore machined to use Premium K injectors.

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Do NOT machine the injector bores with the cylinder heads installed on the engine. Machining chips will fall into the power cylinder and cause damage to the piston rings and cylinder liner. Any of these castings remanufactured by ReCon® after August 1993 will have the injector bores remachined.

These injector bores **must** be machined to 31.75 to 31.877 mm \[1.250 to 1.255 in.\] diameter to a depth of 67.564 to 67.818 mm \[2.660 to 2.670 in.\] from the top deck of the cylinder head.

Cylinder head casting, Part No. 3025042, contains an extra drilling into a coolant passage in the standard cylinder head, Part No. 3176619. This machined casting allows proper venting and coolant flow in horizontally mounted K19 rail car engines. If replacing cylinder head castings, Part No. 3025042, **only** replace with cylinder head casting, Part No. [[3081064]], on horizontally mounted K19 rail car engines.

Specific generator drive applications require cylinder head casting, Part No. 3050491, Part No. 3068171, or Part No. [[3081064]] (Reference Table III). Use cylinder head castings, Part No. 3050491, and Part No. 3068171, **only** on those CPL No.s listed in Table III. These castings are **not** to be rebuilt or reused unless on the same engine in the same application from which it was removed. These cylinder head castings can **not** be purchased through ReCon®. If any of the above mentioned CPL No.s require a rebuilt cylinder head, one of the following assembly numbers, which include cylinder head casting, Part No. [[3081064]] **must** be purchased new.

- Cylinder head assembly, Part No. 3811985 for prime power and base load generator drives.
- Cylinder head assembly, Part No. 3811988 for standby power generator drives only.

**Anti-Swirl Plates:** Anti-swirl plates, also called aid deflectors or swirl plates, are used in some K Series cylinder head assemblies. The plates are installed under the intake valve inserts.

Anti-swirl plates are used to increase performance and reduce emissions by mixing air and fuel. There are two anti-swirl plate types and one spacer plate available for service. In order to meet EPA certifications, certain castings **must** use anti-swirl plates.

![[02600002.png]]

To meet the 1975 EPA emissions, use anti-swirl plates, Part No. 206262 with cylinder head casting, Part No. 205010, 205015 and 205016.

![[02600003.png]]

Use anti-swirl plate, Part No. 3009109 with cylinder head, casting Part No. 3007207, 3009088, and 3176619.

![[02600004.png]]

Use spacer, Part No. 206813 with cylinder head assembly, Part Nos. AR11368, AT12261 or CPL-127.

The spacer, Part No. 206813 can be used in place of the plate, Part No. 3009109 to meet the 1975 EPA emissions on **only** CPL-159 and CPL-171 when castings, Part No. 3007207, 3009088, or 3176619, are used.

> [!note] Note · Примечание

**Valve Springs:** The reuse specifications for the K Series valve spring have been changed with the release of a new valve spring, Part No. 3630628. This new spring can be identified by a pink stripe painted on it. The previous valve spring, 3629007, which can be identified by a green stripe, white stripe or green spray painted section, **must not** be reused during rebuild. use only Part No. 3630628 valve springs on all K engines during repair or rebuild.

Other valve spring reuse guidelines are as follows:

- A bent or broken spring **must not** be reused.
- A spring with a notch worn in the first coil **must not** be reused. Often, a spring that has these notches will make a CLICK noise. to check for a CLICK, the spring is to be hand compressed until the end of the first coil is completely collapsed. A large notch will cause a CLICK noise to be heard. Check both ends of the spring for this CLICK noise.
- Cummins recommends using the new shrome silicone spring at rebuild, Part No. 3630628.

**Valve Leakage Testing:** SPT 91T22-10 was released for a new vacuum leakage testing cup, Part No. 3823852 and seal, Part No. 3823853 for K Series cylinder heads. The new cup and seal was released because the previous design collapsed around the outside diameter of the valve creating an improper No Leakage measurement on the gauge. This new cup and seal will **not** determine if there is a leak between the valve insert and the cylinder head.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Handle the new cup and seal carefully to avoid damage to the new parts.

Before using the tester, test the leakage tester shutoff valve. If the valve is dirty or worn out, it will produce a false leakage measurement on the gauge. Use the following procedure for checking the valve:

- Open the shutoff valve.
- Turn the vacuum pump on.
- Place the cup against a smooth surface.
- Close the shutoff valve.
- Turn the vacuum pump off.
- Wait approximately 10 seconds. The gauge **must not** drop more than 7kPa \[2 in Hg\].

A cylinder head assembly containing the new cylinder head casting, Part No. [[3081064]] can be mixed on the same engine with superseded cylinder head assemblies containing an older part number casting. Before replacing a cylinder head assembly that has a new casting with an assembly that has the old casting, consult the CPL manual. If the new casting part number is the **only** part number given in the CYLINDER HEAD field of the CPL information, use **only** a cylinder head assembly with a new casting as replacement. On some later CPLs, if the new casting is replaced with an old casting, the engine can suffer some performance (fuel consumption) degradation and exhibit more black exhaust smoke.
