## New Valve Guides

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This document announces the release of new valve guides for the cylinder heads installed on the QSK19 CM500 HPI, QSK45/60 CM500 HPI, QSK60 CM850 MCRS, and QSK60 CM2150 MCRS engines.

QSK19 CM850 MCRS, QSK19 CM2150 MCRS, QSK19 Gas, and QSK60 Gas engines are **not** affected by this change to the valve guide.

The new valve guide has a revised internal diameter tolerance to make sure correct valve stem to valve guide clearance is maintained. The new valve guide is also available with two stages of oversize outer diameter for installation in heads where the valve guide bore in the cylinder head has been machined.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Included in the table are changes to the intake valves specified for cylinder heads utilized in Marine applications, and to those used on QSK60 Tier 2 MCRS (CM850 and CM2150) engines. The intake valves specified have changed from a ‘K' marked valve to a ‘C' marked valve, in order to bring these cylinder heads in-line with other industrial cylinder heads.

Due to the revision to the valve guide tolerance, it is necessary to employ a new installation procedure when installing valve guides into cylinder heads.

A drop gauge test has always been required following valve guide installation into the cylinder head. A new drop gauge is required to make sure that the improved valve guide internal diameter tolerance is maintained following installation of the guide into the cylinder head. The new drop gauge has an increased diameter.

A new reamer has also been released, to avoid the requirement to remove and replace valve guides due to the drop gauge test **not** passing. The reamer is **only** to be employed following an unsuccessful drop gauge test.

When a valve guide is pressed into the cylinder head, some slight distortion to the internal diameter of the valve guide can occur. The purpose of the reamer is to remove a very small amount of material from any areas of distortion in the internal diameter of the installed valve guide. This makes sure the correct internal diameter is maintained and allows the drop gauge test to be successful.
