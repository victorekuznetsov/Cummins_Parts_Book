## Electric Fuel Lift Pump Improvement

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Technical Service Bulletin informs the field of a part number change due to improvements made to the electric fuel lift pump used on QST30 Power Generation and some industrial products. The old fuel lift pump, Part Number 4067830, has been obsoleted. The new fuel lift pump, Part Number 4295249, is now available and has been installed on engines starting with Engine Serial Number First 37215814, built on January 31, 2005.

To attain the maximum reliability improvement, do **not** mix old and new pumps.

In accordance with Application Engineering Bulletin 70.30, 140 micron pre-filtration is required for all QST30 engines with electric lift pumps. Engines built since May of 2004 include a 140 micron prefilter. Option FS5033 is an available 140 micron prefilter option.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> This is a product improvement and is **not** subject to campaign.
