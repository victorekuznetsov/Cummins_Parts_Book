## Fuel Line Bracket

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Technical Service Bulletin announces an additional bracket to be placed on the right bank cylinder number 3 fuel line. Due to a large unbracketed span of the fuel line, the fuel line was able to vibrate and some have cracked due to high cycle fatigue.

The bracket components used have the same part numbers used on other fuel lines. No new components were needed or released. The parts will be added in production and are available to order for upfit.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> This is a product improvement and is **not** subject to campaign.
