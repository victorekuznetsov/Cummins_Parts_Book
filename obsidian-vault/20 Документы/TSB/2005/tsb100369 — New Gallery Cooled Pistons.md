## New Gallery Cooled Pistons

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Technical Service Bulletin is to introduce a new galley cooled piston for certain K38-M and K50-M applications. The improved piston is designed to address the failures seen in the bowl rim area of the piston which have been attributed to excessive cylinder temperatures. Contributing factors are exhaust leaks, plugged aftercoolers, and plugged air cleaners.

The new piston is, Part Number 4097889. The old piston, Part Number 3628731, will still be used on industrial and power generation applications and therefore will **not** be made obsolete. Both piston Part Number 4097889 and piston Part Number 3628731, are interchangeable, so that single cylinder repairs are possible with the new piston on engines currently in the field. There is no calibration change associated with the new piston.

The new gallery piston kit is, Part Number 4090013.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Piston, Part Number 4097889, is **not** interchangeable with spray cooled pistons.

The new gallery cooled piston will be available for service orders in March 2005.

The engine serial number (ESN) first for the new piston is 33160766, built July 04, 2005.

It is recommended that the new gallery cooled pistons are installed during rebuilds.
