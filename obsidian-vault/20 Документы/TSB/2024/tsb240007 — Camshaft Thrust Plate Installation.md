**Issue**

Washer listed in Table 1 and Item B within Figure 1 **must** be used with the existing capscrews, and in the specified orientation, when installing new camshaft thrust plates.

![[01r00463.png]]

Figure 1, Factory Step 3 Exploded Diagram.

**Reason for Change**

Factory step 3 action has been implemented to address under head embedment of the bolts in the camshaft thrust plate material. Installation of the washers should be targeted in a fix as fail situation or at a scheduled rebuild interval.

**Service Instructions**

- The washer orientation is critical.
- There are two different faces to the washer; see Figure 2.
- The burr face **must** be facing the camshaft thrust bearing plate during installation. Testing showed that incorrect orientation of the washer will result in a poor joint and embedment of a different form in the camshaft thrust plate material.
