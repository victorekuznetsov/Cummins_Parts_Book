## New Piston Pin

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

**Product Affected**

- ISX (All Versions)
- ISX15 (All Versions)
- X15 (All Versions)
- QSX15 (All Versions)

**Description of Change**

A new piston pin has been released with increased strength and pin joint durability.

**Reason for Change**

This piston pin was developed for use in engines with higher cylinder pressures.

**Service Instructions**

- The new piston pin is backwards compatible and intermixable with existing piston pin.
- X15 CM2450 X142B **must** use the new piston pin, Part Number 6382850.
