## Fuel Shutoff Valve: External Fuel Leak

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

**Product Affected**

- QST30 CM552

**Issue Summary**

Symptom:

- External fuel leak from fuel shutoff valve shield

Root Cause:

- Fuel shutoff valve shield malfunction

**Resolution**

The fuel shutoff valve is a serviceable component. This malfunction can be repaired by replacing the fuel shutoff valve shield. See QST30 Service Manual. Refer to Procedure 005-043 in Section 5 for replacement procedure.

**Customer Communication**

For customers experiencing frequent external fuel leaks from the fuel shutoff shield, customers should work with their local distributor or sales and services team to determine an appropriate interval to routinely replace both shutoff valve shields during planned downtime events.

**Service Instructions**

See QST30 Service Manual. Refer to Procedure 005-043 in Section 5 for instructions on fuel shutoff valve disassembly and assembly. Figure 1 below identifies the fuel shutoff valve shield in the overall assembly.
