## New Engine Heat Shields

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

**Product Affected**

- QSK60 CM2150 K132M
- QSK60 CM2150 MCRS
- QSK60 CM2150 MCRS M
- QSK60 M2150 MCRS G

**Description of Change**

A new heat shield design has been released for the single stage variant, which introduces an improved clearance for other components such as injector fuel supply line, low temperature aftercooling pipe, and turbocharger oil drain.

**Reason for Change**

- This change has come out of product improvement where some clearance issues were seen to cause malfunctions of different components (injector fuel supply line, low temperature aftercooling pipe, and turbocharger oil drain). The design was improved to reduce future malfunctions.
