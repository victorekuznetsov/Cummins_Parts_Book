**Description of Change**

This document announces a new prelube solenoid, which requires related new OEM mounting bracketry and associated parts. The new bracketry ensures the correct orientation of the solenoid and offers the mounting arrangement required by the new prelube solenoid because of a change in the mounting dimensions.

**Reason for Change**

A malfunction rate of the prelube solenoid has existed on multiple machines, stemming from vibration resulting in internal mechanical malfunction. To improve reliability, a new prelube solenoid and associated bracketry have been released to mitigate vibration and the resulting malfunction mechanism.

**Service Parts**

- New OEM mounting bracketry and associated parts are the responsibility of the OEM. See equipment manufacturer service information.
- The new mounting bracketry and associated parts **must** be used when fitting the new prelube solenoid part number. See Table 1 for part number information.

Service parts are offered. See Table 1 for part numbers.

**Part Inventory Action**

- QSK78 Products Affected: Use new prelube solenoid part number at point of TSB release.
- QSK60 Products Affected: The new prelube solenoid part number is **not** used in production units. Use the new prelube solenoid part number as an approved countermeasure if the existing prelube solenoid part number has a high malfunction rate.
- All Other Products: Continue to use existing prelube solenoid part number.
