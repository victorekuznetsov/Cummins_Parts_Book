## Air Leak Past Compressor Inlet Hose Coupling At Cylinder Head

### Core Issue

This Early Field Notification informs the field that some ISX cylinder heads have a spot face at the compressor inlet hose coupling that is smaller than the fitting hex diameter and could possibly result in an air leak at the joint.

### Confirmation

Non EGR ISX family engines starting March 19, 2009 with engine serial number (ESN) First 79366820 and built with hose coupling, Part Number 3679303.

Symptoms include excessive compressor run time, compressed air system slow to build pressure, squealing, and other noise associated with air leaks at the compressor intake.

Confirm there is air leakage at the compressor inlet hose coupling due to interference between the coupling and the cast surface of the cylinder head.

The machined spot face is smaller than fitting diameter on some cylinder heads.

None.

### Resolution

Modify the compressor inlet hose coupling to eliminate interference. Use a lathe to remove up to 3 mm \[0.118 in\] of material from the underside of the wrench flats to avoid interference and allow the coupling to seat in the machined spot face of the cylinder head.
