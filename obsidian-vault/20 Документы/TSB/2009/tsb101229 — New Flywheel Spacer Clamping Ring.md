## New Flywheel Spacer/Clamping Ring

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This service/parts topic is to announce a new flywheel spacer/clamping ring for NT855R and N14ER horizontal railcar engines. It also announces a change to the flywheel/crankshaft bolted joint arrangement along with revised removal, inspection and replacement procedures.

It has been seen that some engines used in high mileage rail applications, which can be subjected to high vibratory torque due to the condition of drivetrain componentry, may exhibit signs of fretting, pitting, or corrosion of the flywheel mounting face of the crankshaft at rebuild.

This revised procedure is to extend the usable life of the crankshaft and ensure the integrity of the flywheel/crankshaft bolted joint.

The addition of a hardened steel spacer/clamping ring, longer mounting capscrews, together with a new torque plus angle tightening procedure has been developed to maintain optimum joint integrity.

The clamping ring kit (Part Number 3288123) and hexagon head capscrews (Part Number 3287753) have been released for service use.

The hardened clamping ring (Part Number 3287628) is **not** available separately.

All used capscrews and washers **must** be discarded and **must not** be reused.

In addition, the flywheel mounting capscrews, Part Number 3287753, must **not** be reused, and should be replaced every time the flywheel/crankshaft joint is disturbed.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> This Cummins clamping ring kit should only be used with genuine Cummins flywheels and crankshafts. If non-genuine parts are used, then contact the supplier of the components for technical guidance.
