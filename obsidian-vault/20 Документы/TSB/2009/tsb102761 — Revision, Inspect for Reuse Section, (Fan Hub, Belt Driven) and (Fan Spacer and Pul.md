## Revision, Inspect for Reuse Section, (Fan Hub, Belt Driven) and (Fan Spacer and Pulley) Procedures

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Service/Parts topic introduces a change being made to the fan hub that is presently used on a variety of B/C/L applications. This change is to alter the dimension of the fan hub chamfer to increase the durability of the fan pulley. The change is to request that in the case of fan pulley damage, the technician also inspects the fan hub for damage to the chamfer and mating surface, and to replace. The fan hub if damage is found. It was decided to make this change requesting the inspection, because the change to the fan hub chamfer does **not** include a part number change.

The change in Procedure 008-039 (Fan Spacer and Pulley) instructs the technician to inspect the fan hub in Procedure 008-036 (Fan Hub, Belt Driven) of the appropriate manual.
