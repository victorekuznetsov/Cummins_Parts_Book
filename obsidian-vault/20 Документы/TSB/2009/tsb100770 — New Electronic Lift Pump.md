## New Electronic Lift Pump

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Technical Service Bulletin replaces Service/Parts Topic 09T5-4. Replace Service/Parts Topic 09T5-4 with this Service/Parts Topic.

This Parts Announcement introduces a new electronic lift pump, Part Number 4975617, for QST30 engines equipped with the electronic fuel supply system. The new lift pump incorporates a new rotor design that increases lift pump robustness.

The engine serial number (ESN) first is 37240363 for the new lift pump. An interim solution was implemented with, Part Number 4975518, which incorporated an inlet screen to prevent large debris particles from entering the fuel system.

The old lift pump, Part Number 4295249, can be scrapped when upgrading to either the interim lift pump, Part Number 4975518, or the new lift pump, Part Number 4975617. Any remaining interim lift pumps, Part Number 4975518, **must** be used before using the new lift pump, Part Number 4965617.
