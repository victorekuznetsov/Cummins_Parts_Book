## Revision to Cooling Fan Drive Belt

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Service/Parts Topic adds a caution applying to the removal of the cooling fan belt for all C and L series engines equipped with an automatic belt tensioner. The caution warns against the use of a socket extension when removing and installing the cooling fan belt. The improper use of a socket extension can cause the automatic belt tensioner arm to break.
