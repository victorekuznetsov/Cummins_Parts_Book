## QST30 New Oil Suction Tube and Brackets

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This document introduces a new lubricating oil suction tube and brackets for QST30 rear deep sump applications; Option OP5137 **only**. The oil suction tube and brackets have been designed to reduce cracking of the oil suction tube, caused by vibration.

The new tube is made of a thicker material. In addition the pickup was moved forward over the previous design. The bracket design has changed from a single center-mount bracket, to a block-mounted bracket and a vertical bracket.

Engine Serial Number (ESN) First: 37257636

Date placed into production: 28-Mar-2013
