## Delco-Remy™ 35MT Starter to Replace 38MT Non-IMS and 38MT IMS

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

The 35MT obsoletes and supersedes all 38MT starters in all options. The 35MT has an integrated magnetic switch (IMS) and improved starting capability compared to the 38MT and 38MT IMS. The 35MT provides these product improvements in a smaller package than the 38MT.
