## New Fuel Pump Gear Pump with Revised Fuel Line Locations

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

**Product Affected**

- ISX15 CM2250 SN
- ISX15 CM2350 X101
- QSX15 CM2250
- QSX15 CM2250 ECF
- QSX15 CM2250 X115
- QSX15 CM2350 X105
- QSX15 CM2350 X106
- QSX15 CM2350 X118
- X15 CM2350 X114B

**Description of Change**

This document releases new and remanufactured fuel pump gear pumps with revised fuel lines.

Top Port Fuel Pump Gear Pump with Revised Fuel Line Locations:

- Engines were released with the fuel lines located on the bottom of the fuel pump gear pump.
- A new design fuel pump gear pump has been released with the fuel line location changed to the top of the fuel pump gear pump. See Figure 1 and 2 below.
- Upgraded fuel lines were released accordingly.

![[05r00171.png]]

Figure 1, Top Port Pump Gear Pump with Revised Fuel Line Locations.

![[05r00172.png]]

Figure 2, Top Port NX or RX Fuel Pump Gear Pump.

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Do not relocate O-ring threaded plugs to top of fuel pump gear pump. Removal of O-ring threaded plugs form fuel pump gear pump assembly will result in external fuel leaks.

Remanufactured Fuel Pump Gear Pump:

- Fuel pump gear pumps with original bottom fuel line ports have been remanufactured to introduce an upper fitting location; required for top fuel supply tube plumbing. The bottom ports on the fuel pump gear pump have been plugged. See Figure 3 for visual representation.

![[05r00173.png]]

Figure 3, Remanufactured Fuel Pump Gear Pump with Plugged Bottom Ports.

For engines built prior to 02 February 2011 that were built with the three-cylinder, two piston high-pressure fuel pump:

- Fuel pump has been obsoleted in favor of a two-cylinder high-pressure fuel pump.
- Reference TSB110064 and Installation Instructions, Bulletin 4907962, for information on replacing the three-cylinder, two piston fuel pump with a two cylinder fuel pump.

For engines built after 02 February 2011, or engines that have already had TSB110064 performed:

- Fuel pump gear pump and fuel lines will need to be replaced with revised components. Reference Table 1 for parts ordering details.
- Release date for the revised fuel pump gear pump and fuel lines for production is 12 December 2012.
- Release date for the revised fuel pump gear pump and fuel lines for service is 04 February 2013.
- Engine serial number (ESN) first is 79629752.
