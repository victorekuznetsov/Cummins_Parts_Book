## QSX15 CM570 - New Wastegate Actuator Hose Kit for Cummins® Marked Turbochargers

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This document announces the release of a new wastegate actuator hose kit, Part Number 4326241, for QSX15 CM570 engines equipped with a Cummins® marked turbocharger. This kit was previously unavailable and is intended to be utilized as a lower cost repair alternative to replacing the entire wastegate actuator assembly or turbocharger assembly as a whole for a damaged wastegate actuator hose. The kit contains a bulk section of hose, two hose clamps, one hose clip, a self-tapping screw and an instruction sheet. These components are **only** available as a kit. Some applications of these kits may require shortening of the hose for best fit.
