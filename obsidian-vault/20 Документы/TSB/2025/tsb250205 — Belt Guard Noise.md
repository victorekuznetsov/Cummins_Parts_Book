## Belt Guard Noise

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

**Product Affected**

- QST30 CM552

**Issue Summary**

Symptom:

- Loud rattling noise coming from front of engine.

Root Cause:

- Loose/cracked belt guard.

**Verification**

- Verify the belt guard has loose capscrews or cracks in belt guard.

**Resolution**

If a loose belt guard is observed, the belt guard can be removed and reinstalled with alternate isolators. If the belt guard is also cracked, the belt guard should be replaced when the alternate isolators are installed. See QST30 Service Manual, Bulletin [[4021539 — QST30 Service Manual\|4021539]]. Refer to Procedure 008-001 in Section 8 to remove belt guard. Follow modifications described in this bulletin to install the belt guard.

**Service Instruction**

See QST30 Service Manual, Bulletin [[4021539 — QST30 Service Manual\|4021539]]. Refer to Procedure 008-001 in Section 8 to remove belt guard.

Install:

1. Install the capscrew assemblies onto the belt guard. The components **must** be in the order shown.

**Three mounting locations will each use:** 1. Part Number [[3093807]] – capscrew – quantity 1 2. Part Number 3643019 – retaining ring – quantity 1 (**Not** visible in picture, located between bracket and first washer) 3. Part Number [[3035083]] – vibration isolator – quantity 1 4. Part Number [[3035084]] – vibration isolator – quantity 1 5. Part Number [[3033800]] – washer – quantity 2

![[13f00011.png]]

Figure 1, Capscrew Assembly with Three Mounting Locations

**One mounting location (bottom) will use:** 1. Part Number 3641716 – capscrew – quantity 1 2. Part Number 3643019 – retaining ring – quantity 1 (**Not** visible in picture, located between bracket and first washer) 3. Part Number [[3035083]] – vibration isolator – quantity 1 4. Part Number [[3035084]] – vibration isolator– quantity 1 5. Part Number [[3033800]] – washer – quantity 2 6. Part Number 3072999 – spacer – quantity 1

![[13f00012.png]]

Figure 2, Capscrew Assembly with One Mounting Location

2. Apply Loctite to capscrews.

3. Install the belt guard.

4. Tighten the capscrew assemblies until the distance between the washers is 9.5 mm \[ 0.37 in \].
