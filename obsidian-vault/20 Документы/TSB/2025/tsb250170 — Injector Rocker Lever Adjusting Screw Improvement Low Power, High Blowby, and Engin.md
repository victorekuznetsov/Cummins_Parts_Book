## Injector Rocker Lever Adjusting Screw Improvement: Low Power, High Blowby, and Engine Noise

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

**Product Affected**

- QSK23 CM500
- QSK78 CM500

**Issue Summary**

Symptom:

- Engine will exhibit low power, high blowby, and noise coming from inside the rocker lever cover.

Root Cause:

- Injector rocker lever adjusting screw fracturing at the location of the horizontal oil drilling.
- The dimensions of the horizontal oil drilling of the injector rocker lever adjusting screw are out of specification.

**Verification**

Verify malfunctions of the injector rocker lever adjusting screw fracturing at the location of the horizontal oil drilling.

**Resolution**

The dimensions of the horizontal oil drilling of the rocker lever adjusting screw have been revised and are now in specification.

**Part Identification**

All injector rocker lever adjusting screws with the updated dimensional controls will be denoted with a green paint mark on the rocker lever assembly (See Figure 1) and a laser etching with the letter “A” on the top of the injector rocker lever adjusting screw (See Figure 2).
