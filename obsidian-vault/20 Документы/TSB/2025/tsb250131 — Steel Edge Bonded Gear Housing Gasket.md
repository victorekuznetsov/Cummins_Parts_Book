**Description of Change**

- A steel edge bonded rubber gasket version of the existing gear housing fiber gasket released and added as an optional addition.
- The sealing of the two different gaskets differs significantly therefore the change in gasket material has also driven dimensional changes to the gear housing, gear cover and camshaft cover (gear cover) designs. A shim has also been released to ensure the air gap for the camshaft position sensor is maintained. A procedure currently exists in the service manuals to allow for shimming of the camshaft position sensor without the need for the specific OEM shim.

**Reason for Change**

Improved lubrication oil sealing between the gear cover and housing and the cylinder block.

**Customer Communication**

The additional gear housing gaskets are a steel edge bonded rubber gasket and have been released alongside the existing fiber gear housing gasket which will continue to be available as a production and service item for existing engines.

**Service Instructions**

Table 1 shows where the camshaft position sensor needs to be shimmed. Should an OEM shim **not** be available, a procedure does exist to machine a shim locally to meet specific requirements. See corresponding Service Manual. Reference Procedure 019-363 in Section 19.

**Part Compatibility**

- The new steel edge bonded gaskets is **not** to be used with the fiber gasket gear covers and housings. Because of the increased thickness of the steel edge bonded gasket and narrower sealing patterns, contact interactions with the gear train and electronics exist.
- When upgrading engines originally fitted with fiber gear housing gaskets to the new steel edge bonded gear housing gaskets the new gear cover and housing part numbers in Table 1 **must** be used to prevent damage to the gear train and electrical components.

**Part Identification**

- New part is stainless-steel and is easily recognizable compared to the fiber gasket.
- The part number permanently applied on the steel carrier of the new gaskets.
