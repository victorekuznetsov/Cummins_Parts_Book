## Hybrid MCRS Fuel Pump Service Kit Upfit

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

**Product Affected**

- QSK60 CM2150 MCRS

**Description of Change**

- A new fuel pump and associated hardware has been released to improve reliability of the pumping element plunger.

**Reason for Change**

- The new fuel pump design has improved fuel flow routing to reduce fuel temperature inside the fuel pump when frequently operating under low load.
- A new high pressure fuel line has been released to allow the new fuel pump to be fitted on two stage turbocharged engines.
- A service kit has been released, containing additional hardware required to upfit an existing engine with the new fuel pump and high-pressure fuel line.

**Service Instructions**

- Service parts are available. See Table 1 and Table 2. Part numbers vary depending on engine model (single stage or two stage).
