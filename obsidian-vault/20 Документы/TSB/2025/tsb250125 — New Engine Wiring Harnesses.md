## New Engine Wiring Harnesses

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

**Product Affected**

- QST30 CM552
- QST30 CM850

**Description of Change**

- New engine wiring harnesses are released in correspondence with the new 3-pump electric fuel lift pump system.
- There are now three electric fuel lift pump power connections on the engine wiring harnesses. The branch of the engine wiring harness that includes the fuel lift pump relay has also been extended in length to achieve a more accessible location when installed on-engine and increased in wire gauge to the appropriate size for the new system. Depending on the mounting location of the fuel lift pump and filter head assembly, the fuel lift pump harness assembly may be routed in one of two paths, as shown in Figures 1 and 2. The wiring diagrams have been updated for all possible engine wiring harness options.
