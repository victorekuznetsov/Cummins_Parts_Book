## Fault Codes 3545 and 1887 Caused by Water or Soap Contaminants in Exhaust System

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

**Product Affected**

- All 2010 and later products equipped with aftertreatment NOx sensors.

**Issue**

Symptom:

- Fault Code 3545 and 1887

Root Cause

- Water and soap are entering the exhaust system when vehicles are washed, contaminating the aftertreatment NOx sensor element or cracking the hot ceramic element.

**Resolution**

- Extra care should be taken to prevent any water or soap from entering the exhaust system.
- Inspect aftertreatment SCR drain hole for any blockage. See corresponding Service Manual. Reference Procedure 011-036 in Section 11.
