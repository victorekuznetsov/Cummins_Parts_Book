## New Fuel Supply Line

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

**Product Affected**

- ISM (All Versions)
- ISMe (All Versions)
- M11 (All Versions)
- QSM11 (All Versions)

**Description of Change**

- New fuel supply line is available for production and service.
- Existing fuel supply line material is rigid metal. New fuel supply line material is steel braided flexible polytetrafluoroethylene (PTFE).

**Reason for Change**

Existing fuel supply line can crack.

**Service Instructions**

Hand tighten both ends of fuel supply line and support fuel supply line with hand while tightening to avoid kinking of fuel supply line. See corresponding Service Manual. Reference Procedure 006-024 in Section 6.

**Service Parts Availability**

Service parts are available. See Table 1 for part numbers.
