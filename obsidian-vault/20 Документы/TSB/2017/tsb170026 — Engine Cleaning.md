**Description of Change**

Engine Steam Cleaning procedure has been updated to cover a broader range of engine cleaning practices and is now called Engine Cleaning.

**Reason for Change**

Updated procedure has precautionary statements to cover appropriate cleaning practices. Cummins Inc. has received malfunctioned parts that were the result of soap entering the air intake system. Do **not** directly spray or allow soap, solvent, or water to enter any passages, ports, or cowlings that lead to air intake system.
