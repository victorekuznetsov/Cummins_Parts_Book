## Coolant in Lubricating Oil: Water Pump Weep Hole Plug Removal

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

**Product Affected**

Engine:

- QSK23 CM500
- QSK23 CM2250 K109
- QSK23 PG CM850

Engine Serial Number (ESN) Range:

- 85000000 and above

**Issue**

Symptom:

- Coolant in lubricating oil.

Root Cause:

- A rubber plug is installed on water pump weep hole, preventing coolant from exiting water pump weep hole and entering into lubricating oil. Water pump weep hole plug is installed at factory and **must** be removed before operating engine.

**Verification**

- Inspect water pump weep hole for plug. See Figure 1 below.

![[08r00425.png]]

Figure 1, Water Pump Weep Hole Plug.

**Resolution**

- Remove plug from water pump weep hole.
