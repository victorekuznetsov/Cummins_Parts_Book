### Contents

**Products Affected**

- B6.7 CM2350 B121B

- X15 CM2350 X114B
- X15 CM2350 X116B

**Customer Communication**

- This document describes the interchangeability of the aftertreatment exhaust gas temperature sensor, aftertreatment outlet NOx sensor, and aftertreatment particulate sensor on the EPA 2017 aftertreatment system.
- The below tables can be used to determine the interchangeability of the sensors used on the aftertreatment system.
- Part number differences are based off of wire lead lengths **only**. Therefore, a sensor with a longer lead length can be used in place of a sensor with a shorter lead length as long as the extra wiring is properly coiled and secured wires do **not** come into contact with hot components and/or areas of wear that can result in damage. See Figure 1 below.

![[19r99413.png]]

Figure 1, Proper Wire Coiling.

Aftertreatment Exhaust Gas Temperature Sensor:

- The aftertreatment exhaust gas temperature sensor is a one-piece unit made up of four sensor probes.
- The aftertreatment temperature sensor module and aftertreatment temperature sensor probes are **not** serviceable separately and **must** be replaced as an assembly.
- Aftertreatment exhaust gas temperature sensor modules are **not** voltage specific and will work on 12v and 24v applications.
- Aftertreatment exhaust gas temperature sensor module probes have unique thread sizes to confirm installation in correct temperature port.
- Horizontal and vertical aftertreatment exhaust gas temperature sensor modules are **not** able to be interchanged as the probe thread sizes are different.
- See Figure 1 below for aftertreatment exhaust gas temperature sensor probe locations.

![[19l00057.png]]

Figure 2, Aftertreatment Exhaust Gas Temperature Sensor Probe Locations.

1. T1: Intake of aftertreatment diesel oxidation catalyst (DOC)
2. T2: Intake of aftertreatment diesel particulate filter (DPF) (DOC outlet)
3. T3: Outlet of aftertreatment DPF
4. T4: Outlet of aftertreatment selective catalytic reduction (SCR) catalyst
