- ISX15 CM2350 X101

- X15 CM2350 X116B
- X15 CM2350 X123B
- X15 CM2450 X124B
- X15 CM2450 X134B
- X15 CM2450 X142B
- X15 CM2450 X155B

**Issue**

Symptom:

- White residue on exhaust stacks and/or exhaust
- White staining on exhaust stacks and/or exhaust
- White powder and/or residue on equipment frames

![[11r00319.png]]

Figure 1, White Staining on Exhaust Stack.

![[11r00320.png]]

Figure 2, White Powder on Frame.

Root Cause:

- Urea **not** being decomposed/atomized to gaseous state completely for any of the following reasons
- However, there may be nothing wrong with DEF system and residue, staining, or powder might be more noticeable for applications that operate with lower aftertreatment temperature less than 250°C \[ 482°F \].
- Presence of white staining / DEF staining on exhaust stack does **notResolution**

- If any fault codes related to aftertreatment and/or DEF system are active, troubleshoot fault codes using corresponding troubleshooting tree.
- If there is a complaint of excessive DEF consumption, follow the appropriate Excessive Diesel Exhaust Fluid (DEF) Consumption troubleshooting.
