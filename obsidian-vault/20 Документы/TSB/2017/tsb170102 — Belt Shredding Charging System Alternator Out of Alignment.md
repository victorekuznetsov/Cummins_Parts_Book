## Belt Shredding: Charging System Alternator Out of Alignment

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

Information Only - OEM Related Matter Not Covered By Cummins® - Contact Appropriate OEM Dealer or OEM Representative For Additional Information

### Contents

**Product Affected**

- X15 CM2350 X114B
- X15 CM2350 X116B

**Issue**

Symptom:

- Belt shredding beginning on inside rib (closest to cab of truck)

Root Cause:

- Charging system alternator is **not** properly aligned

**Verification**

- Use a laser alignment tool to confirm charging system alternator is **not** properly aligned.

**Resolution**

- If charging system alternator is **not** properly aligned, four charging system alternator mounting capscrews **must** be loosened and torqued starting with bottom right capscrew and continuing **counterclockwise**.
- After following charging system alternator capscrew tightening sequence outline above, use laser alignment tool to confirm charging system alternator is properly aligned.
