**Description of Change**

- A new blowby pressure sensor is available. New blowby pressure sensor has a total of eight capscrews on upper housing of sensor body.
- Existing blowby pressure sensor has six capscrews on upper housing of sensor body.

**Reason for Change**

Moisture or dirt ingress into sensor internals can occur as existing blowby pressure sensor has insufficient clamping of internal environmental and bezel seals. New design prevents loss of compression sealing on blowby pressure sensor upper housing.
