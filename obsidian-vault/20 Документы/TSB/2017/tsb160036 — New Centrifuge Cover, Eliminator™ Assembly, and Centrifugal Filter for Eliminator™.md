**Description of Change**

A new centrifugal filter has been introduced for the Eliminator™ filter system. The casting tolerance of the new centrifuge cover has been adjusted and a new complete Eliminator™ assembly part number has been introduced to reflect these changes.

**Reason for Change**

The new centrifugal filter will prevent lower bushing malfunctions. The centrifuge cover has been changed to allow the new centrifuge filter to be used without contacting the centrifuge cover during engine operation.

**Service Instructions**

- If evidence of contact between the centrifuge cover and centrifuge filter is found, the centrifuge cover, Part Number [[4016423]] (QSK78 centrifuge cover), can be purchased separately and installed within the existing Eliminator™ assembly to prevent centrifuge filter contact with the centrifuge cover. Applicable to QSK45/60. See Figure 1 below.
- For installation and removal, see Service Bulletin, Eliminator™ Filter, Bulletin [[3666253 — Eliminator™ Filter|3666253]].

**Part Compatibility**

The new part numbers are backwards compatible and can be mixed with existing part numbers.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> If the one-piece centrifuge is fitted to an engine that has previously had a restrictor orifice fitted to the centrifuge shaft, the restrictor **must** be removed. It can be removed from the shaft with a hex key.

**Recommendation**

Replace the centrifuge every 1000 hours with at least one inspection interval after 500 hours to assess bush wear if present.
