## Single Module Aftertreatment Selective Catalytic Reduction (SCR) Insulation Heat Shield Cracking

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

**Product Affected**

- X15 CM2350 X114B
- X15 CM2350 X116B

**Issue**

Cracks are found on single module aftertreatment selective catalytic reduction (SCR) insulation heat shields.

![[11r00317.png]]

Figure 1, Aftertreatment SCR Insulation Heat Shield Cracking.

![[11r00318.png]]

Figure 1, Aftertreatment SCR Insulation Heat Shield Cracking.

**ResolutionNever** replace an aftertreatment SCR for insulation heat shield cracking. Aftertreatment SCR insulation heat shield cracking does **not** impact emissions or engine operation and is an aesthetic issue **only**.
