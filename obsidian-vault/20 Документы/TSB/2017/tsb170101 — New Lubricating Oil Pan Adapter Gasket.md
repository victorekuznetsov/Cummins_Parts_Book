### Contents

**Product Affected**

- K19 (All Versions)
- KTA19 G (All Versions)
- QSK19 (All Versions)
- QSK19 G (All Versions)

**Description of Change**

- A new style lubricating oil pan adapter gasket with integrated rectangular strip seal is available.
- New lubricating oil pan adapter gasket utilizes:

**Reason for Change**

New edge molded rubber lubricating oil pan gasket resists lubricating oil leaks at lubricating oil pan adapter joint.
