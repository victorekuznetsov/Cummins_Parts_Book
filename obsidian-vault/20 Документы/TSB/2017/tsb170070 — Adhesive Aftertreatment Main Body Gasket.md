- X15 CM2350 X116B

**Description of Change**

This document announces a new adhesive aftertreatment main body gasket.

**Reason for Change**

A new adhesive gasket has been developed to aid in aftertreatment system assembly. When assembling a horizontal aftertreatment system the gasket can become misaligned and cause exhaust leaks or v-band clamp damage. The adhesive strips will maintain gasket alignment during aftertreatment system assembly.
