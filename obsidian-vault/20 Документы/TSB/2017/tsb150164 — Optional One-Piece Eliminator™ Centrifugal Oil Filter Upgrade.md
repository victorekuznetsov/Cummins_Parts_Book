**Description of Change**

This document announces the release of a new Cummins Filtration™ one-piece Eliminator™ centrifugal oil filter with SpiraTec™ technology.

**Reason for Change**

The new Cummins Filtration™ one-piece Eliminator™ centrifugal oil filter with SpiraTec™ technology is a fully disposable and sealed unit that contains the filtering element and bushings. This requires no cleaning as the centrifugal oil filter is just replaced. To prevent premature bushing wear on the Alfa Laval™ disc stack, Cummins Inc. recommends the use of the new Cummins Filtration™ one-piece Eliminator™ centrifugal oil filter with SpiraTec™ technology in all Eliminator™ systems.

**Part Compatibility**

The new upgraded one-piece centrifugal oil filter is backwards compatible.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> If the one-piece centrifuge is fitted to an engine that has previously had a restrictor orifice fitted to the centrifuge shaft, the restrictor **must** be removed. It can be removed from the shaft with a hex key.

**Part Identification**

The new upgraded one-piece centrifugal oil filter can be distinguished using the part number listed on the filter can.

**Part Structure**

The upgraded one-piece centrifugal oil filter part number does **not** obsolete or supersede the existing filtration part numbers. The existing filtration part numbers will remain available.
