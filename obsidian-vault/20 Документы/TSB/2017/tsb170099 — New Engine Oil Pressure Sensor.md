## New Engine Oil Pressure Sensor

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

- X15 CM2350 X114B
- X15 CM2350 X116B

- L9 CM2350 L119B

**Description of Change**

A more robust oil pressure sensor has been released.

**Reason for Change**

New engine oil pressure sensor is more robust.

**Customer Communication**

If existing engine oil pressure sensor has malfunctioned, Cummins Inc. recommends new engine oil pressure sensor be installed. See Table 1 below for part number information.
