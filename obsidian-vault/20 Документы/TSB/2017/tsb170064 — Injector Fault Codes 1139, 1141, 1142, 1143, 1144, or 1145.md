## Injector Fault Codes 1139, 1141, 1142, 1143, 1144, or 1145

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

**Product Affected**

Engine:

- X15 CM2350 X114B
- X15 CM2350 X116B

Build Date Range:

- 1 May 2016 to 3 February 2017

**Issue**

Symptoms:

- Fault Code(s) 1139, 1141, 1142, 1143, 1144, or 1145 becoming active prior to chassis reaching 8,047 km \[ 5,000 miles \].

Root Cause:

- Fueling adaption error in engine control module (ECM).

**Verification**

Engine **must** have a build date between 1 May 2016 and 3 February 2017.

**Resolution**

- Connect INSITE™ electronic service tool
- Troubleshoot any other fault codes **not** listed above first
- Select Advance ECM Data
- Select Fuel Injector Reset
- Reset all Injectors
- Clear all fault codes
- If Fault Code(s) 1139, 1141, 1142, 1143, 1144, or 1145 becoming active again, perform published troubleshooting
