## New Exhaust Muffler Bracket

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

**Product Affected**

- QSK60 CM2150 MCRS

**Description of Change**

A new exhaust muffler bracket with a cutout for increased clearance for turbocharger oil drain line is now available.

**Reason for Change**

There is interference between the turbocharger support bracket and the turbocharger oil drain line. This contact can lead to fretting damage to the turbocharger oil drain line tubing.

**Service Instructions**

- Order existing or new part number to resolve clearance issues. All existing part number stock has been re-worked to match new part number and can still be used.
- If installing the new exhaust muffler bracket and a thicker spacer has been added to the turbocharger oil drain line at the turbocharger connecting joint, this can be removed and restored to the original factory design using correct cap screws and new gaskets.
