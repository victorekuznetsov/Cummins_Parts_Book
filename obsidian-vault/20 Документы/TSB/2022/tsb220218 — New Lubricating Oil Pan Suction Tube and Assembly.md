## New Lubricating Oil Pan Suction Tube and Assembly

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

**Product Affected**

- QSK60 CM2150 MCRS

**Description of Change**

- The manufacturing of the lubricating oil pan suction tube has been improved to achieve better control of the critical dimensions.
- All capscrews connecting the supporting brackets to the cylinder block **must** now have a washer installed to reduce the stresses in the supporting bracket. If this step is **not** completed, a crack can develop in the joint reducing the pre-load available to support the tube.
- A new material has been introduced on the U Clamps to prevent yielding of the material during installation. The new design creates a greater clamping load on the tube, removing some of the system excitation.

**Reason for Change**

- Mid-life inspections have identified cracked lubricating oil suction tubes and large vertical brackets. A factory led project identified the tube manufacturing required improvements to its geometry as the existing process caused the component to remain in high stress once installed.
- The project also identified the brackets would yield when the cylinder block mating capscrew is torqued reducing the pre-load on the joint. To prevent these occurring, washers have been added to all these connections. Yielding was also found on the small horizontal brackets which increased movement of the suction tube; this has been resolved by changing to a stronger U clamp material.

**Service Instructions**

- At engine rebuild the lubricating oil suction tube is to be inspected for the “A” stamping.
- Cummins Inc. recommends lubricating oil suction tubes that do **not** have the “A” stamping are **not** to be re-used and should be replaced with a new lubricating oil suction tube marked with the “A” stamping.

- The assembly already contains capscrew, Part Number [[3902460]], however the quantity of capscrews required has now increased from eight to twelve bolts.
- Lubricating oil suction tube part number has **not** changed for the lubricating oil suction tube. The improved lubricating oil suction tube can be identified by an “A” stamping at the end of the part number.

**Part Identification**

The improved Lubricating oil pan suction tube can be identified by the “A” stamping on the component. The remaining parts can be identified by their individual part number stamping.
