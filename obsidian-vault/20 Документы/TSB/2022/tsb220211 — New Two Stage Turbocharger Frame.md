## New Two Stage Turbocharger Frame

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

**Product Affected**

- QSK60 CM2150 MCRS
- QSK60 CM850 MCRS

**Description of Change**

A new two stage turbocharger frame has been introduced with improved weld geometry. This change to the welds allows an enhanced distribution of the stresses throughout the component reducing high stress areas and cracks.

**Reason for Change**

Two stage turbocharger frame cracks have been reported. A factory investigation identified the initiation points of these cracks to be either at the weld or a high stress location adjacent to the joint.
