**Description of Change**

This document announces a new eliminator body design. New design has increased flange thickness and subsequently bolt length increases.

**Reason for Change**

An external oil leak has occurred on multiple machines, stemming from cracks around the bolt heads on the eliminator body (this is where the eliminator body bolts to the engine cylinder block). To improve reliability, a new oil eliminator body has been released to reduce the stresses at the crack location.
