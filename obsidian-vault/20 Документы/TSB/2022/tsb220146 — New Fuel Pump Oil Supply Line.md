## New Fuel Pump Oil Supply Line

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

**Product Affected**

- QSK60 CM2150 MCRS
- QSK60 CM850 MCRS

**Description of Change**

A new oil supply line has been developed for the modular common rail system fuel pump. The new oil supply line increases the oil pressure available to the fuel pump and improves the reliability and component life.

**Reason for Change**

There have been reports of fuel pump fault codes relating to low oil and fuel pressure. To improve reliability, a new oil supply line has been released that increases the pressure of oil available to the fuel pump by feeding oil directly from the main oil rifle to the fuel pump accessory drive.

**Service Instructions**

- Installation and removal instructions have been added to the Service Manual. See Publications Affected section below for details.
- These parts are **only** compatible with engines manufactured after 02 October 2020 or are utilizing a new cylinder block. See Part Compatibility section below.

**Part Compatibility**

- The oil supply line kit is available on construction application engines built in Daventry. The oil supply line kit can **only** be installed on engines operating in the field if they are using a new cylinder block or were built after 02 October 2020. Following this date, a new cylinder block was introduced with the correct drilling utilized by the new oil pressure supply line. See Technical Service Bulletin, New Cylinder Block to Reduce MCRS Fuel Pump Malfunction, [[tsb210009 — New Cylinder Block to Reduce MCRS Fuel Pump Malfunction\|TSB210009]].
- If the engine was built before 02 October 2020 and utilizes an existing cylinder block, there is an alternative supply line available that uses oil from the flywheel housing of the engine. See Technical Service Bulletin, New Fuel Pump Oil Supply Line: Flywheel Supplied, TSB220042 for details.
