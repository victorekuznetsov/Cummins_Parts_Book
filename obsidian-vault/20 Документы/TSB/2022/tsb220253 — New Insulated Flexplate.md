## New Insulated Flexplate

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

**Product Affected**

- QSK60 CM2150 MCRS

**Description of Change**

A new flexplate is now available and includes the following:

- Clamping plate rather than individual bushings at the crankshaft adapter to resin interface; this reduces the torsional vibration and bending loads absorbed by this joint increasing reliability.
- Press fit on the internal diameter allowing the alternator weight to be absorbed by this joint, reducing stresses in the clamping plate.
- Capscrew quantity has been increased from 15 to 20; this increases the torsional load capability.
- Increased crankshaft adapter length to provide increased support to the bolted joint area.

**Reason for Change**

Increased vibration and adverse noises during operation have been reported. The insulated flexplate membrane can move independently to the crankshaft adapter. An investigation identified this bolted joint experiences high torsional loading which caused fretting and enlarging of the holes.

**Customer Communication**

This flexplate is **only** required on Hitachi haul truck application.
