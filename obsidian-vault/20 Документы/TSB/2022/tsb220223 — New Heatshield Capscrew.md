## New Heatshield Capscrew

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

**Product Affected**

- QSK60 CM2150 MCRS

**Description of Change**

This document announces a new capscrew for the heatshield. New capscrew is longer to achieve a more robust joint.

**Reason for Change**

Heatshield caprscrews have been found to be missing or loose, to achieve an improved bolted joint, a new capscrew/torque combination will be implemented.

**Service Instructions**

The new capscrew torqued up to 54 N•m \[ 40 ft-lb \].
