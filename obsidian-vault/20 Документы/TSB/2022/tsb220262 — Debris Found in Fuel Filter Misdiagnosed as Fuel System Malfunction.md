**Issue Summary**

Symptoms:

- Metal debris present in the dirty (inlet) side of any fuel filter.

Root Cause:

- This TSB should **only** be referenced when the fuel filter is being removed. Do **not** remove the fuel filter solely to inspect for debris.
- Metal identified in any fuel filter could be attributed to normal fuel system component wear. Debris can be ferrous or non-ferrous and is **not** considered a fuel system malfunction.
- Debris seen on the dirty (inlet) side of the fuel filter can be like what is shown in Figure 1.

**Resolution**

- If engine does **not** have symptoms or fault codes, no further action is required. The presence of debris in the dirty (inlet) side of the Fuel Filter is **not** effective in identifying a fuel pump malfunction.
- If engine has symptoms or fault codes, reference published troubleshooting for the diagnosed malfunction.
