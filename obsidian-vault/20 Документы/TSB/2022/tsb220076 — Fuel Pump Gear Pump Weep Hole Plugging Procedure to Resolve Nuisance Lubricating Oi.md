**Issue Summary**

Symptoms:

- Small seepage (normal use) that accumulates over time
- External lubricating oil leak
- External fuel leak

Root Cause:

- Lubricating oil bypassing the fuel pump gear pump shaft seals

**Verification**

1. Determine if the fuel pump is actively leaking.

- Clean the fuel pump gear pump weep hole with contact cleaner, drying thoroughly.
- Operate engine until operating temperature is reached.
- Inspect the weep hole for leakage.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The weep hole is on the inboard side of the fuel pump gear pump. Use of a mirror or borescope and flashlight may be useful.

![[05r00248.png]]

Figure 1, Weep Hole Shown Through Handheld Mirror.

- If the fuel pump gear pump weep hole:

**Resolution**

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Wear appropriate eye and face protection when using compressed air. Flying debris and dirt can cause personal injury.

Plug the fuel pump gear pump weep hole. See Service Instructions section below.

**Service Instructions**

Optional parts and procedure to plug fuel pump gear pump weep hole:

Parts list:

- ¼-20 NC set screw or 5/16-18 NC set screw (weep hole plug) – length can vary between.5 in to 1.5 in.
- ¼-20 NC Tap or 5/16-18 NC
- Loctite® 565 or 577 thread sealant (to improve sealing capability)

See Table 1 for recommended tap for weep hole size.

| Table 1, Hole Size to Recommended Tap Size. |  |
|---|---|
| Hole Size | Recommended Tap and Plug |
| .197” | 1/4 -20 NC |
| .225” | 1/4 -20 NC |
| .276” | 5/16-18 NC |

To plug the fuel pump gear pump weep hole, follow the below instructions:

1. Remove fuel pump gear pump from vehicle.

2. Place fuel pump gear pump in a vice and clean the weep hole region with degreaser, wipe and dry with shop air.

![[05r00263.png]]

Figure 2, Fuel Pump Gear Pump Weep Hole.

3. Measure the weep hole size with a set of dial calipers or equivalent to determine the recommended tap size according to Table 1.

4. Use the appropriate size tap according to Table 1 to create the threads in the fuel pump gear pump weep hole. No drilling necessary, see Figure 3.

![[05r00264.png]]

Figure 3, Fuel Pump Gear Pump Weep Hole Tapping.

5. Length of the threads needs to match the length (“L”) of the plug as shown on Figure 4.

![[05r00251.png]]

Figure 4, Fuel Pump Gear Pump Weep Hole Threaded Plug Diagram.

6. Use shop air to remove any metal shavings from procedure.

![[05r00380.png]]

Figure 5, Fuel Pump Gear Pump Weep Hole Threaded Plug Final Assembly.

7. Apply Loctite® 565 or 577 thread sealant on set screw.

8. Install set screw into the threaded weep hole, torque to 103 N•m \[ 76 in-lb \]. See final assembly in Figure 5.

9. Install the fuel pump gear pump on vehicle. See corresponding Service Manual. Reference Procedure 005-025 in Section 5.

![[05r00266.png]]

Figure 6, Fuel Pump Gear Pump Filter.

To alleviate concerns on machining debris, the fuel pump gear pump comes equipped with a filter that is located inside the fuel pump gear pump weep hole. See Figure 6. This fuel pump gear pump filter will prevent any debris from reaching fuel pump shaft or seals. Do **not** remove the weep hole filter; leave in place inside the fuel pump gear pump weep hole.
