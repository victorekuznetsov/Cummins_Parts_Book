## Cylinder Head Intake Valve Stem Seal Change

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

**Product Affected**

- QSK60 CM2150 MCRS

**Description of Change**

A new cylinder head part number has been released following a change of intake valve stem seal.

**Reason for Change**

A new valve stem seal has been implemented on intake side to increase lubricating oil flow and reduce valve guide wear over the life of the cylinder head, preventing excessive guide wear requiring early cylinder head changes.

**Service Parts Availability**

Service parts are available. See Table 1 for part numbers.

| Table 1, Service Parts |  |  |  |  |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Part Description | Existing Part Number | Obsolete | Superseded | New Part Number |
| Cylinder Head | 5635345 | Yes | Yes | [[5694550]] |

**Part Inventory Action**

Existing cylinder head part numbers have been built with new valve stem seal under deviation since August 2020 and the change in part number is purely for tracing purposes. Continue to use existing cylinder head part number until no longer available and superseded by new part number.
