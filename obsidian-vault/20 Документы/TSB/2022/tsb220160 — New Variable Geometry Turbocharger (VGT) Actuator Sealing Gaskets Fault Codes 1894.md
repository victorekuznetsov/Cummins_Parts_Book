**Description of Change**

New turbocharger actuator mounting kits containing new sealing gaskets available to service a new turbocharger actuator part number.

**Service Instructions**

- If a new VGT actuator is required, the new VGT actuator will come with sealing gaskets installed.
- If a turbocharger is required, the new turbocharger kit will come with multiple sets of VGT actuator sealing gaskets.
- Choose the sealing gaskets from the mounting kit that match the sealing gaskets removed from the VGT actuator.

**Service Parts**

Service parts are available. See Table 1 for part numbers.

| KIT, MOUNTING | 5550398 | No | No | 6378654 |

**Part Identification**

- Use the descriptions and images below to choose the correct sealing gasket from the turbocharger mounting kit.
- Existing VGT actuators have a flat pinion gear cavity and ribs in pairs on the sealing gasket - one on ID, one on OD.
- The new VGT actuator has a chamfer on the pinion gear and a contoured pinion gear cavity.
- The sealing gaskets for the new VGT actuator have ribs grouped in threes - two on inner diameter, one on the outer diameter.
