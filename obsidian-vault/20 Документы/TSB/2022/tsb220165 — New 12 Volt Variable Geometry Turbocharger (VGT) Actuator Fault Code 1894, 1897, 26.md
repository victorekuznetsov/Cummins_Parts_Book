**Description of Change**

New 12-volt variable geometry turbocharger (VGT) actuator service kits available for certain engines.

**Reason for Change**

New 12-volt VGT actuator service kits available on certain engines to verify availability.

**Service Instructions**

- See Table 1 for applicable service kit, if needed.
- Correct setting of the turbocharger sector gear during installation is critical to proper operation. See corresponding Service Manual. Reference Procedure 010-134 in Section 10.
- Actuator part number in kit can deviate from bill of material but is confirmed to be backwards compatible.
- X12 12-Volt applications **must** order a banjo bolt, Part Number [[3924725]], with service kit, Part Number 6476157. See Technical Service Bulletin, New Variable Geometry Turbocharger (VGT) Actuator and Banjo Bolt, TSB250045.
