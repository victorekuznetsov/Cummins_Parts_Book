## Updated Turbocharger Service Parts for Turbine to Bearing Housing Bolted Joint

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

**Product Affected**

- QSK60 CM500
- QSK60 CM850 MCRS
- QSK60 CM2150 MCRS
- QSK60 CM2350 K116
- QSK60 CM2350 K121
- QSK60 CM2350 K135
- QSK60 CM2350 K136

**Issue Summary**

Symptom:

- Broken, loose, or missing turbine to bearing housing capscrews **only** on HE800FG turbochargers

**Verification**

Verify unit is equipped with HE800FG turbochargers utilizing either 16 mm \[ 0.630 in \] or 25 mm \[ 0.984 in \] length turbine to bearing housing capscrews by measuring the length of the capscrew. If the capscrew length is found to be 16 mm \[ 0.630 in \], verify if the turbocharger is equipped with a CSeal. See Figure 1 for identification of a turbocharger with a CSeal versus a turbocharger that does **not** contain a CSeal.

![[09800007.png]]

Figure 1, CSeal Turbocharger Identification

**Resolution**

Lockplates installed under B16 or Inconel capscrews improve torque retention of HE800FG turbochargers and reduce susceptibility to malfunction.

**Service Instructions**

- In the event of a HE800FG Turbocharger having broken, loose, or missing turbine to bearing housing capscrews, all eight (8) turbine to bearing capscrews **must** be replaced.
- Capscrew replacements will require the installation of lockplates underneath the capscrews. When the lockplate tabs are bent upwards after torquing the bolts, the lockplate tabs **must** seat on the flat sides of the capscrew head and **mustnot** be seated on the corner of the capscrew head. Refer to Figure 2. Additional torquing past the specified value up to a maximum of 60 degrees is allowed to align the next capscrew head flat with the lockplate tabs.
- If the turbocharger was originally fitted with 25 mm \[ 0.984 in \] capscrews, 25 mm \[ 0.984 in \] length capscrews **must** be used for replacement. The 25mm capscrew material will depend on the Service Model Name and will be either B16 or Inconel. Any turbocharger configuration utilizing 25 mm \[0.984 in\] capscrews **must not** be fitted with a shim between the flange of the bearing housing and turbine housing. Reference Table 1 for the appropriate bolted joint type for the engine based on the Service Model Name.
- If the turbocharger was originally fitted with 16 mm \[ 0.630 in \] capscrews, 16 mm \[ 0.630 in \] B16 capscrews with lockplates underneath **must** be used for replacement, available for order as a service kit. On turbochargers that do **not** use the CSeal, a 0.25 mm \[ 0.010 in \] shim **must** be fitted between the flange of the bearing housing and the turbine housing. See Figure 3 (ordered separately). Turbochargers that use the CSeal **mustnot** use a shim between the flange of the bearing housing and turbine housing.
- 25 mm \[ 0.984 in \] capscrews of either B16 or Inconel material and 16 mm \[ 0.630 in \] capscrews installed on CSeal turbochargers requires double torquing of the bolts which is critical to verifying the proper torque retention of the bolted joint. 16 mm \[ 0.630 in \] capscrews installed on turbochargers that do **not** use a CSeal do **not** require double torquing of the bolts. Torque the capscrews in the sequence shown in Figure 4 with the torque values shown in Table 1.
- Turbocharger capscrew replacements can be completed in-chassis if service access is available to complete the torquing sequence appropriately. If access is inhibited, remove turbocharger to make this repair.
- If 25 mm \[ 0.984 in \] Inconel capscrews are unavailable, the recommended countermeasure is to install 25 mm \[ 0.984 in \] B16 capscrews with lockplates. The same length capscrew **must** be used for replacement. See Table 1.
