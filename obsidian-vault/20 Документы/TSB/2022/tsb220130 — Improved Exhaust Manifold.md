## Improved Exhaust Manifold

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

**Product Affected**

- QSK60 CM2150 MCRS

**Description of Change**

The exhaust manifold has received a casting change. The change improves the pouring process reducing the shrinkage porosity and core shift increasing component life. A change has also been introduced to the fillet radius connecting the main “trunk” to the cylinder head connecting branch. The radius of this area has been changed from 7 mm to 13 mm reducing the stress at this location.

**Reason for Change**

Customers have been reporting exhaust gas leaks coming from the exhaust manifolds caused by cracks. A factory led engineering project identified a high stress location in the manifold at the radius connecting the main trunk to the cylinder head branch. This high stress caused a crack to initiate that was then accelerated through the component by porosity and core shift.

**Service Parts Availability**

The exhaust manifold part number has **not** changed. The part numbers affected are exhaust manifold, Part Number [[3642650]] and [[3642653]].
