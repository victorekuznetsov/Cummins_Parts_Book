## New Fuel Pump with Revised Outlet Check Valve

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

**Product Affected**

Engine:

- QSK60 CM2150 MCRS

Application and Rating:

- Construction engines with 2237Kw \[ 3000 hp \]

**Description of Change**

A new outlet check valve design has been integrated into the fuel pump assembly to improve reliability for 3000 hp construction applications.

**Reason for Change**

- To reduce fuel pump cavitation at the fuel pump outlet check valve and improve reliability, a new fuel pump part number has been released.
- The new outlet check valve design has improved elongated slots in the check valve to resolve cavitation issues.
