## Remotely Mounted Fuel Pump Dedicated Lubricating Oil Filter

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

**Product Affected**

- QSK60 CM2150 MCRS
- QSK60 CM850 MCRS

**Description of Change**

This remote mount dedicated lubricating oil filter has been released to allow customers to fit the fuel pump oil filter independent to the engine.

**Reason for Change**

In some applications there is insufficient space to install the on engine dedicated lubricating oil filter. To enable units to benefit from extra filtration to the fuel pump and improved robustness, Cummins Inc. has released the off-engine version.

**Customer Communication**

This hardware can also incorporate the oil pressure improvement lines. See Technical Service Bulletin, New Fuel Pump Oil Supply Line: Flywheel Supplied, TSB220042 for flywheel housing supplied and for cylinder block supplied.

**Service Instructions**

This installation should **only** be used in applications where the on engine dedicated lubrication filter is **not** able be used because of space constraints.
