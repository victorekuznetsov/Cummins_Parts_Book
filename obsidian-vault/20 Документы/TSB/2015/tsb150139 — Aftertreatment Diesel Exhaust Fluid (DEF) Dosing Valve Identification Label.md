**Description of Change**

This document informs the field of identification labeling used on Cummins Ecofit™ aftertreatment diesel exhaust fluid (DEF) dosing valves.

**Reason for Change**

Labeling has been implemented to assist the field with aftertreatment DEF dosing valve differentiation.
