**Description of Change**

Fuel pump assembly part numbers with brass tappet roller pins are being obsoleted and superseded by fuel pump assemblies with diamond-like coated tappet roller pins, see Figure 1 below.

**Reason for Change**

The diamond-like coated tappet roller pins are less susceptible to debris or corrosion damage, which can lead to a fuel pump failure.

**Service Instructions**

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Use care when handling the roller tappet as the diamond-like coated tappet roller pin can fall out, resulting in damage to either the tappet roller or the pin.

Unlike tappet rollers with brass roller pins, the diamond-like coated roller pins are free floating and can be removed as shown in Figure 2 below.

If the tappet roller pin does fall out during inspection, but the tappet roller and roller pin are **not** damaged, the roller pin can be reinserted.

The tappet roller pin and the tappet roller can be installed in any tappet body and from both the left and right side of the tappet body.
