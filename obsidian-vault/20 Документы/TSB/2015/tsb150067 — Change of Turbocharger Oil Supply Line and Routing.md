## Change of Turbocharger Oil Supply Line and Routing

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

**Product Affected**

- QSK60 CM850 MCRS (2 Stage Versions)
- QSK60 CM2150 MCRS (2 Stage Versions)

**Description of Change**

This document announces a change to the turbocharger oil supply line routing and turbocharger coolant hose routing. The revised turbocharger oil supply line and turbocharger coolant hose use additional hardware.

**Reason for Change**

Improved clearance of the turbocharger oil supply line and turbocharger coolant hose from surrounding components.

**Customer Communication**

This product improvement is recommended for both banks of the engine.

**Service Instructions**

See Figure 1 and 2 for left bank installation. See Figure 3 and 4 for right bank installation.

All capscrews **must** be tightened to 45 Nm \[ 33 ft-lb\].
