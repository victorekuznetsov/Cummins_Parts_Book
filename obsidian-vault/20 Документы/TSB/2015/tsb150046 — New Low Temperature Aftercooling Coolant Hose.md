## New Low Temperature Aftercooling Coolant Hose

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This document introduces a new Low Temperature Aftercooling (LTA) coolant hose for two stage QSK60 engines.

**Product Affected**

- QSK60 CM500 (Two Stage Only)
- QSK60 CM850 MCRS (Two Stage Only)
- QSK60 CM2150 MCRS (Two Stage Only)

**Description of Change**

The new LTA coolant hose, Part Number 4381485, has a four ply woven aramid reinforced internal material with a silicon outer wrap.

![[08r00140.png]]

Figure 1, New LTA Coolant Hose with New Hose Clamp

**Service Instructions**

Hose clamp, Part Number 3922846, **must** be used with the new LTA coolant hose, Part Number 4381485.
