## CELECT™ Hard Start

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

The purpose of this topic is to identify some CELECT™ hard starting causes and recommend troubleshooting procedures to identify the problem.

Slow or hard starting (long cranking times) have been investigated on several CELECT™ engines. These engines typically had an 8 to 20 seconds cranking time. All of the hard starting complaints investigated, except for low batteries, have been determined to be caused by no or low cranking fuel pressure, which is created by one of the following conditions:

1. Suction line drain-back.
2. Stuck open pressure regulator

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Slow starts is defined as requiring longer than the normal 2 to 5 seconds cranking time.
