**Issue**

Aftertreatment diesel exhaust fluid (DEF) dosing units have been incorrectly identified as failed after finding what was

thought to be a DEF leak.

This document is to communicate that an oily substance near the seams of the Bosch® aftertreatment DEF dosing

unit is normal and does **not** indicate a malfunction has occurred.

**Verification**

Plasticizer is a chemical used for sealing the aftertreatment DEF dosing unit seams. The plasticizer will appear

as an oily or grease like substance. See Figure 2 below.

A DEF leak can be verified by visible signs of DEF crystallization. This will appear as white deposits

![[11r00212.png]]

Figure 1, Example of Plasticizer at the Seams of the Aftertreatment DEF Dosing Unit

**Resolution**

Plasticizer at the seams of the aftertreatment DEF dosing unit is normal and does **not** indicate a malfunction.
