## New Fuel Pump Accessory Drive and Electronic Chip Detector

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

**Product Affected**

- QSK60 CM2150
- QSK60 CM2350 K116
- QSK60 CM2350 K117
- QSK60 CM2350 K121
- QSK60 CM2350 K135
- QSK60 CM2350 K136

**Description of Change**

- A new fuel pump accessory drive has been released.
- The new fuel pump accessory drive introduces an electronic chip detector in the fuel pump oil drain path to identify any metallic debris being created by a failing fuel pump.
- In the event metallic debris is detected by the electronic chip detector a signal will be sent to the engine control module (ECM) to take appropriate action to protect the engine and fuel pump.

**Reason for Change**

- Malfunctions of modular common rail system fuel pumps have been reported. The root cause of malfunction has been identified as debris in the fuel pump.
- The electronic chip detector system will detect metallic debris and flakes being generated by the fuel pump. The electronic chip detector system was designed as an early warning of fuel pump failure, minimizing the chance of other engine components becoming affected by the metallic flakes entering the engine lubricating oil system.

**Service Instructions**

To effectively utilize this new fuel pump accessory drive, the electronic chip detector **must** be connected to the engine wiring harness. Follow the installation instructions below.

**Part Compatibility**

- If the engine is equipped with existing thermistor style exhaust gas temperature sensors, the existing thermistor style exhaust gas temperature sensors **must** be replaced with thermocouple style exhaust gas temperature sensors. Reference [[tsb210002 — New Thermocouple Exhaust Gas Temperature Sensor (EGTS) Introduction\|TSB210002]] for details.
- To utilize this electronic chip detector a change of main engine wiring harnesses is required to part numbers mentioned in [[tsb210002 — New Thermocouple Exhaust Gas Temperature Sensor (EGTS) Introduction\|TSB210002]]. This is required as prior part numbers do **not** contain the connection required for the electronic chip detector.

**Part Identification**

Parts can be identified by part number stamped on the fuel pump accessory drive and electronic chip detector. The extension wiring harness contains a tag with the part number.
