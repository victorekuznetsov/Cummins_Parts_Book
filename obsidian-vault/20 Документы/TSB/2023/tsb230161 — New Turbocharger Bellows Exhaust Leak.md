## New Turbocharger Bellows: Exhaust Leak

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

**Product Affected**

- QSK60 CM2150 MCRS

**Description of Change**

There is a new turbocharger bellows part number. The new turbocharger bellows has a smaller internal sleeve allowing improved clearance to the convolutes and exhaust connector. The design of the V-band mating face has also been changed to enable an improved contact with the V-band clamp.

**Reason for Change**

- There have been reports of exhaust gas leakage during engine operation. There is inadequate clearance between the turbocharger bellows inner sleeve and convolutes leading to contact. The resulting fretting takes place can either break parts away from the inner sleeve or cause a rupture of the convolute leading to exhaust gas leakage.
- V-band clamps can also become loose during operation. The assignable cause of turbocharger bellows malfunction was found to be the low contact area between the clamp and turbocharger bellows causing high stresses in the V-band clamp leading to component fracture.

**Customer Communication**

V Band clamp, Part number 201989, **must** be used or the V-band and/or turbocharger bellows can malfunction.
