**Issue Summary**

Injectors are being replaced unnecessarily.

Symptom:

- Intermittent Fault Codes:

Root Cause:

- Valve crosshead contact with internal actuator wiring harness injector wire causing fatigue near crimp connector

**Verification**

Inspect internal actuator wiring harness for fractured or damaged areas and/or contact with the valve crosshead as seen in Figure 1. As a result of contact with the valve crosshead, the internal actuator wiring harness damage is **not** always obvious. Check for signs of damage to the wiring insulation near the crimp connector. See Figure 2.

![[00r01798.png]]

Figure 1, View of Contact between Crosshead and Internal Actuator Wiring Harness.

![[00r01799.png]]

Figure 2, View of Partial Insulation Break Near Crimp.

**Resolution**

If damage is found, replace injector internal actuator wiring harness, which has been improved to eliminate this failure mode. See corresponding Service Manual. Reference Procedure 019-063 in Section 19.
