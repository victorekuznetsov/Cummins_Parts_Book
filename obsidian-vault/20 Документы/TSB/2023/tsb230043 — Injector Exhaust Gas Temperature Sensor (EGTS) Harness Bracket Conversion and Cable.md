## Injector / Exhaust Gas Temperature Sensor (EGTS) Harness Bracket Conversion and Cable Tie Tension Optimisation to Improve Harness Restraint

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

**Product Affected**

- QSK60 MCRS (All Versions)

**Description of Change**

- The injector / EGTS harness bracket has been updated to include quantity 4 of 6.5 mm +/- 0.25 mm drillings to accept quantity 2 cable ties per bracket.
- The tension of cable ties has been increased.

**Reason for Change**

- The addition of cable ties to the bracket more positively locates the injector and EGT wiring at each cylinder location, preventing contact with other components.
- Increased cable tie tension more positively locates the injector and EGT wiring at each cylinder location, preventing contact with other components.

**Resolution**

Existing bracket variants can be converted to the new specification using the following instructions:

1. Remove the injector / exhaust gas temperature sensor (EGTS) harness bracket from the engine.
2. Locate and centre punch the injector / exhaust gas temperature sensor (EGTS) harness bracket where holes are to be drilled, marked by the dashed boxes in the diagram in Figure 1.
3. Secure the work piece is a suitable vice.
4. Through drill each location using a 6.5 mm drill bit and suitable drill.
5. Debur both sides of each drilling, using a suitable deburring tool.
6. Insert cable tie, Part Number [[5286859]], through the drillings in each location on the injector / exhaust gas temperature sensor (EGTS) harness bracket.
7. Install the injector / exhaust gas temperature sensor (EGTS) harness bracket to the engine.
8. Loosely secure the cable ties around the injector / EGTS harness. Position the harness such that clearance is achieved with the aftercooler cover. Tighten the cable tie. Do **not** to over-tighten the cable tie or damage to the harness may result.
9. Cut off any surplus from the free end of the cable tie, using suitable flush cutters.

New injector / exhaust gas temperature sensor (EGTS) harness bracket variants can also have the harness position optimised and the cable tie replaced, and tension increased, as described in steps 8 and 9 above.
