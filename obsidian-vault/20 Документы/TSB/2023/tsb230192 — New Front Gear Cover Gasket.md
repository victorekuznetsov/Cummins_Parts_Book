## New Front Gear Cover Gasket

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

**Product Affected**

- QSK19 (All versions)

**Description of Change**

This document announces the new edge molded front gear cover gasket.

**Reason for Change**

The new front gear cover gasket addresses engine oil leakage malfunctions.

**Service Instructions**

- Front gear cover removal, cleaning, and inspection, see corresponding Service Manual. Reference Procedure 001-031 in Section 1.
- Do **not** use RTV or gasket adhesive during the installation of the front gear cover.
- The revised front gear cover gasket has four capscrew locations with a rubber retention feature to aid with assembly. See Figure 1.
