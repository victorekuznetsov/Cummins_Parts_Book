### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

Information Only - OEM Related Matter Not Covered By Cummins® - Contact Appropriate OEM Dealer or OEM Representative For Additional Information

### Contents

**Product Affected**

Engines:

- B6.7 CM2350 B121B
- B6.7 CM2450 B155B
- L9 CM2350 L116B
- L9 CM2350 L123B
- L9 CM2450 L126B
- X15 CM2350 X114B
- X15 CM2350 X116B
- X15 CM2450 X124B
- X15 CM2450 X134B
- X15CM2450 X142B

**Issue Summary**

Symptom:

- Vibration

Root Cause:

- Vibration is **not** typically caused by the engine

**Resolution**

Re-direct incident to OEM first by opening a case file in their system about this issue.

**Service Instructions**

OEM recommends opening a case file in their system to start troubleshooting any vibration issues first and once data is collected, OEM will escalate to Cummins Inc. if needed.
