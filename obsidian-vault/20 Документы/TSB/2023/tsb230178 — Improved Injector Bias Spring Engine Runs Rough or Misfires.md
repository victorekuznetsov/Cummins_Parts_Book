## Improved Injector Bias Spring: Engine Runs Rough or Misfires

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

**Product Affected**

- QSK23 CM500
- QSK23 CM2150
- QSK23 CM2250 K109
- QSK78 CM500
- QSK78 CM2250 K104

**Issue Summary**

Symptom:

- Engine runs rough or misfires.
- Increased exhaust gas temperatures: if using PrevenTech®, a warning will be issued by the PrevenTech® team to check the affected cylinder.

Root Cause:

- Injector bias springs have been manufactured with an inadequate shot peening process. This resulted in the potential for the injector bias spring to fracture during normal operation of the injector.

**Verification**

- Visually the injector appears undamaged, however internally the bias spring is fractured.
- Do **not** disassemble the injector.

**Resolution**

- Injectors with improved bias springs are now available.

**Service Instructions**

- All parts on hand **must** be examined and serial numbers on injectors checked to verify if they align to the clean date. See Part Identification section below.
- All suspect injectors **must** be quarantined and returned to Cummins Inc. Fuel Systems. See Part Inventory Action section below.
