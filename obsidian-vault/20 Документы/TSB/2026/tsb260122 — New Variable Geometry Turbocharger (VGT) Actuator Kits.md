**Description of Change**

New Variable Geometry Turbocharger (VGT) actuator service kits are available in a multipart supersession.

**Service Instructions**

- See Table 1 for applicable service kit, if needed.
- Parts may be available in RX, PX, H, HX, or NX.
- See Technical Service Bulletin, Incorrect Variable Geometry Turbocharger (VGT) Actuator Kit Part Number: Navistar®, TSB190153 for Navistar® ISB6.7 CM2350 B101 repairs.
- Correct setting of the turbocharger sector gear during installation is critical to proper operation. See corresponding Service Manual. Refer to Procedure 010-134 in Section 10.
- Variable geometry turbocharger actuator part number in kit can deviate from bill of material but is confirmed to be backwards compatible.
- X12 12-Volt applications **must** order a banjo bolt, Part Number [[3924725]], with service kit, Part Number 6476157. See Technical Service Bulletin, New Variable Geometry Turbocharger (VGT) Actuator and Banjo Bolt, TSB250045.
