## Loose Gear Cover Spacer Plate Capscrews

### Core Issue

Oil leaks reported coming from the 19 liter engines front gear cover spacer plate due to capscrews becoming loose from gasket relaxation.

### Confirmation

This applies to the K19, K19G, QSK19, and QSK19G engines.

Capscrews are becoming loose on the gear cover spacer plate.

None.

Gasket relaxation.

None.

### Resolution

When installing gear cover spacer plate capscrews:

- Install a hardened washer, Part Number [[3042329]], as illustrated in Figure 1. Each capscrew will now have two washers (Figure 1).
- Then apply Loctite™ 271 thread-locker (10 mL \[0.338 oz\] bottle, Part Number 3824038, or 50 mL \[1.69 oz\] bottle, Part Number 3375068) to the capscrews, Part Number [[3014766]]) in the method described below.

![[e04034_01.jpg.png]]

Figure 1.

Apply three drops of Loctite™ 271 thread-locker (available as Cummins Inc. service tool part) to each capscrew starting 2 to 3 threads from the tip of the capscrew (12 capscrews on K19, 14 capscrews on QSK19 and QSK19G).

Tighten the capscrews.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 48 n•m [35 ft-lb]
