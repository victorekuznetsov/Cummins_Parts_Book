## New Cylinder Block to Reduce MCRS Fuel Pump Malfunction

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

**Issue**

Malfunctions of the MCRS fuel pump, caused by low oil supply pressure have been identified.

**Resolution**

- To increase the oil supply pressure to the MCRS fuel pump, a new cylinder block has been released with a new drilling on the left bank side of the cylinder block. The drilling provides a location for a dedicated high-pressure oil supply line to be linked to the MCRS fuel pump. This drilling will be utilized by Daventry production line to install this supply line once available.
- All service orders of this new cylinder block **must** block off this hole with the blanking plate provided. See Table 1 below for part numbers.

A field upfit high pressure line is being developed and will be available in 2021.

**Service Instructions**

The Service Manual procedure has been updated with instructions on how to fit the blanking plate. See corresponding Service Manual. Reference Procedure 001-136 in Section 1
