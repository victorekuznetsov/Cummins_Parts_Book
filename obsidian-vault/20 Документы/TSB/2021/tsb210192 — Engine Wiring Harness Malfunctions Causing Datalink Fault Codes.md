## Engine Wiring Harness Malfunctions Causing Datalink Fault Codes

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

**Product Affected**

- ISX12 CM2350 X102
- ISX15 CM2350 X101
- X12 CM2350 X119B
- X12 CM2450 X137B
- X15 CM2350 X114B
- X15 CM2350 X116B
- X15 CM2350 X130C
- X15 CM2350 X140C
- X15 CM2450 X124B
- X15 CM2450 X134B

**Issue Summary**

Symptom:

- Amber Check Engine Light
- A fault code is active or has more than one inactive count within the last twenty-five engine operating hours from the following list: 1894, 2771, 3232, 3868, 3911, 4151, 4152, 4572, 4677, 5177, or 6688.

Root Cause:

- Use of dielectric grease in connector
- Water intrusion into connectors

**Verification**

1. If checking a connector while following the published troubleshooting tree, visually inspect the connector for evidence of dielectric grease usage. See Figure 1 below.

![[19r99724.png]]

Figure 1, Dielectric Grease in Connector.

2. If checking a 14-pin connector while following the published troubleshooting tree, inspect connector for evidence of water intrusion or corrosion. See Figure 2 and Figure 3 below.

![[19r99725.png]]

Figure 2, Evidence of Water Intrusion or Corrosion.

![[19r99763.png]]

Figure 3, Evidence of Water Intrusion or Corrosion.

**Resolution**

- If there is evidence of dielectric grease usage in a connector, remove the dielectric grease and clean the connector.
- If there is evidence of water intrusion through into a connector, follow the corresponding service manual procedure to repair or replace the connector.
