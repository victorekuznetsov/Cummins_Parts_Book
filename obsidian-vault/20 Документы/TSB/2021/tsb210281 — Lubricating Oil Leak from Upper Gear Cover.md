## Lubricating Oil Leak from Upper Gear Cover

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

**Product Affected**

- X15 CM2350 X114B
- X15 CM2350 X116B
- X15 CM2450 X124B
- X15 CM2450 X134B

**Issue Summary**

Symptom:

- Lubricating oil Leaks from the upper gear cover

Root Cause:

- Loss in retention of the upper gear cover capscrew load

**Verification**

Verify lubricating oil is leaking from the interface of the upper gear cover and the gear housing.

**Resolution**

- Do **not** remove the upper gear cover.
- Torque all the upper gear cover capscrews. See corresponding Service Manual. Reference Procedure 001-079 in Section 1.
- Clean lubricating oil residue off upper gear cover and surrounding components.
- If the engine contains idler pulley bracket, Option FA1611 or FA1608:
- Operate the engine to normal operating temperature. Check for leaks and proper operation.
