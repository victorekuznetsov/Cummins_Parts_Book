**Description of Change**

Some ReCon® Eliminator™ filters may be painted. There is no change in form, fit and function for these Eliminator™ filters.

**Customer Communication**

No change to part number(s), this TSB is to inform field that some ReCon® Eliminator™ filters may look visually different due to paint but have the same form, fit, and function. See Figures 1and 2 below in Part Identification section.
