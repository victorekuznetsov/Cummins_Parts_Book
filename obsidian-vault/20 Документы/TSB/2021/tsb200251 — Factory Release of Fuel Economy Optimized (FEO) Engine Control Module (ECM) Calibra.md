## Factory Release of Fuel Economy Optimized (FEO) Engine Control Module (ECM) Calibration Codes

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

**Product Affected**

- QSK38 CM2150
- QSK50 CM2150 K106
- QSK50 CM2150 MCRS

**Description of Change**

Fuel economy optimized (FEO) engine control module (ECM) calibration codes are non-certified ECM calibration codes that can be used on Tier 2 engines in regions of the world that are **not** emissions regulated. In the past, the FEO ECM calibration codes were offered as "Service Only" and engines were recalibrated by a Cummins Inc. technician in the field. FEO ECM calibration codes are now available at engine plants (able to order them first-fit).

**Service Instructions**

- See Table 1 below for a list of available FEO ECM calibrations codes.
- FEO ECM calibration codes are classified as non-certified and non-emissionized (meaning they do **not** meet any certification regulations), and are **never** to be installed on engines that will work in emissions regulated areas like North America (USA and Canada).
- For FEO ECM calibration codes to be downloaded to certified engines, consult the local and national emissions regulations agencies and verify compliance with the emissions regulation laws.
