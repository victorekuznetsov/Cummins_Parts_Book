## New Flywheel Housing and Rear Crankshaft Seal

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

**Product Affected**

- QSK23 CM500
- QSK23 CM2250 K109
- QSK23 CM2350 K127

**Description of Change**

- Flywheel housing has changed suppliers and has been made common with the Tier 4 product design.
- The rear crankshaft seal bore diameter has changed from 230 mm \[ 9 in \] to 200 mm \[ 7.9 in \] and requires a new rear crankshaft seal.
