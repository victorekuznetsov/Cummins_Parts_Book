## Self-Calibrating Turbocharger Actuator

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

**Product Affected**

- X15 CM2350 X114B
- X15 CM2350 X116B
- X15 CM2450 X124B
- X15 CM2450 X134B

**Description of Change**

- This document announces a new turbocharger actuator that has a self-calibrating capability.
- INSITE™ electronic service tool is **not** required to install/calibrate the new turbocharger actuator.

**Reason for Change**

A new self-calibrating turbocharger actuator can be installed in place of a non self-calibrating turbocharger actuator. See Table 1.

To facilitate field installation of the turbocharger actuator without the use of INSITE™ electronic service tool.

**Service Instructions**

Use of the incorrect procedure will result in an electronic service tool status message indicating the procedure was stopped or failed.

Reference the corresponding Service Manual procedure if using a self-calibrating turbocharger actuator part number, identified by the instructions in the turbocharger actuator kit box.
