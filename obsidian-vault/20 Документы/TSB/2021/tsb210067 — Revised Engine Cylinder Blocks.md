**Description of Change**

- This document announces the introduction of revised cylinder blocks.
- The changes that impact service include cylinder head mounting capscrew counterbore depth and associated cylinder head capscrew part supersession.

**Service Instructions**

- New cylinder blocks and their corresponding cylinder head mounting capscrews are **not** interchangeable with existing cylinder blocks and their associated cylinder head mounting capscrews.
- Torque procedure will vary dependent upon the combination of cylinder head capscrews and cylinder head type.

**Part Compatibility**

- Cylinder head mounting capscrew selection will require special attention when replacing parts.
- Option numbers and service kit part numbers will **not** change. **Only** the part structures within will change.
- Cylinder head capscrew replacement will be driven by which cylinder block is being used. Cylinder head part number is independent of cylinder block/cylinder head capscrew compatibility.
- The cylinder head capscrew torque procedure will vary dependent upon which type of cylinder head and cylinder head capscrews are being installed. See Table 2.

**Part Identification**

New cylinder head capscrews can be identified by a white paint marking on the top surface before first use.

**Part Inventory Action**

Existing cylinder blocks should use existing cylinder head capscrew part numbers. New cylinder blocks should use new cylinder head capscrew part numbers. See Table 2 above.
