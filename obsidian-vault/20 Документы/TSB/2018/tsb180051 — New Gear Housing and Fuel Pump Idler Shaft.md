**Description of Change**

This document announces the release of a new gear housing and fuel pump idler shaft.

**Reason for Change**

A new solid lower idler gear and adjustable fuel pump idler were introduced which led to creation of a new gear housing part numbers. To consolidate to one common part numbers of gear housing, a new gear housing, Part Number [[5468170]], was released to replace the existing part numbers. A new fuel pump idler shaft, Part Number 5484100, (with shallow dowel height) was released and **must** be used to replace the non-adjustable fuel pump idler shaft used with scissor gear arrangements when new housing, Part Number [[5468170]], is used.

**Service Instructions**

If replacing gear housing, Part Number 2893208, for use with scissor style lower idler gear and non-adjustable fuel pump idler shaft:

- New gear housing, Part Number [[5468170]], and new fuel pump idler shaft, Part Number 5484100 **must** be used.

If replacing gear housing, Part Number 3689586, for use with solid lower idler gear and adjustable fuel pump idler shaft:

- New gear housing, Part Number [[5468170]], **must** be used (reuse adjustable fuel pump idler shaft).

**Part Compatibility**

- The new gear housing, Part Number [[5468170]], is completely interchangeable and backwards compatible with engines (and associated hardware) built with gear housing, Part Number 3689586.
- If replacing gear housing, Part Number 2893208, new gear housing, Part Number [[5468170]], and new fuel pump idler shaft, Part Number 5484100, are required.

**Part Identification**

New gear housing, Part Number [[5468170]], can be distinguished by the 4mm \[ 5/32 in \] deep blind hole where the fuel pump idler shaft dowel fits. See Figure 1.

New fuel pump idler shaft, Part Number 5484100, can be distinguished by dowel protrusion decreased from 6mm \[ 15/64 in \] to 3mm \[ 1/8 in \]. See Figure 2.
