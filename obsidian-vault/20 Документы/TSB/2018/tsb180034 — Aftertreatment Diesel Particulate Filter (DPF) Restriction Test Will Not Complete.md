## Aftertreatment Diesel Particulate Filter (DPF) Restriction Test Will Not Complete

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

**Product Affected**

Engine:

- X15 CM2350 X114B
- X15 CM2350 X116B

Engine Serial Number (ESN) Range:

- Prior to 80045948

**Issue**

Symptom:

- Unable to complete aftertreatment diesel particulate filter (DPF) restriction test.

Root Cause:

- A malfunction causes aftertreatment DPF restriction test to abort.

**Verification**

Aftertreatment DPF restriction test aborts while in progress and will display INSITE™ electronic service tool message: “The test exceeded the maximum amount of time allowed to perform the test before a PASS or FAIL result could be determined”.

**Resolution**

- Calibrate engine control module (ECM) using latest INCAL™ DVD. Reference ECM Calibration Revision History on QuickServe® Online for more information.
- After ECM calibration is complete, perform aftertreatment DPF restriction test. See corresponding Service Manual. Reference Procedure 011-085 in Section 11.
- Verify aftertreatment DPF restriction test has successfully completed.
