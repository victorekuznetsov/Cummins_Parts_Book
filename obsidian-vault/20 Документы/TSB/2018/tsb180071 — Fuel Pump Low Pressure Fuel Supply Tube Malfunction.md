## Fuel Pump Low Pressure Fuel Supply Tube Malfunction

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

**Product Affected**

- ISX15 CM2350 X101
- QSX15 CM2350 X105
- QSX15 CM2350 X106
- QSX15 CM2350 X118
- X15 CM2350 X114B

**Issue**

Symptom:

- External fuel leak
- Engine stalls or engine shuts down

Root Cause:

- Fuel pump actuator housing air bleed screen contaminated with debris.
- Debris accumulation prevents fuel flow to drain. Low-pressure fuel system temperature increases. See Figure 1 below.

**Resolution**

If damage to low pressure fuel supply tube is found:

- Identify fuel supply tube configuration installed. See Steel Fuel Supply Tube Installation Instructions 5414654.
- Replace malfunctioning low pressure fuel supply tube with newly released steel fuel supply tube. See Steel Fuel Supply Tube Installation Instructions 5414654.
- Identify plugging in fuel pump actuator housing. Perform the High-Pressure Fuel Pump Return Flow Test. See corresponding Service Manual. Reference Procedure 005-236 in Section 5.
- If fuel pump return flow collected is less than 150 ml/cc in 30 seconds, the fuel pump actuator housing and fuel pump actuator has malfunctioned and **must** be replaced.
