### Warranty Statement

### Contents

- ISBE4 CM850
- ISB CM850
- ISB/ISD4.5 CM2150 SN
- ISB4.5 CM2150

**Description of Change**

Minor casting changes made.

**Service Instructions**

- When replacing esixting water inlet connection, Part Number 2830346, 2830974, or 4899530 with new Part Number 3979118 or 3979119, existing seal, Part Number [[3906697]], **must** be replaced with seal, Part Number 4983908.
- When replacing water inlet connection, Part Number 2830346, a threaded plug, Part Number [[3089567]], **must** be installed with a torque spec of 35 Nm \[ 25 ft-lbs \].
