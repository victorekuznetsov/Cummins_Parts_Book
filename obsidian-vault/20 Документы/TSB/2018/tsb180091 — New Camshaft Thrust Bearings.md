## New Camshaft Thrust Bearings

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

**Product Affected**

- QSK45 CM500
- QSK60 CM500
- QSK60 CM850
- QSK60 CM2150

**Issue**

Symptom:

- Fault Code 1376
- Engine runs rough

Root Cause:

- Camshaft thrust bearing may wear excessively, causing camshaft speed sensor to be out of operating range.

**Resolution**

A new camshaft thrust bearing has been released.

**Description of Change**

New camshaft thrust bearing is made from a more robust material. New camshaft thrust bearing also incorporates a ramp and pad design on the thrust surface, creating an oil film between camshaft thrust bearing and camshaft.

**Part Identification**

New part has 12 oil grooves, existing part has 6 oil grooves. See Figure 1.
