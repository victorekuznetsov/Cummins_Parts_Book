## New Aftertreatment Decomposition Tube

### Contents

**Product Affected**

- B6.7 CM2350 B121B
- L9 CM2350 L116B
- X15 CM2350 X114B
- X15 CM2350 X116B

**Description of Change**

This document announces a new aftertreatment decomposition tube with new aftertreatment diesel exhaust fluid dosing valve mount. This new mount is a one-piece cast metal compared to the past stamped welded design.

This document also announces a new thermal isolator to be used during installation of the aftertreatment diesel exhaust fluid dosing valve. This new isolator has a notch to assist with proper installation, the hole diameter has been reduced, and is backwards compatible with the previous decomposition tube dosing valve mount.

- New thermal isolator **must** be used with new aftertreatment decomposition tube.
- New thermal isolator can be used on previous aftertreatment decomposition tube design.
