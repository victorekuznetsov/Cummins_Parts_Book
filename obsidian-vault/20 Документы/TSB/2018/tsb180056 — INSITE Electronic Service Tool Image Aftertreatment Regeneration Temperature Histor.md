## INSITE Electronic Service Tool Image Aftertreatment Regeneration Temperature History Incorrect

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

**Product Affected**

- X15 CM2350 X114
- X15 CM2350 X116
- L9 CM2350 L116
- L9 CM2350 B120C
- B6.7 CM2350 B121
- B6.7 CM2350 B141
- B6.7 CM2350 B136C

**Issue**

INSITE™ electronic service tool Image Regeneration History Log, located in Advanced ECM Data: Aftertreatment History, is incorrectly recording “Maximum Diesel Particulate Filter Intake Temperature”.

The recorded value is lower than actual temperature and is **not** to be used to diagnose effectiveness of the aftertreatment diesel oxidation catalyst (DOC).

**Resolution**

An engine control module (ECM) calibration code is being developed to resolve this issue.
