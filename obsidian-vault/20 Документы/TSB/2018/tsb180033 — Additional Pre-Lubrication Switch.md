## Additional Pre-Lubrication Switch

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

**Product Affected**

- QSK60 CM850 MCRS
- QSK60 CM2150 MCRS

**Description of Change**

Engines have had an additional pre-lubrication pressure switch installed in fuel pump drive. First engine serial number (ESN) to have these parts installed from Daventry plant is ESN 33210828.A service parts kit, Part Number [[5473011]], is available for service up-fit to help ensure proper fuel pump lubrication prior to start.

**Reason for Change**

The second oil pressure pre-lubrication switch confirms that there is a minimum of 4psi at the fuel pump drive prior to the engine cranking.

**Service Instructions**

In order to up-fit additional pre-lube oil pressure switch on engines in the field, an adapter must be installed into the fuel pump drive. See Figures 1 through 7.

1. Connect existing oil pressure switch plug. Use Part Number [[3903990]]. See Figure 2.
2. Route wire harness across rear of block. See Figure 3.
3. Use capscrew Part Number [[3093923]] to secure harness. See Figures 4, 5, and 6.
4. Connect oil pressure switch, Part Number [[4924925]], to wire harness, Part Number [[5417037]]. See Figure 7
5. Install reducing adapter, Part Number [[3976110]], and oil pressure switch, Part Number [[4924925]], into fuel pump drive adapter.
