## Cooling Fan Drive Belt Shredding on Recreational Vehicle Applications

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

**Product Affected**

- ISX15 CM2350 X101 (**Only** Recreational Vehicle Applications with CPL4588 or CPL3606)
- ISX15 CM2250 (**Only** Recreational Vehicle Applications with CPL3606)

This improvement **only** applies to the ISX15 recreational vehicle (RV) ratings:

- ISX15 550RV
- ISX15 600RV

**Issue**

Symptom:

- Cooling fan drive belt shredding

Root Cause:

- Existing cooling fan drive, Option FA1614, and fan belt drive, Option FA1756, exhibit excessive cooling fan drive belt flap during rapid deceleration on a recreational vehicle applications. This cooling fan drive belt flap leads to the cooling fan drive belt jumping out of alignment.

**Verification**

- Verify the engine has experienced cooling fan drive belt shredding.
- Verify the engine is equipped with fan drive option FA1614 and fan belt drive option FA1756.

![[08r00417.png]]

Figure 1, Example of Belt Shredding

**Resolution**

Install the new cooling fan drive belt and automatic belt tensioner listed in the Service Parts Availability section below.

- See the corresponding Service Manual. Reference Procedure 008-002 in Section 8.
- See the corresponding Service Manual. Reference Procedure 008-080 in Section 8.

**Part Compatibility**

New cooling fan drive, Option FA1683, and fan belt drive, Option FA1762, can **only** be used on the RV ratings listed above because of the belt configuration.
