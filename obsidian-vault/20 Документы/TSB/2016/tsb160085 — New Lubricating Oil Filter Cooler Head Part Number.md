## New Lubricating Oil Filter Cooler Head Part Number

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

**Product Affected**

- ISX (All 15 Liter Versions)
- QSX (All 15 Liter Versions)
- X15 (All Versions)

**Description of Change**

The lubricating oil filter cooler head, Part Number 4965896, is being replaced by part number [[4386026]] which has a smaller lubricating oil thermostat.

**Service Instructions**

- When replacing an existing lubricating oil filter cooler head with the newer lubricating oil filter cooler head, Part Number [[4386026]], the lubricating oil thermostat, retaining ring and threaded plug **must** also be replaced. See corresponding Service Manual. Reference Procedure 007-003 in Section 7.
- If a lubrication oil filter cooler head needs to be replaced, and parts from Table 1 are **not** available, order full assembly for repair. See Table 2 below for part numbers.

The new retaining ring, threaded plug, and lubricating oil thermostat are **not** backwards compatible with the existing lubricating oil filter cooler head.
