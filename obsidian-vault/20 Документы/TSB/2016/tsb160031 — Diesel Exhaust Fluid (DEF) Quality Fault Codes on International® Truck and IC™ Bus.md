## Diesel Exhaust Fluid (DEF) Quality Fault Codes on International® Truck and IC™ Bus Chassis

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

Information Only - OEM Related Matter Not Covered By Cummins® - Contact Appropriate OEM Dealer or OEM Representative For Additional Information

### Contents

**Product Affected**

Engines:

- B6.7 CM2350 B121B
- ISB6.7 CM2350 B101
- ISL9 CM2350 L101
- ISL9 CM2350 L123B
- ISX15 CM2350 X101
- L9 CM2350 L116B
- X15 CM2350 X114B
- X15 CM2350 X116B

Original equipment manufacturer (OEM):

- Navistar
- International

**Issue**

Symptom:

- Fault Code(s) 1715 (SPN 3364 FMI 11), 4277 (SPN 3364 FMI 10), 3867 (SPN 3364 FMI 18), 4842 (SPN 3364 FMI 15), 6765 (SPN 3521 FMI 16), 6766 (SPN 3521 FMI 18), 6767 (SPN 3521 FMI 31)

Root Cause:

- When the diesel exhaust fluid (DEF) level in the DEF tank is low, the DEF quality sensor is more susceptible to calculating the DEF concentration incorrectly. The miscalculation is the result of air bubbles created by the low level of DEF moving in the DEF tank.

**Verification**

If the following conditions are met:

- International® ProStar, 9900, Lonestar, Paystar, HX Series, LT Series, Durastar, Workstar, or IC™ Bus
- Fault Code(s) 1715 (SPN 3364 FMI 11), 4277 (SPN 3364 FMI 10), 3867 (SPN 3364 FMI 18), 4842 (SPN 3364 FMI 15), 6765 (SPN 3521 FMI 16), 6766 (SPN 3521 FMI 18), 6767 (SPN 3521 FMI 31)

See Resolution section below for fault code being diagnosed.

**Resolution**

- Reference Navistar Fault Code Action Plan (FCAP) for individual fault codes.
- Fault Code 1715 (SPN 3364 FMI 11):
- Fault Code(s) 4277 (SPN 3364 FMI 10), 3867 (SPN 3364 FMI 18), 4842 (SPN 3364 FMI 15), 6765 (SPN 3521 FMI 16), 6766 (SPN 3521 FMI 18), 6767 (SPN 3521 FMI 31)
