## Excessive Gear Train Noise/Whine

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document authorizes specific changes to the repair practice for failures covered under product warranty coverages.

### Contents

**Product Affected**

- ISX (All Versions)
- ISX15 (All Versions)
- QSX15 (All Versions)

**Issue**

Symptom:

- High pitched whining noise from the engine gear train that changes with engine rpm.

Root Cause:

- Crankshaft gear tooth profiles are out of specification on select crankshaft gears.

**Verification**

- Crankshaft gear teeth that are out of specification can **not** be visually detected.
- Engines with suspect crankshaft gears were built between May 2015 and January 2016.
- Confirm the crankshaft gear is suspect by looking for the supplier code “AARGP” and “INDIA” stamped on the rear face of the crankshaft gear. See Figure 1 below.
- In some cases, a small mirror may be needed to view the rear face of the crankshaft gear.
- The following audio link below provides an example of an engine with a crankshaft gear with a tooth profile that is out of specification. **Note:** [http://tsb.cumminsvirtualcollege.com/audio/tsb160042.mp3](http://tsb.cumminsvirtualcollege.com/audio/tsb160042.mp3)

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The high pitched whining noise is **not** always noticeable at idle, and can be heard more easily between 1100 rpm and 1500 rpm.

![[01r00171.png]]

Figure 1, Identification Marks on the Rear Face of Suspect Crankshaft Gear

**Resolution**

If an engine is found to have excessive gear train noise/whine AND the crankshaft gear matches the criteria for a suspect crankshaft gear:

- Replace the crankshaft gear. See the corresponding Service Manual. Reference Procedure 001-018 in Section 1.
- Confirm that the gear train noise is no longer present prior to performing other published troubleshooting.
