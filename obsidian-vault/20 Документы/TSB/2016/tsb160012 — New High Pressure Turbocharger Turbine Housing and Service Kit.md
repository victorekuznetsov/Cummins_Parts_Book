## New High Pressure Turbocharger Turbine Housing and Service Kit

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

**Product Affected**

- QSK60 CM850 MCRS
- QSK60 CM2150 MCRS

**Issue**

Select engines with a horsepower rating of 2850 hp and Fuel Rating option FR 6773, have experienced high pressure turbocharger mounting flange cracking. The high pressure turbocharger mounting flange crack can create an exhaust gas leak.

**Verification**

A visual inspection of the high pressure turbocharger to exhaust manifold mounting flange can determine if the high pressure turbocharger mounting flange has cracked.

**Resolution**

The high pressure turbocharger turbine housing should be replaced if found to have a crack at the mounting flange. A new high pressure turbocharger turbine housing service kit has been released to enable the replacement of a high pressure turbocharger turbine housing which has suffered a cracked mounting flange. The new high pressure turbocharger turbine housing has a redesigned mounting flange.

**Service Instructions**

For high pressure turbocharger turbine housing replacement information, see QSK45 and QSK60 Service Manual, Bulletin [[4021530 — QSK45 and QSK60 Service Manual\|4021530]]. [[56-010-033-tr|Refer to Procedure 010-033]] in Section 10.
