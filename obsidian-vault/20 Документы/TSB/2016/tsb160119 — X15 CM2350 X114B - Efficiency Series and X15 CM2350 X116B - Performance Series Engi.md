## X15 CM2350 X114B - Efficiency Series and X15 CM2350 X116B - Performance Series Engine Introduction

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This engine introduction announces the X15 CM2350 X114B - Efficiency Series and X15 CM2350 X116B - Performance Series engines. These engines were designed for use in on-highway applications and complies with:

- Environmental Protection Agency (EPA)
- Air Resources Board (ARB)
- Diagnostic Requirements

**Engine Features**

These engines replace the ISX15 CM2350 X101 engine.

See the following for a complete list of engine features in the Owners Manual, Bulletin [[5411183 — X15 CM2350 X114B - Efficiency Series and X15 CM2350 X116B - Performance Series Owners\|5411183]]. Refer to Procedure 100-006 in Section E.

- Engine family names have been updated:
- Compatible with Cummins® Connected Calibrations. Capable of over-the-air engine control module (ECM) calibration download and installation
- Exhaust System:
- Air Intake System:
- Exhaust Gas Recirculation (EGR) System:
- Fuel System:
- Lubricating Oil System:
- Base Engine:
- Cooling System

**Maintenance Schedule**

Use the following procedures for maintenance intervals in the X15 CM2350 X114B and X15 CM2350 X116B Operation and Maintenance Manuals, Bulletins [[5411182 — X15 CM2350 X114B - Efficiency Series Operation and Maintenance Manual\|5411182]] and 5411187.

- X15 CM2350 X114B: [[377-359-001 — Routine Maintenance Schedule|Refer to Procedure 359-001 in Section 3]].
- X15 CM2350 X116B: Refer to Procedure 359-001 in Section 3.

**Service Tools**

New Cummins® service tools released to support the X15 CM2350 X114B and X15 CM2350 X116B engines are found in the Service Manuals, Bulletins [[5411181 — X15 CM2350 X114B - Efficiency Series Service Manual\|5411181]] and 5411186.

For a complete list of Cummins® service tools needed to support this engine, consult a local Cummins® distributor.

**INSITE™ Electronic Service Tool**

INSITE™ electronic service tool version 8.0.2 or later supports full production engines. Version 8.0.2 or later includes all diagnostic tests and features necessary to fully support the X15 CM2350 X114B and X15 CM2350 X116B engines. INSITE™ electronic service tool updates are available through the Cummins® update manager. Reference the following website to access Cummins® update manager.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> [https://www.cummins.com/support/electronic-service-tools-support/insite-support](https://www.cummins.com/support/electronic-service-tools-support/insite-support)

**Expert Diagnostic System (EDS)**

The EDS troubleshooting tool is available to fully support troubleshooting requirements for the X15 CM2350 X114B and X15 CM2350 X116B engines.
