## New Exhaust Gas Temperature Sensor and Injector Quadrant Wiring Harness and Mounting Bracket

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

**Product Affected**

- QSK60 CM850 MCRS
- QSK60 CM2150 MCRS

**Issue**

Symptom:

- Injector and/or exhaust gas temperature sensor Fault Codes: 1535, 1529, 2125, 2131, 623, 1117, 621, 628, 2137, 2134, 1532
- Physical engine wiring harness damage; engine wiring harness damage can be observed at the connections to either the main engine wiring harness or at the injector and exhaust gas temperature sensor wiring harness connections.

Root Cause:

- The engine wiring harness is incorrectly contacting the cylinder block, cylinder head, camshaft cover, or aftercooler housings.

**Verification**

- Inspect exhaust gas temperature sensor and injector wiring harness and connectors for physical damage or a loose connection and use INSITE™ electronic service tool to check for fault codes listed above.

**Resolution**

- A new engine wiring harness and bracket assembly including the new exhaust gas temperature sensor and injector wiring harness, and new cylinder head mounting bracket has been released.
- The new components reduce the likelihood of the wiring harness connectors coming loose during operation and reroute the wiring harness to prevent wiring harness fretting caused by incorrect contact with the cylinder block, cylinder head, camshaft cover, or aftercooler housing.

**Service Instructions**

Installation instructions are available. See Installation Instruction, New Exhaust Gas Temperature Sensor and Injector Quadrant Wiring Harness and Mounting Bracket Installation Instructions, Bulletin 5414606.

The new part numbers can be mixed with existing part numbers, but Cummins Inc. recommends all engine quadrants have the new part numbers installed if a malfunction of one engine quadrant is experienced.
