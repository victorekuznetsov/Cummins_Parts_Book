## New Tachometer/Hourmeter

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Technical Service Bulletin introduces a new tachometer/hourmeter in kitted option EC 8258 for use on the K19 Series engines.

The new tachometer/hourmeter has a more robust design for improved component durability.

The installation procedure remains unchanged.

Both the mounting studs and the harness studs are recommended to be tightened to the standard of 0.68 to 0.90 N•m \[6 to 8 in-lbs\] with installation nuts.

The estimated time for introducing this change is 15-May-2010.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The new tachometer/hourmeter is available for service. The previous version of the tachometer/hourmeter, Part Number 3049555, will still be available for service installation in control panels used in other applications.
