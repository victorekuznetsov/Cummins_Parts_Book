## Oil Pan Mounting Capscrew Torque Change

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Service/Parts Topic updates the oil pan mounting capscrew torque specification in Procedure 007-025 (Lubricating Oil Pan). This change updates the service torque specification to the value currently being used in the factory. The new torque specification is to be used for **all** field repairs.

| New Torque Specification | Old Torque Specification |
|---|---|
| 54 N•m \[40 ft-lb\] | 47 N•m \[35 ft-lb\] |

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> This is a product improvement and **not** subject to campaign.
