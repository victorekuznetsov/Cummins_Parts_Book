### Warranty Statement

### Contents

This Service/Parts topic introduces a revision to Procedure 001-045 (Piston and Cylinder Liner) in Section 1 of the QSK45 and QSK60 Service Manual, Bulletin [[4021530 — QSK45 and QSK60 Service Manual\|4021530]].

Proper connecting rod capscrew and bearing lubrication requirements have been added to the Install section of the procedure.
