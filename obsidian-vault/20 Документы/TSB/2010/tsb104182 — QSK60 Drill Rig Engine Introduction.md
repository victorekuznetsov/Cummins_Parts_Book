## QSK60 Drill Rig Engine Introduction

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

The Service/Parts Topic announces the introduction of the QSK60M CM2150 Modular Common Rail Fuel System (MCRS) engine.

The QSK60M CM2150 MCRS engine is developed for the constant speed offshore drill rig applications **only**. The QSK60M CM2150 MCRS engine design is based on both the QSK60 Tier 2 Industrial base engine and the QSK60 Marine Tier 2 engine.

The engine has been designed to meet legislated Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) Marine Tier 2 emission levels for the U.S. This engine is International Maritime Organization (IMO) Tier I certified. This product is being designed to meet IMO Tier II emission levels, which will go into effect January 1, 2011.

Reference Procedure 209-018 (New Engine Information) in this Service/Parts Topic for fuel ratings and other general information applying to this engine.
