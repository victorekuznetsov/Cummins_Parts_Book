## New Injector Load Ring

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Technical Service Bulletin announces a new load ring for Signature™, ISX and QSX15 injectors. The new load ring is to be used with fuel injectors, Part Numbers 4088665, 4954434, and 4954888.

The engine serial number (ESN) first for the new load ring is 79309358.

The load ring is located on the top of the fuel injector body and includes the spring retainer. This change is being made to conform the injectors to multiple applications and to increase component robustness. The new injector load ring can be used in all applications; therefore, new replacement injectors will come with the new load ring and will be different in appearance. Remanufactured injectors may come with either load ring. Running an engine equipped with a combination of old and new load rings will cause no adverse effects.
