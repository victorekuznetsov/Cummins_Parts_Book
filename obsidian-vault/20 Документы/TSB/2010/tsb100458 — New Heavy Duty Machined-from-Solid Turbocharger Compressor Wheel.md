## New Heavy Duty Machined-from-Solid Turbocharger Compressor Wheel

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Technical Service Bulletin supersedes topic, 05T10-10. Please replace 05T10-10 with this topic.

This Technical Service Bulletin revises data in the CPL to machined-from-solid turbocharger compressor wheel compatibility chart. CPL2610 is removed as a compatible CPL for the ISM 3590045 (cast) and 4038847 (MFS) turbochargers. It is added as a compatible CPL for the ISM, ISMe 3592779 (cast) and 4039198 (MFS) turbochargers.

This Technical Service Bulletin announces a new machined-from-solid turbocharger compressor wheel for certain heavy duty engine and application combinations. The new turbocharger addresses low-cycle fatigue mode issues.

The previous compressor wheel is a cast design with the outer blade contour machined to achieve the desired profile. The sides of the cast wheel blades are in 'as cast' condition.

The new compressor wheel is a machined-from-solid design which is created by machining the wheel from a solid billet of aluminum. All surfaces of the machined-from-solid wheel are machined.

This product improvement is **not** covered by warranty.

**Only** certain, high-duty cycle applications are eligible to receive machined-from-solid turbocharger compressor wheels.

**Only** certain high-duty cycle applications are subject to a temporary repair practice (TRP). In most instances, cast wheels have **not** been obsoleted and superseded.

Consult QuickServe® Online to determine if a given engine and application combination is eligible to receive this product improvement.
