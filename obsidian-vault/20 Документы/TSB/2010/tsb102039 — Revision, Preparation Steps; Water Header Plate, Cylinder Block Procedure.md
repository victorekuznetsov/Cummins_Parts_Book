## Revision, Preparation Steps; Water Header Plate, Cylinder Block Procedure

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Service/Parts Topic introduces a new procedure to remove and install the coolant header cover plate released with the 2007 cylinder block. The 2007 cylinder block features a new coolant return port that was specifically designed to provide a coolant return for the 2007 EGR cooler. The 2007 cylinder block is backwards compatible with older cylinder blocks, by virtue of installing a coolant header cover plate.
