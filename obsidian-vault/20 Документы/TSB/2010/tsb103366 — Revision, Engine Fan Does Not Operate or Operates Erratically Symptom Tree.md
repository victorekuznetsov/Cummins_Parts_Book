## Revision, Engine Fan Does Not Operate or Operates Erratically Symptom Tree

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Service/Parts Topic revises symptom tree T046 (Engine Fan Does Not Operate or Operates Erratically) for the Signature™, ISX, and QSX15 Electronic Control System Troubleshooting and Repair Manual, Bulletin 3666259. The symptom tree title has been modified to include the fan operating continuously (Engine Fan Does Not Operate, Operates Erratically, Or Operates Continuously). Multiple tree nodes have also been added for troubleshooting this condition.
