## (Flywheel Housing, REPTO) Procedure, Install Section Revision

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Service/Parts Topic revises Procedure 016-007 (Flywheel Housing, REPTO) to include a step to check for backlash between the Hydrive housing gear and the idler gear. It has been determined that a small amount of backlash **must** be present for correct operation. A no backlash condition can cause premature component failure.
