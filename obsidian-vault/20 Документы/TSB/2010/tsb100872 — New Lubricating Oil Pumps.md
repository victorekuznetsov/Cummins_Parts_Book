## New Lubricating Oil Pumps

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Technical Service Bulletin announces the release of new lubricating oil pumps for use on QSK45, QSK60 and QSK78 series engines.

The new lubricating oil pumps have had the internal oil passages revised to allow the lubricating oil pump idler gear bushing to be supplied with filtered oil, increasing the durability of the pump. The new lubricating oil pump part numbers are shown in Table 1.

The effective date of implementation into production for the QSK45, QSK60, and QSK78 engines are shown in Table 2.

Tables 3 and 4 list all the common parts available for rebuild and repair of lubricating oil pumps for the QSK45, QSK60, and QSK78 engines.

![[07601276.png]]

Some QSK45 and QSK60 engines were built with lubricating oil pumps similar to the one shown above, but which did **not** contain a high pressure relief valve. The lubricating oil pumps which did **not** contain high pressure relief valves are, Part Numbers 4100710 (QSK45) and 4100711 (QSK60). All parts, except for the high pressure relief valve components, used in these pumps are the same as those shown in Table 3.

If an engine has a pump that does **not** have a high pressure relief valve, it is recommended that the pump be replaced with one that does.

For further information on removal, disassembly, inspect for reuse, assembly and installation of these pumps, go to the procedures listed below in the QSK45 and QSK60 Service Manual, Bulletin [[4021530 — QSK45 and QSK60 Service Manual\|4021530]] and the QSK78 Series Engine Troubleshooting and Repair Manual, Bulletin, 3666727.

- Procedure 007-021 (Lubricating Oil High Pressure Relief Valve)
- Procedure 007-029 (Lubricating Oil Pressure Regulator (Main Rifle))
- Procedure 007-031 (Lubricating Oil Pump).
