## Revision to Crankshaft Gear, Adapter, and Tone Wheel Procedure

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> This Service/Parts Topic supersedes Topic 07T1-1; replace 07T1-1 with this topic.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> This is a product improvement and is **not** subject to campaign.

This Service/Parts Topic provides information for the removal and installation of the Crankshaft Adapter (Procedure 001-017), Crankshaft Gear (Procedure 001-018), and the Crankshaft Tone Wheel (Procedure 001-069). These procedures were developed to avoid the unnecessary replacement of the crankshaft in the field due to a crankshaft adapter, crankshaft gear or tone wheel repair.
