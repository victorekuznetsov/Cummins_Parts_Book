## Cylinder Head Inspection Specifications Update

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Service/Parts Topic adds the following Clean and Inspect for Reuse specifications, for cylinder heads built after August 21, 2006 with the combustion face scallop, to Procedure 002-004:

- Valve recession
- Operational recession
- Valve recession variation between valves on same cylinder
- Valve tip height
- Insert bore depth.
