## Procedure 001-089, Block Stiffener Plate

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This service/parts topic introduces the block stiffener plate for M11, ISM, and QSM11 series engines and assists repair and service personnel in removal and installation procedures.

The 2002 ISM engine with EGR features a block stiffener to add ridigity to the block. Older non-EGR M11/ISM engines can be upfitted with the block stiffener plate, but will require additional parts.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> This is a product improvement and is **not** subject to campaign.

This topic includes the following:

1. ISM with exhaust gas recirculation (EGR), engine serial number (ESN) first and build date
2. Parts List for EGR and Non-EGR Engines
3. ISM/M11 Non-EGR installation procedures
4. Block stiffener plate remove and install procedures.

Loctite 5999 sealant comes in a tube that can be adequate for up to four block stiffener plate installations. Substitute 3 Bond RTV (gray or red) if Loctite 5999 is unavailable.

Non-EGR engines require additional parts for block stiffener plate initial installation. All oil pans require longer capscrews. Middle and rear sump oil pans require redesigned oil transfer tubes.
