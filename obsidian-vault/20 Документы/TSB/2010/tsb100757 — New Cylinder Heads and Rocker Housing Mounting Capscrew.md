## New Cylinder Heads and Rocker Housing Mounting Capscrew

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Technical Service Bulletin introduces new ISM, QSM11, M11, L10, and L10G cylinder heads.

The new cylinder head part numbers obsolete and supersede the old part numbers.

The new part numbers were introduced on April 16, 2008, ESN first 35225571.

The new cylinder heads have the following new features:

1. There has been a 10mm counterbore added to the cylinder head around the rocker housing mounting capscrew holes. This counterbore has been added to increase the thread grip length and increase bolt load retention, in order to prevent oil leaks at this joint.
2. The new cylinder heads use a different rocker housing mounting capscrew than previous heads. The new capscrew Part Number is 2863697, as shown in Table 2.
3. A new rocker housing gasket was also released with the new cylinder heads.
4. Several of the new cylinder heads require the use of a cup plug, Part Number 205401, which is **not** included with the cylinder head. This cup plug is used to seal off the air compressor supply port on the intake side of the cylinder head, when the engine is either not equipped with an air compressor or the air compressor intake air is plumbed elsewhere, as shown below. The cylinder head part numbers which require the use of this cup plug are identified in Table 1.
