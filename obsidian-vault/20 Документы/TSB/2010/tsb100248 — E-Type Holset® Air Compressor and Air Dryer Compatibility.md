## E-Type Holset® Air Compressor and Air Dryer Compatibility

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Technical Service Bulletin communicates to the field the following information:

- Technical description of the E-Type Holset® Air Compressor and air dryer compatibility
- Engine population affected
- Parts and kit listings.

It is recommended to use **only** E-Type compatible air dryers with Holset® QE, SS, and ST E-Type air compressors. Use of these air dryers will help to avoid excessive oil carry-over associated with incompatibility issues. For more information on air compressor and air dryer recommendations on plumbing and installation please contact 1-800-4Holset.

The E-Type compatible air dryers are listed in the table below.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> There is a Bendix Drop-in AD-9 with hard seat dryer available. When servicing the engine, **only** the Bendix Drop-in AD 9 with soft seat is compatible with the E-Type Holset® air compressors.

| E-Type Air Compressor and Compatible Air Dryers |  |
|---|---|
| Air Compressor Model | E-Type Compatible Air Dryers |
|  | Chicago Rawhide Brakemaster: Turbo-AC, Turbo-2000, and Turbo-3000 |
| Holset® QE E-Type | Bendix Drop-in Models - AD-9 with soft seat, ADSP, ADIP |
| Holset® ST E-Type | ANCHORLOK Condenser |
| Holset® SS E-Type | Wabco System Saver 1200E |
|  | Midland Pure Air Plus |

All engines with the Holset® QE, ST, and SS, E-Type Air Compressors are affected by this recommendation.
