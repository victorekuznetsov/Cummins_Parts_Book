## New Crankshaft

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Technical Service Bulletin supersedes Service/Parts Topic 05T1-13. Please replace Service/Parts Topic 05T1-13 with this Service/Parts Topic.

This Technical Service Bulletin introduces the new ISX crankshaft and documents the crankshaft supersessions and compatibility.

| **Crankshaft Part Number** | **Description** | **Compatible Part Numbers** |
|---|---|---|
| 3681073 | Crankshaft used on first 950 ISX engines, different style tone wheel than used on present design. | None. **Must** use 3681073. |
| 3681911 | Production crankshaft from January, 1999 through June, 2004. | 4923646, 4925762 |
| 4923646 | Temporary crankshaft part number, can be found on engines built from June, 2004 through December, 2004. | 3681911, 4925762 |
| 4925762 | New production crankshaft part number, includes the removal of rear lightening hole as well as non-ground main bearing fillets. Functionally the same as 3681911 and 4923646. | 3681911, 4923646 |
