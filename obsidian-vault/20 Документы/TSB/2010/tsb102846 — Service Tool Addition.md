## Service Tool Addition

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Service/Parts Topic revises Procedure 022-001 (Service Tools) for the Air Intake System with the addition of service tool, Part Number 4919166. This new service tool is used to remove and install the turbocharger actuator cross shaft bushing and oil seal on variable geometry turbochargers.
