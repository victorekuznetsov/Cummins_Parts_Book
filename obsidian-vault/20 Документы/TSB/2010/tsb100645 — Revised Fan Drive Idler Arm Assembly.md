## Revised Fan Drive Idler Arm Assembly

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This purpose of This Technical Service Bulletin is to introduce a revised the fan drive idler arm assembly for the QSK60, WA1200 fan option FA6236. The revised design will improve fan hub reliability and durability.

The revised fan drive idler arm went into production in August 2007 at the Daventry Engine Plant. Engine serial number first was 33170187.
