## New Fuel Tube Brace

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Technical Service Bulletin introduces a new brace, Part Number [[3641308]], for the fuel return block. This new brace, with two slotted holes to reduce fuel tube misalignment, supersedes, Part Number 4001156. The new brace was put into production on August 4, 2008 with engine serial number (ESN) first 33174941.

| New Part Number | Part Description | Old Part Number | Old Part Disposition |
|---|---|---|---|
