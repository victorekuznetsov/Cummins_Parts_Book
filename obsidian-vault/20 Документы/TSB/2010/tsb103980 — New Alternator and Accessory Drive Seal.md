## New Alternator and Accessory Drive Seal

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Service/Parts Topic replaces Service/Parts Topic 01T9-4. Replace Service/Parts Topic 01T9-4 with this Topic.

This Service/Parts Topic introduces new accessory and alternator drive seal and pulleys for the QSK45 and QSK60 engines. The new accessory and alternator drive seal, Part Number [[4080404]], replaces the old accessory drive seal, Part Number 3171909. The new accessory drive seal will be included in Service Kit, Part Number 4085931. The service kit contains the items listed in Table 1.

| Table 1, Service Kit, Part Number 4085931, Contents |  |
|---|---|
| [[4080404]] | Seal, Accessory Drive |
| [[4080395]] | Washer |
| [[4085923]] | Spacer, Mounting |

The new accessory and alternator drive seal is **not** a direct replacement for the old accessory and alternator drive seal. As a result, modification of the old pulley or installation of a new pulley is required. Refer to Table 2, Accessory and Alternator Drive Seal Matrix, below.

Accessory and Alternator Drive Seal Service Procedure Matrix

If replacing the accessory and alternator drive seal, Part Number [[4080404]], the seal is a direct replacement. For installation instructions, use the Troubleshooting and Repair Manual, QSK45 and QSK60 Engines, Bulletin 3666261.

Alternator Drive Seal, Right Bank

Remove the alternator drive pulley and perform one of the following:

1. Machine 2.1 mm \[0.08 in\] From the length A of the pulley or
2. Purchase a new pulley. See Table 3 for the correct new pulley part number.

| Table 3, Alternator Drive Pulley Part Numbers, Right Bank |  |
|---|---|
| Old Drive Pulley Part Number | New Drive Pulley Part Number |
| 3171728 | [[4080396]] |
| 3332188 | 4080397 |
| 3332203 | 4080398 |
| 4001382 | 4080399 |

![[09600117.png]]

Accessory Drive Seal, Left Bank

Remove the accessory drive pulley and perform one of the following:

- Reuse the old pulley by installing the spacer, to increase the distance (B), provided in the seal service kit or
- Purchase a new pulley. See Table 4 for the correct new pulley part number.
