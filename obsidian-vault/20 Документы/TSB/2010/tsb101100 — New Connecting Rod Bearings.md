## New Connecting Rod Bearings

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This document announces the release of a new connecting rod bearing for use on QSK45 and QSK60 series diesel engines.

#### Straight Split Connecting Rods

- The new bearing has reverted back to the original VP2q bearing material to improve durability and has revised geometry to improve oil film characteristics.
- The new bearing will be installed into industrial engines **only**, as of 22-Nov-2010, starting with engine serial number (ESN) first 33184033. The new bearing will be installed in all QSK45 and QSK60 diesel engines starting January 2011.
- Prior to the ESN first above, the following ESN's had the new connecting rod bearings installed: 33183984, 33183989, and 33184007.
- **Only** the new bearing will be available for service as of December 2010.

#### Angle Split Connecting Rods

- The previous VP2q bearing for the angle split connecting rods has been reinstated to improve durability.
- The reinstated bearings will be available as of December 2010.
- Since **only** the straight split connecting rod is used in production, the angle split bearing will be used for service **only**, therefore no engine serial number first is being provided.
