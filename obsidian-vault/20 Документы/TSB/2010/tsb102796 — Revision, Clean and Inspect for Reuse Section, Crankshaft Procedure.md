## Revision, Clean and Inspect for Reuse Section, Crankshaft Procedure

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Service/Parts Topic updates the Clean and Inspect for Reuse section of Procedure 001-016 (Crankshaft) to state that if damage is found to the tone wheel, the tone wheel, **not** the crankshaft, **must** be replaced.
