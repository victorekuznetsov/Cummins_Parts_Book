## Cylinder Head Procedure, Clean and Inspect for Reuse Section Revision

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

The purpose of the Service/Parts Topic is to introducethe changes to the Clean and Inspect for Reuse Section of Procedure 002-004 (Cylinder Head) in the Signature™, ISX, and QSX15 Service Manual, Bulletin [[3666239 — Signature™, ISX, and QSX15 Service Manual\|3666239]]. These changes include adding an inspection of the injector bores. Previously, **only** the combustion surface and valves were inspected.
