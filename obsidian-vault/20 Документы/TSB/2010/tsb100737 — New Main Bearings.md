### Contents

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The information in this document has been superseded. For the latest main bearing information, refer to [[tsb100907 — Main Bearing Part Number Reinstatements\|TSB100907]]. This document with be kept active for information purposes **only**.

This document introduces new main bearings for use on QSK45, QSK60, and QSK78 diesel engines.

The new main bearings are made from an improved material. The new material has a higher resistance to wear and improves durability.

The new main bearings went into production at the Daventry Engine Plant (DEP) on 02-Jun-2008.

The engine serial number (ESN) first for QSK45 and QSK60 engines is 33173996.

The engine serial number (ESN) first for the QSK78 engine is 66301055.
