## New Engine Speed Sensor and Jumper Harness

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

The purpose of This Technical Service Bulletin is to introduce a new engine speed sensor on the QSKV industrial and genset products.

The old sensor has two sets of two exposed lead wires coming from the sensor and terminating into individual 2-pin connectors. The improved design encloses all four leads with convolute and terminates them into a single 4-pin connector.

The old sensor part number has been obsoleted. A jumper harness is required when replacing an older sensor with the new design. The jumper harness adapts the single 4-pin connector on the new sensor to the two individual 2-pin connectors on the harness. The jumper harness is **not** required when replacing sensors on engines built with the new foam-filled metallic harness, which is often referred to as the Phase 2 harness.
