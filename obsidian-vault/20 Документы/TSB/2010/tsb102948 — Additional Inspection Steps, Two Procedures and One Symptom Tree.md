## Additional Inspection Steps, Two Procedures and One Symptom Tree

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Service/Parts Topic revises Troubleshooting Symptom Tree t105 (Lubricating Oil Pressure Low) in Section TS, Procedure 007-013 (Lubricating Oil Filter (Spin-On)) and Procedure 007-067 (Eliminator Filter) in Section 7 of the Service Manual, QSK45 and QSK60, Bulletin [[4021530 — QSK45 and QSK60 Service Manual\|4021530]]. A change was made to inspect the lubricating pump idler shaft and gear in the Inspect section if debris is found.
