Correct Material

Incorrect Material

This document addresses the rubber casing, Part Number 4918939, supplied with the power cylinder replacer, Part Number 4919227, (also used in Part Number 4918985 and Part Number 4918950) that was manufactured using the incorrect material. Replacements for the rubber casing, Part Number 4918939, will be provided to customers that purchased power cylinder replacer, Part Number 4919227, cylinder liner replacer, Part Number 4918985, rubber casing, Part Number 4918939, and/or power cylinder replacer, Part Number 4918950. In addition, it has been determined the replacer plate, Part Number 4918944, can permanently deflect under recommended torque loads. A new replacer plate, Part Number 2892303, has obsoleted and superseded the replacer plate, Part Number 4918944, and will be provided to customers that purchased power cylinder replacer, Part Number 4919227, cylinder liner replacer, Part Number 4918985, and/or power cylinder replacer, Part Number 4918950.

Rubber casings made from the correct material will have a forword slash marking or hyphen between the word Cummins® and the part number. The rubber casings that require replacement will **not** have this mark (see illustrations above). Each rubber casing returned is also eligible for replacement for the replacer plate, Part Number 4918944 (see instructions below).

If your rubber casing requires replacement, a warranty claim will need to be filed through the Service Products group against the rubber casing, Part Number 4918939, and the replacer plate, Part Number 4918944, **only**. Service Products will review the claim and process it for reimbursement. Once you receive reimbursement, an order may be placed for the new rubber casing, Part Number 4918939, and the replacer plate, Part Number 2892303.

**Warranty Claim to Service Products Group Steps:**

1. Reference document number 4021290 Warranty Administration Manual (WAM), Section 8, Other Programs, Page 111-508-011, for claim routing data (file claim for Part Number 4918939 and Part Number 4918944 **only**).
2. Include in the 'narrative' section of the claim, the TSB number located at the top of this document.
3. Return the failed part (**only** Part Number 4918939) to Service Products, including a printed copy of the warranty claim filed, indicating the warranty claim number. Reference the WAM, Section 8, Other Programs, Page 111-508-010, for Service Products shipping address.

Once the rubber casing is received by Service Products and confirmed to be the incorrect material, the warranty claim will be honored. The replacement rubber casing, Part Number 4918939, and replacer plate, Part Number 2892303, can then be ordered. Year 2010 pricing will apply to the order placed.
