## New Aftercooler Assemblies

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Technical Service Bulletin announces new aftercooler manifolds and aftercooler covers for use on QSK60 diesel engines. The new aftercooler assemblies are manufactured from cast iron to increase the durability of the components.

The new cast iron aftercooler assemblies can be fitted to all QSK60 diesel engines which presently contain the aluminium manifolds, however they are **not** compatible with Phase 1.0 or Phase 1.5 CENSE™ harnesses or vented aftercooler cores. If engines with Phase 1.0 or Phase 1.5 CENSE™ harnesses are upgraded with the cast iron manifolds, the entire engine wiring harness will require an upgrade to a Phase 2 wiring harness. If engines with vented cores are upgraded, the core will also require changing. Table 5 list all the required parts for a CENSE™ engine wiring harness upgrade.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Engines without CENSE™ do **not** require engine harness upgrades, however a standard engine wiring harness and sensor parts list can be found in Table 4, if required.

Cast iron aftercooler components **must** be changed as a quadrant assembly. Cast iron covers **must only** be assembled to cast iron manifolds and aluminium covers **must only** be assembled to aluminium manifolds. This is due to the difference in thermal expansion rates of the two materials. Mixing cast iron and aluminium covers and manifolds can result in air leaks. Cast iron and aluminium aftercooler quadrant assemblies may be fitted to the same engine.

This parts announcement does **not** obsolete the present aluminium aftercooler assemblies. These will still be available as they are required for use on QSK60 Fracture Rig engines.

Cast iron aftercoolers went in to production on QSK60 industrial engines, except for Fracture Rig engines, at Daventry Engine Plant, as of 03 March 2008, with an engine serial number (ESN) first of 33172728. QSK60 Fracture Rig and Power Generator engines are still manufactured using aluminium aftercooler assemblies.

The new cast iron aftercooler assemblies are heavier than the aluminium aftercooler assemblies and therefore require a higher tensile steel capscrews with greater torque capability. All the required new capscrews are listed in Table 2. Use of the aluminium aftercooler assembly mounting capscrews on the cast iron aftercooler assemblies can lead to capscrew breakage and possible engine damage or performance issues.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The torque values for the cast iron aftercooler assembly mounting hardware are greater than that of the aluminium aftercoolers. [[56-010-002-tr — Aftercooler Assembly|Refer to Procedure 010-002 (Aftercooler Assembly) in the QSK45 and 60 Service Manual, Bulletin 4021530, for further information on required torques and aftercooler assembly instructions.]] Follow the instructions in this procedure; the increased weight of the cast iron aftercooler assemblies can cause personal injury.
