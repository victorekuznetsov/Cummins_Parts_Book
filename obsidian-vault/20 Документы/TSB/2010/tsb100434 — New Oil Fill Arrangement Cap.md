## New Oil Fill Arrangement Cap

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Technical Service Bulletin introduces a new ISM and QSM11 oil fill arrangement cap, Part Number 101322. The change in design has been made as an improvement to address the oil leaks and to aid the installation and removal of the oil fill cap. The new oil fill cap, Part number 101322, has been introduced on engines built under option VC2116 since 14-February-2005. Oil fill cap, Part number 101322, is available in service and can be used in place of oil fill cap, Part number 4071224, for all service repairs for all ISM and QSM11 engines with the oil fill arrangement in the valve cover.

The oil fill cap, Part Number 101322, can be identified with the 'T' handle.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> This is a product improvement, and **not** subject to campaign.
