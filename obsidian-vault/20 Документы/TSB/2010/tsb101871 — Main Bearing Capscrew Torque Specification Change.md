## Main Bearing Capscrew Torque Specification Change

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Service/Parts topic supersedes 05T1-7. Please replace 05T1-7 with this topic.

This Service/Parts topic updates the torque procedure for ISX Main Bearing caps in Procedure 001-006.

The new torque value of 149 N•m \[110 ft-lb\] plus 180° has been validated for the MEX blocks (blocks after ESN 79012120) and will now be used on all engines.

The procedure change will affect **only** the torque specification. This eliminates the dual torque specifications on the ISX engine.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> This is a product improvement and is **not** subject to campaign.
