## New Fuel Pumps

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Parts Announcement introduces new fuel pumps for use on all MCRS fuel system engines.

The fuel pump part numbers have been changed due to the introduction of new fuel pumps that have improved gerotor seasl. The pumps have been implemented for use on all MCRS fuel system engines, as they will improve product reliability.

The new fuel pumps for the QSK38/50/60 MCRS engines went into production in September, 2008. Refer to Table 1 for new and old fuel pump part numbers. All new part numbers are available for service.

|---|---|---|---|
| Engine | New Part Number | Old Part Number | ESN First |
| QSK38 MRCS | 4998821 | 4964217 | 33175629 |
| QSK50/60 MRCS | 4998822 | 4964216 | 33175425 |
