## Engine Brake Control Procedure Revision, VGT Engine Braking

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Service/Parts topic revises information in Procedure 213-007, Engine Brake Control, to include the use of the Variable Geometry Turbocharger (VGT) for engine braking on the ISB-CM850 engines, since conventional engine brakes are **not** an option.
