## Engine Base Timing Procedure, Multiple Section Revisions

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Service/Parts Topic revises Procedure 001-008 (Engine Base Timing) in Section 1 of the Service Manual, Signature™, ISX, and QSX15 Engines, Bulletin [[3666239 — Signature™, ISX, and QSX15 Service Manual\|3666239]]. This Service/Parts Topic adds a step for cleaning the Loctite™ residue from the camshaft nose after the camshaft gear has been removed. Information has also been added for inspecting the camshaft gear for cracking around the puller holes. A new step was also added for applying Loctite™ 609, Part Number 3823718, to the inside diameter of the camshaft gear before the gear is installed.
