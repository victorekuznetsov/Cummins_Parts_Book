## Symptom Tree t092 (Fuel in the Lubricating Oil), Cause and Correction Additions and Revision

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Service/Parts Topic updates Symptom Tree t092 (Fuel in the Lubricating Oil) to improve troubleshooting logic. Steps have been moved and added to address the following:

- Fuel pump and injector checks were placed before fuel line restriction checks. The improved tree now places the fuel line restriction check prior to the fuel pump and injector checks.
- There was **not** a step to check the injectors for blown or missing plug balls. The improved tree now includes this check.
