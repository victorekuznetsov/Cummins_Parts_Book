## Aftercooler Core Weld Location Change

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This service/parts topic supersedes 00T10-4 and 02T10-3. It introduces a change made to the aftercooler core on the QSK60 Engine and is divided into four sections:

1. New Parts Introduction
2. Compatibility Matrix
3. Machine Procedure
4. Old Aftercooler Core Reuse Guidelines.

The following changes were made to the original aftercooler core in order to improve its strength and durability:

1. Relocation of the aftercooler core capscrew mounting holes inboard of the flange-forming notch. This is similar in design to the QSK45, K38, and K50 products.
2. A 30 percent increase in the flange thickness. The old flange was 3.42 mm \[0.135 in\] and the new flange is 4.55 mm \[0.179 in\] thick.

This aftercooler core, Part Number 4080568, is referred to in this service/parts topic as *revision 1*.

The *revision 1* aftercooler core went into production at the Daventry Engine Plant with engine serial number 33150882.

A second revision with the following change has now been made to the aftercooler core:

- Relocation of the flange welds back to their original position.

This aftercooler core, Part Number 4100636, is referred to in this service/parts topic as *revision 2*.

The *revision 2* aftercooler core went into production at the Daventry Engine Plant with engine serial number 33152630.

Air Intake Manifold and Aftercooler Cover

The air intake manifold and aftercooler covers were modified for *revision 1* of the aftercooler core. No changes have been made to these components for *revision 2* of the aftercooler core. The revised intake manifold and cover will **not** accept the original aftercooler core. The original manifold and cover will **not** accept either of the revised aftercooler core without rework/machining. The rework/machining procedure is given below. If a revised cover is fitted to an old intake manifold then a new longer roll pin, Part Number 4086080, is required to align the cover.
