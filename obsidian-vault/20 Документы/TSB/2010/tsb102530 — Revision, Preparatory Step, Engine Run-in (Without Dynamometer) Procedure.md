## Revision, Preparatory Step, Engine Run-in (Without Dynamometer) Procedure

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

The purpose of this Service/Parts Topic is to revise the Preparatory Step for Procedure 014-004, Engine Run-in (Without Dynamometer). The requirements to pre heat the engine and the use of SAE 10W-30 lubricating oil has been removed.
