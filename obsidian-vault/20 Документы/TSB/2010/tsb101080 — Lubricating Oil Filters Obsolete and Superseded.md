## Lubricating Oil Filters Obsolete and Superseded

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

In November 2009, the StrataPore™ gradient-density oil filter media used in LF9000 and LF9001 filters was adjusted to optimize engine protection, filter life, and cold flow properties of the filter.

This adjustment resulted in the release of two new part numbers and eventual obsolescence of the original part numbers. Reference the supersession table below for detailed part replacement information.

| Part Description | Old Part Number | New Part Number |
|---|---|---|
| Filter, Lubricating Oil | LF9000 (3101868) | LF9070 (2882673) |
| Filter, Lubricating Oil | LF9001 (3101869) | LF9080 (2882674) |

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> This obsolescence and supersession of these filters is a use-up and go-to process. There is no need to exchange or scrap any of the old part number filters. As stock of the old part numbers is exhausted, and the new filter part numbers become available, begin using the new part numbers.

The new part numbers were used exclusively in production beginning October 12, 2010. Because lubricating oil filters are replaced regularly during operation, the production start date is being communicated instead of the use of ESN first.
