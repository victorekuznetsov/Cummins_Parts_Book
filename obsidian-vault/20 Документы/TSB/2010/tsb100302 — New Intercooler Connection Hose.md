## New Intercooler Connection Hose

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Technical Service Bulletin details the introduction of a new intercooler connection hose on the dual stage QSK60 series engines. The new hose has been released to improve hose durability. The hose material has changed from a glass fibre material to a knitted polyester hose material.

| Intercooler Connection Hose Material Change Matrix |  |  |
|---|---|---|
| Criteria | Old Hose | New Hose |
| Part Number | 4100590 | 3636448 |
| Material | Glass Fibre | Knitted Polyester |
| Color | Orange | Blue |

![[10600218.png]]

This change was implemented into production beginning with Engine Serial Number first of 33157569, built 23rd August 2004.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> This is a product improvement **not** subject to campaign.
