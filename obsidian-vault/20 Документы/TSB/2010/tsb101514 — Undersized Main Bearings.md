## Undersized Main Bearings

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Service Parts Topic introduces undersized main bearings for the M11, ISM, and QSM11 series engines. Undersized main bearings can be used as a repair option for main cap fretting instead of cylinder block replacement. Undersized main bearings are approved for National Overhaul Warranty (N.O.W.).
