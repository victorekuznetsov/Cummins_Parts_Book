### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Service/Parts Topic revises Step 2D-2 in Fault Code 222 in the Troubleshooting and Repair Manual, Electronic Control System, Signature, ISX and QSX15 Engines, Bulletin 3666259. The air pressure sensor, **not** the air temperature sensor, should be replaced in the event of a pin-to-pin short circuit in this check.
