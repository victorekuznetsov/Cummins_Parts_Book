## Crankcase Vent Hose Modification for Cold Weather

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Service/Parts Topic announces a modification to the crankcase vent hose on Signature, ISX, and QSX15 engines operating in cold weather. This modification can be made to any vehicles that may be operating in ambient temperatures from -23°C \[-10°F\] and below.

The following illustration shows the basic routing of the original crankcase vent hose.

The approximate length of the crankcase vent hose ranged from 168 cm \[66 in\] to 112 cm \[44 in\] in length.
