## New Oil Pan Adapter Seal

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Technical Service Bulletin introduces a material change for the rectangular strip seal on the rear of the oil pan adapter. The seal will change from Nitrile to Viton to prevent hardening of the seal due to high temperatures. Nitrile seals will work for most applications.

The existing seal has a diagonal line across both ends (Figure 1). The new seal has no mark (Figure 2).

![[01400630.png]]

Figure 1, Current Seal with Diagonal Mark

Figure 2, New Seal Without Diagonal Mark

Repair procedures will remain the same.

The current seal will **not** be removed from kits that are in stock. Order the new seal separately until they are included in affected kits.
