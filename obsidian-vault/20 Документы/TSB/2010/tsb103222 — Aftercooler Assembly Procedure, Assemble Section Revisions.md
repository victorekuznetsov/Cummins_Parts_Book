## Aftercooler Assembly Procedure, Assemble Section Revisions

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Service/Parts Topic announces revisions to the Assemble section of Procedure 010-002 (Aftercooler Assembly).

The revisions clarify the Assemble section of the procedure by removing information about the installation of the lower cooler element gasket.

Only one gasket is used; between the aftercooler support and aftercooler cover.
