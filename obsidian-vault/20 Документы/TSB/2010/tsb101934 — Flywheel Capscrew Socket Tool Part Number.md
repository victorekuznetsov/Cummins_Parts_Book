## Flywheel Capscrew Socket Tool Part Number

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Service Parts Topic identifies the need to add the flywheel mounting capscrew socket part number and correct a capscrew size in Procedure 016-005 of the service manual. The 21mm impact socket, Part Number 3162813, is **not** listed. The flywheel mounting capscrews and guide studs listed as M6 should be M16.
