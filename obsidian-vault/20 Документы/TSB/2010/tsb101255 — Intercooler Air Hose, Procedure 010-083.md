## Intercooler Air Hose, Procedure 010-083

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This service/parts topic introduces a new intercooler air hose for QSK60 two-stage engines and a revised installation procedure. The hose referred to in this topic connects from the bottom of the intercoolers to the high-pressure turbochargers. There are six of these hoses located around the engine, three per intercooler, and three on each side of the engine. On one end of the engine, the intercooler is connected to the high-pressure turbocharger by a single plain rubber hose. On the other end of the engine, the intercooler is connected to the high-pressure turbocharger by two plain rubber hoses attached to each end of a steel pipe. These identical hoses are the ones referred to in this topic.

The cross section of the new hose, Part Number 4100590, has been increased to improve durability compared with the old hoses, Part Numbers 3067428 and 3171790. Part Number 4100590 can be used in place of both Part Numbers 3067428 and 3171790. The Daventry engine plant now uses Part Number 4100590 in all six locations.

The clamps **must** be positioned so the edge of the clamp is between 3 mm \[0.125 in\] and 6 mm \[0.250 in\] from the inner edge of the bead, as shown in the drawing above. Clamps **must not** overhang the edge of the hose. **Do not overtighten the clamps. Torque:** 9 N•m \[80 in-lb\]

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> This hose contains glass fibers. Installing the hose without first moving the intercooler, or forcing the hose into position will cause damage to the glass fibers and seriously reduce the working life of the hose.
