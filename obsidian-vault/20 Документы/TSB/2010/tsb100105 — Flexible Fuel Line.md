## Flexible Fuel Line

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Technical Service Bulletin introduces a new flexible steel-braided fuel line (1) for the M11 and ISM/QSM11 Series engines. This flexible fuel line supersedes the hard steel line that supplies fuel from the fuel pump to the ECM cooling plate. (See Fig. 1.)

This flexible fuel line was implemented into production with engine serial number first 35009935 on June 19, 2000.
