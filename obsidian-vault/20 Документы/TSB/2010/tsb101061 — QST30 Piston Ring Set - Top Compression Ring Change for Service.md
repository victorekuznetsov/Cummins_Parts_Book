## QST30 Piston Ring Set - Top Compression Ring Change for Service

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Technical Service Bulletin informs the field that a new top compression piston ring is being used in the QST30 piston ring set, Part Number [[3804708]]. This top compression piston ring change is for service **only**. Factory installed top compression piston rings will remain unchanged. The switch from the old to new top compression piston ring in the service kits began between June and September 2010. The piston ring set part number will **not** be changing. See Table 1 for changes to piston rings used in production and service.

|  | **New Service Part Number** | **Old Service Part Number** | **Production Part Number** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Piston Ring Set | [[3804708]] | [[3804708]] | N/A |
| Top Compression Ring | [[4975767]] marked as 1RX | [[3092569]] Marked as 1R | [[3092569]] Marked as 1R |
| Second Compression Ring | [[3092570]] Marked as 2RN | [[3092570]] Marked as 2RN | [[3092570]] Marked as 2RN |
| Oil Piston Ring | [[3092571]] | [[3092571]] | [[3092571]] |

The two different top compression rings are interchangeable, but can be identified by the difference in appearance. The new service top compression piston ring has a white paint mark and the top compression piston ring installed from the factory has an orange color paint mark. The two top compression rings also have different profiles that are shown in Figure 1.

![[01l00005.png]]

Figure 1. Profile of New Service Top Compression Piston Ring (left) and Profile of Factory Installed Top Compression Piston Ring and Old Service Top Compression Piston Ring (right).

Different markings appear on the two top compression rings. The top compression ring used for service (1) is marked with “1RX” and the top compression piston ring formerly used for service and presently installed in the factory (2) is marked with “1R” on one side of the end gap and “A” on the other side. These markings are shown in Figure 2.

![[01l00007.png]]

Figure 2.

Figure 2. Locations of distinguishing markings on both top compression piston rings.

When installing all piston rings, make sure the identification markings are facing up.
