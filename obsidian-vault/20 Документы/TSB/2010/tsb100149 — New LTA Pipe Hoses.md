## New LTA Pipe Hoses

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Technical Service Bulletin introduces new low temperature aftercooling(LTA) pipe hoses for the QSK45 and QSK60 engines. The new hose has been released for improved durability.

The old hose, Part Number 3073123, is a three ply design using glass fibre reinforcement.

The new hose, Part Number [[4080544]], is a four ply design using a polyester fibre reinforcement.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The old and new hoses are similar in appearance.

The new LTA pipe hose went into production at the Daventry Engine Plant with engine serial number first 33150762.
