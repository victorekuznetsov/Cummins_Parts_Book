## Main Bearing Inspect for Reuse Guidelines and Repair

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This service/parts topic obsoletes 00T1-4A. Replace 00T1-4A with this topic.

This service/parts topic provides information to assist all repair and service personnel in diagnosing main cap fretting on M11 and ISM series engines. This topic includes the following:

- Main cap fretting general information
- Main bearing and bore inspect for reuse guidelines
- Main bearing Plastigage® procedures
- Main bearing condemnation limits
- Loctite® 518 procedures
- Block repair options.

Main bearing capscrew torque procedures:

- Torque-Plus-Angle method
- Torque-To-Yield method.

The following kit part numbers may be used to repair blocks.

|---|---|
| Service block kit | 4070793NX |
| ReCon® service block kit | 4070793RX |
