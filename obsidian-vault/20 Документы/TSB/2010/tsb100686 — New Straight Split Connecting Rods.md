## New Straight Split Connecting Rods

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Technical Service Bulletin announces a new connecting rod for use on QSK45 and QSK60 diesel engines.

![[01600450.png]]

The new connecting rod has changed from an angle split (2) to a straight split (1) bolted joint connection. See the illustration above. The change was introduced to increase the durability of the connecting rod. Straight split (1) and angle split (2) connecting rods can **not** be mixed within an engine. All connecting rods **must** be the same.

The new straight split connecting rod went into partial production at Daventry engine plant on 21 November 2007, and full production on 10 March 2008. Engine serial number (ESN) first for full production is 33172847. Engines with the following ESN's were built with the new straight split connecting rod between 21 November 2007 and 10 March 2008:

Straight split connecting rods can **only** be used with new-style piston cooling nozzles and cylinder blocks which contain the new casting changes, or have been modified. Cylinder blocks built prior to 22 January 2008 and ESN first 33172060 require machining to allow for the new hardware. Contact an Cummins® Authorized Repair Distributor for further assistance with cylinder block machining requirements.
