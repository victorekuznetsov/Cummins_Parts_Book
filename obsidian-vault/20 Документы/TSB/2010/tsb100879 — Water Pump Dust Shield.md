## Water Pump Dust Shield

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This document announces the release of a water pump dust shield and related components, as a product improvement for QSX15 industrial engines.

A water pump dust shield, for use on QSX15 industrial engines, is being released to address dust intrusion into the water pump weep chamber, which can cause water pump seal leaks. The dust shield will reduce dirt intrusion and, thereby, reduce water pump seal leaks.

The water pump dust shield and related components can be purchased and installed on units that have issues with dirt intrusion and water pump seal leaks due to dirt intrusion.
