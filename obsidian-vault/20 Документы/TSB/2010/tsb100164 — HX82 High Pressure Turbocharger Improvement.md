## HX82 High Pressure Turbocharger Improvement

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Technical Service Bulletin announces the release of a new HX82 turbocharger service kit, Part Number 4089126. The turbocharger has a new turbine housing, designed to eliminate cracking at the exhaust mounting flange due to thermal fatigue.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The complete turbocharger can only be purchased as part of the service kit, although the new turbine housing (Part Number 3597922), is available individually.

| Table 1, Part Number Changes |  |  |
|---|---|---|
| Description | New Part Number | Old Part Number |
| Turbocharger Service Kit | 4089126 | 3800691 |
| Turbocharger Turbine Housing | 3597922 | 3539766 |

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> When replacing the turbine housing, the capscrews, lockplates and exhaust flange gasket **must** be changed. These parts **must** be ordered in addition to the turbine housing.
