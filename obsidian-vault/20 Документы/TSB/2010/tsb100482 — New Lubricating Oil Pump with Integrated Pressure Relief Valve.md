## New Lubricating Oil Pump with Integrated Pressure Relief Valve

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Technical Service Bulletin introduces a redesigned lubricating oil pump with an integrated pressure relief valve.

![[17601177.png]]

This new lubricating oil pump will reduce oil pressure spikes during cold weather start up.

When a lubricating oil pump is replaced in the field, the new lubricating oil pump with the integrated pressure relief valve **must** be used. A temporary repair practice (TRP) will soon authorize the field to install the new lubricating oil pump when an oil filter failure occurs.

The new lubricating oil pumps, obsoletes and supersedes **all** of the old lubricating oil pumps. The remaining inventories of the old lubricating oil pumps have been scrapped.
