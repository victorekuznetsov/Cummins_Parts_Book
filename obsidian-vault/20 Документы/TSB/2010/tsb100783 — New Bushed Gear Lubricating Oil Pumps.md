## New Bushed Gear Lubricating Oil Pumps

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Technical Service Bulletin announces a product improvement to the ISX family lube pump. This change is backwards compatible with all ISX engines. There are several internal dimensional and part changes, resulting in an obsolete and supersede process for old part numbers, as shown in Table 1.

| Table 1 |  |  |  |
|---|---|---|---|
| Part Description | Old Part Number | New Part Number | ESN First |
| Pump, Lubricating Oil (Aluminum Body) | 4026691 | 2864072 | 79311644 on May 11 2008 |
| Pump, Lubricating Oil (Cast Iron Body) | 3100445 | 2864073 | 79321671 on June 27 2008 |
| Kit, Lube Pump (Aluminum Body) | 4024814 | 4955954 | N/A |
| Kit, Lube Pump (Cast Iron Body) | 4024935 | 4955955 | N/A |
