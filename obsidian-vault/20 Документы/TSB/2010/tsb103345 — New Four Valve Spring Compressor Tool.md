## New Four Valve Spring Compressor Tool

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Service/Parts Topic introduces a new four valve spring compressor tool, Part Number 4919358, for use on Signature™, ISX, and QSX15 engines. See Figure 1. The new tool has been revised to accommodate future products. The new four valve spring compressor tool, Part Number 4919358, replaces four valve spring compressor tool, Part Number 3162986. The new tool is backwards compatible with all previous Signature™, ISX, and QSX15 engines which utilized tool, Part Number 3162986.

This Service/Parts Topic also revises Procedure 022-001 (Service Tools) in Section 2 for the Signature™, ISX, and QSX15 engines.
