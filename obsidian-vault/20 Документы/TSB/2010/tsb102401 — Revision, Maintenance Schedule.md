## Revision, Maintenance Schedule

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Service/Parts Topic intends to make it clear that QSK45 and QSK60 engines with the Quantum fuel system (mechanically actuated injectors) no longer require a valve and injector set to be performed at 1500 hours. Instead, the valves and injectors will **only** be adjusted when the injector is serviced.

This Service/Parts Topic revises the maintenance schedule, Procedure 102-002 (Maintenance Schedule) to remove the scheduled item for adjusting the overhead.
