## Revision, Multiple Steps, (Crankshaft Seal, Front) and (Gear Cover, Lower) Procedures

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Service/Parts Topic revises Procedure 001-023 (Crankshaft Seal, Front) and Procedure 001-080 (Gear Cover, Lower). Both procedures were updated to clarify the replacement of the lower gear covers with a beveled half groove or a full o-ring groove under the crankshaft seal flange.

The full o-ring groove covers, contain an o-ring in the lower gear cover groove and were only used prior to June 5, 1999. The lower gear cover **must** be replaced if installing a new seal.

The beveled half groove covers do **not** contain an o-ring and **only** require replacement if damaged.
