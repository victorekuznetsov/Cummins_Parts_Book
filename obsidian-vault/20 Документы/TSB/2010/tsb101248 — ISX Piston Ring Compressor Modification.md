## ISX Piston Ring Compressor Modification

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Service/Parts Topic introduces Procedure 022-002, Service Tool Modification, for Signature, ISX, and QSX15 Series Engines.

The change to the new flat-top cylinder liners on the Signature, ISX, and QSX15 Series Engines made it necessary to release a new piston ring compressor, Part Number 3164846.

The older piston ring compressor, Part Number 3163158, can be modified to be functionally identical to the new tool. After modification, the piston ring compressor will be compatible with both the older stepped cylinder liners and the newer flat-top liners, and both articulated and single-piece alloy pistons. The **only** modification to perform is to remove the machined step on the lower edge of the ring compressor bore.

The newly-machined face **must** remain perpendicular to the ring compressor bore.
