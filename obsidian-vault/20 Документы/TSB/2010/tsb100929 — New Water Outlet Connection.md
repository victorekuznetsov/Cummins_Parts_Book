## New Water Outlet Connection

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Technical Service Bulletin announces the introduction of a new water outlet connection, Part Number 5261014, manufactured from Polyamide HTN51, to replace the present, water outlet connection Part Number [[3943300]], manufactured from metal.

![[08d00560.png]]

Figure1 - New Part Number 5261014 and Figure 2 - Old Part Number [[3943300]]

The new design incorporates the addition of ribs and compression limiters to strengthen the part and distribute stress loads. There is no change to the location interface or fastening process.

| Outside Diameter | Inside Diameter | Pipe Wall Thickness |  |  |  |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Old | New | Old | New | Old | New |
| 44.45 mm | 44.45 mm | 41.15 mm | 38.45 mm | 1.15 mm | 3.0 mm |
