> ### Warranty Statement
>
> The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

>
> This Technical Service Bulletin supersedes topic 05T1-20. Please replace 05T1-20 with this topic.
>
> This Technical Service Bulletin introduces a new front gear cover strip seal. This improvement has been made to address front gear cover oil leaks in the field.
>
> The new strip seal is rust in color and has a dog bone cross-section as compared to the old black strip seal with an oval cross-section.
