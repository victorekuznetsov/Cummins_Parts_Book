## Revision, Leak Test, Lubricating Oil Cooler Procedure

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Service/Parts Topic revises the Leak Test step of Procedure 007-003, Lubricating Oil Cooler, in the Signature, ISX, and QSX15 Service Manual, Bulletin [[3666239 — Signature™, ISX, and QSX15 Service Manual\|3666239]]. The leak test for the oil cooler assembly is most effective when performed in water below 7°C \[44°F\].
