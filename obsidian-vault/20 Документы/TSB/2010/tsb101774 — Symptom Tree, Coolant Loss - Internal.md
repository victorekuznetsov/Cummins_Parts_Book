## Symptom Tree, Coolant Loss - Internal

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Service/Parts Topic revises troubleshooting symptom tree [[3666239 — Signature™, ISX, and QSX15 Service Manual\|3666239]]-t021, Coolant Loss - Internal. The revisions direct the user to pressure test the cooling system prior to removing the cylinder head and, if no visible coolant leaks or rapid pressure loss is observed, to replace the injector o-rings and install the injectors to specification.

Confirm there are no coolant leaks on any of the components listed in the list below and pressure test the cooling system as described in Procedure 008-018 in the Signature, ISX and QSX15 Service Manual. If no rapid pressure loss is observed and no visual indications of bubbles or wetness exist in the exhaust ports, replace the injector o-rings and install the injectors as described in Procedure 006-026 in the Signature, ISX, and QSX15 Service Manual. If pressure loss or visual indications of bubbles are observed, inspect the head for cracks or porosity as described in Procedure 002-004 in the Signature, ISX, and QSX15 Service Manual. If no bubbles are observed and no visual indication of cracks are present, replace the injector o-rings and install the injectors.

Check the following component locations for external coolant loss:

1. Air compressor cylinder head
2. EGR cooler
3. Lubricating oil cooler
4. Fuel heater
5. Transmission oil cooler or torque converter cooler
6. Cylinder head gasket leaking.
