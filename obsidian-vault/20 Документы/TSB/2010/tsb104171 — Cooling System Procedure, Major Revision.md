## Cooling System Procedure, Major Revision

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Service/Parts Topic introduces major revisions to Procedure 008-018 (Cooling System) in Section 8 of the Signature™, ISX, and QSX15 Service Manual, Bulletin [[3666239 — Signature™, ISX, and QSX15 Service Manual\|3666239]]. The revisions clarify statements regarding the proper coolant fill and deaeration process for engines equipped with EGR and add other detail regarding this process. A graphical representation of the coolant fill and deaeration procedure has also been added.
