## Rocker Lever Housing Gasket Changes

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Technical Service Bulletin introduces a revised version of the rocker lever housing gasket for use on the K19, K38, and K50 engines.

A revised version of the gasket, Part Number [[3630839]], was phased into production at the Cummins Industrial Center, reaching 100 percent in October 1999, beginning approximately with engine serial number 37189426. Approximate engine serial number first for the Daventry plant is 33145682, built in March 2000.

![[03600015.png]]

Fig. 1, Rocker Lever Housing Gaskets (1) Flexible Metal Ring (new) and (2) Rigid Metal Ring (old)

Reports from the field of oil and coolant leaks resulted in the following revisions to the gasket:

- Coining of the steel portion of the gasket was made wider and thicker to distribute the loading, which had caused some of the old gaskets to split.
- The seal for a capscrew hole that is inside the housing (**always** nearest the intake valves) was changed to a metal washer that is bonded with an elastomer to the steel portion of the gasket. The previous version had **only** the elastomer.
- The two capscrew holes nearest the water passage were changed to add more steel so that approximately half of the seal circumference is steel and half is elastomer.

When installing the gasket, make certain that all sealing surfaces are aligned properly, particularly around the large water transfer hole. This should be done by looking at the gasket from the side/edge before installation. If the gasket is **not** flat (i.e., has 'twist' in it) or is distorted, do **not** use it. Also, check the gasket, once it has been installed, to make certain that all of the holes are aligned.

The revised gasket, Part Number [[3630839]], is the **only** gasket now available from the supplier. The following service kits will contain the revised gasket:
