## Cylinder Head Procedure, Assemble Section Revision

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Service/Parts Topic revises procedure 002-004 (Cylinder Head) for the QSK45 and QSK60 CM850 MCRS engines. The instruction to repair the cylinder head injector seats, if found out of specification, has been removed from the Assemble section for mechanical and electronically actuated injectors.
