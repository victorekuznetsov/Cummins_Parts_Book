## LTA and Intercooler Tube O-Ring

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Technical Service Bulletin is to introduce a new LTA and intercooler tube o-ring, Part Number [[4066112]], for QSK60 Industrial and Power Generation engines. The new o-ring is a Viton/EPDM blend, which is a material that offers resistance to swell without the long term decomposition associated with the contact with coolant.

This change went into production at the Daventry Engine Plant on 23-Apr-2001. The approximate ESN first is 33149297.

| New Part Number | Quantity | Description | Old Part Number | Quantity | Description |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [[4066112]] | 1 | Seal, O-Ring | 3175545 | 1 | Seal, O-Ring |

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Do not use petroleum-based oil when installing the new o-ring. The use of petroleum-based oil will cause swelling of the o-ring. Use vegetable oil to lubricate the o-ring.

The new o-ring is available for new engine production and service.
