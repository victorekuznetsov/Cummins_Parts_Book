## Main Bearing Part Number Reinstatements

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This document announces the reinstatement of previous part numbers for (VP2q) main bearings on QSK45, QSK60, and QSK78 diesel engines.

Due to reliability issues with the new (VP2i) main bearings introduced in June 2008, Daventry Engine Plant has reinstated previous part numbers into production and service. Some engines may have already been up-fitted in the field with VP2q main bearings.

The engine serial number (ESN) first for reinstatement on the QSK45 and QSK60 engines is 33176301.

The engine serial number (ESN) first for reinstatement on the QSK78 engine is 66301159.

The following ESNs were fitted with the reinstated part numbers before the ESN first: 33175933, 33175975, 33176053, 33176067, 33176122, 33176135, 33176163, 33176167, 33176168, 33176177, 33176199, 33176219, 33176221, 33176226, 33176227, 33176237, 33176242, 33176250, 33176251, 33176259, 33176263, 33176265, 33176274, 33176275, 33176296, and 33176299.
