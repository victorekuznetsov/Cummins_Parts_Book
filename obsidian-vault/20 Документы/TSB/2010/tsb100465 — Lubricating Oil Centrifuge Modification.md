## Lubricating Oil Centrifuge Modification

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Technical Service Bulletin details a modification carried out on the lubricating oil centrifuge (Fleetguard® Part Numbers 389497500S, CH4410500 and CH4412000), fitted on some QSK60 Construction series engines. To resolve an issue of high pressure spikes within the centrifuge and subsequent rotor cracking, the balance notch on the rotor turbine has been modified to include a radius which will lower the stresses and eliminate the cracking.

The modified rotor centrifuge was first fitted to engines in Daventry engine plant in November 2005, ESN 1st 33162018.
