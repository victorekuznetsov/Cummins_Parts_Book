## Cylinder Block Service Tool Addition

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Service/Parts Topic adds a new cylinder block service tool. This new service tool is used with Guide Kit, Part Number 4918339, to inspect the connecting rods for bends, twists, and length.
