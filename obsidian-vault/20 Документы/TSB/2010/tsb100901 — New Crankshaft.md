## New Crankshaft

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Technical Service Bulletin announces the release of a new fully counterweighted crankshaft for QSK60 industrial engines.

The fully counterweighted crankshaft has sixteen counterweights (Figure 1), one on each web, as opposed to the partially counterweighted crankshaft, which has eight counterweights (Figure 2).

The fully counterweighted crankshaft will aid in reducing the higher internal loads experienced within industrial applications, improving main bearing oil film thickness and main bearing life.

The fully counterweighted crankshaft will **only** be installed in industrial engines from production plants, but can be installed in most other applications in service. The **only** engines the fully counterweighted crankshaft can **not** be installed into are the Marine certified engines, because of Marine certification requirements.

The new fully counterweighted crankshaft went into partial production at Daventry Engine Plant, from 9 November 2009, and full, industrial, production on 11 January 2010. Engine serial number (ESN) first for full production in industrial applications is ESN 33180121. QSK60 blocks have had a machine cut added to the left bank pan rail inner wall section at the main bearing locations (Figure 3). QSK60 blocks that do **not** have these machine cuts, will need to have some counterweights removed in order to remove and install the number 8 main bearing cap. Use the following procedures for further information for removing the main bearings and crankshaft. The procedures can be found in the QSK45 and QSK60 Service Manual, Bulletin [[4021530 — QSK45 and QSK60 Service Manual\|4021530]].
