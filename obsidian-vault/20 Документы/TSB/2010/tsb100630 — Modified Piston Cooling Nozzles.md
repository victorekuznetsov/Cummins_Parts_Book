## Modified Piston Cooling Nozzles

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Technical Service Bulletin announces the use of 45,000 modified piston cooling nozzles, Part Number 3412860, on Signature, ISX, and QSX15 engines. This equates to 7,500 engines affected.

The reason for the use of modified cooling nozzles is due to a casting shift in the engine block. The cast piston cooling nozzle alignment notch section of the block did **not** allow the nozzle to seat against the mounting screw spot face without interference. When the nozzle mounting screw is torqued to specification the plastic alignment arm of the nozzle is put in distress. A fracture to the plastic section of the nozzle could result in loose pieces inside the engine, and could cause loss of nozzle alignment.

![[01c00487.png]]

Modified Area of Piston Cooling Nozzle

The piston cooling nozzle has been modified. The plastic alignment arm has been sanded down to fit the cast alignment notch in the block. These nozzles have been modified 'by hand'; therefore, the modifications to the nozzles do vary.

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> If a nozzle is replaced from these engines, the new nozzle must be modified to fit the block. Failure to modify nozzles for these blocks could result in engine damage.
