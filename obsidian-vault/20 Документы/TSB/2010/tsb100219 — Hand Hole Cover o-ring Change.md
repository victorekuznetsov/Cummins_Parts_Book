## Hand Hole Cover o-ring Change

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Technical Service Bulletin provides information regarding the product improvement of the o-ring for the hand hole cover on QSK45 and QSK60 engines. This improvement consists of an o-ring material change.

The purpose of this change is to improve o-ring durability.

![[01600264.png]]

The original o-ring, Part Number [[3089370]], was made from a nitrile based material. The new o-ring, Part Number [[4100660]], is made from a viton based material. Engine serial number first for this change 33153345, build date January 21, 2003

This part is **not** subject to a campaign or TRP.
