## Niehoff Voltage Regulator for Service

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

The purpose of this topic is to announce that the voltage regulator on the Niehoff alternator is now available for service.

The voltage regulator on the Niehoff alternator is now available as a separate service part. Alternator replacement is no longer necessary in the event of voltage regulator damage.
