## New Cylinder Head Gaskets

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

A new cylinder head gasket, Part No. 3076189 has been released for all NT and N14 engines. This new cylinder head gasket is a fully molded gasket and supersedes cylinder head gasket, Part No. 3074982.

The difference between the old and the new cylinder head gasket is that the new gasket does **not** have grommets or the molded cluster. All sealing on the new cylinder head gasket is done with edge molded rubber.

A new 0.25 mm \[0.010–inch\] thicker cylinder head gasket, Part No. 3082563 is also being released to supersede cylinder head gasket, Part No. 3080180. The new 0.25 mm \[0.010–inch\] thick cylinder head gasket is also a fully molded gasket.

The use of this new cylinder head gasket requires clean head and block surfaces. These surfaces can be cleaned with any safety solvent. The use of soluble oil in (water pump lubricant) the cooling system will damage the rubber seal in the new gasket. Do **not** use soluble oil in the cooling system when the new gasket is being used.

Full production of the fully molded cylinder head gasket started in January 1995, with ESN first 11754745, at Columbus Engine Plant. Full production of this cylinder head gasket at Shotts began in March 1995, with ESN first 23235840. Mexico is expected to make this change mid fourth quarter 1995. Recon® is expected to change the new cylinder head gasket in July 1995.

Refer to the Parts Status Table for a listing of all the part numbers that have been superseded to include the new fully molded cylinder head gasket and other kits that have been updated to include the new gasket. Other gasket kits will be updated later this year to include the new gasket. This Service Parts Topic will be revised later this year.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Old cylinder head gaskets, Part Nos. 3801328 and 3801329 are both being superseded to cylinder head gasket, Part No. 3804275. Cylinder head gasket, Part No. 3801328 has standard size water grommets and cylinder head gasket, Part No. 3801329 has 0.25 mm \[0.010-inch\] oversize grommets. Oversize grommets are not needed with the new fully molded cylinder head gasket.
