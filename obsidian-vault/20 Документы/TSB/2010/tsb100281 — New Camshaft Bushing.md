## New Camshaft Bushing

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Technical Service Bulletin updates a change made to the QSK45 and QSK60 series engines. The topic introduces a new camshaft bushing Part Number, [[4007741]], which has a revised material specification to improve the durability of the components. The revised camshaft bushing Part Number [[4007741]] only replaces the cam bushes fitted to the front left bank and rear right bank location, Part Number 3089172. All other camshaft bushings remain unchanged.

Engine Serial Number first is 33155895.
