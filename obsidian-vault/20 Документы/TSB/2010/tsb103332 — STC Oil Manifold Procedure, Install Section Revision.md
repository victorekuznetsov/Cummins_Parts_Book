### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Service/Parts Topic revises the Install section in Procedure 006-038 (STC Oil Manifold) for the K19 engine. Loctite™ 271 is be applied to the capscrews before installation, to prevent loosening.
