## Revision to Procedure 001-039

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This service/parts topic introduces a change to the QSK45 and QSK60 Troubleshooting and Repair Manual. A change has been made to Procedure 001-039. Replace Procedure 001-039 in this manual with this topic.

The original procedure in this manual did **not** make clear the correct orientation of the oil holes in the accessory drive gear shaft in relation to the cylinder block. This service/parts topic clarifies the position of the shaft upon installation.
