## Maintenance Schedule, Radiator Pressure Cap Check Addition

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Service/Parts Topic revises Procedure 102-002 (Maintenance Schedule) in Section 2 of the Signature™ and ISX Operation and Maintenance Manual, Bulletin [[3666251 — Signature and ISX Operation and Maintenance Manual\|3666251]], to add the Radiator Pressure Cap maintenance check. The manual is being revised to ensure the radiator pressure cap is functioning correctly. The system **must** maintain pressure to make sure of proper engine performance.
