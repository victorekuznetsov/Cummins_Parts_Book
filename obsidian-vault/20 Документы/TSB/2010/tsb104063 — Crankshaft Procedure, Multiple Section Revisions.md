## Crankshaft Procedure, Multiple Section Revisions

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Service/Parts Topic revises the remove and install the thrust bearings to the preparatory and finishing steps. The min/max for crankshaft total runout was revised to a maximum of 0.230 mm \[0.009 in\]. The maximum adjacent journal runout has been revised to 0.080 mm \[0.003 in\].
