## New Oil Pan Options

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Technical Service Bulletin announces a new range of oil pan options for certain B and C Series engines that have previously utilized isolated mounting capscrews.

The new options provide a more robust oil pan assembly with improved sealing characteristics.

These new options do **not** utilize isolated mounting capscrews; these isolators are now obsolete for production.

If any of the previous option components are to be replaced in the field, the original replacement parts remain available for service **only**.

The oil pan capscrews are **not** interchangeable.
