## New Exhaust Manifold

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Technical Service Bulletin introduces a new exhaust manifold for use on the QSK45, QSK60, and QSK60 CM2150 (Modular Common Rail System) series engines.

The holes in the manifold flanges have been increased to 14 mm \[0.55 in\] diameter and slots to 14 x 10.5 mm \[055 x 0.41 in\] size in the new version of exhaust manifolds.

The holes and slots center axes remain in the same positions as in the previous version.

The increased clearance between the capscrews, holes, and slots in the manifold flanges, will reduce the capscrew load due to thermal expansion of the manifold during engine operation. Existing manifolds can be reworked to the new dimensions.

Use this procedure for installation instructions and new tool requirements for a dry exhaust manifold in Service Manual, QSK45 and QSK60, Bulletin [[4021530 — QSK45 and QSK60 Service Manual\|4021530]]. [[56-011-007-tr — Exhaust Manifold, Dry|Refer to Procedure 011-007 in Section 11.]]

The estimated time for introducing this change into production at the Daventry Engine Plant is February 2010.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The new exhaust manifolds are available for new engine production and service.
