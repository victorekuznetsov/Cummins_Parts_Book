## New Piston Ring Sets

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This document introduces a new piston ring set for the QSK45 and QSK60 Tier 1, HPI® (High Pressure Injection) Fuel System, (diesel engines **only**).

The piston ring set part number has changed due to the introduction of a new compression (top) piston ring for use on the QSK45 and QSK60 Tier 1, HPI® Fuel System, diesel engines. The new piston ring is presently used on QSK60 MCRS (Tier 2) diesel engines. The Tier 2 compression ring has been structured for use on Tier 1 engines, as it will further improve product durability.

During repair, new and old style piston rings can be mixed within an engine. However, it is recommended that during a rebuild, the new piston ring sets be used through out the engine. The installation procedure remains unchanged.

The new compression (top) piston ring went into production at the Daventry Engine Plant for QSK45/60 Tier 1 engines on 16 March 2009. The engine serial number (ESN) first is 33178004 (QSK60).

This new piston ring is available for service.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The old piston ring sets will still be available for service, as they are required for QSK60G gas engines. Make certain the new piston ring sets are ordered for diesel engines.
