## Crankshaft Seals, RTV on Outer Diameter

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Service/Parts Topic supercedes topic 06T1-28. Replace 06T1-28 with this topic.

This Service/Parts Topic updates Procedure 001-023 (Front Crankshaft Seal) and Procedure 001-024 (Rear Crankshaft Seal).

When the front or rear crankshaft seals are installed, it is necessary to apply a small bead of high performance RTV sealant Part Number 4918771 (Loctite™5920) or equivalent to the outside diameter of the seal. The purpose of the RTV is to reduce the possibility of oil leaks between the seal and the mounting plate. The engine serial number first with this seal configuration is 331157377, built 04 August 2004.

Crankshaft Seals requiring high performance RTV sealant Part Number 4918771 (Loctite™5920) or equivalent:

- Front crankshaft seal, Part Number 4100624
- Rear crankshaft seal, Part Number 4100625
