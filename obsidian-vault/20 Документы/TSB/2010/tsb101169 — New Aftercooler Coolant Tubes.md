## New Aftercooler Coolant Tubes

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This service/parts topic announces the introduction of new one-piece aftercooler coolant tubes on all QSK60 series engines in order to address failures due to coolant tube seal wear.

This change replaces the existing eight-tube arrangement with a new four-tube arrangement.

The two mid-point connection blocks and associated brackets are eliminated. Pipe securing brackets are also introduced.

The new one-piece LTA (Low Temperature Aftercooling) tube arrangement went into production at the Daventry Engine Plant with engine serial number first 33149389.
