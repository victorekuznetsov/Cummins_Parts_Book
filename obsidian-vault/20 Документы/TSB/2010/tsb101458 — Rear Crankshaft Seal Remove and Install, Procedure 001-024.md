## Rear Crankshaft Seal Remove and Install, Procedure 001-024

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Service/Parts Topic revises the rear crankshaft seal Procedure 001-024 on the L10, L10G, M11, ISM, ISM e, and QSM11 Series engines. The revised procedure updates the oil seal removal and installation for Non-REPTO Dry Lip Seal, Non-REPTO Dry Unitized Seal, and REPTO Dry Seal applications. This Service/Parts Topic does **not** change the rear crankshaft seal or the rear crankshaft seal Part Number.
