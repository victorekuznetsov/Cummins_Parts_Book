## Maintenance Schedule, Crankcase Breather Element Revision

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Service/Parts Topic revises Section 7 with the removal of the 1 year specification from the section title in Procedure 102-002 (Maintenance Schedule) in the Signature™ and ISX Operation and Maintenance Manual, Bulletin [[3666251 — Signature and ISX Operation and Maintenance Manual\|3666251]]. The crankcase breather element maintenance, in Section 7, is an emissions related component and can **not** have a time limit specified for a service interval. The customer **must** use the mileage or hours as a guideline.
