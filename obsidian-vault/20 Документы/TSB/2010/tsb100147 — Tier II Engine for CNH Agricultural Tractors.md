## Tier II Engine for CNH Agricultural Tractors

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

In August 2001, CNH (Case New Holland) began using Tier II certification level QSX15 engines in their largest 4WD Ag tractors. For the Case STX 375 tractor, the change was virtually transparent, with the **only** difference being the Tier II certification level of the engine. For the Case STX 440 tractor, CNH decided to uprate the power from 440 HP to 450 HP. The new model designation is the STX 450. In addition, beginning January 2002, Case will offer a third QSX15-powered tractor, the STX 425.

Also in August, CNH introduced the New Holland TJ series tractors, which uses the same engines as their Case STX counterparts. Beginning in January 2002, the New Holland product line will also include a 425 HP model. The tables below describe the complete CNH offering featuring the QSX15 engine.

| **QSX15 Tier I** |  |  |  |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Tractor Model** | **Engine Rating** | **CPL** | **FR Option** |
| Case STX 375 | 375 HP @ 2000 RPM | 2627 | 10293 |
| Case STX 440 | 440 HP @ 2000 RPM | 2627 | 10292 |

| **QSX15 Tier II** |  |  |  |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Tractor Model** | **Engine Rating** | **CPL** | **FR Option** |
| Case STX 375 | 375 HP @ 2000 RPM | 2825 | 10372 |
| Case STX 425 | 425 HP @ 2000 RPM | 2825 | 10453 |
| Case STX 450 | 450 HP @ 2000 RPM | 2825 | 10371 |
| New Holland TJ 375 | 375 HP @ 2000 RPM | 2825 | 10372 |
| New Holland TJ 425 | 425 HP @ 2000 RPM | 2825 | 10453 |
| New Holland TJ 450 | 450 HP @ 2000 RPM | 2825 | 10371 |

Because several Tier II pilot engines were shipped before Tier I production was ended, there is **no** clean engine serial number break separating Tier I and Tier II products. The most obvious difference is the CPL number. In addition, the certification level of a given engine is clearly stated on the engine dataplate.

It is important to note that it is unlawful to use the Tier II 450 HP calibration in a Tier I 440 HP engine. To dissuade any such attempt, CNH has issued the following statement to their field organization:

*The STX 450 engine software was designed to optimize the Tier II emissions engine. It takes advantage of changes to the pistons, injectors, camshaft, exhaust and air intake systems to maximize power and provide efficient operation. The same levels of performance can* **not** *be achieved by placing the new STX 450 engine software into a Tier I engine. In order to uphold the high standards of the STX Series Steiger tractors, updates to previous models will* **not** *be permitted.*

Cummins is committed to fully cooperate with CNH by refusing any request for a power uprate. Regardless of the certification level of the engine, changing the Advertised HP that is listed on the engine's dataplate is strictly prohibited.
