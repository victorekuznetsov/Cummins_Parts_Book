## New Valve Markings for Part Number Identification

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Technical Service Bulletin announces a new marking on C and L engine series valves.

To improve identification of valve part numbers, valves produced after August 2009 have a letter forged on the center of the valve face. Reference the chart below to identify the part numbers by letter.

| Part Number | Letter |
|---|---|
| 4981794 | C |
| 4981795 | X |
| 3942588 | U |
| 3942589 | S |
| 3969637 | O |
| 3970727 | J |
