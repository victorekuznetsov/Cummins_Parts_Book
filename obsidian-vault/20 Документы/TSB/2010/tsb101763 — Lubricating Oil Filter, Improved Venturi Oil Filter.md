## Lubricating Oil Filter, Improved Venturi Oil Filter

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Service/Parts Topic announces a modification to the Heavy Duty line of Fleetguard™ lubricating oil filters.

| Lubricating Oil Filter Part Numbers |  |  |  |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fleetguard™ | LF9000 | LF9001 | LF9031 |
| Cummins | 3101868 | 3101869 | 4906633 |

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Since these filters are being released as a product improvement, there will be no change in part numbers because the form, fit, and function remain unchanged.

The venturi design has been optimized to better distribute oil flow during normal engine operation; however during extreme engine oil conditions (additive dropout, contamination, soot etc.) the improvements increase the overall filtration effectiveness.

While the oil flow control device enhances filtration, it is imperative that the recommended oil and filter change guidelines be followed. Refer to the Operation and Maintenance Manuals, Bulletin 4021345 for ISX.

These filters are being released for all present production heavy duty applications which use an engine mounted oil filter. These revised lubricating oil filters can also be used on any application which is presently capable of using the LF9000, LF9001, and LF9031 filters.

The date code on the bottom of the filter can be used to identify whether the oil flow control device has been added to the venturi.

Engines built prior to October 7, 2005, were produced with a lubricating oil filter that did not utilize the added improvements, including the modified flow control device in the venturi.

Engines built after October 7, 2005, were produced with a lubricating oil filter with a date code 05258 or higher, which feature the new oil flow venturi with the added oil flow control device. The engine serial number first (ESN) for ISX engines is 79136878.

For proper lubricating oil filter installation, refer to Procedure 007-013 in the Signature, ISX and QSX15 Service Manual, Bulletin [[3666239 — Signature™, ISX, and QSX15 Service Manual|3666239]].
