## New Cylinder Block

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Technical Service Bulletin introduces a new ISX cylinder block. The new cylinder block eliminates the access holes for the exhaust gas coolant supply and vent ports. The Part Number for this block is 3684716. Service procedures for the block will remain unchanged. Service block kits, Part Numbers 4955718 and 4955719, will be unaffected and will continue to utilize the block which contains access holes for the EGR coolant supply and vent ports (block Part Number 4965679).

![[01c00624.png]]

Cylinder block illustrating the location of the eliminated holes (1).

| New Part Number | Description | Old Part Number |
|---|---|---|
| 3684716 | Cylinder Block (EGR coolant vent and supply ports removed, not available for service) | N/A |
| 4965679 | Cylinder Block (EGR coolant vent and supply ports included, available in service kits) | 4965679 |
| 4955718 | Cylinder Block Kit (PBS Memphis Assembled) kit includes cylinder block 4965679 and liners | 4955502 |
| 4955719 | Service Cylinder Block Kit (JEP Jamestown engine plant) assembled kit includes cylinder block 4965679, liners, and crankshaft | 4955474 |

Jamestown Engine Plant began phasing the new cylinder block into production on November 17, 2008. The Engine Serial Number first is 79350365.
