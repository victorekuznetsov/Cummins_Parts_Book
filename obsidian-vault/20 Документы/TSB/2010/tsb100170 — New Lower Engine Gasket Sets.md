## New Lower Engine Gasket Sets

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Technical Service Bulletin introduces new lower engine gasket sets which can be purchased for smaller repairs.

Currently, there is a lower gasket set for the QSK60, Part Number [[4089148]], and a lower gasket set for the QSK45, Part Number 4066323. These are large sets with many different o-rings, seals, and hoses. The new gaskets sets are divided into specific functions on the engine. A combination of the smaller sets is the same as the original lower gasket set.

All QSK60 and QSK45 Power Generation and Industrial engines will be affected by this change.
