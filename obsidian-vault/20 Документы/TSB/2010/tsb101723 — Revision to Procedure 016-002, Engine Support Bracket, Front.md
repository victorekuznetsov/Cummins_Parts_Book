## Revision to Procedure 016-002, Engine Support Bracket, Front

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Service/Parts Topic updates Procedure 016-002 in the Signature, ISX and QSX15 Engines Troubleshooting and Repair Manual, Bulletin [[3666239 — Signature™, ISX, and QSX15 Service Manual\|3666239]].

The torque procedure and specifications for the front engine support bracket capscrews are being updated to the same procedure and specifications as used at initial engine assembly.

| Old Torque Specification | New Torque Specification | Engines Affected |
|---|---|---|
| 165 N•m \[122 ft-lb\] | 97 N•m + 60 degrees \[72 ft-lb + 60 degrees\] | Signature, ISX, QSX15 |

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> This is a product improvement and **not** subject to campaign.
