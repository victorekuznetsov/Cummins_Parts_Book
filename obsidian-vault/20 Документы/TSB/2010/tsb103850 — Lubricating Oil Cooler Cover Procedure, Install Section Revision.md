## Lubricating Oil Cooler Cover Procedure, Install Section Revision

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Service/Parts Topic revises the Install section of Procedure 007-045 (Lubricating Oil Cooler Cover) in the QSK45 and QSK60 Service Manual, Bulletin [[4021530 — QSK45 and QSK60 Service Manual\|4021530]], to include the use of Loctite™ 271, Part Number 3375068, on the oil cooler stud threads on the oil cooler cover. This change is due to the introduction of the edge-molded oil cooler stud gasket.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The new lubricating oil cooler mounting stud gasket is being introduced with a separate Service/Parts Topic. Also, Procedure 007-045 (Lubricating Oil Cooler Cover) in the following bulletins is being revised using two separate Service/Parts Topics:

- QSK78 Series Engines Troubleshooting and Repair Manual, Bulletin 3666727
- Industrial QSK45G and QSK60G Series Engines Troubleshooting and Repair Manual, Bulletin 4021383.
