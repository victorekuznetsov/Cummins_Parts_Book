## Revision, Maintenance Schedule

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Service/Parts Topic removes Procedure 003-018 (Crankcase Breather Tube) from Section 8 (Maintenance Procedures at 200,000 Kilometers \[125,000 Miles\], 3000 Hours, or 2 Years) of the Signature and ISX Operation and Maintenance Manual, Bulletin [[3666251 — Signature and ISX Operation and Maintenance Manual\|3666251]]. This procedure is already listed in Section 3 (Maintenance Procedures at Daily Interval). In addition, Procedure 102-002 (Maintenance Schedule) is revised accordingly in the Signature and ISX Operation and Maintenance Manual, Bulletin [[3666251 — Signature and ISX Operation and Maintenance Manual\|3666251]] and the ISX Engines Owners Manual, Bulletin [[4960314 — ISX Owners Manual\|4960314]].
