## Procedure for Head Casting Erosion Repair

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Service/Parts Topic introduces a new service kit used to repair cylinder head erosion around the intake ports.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Each kit contains intake port plugs, epoxy filler material, releasing agent, a drill bit, and tool instructions. This kit can be used for multiple repairs, depending on the amount of filler material required.
