## CM870 Shutoff Solenoid Groove

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Technical Service Bulletin provides information on the new shutoff solenoid stator for the Signature and ISX with CM870, and QSX15 series engines. The shutoff solenoid stator incorporates a new groove located on the shutoff valve coil. A groove has been added to the face of the fuel shutoff solenoid stator to improve the assembly process. The groove will help detect missing shield gaskets during assembly of the integrated fuel system module. The revision is functionally transparent to the field; however a difference on the face of the solenoid stator will be seen.

Illustrated below are the old shutoff solenoid stator (1) and the new shutoff solenoid stator (2) with the groove.

![[05c00234.png]]

The engine population affected is engine serial numbers (ESN) 14029332 to ESN 79096861.

Engines built after March 8, 2005 will use the new single post shutoff solenoid stator with the groove. Shutoff Solenoid Kit, Part Number [[3408421]] RX/NX, which has **not** changed, was implemented in production on March 8, 2005.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> This is a product improvement and not subject to campaign.
