## Revision 8.0 Injectors

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> This Technical Service Bulletin supercedes SPT 02T6-4; replace 02T6-4 with this one.

The purpose of This Technical Service Bulletin is to introduce the new revision 8.0 injectors which supercede the revision 6.0 CF (cavitation fix) injectors for QSK45/60 applications. The revision 8.0 injectors have an improved design which will increase reliability.

No calibration change is required, and they can be used interchangeably with the old injectors. The parts matrix below illustrates the part number supercession trail.

The Engine Serial Number first (ESN) for the injector is 33154438.
