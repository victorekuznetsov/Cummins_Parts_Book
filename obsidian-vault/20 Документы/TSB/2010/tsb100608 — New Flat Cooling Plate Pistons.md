## New Flat Cooling Plate Pistons

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

The underside of the articulated piston crown has two domed plates that form the piston cooling gallery. The oil that flows through this gallery removes heat from the piston crown. The domed plates are **not** serviceable. Refer to Figure 1 to see this design.

Three new flat cooling plate pistons have been released. The underside of the crown has been redesigned with two nonserviceable flat cooling plate segments. Refer to Figure 2 to see this design.

When an M11 industrial engine (CPL 2119, 2336, 2363, 2503, 2916, 2917, 2918, 2828, and 2829) is repaired or rebuilt with the new flat cooling plate pistons, the engine dataplate **must** be stamped with FFNR-8.

When an M11 Plus automotive engine (CPL 2036, 2037, 2370, and 2371) is repaired or rebuilt with the new flat cooling plate piston, the engine dataplate **must** be stamped with FF255. Targeted applications include refuse/disposal trucks, mixers, food delivery, dump trucks, and bulk haulers.

When an ISM automotive engine (CPL 2607 and 2608) is repaired or rebuilt with a new flat cooling plate piston, the engine dataplate **must** be stamped with FF337. Targeted applications include refuse/disposal trucks, highway department/DOT dump trucks, and mixers.

New piston kits have been created for the new pistons. They are shown in the following table.

| New Piston Kits |  |  |
|---|---|---|
| Engine Model | CPL | New Piston Kit Part Number |
| M11 Industrial | 2119, 2336, 2363, 2503, 2916, 2917, 2918 | 4089481 |
| M11 Industrial | 2828, 2829 | 4955348 |
| M11 Plus Automotive | 2036, 2037, 2370, 2371 | 4089481 |
| ISM Automotive | 2607, 2608 | 4089480 |

NOTE: Repairs and rebuilds with the new pistons/kits **must** be done in complete engine sets, due to piston weight differences.
