## Gear Cover Bushing Installation Instructions

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Technical Service Bulletin introduces the new front support trunnion forthe QSK45 and QSK60 Series engines. This Technical Service Bulletin also revisesProcedure 001-031, Front Gear Cover, for the Troubleshooting and Repair Manual,QSK45 and QSK60 Series Engines, Bulletin 3666217. The new trunnion, Part Number4066304, is cast-steel design and was first used in production on engine serialnumber first 33148569.

To fit this trunnion to engines using the previous design, you **must** change the wear sleeve fitted to the front gear cover. The newwear sleeve, Part Number 4065741, has different dimensions and uses a hardermaterial.

The grease fitting (1) has been moved to the lower left-bank side ofthe trunnion.
