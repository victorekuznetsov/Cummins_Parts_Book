## 15L ISX/QSX Timing Reference Availability on QuickServe™ Online

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

The reference document providing timing wedge and nominal measured static timing is now available in two locations on QuickServe™ Online (QSOL) for all Dual Cam ISX/QSX engines. Previously, timing wedges were only listed on the engine dataplate. Procedure 99-850-029 (ISX, QSX Static Timing Wedge), lists both the correct timing wedges and nominal measured timing value by ISX/QSX15 engine Critical Parts List (CPL). This procedure is listed under Related Information on the CPL page in the parts section of QSOL and also in Section V - Specifications of the Signature™, ISX, and QSX15 Service Manual, Bulletin [[3666239 — Signature™, ISX, and QSX15 Service Manual\|3666239]].
