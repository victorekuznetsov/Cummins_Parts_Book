## Improved Water Transfer Connection

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

![[08i00002.png]]

Figure 1: New End Cap

This document introduces a new water transfer connection for the K19 and QSK19 engines. The new water transfer tube, Part Number 5288358, obsoletes and supersedes the old tube, Part Number 3008862. The new tube has an improved cap design for increased durability. The new design has a cap that is fillet welded for more consistent manufacturing. See Figure 1.

The new tube design does **not** affect the removal or installation procedures.

The ESN first with the new water transfer connection is 37251078.

Any ReCon® engines built after 06-Sep-2011 will have the new water transfer connection.

Table 1 lists the new and old part numbers.

| Table 1: K19 and QSK19 Water Transfer Connection |  |  |
|---|---|---|
| Description | New Part Number | Old Part Number |
| Connection, Water Transfer | 5288358 | 3008862 |
