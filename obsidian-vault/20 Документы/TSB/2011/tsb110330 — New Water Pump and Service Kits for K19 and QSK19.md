## New Water Pump and Service Kits for K19 and QSK19

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

**Introduction**

This document introduces new water pump and service kits for K19 and QSK19 diesel engines.The new water pump is used in applications that operate in high levels of atmospheric contamination. The new service kits improve water pump durability by reducing the possibility of environmental contamination through the addition of a weep hole vent tube.

The rail application water pumps have also been updated with a new controlled dimension weep hole to accommodate the vent tube and fitting. The orientation of the weep hole was moved on the Rail Application water pump. The new part number, for rail application **only,** is in Table 1.

Assembly instructions are outlined below.

Railcar Upgrade Kit Instruction

This upfit requires a replacement of the water pump assembly, from Part Number 4081093 to Part Number 4998217.

Coat the first two barbs of the 90° fitting, Part Number [[4998211]], with medium strength silicone sealant, Part Number 3823494, or equivalent. Drive it into the weep hole of the pump until the shank touches the pump housing.

Do **not** put sealant on the tip of the barbed fitting, as this could cause contamination of the pump and/or plugging of the fitting upon installation.

Once the fitting is installed in the weep hole, install the hose, Part Number 4998213, by hand, over the fitting until the end of the hose touches the shank of the fitting.

Use the following procedure in the Service Manual, QSK19, QSK19 CM850 MCRS, and QSK19 CM2150 MCRS, Bulletin [[4021592 — QSK19, QSK19 CM850 MCRS, and QSK19 CM2150 MCRS Service Manual\|4021592]], to install the water pump. [[20-008-062-tr — Water Pump|Refer to Procedure 008-062 in Section 8.]]

Slide the p-clip, Part Number 4992548, over the loose end of the hose. Align the bolt hole of the p-clip with the tapped boss at the lower right corner of the oil cooler housing.

Secure the hose and p-clip to the block using capscrew, Part Number 3910665. Tighten the capscrew.

**Torque Value** 45 N•m \[33 ft-lb\]

Be sure **not** to twist or kink the hose.

![[08i00003.png]]

If necessary, cut any extra length of hose that would potentially be submerged from lying too close to the block.

**Industrial Pump Upgrade Kit Instructions**

This upfit can be performed on new water pumps **only** to protect the shaft from atmospheric contamination.

Coat the first two barbs of the 90° fitting, Part Number [[4998211]], with medium strength silicone sealant, Part Number 3823494, or equivalent. Drive the barb into the weep hole of the water pump until the shank touches the pump housing.

Do **not** put sealant on the tip of the barbed fitting, as this could cause contamination of the pump and/or plugging of the fitting upon installation.

Once the fitting is installed in the weep hole, install the hose, Part Number 4998213, by hand over the fitting, until the end of the hose touches the shank of the fitting.

Use the following procedure in the Service Manual, QSK19, QSK19 CM850 MCRS, and QSK19 CM2150 MCRS, Bulletin [[4021592 — QSK19, QSK19 CM850 MCRS, and QSK19 CM2150 MCRS Service Manual\|4021592]], to install the water pump. [[20-008-062-tr — Water Pump|Refer to Procedure 008-062 in Section 8.]]

Secure the hose and p-clip, Part Number 4992548, to the hand hole cover, using the hand hole cover capscrew.

**Torque Value** 45 N•m \[33 ft-lb\]

Be sure **not** to twist or kink the hose. The hose **must** go continuously down from the weep hole. Its lowest point **must** be its open end.

![[08i00004.png]]

If necessary, cut the hose to make sure its open end is unobstructed and in clear space.
