## New Accessory Drive Belt Tensioner

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This document announces the release of a new accessory drive belt tensioner, Part Number 2891940, for all ISX and QSX platforms. This new belt tensioner has a dual ball bearing pulley and improved grease to improve overall reliability.

The new belt tensioner, Part Number 2891940, will have a slightly different arm and pulley, but is a direct replacement for the old belt tensioner, Part Number 3104149. The present mounting hardware will continue to be used.

The new accessory drive belt tensioner, Part Number 2891940, obsoletes and supersedes the previous tensioner, Part Number 3104149. See Table 1 for the engine serial number (ESN) first.

| Engine Model | ESN | Build Date |
|---|---|---|
| ISX15 | 79493464 | 7 July 2011 |
| ISX11.9 | 75004331 | 7 July 2011 |
