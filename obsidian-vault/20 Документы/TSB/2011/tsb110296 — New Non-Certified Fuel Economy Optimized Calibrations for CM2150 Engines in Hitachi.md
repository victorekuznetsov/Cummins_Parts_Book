## New Non-Certified Fuel Economy Optimized Calibrations for CM2150 Engines in Hitachi™ Applications

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This document announces the release of new Fuel Economy Optimized calibrations for QSK38/50/60 CM2150 engines powering Hitachi™ hydraulic excavators and dump trucks. These calibrations are considered non-certified and non-emissionized calibrations and have been tested showing a 4% increase (on average) in fuel economy performance, based on a typical excavator or dump truck duty cycle. This number can vary, depending on altitude, duty cycle, etc.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> As these Fuel Economy Optimized calibrations are classified as non-certified and non-emissionized (do **not** meet any certification regulations), they **mustnot** be installed on engines that will work in emissions regulated areas like North America (USA and Canada). For a Fuel Economy Optimized calibration to be downloaded to certified engines, please consult the local and national emissions regulations agencies and make sure this is in compliance with the emissions regulation laws.

Reference Warranty Administration Manual Bulletin, 4021290 in Section 13 - Engine History for engine modifications, modification requirements, and further information regarding engine dataplate updates.
