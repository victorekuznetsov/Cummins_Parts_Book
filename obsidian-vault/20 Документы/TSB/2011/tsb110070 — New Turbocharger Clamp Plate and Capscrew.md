## New Turbocharger Clamp Plate and Capscrew

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

Subject:

Some MidRange, Heavy Duty, and Gas engines operating with wastegate turbochargers can possibly experience exhaust leaks from the turbine and bearing housing mounting joint. This exhaust leak is caused by the mounting capscrews relaxing after operation. New clamp plates and capscrews, which are made of a different material, have been released to correct this issue. This change requires technicians to replace the current clamp plate and capscrews using the repair instructions included in the kit.

Issue:

The typical complaint of this issue is excessive noise due to an exhaust leak. Upon inspection, an exhaust leak is found between the turbine housing and the bearing housing of the turbocharger. The capscrews holding the housings together can possibly be loose or broken.

Verification/Confirmation:

Once it is determined that an exhaust leak exists at the turbine and bearing housing mating joint, check the capscrews for signs of loosening or damage.

Resolution:

Several turbocharger part numbers are affected. Table 1 lists the turbocharger models affected by the new clamp plate and capscrews. Installation instructions are included in the new kits.
