## New Modular Common Rail System Fuel Pumps and Weep Hole Plugs

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This document introduces new MCRS fuel pumps with the weep hole removed from the gerotor body and plugs that can be used to plug existing pumps with a weep hole. The weep hole was removed from the pump to prevent external leaks that were caused when debris was ingested into the seals.

#### Engines

- QSK19 CM850 and CM2150
- QSK38 CM850 and CM2150
- QSK50 CM850 and CM2150
- QSK60 CM850 and CM2150

The fuel pump weep hole can **only** be plugged if it is has a slow oil leak or if it is **not** leaking. If a steady stream of oil is coming from it, the pump can **not** be plugged. A fuel leak of any amount can **not** be plugged.

A standard rivet gun with a 2 mm \[0.078 in\] shank adapter can be used to install the 3 mm \[0.118 in \] plug. Special tooling is needed to install the 7 mm \[0.276 in\] plug. Part Numbers KW-003 and HKW-003908 are needed, which can be purchased from www.kvt-koenig.com.

#### Plug installation instructions:

- Make sure all fuel pump caps are installed in fuel pump ports to avoid contamination. Carefully remove and clean any dirt and debris from the gerotor assembly of the MCRS pump with a clean cloth. Make sure the weep hole bore is dry and clean. For off-engine installation, it is recommended to prop the pump up such that the weep hole is oriented slightly downwards, to prevent any debris or component pieces from falling into it.
- Place the plug into the tool shank end first. Make sure the expander plug body bottoms out against the tool and the shank is snug against the expander plug body.
- Activate the tool until the expander plug is successfully installed and the stem snaps off cleanly. Inspect the plug to confirm it is installed fully into the weep hole. Discard the shank section in an appropriate manner.
