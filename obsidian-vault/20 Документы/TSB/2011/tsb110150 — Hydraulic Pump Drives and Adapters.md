## Hydraulic Pump Drives and Adapters

### Contents

This document was originally released between 1994 and 2001. It has been added to QSOL for informational purposes

This document introduces a new hydraulic pump adapter for hydraulic pump drive options in applications where an air compressor is **not** used. The new adapter was introduced on January 20, 1993 at CDC. Engine Serial Number first 44828852. The new adapter includes a drilling that transfers lubricating oil from the gear housing to the hydraulic pump drive connection to the hydraulic pump spline shaft to reduce wear on the spline drive teeth. Failure of lubricating oil reaching this connection could result in spline shaft or spline drive failure.

Install by orientating the adapter so that the oil collection hole is on the top side of the accessory gear. The word "TOP" is printed on the face of the adapter and should be positioned so that it is on the top end of the adapter when installing. Failure to install properly will **not** allow lubricating oil to reach the spline shaft connection. See page 2 for a list of new part numbers which have the lubricating oil collection hole improvement.
