## Press-Fit Barrel and Plungers and Ceramic Links for Family K STC Injectors (1225)

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This document was originally released between 1994 and 2001. It has been added to QSOL for informational purposes

This document announces the release of a press-fit barrel and plunger assembly and a ceramic link for family K STC injectors. These new parts will provide the same improvement in reliability and durability as Premium K STC injectors. The new barrel and plunger assembly and ceramic link supersede and obsolete the barrel and plunger assembly and steal link. Refer to Table 1.

| **Table 1: Superseding Part Nos.** |  |  |  |  |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Family K STC Injector Part No.** | **New Barrel and Plunger Part No.** | **Old Barrel and Plunger Part No.** | **New Ceramic Link Part No.** | **Old Steel Link Part No.** |
| 3059927 | 3076126 | 3053483 | [[3279819]] | 3042426 |
| 3067393 | 3076126 | 3053483 | [[3279819]] | 3042426 |
| 3279719 | 3076126 | 3053483 | [[3279819]] | 3042426 |

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The new ceramic link, Part No. 3279189, is 0.070 inches longer than the old steel link, Part No. 3042426.

This is a product improvement and is **not** subject to campaign.
