## New Service Kits for Bosch® Fuel Pumps

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

This Technical Service Bulletin explains that four QST30 fuel pumps have been obsoleted by Bosch®. See the table below to determine affected fuel pumps.

Refer to Procedure 005-045 for removal and installation instructions of the fuel lift pump. When installing the mechanical fuel lift pump gasket and mechanical fuel lift pump, tighten the three capscrews to 8 N•m \[72 in-lb\]. When installing the sealing washer and adapter into the M16 fuel inlet port on the injection pump, tighten to 41 N•m \[30 ft-lb\].
