## Dedicated Fuel Pump Lubricating Oil Filter Introduction

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

**Product Affected**

- QSK60 CM850 MCRS
- QSK60 CM2150 MCRS
- QSK60 CM2350 K116
- QSK60 CM2350 K121
- QSK78 CM2350 K126

**Description of Change**

![[17r00269.png]]

Figure 1, New Dedicated Fuel Pump Lube Oil Filter Introduction.

A new dedicated lubricating oil filter for the fuel pump is now available.

New fuel pump lubricating oil filter provides direct filtration for the fuel pump lubricating oil supply immediately before the inlet feed drilling of the fuel pump. This is accomplished through:

- A New fuel pump drive adapter
- Fuel pump drive adapter integrated filter head
- Fleetguard® 20 micron lubricating oil filter, Part Number LF17560.
- New engine control module (ECM) calibration code(s) with auto-detect new engine main wiring harness (left bank)
- New extension wiring harness

Additional features include:

- Additional pre-lube engine cranking circuit switch
- Fuel pump lubricating oil filter outlet pressure sensor and new fault code(s)

![[17r00270.png]]

Figure 2, New Fault Codes.

**Reason for Change**

Improve modular common rail fuel system (MCRS) fuel pump life.

**Customer Communication**

Cummins Inc. recommends that Master Rebuild Centers retrofit the dedicated lubricating oil filter. Factory option(s) on applicable engines during engine rebuild.
