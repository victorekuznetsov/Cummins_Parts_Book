### Contents

**Product Affected**

Engines:

- X15 CM2350 X114B
- X15 CM2350 X116B

Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM):

- Navistar®

**Issue**

Symptom:

- Fault Code 3558.

Root Cause:

- Improper voltage sense wire placement can cause an interference in the ground supply circuit for the entire powertrain wiring harness, which may result in instances of Fault Code 3558.

**Resolution**

- Reference IK0800555 in Navistar® Service Portal.
