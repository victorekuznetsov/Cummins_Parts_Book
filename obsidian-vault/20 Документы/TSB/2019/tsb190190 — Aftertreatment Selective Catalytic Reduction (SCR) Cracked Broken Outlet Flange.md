## Aftertreatment Selective Catalytic Reduction (SCR) Cracked/Broken Outlet Flange

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

**Product Affected**

- X15 CM2350 X114B
- X15 CM2350 X116B

**Issue**

Symptom:

- An exhaust leak is present on the outlet of the aftertreatment selective catalytic reduction (SCR) outlet flange.

Root Cause:

- Cracking or breakage of the aftertreatment SCR catalyst outlet flange.

**Verification**

Inspect the aftertreatment SCR outlet for cracks or breakage.

See Figures 1 and 2 below for examples of cracked/broken outlet of the aftertreatment SCR catalyst.

**Resolution**

If the aftertreatment SCR catalyst outlet flange is cracked or broken; replace the aftertreatment SCR catalyst. See corresponding Service Manual. Reference Procedure 011-036 in Section 11.

Do **not** weld or modify the aftertreatment system to correct the malfunction.
