## QSK60 CM850 and CM2150 Exhaust Gas Temperature Sensors Shorted Low or High

### Core Issue

This technical service bulletin introduces an additional countermeasure that has been developed to reduce customer downtime due to Exhaust Gas Temperature sensors shorted low or high. An issue with the Exhaust Gas Temperature sensor adapter internal thread depth may cause higher than normal vibration on the Exhaust Gas Temperature sensor. This results in the indicated failure mode. Exhaust Gas Temperature sensor failures are often damaged internally with no visible damage.

### Confirmation

Use the fault code troubleshooting tree to confirm and troubleshoot the corresponding fault codes.

### Resolution

If directed by the fault code troubleshooting tree, replace the Exhaust Gas Temperature sensor.

Use the following procedure in the QSK38, QSK50 and QSK60 (CM850 Modular Common Rail System) Troubleshooting and Repair Manual, Bulletin 4021533. Refer to Procedure 019-013 in Section 19.

Use the following procedure in the QSK38, QSK50, and QSK60 CM2150 Electronic Control System Troubleshooting and Repair Manual, Bulletin [[4022102 — QSK38, QSK50, and QSK60 CM2150 Electronic Control System Troubleshooting and Repair M\|4022102]]. [[122-019-013 — Exhaust Temperature Sensor|Refer to Procedure 019-013 in Section 19.]]

In addition, replace **all** the Exhaust Gas Temperature sensor adapters on the engine.
