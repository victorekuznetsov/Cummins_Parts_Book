## Crank No Start Condition: Units Equipped with Endurant™ Transmission

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

Information Only - OEM Related Matter Not Covered By Cummins® - Contact Appropriate OEM Dealer or OEM Representative For Additional Information

### Contents

**Product Affected**

- X15 CM2350 X114B
- X15 CM2350 X116B
- X12 CM2350 X119B

**Issue**

Symptom:

- Units equipped with Eaton™ Endurant™ transmission experience a crank no start event with or without transmission fault codes.
- The following transmission fault codes may be present:

Root Cause:

- Fueling to the engine has been disabled, resulting in a crank no-start condition without logging fault codes.

**Verification**

- Turn keyswitch ON.
- Crank the engine.
- Verify crank no-start condition.
- Stop engine cranking.
- Disconnect the datalink harness to the transmission. See equipment manufacturer service information.
- Verify the engine cranks and starts.
- Turn keyswitch OFF.
- Reconnect the vehicle wiring harness at the transmission. See equipment manufacturer service information.

**Resolution**

- This condition has been resolved in Eaton™ Endurant™ TCM software version 5516018.
