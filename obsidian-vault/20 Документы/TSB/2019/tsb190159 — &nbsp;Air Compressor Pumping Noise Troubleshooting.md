**Issue**

Symptom:

- Drumming noise from the air compressor during operation.

Root Cause:

- Inability of the air plumbing system to dampen excessive sound waves is causing resonance in the system and leading to excessive noise.
- Air compressor drumming noise caused by system resonance will **not** lead to progressive engine damage or mechanical malfunction.

**Verification**

- Verify the air compressor pumping or drumming noise is present.
- The following audio link is a representation of air compressor pumping noise or drumming noise.
