**Description of Change**

A new coolant thermostat housing seal has been implemented for heavy duty platforms. The material of the coolant thermostat seal was changed from brass to stainless steel.

**Reason for Change**

A more robust stainless steel design prevents premature malfunction that may be experienced when utilizing the existing brass seal.

**Customer Communication**

For malfunctions related to the coolant thermostat, coolant thermostat seal or any other malfunctions where the coolant thermostat seal must be replaced, the stainless-steel seal, Part Number [[3084879]], is recommended for replacement.
