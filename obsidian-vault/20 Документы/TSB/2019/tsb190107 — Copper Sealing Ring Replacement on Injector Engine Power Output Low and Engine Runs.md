## Copper Sealing Ring Replacement on Injector: Engine Power Output Low and Engine Runs Rough or Misfires

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

- QSK78 CM850
- QSK78 CM2250 K104

- QSK78 PG CM850

**Issue**

Symptom:

- Engine runs rough or misfire
- Engine power output low/ Low EGT Fault Code(s) 661-668, 671-678, 711-718, or 721-728
- Engine black smoke excessive

Root Cause:

- Corrosion and stress cracks found on copper injector sealing rings. Issue is more prominent at altitude.

**Resolution**

The existing copper injector sealing ring is being replaced by a new stainless-steel injector sealing ring.

**Service Instructions**

- Replace existing copper injector sealing ring with new stainless-steel injector sealing ring.
- See corresponding Service Manual. Reference Procedure 006-026 in Section 6. Remove injector sealing ring by prying one hold tab over the injector nozzle rim at a time.
- The stainless-steel sealing rings are **only** available in standard sizes. If oversized sealing rings are required these are **only** available in copper.
