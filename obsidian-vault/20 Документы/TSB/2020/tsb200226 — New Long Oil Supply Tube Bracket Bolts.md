## New Long Oil Supply Tube Bracket Bolts

- QSK60 CM2350 K137

**Description of Change**

The 4 single bolts used to support bracket part number 4101331 have been changed to a bolt and washer to prevent yielding of the bracket following torquing.

**Reason for Change**

The current bolt used to support bracket 4101331, when torqued, leads to damage to the bracket face. This can lead to a weaker bracket during operation. A single bolt has been replaced with a different bolt and washer part number which should be used to prevent yielding of the bracket. See part number table below.

**Customer Communication**

This change should only be made when an engine is overhauled or if the pickup bracket is removed for other repair/maintenance operations.

**Service Instructions**

The bolts should be torqued at the tube end of the slotted hole.
