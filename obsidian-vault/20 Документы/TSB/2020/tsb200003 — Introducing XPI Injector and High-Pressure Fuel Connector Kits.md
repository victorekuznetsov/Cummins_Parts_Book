**Description of Change**

Starting January 2020, XPI injectors for the aforementioned engines will be offered in kits that include the required high-pressure fuel connector. This means each injector kit will contain (1) injector and (1) high-pressure fuel connector as shown in Table 1 below.

High-pressure fuel connectors will still be offered for sale individually, however, the aforementioned injectors will only be sold in the corresponding kits.

**Reason for Change**

This is a product offering enhancement that is driven by customer feedback related to ease of repair. These kits will ensure that each injector replacement event now gets the required high-pressure fuel connector without the repair location having to identify the part number and place a separate order.

**Service Instructions**

This TSB does not affect any service procedures.
