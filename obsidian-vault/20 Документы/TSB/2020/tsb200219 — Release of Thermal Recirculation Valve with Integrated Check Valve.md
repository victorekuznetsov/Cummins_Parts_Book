## Release of Thermal Recirculation Valve with Integrated Check Valve

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Within this document the thermal recirculation valve will be referred to as TRV.

**Product Affected**

- X15 CM2350 X114B
- X15 CM2350 X116B

**Reason for Change**

The New TRV has an integrated check valve which will help

- Reduce air recirculation in the fuel system
- Higher temperature Thermostat helps reduce fuel gelling during operation
- Allow for upfit to extended life fuel filter for increased fuel filter intervals
- Reduce suction side fuel filter plugging

**Customer Communication**

Customer optional product improvement. The components in this document provide the following benefits:

- Integrated check valve Improving startability if air is trapped in fuel system from service or ingress at other connection
- Robustness to stage 1 filter plugging by higher temperature Thermal Recirculating Valve
- New plumbing provides clearance for longer stage 2 fuel filter for extended intervals

**OverviewX15 CM2350 X114B**

X15 CM2350 X114B uses two fuel line routings, configuration A or configuration B.

- For engines equipped with configuration A fuel lines, currently a service kit is **not** available.
- For engines equipped with configuration B fuel lines, kit part number 5633513 can be used.

**Configuration A**

There may be an overlap in engine serial number (ESN). Verify the vehicle's appropriate fuel supply tube configuration and part number before commencing repairs. See Figure 1 and Figure 2 for visual confirmation and Table 2 for part number verification.

Approximate engine serial number (ESN) range for configuration A: 79890940 to 80038419. For ESN outside of this range confirm part numbers from Table 2.

No service kit is available for this configuration.
