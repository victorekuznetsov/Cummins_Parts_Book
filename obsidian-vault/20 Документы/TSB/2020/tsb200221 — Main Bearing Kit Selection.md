## Main Bearing Kit Selection

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

**Product Affected**

- ISX (All Versions)
- ISX15 (All Versions)
- X15 (All Versions)
- QSX15 (All Versions)

**Description of Change**

This document provides guidance for selection of main bearing kits to verify compatibility with the installed cylinder block and crankshaft combination.

**Reason for Change**

Proliferation of cylinder blocks and crankshafts introduces unique thrust bearings and need for additional main bearing kits.

**Service Instructions**

1. Identify the cylinder block part number by searching the engine serial number (ESN) using parts.cummins.com [https://parts.cummins.com/](https://parts.cummins.com/) parts.cummins.com.
2. Identify the crankshaft part number by searching the ESN on parts.cummins.com.
3. Use Table 2 below to select the correct main bearing kit.
