**Issue**

Symptom: Heads may have color difference at the exhaust face.

**Resolution**

The recon process is salvaging the exhaust face. The parts will have a color difference after finish machining at the exhaust face. This color difference is acceptable.

**Description of Change**

There is no change in form, fit, and function for these heads.

**Customer Communication**

There is no change to part number(s). This TSB is to inform the field that parts produced and sold using the machining processes may look visually different but have the same fit, form, and function. See figure in Part Identification section.
