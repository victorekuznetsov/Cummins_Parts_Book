## QSK60 Construction 3000hp Rebuild Steel to FCD Power Cylinder

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

**Product Affected**

QSK60 CM2150 MRCS

**Description of Change**

All rebuild QSK60 CM2150 MCRS models running 3000hp uprate to be rebuilt with FCD (Ferrous Cast Ductile) power cylinder kit rather than the steel piston variant.

**Reason for Change**

There have been multiple reports of customers experiencing liner cavitation due to excessive secondary motion of Tier 2 steel pistons. Research has been conducted into whether a similar failure mode occurs on FCD, and data shows reduced secondary motion. This reduction in secondary motion results in less liner wall excitation; therefore, more margin against cavitation occurring.

**Customer Communication**

This change only applies to engines running the 3000Hp Tier 2 industrial configuration.

**Service Instructions**

This doesn't authorize field changeouts. This change should only be completed at rebuild or when full power cylinder replacement is required. FCD and steel pistons **must** not be mixed on an engine, a complete set is required.
