## Fractured Low Temperature Aftercooler Water Pump Shaft

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

**Product Affected**

- QST30 CM552

**Issue**

Symptom:

- High intake manifold temperature.

Root Cause:

- Fractured low temperature aftercooler water pump shaft.

**Verification**

Inspect low temperature aftercooler water pump shaft. See Figure 1 below.

![[17r00683.png]]

Figure 1, Fractured Low Temperature Aftercooler Water Pump Shaft.

**Resolution**

A new water pump and water pump brace were released. See Figure 2 below for new mounting arrangement.

![[17r00684.png]]

Figure 2. New Low Temperature Aftercooler Water Pump Mounting Arrangement.

Install two mounting spacers (5) between the water pump housing (1) and water pump brace (4).

Install water pump brace mounting capscrews (2).

- Torque Value: 65 N•m \[ 48 ft-lb \]

Install water pump brace mounting capscrews (3).

- Torque Value: 33 N•m \[ 24 ft-lb \]
