## Updated In-Service Liner Protrusion

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

**Product Affected**

- QSK60 CM500
- QSK60 CM850
- QSK60 CM2150
- QSK60 CM2250
- QSK60 CM2350 K116
- QSK60 CM2350 K117
- QSK60 CM2350 K121
- QSK60 CM2350 K135
- QSK60 CM2350 K136

**Issue**

A project was launched as technicians have been measuring liner protrusion out of specification in the field after the engine has been in service.

The root cause was found to be the service specification being set the same as production. When the engine starts operation, the counterbores relax and the liners move down in the block giving a lower liner protrusion. This has no adverse affect on engine performance, but will give a low liner measurement even after it has been dyno tested.

**Verification/Confirmation**

If the liner protrusion is measured after operation, some engines may be measured lower than the 0.15 mm lower limit.

**Description of Change**

A new liner protrusion table has been added to all QSK60 manuals. This provides an in-service specification and a new rebuild specification.

All rebuild engines must meet the new engine limits and once they have been dyno tested, they can follow the in-service limits.

**Reason for Change**

This change is required as engines were being repaired to bring the liner protrusion back into the required specifications. This work was not required as the low liners should have no adverse affects up to 0.1 mm.
