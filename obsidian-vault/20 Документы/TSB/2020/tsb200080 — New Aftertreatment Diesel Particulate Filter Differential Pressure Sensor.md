## New Aftertreatment Diesel Particulate Filter Differential Pressure Sensor

### Warranty Statement

The information in this document has no effect on present warranty coverage or repair practices, nor does it authorize TRP or Campaign actions.

### Contents

**Product Affected**

- X15 CM2350 X114B
- X15 CM2350 X116B
- X15 CM2350 X123B

**Description of Change**

Due to the implementation of the new Aftertreatment Diesel Particulate Filter Differential Pressure Sensor, new service kits are required to service the components. The new sensor has larger connection ports and larger diameter tubes.

**Service Instructions**

Any service event that requires DPF differential pressure sensor replacement, will require the new DPF differential pressure sensor and new DPF differential pressure tubes, provided in a service kit. Service kit part numbers are shown below.

**Service Parts Availability**

Different service kits are available for different aftertreatment configurations.

Reference Table 1 for applicable aftertreatment configuration part numbers, located on the aftertreatment decomposition reactor tube.

Once the aftertreatment configuration is identified, use Table 2 to find the correct service kit.
