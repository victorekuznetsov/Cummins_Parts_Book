**Description of Change**

New gear pumps are being offered as an optional service parts. With these gear pump options, there are different fuel lines. This document will serve as a guide for awareness on the differences between the fuel lines.

**Reason for Change**

This new gear pump will have equal performance to the existing gear pump, but the pumps are **not** backwards compatible due to the different fuel line attachment points. Parts should be serviced like-for-like.
