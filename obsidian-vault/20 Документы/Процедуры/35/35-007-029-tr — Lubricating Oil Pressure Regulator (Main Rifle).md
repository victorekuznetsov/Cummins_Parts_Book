# Lubricating Oil Pressure Regulator (Main Rifle)
**Регулятор давления масла (главная магистраль)**

> **Двигатели:** [[35354607 — QSM11 CM570 CPL 8608|35354607]], [[35373113 — QSM11 CM570 CPL 8471|35373113]], [[71156161 — QSM11 CM570 CPL 8543|71156161]]

### Preparatory Steps

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> To reduce the possibility of personal injury, avoid direct contact of hot oil with your skin.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Some state and federal agencies have determined that used engine oil can be carcinogenic and cause reproductive toxicity. Avoid inhalation of vapors, ingestion, and prolonged contact with used engine oil. If not reused, dispose of in accordance with local environmental regulations.

- Drain the lubricating oil. [[35-007-037-tr — Lubricating Oil System|Refer to Procedure 007-037 in Section 7.]]
- Remove the oil pan. [[101-007-025-tr — Lubricating Oil Pan|Refer to Procedure 007-025 in Section 7.]]

![[ck800wa.png]]

### Remove

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Carefully remove the retainer plug. The pressure regulator spring is under compression. Personal injury can result. Wear face and eye protection.

Remove the retainer plug (1), regulator spring (2), and regulator plunger (3).

Remove the orifice from the plunger.

![[ck2pgha.png]]

### Inspect for Reuse

Inspect the regulator plunger for scratches or scoring. If scratches are deep enough to be felt with a fingernail, the plunger **must** be replaced.

Check the orifice in the plunger to make sure it is **not** plugged. If the orifice is plugged, clean it before replacing.

The orifice is threaded into the plunger.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 1 n•m [6 in-lb]

![[bp2pgsa.png]]

Measure the free length of the regulator spring.

| Regulator Spring Free Length |  |  |
|---|---|---|
| mm |  | in |
| 84.1 | NOM | 3.31 |

![[bp2sptb.png]]

Use a spring compression tester to determine if the spring is defective.

Replace the spring with a new spring if it is defective.

![[lp2cntb.png]]

| Main Oil Pressure Regulator Spring Load at 48.3 mm \[1.90 in\] |  |  |
|---|---|---|
| n |  | lbf |
| 91.1 | MIN | 20.50 |
| 94.7 | MAX | 21.30 |

![[bp2sptc.png]]

Measure the retainer plug to determine if it is defective. If it does **not** meet the specification, replace it with a new one.

| Main Oil Pressure Regulator Retainer Plug |  |  |
|---|---|---|
| mm |  | in |
| 11.1 | MIN | 0.44 |
| 13.4 | MAX | 0.53 |

![[bp2rtta.png]]

### Install

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> The regulator spring must be compressed to install the retainer plug. Personal injury can result. Wear face and eye protection.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> A counterbore plunger and orifice is unique to engines with a viscosity sensor. Low oil pressure will result if a solid plunger is used with a viscosity sensor.

Install the regulator plunger (3) and regulator spring (2).

Install and tighten the retainer plug (1).

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> Rear Sump Oil Pan 75 n•m [55 ft-lb]

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> Front Sump Oil Pan 122 n•m [90 ft-lb]

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> Center Sump Oil Pan 75 n•m [55 ft-lb]

![[ck2pgha.png]]

### Finishing Steps

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Engine oil pressure must be indicated on the gauge within 15 seconds after starting. If oil pressure is not registered within 15 seconds, shut off the engine immediately to avoid engine damage. Confirm the correct oil level in the oil pan.

- Install the oil pan. [[101-007-025-tr — Lubricating Oil Pan|Refer to Procedure 007-025 in Section 7.]]
- Fill the engine with clean 15W-40 engine oil. [[35-007-037-tr — Lubricating Oil System|Refer to Procedure 007-037 in Section 7.]]
- Operate the engine at idle for 3 minutes and check for leaks.
