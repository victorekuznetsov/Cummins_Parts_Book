### Подготовительные операции

> [!danger] ОПАСНО
> Не снимайте пробку радиатора с горячего двигателя. Снимайте пробку радиатора только после того, как температура охлаждающей жидкости опустится ниже 50 °C \[120 °F\]. Струя горячей охлаждающей жидкости или пар могут привести к травме.

> [!danger] ОПАСНО
> Охлаждающая жидкость токсична. Хранить в недоступном для детей и животных месте. Если деталь не используется повторно, утилизируйте её по местным природоохранным требованиям.

### Тест на утечку

Подключите один конец теплообменника и прикрепите фланец (местного производства) с подгонкой воздушного шланга и воздушным шлангом к другому концу теплообменника.

![[08200295.png]]

> [!danger] ОПАСНО
> При работе со сжатым воздухом применяйте защиту глаз и лица. Разлетающиеся частицы и грязь могут привести к травме.

Поместите теплообменник в резервуар с водой.

Применять 103,4 кПа \[15 psi\] давления воздуха к теплообменнику в течение 1 минуты.

Следите за пузырьками воздуха. Если присутствуют какие-либо пузырьки, ядро теплообменника должно быть заменено.

Отключите подачу воздуха и удалите теплообменник из резервуара для воды.

Удалите оборудование для испытания на давление.

Используйте сжатый воздух для высушивания теплообменника.

![[08200296.png]]

1. Передние крепежные болты

3. Верхние крепежные болты

- Ремонт любых обнаруженных утечек.

> ### Preparatory Steps
>
> **WARNING · Опасно**
> Do not remove the pressure cap from a hot engine. Wait until the coolant temperature is below 50°C \[120°F\] before removing the pressure cap. Heated coolant spray or steam can cause personal injury.
>
> **WARNING · Опасно**
> Coolant is toxic. Keep away from children and pets. If not reused, dispose of in accordance with local environmental regulations.
>
> Remove the coolant pressure cap.
>
> Remove the drain plug from the bottom of the heat exchanger.
>
> After coolant is drained, apply thread sealant and install the drain plug.
>

> |---|---|---|

>
> - Secure the sea water supply valve
> - Locate a point in the system that is lower than the heat exchanger to drain the sea water.
>
> ### Remove
>
> Disconnect the coolant vent line from the expansion tank.
>
> Disconnect the wiring harness from the coolant level sensor.
>
> Remove the two capscrews from the lower sea water pipe flange at the aftercooler assembly.
>
> Remove the two capscrew and tube clamps.
>
> Remove the sea water inlet pipe from the heat exchanger assembly.
>
> Remove the two capscrews from the marine gear oil cooler and disconnect it from the outlet side of the heat exchanger assembly.
>
> Remove the two capscrews from the flange at the coolant water pump inlet.
>
> The coolant tube is a floating connection.
>
> Remove the coolant tube and o-ring from the heat exchanger by twisting and pulling the coolant tube straight out.
>
> Remove the two capscrews and coolant return tube from the upper portion of the expansion tank.
>
> Loosen the two hose clamps at the thermostat housing and remove the coolant return tube.
>
> Remove the two mounting capscrews from the front of the heat exchanger.
>
> Remove the two mounting capscrews from the lower heat exchanger rear mounting bracket.
>
> Remove the two mounting capscrews from the upper heat exchanger mounting bracket.
>
> **WARNING · Опасно**
> This component or assembly weighs greater than 23 kg \[50 lb\]. To prevent serious personal injury, be sure to have assistance or use appropriate lifting equipment to lift this component or assembly.
>
> Remove the heat exchanger and expansion tank assembly from the engine.

> ### Disassemble
>

>

>
> Remove the 22 capscrews from the outer housing that connects to the heat exchanger core.
>
> Remove the four capscrews from the inner housing. These capscrews are threaded into the heat exchanger core.
>
> Remove the heat exchanger core and inner housing from the outer housing.
>
> Rotate the heat exchanger core over so it rests on the inner housing.
>
> Remove the 22 capscrews connecting the heat exchanger core to the inner housing.
>
> Separate the heat exchanger core from the inner housing.
>
> ### Leak Test
>
> Plug one end of the heat exchanger core and attach a flange (locally manufactured) with a air hose fitting and air hose to the other end of the heat exchanger.
>
> **WARNING · Опасно**
> Wear appropriate eye and face protection when using compressed air. Flying debris and dirt can cause personal injury.
>
> Place the heat exchanger core into a tank of water.
>
> Apply 103.4 kPa \[15 psi\] of air pressure to the heat exchanger core for 1 minute.
>
> Watch for air bubbles. If any bubbles are present, the heat exchanger core **must** be replaced.
>
> Shut off the air supply and remove the heat exchanger core from the water tank.
>
> Remove the pressure test equipment.
>
> Use compressed air to dry the heat exchanger core.
>

>
> Use Lubriplate™ paste on the o-ring to be certain of a proper seal without damage.
>

>
> **Note · Примечание**

>

> ### Install
>
> **WARNING · Опасно**
> This component or assembly weighs greater than 23 kg \[50 lb\]. To prevent serious personal injury, be sure to have assistance or use appropriate lifting equipment to lift this component or assembly.
>
> Install the heat exchanger and expansion tank assembly onto the engine.
>
> Install but do **not** tighten the two mounting capscrews to the front of the heat exchanger.
>
> Install the two mounting capscrews to the lower heat exchanger rear mounting bracket.
>
> Install the two mounting capscrews to the upper heat exchanger mounting bracket.
>
> Hand tighten all the capscrews once the heat exchanger assembly is in place.
>
> Tighten the capscrews in the following sequence:
>
> 1. Front mounting capscrews
> 2. Lower mounting capscrews
> 3. Top mounting capscrews
>
