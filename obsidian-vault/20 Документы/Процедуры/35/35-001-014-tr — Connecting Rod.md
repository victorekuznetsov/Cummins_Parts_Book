### Preparatory Steps

The piston and connecting rod **must** be removed as an assembly on M Series engines. [[101-001-054-tr — Piston and Connecting Rod Assembly|Refer to Procedure 001-054 in Section 1.]]

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Batteries can emit explosive gases. To reduce the possibility of personal injury, always ventilate the compartment before servicing the batteries. To reduce the possibility of arcing, remove the negative (-) battery cable first and attach the negative (-) battery cable last.

- Disconnect the battery. [[99-013-009 — Battery Cables and Connections|Refer to Procedure 013-009 in Section 13.]]
- Remove the piston and connecting rod assembly. [[101-001-054-tr — Piston and Connecting Rod Assembly|Refer to Procedure 001-054 in Section 1.]]
- Disassemble the pistons from the connecting rods. [[101-001-054-tr — Piston and Connecting Rod Assembly|Refer to Procedure 001-054 in Section 1.]]

### Clean and Inspect for Reuse

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> When using a steam cleaner, wear safety glasses or a face shield, as well as protective clothing. Hot steam can cause personal injury.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Wear appropriate eye and face protection when using compressed air. Flying debris and dirt can cause personal injury.

Use steam or solvent to clean the connecting rods.

Dry with compressed air.

![[cx2bdeb.png]]

Inspect the connecting rods and caps for damage.

Replace the connecting rod if the "l-beam" is nicked or damaged.

![[cx200sa.png]]

Inspect the connecting rod pin bore bushing for damage.

![[cx200sb.png]]

Install the connecting rod caps and capscrews.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The connecting rod cap number **must** match the number on the connecting rod and be installed with the numbers aligned.

Tighten the capscrews in alternating sequence. [[101-001-054-tr — Piston and Connecting Rod Assembly|Refer to Procedure 001-054 in Section 1.]]

![[cx200aa.png]]

Measure the connecting rod crankshaft bore inside diameter.

|---|---|---|
| mm |  | in |
| 83.975 | MIN | 3.3061 |
| 84.025 | MAX | 3.3080 |

![[cx200sc.png]]

Measure the piston pin bushing inside diameter.

| Piston Pin Bushing I.D. |  |  |
|---|---|---|
| mm |  | in |
| 54.054 | MIN | 2.1281 |
| 54.099 | MAX | 2.1299 |

![[cx200sd.png]]

Remove the capscrews and rod caps.

Measure the connecting rod capscrew outside diameter.

### Bend and Twist Inspection

Use checking fixture, Part Number ST-561, and mandrel set, Part Number 3376690, to inspect the bend and twist of the connecting rod.

Calibrate the checking fixture with a new rod that has be measured for the correct center to center length, 217.975 to 218.025 mm \[8.5817 to 8.5836 in\].

Assemble the connecting rod cap to the rod. [[101-001-054-tr — Piston and Connecting Rod Assembly|Refer to Procedure 001-054 in Section 1.]]

Assemble the connecting rod cap to the rod. Refer to Procedure 001-054 in Section 1.

![[cx2toga.png]]

Install the piston pin mandrel, Part Number 3376693, from the mandrel set, Part Number 3376690, into the piston pin bore.

Install the mandrel, Part Number 3376691, in the piston crankshaft bore and expand the mandrel.

Make sure the pin on the mandrel is down and locked in position in the center of the connecting rod.

![[cx2tohc.png]]

Install the connecting rod into the fixture.

Move the dial holder to position the contact points of the indicators on the mandrel in the piston pin bore.

Tighten the bracket to hold the indicators in position.

Set the dial indicators to zero (0).

![[cx2tova.png]]

Remove the connecting rod from the fixture.

Turn the rod 180 degrees horizontally and install the rod into the fixture again.

![[cx200kc.png]]

Check the dial indicators for zero (0) position again.

If the dial indicators show any change from zero (0), adjust the dials to half the indicated reading.

The fixture is now calibrated to allow the connecting rod to be installed into the fixture in either direction and the dials will indicate an equal defection on either side of the zero (0).

![[cx200kd.png]]

Install the mandrel and arbor into the connecting rod to be inspected.

Install the connecting rod into the fixture.

![[cx2tohd.png]]

Measure the rod length and bend (alignment).

| Connecting Rod Length |  |  |
|---|---|---|
| mm |  | in |
| 217.975 | MIN | 8.5817 |
| 218.025 | MAX | 8.5836 |

| Connecting Rod Alignment (Bushing Installed) |  |  |
|---|---|---|
| mm |  | in |
| 0.10 | MAX | 0.004 |

![[cx200na.png]]

Install a feeler gauge between the mandrel and the dial indicator holding plate as shown.

| Connecting Rod Twist (Bushing Installed) |  |  |
|---|---|---|
| mm |  | in |
| 0.25 | MAX | 0.010 |

Replace the connecting rod if **not** within specification.

### Finishing Steps

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Batteries can emit explosive gases. To reduce the possibility of personal injury, always ventilate the compartment before servicing the batteries. To reduce the possibility of arcing, remove the negative (-) battery cable first and attach the negative (-) battery cable last.

The piston and connecting rod **must** be installed as an assembly on M Series engines. [[101-001-054-tr — Piston and Connecting Rod Assembly|Refer to Procedure 001-054 in Section 1.]]

- Assemble the connecting rod to the piston. [[101-001-054-tr — Piston and Connecting Rod Assembly|Refer to Procedure 001-054 in Section 1.]]
- Install the connecting rod and piston assembly. [[101-001-054-tr — Piston and Connecting Rod Assembly|Refer to Procedure 001-054 in Section 1.]]
- Connect the battery. [[99-013-009 — Battery Cables and Connections|Refer to Procedure 013-009 in Section 13.]]
- Operate the engine and check for leaks.
