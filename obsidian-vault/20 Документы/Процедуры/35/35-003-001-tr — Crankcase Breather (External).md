# Crankcase Breather (External)
**Сапун картера (наружный)**

> [!abstract] Процедура · `35-003-001-tr`
> **Двигатели:** [[35354607 — QSM11 CM570 CPL 8608|35354607]], [[35373113 — QSM11 CM570 CPL 8471|35373113]], [[71156161 — QSM11 CM570 CPL 8543|71156161]]
> **Семейство:** QSM11
> **Входит в руководства:** [[3666322 — ISM, ISMe, and QSM11 Service Manual|3666322]]
> **Секции:** Section 3 - Rocker Levers - Group 03
> **Даты:** изменён 2012-04-23
> **Источник:** [QuickServe](https://quickserve.cummins.com/qs3/pubsys2/xml/en/procedures/35/35-003-001-tr.html) · [PDF-оригинал](https://github.com/victorekuznetsov/Cummins_Parts_Book/raw/main/bulletins/procedures/35-003-001-tr.pdf)

### Remove

Disconnect the wiring connector from the crankcase pressure sensor.

Loosen the two hose clamps on the inlet hose.

Disconnect the hose from the breather. Loosen the outlet hose clamp.

Disconnect the outlet hose.

Disconnect the breather oil drain line.

Remove the drain line in line with the connection nipple; do **not** bend.

![[03z00001.png]]

Remove the four mounting capscrews from the crankcase breather element housing to the breather housing bracket and remove the housing assembly.

![[03z00002.png]]

### Disassemble

Disassemble the crankcase breather assembly. [[101-003-019-tr — Crankcase Breather Element|Refer to Procedure 003-019 in Section 3.]]

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> All capscrews are captive capscrews.

![[ck800wa.png]]

### Clean and Inspect for Reuse

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Wear appropriate eye and face protection when using compressed air. Flying debris and dirt can cause personal injury.

Clean the breather crankcase housing and the breather crankcase cover with solvent.

Dry with compressed air.

Check for cracks in the crankcase breather element housing and the crankcase breather cover. Replace if necessary.

![[03c00148.png]]

### Assemble

Assemble the crankcase breather assembly. [[101-003-019-tr — Crankcase Breather Element|Refer to Procedure 003-019 in Section 3.]]

![[ck800wa.png]]

### Install

Connect the inlet and outlet rubber hoses and the oil drain line.

Install the three hose clamps to the housing. Be sure to push the hose on until it stops on the housing connector.

Verify that the hose clears the fan blade.

Connect the wiring connector to the crankcase pressure sensor.

![[03z00003.png]]

Install the four mounting capscews.

Tighten the capscrews.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> M10 Capscrews 47 n•m [35 ft-lb]

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> M8 Capscrews 20 n•m [177 in-lb]

Remove and install the crankcase pressure sensor. Use the following procedure in the Troubleshooting and Repair CM871 and CM876 Electronic Control System, ISX and ISM Engines, Bulletin 4021560. Refer to Procedure 019-445 in Section 19.

![[03z00004.png]]
