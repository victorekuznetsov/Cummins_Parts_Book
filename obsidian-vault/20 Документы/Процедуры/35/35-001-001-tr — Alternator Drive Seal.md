### Preparatory Step

- Remove the alternator drive belt. [[35-013-005-tr — Charging System Alternator Drive Belt|Refer to Procedure 013-005 in Section 13.]]
- Remove the alternator drive pulley. [[35-009-010-tr — Alternator Drive Pulley|Refer to Procedure 009-010 in Section 9.]]

![[ck800wa.png]]

### Remove

Remove the five capscrews and clamping ring.

Remove the dust seal and oil seal together.

![[08200017.png]]

### Clean

Clean the gasket surface of the gear cover.

![[08200026.png]]

### Install

Do **not** use lubricant to install the seal. The oil seal **must** be installed with the lip of the seal and the shaft clean and dry. The yellow dust lip of the seal **must** be facing out.

Use the installation sleeve provided with the new seal to install the new seal and gasket.

Apply sealant, Part Number 3375066 or equivalent, to the threads of the capscrews.

Install the clamping ring with five (M8-1.25 x 20) capscrews. Tighten the capscrews in a star pattern.

Torque Value:

![[wp2sehn.png]]

Place a light film of oil or antifreeze on the inside diameter of a new oil seal dust seal.

Install the dust seal onto the shaft with the larger outside diameter facing toward the engine.

Push the dust seal back by hand on the shaft until the entire dust seal contacts the oil seal case.

![[wp2seho.png]]

### Finishing Steps

- Install the alternator drive pulley. [[35-009-010-tr — Alternator Drive Pulley|Refer to Procedure 009-010 in Section 9.]]
- Install and adjust the alternator drive belt. [[35-013-005-tr — Charging System Alternator Drive Belt|Refer to Procedure 013-005 in Section 13.]]
