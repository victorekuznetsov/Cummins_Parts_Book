### Maintenance Check

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> The engine's intake air must be filtered to prevent dirt and debris from entering the engine. If any of the intake air piping is damaged or loose, unfiltered air will enter the engine, which can cause premature wear and engine failure.

Inspect the intake air piping once a week for cracked hoses, damage, or loose clamps.

![[tb2cosa.png]]

Replace damaged pipes and tighten loose clamps as necessary to make sure the air intake system does **not** leak.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 8 n•m [71 in-lb]

Check for corrosion of the intake system piping under the clamps and hoses. Corrosion can allow corrosive products and dirt to enter the intake system.

Disassemble and clean the intake air piping as required.

![[tb2cokc.png]]

Noise can be caused by an air leak from the:

- Turbocharger-to-discharge-elbow connection

Inspect for damage. Tighten loose clamps.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 8 n•m [71 in-lb]

![[tb2cosc.png]]

- Turbocharger-to-exhaust-manifold mounting gasket

Replace the gasket. Refer to a Cummins® Authorized Repair Location for turbocharger removal and installation.

![[tb2cosd.png]]

- Turbine housing sealing surface exhaust leak

Tighten the turbine housing capscrews or V-band clamps.
