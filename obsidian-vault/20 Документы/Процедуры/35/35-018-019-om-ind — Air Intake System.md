### Specifications

Industrial Applications

| Maximum Intake Restriction (Clean Element) | 381 mm H 2 O \[15 in H 2 O\] |
|---|---|

| Maximum Intake Restriction (Dirty Element) | 635 mm H 2 O \[25 in H 2 O\] |
|---|---|

#### Maximum Temperature Rise Between Ambient Air and Engine Inlet Air:

| Ambient above 0°C \[32°F\] | 35°C \[63°F\] difference |
|---|---|

#### Maximum Intake Air Restriction Including Air Filter Plumbing

| Clean Filter Element | 635 mm H 2 O \[25 in H 2 O\] |
|---|---|

| Dirty Filter Element | 381 mm H 2 O \[15 in H 2 O\] |
|---|---|

#### Maximum Allowable Pressure Drop from Turbocharger Outlet to Intake Manifold

| 1500 rpm | 2.5 in Hg \[64 mm Hg\] |
|---|---|

| 1800 rpm | 4 in Hg \[102 mm Hg\] |
|---|---|

| Maximum Temperature Rise Between Engine Air Inlet and Intake Manifold | 25°C \[45°F\] |
|---|---|
