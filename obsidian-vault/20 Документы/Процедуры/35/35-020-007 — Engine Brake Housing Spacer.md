# Engine Brake Housing Spacer

> [!abstract] Процедура · `35-020-007`
> **Двигатели:** [[35354607 — QSM11 CM570 CPL 8608|35354607]], [[35373113 — QSM11 CM570 CPL 8471|35373113]], [[71156161 — QSM11 CM570 CPL 8543|71156161]]

> **Секции:** Section 20 - Vehicle Braking - Group 20
> **Даты:** изменён 2015-06-25
> **Источник:** [QuickServe](https://quickserve.cummins.com/qs3/pubsys2/xml/en/procedures/35/35-020-007.html) · [PDF-оригинал](https://github.com/victorekuznetsov/Cummins_Parts_Book/raw/main/bulletins/procedures/35-020-007.pdf)

### Preparatory Steps

- Remove the rocker lever cover. [[35-003-011-tr — Rocker Lever Cover|Refer to Procedure 003-011 in Section 3.]]
- Remove the engine brake housings. [[35-020-024-tr — Engine Brake|Refer to Procedure 020-024 in Section 20.]]

![[ck800wa.png]]

### Remove

Remove the external oil supply hose from the engine brake spacer.

![[20200011.png]]

Disconnect the electrical connectors in the engine brake spacer.

![[20200007.png]]

Disconnect the internal oil supply to the engine brake.

![[20200016.png]]

Remove the 18 spacer capscrews.

![[20200017.png]]

### Clean and Inspect for Reuse

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Wear safety glasses or a face shield, as well as protective clothing, to prevent personal injury when using a steam cleaner or high-pressure water.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Wear appropriate eye and face protection when using compressed air. Flying debris and dirt can cause personal injury.

Remove any RTV sealant or gasket material. Steam clean and dry with compressed air.

![[20200012.png]]

Inspect the spacer for cracks or other damage. Replace if necessary.

![[20200013.png]]

### Install

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The engine brake housing spacer can be sealed with either RTV sealant, if a gasket is **not** available or a part number is **not** listed. Do **not** use RTV sealant and a gasket together.

If using RTV sealant apply a 3.0 mm \[0.118 in\] bead of RTV sealant to the engine brake housing sealing surface.

- Make sure the sealing surface is free of oil film
- The sealant **must** loop around the capscrew holes on the back side (fuel pump side) of the spacer.

If using a gasket, install the gasket on the rocker lever housing.

![[20200014.png]]

Install the spacer on the rocker lever housing.

Install the 18 capscrews in the housing.

![[20200017.png]]

Tighten the capscrews in the sequence shown.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 20 n•m [177 in-lb]

![[20200015.png]]

Connect and tighten the internal engine brake oil supply hose.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 16 n•m [142 in-lb]

![[20200016.png]]

Connect the solenoid wires to the electrical connectors in the engine brake spacer.

![[20200007.png]]

Connect and tighten the external engine brake oil supply hose.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 16 n•m [142 in-lb]

![[20200011.png]]

### Finishing Steps

- Install the engine brake housings. [[35-020-024-tr — Engine Brake|Refer to Procedure 020-024 in Section 20]].
- Install the rocker lever cover. [[35-003-011-tr — Rocker Lever Cover|Refer to Procedure 003-011 in Section 3.]]
- Operate the engine and check for leaks.
