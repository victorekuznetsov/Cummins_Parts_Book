### Preparatory Steps

With EGR

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Do not remove the pressure cap from a hot engine. Wait until the coolant temperature is below 50°C \[120°F\] before removing the pressure cap. Heated coolant spray or steam can cause personal injury.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Coolant is toxic. Keep away from children and pets. If not reused, dispose of in accordance with local environmental regulations.

- Drain the cooling system. [[35-008-018-tr — Cooling System|Refer to Procedure 008-018 in Section 8.]]
- Remove the air intake piping.
- Remove the alternator belt. [[35-013-005-tr — Charging System Alternator Drive Belt|Refer to Procedure 013-005 in Section 13.]]
- Remove the alternator. Refer to Procedure 013-001 in Section 13.
- Remove the lubricating oil cooler. Refer to Procedure 007-003 in Section 7.
- Remove the coolant filter and filter head. [[35-008-007-tr — Coolant Filter Head|Refer to Procedure 008-007 in Section 8.]]
- Remove the coolant heater housing. [[35-008-012-tr — Coolant Heater Housing|Refer to Procedure 008-012 in Section 8.]]
- Remove the turbocharger. [[35-010-033-tr — Turbocharger|Refer to Procedure 010-033 in Section 10.]]
- Remove the coolant thermostat housing and the coolant thermostat housing support. [[35-008-015-tr — Coolant Thermostat Housing Support|Refer to Procedure 008-015 in Section 8.]]
- Remove the EGR cooler coolant return line. [[35-011-031-tr — EGR Cooler Coolant Lines|Refer to Procedure 011-031 in Section 11.]]
- Remove the water pump. [[35-008-062-tr — Water Pump|Refer to Procedure 008-062 in Section 8.]]

### Clean and Inspect for Reuse

With EGR

Use a wire brush and a gasket scraper to clean the water header plate and the cylinder block gasket surface.

![[01200148.png]]

### Install

With EGR

Install two guide pins, Part Number 3376488, in the top row of the water header plate mounting capscrew holes.

Install the water header plate and capscrews. Use a new gasket

Remove the two guide pins and install the other two capscrews.

The capscrews are **not** tightened to their final value until all related components are installed.

Install the coolant heater housing (3). [[35-008-012-tr — Coolant Heater Housing|Refer to Procedure 008-012 in Section 8.]]

Install the coolant filter head and filter (2). [[35-008-007-tr — Coolant Filter Head|Refer to Procedure 008-007 in Section 8.]]

Install the coolant thermostat housing support and gasket (6). [[35-008-015-tr — Coolant Thermostat Housing Support|Refer to Procedure 008-015 in Section 8.]]

![[01200150.png]]

Tighten all capscrews in the sequence shown.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 54 n•m [40 ft-lb]

![[01200152.png]]

Without EGR

Install two guide pins, Part Number 3376488, in the top row of the water header plate mounting capscrew holes.

Install the water header plate and capscrews. Use a new gasket.

Remove the two guide pins, and install the other two capscrews.

The capscrews are **not** tightened to their final value until all related components are installed.

### Finishing Steps

With EGR

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Do not remove the pressure cap from a hot engine. Wait until the coolant temperature is below 50°C \[120°F\] before removing the pressure cap. Heated coolant spray or steam can cause personal injury.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Coolant is toxic. Keep away from children and pets. If not reused, dispose of in accordance with local environmental regulations.

- Install the water pump. [[35-008-062-tr — Water Pump|Refer to Procedure 008-062 in Section 8.]]
- Install the EGR cooler coolant return line. [[35-011-031-tr — EGR Cooler Coolant Lines|Refer to Procedure 011-031 in Section 11.]]
- Install the coolant thermostat housing support and the coolant thermostat housing. [[35-008-015-tr — Coolant Thermostat Housing Support|Refer to Procedure 008-015 in Section 8.]]
- Install the turbocharger. [[35-010-033-tr — Turbocharger|Refer to Procedure 010-033 in Section 10.]]
- Install the coolant heater housing. [[35-008-012-tr — Coolant Heater Housing|Refer to Procedure 008-012 in Section 8.]]
- Install the coolant filter and filter head. [[35-008-007-tr — Coolant Filter Head|Refer to Procedure 008-007 in Section 8.]]
- Install the lubricating oil cooler. [[35-007-003-tr — Lubricating Oil Cooler|Refer to Procedure 007-003 in Section 7.]]
- Install the alternator. [[35-007-003-tr — Lubricating Oil Cooler|Refer to Procedure 007-003 in Section 7.]]
- Install the alternator belt. [[35-013-005-tr — Charging System Alternator Drive Belt|Refer to Procedure 013-005 in Section 13.]]
- Install the air intake piping.
- Fill the cooling system. [[35-013-001-tr — Charging System Alternator|Refer to Procedure 013-001 in Section 13.]]
- Operate the engine until it reaches a temperature of 80°C \[180°F\] and check for coolant leaks.
