### Install

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> The regulator spring must be compressed to install the retainer plug. Personal injury can result. Wear face and eye protection.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> A counterbore plunger and orifice is unique to engines with a viscosity sensor. Low oil pressure will result if a solid plunger is used with a viscosity sensor.

Install the regulator plunger (3) and regulator spring (2).

Install and tighten the retainer plug (1).

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> Rear Sump Oil Pan 75 n•m [55 ft-lb]

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> Front Sump Oil Pan 122 n•m [90 ft-lb]

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> Center Sump Oil Pan 75 n•m [55 ft-lb]
