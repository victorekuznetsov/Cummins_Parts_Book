### Remove

Three idler gear assemblies are used:

- Water pump idler gear (1)
- Camshaft idler gear (2)
- Hydraulic pump idler gear (3).

The water pump **must** be removed before the water pump idler gear (1) can be removed. [[35-008-062-tr — Water Pump|Refer to Procedure 008-062 in Section 8.]]

![[gh2gegb.png]]

To remove the water pump idler gear assembly, remove:

- The three capscrews
- Cover plate (4)
- Front thrust bearing (5)
- Idler gear (6)
- Rear thrust bearing (7)
- Idler gear shaft (8).

![[01200061.png]]

Mark or tag the individual pieces of the idler gear assembly and attach them together.
