### Install

Remove capscrew (1) from the gear cover.

Use the capscrew to install the coolant outlet clamp and tube to the engine.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 20 n•m [15 ft-lb]

Install a new rubber grommet on the air compressor tube, and install and tighten the tube connection to the air compressor fitting (2).

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 20 n•m [15 ft-lb]
