> ### Flow Diagram
>
> Automotive Applications with EGR
>
> 1. Intake air inlet to turbocharger
> 2. Turbocharger air to charge-air cooler
> 3. Charge-air cooler to intake manifold
> 4. Exhaust gas to EGR mixer
> 5. Air intake connection
> 6. Intake valve port
> 7. Intake valves.
>
> Automotive Applications Without EGR
>
> 1. Filtered intake air to turbocharger
> 2. Turbocharger air to charge-air cooler
> 3. Charge-air cooler intake air to intake manifold
> 4. Intake manifold
> 5. Intake valve ports
> 6. Intake valves.
>
> Marine Applications
>
> 1. Filtered intake air to turbocharger
> 2. Turbocharger air to aftercooler
> 3. Intake air through the aftercooler
> 4. Aftercooled air through intake manifold
> 5. Intake valve ports.
>
> Industrial and Generator Drive
>
> 1. Filtered intake air to turbocharger
> 2. Turbocharger air to charge air cooler
> 3. Charge air cooler intake air to intake manifold
> 4. Intake manifold
> 5. Intake valve ports
> 6. Intake valves.
>
> Marine Applications
>
> 1. Filtered intake air to turbocharger
> 2. Turbocharger air to charge air cooler
> 3. Charge air cooler intake air to intake manifold
> 4. Intake manifold
> 5. Intake valve ports
> 6. Intake valves.
