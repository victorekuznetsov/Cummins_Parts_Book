### General Information

The aftertreatment fuel pressure sensor is used to monitor the fuel pressure in the aftertreatment system.

The aftertreatment fuel pressure sensor is located on the aftertreatment fuel shutoff manifold.

![[19c01640.png]]

### Remove

Clean the area around the aftertreatment fuel pressure sensor.

Disconnect the pressure sensor connector from the engine harness.

Remove the aftertreatment fuel pressure sensor.

![[19c01641.png]]

### Install

Install a new aftertreatment fuel pressure sensor.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 14 n•m [124 in-lb]

Connect the engine harness to the aftertreatment fuel pressure sensor. An audible click will be heard when the connector locks in place.

Operate the engine and check for leaks.

Use INSITE™ electronic service tool to check for fault codes.

![[19c01641.png]]
