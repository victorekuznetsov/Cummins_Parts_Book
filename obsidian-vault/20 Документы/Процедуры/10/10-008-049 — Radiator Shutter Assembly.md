### General Information

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Shutters and fan clutch **must** be set to operate in the same temperature range as the thermostat with which they are used.

Check the shutters and the fan clutch every 1500 hours.

Every 192,000 km \[120,000 mi\], 3000 hours, or 2 years, whichever comes first, check the shutters and the fan clutch.

Check the shutters in the closed position to be sure they are completely closed.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> If the shutters are **not** closed, refer to the manufacturer's instructions.

Be sure the shutters open completely at the desired temperature setting.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> If the shutters are closed, refer to the manufacturer's instructions.
