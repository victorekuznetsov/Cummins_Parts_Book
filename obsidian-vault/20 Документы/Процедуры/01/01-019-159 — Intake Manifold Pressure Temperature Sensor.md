# Intake Manifold Pressure/Temperature Sensor
**Датчик давления и температуры впускного коллектора**

> **Двигатели:** [[37292556 — QST30 CM552 CPL 1244|37292556]], [[37295879 — QST30 CM552 CPL 2139|37295879]]
> **Семейство:** QST30
> **Входит в руководства:** [[4021419 — QSX15, QSK23, QSK45, QSK60, QSK78, and QST30 Power Generation Electronic Control Syst|4021419]]
> **Секции:** Section 19 - Electronic Engine Controls - Group 19
> **Даты:** изменён 2002-12-05

### Remove

Lift up on the locking tab and pull the electrical connectors apart.

![[19900789.png]]

Remove the sensor from the engine.

![[17c00051.png]]

### Install

Make sure the new sensor has an o-ring.

Install the new sensor into the engine. Tighten the sensor.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 14 n•m [124 in-lb]

![[17c00051.png]]

Push the connectors together until they lock. A click will be heard as the connector locks in place.

![[19900789.png]]
