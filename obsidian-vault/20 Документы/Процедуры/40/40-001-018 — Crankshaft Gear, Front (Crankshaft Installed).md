### General Information

The crankshaft gear can **not** be removed from the crankshaft with the crankshaft installed. Refer to [[40-001-016-tr — Crankshaft|Procedure 001-016]] to remove the crankshaft and [[40-001-019 — Crankshaft Gear, Front (Crankshaft Removed)|Procedure 001-019]] to remove the crankshaft gear.
