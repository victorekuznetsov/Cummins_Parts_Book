### Preparatory Steps

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Batteries can emit explosive gases. To reduce the possibility of personal injury, always ventilate the compartment before servicing the batteries. To reduce the possibility of arcing, remove the negative (-) battery cable first and attach the negative (-) battery cable last.

- Disconnect the batteries.

![[13900050.png]]

- Remove the drive belt. Refer to [[40-008-002-tr — Drive Belt, Cooling Fan|Procedure 008-002]]
- Remove the vibration damper/crankshaft pulley. If equipped with a viscous damper refer to [[40-001-052-tr — Vibration Damper, Viscous|Procedure 001-052]]. If equipped with a rubber damper refer to [[40-001-051-tr — Vibration Damper, Rubber|Procedure 001-051]]. If equipped with crankshaft pulley only, refer to [[40-001-022-tr — Crankshaft Pulley|Procedure 001-022]]
- Remove the front gear cover. Refer to [[40-001-031-tr — Gear Cover, Front|Procedure 001-031]].

![[ck800wa.png]]

### Initial Check

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The oil pump gear backlash limits for rear gear train engines and front gear train engines are different. Rear gear train engine use a lubricating oil pump with straight cut gears and front gear train engines use a lubricating oil pump with helical cut gears.

Use gauge, Part Number 3824564, and magnetic base, Part Number 3377399, measure the lubricating pump driven gear backlash.

| mm |  | in |
|---|---|---|
| 0.170 | MIN | 0.007 |
| 0.300 | MAX | 0.012 |

| mm |  | in |
|---|---|---|
| 0.076 | MIN | 0.003 |
| 0.330 | MAX | 0.013 |

If the backlash is out of specification, the lubricating oil pump **must** be replaced.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> If the adjoining gear moves when you measure the backlash, the reading will be incorrect.

![[lp9geia.png]]

### Remove

Remove the four mounting capscrews (1, 2, 3, and 4).

Remove the lubricating oil pump from the bore in the cylinder block.

![[lp900wc.png]]

### Clean and Inspect for Reuse

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> When using solvents, acids, or alkaline materials for cleaning, follow the manufacturer's recommendations for use. Wear goggles and protective clothing to reduce the possibility of personal injury.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Some solvents are flammable and toxic. Read the manufacturer's instructions before using.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Wear appropriate eye and face protection when using compressed air. Flying debris and dirt can cause personal injury.

Clean the lubricating oil pump with solvent.

Dry with compressed air.

![[07d00208.png]]

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> When using solvents, acids, or alkaline materials for cleaning, follow the manufacturer's recommendations for use. Wear goggles and protective clothing to reduce the possibility of personal injury.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Some solvents are flammable and toxic. Read the manufacturer's instructions before using.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Wear appropriate eye and face protection when using compressed air. Flying debris and dirt can cause personal injury.

With the pump removed use solvents to clean the cylinder block cavity.

Dry with compressed air.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Do **not** allow solvents to enter the lubricating oil system when cleaning the cylinder block cavity.

![[07d00201.png]]

Inspect the lubricating oil pump gears for chips, cracks, or excessive wear.

![[lp900sa.png]]

If debris has been expected to have gone through the lubricating oil pump, the rear cover plate can be removed to inspect for internal damage.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The lubricating oil pump is serviced/replaced as an assembly. No internal parts are available for rebuild/replacement.

![[lp9plmb.png]]

Inspect the rear cover plate for scoring or grooves. If the cover is scored or grooved too badly, the lubricating oil pump **must** be replaced.

![[07d00202.png]]

Inspect the lubricating oil pump housing and gerotor drive for damage and excessive wear.

If the lubricating oil pump is damaged, the lubricating oil pump **must** be replaced.

![[lp9hssb.png]]

### Measure

Measure the tip clearance.

| mm |  | in |
|---|---|---|
| 0.178 | MAX | 0.007 |

![[07d00209.png]]

Measure the clearance of the gerotor drive/gerotor planetary to port plate.

| mm |  | in |
|---|---|---|
| 0.127 | MAX | 0.005 |

![[07d00210.png]]

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The oil pump gear backlash limits for rear gear train engines and front gear train engines are different. Rear gear train engine use a lubricating oil pump with straight cut gears and front gear train engines use a lubricating oil pump with helical cut gears.

Measure the gear backlash.

| mm |  | in |
|---|---|---|
| 0.170 | MIN | 0.007 |
| 0.300 | MAX | 0.012 |

| mm |  | in |
|---|---|---|
| 0.076 | MIN | 0.003 |
| 0.330 | MAX | 0.013 |

![[lp900nd.png]]

### Install

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Failure to fill the pump with oil during installation can result in a slow prime at initial engine start-up, resulting in severe engine damage.

Lubricate the lubricating oil pump with clean 15W-40 engine oil.

![[lp9hswa.png]]

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> To reduce the possibility of engine damage, make sure the idler gear pin is installed in the locating bore in the cylinder block.

Install the lubricating oil pump.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> If installing a new lubricating oil pump ensure that the pump is correct for your engine. For example, lubricating oil pumps for 4 and 6 cylinder engines are physically interchangeable, but have different flow characteristics.

![[lp900hf.png]]

Using the sequence shown, torque the capscrews in the following sequence:

Initial Torque

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 8 n•m [70 in-lb]

Final Torque

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 24 n•m [212 in-lb]

![[lp900wc.png]]

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The back plate on the pump seats against the bottom of the bore in the cylinder block. When the lubricating oil pump is correctly installed, the flange on the lubricating oil pump will **not** touch the cylinder block.

![[lp900wd.png]]

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Be sure to check if gear backlash is correct when installing a new lubricating oil pump.

Use gauge, Part Number 3824564, and magnetic base, Part Number 3377399, measure the lubricating pump driven gear backlash.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The oil pump driven gear backlash limits for rear gear train engines and front gear train engines are different. Rear gear train engine use a lubricating oil pump with straight cut gears and front gear train engines use a lubricating oil pump with helical cut gears.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> If the adjoining gear moves when you measure the backlash, the reading will be incorrect.

|  | mm |  | in |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rear Gear Train | 0.300 | MIN | 0.011 |
|  | 0.500 | MAX | 0.019 |
| Front Gear Train | 0.076 | MIN | 0.003 |
|  | 0.330 | MAX | 0.013 |

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> If you are reinstalling a used pump and have already measured the backlash you do **not** need to complete this step.

![[lp9geia.png]]

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Be sure to check if gear backlash is correct when installing a new lubricating oil pump.

Use gauge, Part Number 3824564, and magnetic base, Part Number 3377399, measure the lubricating pump idler gear backlash.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The oil pump idler gear backlash limits for rear gear train engines and front gear train engines are different. Rear gear train engine use a lubricating oil pump with straight cut gears and front gear train engines use a lubricating oil pump with helical cut gears.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> If the adjoining gear moves when you measure the backlash, the reading will be incorrect.

|  | mm |  | in |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rear Gear Train | 0.150 | MIN | 0.005 |
|  | 0.250 | MAX | 0.009 |
| Front Gear Train | 0.076 | MIN | 0.003 |
|  | 0.330 | MAX | 0.013 |

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> If you are reinstalling a used pump and have already measured the backlash you do **not** need to complete this step.

### Finishing Steps

- Install the front gear cover. Refer to [[40-001-031-tr — Gear Cover, Front|Procedure 001-031]]
- Install the vibration damper/crankshaft pulley. If equipped with a viscous damper, refer to [[40-001-052-tr — Vibration Damper, Viscous|Procedure 001-052]]. If equipped with a rubber damper, refer to [[40-001-051-tr — Vibration Damper, Rubber|Procedure 001-051]]. If equipped with a crankshaft pulley only, refer to [[40-001-022-tr — Crankshaft Pulley|Procedure 001-022]]
- Install the belt. Refer to [[40-008-002-tr — Drive Belt, Cooling Fan|Procedure 008-002]].

![[ck800wa.png]]

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Batteries can emit explosive gases. To reduce the possibility of personal injury, always ventilate the compartment before servicing the batteries. To reduce the possibility of arcing, remove the negative (-) battery cable first and attach the negative (-) battery cable last.

- Connect batteries.

![[13900050.png]]

- Operate the engine and check for leaks.
