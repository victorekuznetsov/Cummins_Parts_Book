### Remove

Rear Gear Train

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Check the gasket while it is installed in the rocker housing. Once the gasket is removed it **must** be replaced.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Check for cracks in the rocker housing bridge area before removing the mounting capscrews.

Remove the rocker housing, capscrews, and gasket.
