### Measure

Measure the boost pressure at the intake manifold by using one of the tapped or plugged intake access holes shown in the illustration. Refer to the specifications in [[40-018-019-tr — Air Intake System|Procedure 018-019]] (Air Intake System in Section V) for the engine being worked on. If the specifications for the engine being worked on are **not** listed, see the engine data sheet.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> If the engine has charge-air-cooling, testing **must** be done to make certain that the charge-air-cooler system is **not** leaking or restricting the turbocharger boost pressure. Refer to [[40-010-027-tr — Charge-Air Cooler|Procedure 010-027]] for charge-air-cooler testing.

![[tb900kb.png]]

Exhaust leaks can usually be detected audibly or visually by a discoloration caused by the escaping hot gases.

Do **not** overlook exhaust restriction as a cause of low power. If the exhaust gases can **not** flow freely, the turbocharger will **not** operate efficiently.
