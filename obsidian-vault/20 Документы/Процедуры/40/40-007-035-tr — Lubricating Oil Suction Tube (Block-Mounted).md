# Lubricating Oil Suction Tube (Block-Mounted)
**Маслозаборная трубка (на блоке)**

> **Даты:** изменён 2006-03-31

### General Information

A front sump (1), rear sump (2), or center sump (3) lubricating oil pan option can be used depending on the application. The type and mounting of the lubricating oil suction tube will vary with the lubricating oil pan used.

![[07900222.png]]

### Preparatory Steps

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Batteries can emit explosive gases. To reduce the possibility of personal injury, always ventilate the compartment before servicing the batteries. To reduce the possibility of arcing, remove the negative (-) battery cable first and attach the negative (-) battery cable last.

- Disconnect the batteries.

![[13900050.png]]

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> To reduce the possibility of personal injury, avoid direct contact of hot oil with your skin.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Some state and federal agencies have determined that used engine oil can be carcinogenic and cause reproductive toxicity. Avoid inhalation of vapors, ingestion, and prolonged contact with used engine oil. If not reused, dispose of in accordance with local environmental regulations.

- Drain the lubricating oil. Refer to [[40-007-037 — Lubricating Oil System|Procedure 007-037]]
- Remove the lubricating oil pan and gasket. Refer to [[40-007-025-tr — Lubricating Oil Pan|Procedure 007-025]].

![[ck800wa.png]]

### Remove

Remove the suction tube mounting capscrews.

Remove the suction tube.

![[07900114.png]]

### Clean and Inspect for Reuse

Using a putty knife, clean the gasket surfaces.

Clean the suction tube surface with hot, soapy water.

![[op9tbeb.png]]

Inspect the suction tube for cracks.

Replace the suction tube if any damage is found.

![[op9tbsa.png]]

### Install

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> The suction tube gasket is not symmetrical. Failure to install it properly can result in low oil pressure and engine damage.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> For front gear train engines with a bedplate, the bedplate **must** be installed before the suction tube is installed. Also for front gear train engines, the bedplate **must** be installed in sequence with the oil pan. Refer to [[40-007-025-tr — Lubricating Oil Pan|Procedure 007-025]].

Install the lubricating oil suction tube gasket and suction tube.

Install and tighten the suction tube mounting capscrews.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 24 n•m [212 in-lb]

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Take care to ensure the oil suction tube gasket openings align with both the oil suction tube and the block.

![[op9tbhd.png]]

### Finishing Steps

- Install oil pan and gasket. Refer to [[40-007-025-tr — Lubricating Oil Pan|Procedure 007-025]]
- Fill the engine with lubricating oil. Refer to [[40-007-037 — Lubricating Oil System|Procedure 007-037]].

![[ck800wa.png]]

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Batteries can emit explosive gases. To reduce the possibility of personal injury, always ventilate the compartment before servicing the batteries. To reduce the possibility of arcing, remove the negative (-) battery cable first and attach the negative (-) battery cable last.

- Connect the batteries.

![[13900050.png]]

- Operate the engine and check for leaks
- Stop the engine, and check the lubricating oil level with the dipstick.

![[00d00049.png]]
