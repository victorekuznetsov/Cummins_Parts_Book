### General Information

Front Gear Train

Piston features include high-swirl combustion bowl cast-aluminum body and three-ring grooves. The piston for turbocharged, turbocharged/aftercooled engines includes a Ni-resist insert with a keystone profile for the top piston ring. **Always** check the part number to be sure the correct configuration is used during piston replacement.

In addition to the Ni-resist insert, automotive turbocharged engines also feature pistons with a hard-anodized combustion surface.

![[pi900ga.png]]

The piston ring sets are also different. While both sets consist of three rings, the top ring of the turbocharged/aftercooled set has a keystone profile that operates in a Ni-resist insert cast into the piston. The naturally aspirated top ring is square cut and operates in a groove machined into the aluminum piston.

![[pi9riga.png]]

The 1994 automotive pistons utilize different top ring locations. The 160-hp to 175-hp ratings use 8 mm crown to ring land pistons and 190 hp to 210 hp use 10 mm. The 1994 industrial pistons continue with the 14 mm ring position.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> **Not** all industrial two-valve pistons have a 6 mm top ring and a single Ni-resist insert, beginning in 2003.

![[pi9rigf.png]]

Rear Gear Train

The piston features:

- A centered combustion bowl for more efficient combustion
- An offset piston pin for reduced combustion noise
- An internal oil gallery for piston cooling. Oil is sprayed at the underside of the piston by directed J-jet piston cooling nozzles. When the piston is near BDC, oil is sprayed through the internal oil gallery for maximum piston cooling.

![[01d00341.png]]

The piston rings set consists of three rings:

1. The Top Piston Ring — which has a keyway profile that operates in a Ni-resist insert cast into the piston
2. The Intermediate Ring (second ring)
3. The Oil Control Ring.

### Clean and Inspect for Reuse

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Do not use the bead-blast method to clean the pistons. The pistons will be damaged by blast material embedded in the aluminum.

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Do not clean the pistons in an acid tank. Damage to the piston can occur.

Soak the pistons in cold parts cleaner.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Soaking the pistons overnight will usually loosen the carbon deposits.

![[pi900ec.png]]

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Do not clean the pistons in an acid tank. Damage to the pistons can occur.

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Do not use a metal brush. A metal brush will damage the piston ring grooves.

Wash the pistons in a strong solution of laundry detergent and hot water.

![[01900121.png]]

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Do not use a ring groove cleaner and make sure not to scratch the ring sealing surface in the piston groove.

Clean the remaining deposits from the ring grooves with the square end of a broken ring.

![[01900122.png]]

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> When using solvents, acids, or alkaline materials for cleaning, follow the manufacturer's recommendations for use. Wear goggles and protective clothing to reduce the possibility of personal injury.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Wear appropriate eye and face protection when using compressed air. Flying debris and dirt can cause personal injury.

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Do not clean the pistons and connecting rods in an acid tank. Damage to the pistons and connecting rods can occur.

Wash the pistons again in a detergent solution or solvent.

Rinse the pistons in clean, hot water.

Dry the pistons with compressed air.

![[01900121.png]]

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> B4.5 RGT engines have an oil passage cast into the top of the piston for cooling purposes. When cleaning the pistons, make sure the oil passage is clean and free of debris.

![[01d00341.png]]
