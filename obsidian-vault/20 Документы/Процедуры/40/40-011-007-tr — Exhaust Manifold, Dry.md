# Exhaust Manifold, Dry

### General Information

Inspect for exhaust leaks at the exhaust manifold, turbocharger (if equipped), mounting gaskets, or exhaust pipe, muffler or catalyst restrictions. Refer to [[40-010-024-tr — Air Leaks, Air Intake and Exhaust Systems|Procedure 010-024]] and [[40-010-019-tr — Air Crossover|Procedure 010-019]].

For turbocharged engines, leaks or restrictions will cause turbine and impeller to operate at a lower speed and reduce the amount of air being forced into the cylinder. Again, the symptom will be excessive smoke, low manifold pressure, and low power.

### Preparatory Steps

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Batteries can emit explosive gases. To reduce the possibility of personal injury, always ventilate the compartment before servicing the batteries. To reduce the possibility of arcing, remove the negative (-) battery cable first and attach the negative (-) battery cable last.

- Disconnect the batteries.

![[13900050.png]]

- If equipped, remove the air crossover. Refer to [[40-010-019-tr — Air Crossover|Procedure 010-019]]
- If equipped, disconnect the charge air cooler piping. Refer to the OEM service manual
- Disconnect the exhaust piping. Refer to the OEM service manual
- For turbocharged engines, disconnect the air intake piping. Refer to the OEM service manual
- If equipped, remove the turbocharger. Refer to [[40-010-033-tr — Turbocharger|Procedure 010-033]].

![[ck800wa.png]]

### Remove

For 6 cylinder engines, remove the exhaust manifold mounting capscrews and spacers.

Remove the exhaust manifold and gaskets.

Discard the exhaust manifold gaskets.

![[xm9gkma.png]]

For 4 cylinder engines, remove the exhaust manifold mounting capscrews and spacers.

Remove the exhaust manifold and gaskets.

Discard the exhaust manifold gaskets.

![[02d00065.png]]

### Clean and Inspect for Reuse

Clean the sealing surfaces of the head.

Clean the sealing surfaces of the exhaust manifold.

Use 240-grit inert cloth to remove carbon deposits from the sealing surface.

![[xm9bdec.png]]

Inspect the parts for cracks, burnout, or damage.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> If the exhaust manifold is damaged, for turbocharged engines, check the charge air cooler. A charge air cooler failure can cause progressive damage to the exhaust manifold. Refer to [[40-010-027-tr — Charge-Air Cooler|Procedure 010-027]].

Inspect the gasket surfaces for gouges, scratches, or burnout.

![[11900007.png]]

### Install

Coat all capscrew threads with an unleaded anti-seize compound.

![[xm9xsaa.png]]

For 6 cylinder engines, install the exhaust manifold, new gaskets, spacers and mounting capscrews.

Follow the tightening sequence shown in the illustration.

Tighten the exhaust manifold mounting capscrews.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 43 n•m [32 ft-lb]

![[xm9gkhc.png]]

For 4 cylinder engines, install the exhaust manifold, new gaskets, spacers and mounting capscrews.

Follow the tightening sequence shown in the illustration.

Tighten the exhaust manifold mounting capscrews.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 43 n•m [32 ft-lb]

![[11900078.png]]

### Finishing Steps

- If equipped, install the turbocharger. Refer to [[40-010-033-tr — Turbocharger|Procedure 010-033]].
- Connect the exhaust piping. Refer to the OEM service manual
- For turbocharged engines, connect the air intake piping. Refer to the OEM service manual
- If equipped, install the air crossover. Refer to [[40-010-019-tr — Air Crossover|Procedure 010-019]]
- If equipped, connect the charge air cooler piping. Refer to the OEM service manual.

![[ck800wa.png]]

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Batteries can emit explosive gases. To reduce the possibility of personal injury, always ventilate the compartment before servicing the batteries. To reduce the possibility of arcing, remove the negative (-) battery cable first and attach the negative (-) battery cable last.

- Connect the batteries
- Operate the engine and check for leaks.
