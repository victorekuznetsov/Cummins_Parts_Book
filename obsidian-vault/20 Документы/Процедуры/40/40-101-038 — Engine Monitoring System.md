### General Information

The annunciator panel is equipped with warning lamps and an alarm horn that will go off when an engine fault is detected.

![[15900034.png]]

Low Engine Oil Pressure

The low oil pressure alarm is preset to activate when the oil pressure drops below minimum specifications. In this event, the warning lamp will illuminate and the horn will sound to alert the operator. Refer to Procedure [[40-018-017-om-mar — Lubricating Oil System|018-017]] (Lubricating Oil System) in Section V for oil pressure specifications.

![[15900036.png]]

High Coolant Temperature

The high coolant temperature warning lamp will illuminate and the horn will sound to alert the operator when the coolant temperature is above specifications. Refer to Procedure [[40-018-018-om-mar — Cooling System|018-018]] (Cooling System) in Section V for cooling system specifications.

![[15900037.png]]

Low Battery Voltage

The low battery voltage warning lamp will illuminate and the horn will sound to alert the operator when the system voltage drops below specifications. Refer to Procedure [[40-018-021-om-ind — Electrical System|018-021]] (Electrical System) in Section V for electrical specifications.

![[15900038.png]]

Water in Fuel

The water-in-fuel lamp interfaces with the optional water-in-fuel sensor in the primary fuel filter. It comes on when there is water in the fuel filter. This feature is **not** presently available.

![[15900039.png]]

Audible Alarm/Alarm Silence

The alarm reset button silences the alarm horn and extinguishes the alarm light(s) when pressed. If the fault condition causing the alarm is not corrected, the horn and lights will reactivate after 1.5 to 2 minutes.
