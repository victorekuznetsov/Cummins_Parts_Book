### Install

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Some flexplates require mounting plates and/or clamp rings. It may be necessary to install any mounting plates and/or clamp rings prior to or with the flexplate as noted during removal.

Install the flexplate capscrews, flexplate, and tighten.
