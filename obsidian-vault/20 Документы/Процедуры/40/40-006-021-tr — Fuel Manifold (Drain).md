### General Information

The fuel system is designed to use fuel to cool and lubricate the injection pump and injectors. Fuel is continually vented from the injection pump, and a small amount of fuel leaks near the injector needle valve during injection. This fuel is returned to the supply tank by the fuel drain manifold.

![[fs901gz.png]]

Restriction of the fuel drain manifold can affect the Lucas CAV DPA/DPS fuel injection pump metering controls and the operation of the injectors. Restricting the fuel drain manifold raises the case pressure of the fuel injection pump, which can prevent injection.

If the engine will restart after a waiting period following an unexplained shutdown, look for any restriction of the manifold drain line.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Restriction can cause the fuel temperature to rise, reducing power from the engine.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Batteries can emit explosive gases. To reduce the possibility of personal injury, always ventilate the compartment before servicing the batteries. To reduce the possibility of arcing, remove the negative (-) battery cable first and attach the negative (-) battery cable last.

- Disconnect the batteries.

![[13900050.png]]

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> When using solvents, acids, or alkaline materials for cleaning, follow the manufacturer's recommendations for use. Wear goggles and protective clothing to reduce the possibility of personal injury.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Wear appropriate eye and face protection when using compressed air. Flying debris and dirt can cause personal injury.

- Clean any debris.

![[fs9ftec.png]]

### Remove

Distributor-Type Pump

Remove the capscrews from the hold-down clamps, and complete the following steps:

![[ft9csmb.png]]

Remove the banjo fitting capscrews and washers.

![[ft9wama.png]]

Disconnect the fuel drain line fittings.

![[fp9ftma.png]]

In-Line Pump

Remove the banjo capscrews and copper sealing washers at the fuel filter head.

Remove the fuel line support bracket capscrew from the intake manifold.

![[ft9wamc.png]]

Remove the banjo capscrews and copper sealing washers from the injectors.

![[ft9wama.png]]

### Clean and Inspect for Reuse

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> When using solvents, acids, or alkaline materials for cleaning, follow the manufacturer's recommendations for use. Wear goggles and protective clothing to reduce the possibility of personal injury.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Wear appropriate eye and face protection when using compressed air. Flying debris and dirt can cause bodily injury.

Wash the low-pressure fuel lines in solvent.

Dry the fuel lines with compressed air.

![[ft9tbeb.png]]

Inspect the fuel drain manifold for cracks and other damage.

![[ft9tbsc.png]]

Inspect the fuel drain manifold banjo connections.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The banjo connection seals **must** be discarded when removed. The seals **must not** be used again.

![[ft9tbsd.png]]

### Install

Distributor-Type Pump

Assemble the fuel drain line and fuel drain manifold in the reverse order of disassembly.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Use new seals for the fittings.

![[ft9tbhg.png]]

The installation torque for the banjo fitting screw is as follows:

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 9 n•m [80 in-lb]

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Use new sealing washers for the fuel drain manifold.

![[ft9waha.png]]

Install the clamp capscrews.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 24 n•m [18 ft-lb]

![[fs9csma.png]]

Bosch® In-Line pump

Install the fuel drain manifold in the reverse order of removal.

| Injector Banjo | 9 n.m | \[80 in-lb\] |
|---|---|---|

| Fuel Filter Head | 13 n.m | \[115 in-lb\] |
|---|---|---|

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The fuel line support bracket capscrew in the intake manifold requires liquid Teflon™ sealant.

![[ft9wama.png]]
