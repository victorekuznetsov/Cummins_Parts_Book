# Crankcase Breather Tube
**Трубка сапуна картера**

> [!abstract] Процедура · `40-003-018-tr`
> **Двигатели:** [[93047320 — 6B5.9 CPL 3111|93047320]]
> **Семейство:** 6B5.9
> **Входит в руководства:** [[3666087 — B3.9, B4.5, B4.5 RGT, and B5.9 Service Manual|3666087]]
> **Секции:** Section 3 - Rocker Levers - Group 03
> **Даты:** изменён 2015-11-04
> **Источник:** [QuickServe](https://quickserve.cummins.com/qs3/pubsys2/xml/en/procedures/40/40-003-018-tr.html) · [PDF-оригинал](https://github.com/victorekuznetsov/Cummins_Parts_Book/raw/main/bulletins/procedures/40-003-018-tr.pdf)

### Remove

Front Gear Train

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Some engines have the crankcase breather located in the front gear cover. The procedure is the same.

Remove the crankcase breather tube clamp.

Remove the crankcase breather tube from the tappet cover located on side of the engine block.

![[03900081.png]]

Rear Gear Train

Disconnect the breather tube at the back of the rocker lever cover.

Two capscrews directly mount the breather tube to the rocker lever cover. Remove the capscrews to disconnect the breather tube from the rocker lever cover.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Some breather tubes use internal Torx capscrews to secure the breather tube to the rocker lever cover.

![[03900112.png]]

Remove the breather tube from the rear gear housing.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> There is an o-ring sealed connection to the rear gear housing. Remove the breather tube by pulling straight up.

![[03900113.png]]

### Clean and Inspect for Reuse

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Wear appropriate eye and face protection when using compressed air. Flying debris and dirt can cause bodily injury.

Inspect the crankcase breather tube internally for obstructions or sludge buildup.

If the tube is blocked, the crankcase breather tube **must** be cleaned with a strong solution of detergent or replaced to prevent excess crankcase pressure buildup.

Dry crankcase breather and tube with compressed air.

![[03d00017.png]]

Check the crankcase breather tube for cracks or other debris, which could obstruct the tube.

![[03900083.png]]

For rear gear train engines, inspect the o-ring seals on the breather tube.

Make sure to inspect the o-ring for cracks, tears, or brittleness. If any damage is found, replace the o-ring.

![[03900114.png]]

### Install

Front Gear Train

Connect the crankcase breather tube to the tappet cover on the side of the cylinder block.

Install the crankcase breather tube clamp.

![[03900081.png]]

Rear Gear Train

Prior to installing the breather tube, apply engine oil to the o-rings located on the breather tube.

![[03d00159.png]]

Install the breather tube into the rear gear housing.

![[03900113.png]]

Connect the breather tube connection to the rocker lever cover.

Two capscrews directly mount the breather tube to the rocker lever cover. Install the capscrews to connect the breather tube connection to the rocker lever cover.

Tighten the capscrews.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 10 n•m [89 in-lb]

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Some breather tube connections use internal Torx capscrews to secure the breather tube to the rocker lever cover.

![[03900112.png]]
