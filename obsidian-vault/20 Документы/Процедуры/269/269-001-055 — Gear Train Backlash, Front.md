### Measure

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> It is important that the adjacent mating gear does **not** turn during the measurement.

Rotate the gear being measured (1) **clockwise** to remove any backlash with the mating gear (2).

Position a dial indicator so the tip is contacting the surface of the gear tooth, as shown in the illustration. Do **not** allow the mating gear (2) to turn.

Position the indicator to "0".

Rotate the gear (1) **counterclockwise** and read the indicator.

Measure the backlash at the following locations:

- Front camshaft gear to front camshaft idler gear
- Rear camshaft gear to rear camshaft idler gear
- Crankshaft gear (rear) to rear camshaft idler gear
- Lubricating oil pump idler gear to crankshaft gear
- Fuel pump gear to rear camshaft gear.
