# Sea Water Pressure Sensor
**Датчик давления забортной воды**

> [!abstract] Процедура · `116-019-452`
> **Двигатели:** [[41349633 — QSK19 CM2150 MCRS CPL 3666|41349633]], [[41353297 — QSK19 CM2150 MCRS CPL 3666|41353297]]
> **Семейство:** QSK19
> **Входит в руководства:** [[4021617 — C Command Elite and C Command Elite Plus Panel System Marine Master Repair Manual|4021617]]
> **Секции:** Section 19 - Electronic Engine Controls
> **Даты:** изменён 2007-04-16
> **Источник:** [QuickServe](https://quickserve.cummins.com/qs3/pubsys2/xml/en/procedures/116/116-019-452.html) · [PDF-оригинал](https://github.com/victorekuznetsov/Cummins_Parts_Book/raw/main/bulletins/procedures/116-019-452.pdf)

### Remove

Loosen the sea water pressure sensor connector screw.

Disconnect the sea water pressure sensor connector.

![[19601013.png]]

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> The sea water pressure sensor is threaded into a check valve. If the sensor is changed while the engine is running, the check valve must remain in place. To reduce the possibility of personal injury and engine damage, do not remove the sea water pressure sensor check valve when the engine is running.

Use a wrench to turn the sea water pressure sensor **counterclockwise**.

Remove the sea water pressure sensor from the check valve.

![[19601014.png]]

### Install

Make sure the o-ring is **not** damaged.

Thread the sea water pressure sensor **clockwise** into the check valve on the sea water tube.

Tighten the sea water pressure sensor.

![[19601014.png]]

Connect the sea water pressure sensor connector.

If the connector does **not** align, refer to Engine Wiring Harness, Procedure [[116-019-043 — Engine Wiring Harness|019-043]].

Tighten the sea water pressure sensor connector screw.

![[19601013.png]]
