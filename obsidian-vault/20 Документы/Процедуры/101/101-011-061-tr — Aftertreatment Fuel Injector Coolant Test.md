# Aftertreatment Fuel Injector Coolant Test

### Preparatory Steps

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Coolant is toxic. Keep away from children and pets. If not reused, dispose of in accordance with local environmental regulations.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Do not remove the pressure cap from a hot engine. Wait until the coolant temperature is below 50°C \[120°F\] before removing the pressure cap. Heated coolant spray or steam can cause personal injury.

- Drain the cooling system. Use the following procedure in the Signature™, ISX, and QSX15 Service Manual, Bulletin [[3666239 — Signature™, ISX, and QSX15 Service Manual\|3666239]]. [[10-008-018-tr — Cooling System|Refer to Procedure 008-018 in Section 8.]] Use the following procedure in the ISM, ISMe, and QSM11 Service Manual, Bulletin [[3666322 — ISM, ISMe, and QSM11 Service Manual\|3666322]]. [[35-008-018-tr — Cooling System|Refer to Procedure 008-018 in Section 8.]]
- Disconnect the fuel line to the aftertreatment fuel injector. [[101-011-051-tr — Aftertreatment Injector Lines|Refer to Procedure 011-051 in Section 11.]]
- Disconnect the coolant lines to the aftertreatment fuel injector. [[101-011-051-tr — Aftertreatment Injector Lines|Refer to Procedure 011-051 in Section 11.]]
- Remove the aftertreatment fuel injector from the exhaust adapter pipe. [[101-011-042-tr — Aftertreatment Fuel Injector|Refer to Procedure 011-042 in Section 11.]]

![[ck800wa.png]]

### Setup

Plug one of the coolant connections with an M16 x 1.5 plug.

![[11c00365.png]]

Fill the nozzle area with water.

![[11c00366.png]]

### Test

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Wear appropriate eye and face protection when using compressed air. Flying debris and dirt can cause personal injury.

Apply compressed air regulated at 241 kpa \[35 psi\] to the opposite coolant connection.

If air bubbles are seen in the nozzle area, the aftertreatment injector has a crack that is allowing coolant to leak through.

The injector **must** be replaced.

![[11c00367.png]]

### Finishing Steps

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Coolant is toxic. Keep away from children and pets. If not reused, dispose of in accordance with local environmental regulations.

- Remove the plug and compressed air connection from the coolant connection points.
- Install a new fibrous insulator.
- Install a new metal gasket.
- Install the aftertreatment fuel injector assembly onto the adapter pipe. [[101-011-042-tr — Aftertreatment Fuel Injector|Refer to Procedure 011-042 in Section 11.]]
- Connect the fuel line to the aftertreatment fuel injector. [[101-011-051-tr — Aftertreatment Injector Lines|Refer to Procedure 011-051 in Section 11.]]
- Connect the coolant lines to the aftertreatment fuel injector. [[101-011-051-tr — Aftertreatment Injector Lines|Refer to Procedure 011-051 in Section 11.]]
- Connect the wiring harness to the aftertreatment injector.
- Fill the cooling system. Use the following procedure in the Signature™, ISX, and QSX15 Service Manual, Bulletin [[3666239 — Signature™, ISX, and QSX15 Service Manual\|3666239]]. [[10-008-018-tr — Cooling System|Refer to Procedure 008-018 in Section 8.]] Use the following procedure in the ISM, ISMe, and QSM11 Service Manual, Bulletin [[3666322 — ISM, ISMe, and QSM11 Service Manual\|3666322]]. [[35-008-018-tr — Cooling System|Refer to Procedure 008-018 in Section 8.]]
- Start the engine, check for leaks, and verify all fault codes are inactive.
