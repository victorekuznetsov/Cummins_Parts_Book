### Specifications

| Table 1: Remote Oil Level Switch Specifications |  |
|---|---|
| Item | Requirement |

| OIL DETECTED | Latched action: Terminals 1 and 2 closed |
| OIL NOT DETECTED | Latched action: Terminals 1 and 2 open |
| Contact resistance (closed) | \< 10 ohms |
| Contact resistance (open) | \> 50k ohms |
| Nominal current | 10 mA |
| Nominal voltage | 10 VDC |
| Contacts | Corrosion-resistant |
| Terminals | Soldered or corrosion-resistant |
