### Engine Dataplate

The engine dataplate provides the model identification and other important information about the engine.

Have the following engine data available when communicating with a Cummins Authorized Repair Location. The information on the dataplate is **mandatory** when sourcing service parts:

1. Engine serial number (ESN)
2. Control parts list (CPL)
3. Model
4. Advertised horsepower and rpm.

![[oi1plgc.png]]

For example, on CELECT™ Plus engines, the electronic control module (ECM) dataplate is located on top of the ECM.

The abbreviations in the accompanying illustration are explained as follows:

p/n = part number

s/n = serial number

d/c = date code.

The Centinel™ control module has a dataplate located on the front cover. The information contained on the dataplate is:

1. Serial number
2. Base module part number.

There are three different Centinel™ control module part numbers available for use on the Centinel™ system. They are:

1. High-Horsepower 12 VDC or 24 VDC, module part number
2. Heavy-Duty 12/24 VDC, module part number.

When ordering replacement parts, order by the part number that is specific to your system configuration.
