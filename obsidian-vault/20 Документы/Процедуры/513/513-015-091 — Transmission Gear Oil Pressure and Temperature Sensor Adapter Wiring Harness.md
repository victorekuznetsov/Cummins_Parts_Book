### General Information

The transmission gear oil pressure and temperature sensor adapter wiring harness connects the drive application wiring harness or original equipment manufacturer (OEM) sensor wiring harness to the transmission gear oil pressure and temperature sensors.

The primary purpose of this harness is to provide the length necessary to connect the components and provide the connection between the sensors and customer interface box (CIB).

Use the wiring diagram for details.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Not all vessels require the use of the harness. The harness may be supplied by the OEM, therefore the Cummins® service tools listed in this procedure may **not** fit. See equipment manufacturer service information.

### Preparatory Steps

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Batteries can emit explosive gases. To reduce the possibility of personal injury, always ventilate the compartment before servicing the batteries. To reduce the possibility of arcing, remove the negative (-) battery cable first and attach the negative (-) battery cable last.

- Disconnect the batteries. See equipment manufacturer service information.

### Remove

Disconnect the transmission gear oil cooler pressure temperature sensor adapter wiring harness from the sensors and drive application wiring harness or OEM sensor wiring harness.

If necessary for repair, remove the harness.

Note the harness routing and location of harness mounting points for installation.

![[15a00051.png]]

### Install

Connect the transmission gear oil cooler pressure temperature sensor adapter wiring harness to the sensors and drive application wiring harness or OEM sensor wiring harness.

Route and secure the harness to mounting points noted during removal.

![[15a00051.png]]

### Finishing Steps

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Batteries can emit explosive gases. To reduce the possibility of personal injury, always ventilate the compartment before servicing the batteries. To reduce the possibility of arcing, remove the negative (-) battery cable first and attach the negative (-) battery cable last.

- Connect the batteries. See equipment manufacturer service information.
- Perform system test to verify proper function. [[513-015-047 — Final Verification|Refer to Procedure 015-047 in Section 15.]]
