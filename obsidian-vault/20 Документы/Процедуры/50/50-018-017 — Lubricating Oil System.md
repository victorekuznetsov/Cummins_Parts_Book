### Specifications

#### Lubricating Oil Pressure, Main Oil Rifle (15W-40 oil at 107°C \[225°F\]):

| Minimum at rated rpm | 465 kPa \[67.5 psi\] |
|---|---|

| Maximum at rated rpm | 552 kPa \[80 psi\] |
|---|---|

| Minimum at idle rpm | 172 kPa \[25 psi\] |
|---|---|

|---|---|

| Maximum | 20 liters \[5.2 gal\] |
|---|---|
