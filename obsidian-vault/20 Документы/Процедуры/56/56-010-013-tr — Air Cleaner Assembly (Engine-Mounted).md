# Air Cleaner Assembly (Engine-Mounted)
**Воздухоочиститель в сборе (на двигателе)**

> [!abstract] Процедура · `56-010-013-tr`
> **Двигатели:** [[33210083 — QSK60 CM500 CPL 2699|33210083]], [[33219033 — QSK60 CM500 CPL 2848|33219033]], [[33224343 — QSK60 CM500 CPL 2849|33224343]]
> **Семейство:** QSK60
> **Входит в руководства:** [[4021530 — QSK45 and QSK60 Service Manual|4021530]]
> **Секции:** Section 10 - Air Intake System - Group 10
> **Даты:** изменён 2008-11-24
> **Источник:** [QuickServe](https://quickserve.cummins.com/qs3/pubsys2/xml/en/procedures/56/56-010-013-tr.html) · [PDF-оригинал](https://github.com/victorekuznetsov/Cummins_Parts_Book/raw/main/bulletins/procedures/56-010-013-tr.pdf)

### Preparatory Steps

- Remove the aftercooler heat shields. [[56-010-129-tr — Aftercooler Heat Shield|Refer to Procedure 010-129 (Aftercooler Heat Shield) in Section 10.]]

![[ck800wa.png]]

### Remove

- Loosen the two hose clamps securing the turbocharger inlet connection to the filter box.
- Remove the four capscrews, nuts, and isolators in the bottom of the air filter box.

![[10600354.png]]

- Loosen the two hose clamps securing the turbocharger inlet connection to the air cleaner housing.
- Remove the four capscrews, nuts, and isolators from the bottom of the air cleaner mounting brackets.

![[10600631.png]]

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> This component weighs 23 kg \[50 lb\] or more. To reduce the possibility of personal injury, use a hoist or get assistance to lift this component.

- Lift and remove the air filter box.
- The opposite bank is similar, repeat the previous steps to remove the air filter box.

![[10600355.png]]

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> This component weighs 23 kg \[50 lb\] or more. To reduce the possibility of personal injury, use a hoist or get assistance to lift this component.

- Lift and remove the air cleaner housing.
- The opposite bank is similar, repeat the previous steps to remove the other bank air cleaner housing.

![[10600633.png]]

If the air cleaner support structure is being removed, remove the following components:

- Remove the fuel filter head. [[56-006-017-tr — Fuel Filter Head|Refer to Procedure 006-017 (Fuel Filter Head) in Section 6.]]
- Remove the marine gear oil cooler. [[56-008-041-tr — Marine Gear Oil Cooler|Refer to Procedure 008-041 (Marine Gear Oil Cooler) in Section 8.]]

Remove the 16 capscrews securing the air cleaner support runners to the air cleaner support frame and remove the support runners.

![[10600356.png]]

Remove the capscrews securing the fuel line bracket to the cross beam.

![[10600357.png]]

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> This component weighs 23 kg \[50 lb\] or more. To reduce the possibility of personal injury, use a hoist or get assistance to lift this component.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> It may be necessary to loosen the four capscrews securing the air cleaner support frame to the flywheel housing.

Remove the eight capscrews securing the cross beam to the support frame and remove the cross beam.

![[10600358.png]]

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> This component weighs 23 kg \[50 lb\] or more. To reduce the possibility of personal injury, use a hoist or get assistance to lift this component.

Remove the four capscrews securing the support frame to the flywheel housing and remove the support frame.

The opposite side is similar, repeat this step to remove the other support frame.

![[10600359.png]]

### Install

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> This component weighs 23 kg \[50 lb\] or more. To reduce the possibility of personal injury, use a hoist or get assistance to lift this component.

Install and finger tighten the four capscrews securing the support frame to the flywheel housing. Capscrews will be tightened later in this procedure.

The opposite side is similar, repeat this step to install other support frame.

![[10600359.png]]

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> This component weighs 23 kg \[50 lb\] or more. To reduce the possibility of personal injury, use a hoist or get assistance to lift this component.

Install and finger tighten the eight capscrews securing the cross beam to the support frame. The capscrews will be tightened later in this procedure.

![[10600358.png]]

Install the capscrews securing the fuel line bracket to the cross frame.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 44 n•m [32 ft-lb]

![[10600357.png]]

Tighten the eight capscrews securing the cross beam to the support frames.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 81 n•m [60 ft-lb]

![[10600358.png]]

Tighten the four capscrews securing the support frame to the flywheel housing.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 190 n•m [140 ft-lb]

The opposite side is similar, repeat this step to install the other support frame.

![[10600359.png]]

If the air cleaner support structure is being installed, install the following components:

- Install the gear oil cooler. [[56-008-041-tr — Marine Gear Oil Cooler|Refer to Procedure 008-041 (Marine Gear Oil Cooler) in Section 8.]]
- Install the fuel filter head. [[56-006-017-tr — Fuel Filter Head|Refer to Procedure 006-017 (Fuel Filter Head) in Section 6.]]

Install the 16 capscrews securing the air cleaner support runners to the air cleaner support frames.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 44 n•m [32 ft-lb]

![[10600356.png]]

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> This component weighs 23 kg \[50 lb\] or more. To reduce the possibility of personal injury, use a hoist or get assistance to lift this component.

- Install the four isolators on the air filter box bracket.
- Install the air filter box onto the isolators and align the capscrews holes.

![[10600355.png]]

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> This component weighs 23 kg \[50 lb\] or more. To reduce the possibility of personal injury, use a hoist or get assistance to lift this component.

- Install the four isolators on the air cleaner brackets.
- Install the air cleaner housings onto the isolators and align the capscrew holes.

![[10600633.png]]

- Make sure the turbocharger inlet connection boots are over the filter box flanges.
- Tighten the hose clamps.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 9 n•m [80 in-lb]

- Install the four capscrews and nuts.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 102 n•m [75 ft-lb]

- Opposite bank is similar, repeat previous steps to install the other filter box.

![[10600354.png]]

- Make sure the turbocharger inlet connection boots are over the air cleaner housing flanges.
- Tighten the hose clamps.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 9 n•m [80 in-lb]

- Install the four capscrews and nuts.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 102 n•m [75 ft-lb]

- Opposite bank is similar, repeat previous steps to install the other air cleaner housing.

![[10600631.png]]

### Finishing Steps

- Install the aftercooler heat shields. [[56-010-129-tr — Aftercooler Heat Shield|Refer to Procedure 010-129 (Aftercooler Heat Shield) in Section 10.]]
