### Fluorescent Dye Tracer

Add one unit of Fluorescent Tracer, to each 38 liters \[10 gal\] of engine lubricating oil. Refer to Procedure [[56-018-017 — Lubricating Oil System|018-017]] in Section V for oil pan capacity.

![[07600297.png]]

Operate the engine at low idle for 10 minutes.

![[07600298.png]]

Shut the engine off.

![[07600299.png]]

Use a High-Intensity Black Light to inspect for oil leakage.

Oil will glow a dark blue color.

![[07600300.png]]

If an oil leak into the coolant is suspected, check the coolant.

![[oi400vr.png]]

### Clean and Inspect for Reuse

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Wear safety glasses or a face shield, as well as protective clothing, to prevent personal injury when using a steam cleaner or high-pressure water.

Use a steam cleaner or a high-pressure washer to clean the engine.

![[00600029.png]]

Operate the engine until the coolant temperature reaches 80°C \[176°F\]. Inspect the exterior of the engine for leaking gaskets, seals, o-rings, pipe plugs, or fittings.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Before replacing any gaskets, verify that the capscrews are tightened to the correct torque values. Refer to Procedure [[99-018-009 — Capscrew Markings and Torque Values|018-009]] for the capscrew torque listings.

![[07600232.png]]

Check for a loose, broken, or missing oil dipstick tube, dipstick, or oil filler cap.

![[03600004.png]]

Check the engine crankcase breather elements and breather hose for restriction.

If **not** restricted, check blowby. Refer to Procedure [[56-014-005-tr — Engine Testing (Engine Dynamometer)|014-005]]. High blowby can cause gasket and seal leakage.
