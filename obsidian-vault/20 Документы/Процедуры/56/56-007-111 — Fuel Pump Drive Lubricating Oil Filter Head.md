### General Information

The fuel pump drive lubricating oil filter head is mounted on the gear housing near the high-pressure fuel pump drive. The filter head receives oil flow from the main oil rifle through an oil supply hose.

All components handled in this procedure weigh less than 23 kg \[50 lb\].

![[ck800wa.png]]

### Preparatory Steps

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> To reduce the possibility of personal injury, avoid direct contact of hot oil with your skin.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Some state and federal agencies have determined that used engine oil can be carcinogenic and cause reproductive toxicity. Avoid inhalation of vapors, ingestion, and prolonged contact with used engine oil. If not reused, dispose of in accordance with local environmental regulations.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Batteries can emit explosive gases. To reduce the possibility of personal injury, always ventilate the compartment before servicing the batteries. To reduce the possibility of arcing, remove the negative (-) battery cable first and attach the negative (-) battery cable last.

- Disconnect batteries and power supplies. See equipment manufacturer service information.
- Remove high-pressure fuel pump lubricating oil filter. [[56-007-110-tr — Fuel Pump Drive Lubricating Oil Filter|Refer to Procedure 007-110 in Section 7.]]

![[ck800wa.png]]

### Remove

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> **Always** cover exposed ports and fittings using protective caps from appropriate clean care kit.

Remove fitted filter can from fuel pump drive lubricating oil filter head.

![[07e00230.png]]

Remove the pressure switch from the elbow.

![[07e00231.png]]

Remove three capscrews from the fuel pump drive lubricating oil filter head.

![[07e00232.png]]

Discard the two o-rings from the recessed holes on the fuel pump drive.

![[07e00233.png]]

Remove the elbow from the fuel pump drive lubricating oil filter head.

![[07e00234.png]]

Remove the pressure sensor from the fuel pump drive lubricating oil filter head behind the can.

![[07e00235.png]]

Remove the two plugs from the fuel pump drive lubricating oil filter head.

Remove the fuel pump drive lubricating oil filter head.

![[07e00236.png]]

### Clean and Inspect for Reuse

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> When using solvents, acids, or alkaline materials for cleaning, follow the manufacturer's recommendations for use. Wear goggles and protective clothing to reduce the possibility of personal injury.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Wear appropriate eye and face protection when using compressed air. Flying debris and dirt can cause personal injury.

Use solvent to clean the fuel pump drive lubricating oil filter head, male unions, and lubricating oil supply hoses.

Dry with compressed air.

![[07j00035.png]]

Inspect lubricating oil filter head.

Replace filter head if cracked or damaged.

![[07j00036.png]]

Inspect each male union.

Replace component if corroded or damaged.

![[07j00276.png]]

Inspect each straight-thread o-ring plug.

Replace plug if corroded or damaged.

![[07j00151.png]]

### Finishing Steps

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Batteries can emit explosive gases. To reduce the possibility of personal injury, always ventilate the compartment before servicing the batteries. To reduce the possibility of arcing, remove the negative (-) battery cable first and attach the negative (-) battery cable last.

- Install high-pressure fuel pump lubricating oil filter. [[56-007-110-tr — Fuel Pump Drive Lubricating Oil Filter|Refer to Procedure 007-110 in Section 7.]]
- Confirm lubricating oil level in oil pan is to FULL mark.
- Connect batteries and power supplies. See equipment manufacturer service information.
- Operate engine. Check for leaks.
