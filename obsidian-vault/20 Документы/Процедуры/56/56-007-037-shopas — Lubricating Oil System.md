### Fill

Use a high quality 15W-40 multiviscosity oil that meets the American Petroleum Institute (API) performance classification CE or CF4 or better.

Choose the correct oil for the operating climate. [[102-018-003 — Lubricating Oil Recommendations and Specifications|Refer to Procedure 018-003]] in the Operation and Maintenance Manual, QSK45 and QSK60 Series Engines, Bulletin [[3666260 — QSK45 and QSK60 Operation and Maintenance Manual\|3666260]] correct oil.

![[pl800gb.png]]

Fill the engine to the correct level with clean engine oil.

[[56-018-017 — Lubricating Oil System|Refer to Procedure 018-017]] for engine sump capacity.

Check the oil level on the dipstick. The level **must** be to the high (H) mark on the dipstick.

Do **not** overfill. Drain off excess oil in a pan.

![[oi801vw.png]]

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Before starting engines not equipped with a PRELUB™ system, complete the following steps. Engine damage will occur because of inadequate lubrication at startup if these steps are not followed.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Engines that are equipped with a PRELUB™ system are self priming and do **not** require completion of this and the next step.

The following steps **must** be completed after each oil change or after the engine has been shutdown for more than five days to make sure the engine receives the correct oil flow through the lubricating system.

Disconnect the engine position sensor.

Using the starter motor, rotate the engine crankshaft until oil pressure appears on the gauge or the warning light goes out.

![[14400007.png]]

Connect the engine position sensor.

![[19400429.png]]

Operate the engine at idle speed to inspect for leaks at the filters and drain plug(s).

![[07600232.png]]

Shut off the engine.

Wait approximately 5 minutes to allow the oil to drain from the upper parts of the engine.

Check the oil level.

Add oil as necessary to bring the oil level to the high (H) mark on the dipstick.

![[oi801vw.png]]
