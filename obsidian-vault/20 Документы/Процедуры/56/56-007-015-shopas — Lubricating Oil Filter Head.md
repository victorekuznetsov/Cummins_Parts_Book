### Install

Two-Piece Filter Head, Spin-on Filter

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> This component or assembly weighs greater than 23 kg \[50 lb\]. To prevent serious personal injury, be sure to have assistance or use appropriate lifting equipment to lift this component or assembly.

Position the filter head (2) and the gaskets (3 and 4) on the engine (5) with 12 capscrews (1).

![[07600347.png]]

Tighten the capscrews in the sequence shown.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 45 n•m [33 ft-lb]

If the engine is equipped with pre-oil filter and post-oil filter sensors, install and connect the sensors.

![[07600349.png]]

One-Piece Filter Head, Spin-on Filter

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> This component or assembly weighs greater than 23 kg \[50 lb\]. To prevent serious personal injury, be sure to have assistance or use appropriate lifting equipment to lift this component or assembly.

Position the filter head (2) and the gaskets (3 and 4) on the engine (5) with eight capscrews (1).

![[07600489.png]]

Tighten the capscrews in the sequence shown.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 45 n•m [33 ft-lb]

If the engine is equipped with pre-oil filter and post-oil filter sensors, install and connect the sensors.

![[07600479.png]]
