### Снятие

с форсункой механического управления

Удалите правую береговую проводку с заземления свинца из скобки.

![[19803732.png]]

Отключите все разъемы для проводов правого берега от электропроводки левого берега.

![[19600210.png]]

Отключите все разъемы датчика температуры выхлопных газов с правой стороны проводов, если они оборудованы.

![[19803733.png]]

Отключите следующие датчики:

- Предфильтровый датчик давления масла, если он оборудован.
- Датчик давления масла после фильтра, если он оборудован.
- Датчик температуры впускного коллектора справа на берегу, если он оборудован.
- Датчик давления среднего впускного коллектора правого берега, если он оборудован.
- Датчик температуры заднего впускного коллектора справа, если он оборудован.

![[19600107.png]]

Удалите болты и P-затворы с крышки крана.

![[19600277.png]]

> [!note] Примечание
> Для двигателей, оснащенных жгутом для проводов фазы 2.0, ремень для проводов двигателя и опорная кронштейн для жгутов проводов для проводов двигателя являются одним элементом.

Убедитесь, что все разъёмы жгутов проводов двигателя отключены и не запутаны.

Тщательно удалите крепежные болты из опорной скобки.

Медленно вытащите опорный кронштейн из двигателя, убедившись, что проводная упряжка вытаскивается из двигателя без связывания или запутывания.

Удалите зажимы проводов из опорной скобы проводов.

Удалите жгут проводов двигателя из зажимов и скоб.

> [!quote]- Original (English) · английский оригинал
> ### Remove
>
> with Mechanically Actuated Injector
>
> Remove the right bank wiring harness ground lead from the bracket.
>
> Disconnect all the right bank wiring harness connectors from the left bank wiring harness.
>
> Disconnect all the right bank exhaust temperature sensor connectors from the right bank wiring harness, if equipped.
>
> Disconnect the following sensors:
>
> - Pre-filter oil pressure sensor, if equipped.
> - Post-filter oil pressure sensor, if equipped.
> - Right bank front intake manifold temperature sensor, if equipped.
> - Right bank middle intake manifold pressure sensor, if equipped.
> - Right bank rear intake manifold temperature sensor, if equipped.
>
> Remove the capscrews and P-clips from the cam follower cover.
>
> **Note · Примечание**
> For engines equipped with the phase 2.0 wiring harness, the engine harness and the engine harness support bracket are one piece.
>
> Make sure all engine harness connectors are disconnected and untangled.
>
> Carefully remove the attaching capscrews from the support bracket.
>
> Slowly pull the support bracket from the engine, making sure that the harness is pulled from the engine without binding or tangling.
>
> Remove the wire clamps from the harness support bracket.
>
> Remove the engine harness from the clamps and bracket.
>
> Disconnect all of the left bank wiring harness connectors from the right bank wiring harness.
>
> Disconnect all the original equipment manufacturer (OEM) connectors from the left bank wiring harness.
>
> Disconnect the 3-pin Deutsch™ J1939 connectors from the communication harness.
>
> Disconnect the wiring harness from all the left bank exhaust temperature sensors.
>
> Disconnect the following sensors:
>
> - Left bank rear intake manifold temperature sensor, if equipped.
> - Oil temperature sensor, if equipped.
> - Left bank rear turbocharger compressor inlet temperature sensor, if equipped.
> - Main rifle oil pressure sensor.
> - Speed sensor.
> - Analog oil level sensor.
> - Fuel temperature sensor.
> - Timing pressure sensor.
> - Fuel rail pressure sensor.
> - Barometric/ambient air pressure sensor.
> - Blowby pressure sensor.
> - Left bank rear intake manifold pressure sensor, if equipped.
> - Coolant level sensor.
> - Fuel pump pressure sensor.
> - Coolant pressure senor.
> - Coolant temperature sensor.
> - Left bank front intake manifold pressure sensor.
> - Left bank front intake manifold temperature sensor.
> - Aftercooler water temperature sensor, if equipped.
>
> Disconnect the left bank wiring harness ground lead from the bracket.
>
> Remove the capscrews from the Centinel™ actuator support bracket P-clips, if equipped.
>
> Disconnect the left bank wiring harness from the Centinel™ system, if equipped.
>
> Remove the capscrews from the Rockford™ fan clutch and coolant temperature P-clip, if equipped.
>
> Remove the capscrews from the Rockford™ fan clutch P-clip, located next to the thermostat housing, if equipped.
>
> Remove the two capscrews (1) and disconnect the left bank wiring harness from the engine control module (ECM).
>
> Remove the nuts and disconnect the two fuel shutoff valve terminal connectors (1).
>
> Disconnect the left bank wiring harness from the electronic control valve assembly.
>
> Remove the capscrews and left bank wiring harness routing block, if equipped.
>
> Remove the capscrews and left bank wiring harness connector block, if equipped.
>
> Disconnect harness from the Centinel™ actuator, if equipped.
>
> Disconnect the Centinel™ oil level extension harness, if equipped.
>
> **Note · Примечание**
> For engines equipped with the phase 2.0 wiring harness, the engine harness and the engine harness support bracket are one piece.
>
> Make sure all engine harness connectors are disconnected and untangled.
>
> Carefully remove the attaching capscrews from the support bracket.
>
> Slowly pull the support bracket from the engine, making sure that the harness is pulled from the engine without binding or tangling.
>
> Remove the wire clamps from the harness support bracket.
>
> Remove the engine harness from the clamps and bracket.
>
> with Electronically Actuated Injector
>
> Disconnect the engine harness from the sensors and switches.
>
> - Engine crankshaft speed/position sensor.
> - Engine camshaft speed/position sensor.
> - Intake manifold 1 temperature sensor.
> - Intake manifold 1 pressure sensor.
> - Intake manifold 2 temperature sensor.
> - Intake manifold 2 pressure sensor.
> - Intake manifold 3 temperature sensor.
> - Intake manifold 3 pressure sensor.
> - Intake manifold 4 temperature sensor.
> - Intake manifold 4 pressure sensor.
> - Engine coolant temperature 1 sensor.
> - Coolant level sensor 1.
> - Coolant pressure sensor.
> - Engine oil temperature sensor 1.
> - Engine oil rifle pressure 1 sensor.
> - Barometric pressure sensor.
> - Engine oil burn valve solenoid driver.
> - Injector metering rail 1 pressure sensor.
> - Fuel delivery pressure sensor.
> - Engine fuel temperature sensor 1.
> - Fuel pump pressurizing assembly 1.
> - Injector solenoid drive (each cylinder).
> - Water in fuel indicator sensor.
> - Crankcase pressure sensor.
> - Exhaust gas temperature sensor (each cylinder).
> - Pre-oil filter pressure sensor.
> - Post-oil filter pressure sensor.
> - Turbocharger 1 speed sensor.
>
> Remove the harness wire ties from the harness support bracket and P-clips holding the harness onto the engine.
>
> Disconnect the harness connectors from the ECMs.
