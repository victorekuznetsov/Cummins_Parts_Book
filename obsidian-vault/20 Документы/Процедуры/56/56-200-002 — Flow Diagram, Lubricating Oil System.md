> ### Flow Diagram
>
> Lubricating Oil System
>
> 1. Lubricating oil suction tube
> 2. Block connection
> 3. Lubricating oil flow to filter head
> 4. Lubricating oil filters
> 5. Lubricating oil flow out of filters
> 6. Main lubricating oil rifle
> 7. Cooled lubricating oil to flywheel housing
> 8. Lubricating oil cooler
> 9. Lubricating oil rifle/supply to oil coolers
> 10. Lubricating oil supply to main bearings
> 11. Pressure regulator valve
> 12. Lubricating oil flow from rotor to discharge port.
>
> Piston Cooling, Connecting Rod, Overhead
>
> 1. Lubricating oil supply to turbochargers
> 2. Lubricating oil supply to right bank piston cooling nozzle
> 3. Right bank piston cooling nozzle regulator
> 4. Lubricating oil supply rifles to right bank camshaft
> 5. Lubricating oil supply from lubricating oil coolers
> 6. Lubricating oil supply to main lubricating oil rifle
> 7. Lubricating oil supply rifles to left bank camshaft
> 8. Left bank piston cooling nozzle regulator
> 9. Piston cooling nozzle
> 10. Lubricating oil supply to camshaft and cam followers
> 11. Lubricating oil supply to rocker levers.
>
> Front Gear Train
>
> 1. Main lubricating oil rifle
> 2. Lubricating oil supply to idler gear
> 3. Lubricating oil supply to accessory drive
> 4. Lubricating oil supply to gear cover
> 5. Lubricating oil supply to rockford fan clutch (either passage)
> 6. Lubricating oil supply to water pump drive.
>
> Full-Flow Lubricating Oil Filter Head
>
> 1. Lubricating oil supply from lubricating oil pump
> 2. Oil supply through filter head to oil filters
> 3. Lubricating oil filter
> 4. Bypass valve - open position
> 5. Lubricating oil supply from lubricating oil filter
> 6. Lubricating oil supply lubricating oil coolers
> 7. Bypass valve - closed position.
>
> Eliminator Oil Filter
>
> 1. Lubricating oil inlet from engine
> 2. Filtered lubricating oil returned to engine block
> 3. Back flushed lubricating oil
> 4. Lubricating oil being filtered by centrifuge
> 5. Clean lubricating oil returned to sump.
>
> Fuel Pump Lubrication - with Electronically Actuated Injectors
>
> 1. Lubricating oil supply to accessory drive bushing and fuel pump
> 2. Lubricating oil return.
>
> QSK60 MCRS CM850 Marine Turbocharger Lubricating Oil Plumbing
>
> 1. Lubricating oil supply from lubricating oil coolers
> 2. Lubricating oil supply tee block
> 3. Turbocharger lubricating oil supply manifold
> 4. Turbocharger bearing housing lubrication oil supply tube
> 5. Lubricating oil flow through bearing housing
> 6. Turbocharger lubricating oil return hose
> 7. Turbocharger lubricating oil block return port.
