> Observe the routing, clipping and clearances of fluid plumbing lines and presence of any shielding in order to reference when reinstalling.

### Install

QSK45 and QSK60 Single Stage

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> When installing or reinstalling flexible hoses and tubing be sure that all the original factory installed clips are inspected and fitted. Reinstall so that the routing of fluid plumbing follows the original factory design and ensure the routing provides adequate clearances to prevent contact with any other engine components or high temperature engine areas. Inspect and reinstall any heat shielding to original factory configuration. Not providing adequate clearances between fluid plumbing and other engine components can lead to possible component damage, leakage and potentially hazardous conditions.
