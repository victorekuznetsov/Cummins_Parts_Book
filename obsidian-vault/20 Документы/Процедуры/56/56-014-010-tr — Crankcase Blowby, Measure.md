**Измерение прорыва газов в картер**

### General Information

Excessive blowby indicates an air compressor, turbocharger, or engine malfunction that allows combustion gasses or air to enter the crankcase and build a pressure higher than normal.

This procedure describes how to measure crankcase pressure in single and dual bank crankcase breather equipped models.

![[10400028.png]]

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> This procedure requires the use of two Blowby Tools, Part Number 3822571.

It is essential that two 10.3 mm \[ 0.406 in \] Orifice Blowby Tools are used. One barbed connection must be connected to a manometer and one barbed connection plugged or capped off.

Failure to do so will cause the blowby readings to be inaccurate.

![[14600063.png]]

### Measure

The following is for a single bank crankcase breather blowby tool installation.

Fabricate a “Y” piece to the following specifications:

1. 25.4 mm \[ 1 in \] (internal diameter)
2. 60 degrees
3. 101.6 mm \[ 4 in \]
4. 101.6 mm \[ 4 in \]
5. 50.8 mm \[ 2 in \] (internal diameter).

Pipe thickness - 1.5 mm \[ 0.059 in \].

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The “Y” piece, must have a 60 degree angle at the vee (2) and be symmetrical to make sure blowby gases are split equally.

![[14600059.png]]

If applicable, remove the 50.4 mm \[ 2 in \] flexible crankcase breather pipe.

Use a short length of 50.4 mm \[ 2 in \] internal diameter hose to connect directly from the crankcase breather body to the 50.4 mm \[ 2 in \] “Y” pipe (1).

Use a suitable pipe (2) and attach two 10.3 mm \[ 0.406 in \] orifice blowby tools, Part Number 3822571, to each arm of the “Y” pipe.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> It is essential that two 10.3 mm \[ 0.406 in \] Orifice Blowby Tools are used. One barbed connection **must** be connected to a manometer and one barbed connection plugged or capped off. Failure to do so will cause the blowby reading to be inaccurate.

![[14600060.png]]

Attach a water manometer, Part Number ST-1111-3 or equivalent, to the unplugged barbed connection of the first blowby measurement tool (1). Make sure the barbed connection on the second blowby measurement tool is plugged or capped off.

![[14600061.png]]

The following information is for a dual bank crankcase breather blowby tool installation.

Use a short length of suitable hose (1) to attach each 10.3 mm \[ 0.406 in \] orifice blowby tool, Part Number 3822571, to each of the crankcase breathers.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> It is essential that two 10.3 mm \[ 0.406 in \] Orifice Blowby Tools are used. One barbed connection **must** be open and one barbed connection plugged or capped off. Failure to do so will cause the blowby reading to be inaccurate.

![[14600062.png]]

Attach a water manometer, Part Number ST-1111-3 or equivalent, to the unplugged barbed connection of the first blowby measurement tool (1). Make sure the barbed connection on the second blowby measurement tool is plugged or capped off.

![[14600061.png]]

### Test

Run the engine until it reaches normal operating temperatures and pressures.

Operate the engine at rated RPM and full load (wide-open throttle) until a steady reading is obtained on the manometer.

A sudden increase in the blowby readings indicates a problem. A gradual increase over time is normal (due to wear on the internal engine components).

![[eg8toja.png]]

![[10400028.png]]

Compare the blowby readings to previous readings on the engine.

If the previous readings for the engine are **not** available, use the information in the table below.

| QSK45 and QSK60 Blowby Limits |  |  |  |
|---|---|---|---|
| Use two 10.3 mm \[ 0.406 in \] diameter orifice Blowby Measurement Tools at Rated rpm and load. |  |  |  |
| Engine Family | New Engine Limits | Rebuilt Engine Limits | Used Engine Limits |
| QSK45 and QSK60 | 2.49 kPa \[ 10 in H 2 O \] | 2.49 kPa \[ 10 in H 2 O \] | 5.47 kPa \[ 22 in H 2 O \] |
