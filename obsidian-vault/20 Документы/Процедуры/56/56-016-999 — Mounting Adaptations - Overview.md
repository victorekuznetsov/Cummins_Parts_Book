### General Information

The mounting adaption's group consists of the flywheel/flexplate housing, flywheel/flexplate, engine mounts, and engine lifting brackets.

For the various applications, the flywheel housing is available in differed styles, sizes, and materials. Refer to the appropriate parts book and the engine parts listing for the correct part number for the engine application.

The flexplate is available as an assembly **only**. Do **not** disassemble the flexplate assembly. The alignment of the crankshaft adapter (hub) to the ring gear adapter **must** be correct so the valve and injector setting index marks are in the correct position relative to the crankshaft. The flexplate is balanced as an assembly during manufacture. Changing any of the parts of the assembly will affect the balance. The flexplate assembly includes the ring gear. The ring gear is available for service. The ring gear can **not** be changed without affecting the balance of the assembly.

The flywheel is available as an assembly. The assembly includes the flywheel and the ring gear. The ring gear is available for service.

There are two dowel pins in the cylinder block. The pins protrude into holes in the flywheel housing. The pins are used to align the flywheel housing properly to the cylinder block.

The flywheel housing **must** be in proper alignment. If the housing is **not** in proper alignment the holes in the flywheel housing and the block **must** be reamed for a bigger dowel.

The dowel holes on the QSK45 and QSK60 engine blocks and flywheel housings are precision drilled. The holes are machined to 12.7 0 mm \[0.50 in\] separately. The parts are assembled and the alignment checked. If the alignment is **not** within specification, oversized dowels are installed at the factory. Replacing the block or the flywheel housing does **not** necessarily require the flywheel housing and the engine block to have oversized dowels installed.

A new front trunnion was released on engine serial number first 33148569, all engines after this serial number will be fitted with the updated trunnion with a larger inside diameter. The updated trunnion was released to match an improved sleeve fitted on the front gear housing. The grease nipple location was changed on the updated trunnion to side of the trunnion.
