### Preparatory Steps

with Mechanically Actuated Injector

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Some state and federal agencies have determined that used engine oil can be carcinogenic and cause reproductive toxicity. Avoid inhalation of vapors, ingestion, and prolonged contact with used engine oil. If not reused, dispose of in accordance with local environmental regulations.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> To reduce the possibility of personal injury, avoid direct contact of hot oil with your skin.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> This component weighs 23 kg \[50 lb\] or more. To reduce the possibility of personal injury, use a hoist or get assistance to lift this component.

- Drain the lubricating oil. Refer to Procedure [[56-007-037-tr — Lubricating Oil System|007-037]].

- Remove the fuel filters, if needed. Refer to Procedure [[56-006-015-tr — Fuel Filter (Spin-On Type)|006-015]].
- Remove the fuel filter head. Refer to Procedure [[56-006-017-tr — Fuel Filter Head|006-017]]
- Disconnect the pre-lubrication suction hose. Refer to Procedure [[56-007-063-tr — Pre-Lubricating Oil Pump|007-063]].
- Remove the oil pan from the oil pan adapter. Refer to Procedure [[56-007-025-tr — Lubricating Oil Pan|007-025]].
- Remove the cover plates from the oil pan adapter, if needed. Refer to Procedure [[56-007-026-tr — Lubricating Oil Pan Adapter Cover Plate|007-026]].

### Remove

The clearance **must** be sufficient from the oil suction tube before the adapter can be removed from the bottom of the engine.

The QSK45 oil pan adapter assembly is held on by 36 capscrews, and the QSK60 oil pan adapter assembly is held on by 46 capscrews.

Remove the capscrews (1) from the oil pan adapter assembly (2), the gasket (3), and the engine (4).

Remove the oil pan adapter assembly (2) and the gasket (3).

![[07600311.png]]

### Clean and Inspect for Reuse

Inspect the gasket (3) for reuse.

![[07600312.png]]

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> When using a steam cleaner, wear safety glasses or a face shield, as well as protective clothing. Hot steam can cause serious personal injury.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Wear appropriate eye and face protection when using compressed air. Flying debris and dirt can cause personal injury.

Clean all gasket surfaces on cover plates, sumps, and oil pan adapter.

Steam clean the oil pan adapter and dry with compressed air.

![[07600313.png]]

Inspect all areas of the oil pan adapter for cracks or other damage. If any area is cracked or damaged, the oil pan adapter **must** be replaced.

![[07600314.png]]

### Install

QSK45 Engines

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Do not use gasket cement. Gasket cement will prevent the gasket from sealing properly.

To ease in assembly, plastic wire ties can be used to retain the gasket until the capscrews can be installed, then cut and remove the ties before tightening the capscrews.

Install the gasket (3) on the oil pan adapter assembly (2).

![[07600312.png]]

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> This component weighs 23 kg \[50 lb\] or more. To reduce the possibility of personal injury, use a hoist or get assistance to lift this component.

Position the oil pan adapter assembly (2) with the gasket (3) on the engine (4) with the capscrews (1).

![[07600311.png]]

Tighten the capscrews in the sequence shown.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 45 n•m [33 ft-lb]

![[07600315.png]]

### Finishing Steps

with Mechanically Actuated Injector

- Install the oil pan onto the oil pan adapter. Refer to Procedure [[56-007-025-tr — Lubricating Oil Pan|007-025]].
- Install the cover plates on the oil pan adapter, if removed. Refer to Procedure [[56-007-026-tr — Lubricating Oil Pan Adapter Cover Plate|007-026]].
- Install the fuel filter head. Refer to Procedure [[56-006-017-tr — Fuel Filter Head|006-017]]
- Install the new fuel filters, if removed. Refer to Procedure [[56-006-015-tr — Fuel Filter (Spin-On Type)|006-015]].
- Install the new lubricating oil filters. Refer to Procedure [[56-007-013-tr — Lubricating Oil Filter (Spin-On)|007-013]].
- Fill the engine with clean lubricating oil. Refer to Procedure [[56-007-037-tr — Lubricating Oil System|007-037]].
- Connect the pre-lubrication suction hose. Refer to Procedure [[56-007-063-tr — Pre-Lubricating Oil Pump|007-063]].
- Operate the engine at idle speed and inspect for oil leaks.
