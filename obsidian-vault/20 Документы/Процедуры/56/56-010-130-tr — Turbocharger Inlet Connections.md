# Turbocharger Inlet Connections

> [!abstract] Процедура · `56-010-130-tr`
> **Двигатели:** [[33210083 — QSK60 CM500 CPL 2699|33210083]], [[33219033 — QSK60 CM500 CPL 2848|33219033]], [[33224343 — QSK60 CM500 CPL 2849|33224343]]

> **Секции:** Section 10 - Air Intake System - Group 10
> **Даты:** изменён 2008-11-21
> **Источник:** [QuickServe](https://quickserve.cummins.com/qs3/pubsys2/xml/en/procedures/56/56-010-130-tr.html) · [PDF-оригинал](https://github.com/victorekuznetsov/Cummins_Parts_Book/raw/main/bulletins/procedures/56-010-130-tr.pdf)

### Preparatory Steps

- Remove the aftercooler heat shields. Refer to Procedure [[56-010-129-tr — Aftercooler Heat Shield|010-129 (Aftercooler Heat Shield) in Section 10.]]
- Remove turbocharger heat shield. [[56-010-076-tr — Turbocharger Heat Shield|Refer to Procedure 010-076 (Turbocharger Heat Shield) in Section 10.]]

![[ck800wa.png]]

### Remove

Loosen the two hose clamps securing the turbocharger inlet connection to the air cleaner and compressor inlet of the front turbocharger.

Remove the front turbocharger inlet connection.

![[10600648.png]]

Loosen the two hose clamps on the boot securing the rear turbocharger inlet connection to the filter box.

Slide the boot completely onto the turbocharger inlet connection.

Loosen the hose clamp securing the turbocharger inlet connection to the rear turbocharger compressor and remove the turbocharger inlet connection.

The opposite bank is similar, repeat the previous steps to remove the other bank turbocharger inlet connection.

![[10600649.png]]

### Install

Install the rear turbocharger inlet connection onto the rear turbocharger inlet connection and onto the rear turbocharger compressor flange and tighten hose clamp.

Slide the other boot onto the filter box flange and tighten both clamps.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 9 n•m [80 in-lb]

![[10600649.png]]

Install the turbocharger inlet connection onto the flanges of the filter box and the compressor side of the front turbocharger.

Install the two capscrews securing the right front turbocharger inlet connection to the bracket on the right front turbocharger heat shield.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 23 n•m [204 in-lb]

Tighten the two hose clamps securing the turbocharger inlet connection to the filter box and the compressor side of the front turbocharger.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 9 n•m [80 in-lb]

The opposite bank is similar, repeat the previous steps to install the other bank turbocharger inlet connection.

![[10600648.png]]

### Finishing Steps

- Install the turbocharger heat shield. [[56-010-076-tr — Turbocharger Heat Shield|Refer to Procedure 010-076 (Turbocharger Heat Shield) in Section 10.]]
- Install the aftercooler heat shields. [[56-010-129-tr — Aftercooler Heat Shield|Refer to Procedure 010-129 (Aftercooler Heat Shield) in Section 10.]].
