### Remove

Remove the plug from the center of the block of plate.

![[00r00919.png]]

Remove the four capscrews holding the block of plate and discard the gasket.

![[00r00920.png]]

### Install

Install a new gasket and four capscrews to the blanking plate.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 46 n•m [34 ft-lb]

![[00r00921.png]]

Install plug in to the center of the blanking plate.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 61 n•m [45 ft-lb]

![[00r00919.png]]
