Однокомпонентная кронштейн

> [!danger] ОПАСНО
> Чтобы снизить риск травмы, не допускайте попадания горячего масла на кожу.

> По заключению ряда государственных органов отработанное моторное масло может обладать канцерогенным действием и вредить репродуктивной функции. Не вдыхайте пары, не допускайте попадания внутрь и длительного контакта с отработанным моторным маслом. Если деталь не используется повторно, утилизируйте её по местным природоохранным требованиям.

> [!quote]- Original (English) · английский оригинал
> ### Remove
>
> Single-piece Bracket
>
> **WARNING · Опасно**
> To reduce the possibility of personal injury, avoid direct contact of hot oil with your skin.
>
> **WARNING · Опасно**
> Some state and federal agencies have determined that used engine oil can be carcinogenic and cause reproductive toxicity. Avoid inhalation of vapors, ingestion, and prolonged contact with used engine oil. If not reused, dispose of in accordance with local environmental regulations.
>
> Place a suitable container under the drain plug (2).
>
> Remove the plug and let the oil drain.
>
> **Note · Примечание**
> Remove the vent plug (1) to help empty the filter.
>
> Install the drain plug (2) and vent plug (1) in the filter body.
>
> **WARNING · Опасно**
> This component or assembly weighs greater than 23 kg \[50 lb\]. To prevent serious personal injury, be sure to have assistance or use appropriate lifting equipment to lift this component or assembly.
>
> Remove the four mounting capscrews from Eliminator™ filter head at indicated locations.
>
> Install the four guide studs (1) and remove the six remaining capscrews.
>
> Remove the four support capscrews (1).
>
> Remove the filter over the guide studs while removing the connection tube (2) from the hand hole cover.
>
> Remove the gaskets over the guide studs and discard the gaskets.
>
> Remove the four guide studs from the engine block.
>
> Remove the bracket mounting capscrews and the bracket.
>
> Remove the Eliminator™ handhole cover and discard the handhole cover o-ring. [[3666253 — Eliminator™ Filter|3666253]]
>
> Remove and discard the connection tube o-rings.
>
> Three-piece Bracket
>
> **WARNING · Опасно**
> To reduce the possibility of personal injury, avoid direct contact of hot oil with your skin.
>
> **WARNING · Опасно**
> Some state and federal agencies have determined that used engine oil can be carcinogenic and cause reproductive toxicity. Avoid inhalation of vapors, ingestion, and prolonged contact with used engine oil. If not reused, dispose of in accordance with local environmental regulations.
>
> Place a suitable container under the drain plug (2).
>
> Remove the plug and let the oil drain.
>
> **Note · Примечание**
> Removing the vent plug (1) helps to empty the filter.
>
> Install the drain plug (2) and vent plug (1) in the filter body.
>
> **WARNING · Опасно**
> This component or assembly weighs greater than 23 kg \[50 lb\]. To prevent serious personal injury, be sure to have assistance or use appropriate lifting equipment to lift this component or assembly.
>
> Remove the four mounting capscrews from Eliminator™ filter head at indicated locations.
>
> Install the four guide studs (1) and remove the six remaining capscrews.
>
> Remove the two support capscrews (1) and spacers (2).
>
> Remove the filter over the guide studs while removing the connection tube (3) from the hand hole cover.
>
> Remove the gaskets over the guide studs and discard the gaskets.
>
> Remove the four guide studs from the engine block.
>
> Remove the front bracket mounting capscrews with nuts and the front bracket (1).
>
> Remove the middle bracket mounting capscrews and the middle bracket (1).
>
> Remove the rear bracket mounting capscrews and the rear bracket (1).
>
> Remove the Eliminator™ filter handhole cover and gasket.
>
> Remove and discard the connection tube o-rings.
