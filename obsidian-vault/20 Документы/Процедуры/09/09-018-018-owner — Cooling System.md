### Specifications

#### Coolant Capacity (Engine Only)

| NTA855 | 21 liters \[22 U.S.qt\] |
|---|---|

| NT855 | 19 liters \[20 U.S.qt\] |
|---|---|

| Standard Modulating Thermostat Range | 79 to 91°C \[175 to 195°F\] |
|---|---|

| Maximum coolant pressure (pressure cap removed) | 241 kPa \[35 psi\] |
|---|---|

| Maximum Allowable Top Tank Temperature | 95°C \[203°F\] |
|---|---|

#### Minimum Recommended Top Tank Temperature

| Construction/Industrial | 70°C \[158°F\] |
|---|---|

| G-Drive | 70°C \[158°F\] |
|---|---|

| Maximum Allowable Deaeration Time | 25 minutes |
|---|---|

| Minimum Allowable Draw Down or 20 Percent of System Capacity (whichever is greater) | 10.4 liters \[11 U.S.qt\] |
|---|---|

| Minimum allowable pressure cap | 48 kPa \[7 psi\] |
|---|---|
