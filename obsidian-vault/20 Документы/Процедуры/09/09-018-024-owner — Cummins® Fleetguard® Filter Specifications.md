### Specifications

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Under NO circumstances must a customer exceed one oil change interval before adding chemicals (by filter or liquid) to the coolant. If the recommended service intervals are neglected, there is a high probability that cylinder liner corrosion will occur.

Cummins Filtration®/Fleetguard® filters are developed through joint testing at Cummins Inc. and Cummins Filtration®. Cummins Filtration®/Fleetguard® filters are standard on new Cummins® engines. Cummins Inc. recommends their use.

Cummins Filtration®/Fleetguard® products meet all Cummins Inc. Source Approval Test standards to provide the quality filtration necessary to achieve the engine's design life. If other brands are substituted, the purchaser **must** insist on products that the supplier has tested to meet Cummins Inc. high quality standards.

Cummins Inc. can **not** be responsible for problems caused by non-genuine filters that do **not** meet Cummins Inc. performance or durability requirements.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Filters **must** meet Cummins Inc. Source Approval Method 10,769. Cummins Filtration®/Fleetguard® filters meet Cummins Inc. Source Approval Method 10,769.

1. Consult the vehicle equipment manufacturer's maintenance information for total cooling system capacity.
2. After draining and replacing the coolant, **always** pre-charge the cooling system to an SCA level of 1.5 units per gallon. This concentration level **must never** be allowed to fall below 1.2 units and **must** be controlled when the level is greater than 3 units. Action needed when the level falls below 1.2: a filter and liquid; above 1.2 to 3.0: filter **only**; above 3.0: test and add filter when 3.0 and below. **Note:** When performing service which requires draining the cooling system, take special precautions to collect it in a clean container, seal it to prevent contamination, and save for reuse.
3. Change the coolant filters at each oil change to protect the cooling system. The service filter listed above is satisfactory for use with maintenance intervals from 16,000 to 24,000 km \[10,000 to 15,000 mi\] or 250 to 600 hours.
