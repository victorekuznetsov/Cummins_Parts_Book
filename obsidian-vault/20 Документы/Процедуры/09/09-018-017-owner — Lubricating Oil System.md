### Specifications

#### Oil Pressure

#### Oil Pressure, Main Oil Rifle (15W40 oil at 107°C \[225°F\]

At idle (minimum allowable) 70 kPa \[10 psi\]At no load governed speed 240 to 310 kPa \[35 to 45 psi\]

#### Oil Filter Capacity

Bypass filter (spin-on) 2.65 liters \[0.7 U.S.gal\]Full flow filter (spin-on) 2.65 liters \[0.7 U.S.gal\]Combination filter 2.65 liters \[0.7 U.S.gal\]

#### Oil Pan Capacity

G-Drive 28.4 to 36 liters \[7.5 to 9.5 U.S.gal\]Other applications 26 to 34 liters \[7.0 to 9.0 U.S.gal\]

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Total system capacity is the summation of the oil pan capacity at the high mark on the dipstick, the full flow oil filter capacity, and the capacity of any bypass filters that are used.
