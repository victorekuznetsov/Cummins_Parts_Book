### General Information

Use the following steps to determine the maximum fuel filter change intervals.

1. Select appropriate application interval table.
2. Determine fuel type used (Liquefied Natural Gas (LNG)/Compressed Natural Gas (CNG)).

To reduce the number of maintenance intervals, the fuel filter change interval can be extended and aligned with the lubricating oil change interval.

Below is the maximum fuel filter change interval limit for LNG and CNG.

### Intervals

| Tractor Application Intervals |  |  |  |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fuel Used | Kilometers | Hours | Months |
| LNG | 120,000 | 1600 | 12 |
| CNG | 60,000 | 800 | 6 |
