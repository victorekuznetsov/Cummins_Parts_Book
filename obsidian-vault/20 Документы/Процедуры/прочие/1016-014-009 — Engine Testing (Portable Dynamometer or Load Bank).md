### General Information

When it is **not** possible to load an engine immediately after rebuild or repair (for example, on-highway tractor that **must** be “bobtailed” for delivery, a standby generator or fire pump that can **not** be operated because of customer restrictions, a fire truck that can **not** be loaded with the water pump, and so forth), the engine **must** be run in on a chassis dynamometer, portable dynamometer, or load bank following the recommendations outlined in the preceding procedures.
