# Intake Manifold Air Temperature Sensor
**Датчик температуры воздуха во впускном коллекторе**

### General Information

The intake manifold air temperature (2) and intake manifold air pressure (1) is located on the top front half of the intake manifold. The ECM uses this information for the engine protection system.

![[19200345.png]]

### Remove

Lift up on the locking tab and pull the electrical connectors apart.

Remove the sensor from the engine.

![[19400434.png]]

### Install

Make sure the new sensor has an o-ring.

Install the new sensor into the engine. Tighten the sensor.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 14 n•m [124 in-lb]

![[19400435.png]]

Push the connectors together until they lock.

![[19400436.png]]
