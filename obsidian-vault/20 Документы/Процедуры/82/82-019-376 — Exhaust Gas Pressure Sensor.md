### General Information

The exhaust gas pressure sensor monitors the exhaust gas pressure and passes the information on to the engine control module (ECM) through the engine harness. If the exhaust gas pressure sensor reading becomes too low, it will cause a derate condition.

The exhaust gas pressure sensor is located on the exhaust gas recirculation (EGR) cooler on the exhaust side of the engine.

![[nobox.png]]

### Remove

Slide the locking tab on the connector sideways. Push down on the button toward the rear of the connector and disconnect it from the sensor.

Remove the sensor from the engine.

![[19d01891.png]]

### Install

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Make sure the new sensor has an o-ring.

Lubricate the o-ring with clean engine oil.

Install the sensor into the engine.

Use a deep well socket to tighten the sensor, being careful **not** to overtighten.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 18 n•m [159 in-lb]

![[19d01891.png]]

Push the connectors together until they lock.

Slide the locking tab to the lock position.

Operate the engine and verify proper operation.
