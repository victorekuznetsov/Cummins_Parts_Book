### General Information

**Introduction**

This procedure describes the proper method for placing an engine into long-term storage (more than 6 months), and returning a stored engine to service. The storage procedure applies to engines in running condition and applies whether the engine will remain in the equipment or will be removed for storage.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Do not remove the pressure cap from a hot engine. Wait until the coolant temperature is below 50°C \[ 122°F \] before removing the pressure cap. Heated coolant spray or steam can cause personal injury.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Coolant is toxic. Keep away from children and pets. If not reused, dispose of in accordance with local environmental regulations.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> To reduce the possibility of personal injury, avoid direct contact of hot oil with your skin.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Some state and federal agencies have determined that used engine oil can be carcinogenic and cause reproductive toxicity. Avoid inhalation of vapors, ingestion, and prolonged contact with used engine oil. If not reused, dispose of in accordance with local environmental regulations.

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Never use diesel fuel containing bio components for internal preservation of fuel system for engine storage. Fuel degradation may cause damage and lead to malfunction of fuel system components.

**Preparing Spare or Rebuilt Engine for Long Term Storage**

The steps below **only** apply to engines that are received directly from a Cummins Inc. assembly plant or a Master Rebuild Center.

- Apply a lightweight lubricant or rust preventative to machined surfaces that are **not** painted.
- Apply a lightweight lubricant or rust preventative to any internal cast surfaces, such as coolant inlet and outlet connections, that are visible and **not** painted.
- Cover engine and component openings with clean care caps or heavy paper and tape to prevent dirt and moisture from entering engine.
- Remove all external drive belts.
- Install appropriate desiccant on engine to absorb any moisture.
- Cover entire engine with plastic.
- Move engine or equipment to an appropriate storage area.

**Preparing an In-Use Engine for Long Term Storage**

To prepare an engine for long term storage, complete the following steps. Reference corresponding service procedures as necessary.

- If engine will be stored in equipment:
- If engine is to be removed from equipment, but is still presently installed:
- If engine has been removed from equipment:
- If engine is equipped with a marine heat exchanger:
- Cover all of engine and component openings with clean care caps or heavy paper and tape to prevent dirt and moisture from entering engine.
- Remove all external drive belts.
- Cover entire engine with plastic.
- Move engine or equipment to an appropriate storage area.

**Preparing Aftertreatment Components for Long Term Storage**

- For Engine with a displacement of 19 L and above refer to Service Bulletin, Storage of Individual UL2 Aftertreatment Diesel Exhaust Fluid (DEF) System Components, Bulletin 5504262 for instructions on preparing aftertreament components for long term storage. Refer to procedure 011-143 in Section 11 for instructions on Purging the Diesel Exhaust Fluid in preparation for storage.

Refer to procedure 011-143 Aftertreatment Diesel Exhaust Fluid Dosing Unit Manual Purge, for instructions on Purging the Diesel Exhaust Fluid in preparation for storage

**Removing Engine from Long Term Storage**

To remove the engine from long term storage, complete the following steps below. Also reference corresponding Service Manual procedures.

- Install new fuel and coolant filters, if equipped.
- Fill engine to high mark with engine lubricating oil.
- Cooling system procedures:
- Prime lubricating oil system.
- Install all external drive belts.
- Replace all spark plugs, if applicable.
- If engine is equipped with a marine heat exchanger:
- Verify all fuel supply hoses have been tightened.
- Verify all fuel supply and return shutoff valves are open.
- Install all aftertreatment components, if applicable.
- Start engine. See Normal Starting Procedure in Section 1 of corresponding Owners or Operation and Maintenance Manual.
- For electronically controlled engines, troubleshoot any active fault codes.

**Removing Aftertreatment Components for Long Term Storage**

For Engine with a displacement of 19 L and above refer to Service Bulletin, Storage of Individual UL2 Aftertreatment Diesel Exhaust Fluid (DEF) System Components, Bulletin 5504262 for instructions on preparing aftertreament components for removing from long term storage.
