### General Information

There are two variations of fan hub brackets in use. These are refered to as one and two piece brackets (below).

Both types of bracket come in two different thicknesses. The thin bracket is used on single damper engines and the thick bracket is used with dual damper engines.

All brackets have the same mounting hole positions.

![[fa6bkha.png]]

The two piece bracket can be identified by having a tensioning screw on the front of the bracket.

![[fa6suaa.png]]

Within the one piece thick bracket there are two different sizes of bracket available. These can be identified by the diameter of the fan hub shaft mounting holes. There are different hole sizes to allow for 5/8 (inch) or 3/4 (inch) fan hub shaft mounting capscrews.

Assemblies using the 5/8 (inch) fan hub shaft mounting capscrews can be identified by the capscrew having a twelve point head while the 3/4 (inch) fan hub shaft mounting capscrews have a six point head.

![[08s00049.png]]

### Remove

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> This component or assembly weighs greater than 23 kg \[50 lb\]. To prevent serious personal injury, be sure to have assistance or use appropriate lifting equipment to lift this component or assembly.

#### Fan Hub Bracket (Standard)

- Remove the capscrews.
- Remove the fan hub bracket (1).

![[fa6bkha.png]]

#### Fan Hub Bracket (Revised)

- Remove the mounting capscrews (15) and lock washers (14).
- Remove the fan hub bracket.

![[fa6suaa.png]]

### Clean and Inspect for Reuse

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> When using a steam cleaner, wear safety glasses or a face shield, as well as protective clothing. Hot steam can cause serious personal injury.

Use steam to clean the fan hub support.

![[08600150.png]]

Check the support for cracks or fretting.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> If the support **must** be replaced, be sure to install the correct part. All fan supports are **not** the same because of fan center location and spacing from the cylinder block.

![[fa6susa.png]]

### Install

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> This component or assembly weighs greater than 23 kg \[50 lb\]. To prevent serious personal injury, be sure to have assistance or use appropriate lifting equipment to lift this component or assembly.

#### Fan Hub Bracket (One Piece)

- Install guide pins in two of the fan support capscrew holes in the cylinder block for alignment.
- Install the fan hub bracket (1) over the guide pins. Install the lock washers and mounting capscrews.
- Remove the guide pins and install the remaining lock washers and capscrews.

Torque Value:

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Engines which have the one piece thick bracket with 3/4 (in) fan hub shaft mounting capscrews **must** have the fan hub bracket and fan hub shaft installed as an assembly. The fan hub can be assembled on engine to reduce the weight of the assembly. [[28-008-036-tr — Fan Hub, Belt Driven|Refer to Procedure 008-036 in Section 8.]]

![[fa6bkha.png]]

#### Fan Hub Bracket (Two Piece)

- Install the heavy nut (17) on the adjusting capscrew. Install the adjusting capscrew into the bottom of the boss in the fan bracket support (13).
- Install the retaining ring (18) into the groove of the adjusting capscrew.
- Install guide pins in two of the six fan support capscrew holes in the cylinder block for alignment.
- Install the fan bracket support (13) over the guide pins. Install the lock washers (14) and capscrews (15). Remove the guide pins and install the remaining lock washers and capscrews.
