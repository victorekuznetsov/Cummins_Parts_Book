Marine Thermostat Flow

1. Jacket water flow to thermostat housing
2. Exhaust manifold coolant flow to thermostat housing
3. Thermostats open
4. Coolant flow to keel cooler or heat exchanger
5. Thermostats closed
6. Coolant bypass to water jacket pump. **Note:** QSK50 Marine engines with CM2150 and in a keel cooled configuration will have an insert in place of one thermostat. When facing the engine, the insert will be located in the left-most position in the thermostat housing. This insert will cause the flow to that position to always follow the closed thermostat path.
