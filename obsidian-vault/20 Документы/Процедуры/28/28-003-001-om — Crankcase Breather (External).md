# Crankcase Breather (External)
**Сапун картера (наружный)**

> [!abstract] Процедура · `28-003-001-om`

> **Даты:** изменён 2011-01-07
> **Источник:** [QuickServe](https://quickserve.cummins.com/qs3/pubsys2/xml/en/procedures/28/28-003-001-om.html) · [PDF-оригинал](https://github.com/victorekuznetsov/Cummins_Parts_Book/raw/main/bulletins/procedures/28-003-001-om.pdf)

### Remove

Power Generation

Loosen the hose clamps (2) securing the four elbow hoses (1) to the crankcase breathers.

Disconnect the four elbow hoses from the crankcase breathers.

![[03s00024.png]]

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> The oil drain lines will need to be supported after removal to avoid kinking and other damage.

Remove the four oil drain lines from the crankcase breather drain connections (1).

![[03s00006.png]]

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> The blowby diffuser will need to be supported after removal to avoid kinking or other damage of the inlet hose.

Remove the two capscrews securing the blowby diffuser to the crankcase breather braces (2).

Remove the blowby diffuser (1).

![[03s00023.png]]

Remove the two capscrews securing the brace (1) to the mounting bracket (2).

Lift up on the brace to remove it from the mounting bracket slot.

Repeat the above steps to remove the second brace.

![[03s00010.png]]

Loosen, but do **not** completely remove, the three mounting capscrews securing the crankcase breather to the brace.

Remove the crankcase breathers in the order specified in the illustration by lifting up on the crankcase breather.

![[03s00011.png]]

### Disassemble

Power Generation

Disassemble the crankcase breather housing. Refer to Procedure 013-019 in Section A.

![[03s00044.png]]

### Assemble

Power Generation

Assemble the crankcase breather housing. Refer to Procedure 013-019 in Section A.

![[03s00044.png]]

### Install

Power Generation

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> There will be two crankcase breathers installed to each of the two braces.

Install, but do **not** tighten, the three capscrews with washers and nut to the crankcase breather housing. Align the crankcase breather capscrews with the three notches located on the brace, as shown in the illustration. Slide the capscrews down into the notches. Be sure that there is a washer on each side of the brace.

Tighten the capscrews to the required torque value.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 24 n•m [212 in-lb]

![[03s00016.png]]

Install, but do **not** tighten, the three capscrews with washers and nut to the crankcase breather housing. Align the crankcase breather capscrews with the three notches located on the brace, as shown in the illustration. Slide the capscrews down into the notches. Be sure that there is a washer on each side of the brace.

Tighten the capscrews to the required torque value.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 24 n•m [212 in-lb]

![[03s00017.png]]

With the crankcase breathers attached to the brace, hang the brace in the slot (1) at the top of the mounting bracket.

Secure the brace to the mounting bracket by installing two capscrews (2) with washers (3) through the two holes located at the bottom of the brace.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 24 n•m [212 in-lb]

![[03s00018.png]]

With the crankcase breathers attached to the brace, hang the second brace in the slot (1) at the top of the mounting bracket.

Secure the brace to the bracket by installing two capscrews (2) with washers (3) through the two holes located at the bottom of the brace.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 24 n•m [212 in-lb]

![[03s00019.png]]

Use two capscrews with washers and nuts to attach the blowby diffuser (1) to the crankcase breather braces (2).

Tighten the capscrews.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 48 n•m [35 ft-lb]

![[03s00023.png]]

Install the free end of each elbow hose (1) to the inlet port of each of the crankcase breathers. Install the four hose clamps (2) and tighten.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 8 n•m [71 in-lb]

![[03s00024.png]]

Connect the four oil drain lines to the oil drain barb fittings (1) of the four crankcase breather element housings.

![[03s00006.png]]
