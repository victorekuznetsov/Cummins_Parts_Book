### Preparatory Steps

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Batteries can emit explosive gases. To reduce the possibility of personal injury, always ventilate the compartment before servicing the batteries. To reduce the possibility of arcing, remove the negative (-) battery cable first and attach the negative (-) battery cable last.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Some state and federal agencies have determined that used engine oil can be carcinogenic and cause reproductive toxicity. Avoid inhalation of vapors, ingestion, and prolonged contact with used engine oil. If not reused, dispose of in accordance with local environmental regulations.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> To reduce the possibility of personal injury, avoid direct contact of hot oil with your skin.

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Use caution when draining oil or replacing filters that the oil is not spilled into the bilge area. The oil and filters must be disposed of in accordance with local environmental regulations.

- Disconnect the batteries. [[99-013-009 — Battery Cables and Connections|Refer to Procedure 013-009 in Section 13.]]
- Remove the lubricating oil filters. [[28-007-013-tr — Lubricating Oil Filter (Spin-On)|Refer to Procedure 007-013 in Section 7.]]

### Disassemble

with Mechanically Actuated Injector

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> The parts are under spring pressure. The parts can move suddenly with enough force to cause personal injury. Use a tool that will control the parts firmly.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The K50 cast iron oil filter head is shown as an example. The procedure for the K38 cast iron filter head is the same. The filter head housing is the only part that is different for K38 and K50 engines.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Newer filter heads have a straight thread plug and copper washer. Older filter heads can have a heavy plain washer or a retainer tab. The newer filter head does **not** have the stop and o-ring.

> Engines with inboard piston cooling nozzles do **not** require the piston cooling regulator.

### Clean and Inspect for Reuse

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> When using solvents, acids, or alkaline materials for cleaning, follow the manufacturer's recommendations for use. Wear goggles and protective clothing to reduce the risk of personal injury.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Wear appropriate eye and face protection when using compressed air. Flying debris and dirt can cause personal injury.

If the gaskets are stuck to the filter head assembly, use a gasket scraper. Remove the two mounting gaskets.

Use solvent to clean the lubricating oil filter head assembly.

Dry with compressed air.

![[lf6hdea.png]]

Check the lubricating oil filter head for cracks or other damage. Check the visible portion of the valve plungers for signs of scoring or other damage.

![[lf6hdsa.png]]

Check the surfaces of the pressure regulator and valve plungers and their mating bores in the housings or the filter head. The part **must** be replaced if it is **not** possible to remove the scratches or marks with a fine crocus cloth.

![[lf600sd.png]]

Measure the free length of the spring. Use a spring tester. Measure the force of the spring (7) at the working height (6).

### Assemble

with Mechanically Actuated Injector

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> **Only** a portion of the filter head is illustrated for clarity.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Do **not** remove the adapters unless they are damaged or loose.

Check the filter adapters (24).

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Four types of adapters have been used. The adapter that requires a lock plate (25), and the adapter with the external hex (26), an adapter with internal grooves (27) and an adapter with increased engagement. The internal grooves allow the use of a section of \[0.750 in\] square keystock to turn and tighten the part.

If the adapter **must** be replaced, Loctite™ 270 or equivalent, **must** be put on the threads that contact the threads in the filter head.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 95 n•m [70 ft-lb]

If the older adapters with the external hex are installed, put Loctite™ 270 on the threads.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 200 n•m [148 ft-lb]

![[lf6hdse.png]]

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> All K38 and K50 engines produced after July, 1990, ESN first 33118127, have an adapter fitted with increased thread engagement. This change was made to confirm that a minimum of three full threads are engaged.

![[lf6adga.png]]

Install the pipe plug (23) or the restriction plug (22).

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 20 n•m [15 ft-lb]

![[lf6hdfl.png]]

Install the plunger in the filter head bore.

Be sure the part moves freely in the housing. If the part does **not** move freely, check for debris and burrs on the part and assemble again.

Use clean engine oil. Lubricate the o-ring.

Install the pressure regulator spring (19).

> 47 n•m [35 ft-lb]

### Install

with Mechanically Actuated Injector

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> This component or assembly weighs greater than 23 kg \[50 lb\]. To prevent serious personal injury, be sure to have assistance or use appropriate lifting equipment to lift this component or assembly.

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> The oil filter head can be installed on either engine bank. Verify the lubricating oil transfer tube has been installed correctly. The oil filter head must be installed OPPOSITE the bank to where the oil transfer tube is connected. If the oil filter is installed on the wrong bank, the engine will be damaged severely because of lack of lubrication.

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> The gaskets must be installed as shown or the engine can be damaged because of lack of lubrication.

In the illustration, the filter head is being installed on the left bank.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> **Only** a K38 engine with an oil filter head manufactured from aluminum requires two studs.

Install the two studs (1).

The use of guide bolts will aid the assembly procedure.

The gasket (3) has a rib down the center. The gasket is installed for the right end **only** of the oil filter head.

Install the gaskets (2) and (3).

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Engines with outboard piston cooling nozzles **must** have two gaskets (2 and 3) installed, as described and illustrated above. Engines with inboard piston cooling nozzles can have the same gasket (3) installed to both locations. Make sure all six mounting holes are visible through each gasket.

Install the oil filter head (4).

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Two nuts are required on a K38 engine **only** with a filter head manufactured from aluminum.

Install the 12 lock washers and capscrews.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 47 n•m [35 ft-lb]

### Finishing Steps

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Batteries can emit explosive gases. To reduce the possibility of personal injury, always ventilate the compartment before servicing the batteries. To reduce the possibility of arcing, remove the negative (-) battery cable first and attach the negative (-) battery cable last.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Some state and federal agencies have determined that used engine oil can be carcinogenic and cause reproductive toxicity. Avoid inhalation of vapors, ingestion, and prolonged contact with used engine oil. If not reused, dispose of in accordance with local environmental regulations.

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Use caution when draining oil or replacing filters that the oil is not spilled into the bilge area. The oil and filters must be disposed of in accordance with local environmental regulations.

- Install the lubricating oil filters. [[28-007-013-tr — Lubricating Oil Filter (Spin-On)|Refer to Procedure 007-013 in Section 7.]]
- Connect the bypass filter hoses, if equipped.
- Connect the batteries. [[99-013-009 — Battery Cables and Connections|Refer to Procedure 013-009 in Section 13.]]
- Operate the engine to normal operating temperature. Check for leaks.
