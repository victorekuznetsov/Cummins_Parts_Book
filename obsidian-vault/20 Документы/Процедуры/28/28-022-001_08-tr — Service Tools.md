> ### Cooling System
>
> | **Tool Number** ST-647 | **Gear Puller** Used to remove the water pump pulley and impeller. |  |
> |---|---|---|
> | **Tool Number** 3376326 | **Pulley Installation Tool** Used to install drive pulleys. |  |
> | **Tool Number** 3375318 | **Water Pump Bearing Mandrel** Used to press the bearing and shaft into the water pump body. |  |
> | **Tool Number** 3375319 | **Water Pump Bearing Mandrel** Used to install the front water pump oil seal into the water pump body. |  |
> | **Tool Number** 3375326 | **Bearing Separator** Used to hold the bearing when removing it from the shaft. |  |
> | **Tool Number** 3375411 | **Thermostat Seal Driver** Used to install thermostat seal. |  |
> | **Tool Number** 3824836 | **Water Pump Seal Installer** Used to install and position the seal onto the water pump drive shaft without damage to the seal. |  |
> | **Tool Number** 3375066 | **Pipe Sealant** Used when installing pipe plugs or cup plugs on the engine in order to prevent leaks. |  |
> | **Tool Number** 3377462 | **Digital Optical Tachometer** Used to measure the rpm of the fan. |  |
> | **Tool Number** 3377464 | **Reflective Tape** Used with optical tachometer, Part Number 3377462, to measure the rpm of the fan. |  |
> | **Tool Number** CC-2602 | **Cooling System Test Kit - Fleetguard®** Used to measure supplemental cooling additive (SCA) concentration. |  |
> | **Tool Number** 1650 | **Snap-On Pry Bar** Used to remove thermostat seals. |  |
> | **Tool Number** 3164794 | **Torque Wrench** Used to tighten capscrews 13 to 136 N•m \[10 to 100 ft-lb\]. |  |
