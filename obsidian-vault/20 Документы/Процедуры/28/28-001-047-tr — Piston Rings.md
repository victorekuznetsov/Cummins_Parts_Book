### Remove

Use piston ring expander, Part Number ST-1269, or equivalent, to remove the piston rings.

![[pi6rihb.png]]

### Measure

with Mechanically Actuated Injector

Make certain that the liners are within specification prior to measuring piston ring gaps. [[28-001-028-tr — Cylinder Liner|Refer to Procedure 001-028 in Section 1.]]

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Once a set of piston rings has been measured against a cylinder liner, they **must only** be used on a piston that is to be inserted into that liner. If the piston rings are subsequently required to be used in another cylinder location, the ring gap measuring process described here **must** be carried out again in the new cylinder liner.

![[06400092.png]]

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Engines built after Engine Serial Number (ESN) 33175467 have a revised top piston ring and the top piston ring gap should be measured in accordance with the specification in the 'Measure Electronically Actuated Injector' section. Engines that have had rebuild work carried out in the past may have been upfitted with the revised top piston ring. Check the part number on the top piston ring to make sure the correct tolerances for the piston ring gap are being utilized.

To check the ring gaps, place an ink mark on the side of a serviceable piston (with no piston rings fitted) which is 100 mm \[3.94 in\] from the crown.

Install a piston ring in the top of a cylinder liner that meets specifications and will be used with the piston rings having their gaps measured. Use the top part of the piston to position the ring in the liner. Drop the piston down until the 100 mm \[3.94 in\] marking on the piston reaches the top of the liner. Carefully remove the piston and carry out the ring gap measurement.

![[01600639.png]]

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> If the ring gap is not within the minimum specification, do not attempt to adjust the end gap by filing or grinding. The chrome plating on the ring will be damaged and the ring will fail. Check to make sure the cylinder bore inside diameter is correct. If the end gap remains less than the minimum specification, replace the piston ring.

Use a feeler gauge to check the ring gap.

| Piston Ring Gap |  |  |  |
|---|---|---|---|
|  | mm |  | in |
| Top | 0.64 | MIN | 0.025 |
|  | 1.02 | MAX | 0.040 |
| Intermediate | 0.64 | MIN | 0.025 |
|  | 1.02 | MAX | 0.040 |
| Oil | 0.30 | MIN | 0.015 |
|  | 0.76 | MAX | 0.030 |

If the piston ring is **not** within specification, the piston ring **must** be replaced.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Add 0.08 mm \[0.003 in\] to the maximum limit for each 0.03 mm \[0.002 in\] that the liner bore inside diameter exceeds 158.75 mm \[6.25 in\].

### Install

Do **not** install the piston rings until the pistons are installed on the rods.

The piston rings are marked with the part number. The shipping package is marked with the location. The part number on the ring **must** be positioned toward the top of the piston.

Use a ring expander, Part Number ST-1269 or equivalent, to install the piston rings.

![[pi6rihb.png]]

Rotate the rings until the gaps are positioned as shown.

Do **not** align the gap of a ring with the piston pin bore.

The gap in the oil ring expander (1) **must** be turned 180 degrees opposite the gap on the oil ring (2).
