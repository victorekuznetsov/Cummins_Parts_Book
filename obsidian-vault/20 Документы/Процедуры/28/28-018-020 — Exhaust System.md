75 mm Hg \[3.0 in Hg\].

Industrial (K2000E)

38 mm Hg \[1.5 in Hg\]

KT(A)38, KTTA38, KTA50

152 mm \[6 inch\]

KTTA50

203 mm \[8 inch\]
