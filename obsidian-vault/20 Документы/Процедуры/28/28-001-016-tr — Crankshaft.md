### Clean and Inspect for Reuse

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> When using solvents, acids or alkaline materials for cleaning, follow the manufacturer's recommendations for use. Wear goggles and protective clothing to reduce the possibility of personal injury.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Wear appropriate eye and face protection when using compressed air. Flying debris and dirt can cause personal injury.

Clean the crankshaft using solvent and dry with compressed air.

Inspect the crankshaft for nicks or scratches. If a mark can **not** be felt with a fingernail, there is no value in polishing the crankshaft.

![[ks8bdec.png]]

Use a Scotch-Brite™ 7448 abrasive pad, Part Number 3823258, or equivalent, to remove discoloration or light scratches from the machined surfaces.

The crankshaft **must** be cleaned again if this polishing is performed.

![[ks8bded.png]]

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> When using solvents, acids or alkaline materials for cleaning, follow the manufacturer's recommendations for use. Wear goggles and protective clothing to reduce the possibility of personal injury.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Wear appropriate eye and face protection when using compressed air. Flying debris and dirt can cause personal injury.

The K2000E, K1800E, and K1500E engines use a crankshaft having a 77.47 mm \[3.050 in\] diameter bolt circle in the nose. The threads are rolled, **not** cut, as on the earlier K50 crankshafts.

To clean the rolled threads, flush with solvent. Dry the threads with compressed air. If additional cleaning is required, brush with a nylon bristle brush.

![[ks600sa.png]]

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> When using solvents, acids or alkaline materials for cleaning, follow the manufacturer's recommendations for use. Wear goggles and protective clothing to reduce the possibility of personal injury.

Use solvent and a bristle brush to clean all oil drillings.

Use a light preservative oil to lubricate the part to prevent rust.

![[ks6sheb.png]]

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> When using solvents, acids or alkaline materials for cleaning, follow the manufacturer's recommendations for use. Wear goggles and protective clothing to reduce the possibility of personal injury.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> New crankshafts are coated with a heavy preservative. Use solvent to thoroughly remove the coating. Brush or flush the packing debris from the oil drillings before installing the crankshaft in the engine.

Use a hone stone to polish the outside diameter surface at the front and rear oil seal locations, the flywheel mounting location, and the vibration damper location. Remove all small scratches and grooves.

![[ks6shea.png]]

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> When using solvents, acids or alkaline materials for cleaning, follow the manufacturer's recommendations for use. Wear goggles and protective clothing to reduce the possibility of personal injury.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Wear appropriate eye and face protection when using compressed air. Flying debris and dirt can cause personal injury.

Use solvent to clean the crankshaft.

Dry with compressed air.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Make sure to blow out the threaded holes on each end of the crankshaft and the oil drillings. Use a light preservative oil to prevent rust during engine rebuild. Tape over the thread holes.

If a counterweight **must** be replaced, the crankshaft **must** be balanced again. See the Alternative Repair Manual, Bulletin 3379035.

Newer crankshafts contain a ring dowel for the counterweights. The counterweights are **not** interchangeable between the newer and older crankshafts.

Inspect the crankshaft seal contact areas for wear. The wear groove is acceptable if it is continuous and no more than 0.12 mm \[0.05 in\] deep.

If the wear groove depth is greater than the specification above, the crankshaft **must** be replaced.

If the wear groove depth is less than the specification above, a wear sleeve can be installed to repair the surface. Use the following procedure for front crankshaft seal installation with a wear sleeve. [[28-001-023-tr — Crankshaft Seal, Front|Refer to Procedure 001-023 in Section 1.]]

Installation of a repair sleeve to the front crankshaft nose is recommended during rebuild to protect the crankshaft nose.

A repair sleeve is **not** available for the rear crankshaft nose as it is already incorporated into the rear crankshaft seal.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The crankshaft nose must not be repaired using any metal spray or welding process.

### Magnetic Crack Inspect

Use a magnetic particle testing machine.

Perform the head shot and inspection method. Then perform the coil shot and inspection method.

Adjust the machine to 1800 ampere VDC or rectified VAC.

Use the continuous method. Wet **only** 1/3 of the crankshaft at a time.

Check the crankshaft for cracks.

![[ks8bdsa.png]]

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Ampere turn is an electrical current of one ampere flowing through the coil, multiplied by the number of turns in the coil.

Use the coil shot method. Use a coil that is a minimum of 514 mm \[20.250 in\] in diameter.

Use the continuous method to apply coil shot.

| Amperage - (Ampere Turns) |  |
|---|---|
| Minimum | 4500 VDC or Rectified VAC |
| Maximum | 5000 VDC or Rectified VAC |

Check the crankshaft for cracks.

![[ks8bdsb.png]]

Limits of Acceptance - Open Indications:

An open indication is visible to the eye after the wetting operation has been completed. An indication below the surface is **not** visible to the eye after the wetting operation has been completed. An indication below the surface can be seen with the use of the ultraviolet light that is part of the machine.

Do **not** use the crankshaft if:

- There is an indication in the fillet or in the shaded area
- There is an indication that passes through the 45 degree diagonal from the oil hole or goes into the oil hole chamfer
- There is an indication that is longer than 6 mm \[0.250 inch\]
- There are more than four indications on one journal.

![[ks8bdna.png]]

Limits of Acceptance - Indications Below the Surface:

Do **not** use the crankshaft if:

- There is an indication in the fillet or in the shaded area that is in a circumferential direction.
- There is an indication in a circumferential direction that is longer than 25.4 mm \[1.00 in\].
- There is an indication in an axial direction that is longer than 9.5 mm \[0.375 in\].
- There is an indication that is closer than 1.5 mm \[0.0625 in\] to an oil hole chamfer.
- There is an indication that passes through the 45 degree diagonal from the oil hole.

![[ks8bdnb.png]]

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно

Demagnetize the crankshaft.

Use steam to clean the crankshaft and the oil drillings.

### Bend and Twist Inspect

The crankshaft straightness is determined by the amount of total runout and the adjacent journal runout.

The following definitions will help in understanding the procedure:

![[10600725.png]]

- Journal Runout: The top indicator reading (total sweep of the needle) of the main bearing journal as the crankshaft is rotated one complete revolution (360 degrees). Total runout refers to the maximum defection taken at the journal in the center.
- Adjacent Journal Runout: The relationship of the top indicator reading of a main bearing journal as it is rotated on a common axis to the top indicator reading of an adjacent journal.

Adjacent Journal Runout is often referred to as the step runout, the bearing-to-bearing runout, or the journal-to-journal runout.

![[ks8bdtb.png]]

- Clock Position: The location of the journal at the highest top indicator reading point. Compare its angular relationship with the number 1 crankshaft pin as viewed from the front of the crankshaft. In this example, the crankshaft pin is at the 8 o'clock position. This is the clock position of the journal being measured.

![[ks8bdtc.png]]

Measurement of crankshaft straightness with the specified accuracy can **only** be achieved if the crankshaft:

- Is correctly supported
- Is at thermal equilibrium
- Can be rotated smoothly.

These requirements can **not** be met by measuring the crankshaft while supported by bearing shells in a cylinder block. Rotation of the crankshaft with such an arrangement will lead to errors as the result of lateral and vertical displacement arising from curvature and clearance in the main bearings.

#### Measurement Environment

- Measurement **must** be conducted in a temperature controlled room at a temperature of 20°C \[ 68°F \] +/- 1 degree.
- The crankshaft to be measured **must** be stored in this room for at least twenty four hours to allow the crankshaft to stabilize at room temperature.
- Temperature of the crankshaft **must** be verified before measurements starts, and recorded on the measurement data worksheet included in this document. See measurement data worksheet section 1.8. below.

#### Measurement Setup

- The measurement table **must** be rated to support at least 450 kg \[ 992 lb \] and provide a stable, flat surface that allows V-blocks to be aligned and secured with the crankshaft centerline in a straight horizontal position.

The use of a granite measurement table and roller V-blocks are recommended. See Figure 1. Roller V-blocks have lower rolling resistance than standard V-blocks when rotating the crankshaft during measurement process.

![[01k00101.png]]

Figure 1.

#### Figure 1: Crankshaft on Roller V-blocks

- A motorized drive is preferred to be used to rotate the crankshaft smoothly over the full 360 degrees of rotation. Direct manual rotation **must** be done very carefully to provide the smooth motion necessary for consistent measurements.
- The height of V-blocks **must** be equal (Z-axis). The height difference between the V-blocks **must** be much smaller than part specified tolerance.

Example: If straightness specification is less than 0.06 mm \[ 0.002 in \] then the height difference **must** be 0.01 mm \[ 0.0004 in \] or less. The V-blocks height **must** be verified in the contact points of V-blocks with the crankshaft journal using a calibrated shaft or bar. Measurements can be made with a mechanical dial indicator or an electronic precision measurement system.

- The measuring device used **must** have a valid calibration certificate and **must** have resolution, repeatability, and accuracy (relationship of the real dimension to what the measurement device indicates) appropriate for the straightness specification being evaluated.

Place two V-blocks on a flat surface. Support the crankshaft on the V-blocks on main journal number 2 and main journal number 8, as shown.

Verify that the crankshaft is free **only** to rotate, with no vertical or lateral motion as the result of clearance in the rollers or rocking of the V-blocks.

![[01s00116.png]]

Use a dial indicator to position the indicator so the stem touches the bearing journal being measured at the journal centerline.

The point of measurement chosen **must** be used for measuring all bearing journals.

The K50 crankshaft has nine main bearing journals (not shown).

The K38 crankshaft has seven main bearing journals, as illustrated.

![[ks8bdte.png]]

Rotate the crankshaft and measure the top indicator reading at each bearing journal. Record the value and the clock position for each journal.

![[ks8bdtf.png]]

Check the part number to determine if the crankshaft is a fully fillet hardened type, or is a non-fully fillet hardened type. Refer to Procedure 001-999 in Section F.

A fully fillet hardened or shot-peened crankshaft **must** be discarded if the total runout is **not** within specifications. A crankshaft that has no fillet treatment can be straightened. Refer to the Alternative Repair Manual, Bulletin 3379035.
