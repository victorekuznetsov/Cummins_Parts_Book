### Preparatory Steps

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> To reduce the possibility of personal injury, avoid direct contact of hot oil with your skin.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Some state and federal agencies have determined that used engine oil can be carcinogenic and cause reproductive toxicity. Avoid inhalation of vapors, ingestion, and prolonged contact with used engine oil. If not reused, dispose in accordance with local environmental regulations.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> This component or assembly weighs greater than 23 kg \[50 lb\]. To prevent serious personal injury, be sure to have assistance or use appropriate lifting equipment to lift this component or assembly.

- Drain the oil. Refer to Procedure 007-037.
- Remove the lubricating oil pan. Refer to Procedure [[28-007-025-tr — Lubricating Oil Pan|007-025]].
- Remove the oil pan adapter. Refer to Procedure [[28-007-027-tr — Lubricating Oil Pan Adapter|007-027]].
- Remove the oil pump, if necessary. Refer to Procedure [[28-007-031-tr — Lubricating Oil Pump|007-031]].
- Remove the block stiffener plate.

### Clean and Inspect for Reuse

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> When using solvents, acids, or alkaline materials for cleaning, follow the manufacturer's recommendations for use. Wear goggles and protective clothing to reduce the possibility of personal injury.

Use a scraper or a fibrous, abrasive pad, such as Scotch-Brite™ 7477, or equivalent, and solvent. Clean the top deck of the block. Do **not** allow any dirt into the cylinder. The surface **must** be free of dirt, oil, and gasket material, but does **not** have to look like new metal.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The counterbore ledge **must** be clean and dry. The sealant used in assembly will **not** dry if oil is present.

Check the top surface of the block for wear. If fretting damage is present in an area where a head gasket seal ring or a grommet makes contact, the surface **must** be repaired. Refer to the Alternative Repair Manual, Bulletin 3379035.

Fretting damage in any other area is acceptable **only** if it does **not** change the protrusion measurement of the counterbore or liner.

![[01600341.png]]

Inspect the dowel pins for damage.

Use the dowel pin extractor, Part Number ST-1134, or equivalent, to remove the pins if they are damaged.

![[01400279.png]]

Inspect the counterbore ledge for pitted areas. The ledge **must** be machined if pitting is present.

![[01600342.png]]

Check for cracks.

Use a crack detection kit, Part Number 337532 or equivalent, and check the counter bore ledges for cracks. Circumferential cracks are acceptable if they do **not** extend more than 1/2 of the distance across the ledge.

![[ck8brnb.png]]

Cracks that extend into a capscrew hole are **not** acceptable. The block can be used if a capscrew hole thread insert is installed in the capscrew hole. See the Alternative Repair Manual, Bulletin 3379035.

![[01600342.png]]

Inspect the counterbore

To inspect the block properly, the counterbore design **must** be determined.

Terms used are:

1. Upper counterbore inside diameter
2. Counterbore depth
3. Lower counterbore inside diameter
4. Packing ring bore
5. Counterbore ledge
6. Counterbore radius.

Measure the inside diameter of the upper counterbore (1). The point of measurement **must** be within 2.5 mm \[0.100 inch\] from the top surface of the block.

The diameter of the upper counterbore **must** be round within 0.025 mm \[0.001 inch\]. If the measurement is **not** within specification, determine if the block can be machined for oversize liners. See Procedure cylinder block - Cut for Oversize Liners.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The upper counterbore **must** be no more than 0.025 mm \[0.001 inch\] larger than the cylinder liner flange, or no more than 0.076 mm \[0.003 inch\] smaller than the cylinder liner flange.

![[ck6brsc.png]]

Measure the inside diameter of the lower counterbore (3). The point of measurement **must** be within 2.5 mm \[0.100 inch\] from the top of the counterbore ledge. Measure at two locations, 90 degrees apart

The diameter of the lower counterbore **must** be within 0.025 mm \[0.001 inch\].

![[ck6brsd.png]]

If the diameter of the lower counterbore is larger than the specification, the block can be used **only** if the outside diameter of the liner flange is 0.025 mm \[0.001 inch\] larger than the inside diameter of the upper counterbore. If there is **not** a press fit between the liner and the upper counterbore, the block **must** be machined for oversize liners (dimension 1).

![[01600344.png]]

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Be sure the micrometer contacts the flat surface of the counterbore ledge. It must not touch the counterbore face.

Use a depth micrometer. Measure the counterbore depth at the four places shown. The four measurements **mustnot** vary more than 0.025 mm \[0.001 inch\]. If the measurement are **not** in accordance with the specifications, the counterbore ledge **must** be machined. In this case, see the Procedure 001-058.

![[ck4brta.png]]

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Be sure the indicator does not contact the counterbore face (radius) on a block that does not have a double undercut.

Use Part Number ST-537 Dial Depth Gauge. Measure the angle of the counterbore ledge at no less than four locations around the ledge. One measurement at each location **must** be as close as possible to the counterbore edge.

![[ck4brtb.png]]

It is acceptable if the counterbore ledge has an angle no greater than described as follows:

- If the distance measured near the counterbore radius (face) is the same or no more than 0.036 mm \[0.0014 inch\] farther than the distance measured near the counterbore edge, the counterbore ledge is acceptable.
- The counterbore ledge angle is **not** acceptable if the distance near the counterbore edge is farther than the distance measured near the counterbore radius (face), In this case, the ledge **must** be machined. Reference Alternative Repair Manual, Bulletin 3379035 for repairing the packing ring bore with a bushing.

![[ck8brna.png]]

Closely inspect the chamfer at the top of the packing ring bore.

Excessive pitting of the chamfer or the ring bore is **not** acceptable. See the Alternative Repair Manual, Bulletin 3379035 for repairing the packing ring bore with a bushing.

Measure the diameter of the packing ring bore.

### Leak Test

Pressurize the engine cooling system. Refer to Procedure [[28-008-018-tr — Cooling System|008-018]].

Apply the air pressure 15 minutes before inspecting the cylinder liner seats. Make sure the system is holding air pressure before beginning inspection.

| Measurements |  |  |
|---|---|---|
|  | kpa | psi |
| Air Pressure | 138 | 20 |

![[ck800ka.png]]

Inspect the outside diameter of the cylinder liners and the area below the cylinder liner seats in the cylinder block for coolant leaks.

If a leak is found, remove the cylinder liner(s). Inspect the sealing ring and cylinder liner. Refer to Procedure [[28-001-028-tr — Cylinder Liner|001-028]].

If a leak is found in the cylinder liner seat. Refer to Procedure [[28-001-028-tr — Cylinder Liner|001-028]].

Inspect the cylinder block liner counterbore area.

![[cy8cysb.png]]

### Finishing Steps

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> This component or assembly weighs greater than 23 kg \[50 lb\]. To prevent serious personal injury, be sure to have assistance or use appropriate lifting equipment to lift this component or assembly.

- Install the block stiffener plate.
- Install the oil pump, if removed. Refer to Procedure [[28-007-031-tr — Lubricating Oil Pump|007-031]].
- Install the oil suction and transfer tubes. Refer to Procedure 007-035.
- Install the oil pan adapter. Refer to Procedure [[28-007-027-tr — Lubricating Oil Pan Adapter|007-027]].
- Install the oil pan. Refer to Procedure [[28-007-025-tr — Lubricating Oil Pan|007-025]].
- Fill the engine with clean engine oil. Refer to Procedure 007-037.
- Operate the engine until it reaches a temperature of 80°C \[176°F\].
- Check for coolant and lubricating oil leaks.
