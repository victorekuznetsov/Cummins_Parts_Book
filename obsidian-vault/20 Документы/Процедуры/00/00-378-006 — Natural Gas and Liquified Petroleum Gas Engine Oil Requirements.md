**Re-Refined Oils**

New commercially available re-refined or recycled engine oils can be used **only** if the oil brand name and viscosity grade are found on the QuickServe™ oil registration list. Used reclaimed oils or new re-refined/recycled engine oils **not** found on the oil registration lists should **never** be used.

**Friction Modifiers**

Engine oil that contains friction modifiers should **not** be used unless the oil brand name and viscosity grade are found on the QuickServe™ oil registration list. Friction modifiers can compete with the anti-wear additives in your engine oil to compromise wear performance.

**Break-In Oils**

Special break-in oils are **not** recommended for use in any Cummins® engine. These oils are **not** designed to meet our wear standards and may negatively affect durability.

**Top-Treat or Aftermarket Oil Additives**

Cummins® neither approves or disapproves of any additives **not** manufactured or sold by Cummins Inc. or its subsidiaries. Engine malfunction or performance problems which result from the use of such additives are **not** warrantable by Cummins Inc.
