# Lubricating Oil Suction Tube (Block-Mounted)
**Маслозаборная трубка (на блоке)**

### Preparatory Steps

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Some state and federal agencies have determined that used engine oil can be carcinogenic and cause reproductive toxicity. Avoid inhalation of vapors, ingestion, and prolonged contact with used engine oil. If not reused, dispose of in accordance with local environmental regulations.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> To reduce the possibility of personal injury, avoid direct contact of hot oil with your skin.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Wear appropriate eye and face protection when using compressed air. Flying debris and dirt can cause personal injury.

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Do not spill or drain fuel into the bilge area when disconnecting or removing fuel lines, replacing filters, and priming the fuel system. Do not drop or throw filter elements into the bilge area. The fuel and fuel filters must be disposed of in accordance with local environmental regulations.

- Drain the oil. Refer to Procedure [[20-007-037 — Lubricating Oil System|007-037]].
- Remove the oil pan. Refer to Procedure 007-025.
- Remove the oil pan adapter cover. Refer to Procedure 007-027

![[ck800wa.png]]

### Remove

Standard Suction Tube.

Remove the three capscrews from the suction tube.

Remove the oil pan suction tube.

Remove and discard the gasket.

![[07400017.png]]

Deep Sump Suction Tube.

Remove the capscrews from the p-clips, bracket, and flange connection to the engine block.

Remove the oil pan suction tube.

Remove and discard the gasket.

![[07400206.png]]

### Clean and Inspect for Reuse

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> When using solvents, acids or alkaline materials for cleaning, follow the manufacturer's recommendations for use. Wear goggles and protective clothing to reduce the possibility of personal injury.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Wear appropriate eye and face protection when using compressed air. Flying debris and dirt can cause personal injury.

Clean the oil suction tube with solvent and dry with compressed air.

Inspect the oil suction tube for cracks or other damage.

If the oil suction tube is cracked or otherwise damaged, the oil suction tube **must** be replaced.

![[07400355.png]]

Use a putty knife to clean the gasket surfaces.

![[op9tbeb.png]]

### Install

Standard Suction Tube.

Install the gasket, standard suction tube, and capscrews.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 60 n•m [44 ft-lb]

Tighten the clamp capscrew.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 35 n•m [24 ft-lb]

![[07400017.png]]

Deep Sump Suction Tube.

Install the gasket, deep sump suction tube, brackets, and P-clips. Tighten the two suction tube connector capscrews.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 60 n•m [44 ft-lb]

Tighten the bracket and P-clip capscrews.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 41 n•m [30 ft-lb]

![[07400206.png]]

### Finishing Steps

- Install the oil pan. Refer to Procedure 007-025.
- Install the oil pan adapter cover. Refer to Procedure 007-027.
- Operate the engine and check for leaks.
