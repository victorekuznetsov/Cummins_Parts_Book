### Remove

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> The bypass and combination filters both have the same threads. Verify correct filter is used for replacement to avoid damage to the engine.

The external appearance of the full-flow (1), the bypass (2), and the combination (3) filters are the same. The accompanying picture identifies the differences among the three filters.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The full-flow filter contains \[1 1/2-16 in\] threads. The bypass and combination filters contain \[2 1/4-12 in\] threads.

![[07400059.png]]

A combination oil filter is used on **most** engines. The upper section of the filter contains the full-flow filter element, while the lower section contains the bypass element.

![[lf1etgc.png]]

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Use caution when draining oil or replacing filters that the oil is not spilled or drained into the bilge area. The oil and oil filters must be disposed of in accordance with local environmental regulations.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The following illustrations show the combination oil filter. Use the same procedure when changing the remote bypass oil filters.

Use an oil filter wrench, Part Number 3375049, or equivalent. Remove the oil filters.

Discard the filters if they are **not** needed for a failure analysis.

![[07400045.png]]
