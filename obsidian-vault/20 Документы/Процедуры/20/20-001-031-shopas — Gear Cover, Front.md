### Install

Remove the old pan adapter gasket at perforations. The gasket is designed to be removed at the perforation.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The steel core gasket can **not** be cut without causing damage to pan adapter.

Trim the gasket to the correct width since the spacer plate is already installed.

Install the service gasket, Part Number 3042591, on top of the oil pan adapter. Use a spray adhesive to hold it in position. Use Cummins® sealant, Part Number 3823494, at the joints.

![[01400105.png]]

This illustration shows the capscrew length requirements for the front cover on all engines, except rail applications.

![[01400038.png]]

This illustration shows the capscrew length requirements for the front cover on rail application engines.

![[17400012.png]]

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> This component or assembly weighs greater than 23 kg \[50 lb\]. To prevent serious personal injury, be sure to have assistance or use appropriate lifting equipment to lift this component or assembly.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Do **not** tighten the capscrews until the master dowel pin is installed.

The use of guide bolts will aid the assembly.

Install the front cover gasket, cover, and capscrews.

![[06400075.png]]

Put the clamping plate in position and begin the installation of the capscrew.

![[gc4plha.png]]

Use a \[5/16-8 x 3 inch\] capscrew and a mallet. Install the threaded master dowel. Drive the dowel in until it touches the bottom of the hole in the block.

![[01400109.png]]

Tighten the mounting capscrews in the sequence shown.

Sequence Numbers 1, 2, and 3 capscrews are located at the back of the spacer plate.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> Grade 5 Capscrews 45 n•m [33 ft-lb]

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> Grade 8 Capscrews 61 n•m [45 ft-lb]

![[01400039.png]]

Install the five capscrews that attach to the bottom of the oil pan adapter. Tighten the capscrews.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 45 n•m [33 ft-lb]

![[01400142.png]]

Install the hydraulic pump drive.

Use clean engine oil. Lubricate the bushing in the cover.

Install the gasket, drive or cover, washers, and capscrews. Tighten the capscrews.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> Grade 5 Capscrews 45 n•m [33 ft-lb]

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> Grade 8 Capscrews 61 n•m [45 ft-lb]
