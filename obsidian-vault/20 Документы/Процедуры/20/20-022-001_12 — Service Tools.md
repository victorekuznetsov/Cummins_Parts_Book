> [!quote]- Original (English) · английский оригинал
> | **Tool Number** ST-302 | **Ball Joint Vise** Used to hold the air compressor for disassembly or assembly. |  |
> |---|---|---|
> | **Tool Number** ST-647 | **Standard Puller** Used to pull the drive gear. Use with puller capscrews that have M8 x 1.25-6H thread. |  |
> | **Tool Number** ST-749 | **Mounting Plate** Used to mount the air compressor to the vise. |  |
> | **Tool Number** 3375182 | **Valve Spring Tester** Used to check the exhaust valve, intake valve, and unloader valve cap springs. |  |
> | **Tool Number** 3376663 | **Coupling Puller** Used to remove the spline coupling hub. |  |
> | **Tool Number** 3823923 | **Spacer** Used with coupling puller, Part Number 3376663, to remove the hydraulic pump drive coupling. |  |
> | **Tool Number** 3377415 | **Air Compressor Seat Installation Tool** Used to install the exhaust valve seats. |  |
> | **Tool Number** 3377416 | **Air Compressor Seat Removal Tool** Used to remove the exhaust valve seats. |  |
> | **Tool Number** 3823528 | **Air Compressor Seat Socket** Used to remove the exhaust valve seat and the inlet valve cage. |  |
> | **Tool Number** ST-544 | **Valve Seat Puller** Used to remove the exhaust valve seat on older model air compressors. Tool can also be used to remove needle bearing from the fuel pump housing or other small bores. |  |
> | **Tool Number** 3822674 | **Compressor Exhaust Valve Seat Kit** Tools used to pull and install the press fit nine hole exhaust seat in single cylinder Cummins air compressors. |  |
> | **Tool Number** 3822681 | **Compressor Exhaust Valve Seat Kit** Kit that converts Compressor Exhaust Valve Seat Kit, Part Number 3822674, into a two cylinder air compressor kit. |  |
