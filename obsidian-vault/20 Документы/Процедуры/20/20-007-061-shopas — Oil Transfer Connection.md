### Install

Rail Applications

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The transfer connection gasket is the same as the filter head gasket, but the tab on the end of the gasket **must** be removed prior to installing the new gasket.

Install the gasket, lubricating oil transfer connection, and five washers and capscrews. Tighten the capscrews.

| 3” capscrew (1 each) | 45 n.m | \[35 ft-lb\] |
|---|---|---|

| 1 1/4” capscrew (2 each) | 45 n.m | \[35 ft-lb\] |
|---|---|---|

| 3 3/4” capscrew (2 each) | 45 n.m | \[35 ft-lb\] |
|---|---|---|
