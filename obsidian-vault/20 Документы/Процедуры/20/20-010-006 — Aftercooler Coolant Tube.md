# Aftercooler Coolant Tube

### Preparatory Steps

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Do not remove the pressure cap from a hot engine. Wait until the coolant temperature is below 50° C \[120° F\] before removing the pressure cap. Heated coolant spray or steam can cause personal injury.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Coolant is toxic. Keep away from children and pets. If not reused, dispose of in accordance with local environmental regulations.

- Drain the cooling system. Refer to Procedure 008-018.

![[nobox.png]]

### Remove

Right Bank

Remove the P-clips (1) and capscrews (2) which mount the upper and lower tubes to the brackets on the number 5 rocker housing.

![[08600347.png]]

Remove the capscrew (1), P-clips (2), spacer (3), washer (4), and nut (5) that mount both the upper and lower water tubes to the support bracket, mounted on rocker housing number 2.

![[08600348.png]]

Remove the capscrew (1), P-clip (2), washer (3), and nut (4) that mount the lower water tube to the bracket, mounted on thermostat housing support.

![[08600351.png]]

Remove the lower and upper water transfer tube from the rubber tee pieces.

![[08600345.png]]

Remove the upper water transfer tube from the thermostat housing.

Remove and discard the o-rings.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The circled area of the graphic is for location purposes **only**.

![[08600356.png]]

Remove the lower tube from the junction block.

Remove and discard the o-rings.

![[08600355.png]]

Left Bank

Remove the P-clips (1) and capscrews (2) that mount the upper and lower water transfer tubes to the brackets on rocker housing number 5.

![[08600349.png]]

Remove the P-clips (1) and capscrews (2) that mount both the upper and lower water transfer tubes to the bracket mounted on rocker housing number 2.

![[08600350.png]]

Remove the upper water transfer tube from the thermostat housing.

Remove and discard the o-rings.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The circled area of the graphic is for location purposes **only**.

![[08600356.png]]

Remove the upper and lower water tubes from the rubber tee pieces.

![[08600345.png]]

Remove the lower tube from the junction block.

![[08600352.png]]

### Clean

Flush the pipes with water to be sure the water pipes are internally clean and free of debris before re-fitting.

![[07a00048.png]]

### Inspect for Reuse

Check that the pipes are free from dents, cracks, etc.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> O-rings **must** always be replaced following removal of the water transfer tubes.

![[lp5tbsa.png]]

### Install

Right Bank

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Do not use petroleum based oil. This will cause swelling of the o-ring.

Use vegetable oil to lubricate the o-ring.

Fit the lower water tube into the junction block.

![[08600353.png]]

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Do not use petroleum based oil. This will cause swelling of the o-ring.

Use vegetable oil to lubricate the o-ring.

Fit the upper tube into the thermostat housing.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The circled area of the graphic is for location purposes **only**.

![[08600356.png]]

Fit the upper and lower water tubes into the rubber tee pieces.

![[08600345.png]]

Secure both the upper and lower pipes to the brackets on the number 5 rocker housing using P-clips (1) and capscrews (2). To aid assembly, do **not** fully tighten the P-clip capscrews until the pipe is loosely clipped on all brackets.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 45 n•m [33 ft-lb]

![[08600347.png]]

Secure both the upper and lower pipes to the brackets on the number 2 rocker housing using capscrews (1), P-clips (2), spacer (3) and nut (4). To aid assembly, do **not** fully tighten the P-clip capscrew until the pipe is loosely clipped on all the brackets.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 45 n•m [33 ft-lb]

![[08600348.png]]

Secure the lower water pipe to the bracket on the thermostat housing support.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 45 n•m [33 ft-lb]

![[08600351.png]]

Left Bank

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Do not use petroleum based oil. This will cause swelling of the o-ring.

Use vegetable oil to lubricate the o-ring; fit lower tube into the junction block.

![[08600354.png]]

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Do not use petroleum based oil. This will cause swelling of the o-ring.

Use vegetable oil to lubricate the o-ring.

Fit upper tube into the thermostat housing.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The circled area of the graphic is for location purposes **only**.

![[08600356.png]]

Fit the upper and lower water tubes into the rubber tee pieces.

![[08600345.png]]

Secure both the upper and lower pipes to the brackets on the number 5 rocker housing using P-clips (1) and capscrews (2). To aid assembly, do **not** fully tighten the P-clip capscrews until the pipe is loosely clipped on all of the brackets.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 45 n•m [33 ft-lb]

![[08600349.png]]

Secure both the upper and lower pipes to the brackets on number 2 rocker housing using P-clips (1) and capscrews (2).

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 45 n•m [33 ft-lb]
