### Specifications

#### Oil Pressure (with 15W-40 oil at 107°C \[ 225°F \]):

| At Idle (minimum allowable) | 138 kPa \[ 20 psi \] |
|---|---|

#### Oil Pressure (with 15W-40 oil at 93°C to 121°C \[ 200°F to 250°F \]):

| At No-Load Governed Speed | 345 to 551 kPa \[ 50 to 80 psi \] |
|---|---|

| Oil Temperature Maximum: | 120°C \[ 250°F \] |
|---|---|

#### Oil Filter Capacity:

| Bypass Filter | 2.3 liters \[ 0.6 U.S. gal \] |
|---|---|

| Full-Flow Filter | 2.7 liters \[ 0.7 U.S. gal \] |
|---|---|

| Combination Filter | 2.3 liters \[ 0.6 U.S. gal \] |
|---|---|

| Venturi Combination Filter | 2.5 liters \[ 0.66 U.S. gal \] |
|---|---|

| Centrifuge Filter | 1.0 liters \[ 0.26 U.S. gal \] |
|---|---|
