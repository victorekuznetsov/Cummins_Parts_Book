# Rear Gear Drive (Upper Assembly)
**Задняя шестерённая передача (верхний узел)**

> [!abstract] Процедура · `20-009-024-shopds`

### Remove

Remove the outer hydraulic pump support drive.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Some engines will **not** have an outer hydraulic drive. These engines require a cover plate and a non-splined shaft, but do **not** use a hydraulic gear.

If using a cover plate, remove the cover plate and non-splined shaft.

Remove the seven capscrews from each outboard hydraulic drive or cover plate.

![[hd4plma.png]]

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> This component or assembly weighs greater than 23 kg \[50 lb\]. To prevent serious personal injury, be sure to have assistance or use appropriate lifting equipment to lift this component or assembly.

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Remove the outboard hydraulic pump supports as described below to avoid breaking the capscrew mounting flanges.

The outboard hydraulic pump supports **must** be rotated to be removed from the housing. Use a mallet. Carefully tap the side of the support.

![[hd4drwa.png]]

Use a pry bar. Remove both outboard hydraulic pump supports. Remove and discard the gaskets.

![[hd4drwb.png]]

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> This component or assembly weighs greater than 23 kg \[50 lb\]. To prevent serious personal injury, be sure to have assistance or use appropriate lifting equipment to lift this component or assembly.

Use a hoist and lifting sling. Install the lifting sling around both sides of the upper housing. Adjust the hoist until there is tension in the lifting sling.

![[gh4sgma.png]]

Remove the four capscrews from the top of the housing (A), the four internal capscrews (B), the two capscrews from the bottom of the upper housing on the left side (C), and the three capscrews from the bottom on the right side (D).

![[gh4csma.png]]

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> The hydraulic gear protrudes out of the bottom of the housing. Put the upper housing upside down on a workbench to prevent damage to the gear or bushing.

Remove the rear gear train upper housing from the lower housing. Remove the gasket (1). Keep the gasket for future use.

![[dr4gkma.png]]
