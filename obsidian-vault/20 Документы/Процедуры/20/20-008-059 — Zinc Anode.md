### Remove

Marine Applications

Shut off the sea water inlet valve on the vessel hull.

![[08600044.png]]

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> In some cases, it will be necessary to hold the welded fitting on the heat exchanger with an additional wrench to prevent damage to the heat exchanger.

Remove the zinc plug(s).

The illustration shown is generic. For engine-specific locations, [[20-100-002-tr — Engine Diagrams|Refer to Procedure 100-002 in Section E.]]

![[08400547.png]]

### Inspect for Reuse

Marine Applications

Measure the length of the zinc plug(s) removed from the heat exchanger. Replace any zinc plug if it is 50 percent or more eroded.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The frequency of change depends on the chemical reaction of the sea water circulated through the heat exchanger.

![[hx4pxna.png]]

| Zinc Plug Erosion Limits |  |  |  |  |
|---|---|---|---|---|
|  | mm |  | in |  |
| A | 19.0 | MIN | 0.750 |  |
| New |  | 51.0 | MAX | 2.000 |
| B | 6.4 | MIN | 0.250 |  |
| New |  | 16.0 | MAX | 0.630 |

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> If any zinc plug does **not** meet specification, replace it with a new zinc plug.

![[hx4pxna.png]]

### Install

Marine Applications

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Do not use Teflon™ tape or pipe sealant on the threads of the sacrificial zinc anode plugs. Use of any type of sealant will insulate the zinc anode plugs and prevent a good ground, resulting in engine damage.

Install and tighten the zinc plug(s).

Torque Value:

3.35 mm \[0.25 in\] Plug

Torque Value:

9.525 mm \[0.375 in\] Plug

Torque Value:

12.7 mm \[0.5 in\] Plug

![[08400547.png]]

Open the sea water valve on the vessel hull.

Operate the engine and check for leaks.
