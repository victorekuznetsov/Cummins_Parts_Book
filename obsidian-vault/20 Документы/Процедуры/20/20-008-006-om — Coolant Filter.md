### Remove

Use the correct Fleetguard® coolant filter to maintain the correct supplemental coolant additive (SCA) concentration in the system.

Maintain the correct concentration by changing the service coolant filter at each oil drain interval.

See Fleetguard® DCA4 Service Filters and Liquid Precharge in the following procedure. [[102-018-004 — Coolant Recommendations and Specifications|Refer to Procedure 018-004 in Section V.]]

![[wf4etva.png]]

#### Change the service coolant filter at every oil and filter change interval.

- The correct service coolant filter to be used is determined by the total cooling system capacity and other operational factors.
- See Fleetguard® DCA4 Service Filters and Liquid Precharge in the following procedure. [[102-018-004 — Coolant Recommendations and Specifications|Refer to Procedure 018-004 in Section V.]]

![[oi800vf.png]]

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Coolant is toxic. Keep away from children and pets. If not reused, dispose of in accordance with local environmental regulations.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Do not remove the pressure cap from a hot engine. Wait until the coolant temperature is below 50°C \[120°F\] before removing the pressure cap. Heated coolant spray or steam can cause personal injury.

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Use caution when draining coolant that the coolant is not spilled or drained into the bilge area. Do not pump coolant overboard. If the coolant is not reused, it must be disposed of in accordance with local environmental regulations

Turn the valve on the filter head to the OFF position.

Remove and discard the coolant filter.

![[wf4wdua.png]]

### Install

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Do **not** allow oil to get in the filter. It will break down the SCA.

Lubricate the seal on the filter with engine oil.

![[wf8etwa.png]]

Install the coolant filter. Turn the filter until the seal touches the filter head. Turn an additional ½ to ¾ of a turn after contact.

![[wf800ha.png]]

Turn the valve to the ON position.

Operate the engine and check for leaks.

![[wf4hdwa.png]]
