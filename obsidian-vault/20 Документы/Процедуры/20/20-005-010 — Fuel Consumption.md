### Initial Check

with Mechanically Actuated Injector

Refer to the fuel consumption check list sheets in the back of this section.

![[oi800ca.png]]

Install a remote fuel tank (1) with enough capacity to run 1 hour.

Fill the fuel tank. Weigh the tank with the fuel. The weight of Number 2 diesel fuel is nominally 0.844 kg per liter \[7.03 pounds per gallon\].

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Use a scale capable of measuring within 0.045 kg \[0.1 lb\] to weigh the fuel tank.

![[oi800ha.png]]

### Test

Off-Highway Applications

Measure the time with a stopwatch.

![[05400003.png]]

After one hour of operation, weigh the fuel remaining, and compute the fuel used in liters \[gallons\] as required.

Compute the fuel consumption. Fuel used divided by the time equals the fuel consumption rate.

![[05400004.png]]

with Electronically Actuated Injector

Use INSITE™ electronic service tool to monitor fuel consumption.
