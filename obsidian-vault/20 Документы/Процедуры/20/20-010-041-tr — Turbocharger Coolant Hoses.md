### Preparatory Steps

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Coolant is toxic. Keep away from children and pets. If not reused, dispose of in accordance with local environmental regulations.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Do not remove the pressure cap from a hot engine. Wait until the coolant temperature is below 50°C \[120°F\] before removing the pressure cap. Heated coolant spray or steam can cause personal injury.

Drain the cooling system. [[20-008-018-tr — Cooling System|Refer to Procedure 008-018 in Section]]

![[ck800wa.png]]

### Remove

Disconnect the coolant supply hose from the turbocharger and the torque converter cooler cover.

Remove the male union from the torque converter cooler cover.

Discard the o-ring.

![[10400211.png]]

Disconnect the coolant return hose from the turbocharger and the water manifold tube.

Discard the o-rings.

Remove the male union fitting from the turbocharger and water manifold tube.

Discard the o-rings.

![[10400215.png]]

### Inspect for Reuse

Check the coolant supply and return hoses for damage, kinks, fretting, or restrictions.

Inspect the male unions for damage, stripped threads, and cracks.

![[10k00005.png]]

### Install

Install the new o-ring on the male union fittings and install the fittings on the torque converter cooler cover.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 34 n•m [25 ft-lb]

Install the new face seal o-rings.

Connect the turbocharger coolant supply hose to the male union fitting on the torque converter cooler cover and the turbocharger.

Torque Value:

Turbocharger Coolant Supply Hose

![[10400211.png]]

Install the new o-rings on the male union fittings and install the fittings in the water manifold tube and on the turbocharger.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 73 n•m [55 ft-lb]

Install the new face seal o-rings and connect the turbocharger coolant return hose to the turbocharger and the water manifold tube.

Make sure that the coolant return hose is **not** in contact with the oil supply hose. Use the following procedure if they are in contact and more clearance is needed. [[20-010-046 — Turbocharger Oil Supply Line|Refer to Procedure 010-046 in Section 10.]]

### Finishing Steps

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Coolant is toxic. Keep away from children and pets. If not reused, dispose of in accordance with local environmental regulations.

- Fill the cooling system. [[20-008-018-tr — Cooling System|Refer to Procedure 008-018 in Section.]]
- Operate the engine and check for proper operation.
