### Assemble

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> The turbine blades can be easily damaged when installing the assembly in the turbine housing.

Assemble the center housing rotating assembly on to the turbine housing.

![[10400261.png]]

Lubricate the threads of the new capscrews with anti-seize compound, Part Number 3824732, or equivalent.

Install the capscrews, with new lock plates, to the turbocharger turbine housing.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 20.3 n•m [180 in-lb]

![[10400262.png]]

Use a hammer and punch to fold the locking plate onto the flat of the capscrew to prevent loosening.

![[10400263.png]]

Assemble the inlet baffle into the compressor cover and secure with a retaining ring. Hold one end of the retaining ring in the groove. Press the remainder of the retaining ring into position. Use a flat tip screwdriver to make sure the retaining ring is correctly seated in the compressor cover groove.

![[10400264.png]]

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Turbocharger HX83 uses a titanium compressor wheel. The edge of the wheel is very sharp. Handle the compressor wheel with care.

Assemble the compressor housing new o-ring on the turbocharger.

![[10400265.png]]

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Do not damage the compressor wheel blades during assembly.

Assemble the v-band clamp and compressor cover onto the turbocharger.

![[10400266.png]]

Place the v-band clamp in position.

Tighten the v-band clamp.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 12 n•m [106 in-lb]

![[10400267.png]]

Make sure the compressor wheel and turbine wheel rotate freely.

![[10400268.png]]

### Install

All Applications Except Marine and Rear Mount Rail

Cummins Turbo Technologies™ turbocharger(s) are used on the QSK19 engine. See the data tag on the turbocharger to determine the model.

The name is also cast on the housing of a turbocharger.

![[10400034.png]]

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> All turbocharger fittings are the flat face o-ring type fittings. Make sure the o-ring is in place before attaching the hose(s) to the fittings.

The Cummins Turbo Technologies™ turbocharger uses a 9/16-18 UNF, straight thread, o-ring type oil supply fitting.

![[10400015.png]]

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> This component or assembly weighs greater than 23 kg \[50 lb\]. To prevent serious personal injury, be sure to have assistance or use appropriate lifting equipment to lift this component or assembly.

Apply an anti-seize compound to the mounting capscrews.

Install the gasket.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The word OUT or raised gasket bead **must** be positioned toward the turbocharger.

For engines with mechanically and electronically actuated injectors, install the turbocharger, turbocharger capscrews, nuts, and spacers. The 12.7 mm \[1/2 inch\] spacer **must** be on the turbocharger side of the joint, while the 9.525 mm \[3/8 inch\] spacer **must** be on the manifold side or the bolted joint.

![[10400333.png]]

Tighten the turbocharger capscrews using sequence and torque steps below.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> Step 1: 20 n•m [177 in-lb]

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> Step 2: 47 n•m [35 ft-lb]

Perform the final torque value in Step 2 using the sequence shown a second time.

![[10500147.png]]

Position the turbocharger drain tube. The drain tube **must** be within 35 degrees of vertical. Turn the bearing housing to align the tube, if necessary.

The turbocharger uses capscrews to attach the bearing housing to the turbine housing.

![[tb400ua.png]]

For bearing housing adjustment, bend the lockplate off the capscrew heads.

Remove the capscrews.

Rotate the bearing housing. Align the drain tube and the capscrew holes.

![[10400016.png]]

Install the lockplates and capscrews.

Tighten the capscrews.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 20 n•m [177 in-lb]

Bend the lockplate tabs over the capscrews.

![[10400017.png]]

Install the oil drain line. [[20-010-045 — Turbocharger Oil Drain Line|Refer to Procedure 010-045 in Section 10]]

Pour clean engine oil into the turbocharger oil supply fitting.

Turbocharger Oil Supply: 50 to 60 cc \[2.0 to 3.0 oz.\]

![[10400018.png]]

Connect the inlet piping to the turbocharger.

Tighten the clamps.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 8 n•m [71 in-lb]

![[tb4ilna.png]]

Connect the exhaust piping to the turbocharger.

Tighten the v-band clamp.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 8 n•m [71 in-lb]

![[tb4ilnb.png]]

Rear Mount Rail Applications

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> This component or assembly weighs greater than 23 kg \[50 lb\]. To prevent serious personal injury, be sure to have assistance or use appropriate lifting equipment to lift this component or assembly.

Apply an anti-seize compound to the mounting studs.

Install the gasket on the studs. The word OUT **must** be positioned toward the turbocharger.

Install the turbocharger.

Install and finger tighten the nuts on the studs.

Tighten the mounting nuts in a criss-cross pattern.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 57 n•m [42 ft-lb]

![[10400468.png]]

Install the two lockplates over the turbocharger mounting nuts.

![[10400469.png]]

Position the turbocharger drain tube. The drain tube **must** be within 35 degrees of vertical. Turn the bearing housing to align the tube, if necessary.

The turbocharger uses capscrews to attach the bearing housing to the turbine housing.

![[tb400ua.png]]

For bearing housing adjustment, bend the lockplate off the capscrew heads.

Remove the capscrews.

Rotate the bearing housing. Align the drain tube and the capscrew holes.

![[10400016.png]]

Install the lockplates and capscrews.

Tighten the capscrews.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 20 n•m [177 in-lb]

Bend the lockplate tabs over the capscrews.

![[10400017.png]]

Install the oil drain line. [[20-010-045 — Turbocharger Oil Drain Line|Refer to Procedure 010-045 in Section 10.]]

Pour clean engine oil into the turbocharger oil supply fitting.

Turbocharger Oil Supply: 50 to 60 cc \[2.0 to 3.0 oz.\]

![[10400018.png]]

Connect the inlet piping to the turbocharger.

Tighten the clamps.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 8 n•m [71 in-lb]

![[tb4ilna.png]]

Connect the exhaust piping to the turbocharger.

Tighten the v-band clamp.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 8 n•m [71 in-lb]
