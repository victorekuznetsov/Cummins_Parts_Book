### Install

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Do not use gasket cement. Gasket cement will prevent the gasket from sealing properly.

Use a contact adhesive such as 3M Spray 77 or 3M 4693 to hold the gasket in position.

Install the gasket and oil pan adapter cover.

![[07400012.png]]

Install the capscrews.

Tighten the capscrews in sequence.

Use the sequence shown to tighten the capscrews.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 45 n•m [33 ft-lb]
