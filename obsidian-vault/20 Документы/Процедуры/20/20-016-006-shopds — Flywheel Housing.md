### Remove

Remove the two \[7/16-14 inch\] capscrews, and the five \[3/8-16 inch\] capscrews from the flywheel housing.

![[op4adma.png]]

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> This component or assembly weighs greater than 23 kg \[50 lb\]. To prevent serious personal injury, be sure to have assistance or use appropriate lifting equipment to lift this component or assembly.

Use two guide studs to prevent the flywheel housing from rotating during disassembly. Remove two capscrews. Install the guide studs.

![[fh400ma.png]]

Use a hoist, a tee handle, and a lifting sling. Install the tee handle. Adjust the hoist until there is tension in the lifting sling. Remove the remaining capscrews.

![[fh400ma.png]]

Use a mallet and tap the flywheel housing off the two locating dowels. Remove and discard the rectangular seal and the bolt seals.

![[fh4hsma.png]]
