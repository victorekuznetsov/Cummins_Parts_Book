# Engine Support Bracket, Front
**Передний кронштейн опоры двигателя**

> [!abstract] Процедура · `20-016-002-tr`
> **Двигатели:** [[41349633 — QSK19 CM2150 MCRS CPL 3666|41349633]], [[41353297 — QSK19 CM2150 MCRS CPL 3666|41353297]]
> **Семейство:** QSK19
> **Входит в руководства:** [[4021592 — QSK19, QSK19 CM850 MCRS, and QSK19 CM2150 MCRS Service Manual|4021592]]
> **Секции:** Section 16 - Mounting Adaptations - Group 16
> **Даты:** изменён 2006-06-30
> **Источник:** [QuickServe](https://quickserve.cummins.com/qs3/pubsys2/xml/en/procedures/20/20-016-002-tr.html) · [PDF-оригинал](https://github.com/victorekuznetsov/Cummins_Parts_Book/raw/main/bulletins/procedures/20-016-002-tr.pdf)

### Remove

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> This component or assembly weighs greater than 23 kg \[50 lb\]. To prevent serious personal injury, be sure to have assistance or use appropriate lifting equipment to lift this component or assembly.

Install the engine lifting fixture, Part Number 3162871.

![[ew400ha.png]]

Remove the two capscrews that attach the support to the equipment frame.

Use a hoist to lift the weight of the engine off the front support.

![[em4csma.png]]

Use the engine support bracket kit, Part Number 3375272. Install one support bracket on each side of the engine.

![[em4suhb.png]]

Lower the engine until the support brackets are supporting the weight.

![[ew400la.png]]

Remove the front engine support bracket.

![[em4suha.png]]

Rail Applications

Remove the front support mounting bracket by removing fifteen capscrews and washers.

![[17400007.png]]

### Clean and Inspect for Reuse

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> When using a steam cleaner, wear safety glasses or a face shield, as well as protective clothing. Hot steam can cause serious personal injury.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> When using solvents, acids, or alkaline material for cleaning, follow the manufacturer's recommendations for use. Wear goggles and protective clothing to reduce the possibility of personal injury.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Wear appropriate eye and face protection when using compressed air. Flying debris and dirt can cause bodily injury.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> This component or assembly weighs greater than 23 kg \[50 lb\]. To prevent serious personal injury, be sure to have assistance or use appropriate lifting equipment to lift this component or assembly.

Use solvent or steam to clean the engine support bracket.

Dry with compressed air.

![[em4suha.png]]

Check the engine support bracket for cracks or damage.

Clean and inspect the rubber mounts for reuse. Check all rubber-cushioned mounts for cracks or damage.

Inspect **all** frame mounting brackets for cracks or damaged bolt holes.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Damaged engine mounts and brackets can cause the engine to move out of alignment, damage the driveline components in the equipment, and result in vibration complaints.

![[em8grsa.png]]

### Install

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> This component or assembly weighs greater than 23 kg \[50 lb\]. To prevent serious personal injury, be sure to have assistance or use appropriate lifting equipment to lift this component or assembly.

Install the support, washers, and capscrews. Tighten the capscrews.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 195 n•m [144 ft-lb]

![[em4suha.png]]

Raise the engine to remove the weight from the support brackets, Part Number 3375272. Remove the support brackets.

![[em4suma.png]]

Lower the engine until the front engine support is in position. Install and tighten the capscrews.

Refer to the OEM troubleshooting and repair manual for torque values.

![[em4csha.png]]

Rail Applications

Install the front engine mount and fifteen capscrews. Tighten the fifteen capscrews.
