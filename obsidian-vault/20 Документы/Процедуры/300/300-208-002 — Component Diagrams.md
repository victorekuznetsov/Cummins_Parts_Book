### General Information

![[00c00235.png]]

C Command HD Elite Plus - Single or Multi-Engine, Single or Multi-Station

1. Station 1
2. Engine room.

1. J1939 display (Cummins® or original equipment manufacturer (OEM)-supplied)
2. Helm components (OEM-supplied)
3. Display adapter wiring harness (Standard, Advanced, or Twin engine) (Cummins® or OEM-supplied)
4. Customer interface box (CIB) (HD Elite Plus) (Cummins®-supplied)
5. Engine interface wiring harness (Cummins®-supplied)
6. Application dependent J1939 trunk wiring harness used when both engines need to be connected to a common canbus. (Unique engine control module (ECM) calibration required) (OEM)
7. Engine (Cummins®)
8. Battery bank (OEM-supplied)
9. Battery system wiring harness (OEM-supplied).

#### Not Shown:

- Transmission gear oil pressure and temperature sensors with adapter harness located at the marine gear oil cooler or transmission (Cummins® or OEM-supplied)
- Exhaust temperature sensor/switch located in exhaust plumbing (Cummins® or OEM-supplied)
- Water in fuel sensor extension wiring harness located at suction side filter mounted off engine (Cummins® or OEM-supplied).
