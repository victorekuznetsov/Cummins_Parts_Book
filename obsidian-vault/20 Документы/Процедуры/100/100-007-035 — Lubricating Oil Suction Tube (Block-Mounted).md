# Lubricating Oil Suction Tube (Block-Mounted)
**Маслозаборная трубка (на блоке)**

### Preparatory Steps

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Some state and federal agencies have determined that used engine oil can be carcinogenic and cause reproductive toxicity. Avoid inhalation of vapors, ingestion, and prolonged contact with used engine oil. If not reused, dispose on in accordance with local environmental regulations.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> To reduce the possibility of personal injury, avoid direct contact of hot oil with your skin.

Drain the lubricating oil. Refer to Procedure [[100-007-037 — Lubricating Oil System|007-037]].

![[op900wa.png]]

Remove the lubricating oil pan and gasket. Refer to Procedure [[100-007-025 — Lubricating Oil Pan|007-025]].

![[op9gkea.png]]

### Remove

Remove the lubricating oil suction tube and support bracket mounting capscrews. Remove the lubricating oil suction tube and support bracket.

![[07900114.png]]

### Clean and Inspect for Reuse

Clean the gasket surfaces.

![[07900002.png]]

Inspect the suction tube and support bracket for cracks.

![[op9tbsa.png]]

### Install

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Some engines use 10-mm capscrews and others use 6-mm capscrews. Follow the torque procedure based on the capscrew size.

Install the lubricating oil suction tube and a new gasket.

1. Install all capscrews finger tight and check for correct alignment.

2. Torque the lubricating oil suction tube to the block.

6-mm Capscrews

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 10 n•m [89 in-lb]

10-mm Capscrews

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 24 n•m [18 ft-lb]

3. Torque the lubricating oil suction tube support bracket to the block.

6-mm Capscrews

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 10 n•m [89 in-lb]

10-mm Capscrews

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 24 n•m [18 ft-lb]

4. Torque the lubricating oil suction tube to the support bracket.

6-mm Capscrews

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 10 n•m [89 in-lb]

10-mm Capscrews

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 24 n•m [18 ft-lb]

![[op9tbhd.png]]

### Finishing Steps

Install the lubricating oil pan and gasket. Refer to Procedure [[100-007-025 — Lubricating Oil Pan|007-025]]. Fill with lubricating oil. Refer to [[100-007-037 — Lubricating Oil System|007-037]]

Operate the engine and check for leaks.
