### Clean

Use a strong solution of hot water and detergent to clean the cylinder bores.

![[ck9breh.png]]

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Wear appropriate eye and face protection when using compressed air. Flying debris and dirt can cause bodily injury.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Clean the cylinder bores immediately after being deglazed.

Rinse the cylinder bores until the detergent is removed.

Dry the cylinder block with compressed air.

![[ck9brei.png]]

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> When using solvents, acids, or alkaline materials for cleaning, follow the manufacturer's recommendations for use. Wear goggles and protective clothing to avoid personal injury.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Remove the tape that covers the tappet and lubricating oil holes after the cleaning process is complete.

Check the cylinder bore cleanliness by wiping them with a white, lint-free, lightly oiled cloth.

If grit residue is still present, repeat the cleaning process until all residue is removed.

Wash the cylinder bores with solvent.

Dry the cylinder block with compressed air.

![[ck9brej.png]]

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Remove the covering from the piston cooling nozzles.

Remove the protective tape and cloth from the crankshaft journals.

Clean the crankshaft journals.

![[ks900ej.png]]

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> When using solvents, acids, or alkaline materials for cleaning, follow the manufacturer's recommendations for use. Wear goggles and protective clothing to avoid personal injury.

Use solvent and a brush to clean any residue that possibly splashed on the camshaft.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Inspect the camshaft lobes and tappet faces for signs of wear or damage. Refer to Procedure [[41-001-008 — Camshaft|001-008]].

![[cg900eb.png]]

### Inspect for Reuse

Measure the cylinder bore diameter at 25.4 mm \[1 in\] and 170.7 mm \[5.5 in\] from the top of the cylinder block.

|  | mm |  | in |  |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bore Inner Diameter | 114.00 | MIN | 4.488 |  |
|  | 114.04 | MAX | 4.990 |  |
| Bore Out of Round |  | 0.08 | MAX | 0.003 |
| Bore Taper |  | 0.02 | MAX | 0.001 |

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Do **not** proceed with in chassis overhaul if the cylinder bores are worn beyond specifications.
