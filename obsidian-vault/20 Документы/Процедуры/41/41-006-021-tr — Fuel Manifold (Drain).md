### General Information

The fuel system is designed to use fuel to cool and lubricate the fuel injection pump plungers and injectors. Fuel is continually vented from the fuel injection pump and a small amount of fuel leaks by the injector needle valve during injection. This fuel is returned to the fuel filter head (1) by the fuel drain manifold (2).

![[ft900gp.png]]

### Preparatory Steps

Clean all debris from around the fuel drain manifold.

![[nobox.png]]

### Remove

Remove the banjo capscrews from the injectors and fuel filter head.

![[ft900ma.png]]

### Inspect for Reuse

Inspect the fuel drain lines for cracks and/or damage.

![[ft9tbsc.png]]

Inspect the fuel drain line banjo connections.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The banjo connection seals **must** be discarded when removed. The seals **must not** be reused.

![[ft9tbsd.png]]

### Install

Install the fuel drain manifold in the reverse order of removal.

| Filter Head Banjo | 15 n.m | \[133 in-lb\] |
|---|---|---|

| Injector Banjo | 9 n.m | \[80 in-lb\] |
|---|---|---|
