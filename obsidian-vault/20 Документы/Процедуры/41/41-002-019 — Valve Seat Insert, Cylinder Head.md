### Inspect for Reuse

Inspect the valve seats for cracks and burn spots.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Service valve seats are available for valve seats that can **not** be ground within specification.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> If a valve seat can **not** be cleaned up by grinding it, the valve seat **must** be replaced; refer to the Shop Manual, C Series Engines, Bulletin 3666008.

![[kn900km.png]]

### Grind

Use valve seat grinding machine, Part No. ST-685, and valve guide arbor set, Part No. 3375946, to grind the valve seat inserts.

Grind the valve seats at a 30-degree angle.

![[02400040.png]]

Measure the valve seat width.

|  | mm |  | in |
|---|---|---|---|
| Intake | 3.05 | MIN | 0.120 |
|  | 3.55 | MAX | 0.140 |
| Exhaust | 1.52 | MIN | 0.060 |
|  | 2.54 | MAX | 0.100 |

![[02400040.png]]

If the width of the valve seat is **not** within specifications, remove surface material on the inner diameter and the outer diameter to decrease the width of the seat.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> If the valve seat specifications are **not** obtained by grinding the valve seat, the insert **must** be replaced.

![[02400041.png]]

Measure the valve-seat-to-valve-guide concentricity.

| mm |  | in |
|---|---|---|
| 0.05 | MAX | 0.002 |
