# Coolant Filter Valve
**Клапан фильтра охлаждающей жидкости**

> **Двигатели:** [[93058669 — 6C8.3 CPL 3105|93058669]], [[93087701 — 6C8.3 CPL 3105|93087701]]

> **Секции:** Section 8 - Cooling System - Group 08
> **Даты:** изменён 2003-05-13
> **Источник:** [QuickServe](https://quickserve.cummins.com/qs3/pubsys2/xml/en/procedures/41/41-008-009.html) · [PDF-оригинал](https://github.com/victorekuznetsov/Cummins_Parts_Book/raw/main/bulletins/procedures/41-008-009.pdf)

### Remove

Remove the retaining capscrew (1) from the thermostat housing (2) and remove the shutoff valve (3).

Remove and discard the o-ring (4).

![[th9boha.png]]

### Clean

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> When using solvents, acids, or alkaline materials for cleaning, follow the manufacturer's recommendations for use. Wear goggles and protective clothing to avoid personal injury.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Wear appropriate eye and face protection when using compressed air. Flying debris and dirt can cause bodily injury.

Use solvent to clean the parts.

Dry with compressed air.

![[th9bdea.png]]

### Inspect for Reuse

Inspect the water passages in the shutoff valve.

Inspect the shaft for corrosion or pitting. Use 240-grit aluminum oxide paper to remove the corrosion.

If the parts are cracked or damaged, the filter head assembly **must** be replaced.

![[th9vasa.png]]

### Install

Install a new o-ring in the groove of the shaft.

![[th9orha.png]]

Use clean engine oil to lubricate the shaft and o-ring.

![[wf2vawa.png]]

Install the shutoff valve shaft (3) in the thermostat housing (2).

Install the retaining ring capscrew (1) in the thermostat housing.

Tighten the retaining ring capscrews.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 7 n•m [60 in-lb]

![[th9boha.png]]
