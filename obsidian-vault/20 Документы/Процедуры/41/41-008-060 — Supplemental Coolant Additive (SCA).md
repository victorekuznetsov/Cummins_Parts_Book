### Initial Check

Additive Concentration - Checking

The cooling system **must** contain the proper coolant additive units to provide the best chemical protection. Refer to the Operation and Maintenance Manual, C Series Engines, Bulletin No. 3666183.

**DCA4 Test Kit:** Use **only** DCA4 coolant test kit, Fleetguard® Part No. CC-2626, to measure the coolant additive concentration in the cooling system.
