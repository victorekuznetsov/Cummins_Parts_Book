### Inspect for Reuse

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> When using solvents, acids, or alkaline materials for cleaning, follow the manufacturer's recommendations for use. Wear goggles and protective clothing to reduce the possibility of personal injury.

Clean the alternator pulley and alternator drive pulley with steam or solvent.

Check the grooves of the pulleys for wear. If the part **must** be replaced, refer to Procedures [[89-009-010 — Charging System Alternator Drive Pulley|009-010]] and [[89-013-001 — Charging System Alternator|013-001]].
