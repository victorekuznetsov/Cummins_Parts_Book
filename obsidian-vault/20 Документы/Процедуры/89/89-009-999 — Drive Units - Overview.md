### General Information

The Drive Units section consists of the removal, inspection, and installation of the alternator drive gear and shaft.

The alternator drive gear and shaft also drives the fuel pump and an OEM supplied refrigerant compressor on some industrial applications. The fuel pump engages the drive gear via a spider gear from the rear of the front cover and is mounted to the block.

The alternator drive gear and shaft revolves at a 1:1 ratio to the crankshaft and in the same direction. The drive contains 55 teeth and is driven by the crank gear through an idler gear and is splash lubricated.
