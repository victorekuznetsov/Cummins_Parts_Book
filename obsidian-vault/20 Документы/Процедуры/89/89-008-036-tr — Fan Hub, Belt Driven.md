# Fan Hub, Belt Driven
**Ступица вентилятора с ремённым приводом**

> [!abstract] Процедура · `89-008-036-tr`
> **Двигатели:** [[85017333 — QSK23 CM500 CPL 2858|85017333]]
> **Семейство:** QSK23
> **Входит в руководства:** [[4021375 — QSK23 Troubleshooting and Repair Manual|4021375]]
> **Секции:** Section 8 - Cooling System - Group 08
> **Даты:** изменён 2003-03-11
> **Источник:** [QuickServe](https://quickserve.cummins.com/qs3/pubsys2/xml/en/procedures/89/89-008-036-tr.html) · [PDF-оригинал](https://github.com/victorekuznetsov/Cummins_Parts_Book/raw/main/bulletins/procedures/89-008-036-tr.pdf)

### Preparatory Steps

- Remove the fan. Refer to Procedure [[89-008-040 — Fan, Cooling|008-040]].
- Remove the fan belt. Refer to Procedure [[89-008-002 — Drive Belt, Cooling Fan|008-002]].

![[ck800wa.png]]

### Remove

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> This assembly weighs 23 kg \[50 lb\] or more. To reduce the possibility of personal injury, use a hoist or get assistance to lift this assembly.

Remove the six bolts and the fan pulley.

![[08400350.png]]

Remove the six bolts and the fan hub.

![[08400351.png]]

### Inspect for Reuse

Inspect the fan hub for reuse. Rotate the hub to check for rough or damaged bearings.

Check bearing end clearance.

| mm |  | in |
|---|---|---|
| 0.03 | MIN | 0.001 |
| 0.25 | MAX | 0.010 |

Inspect the grease seals for damage.

![[08400352.png]]

### Install

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> This assembly weighs 23 kg \[50 lb\] or more. To reduce the possibility of personal injury, use a hoist or get assistance to lift this assembly.

Install the fan hub and the six mounting bolts.

Tighten the fan hub bolts.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 113 n•m [83 ft-lb]

![[08400351.png]]

Install the fan hub pulley and six mounting bolts. Tighten the bolts.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 118 n•m [87 ft-lb]

![[08400350.png]]

### Finishing Steps

- Install the fan belt. Refer to Procedure [[89-008-002 — Drive Belt, Cooling Fan|008-002]].
- Install the fan. Refer to Procedure [[89-008-040 — Fan, Cooling|008-040]].
