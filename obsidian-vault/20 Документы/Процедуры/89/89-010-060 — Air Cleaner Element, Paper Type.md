### Maintenance Check

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The illustrations in this section show typical dry-type air cleaner parts. The particular engine parts can vary.

Replace the element if the inlet restriction or vacuum at full power exceeds 635 mm H 2 O \[25 in H 2 O\]. Changing filters or breaking the seal on the intake system more than necessary will result in excess dirt in the engine and **must** be avoided.

![[ac4etga.png]]

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Holes, loose-end seals, dented sealing surfaces, and other forms of damage render the cleaner inoperative and require immediate element replacement.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Cummins Inc. does **not** recommend cleaning paper-type air cleaner elements.

Elements that have been cleaned several times will finally clog and airflow to the engine will be restricted. After cleaning, check the restriction as previously described. Replace the element, if necessary.

![[ac4etgb.png]]

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Pull the cover and the element straight out during removal from the housing to reduce the possibility of damage to the element.

Remove the wing nut (1) that secures the bottom cover (2) to the cleaner housing (3). Remove the cover.

Pull the element (6) down from the center bolt (4).

Remove the gasket (5) from the outlet end (7) of the housing.
