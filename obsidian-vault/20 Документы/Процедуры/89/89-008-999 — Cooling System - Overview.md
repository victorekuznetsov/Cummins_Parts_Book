### General Information

Cooling System

The QSK23 engine uses a standard jacket water cooling system for the engine. Each head has four coolant inlet ports and one coolant outlet port. There is also a coolant vent port at the top of each head on the exhaust side.

Coolant flow from the water pump runs through the oil cooler cavities, around the cylinder liners, then up through the four inlet passages in the cylinder heads. Coolant then exits the heads through a single outlet port, into the return manifold and to the thermostat housing.

Fan Drives

The power fan drive used on the QSK23 engine uses an adjustable fan belt tensioner for power generation applications or an automatic fan belt tensioner, as shown, on industrial applications.
