### General Information

The QSK23 exhaust manifold has been aerodynamically designed to reduce pumping losses and to enhance pulse flow. In addition, the manifold connection has been designed to allow for thermal expansion.

The maximum allowable exhaust system back pressure for the QSK23 engine is 76.2 mm Hg \[3 in Hg\].
