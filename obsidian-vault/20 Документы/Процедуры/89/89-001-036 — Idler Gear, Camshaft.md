### Preparatory Steps

- Remove the oil pan and associated components. [[89-007-025 — Lubricating Oil Pan|Refer to Procedure 007-025 in Section 7]].
- Remove the flywheel housing. [[89-016-006 — Flywheel Housing|Refer to Procedure 016-006 in Section 16]].
- Remove the camshaft gear. [[89-001-008 — Camshaft|Refer to Procedure 001-008 in Section 1.]]

### Remove

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Check the punch mark on the mounting capscrew head. If seven marks are punched, replace the capscrew with a new capscrew. Failure to replace capscrew could result in breakage.

For CKEC engines built before engine serial number (ESN) 00327150, and PHP engines built after ESN 85000848, remove the three capscrews, the shaft, the camshaft idler gear assembly, and the rear thrust bearing.

For CKEC engines built after ESN 00327150, and PHP engines built after ESN 85000848, remove the three capscrews, the shaft, the camshaft idler gear assembly, and the gear retainer.

### Install

If the shaft is out of specification, discard and replace with a new idler shaft.

Align the large gear and small gear using a dowel pin.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 110 n•m [80 ft-lb]

![[01d00869.png]]

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> The chamfer in the thrust washers must be positioned toward the gear. Improper installation of the thrust washer can cause engine damage.

For CKEC engines built before ESN 00327150, the timing marks on the camshaft gear **must** be visible when the gear is installed.

Use Lubriplate™ 105, or equivalent, to lubricate the gear bushing, shaft, and the retainer plate.

After lubricating, align the idler shaft, gear, and retainer plate before installing onto the block.

![[01400576.png]]

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно

The camshaft idler gear is the **only** idler gear that has timing marks.

Align the "A" on the larger idler gear with the "A" on the crankshaft gear.

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Check the punch mark on the mounting capscrew head. If seven marks are punched, replace the capscrew with a new capscrew. Failure to replace the capscrew could result in breakage.

#### Capscrew Inspection Criteria

- Inspect the capscrews for damage.

The capscrews **must** be replaced if:

- Seven punch marks are visible on the head of the capscrew.
- The threads are damaged.
- Rust or corrosion has caused pitting in the body.
- The body is nicked, galled, bent, or stretched.
- Fretting is found under the capscrew head.

![[bp8cssa.png]]

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> The outer face of the idler gear shaft is stamped with the word “UP”. This stamp must be positioned toward the cylinder heads. Improper installation of the idler gear shaft can cause engine damage.

Use clean engine oil to lubricate the capscrews.

Use the capscrew to pull the shaft into the bore.

Use the following steps to tighten the capscrews in the sequence shown.

Torque Value:

After tightening capscrew, apply a punch mark to the head of the capscrew.

![[01400578.png]]

Use a dial indicator to measure the idler gear end clearance.

| Camshaft Idler Gear End Clearance |  |  |
|---|---|---|
| mm |  | in |
| 0.04 | MIN | 0.002 |
| 0.18 | MAX | 0.007 |

If the clearance is **not** within specifications, check for foreign material between the parts, or check for proper location of the thrust washers. Oversize thrust washers are available.
