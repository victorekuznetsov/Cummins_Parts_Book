### General Information

The QST30 G-Drive engine uses two separate wiring harnesses to control the engine and some of the equipment operations

1. Engine harness.
2. Original equipment manufacturer (OEM) harness.

The QST30 G-Drive also uses extension cables and an adaptor cable to attach the engine harness to the engine control module (ECM).

![[19a00032.png]]

Replace a harness, or extension/adaptor cable, if there is an open circuit or a short circuit is found under the protective covering of the harness body.

![[19400386.png]]

### Remove

Remove the engine harness clamps.

![[19801065.png]]

Disconnect the coolant temperature sensor.

![[00a00036.png]]

Disconnect the engine harness from the oil pressure sensor.

![[00a00037.png]]

Disconnect the fuel shut off control wires to the fuel shut off solenoids (left bank and right bank).

![[19a00101.png]]

Disconnect the engine block ground from the block.

![[19a00097.png]]

Use a 4 mm \[5/32 inch\] hex head wrench to disconnect the engine harness Deutsch™ connector from any engine harness extension used or the engine harness adaptor cable.

![[19400242.png]]

Cut the remaining ties holding the engine harness to the engine.

Remove the engine harness.

![[19801077.png]]

**Engine Harness Extension Cable**

Use a 4 mm \[5/32 inch\] hex head wrench to disconnect the attached cable's Deutsch™ connectors.

![[19400242.png]]

**Engine Harness Adaptor Cable**

Use a 4 mm \[5/32 inch\] hex head wrench to disconnect the attached cables Deutsch™ connectors from the ECM.

![[19400242.png]]

### Install

Connect the engine harness to the engine using cable ties.

![[19801077.png]]

Connect the engine harness Deutsch™ connector to the engine harness extension cable, if used, or the engine harness adaptor cable.

Use an inch-pound torque wrench, Part Number 3376592, with 4 mm \[5/32 in\] hex head adapter to tighten the connector jackscrew.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 2.8 n•m [25 in-lb]

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Do **not** over-torque as connector damage can occur.

![[19400242.png]]

Connect the engine harness to the oil pressure sensor.

![[00a00037.png]]

Connect the engine block ground to the engine block.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 20 n•m [15 ft-lb]

![[19a00097.png]]

Connect the coolant temperature sensor.

![[00a00036.png]]

**Engine Harness Extension Cable**

Connect the connectors of the extension cable to the wiring harness. Use a 4 mm \[5/32 inch\] hex head wrench to tighten.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 3 n•m [25 in-lb]

![[19400242.png]]

**Engine Harness Adaptor Cable**

Connect the engine harness, any engine harness extension cable used, and the ECM Deutsch connectors to the engine harness adaptor cable.

Use an inch-pound torque wrench, Part Number 3376592, with 4 mm \[5/32 in\] hex head adapter to tighten the connector jackscrew.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 2.8 n•m [25 in-lb]

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Do **not** over-torque as connector damage can occur.
