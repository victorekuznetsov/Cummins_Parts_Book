### Connector Replacement

The connectors are located in the middle of the main engine harness.

![[19802404.png]]

Cut and remove the plastic hold-down wire tie. Cut and remove the connector from the harness.

![[19802403.png]]

Remove approximately 6 mm \[1/4 in\] of insulation from the supply wires.

![[19802402.png]]

Install the replacement fuse holder, Part No. 3823758, on the bare wire.

![[19802401.png]]

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Only use wire crimping pliers Part No. 3822930.

Crimp the repair wire on the bare wire.

Use a heat gun, Part No. 3823860, or an open flame to heat the shrink tubing. The tubing will shrink and make the connection waterproof.
