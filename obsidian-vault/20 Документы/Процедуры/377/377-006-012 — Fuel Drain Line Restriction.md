# Fuel Drain Line Restriction
**Сопротивление магистрали слива топлива**

### Select Service Tools

#### Recommended Cummins® Service Tools

- Banjo test fitting, Part Number 4918413
- Compuchek™ fitting, Part Number 3042618.
- M22 Straight Thread O-Ring Plug (STOR) fitting, Part Number [[3089238]].

#### Additional Service Items

- 0 to 762 mm-hg \[0 to 30 in-Hg\] pressure/vacuum gauge.

### General Information

The fuel drain line restriction is measured at the fuel drain manifold.

![[nobox.png]]

### Setup

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Hold the banjo fittings while tightening the banjo bolt to prevent fitting rotation. Allowing the banjo fitting to rotate may damage the fuel line.

Remove the banjo bolt from the fuel pressure relief valve drain connection at the fuel drain manifold.

Install the banjo test fitting, Part Number 4918413, in place of the banjo bolt. Install Compuchek™ fitting, Part Number 3042618, or equivalent, into the banjo test fitting.

Attach a 0 to 762 mm-Hg \[0 to 30 in-Hg\] pressure/vacuum gauge to the banjo test fitting at the fuel injector drain connection at the fuel drain manifold.

If equipped, the fuel thermal recirculation valve should be bypassed by plugging the M22 o-ring threaded fittings using Part Number [[3089238]].

Make sure all fittings are installed and torqued to the correct specification. [[377-006-021 — Fuel Manifold (Drain)|Refer to Procedure 006-021]] in Section 6.

![[06l00057.png]]

### Measure

Operate the engine at cranking, low idle, or high idle, and measure the fuel pressure.

Observe the reading on the gauge.

| Drain Line Restriction |  |  |
|---|---|---|
| mm-hg |  | in-hg |
| 101.6 | MAX | 4 |

![[05900296.png]]

If the fuel drain line restriction is too high:

- Check the original equipment manufacturer (OEM) fuel lines to the tank for proper size, leaks, bends, or clogs.
- Check the fuel tank vents for plugging.

![[06d00193.png]]

### Finishing Steps

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Hold the banjo fittings while tightening the banjo bolt to prevent fitting rotation. Allowing the banjo fitting to rotate may damage the fuel line.

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Bleeding air from the fuel system is an important step. Air in fuel will make an engine hard to start or no start.

Connect the fuel lines, ensure the connection gives an audible click and then give a slight pull on the fuel line connector to make sure the connector is fully seated and locked.

Bleed air from the fuel system. Refer to Procedure 005-234 in Section 5.

Operate the engine. Check for leaks.
