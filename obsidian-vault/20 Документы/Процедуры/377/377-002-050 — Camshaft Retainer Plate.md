# Camshaft Retainer Plate

### General Information

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> This procedure deals primarily with the valve camshaft retainer.

### Remove

Remove the retainer capscrews, retainer seal, and o-ring.

Discard the seal and o-ring.

![[02c00162.png]]

### Clean and Inspect for Reuse

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> To reduce the possibility of personal injury, wear protective gloves when handling parts with sharp edges.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> When using solvents, acids, or alkaline materials for cleaning, follow the manufacturer's recommendations for use. Wear goggles and protective clothing to reduce the possibility of personal injury.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Wear appropriate eye and face protection when using compressed air. Flying debris and dirt can cause personal injury.

Inspect the camshaft retainer for cracks or other damage. If the retainer is cracked or otherwise damaged, it must be replaced.

### Install

Install a new o-ring and seal onto the retainer plate.

Install the retainer plate and capscrews.

Tighten the capscrews.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 40 n•m [30 ft-lb]
