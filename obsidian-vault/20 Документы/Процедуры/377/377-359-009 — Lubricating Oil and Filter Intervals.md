### General Information

Lubricating oil and filter change intervals are based on the following:

- Application
- Fuel consumption.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Extending the oil and filter change interval beyond recommended will decrease engine life due to corrosion, deposits, and wear.

The use of Centinel™ or any type of oil blending is prohibited. The use of a high quality filter is mandatory.

See the following bulletin for information regarding engine oil analysis:

- Fluid for Cummins® Products Service Manual, Bulletin [[5411406 — Fluids for Cummins® Products Service Manual\|5411406]].

See Table 1 for oil drain maintenance intervals for the following vehicle applications:

- Recreational
- Refuse
- Mixer
- Dump
- Delivery
- Logging
- Fire Truck
- Crane.

Oil drain maintenance intervals for all other applications are based on fuel consumption. See Table 2.

1. Use distance value found in Table 2 or 12 months, whichever comes first.
2. Drain intervals can be extended with participation in the Cummins® OilGuard™ oil analysis program.
3. If combined percent idle time plus power takeoff (PTO) time is greater than 40 percent for Short Haul, Normal, or Light intervals, use the next lower drain interval. For Extreme Severe and Severe intervals, use the published drain interval regardless of the combined idle plus PTO time.
4. [[377-018-003 — Lubricating Oil Recommendations and Specifications|Refer to Procedure 018-003 in Section V]] for information on oil type and grade.
5. To align fuel filter intervals with oil drain intervals, an Extended Life FF5971NN is recommended and requires an upfit kit to be fitted on the engine. [[377-359-008 — Fuel Filter Intervals|Refer to Procedure 359-008]] in Section 3.
6. An Extended Life LF14001NN oil filter is recommended to achieve intervals beyond 97,000 km \[60,000 mi\].
