# Air Intake Connection Adapter

> [!abstract] Процедура · `377-010-131`
> **Входит в руководства:** [[5411181 — X15 CM2350 X114B - Efficiency Series Service Manual|5411181]]
> **Секции:** Section 10 - Air Intake System - Group 10
> **Даты:** изменён 2017-02-03
> **Источник:** [QuickServe](https://quickserve.cummins.com/qs3/pubsys2/xml/en/procedures/377/377-010-131.html) · [PDF-оригинал](https://github.com/victorekuznetsov/Cummins_Parts_Book/raw/main/bulletins/procedures/377-010-131.pdf)

### Select Service Tools

#### Recommended Cummins® Service Tools

- Air Handling Clean Care Kit, Part Number 4919588.

#### Additional Service Items

- No additional service items required.

### Preparatory Steps

- Remove the charge air piping connection from the air inlet connection. See equipment manufacturer service information.
- Remove the air inlet connection from the air intake connection adapter. Refer to Procedure 010-022 in Section 10.
- Disconnect the air compressor inlet tube from the air intake connection adapter. [[377-012-109 — Air Compressor Inlet Tube|Refer to Procedure 012-109 in Section 12.]]

### Remove

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Make sure to support the air intake connection before removing the mounting capscrews. If the air intake connection adapter is not supported, it can fall off the engine and damage the components.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Brush away all loose dirt from around the area of the air handling connections to avoid contamination of the interior of the engine.

Remove the four capscrews that hold the air intake connection adapter to the intake manifold.

Remove and discard the o-ring.

Use protective caps from Air Handling Clear Care Kit, Part Number 4919588, to cover the intake manifold inlet and prevent debris from entering the engine while the air intake connection adapter is removed.

![[10t00051.png]]

### Inspect for Reuse

Inspect the snorkel of the air intake connection adapter for signs of soot buildup.

Inspect the air compressor if signs of soot buildup are found. Refer to Procedure 012-003 in Section 12.

![[10t00052.png]]

Inspect the air intake connection adapter for cracks or other damage.

Inspect the o-ring groove on the air intake connection adapter for signs of damage.

Replace the air intake connection adapter if cracks or other damage is found.

![[10t00053.png]]

Inspect the sealing surface on the front of the air intake manifold.

Repair or replace the air intake manifold if damage is found. [[377-010-023 — Air Intake Manifold|Refer to Procedure 010-023 in Section 10.]]

![[10t00018.png]]

Inspect the four mounting capscrews for stripped threads and other damage.

Replace the mounting capscrews if damage is found.

![[10200103.png]]

### Install

Remove the covering from the intake manifold inlet prior to installing the air intake connection adapter.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Be sure that the o-ring is fully seated and tight in the o-ring groove. If the o-ring is **not** fully seated, it can fall out during installation.

Install a new o-ring on the air intake connection adapter.

![[10t00054.png]]

Install the air intake connection adapter and four mounting capscrews hand-tight.

Tighten the capscrews.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 20 n•m [177 in-lb]

![[10t00051.png]]

### Finishing Steps

- Install the air inlet connection to the air intake connection adapter. Refer to Procedure 010-022 in Section 10.
- Install the charge air piping. See equipment manufacturer service information.
- Connect the air compressor supply line. Refer to Procedure 012-024 in Section 12.
- Operate the engine to normal operating temperature and check for leaks.
