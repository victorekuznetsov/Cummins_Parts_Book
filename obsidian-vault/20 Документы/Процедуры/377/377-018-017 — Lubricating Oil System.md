### Specifications

#### Oil Pressure

| At Idle (minimum allowable at 93°C \[ 200°F \] oil temperature) | 103 kPa \[ 15 psi \] |
|---|---|

| At No-Load Governed Speed | 155 to 324 kPa \[ 22.5 to 47 psi \] |
|---|---|

#### Pressure Drop Across Oil Filter

| Maximum Allowable | 172 kPa \[ 25 psi \] |
|---|---|

#### Oil Pan Drain

| Fitting Size | M27x2 STOR |
|---|---|

The lubricating oil change fill volume is the amount of oil added if the lubricating oil pan has been fully drained for a service event, then refilled for operation. Lubricating oil fill volumes are unique to the installed dipstick of the engine being serviced.

If the lubricating oil change fill volume is **not** known:

- Contact a Cummins® Distributor/Dealer.
- Determine the capacity of the lubricating oil pan option for the engine being serviced by using QuickServe™ Online (QSOL) and the engine serial number.

Fill the lubricating oil pan to the smallest lubricating oil pan capacity listed for the engine being serviced. Then add 0.95 liters \[ 1 U.S. qt \] of lubricating oil at a time until the lubricating oil reaches the high mark on the lubricating oil dipstick. Record the number of liters/quarts or lubricating oil added, so the lubricating oil capacity is known the next time the lubricating oil is drained.
