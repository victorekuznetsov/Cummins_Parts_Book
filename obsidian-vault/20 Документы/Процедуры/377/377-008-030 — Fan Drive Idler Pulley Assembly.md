# Fan Drive Idler Pulley Assembly
**Натяжной ролик привода вентилятора в сборе**

> [!abstract] Процедура · `377-008-030`
> **Входит в руководства:** [[5411181 — X15 CM2350 X114B - Efficiency Series Service Manual|5411181]]
> **Секции:** Section 8 - Cooling System - Group 08
> **Даты:** изменён 2022-11-14
> **Источник:** [QuickServe](https://quickserve.cummins.com/qs3/pubsys2/xml/en/procedures/377/377-008-030.html) · [PDF-оригинал](https://github.com/victorekuznetsov/Cummins_Parts_Book/raw/main/bulletins/procedures/377-008-030.pdf)

### Preparatory Steps

- Remove the fan drive belt. Refer to Procedure 008-002 in Section 8.

![[ck800wa.png]]

### Remove

On initial installation, remove the three capscrews indicated in the illustration from the front cover.

![[08r00580.png]]

### Install

Measure the idler bracket mounting capscrew lengths. If the idler bracket mounting capscrews measure less than 65 mm \[ 2.56 in \] long, replace the capscrews with new Class 10.9 equivalent bolts of 65 mm in length.

Route the crankshaft speed sensor wire harness behind the bracket.

Install the bracket on the engine with the three capscrews.

Tighten the capscrews.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 41 n•m [30 ft-lb]

![[08r00582.png]]

Mount the fan drive idler pulley assembly to the fan drive idler pulley bracket and tighten the capscrew. The flange-head side of the capscrew **must** be positioned on the deeper recessed side of the pulley and the washer **must** be centered.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 47 n•m [35 ft-lb]

![[08r00583.png]]

Install the dust shield by pressing on the outside diameter of the shield to prevent damage to the dust shield.

Use an appropriate size driver or other suitable tool.

![[08r00581.png]]

### Finishing Steps

Install the drive belt. Refer to Procedure 008-002 in Section 8.

![[08c00320.png]]
