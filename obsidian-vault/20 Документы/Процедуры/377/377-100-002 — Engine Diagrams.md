### Engine Views

The following illustrations show the location of the major external engine components, filters, and other service and maintenance points. Some external components will be at different locations for different engine models.

2. Refrigerant compressor belt drive
3. Camshaft position sensor
4. Naturally aspirated air compressor tube
5. Crankcase breather pressure sensor
6. Exhaust gas recirculation (EGR) crossover tube
7. Air intake connection
8. Gear housing cover, upper
9. Lubricating oil fill tube
10. Water pump belt drive
11. Gear housing cover, lower
12. Vibration damper
13. Lubricating oil pan
14. Water pump, dual outlet
15. Belt tensioner, water pump
16. Alternator
17. Turbocharger, variable geometry
18. Belt tensioner, refrigerant compressor.
