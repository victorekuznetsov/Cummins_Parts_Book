### General Information

Before the installation or removal of any gears, you **must** read the Engine Base Timing procedure. [[377-001-088 — Engine Base Timing|Refer to Procedure 001-088 in Section 1.]]

### Disassemble

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Do not damage the outer diameter of the idler shaft.

Separate the idler shaft (1) from the mounting spacer (2).

Remove the dowel ring.

![[01010021.png]]

### Assemble

Install the dowel ring into the idler shaft counterbore (1). The dowel ring will bottom out on the counterbore ledge.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The counterbore ledge will allow the dowel ring to maintain its position during installation of the mounting spacer. If the dowel ring is installed in the mounting spacer first, there is no counterbore ledge to provide positive stop for the dowel ring.

Install the mounting spacer onto the idler shaft until it bottoms out on the back of the idler shaft.

![[01010022.png]]

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Make sure the idler shaft and mounting spacer are oriented correctly. The idler shaft face that shows OIL PAN with a downward arrow should be facing away from the block. The arrow should be pointing in the 6 o'clock direction toward the oil pan.

Verify dowel depth. [[377-001-088 — Engine Base Timing|Refer to Procedure 001-088 in Section 1.]]
