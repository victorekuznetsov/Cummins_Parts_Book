### General Information

Fuel filter change intervals are based on the following:

- Fuel consumption.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Extending the fuel filter change interval beyond recommended will decrease engine life due to corrosion, depositions, and wear.

Suction-Side Fuel Filter Change Interval Fuel Consumption

Check the suction-side fuel filter during oil drain maintenance intervals. See Lubricating Oil and Filter Intervals procedure. [[377-359-009 — Lubricating Oil and Filter Intervals\|Refer to Procedure 359-009]] in Section 3.

To check the suction-side fuel filter, see equipment manufacture service information.

Suction-side fuel filters are OEM installed and can vary based on OEM hardware selection and fuel quality.

| Pressure-Side Fuel Filter Change Interval Fuel Consumption |  |  |  |  |  |  |  |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Extreme Severe \< 1.27 km/liter \[3 mpg\] | Severe 1.27 to 2.1 km/liter \[3 to 4.9 mpg\] | Short Haul 2.1 to 2.5 km/liter \[5 to 5.9 mpg\] | Normal 2.5 to 3 km/liter \[6 to 6.9 mpg\] | Light \> 3 km/liter \[7 mpg\] |  |  |  |
| Standard | Standard | Standard | Extended (1)(2)(3) | Standard | Extended (1)(2)(3) | Standard | Extended (1)(2)(3) |
| 7,000 km \[4,500 mi\] | 40,000 km \[25,000 mi\] | 64,000 km \[40,000 mi\] | 81,000 km \[50,000 mi\] | 81,000 km \[50,000 mi\] | 97,000 km \[60,000 mi\] | 97,000 km \[60,000 mi\] | 121,000 km \[75,000 mi\] |

1. An Extended Life FF5971NN pressure-side fuel filter is recommended to achieve this interval and align with the oil drain interval.
2. To fit an Extended Life FF5971NN pressure-side fuel filter, an upfit is required. See Installation Instruction, Thermal Recirculation Valve Upfit Kit, Bulletin 5414689.
3. A Davco 400 series, or equivalent, capacity suction-side filter is recommended to align the suction-side filter change interval with the oil drain interval.
