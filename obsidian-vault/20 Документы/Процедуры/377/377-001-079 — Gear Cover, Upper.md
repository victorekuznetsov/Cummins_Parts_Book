# Gear Cover, Upper

> [!abstract] Процедура · `377-001-079`
> **Входит в руководства:** [[5411181 — X15 CM2350 X114B - Efficiency Series Service Manual|5411181]]

> **Даты:** изменён 2025-10-10
> **Источник:** [QuickServe](https://quickserve.cummins.com/qs3/pubsys2/xml/en/procedures/377/377-001-079.html) · [PDF-оригинал](https://github.com/victorekuznetsov/Cummins_Parts_Book/raw/main/bulletins/procedures/377-001-079.pdf)

### Select Service Tools

#### Recommended Cummins® Service Tools

- No Cummins® service tools required.

#### Additional Service Items

- Loctite® 242, Part Number 3824040.

### Preparatory Steps

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Batteries can emit explosive gases. To reduce the possibility of personal injury, always ventilate the compartment before servicing the batteries. To reduce the possibility of arcing, remove the negative (-) battery cable first and attach the negative (-) battery cable last.

- Disconnect the batteries. See equipment manufacturer service information.
- Remove the fan drive belt. Refer to Procedure 008-002 in Section 8.
- Remove the water pump drive belt. Refer to Procedure 008-003 in Section 8.
- Remove the fan hub assembly. Refer to Procedure 008-036 in Section 8.
- Remove the vibration damper. [[377-001-052 — Vibration Damper, Viscous|Refer to Procedure 001-052 in Section 1.]]
- Remove the breather tube and hose. Refer to Procedure 003-001 in Section 3.

### Remove

Remove the capscrews.

Loosen the three hex nuts until there is enough clearance for the upper gear cover to be lifted off the studs.

Remove the upper gear cover.

Remove and discard the strip seal.

![[01l00041.png]]

### Clean and Inspect for Reuse

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> When using solvents, acids, or alkaline materials for cleaning, follow the manufacturer's recommendations for use. Wear goggles and protective clothing to reduce the possibility of personal injury.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Wear appropriate eye and face protection when using compressed air. Flying debris and dirt can cause personal injury.

Use solvent to clean the gear cover.

Dry with compressed air.

![[01l00042.png]]

Clean the sealing surface on the rear face of the upper gear cover and the seal groove on the gear housing.

![[01l00043.png]]

Inspect the upper gear cover and stiffening plates for cracks or other damage.

If the cover is cracked or otherwise damaged, it **must** be replaced.

![[01l00045.png]]

Put the upper gear cover on a flat surface and inspect it for flatness.

![[01c00009.png]]

It is an acceptable practice to use a mallet to straighten an upper gear cover with minor bends.

If necessary, lay the gear cover on a flat surface and hit the upper gear to remove minor bends.

![[01c00010.png]]

### Install

Install a new strip seal.

Orient the overlap at the top and left of the gear housing.

Use a small amount of RTV at the overlap joint of the strip seal.

![[01c00788.png]]

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Both the gear cover and lower stiffener plate are slotted to aid in the installation.

Install the cover onto the lower three studs.

Use a lint free cloth to wipe any debris or loose thread locker residue from capscrews.

Apply Loctite® 242, Part Number 3824040, thread locker to the capscrew threads and bottom three studs.

Install the capscrews and three hex nuts hand-tight.

![[01l00041.png]]

Tighten the capscrews and hex nuts in the sequence shown.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 31 n•m [23 ft-lb]

![[01l00044.png]]

### Finishing Steps

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Batteries can emit explosive gases. To reduce the possibility of personal injury, always ventilate the compartment before servicing the batteries. To reduce the possibility of arcing, remove the negative (-) battery cable first and attach the negative (-) battery cable last.

- Install the vibration damper. [[377-001-052 — Vibration Damper, Viscous|Refer to Procedure 001-052 in Section 1.]]
- Install the fan hub assembly. Refer to Procedure 008-036 in Section 8.
- Install the water pump drive belt. Refer to Procedure 008-003 in Section 8.
- Install the fan drive belt. Refer to Procedure 008-002 in Section 8.
- Install the breather tube and hose. Refer to Procedure 003-001 in Section 3.
- Connect the batteries. See equipment manufacturer service information.
- Operate the engine to normal operating temperature. Check for leaks and proper operation.
