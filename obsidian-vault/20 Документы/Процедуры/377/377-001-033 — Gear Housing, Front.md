- Reusable plug, Part Number 4918874

### General Information

This illustration depicts the gear housing with the gear covers removed for gear trains with a solid style lower idler gear.

1. Camshaft gear, valve
2. Idler gear, adjustable
3. Idler gear, upper
4. Accessory gear
5. Idler gear, fuel pump
6. Fuel pump gear
7. Idler gear, lower solid.

The crankshaft gear is **not** shown and does **not** need to be removed in order to remove the gear housing.

### Preparatory Steps

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Batteries can emit explosive gases. To reduce the possibility of personal injury, always ventilate the compartment before servicing the batteries. To reduce the possibility of arcing, remove the negative (-) battery cable first and attach the negative (-) battery cable last.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Do not remove the pressure cap from a hot engine. Wait until the coolant temperature is below 50°C \[120°F\] before removing the pressure cap. Heated coolant spray or steam can cause personal injury.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Coolant is toxic. Keep away from children and pets. If not reused, dispose of in accordance with local environmental regulations.

### Remove

Remove two capscrews and install two guide studs.

![[01c00819.png]]

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> This component or assembly weighs greater than 23 kg \[50 lb\]. To prevent serious personal injury, be sure to have assistance or use appropriate lifting equipment to lift this component or assembly.

Remove and discard capscrew(s) 2 through 13.

- Captive washer capscrew M10 x 1.5 x 25.

![[01c00825.png]]

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> This component or assembly weighs greater than 23 kg \[50 lb\]. To prevent serious personal injury, be sure to have assistance or use appropriate lifting equipment to lift this component or assembly.

Remove the remaining two capscrews.

Remove the housing and gasket.

Discard the gasket.

Remove the guide studs.

![[01c00820.png]]

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Immediately upon removal of the front gear housing, a plug must be inserted into the cylinder block oil passage drilling. Failure to insert the oil passage plug can result in a bearing failure, crankshaft failure, or both.

The reusable plug, Part Number 4918874, is necessary to prevent debris from entering the lubrication system during the repair.

Insert the plug into the front oil passage.

Firmly push the plug into the oil passage to prevent debris from entering the lubricating oil system when cleaning the gasket surface.

![[02c00144.png]]

### Clean and Inspect for Reuse

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> When using solvents, acids, or alkaline materials for cleaning, follow the manufacturer's recommendations for use. Wear goggles and protective clothing to reduce the possibility of personal injury.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Wear appropriate eye and face protection when using compressed air. Flying debris and dirt can cause personal injury.

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> If the oil passages are not plugged before cleaning, a crankshaft failure can occur.

Clean the gasket surfaces by hand with a gasket scraper or cleaning pad, Part Number 3823258. If the gasket material residue can **not** be felt with a finger, the surface is ready to accept the new gasket.

Use solvent to clean the gear housing.

Dry with compressed air.

![[01c00454.png]]

Use compressed air to clear the oil galley to the air compressor and fuel pump of any obstructions.

![[01c00821.png]]

Inspect the gear housing for cracks or other damage.

Inspect the seal grooves of the housing for debris or damage.

If the gear housing is cracked or otherwise damaged, it **must** be replaced.

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> If an incompatible gear housing and fuel pump idler shaft are assembled together engine damage can occur.

If replacing the front gear housing with a new part, ensure that the fuel pump idler is compatible with the new gear housing.

![[01c00822.png]]

### Install

Remove the protective plug from the cylinder block oil passage.

Make sure **no** debris enters the lubricating oil system.

Use a clean shop towel to carefully wipe out the oil passage. Inspect the oil passages to make sure **no** debris is left.

![[01c00453.png]]

Install two guide studs.

Install a new gear housing mounting gasket.

![[01c00823.png]]

The gear housing has the words “Special Capscrew” cast into the housing at the mounting location (see illustration). This capscrew **must** be the short head capscrew to prevent contact between the capscrew and the idler gear.

![[01c00824.png]]

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> This component or assembly weighs greater than 23 kg \[50 lb\]. To prevent serious personal injury, be sure to have assistance or use appropriate lifting equipment to lift this component or assembly.

Install the gear housing.

![[01c00819.png]]

Install the mounting capscrews.

Four different length capscrews are utilized on the front gear housing. Use the table below to identify the capscrews and their mounting locations.

| Capscrew Mounting Location |  |  |
|---|---|---|
| Location | Size | Quantity |
| 14 | M10 x 1.5 x 100 | 1 |
| 15, 16, 17 | M10 x 1.5 x 70 | 3 |
| 1 | M10 x 1.5 x 21.8 (Special Short Head) | 1 |
| All remaining | M10 x 1.5 x 25 | 12 |

Remove the guide studs and install the two remaining capscrews.

Tighten the capscrews in the sequence shown.

Torque Value:

All Capscrews Except (1)

Torque Value:

Capscrew (1)
