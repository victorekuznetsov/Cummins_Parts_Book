# Crankcase Breather (External)
**Сапун картера (наружный)**

> [!abstract] Процедура · `57-003-001-tr`
> **Двигатели:** [[37292556 — QST30 CM552 CPL 1244|37292556]], [[37295879 — QST30 CM552 CPL 2139|37295879]]
> **Семейство:** QST30
> **Входит в руководства:** [[4021539 — QST30 Service Manual|4021539]]
> **Секции:** Section 3 - Rocker Levers - Group 03
> **Даты:** изменён 2019-02-06
> **Источник:** [QuickServe](https://quickserve.cummins.com/qs3/pubsys2/xml/en/procedures/57/57-003-001-tr.html) · [PDF-оригинал](https://github.com/victorekuznetsov/Cummins_Parts_Book/raw/main/bulletins/procedures/57-003-001-tr.pdf)

### Remove

Remove the support bracket capscrews and the bracket.

Remove the breather tube and the hose from the engine.

Remove the crankcase breather.

Discard the o-ring.

![[03a00029.png]]

### Clean

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> If breather tubes are blocked or capped, oil pans can explode under pressure. To reduce the possibility of engine damage or personal injury, always ensure both breather tubes are free of any blockage or caps before starting the engine.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> When using solvents, acids, or alkaline materials for cleaning, follow the manufacturer's recommendations for use. Wear goggles and protective clothing to reduce the possibility of personal injury.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Wear appropriate eye and face protection when using compressed air. Flying debris and dirt can cause personal injury.

Use solvent to clean the breather tube, hose, and crankcase breather. Dry with compressed air.

![[03a00013.png]]

### Inspect for Reuse

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Wear appropriate eye and face protection when using compressed air. Flying debris and dirt can cause personal injury.

Use compressed air to blow through the breather cavity.

If the breather cavity is clogged and the restriction can **not** be removed by cleaning, the breather **must** be replaced.

Use air pressure to blow through the tube.

If the tube is clogged and the restriction can **not** be removed by cleaning, the tube **must** be replaced.

![[07a00048.png]]

### Install

Install the o-ring onto the breather.

Install the breather onto the front gear cover.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 27 n•m [239 in-lb]

![[03a00014.png]]

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> If breather tubes are blocked or capped, oil pans can explode under pressure. To reduce the possibility of engine damage or personal injury, always ensure both breather tubes are free of any blockage or caps before starting the engine.

Install the tube and the hose on the engine.

Install the tube support bracket and capscrew.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 65 n•m [48 ft-lb]

Tighten the hose clamp at the breather vent tube.

![[03a00005.png]]
