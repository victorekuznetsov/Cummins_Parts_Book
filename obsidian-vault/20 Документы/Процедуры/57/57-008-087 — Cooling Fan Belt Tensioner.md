# Cooling Fan Belt Tensioner
**Натяжитель ремня вентилятора**

> [!abstract] Процедура · `57-008-087`
> **Двигатели:** [[37292556 — QST30 CM552 CPL 1244|37292556]], [[37295879 — QST30 CM552 CPL 2139|37295879]]
> **Семейство:** QST30
> **Входит в руководства:** [[3666134 — QST30 Operation and Maintenance Manual|3666134]]
> **Секции:** Section A - Adjustment, Repair, and Replacement
> **Даты:** изменён 2002-11-22

### Remove

Shock Absorber System

Loosen the fan belt tension. Refer to Procedure 008-002.

Remove the fan drive belt. Refer to Procedure 008-002.

![[fa6blha.png]]

Remove the backside idler pulley end of the shock absorber.

Loosen the upper capscrew (2). Remove the lower capscrew (1).

![[08a00024.png]]

Turnbuckle Idler System

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> One of the jam nuts on the solid control rod (turnbuckle) has left-handed threads.

Loosen the solid control rod (turnbuckle) jam nuts (1). Turn the adjusting screw (2) until the spacer (3) is **not** touching the end of the slot in the control rod (4).

![[08a00028.png]]

Remove the capscrew (1), washers (2, 3), and spacers (4).

Remove the control rod assembly from the idler assembly.

![[08a00029.png]]

Industrial System

Remove the capscrews and washers from the lower clevis and idler arm.

Remove the nut and the capscrew from the threaded rod end of the tensioner at the upper anchor bracket.

Remove the industrial fan belt tensioner.

![[08a00169.png]]

### Inspect for Reuse

Power Generation

Check the clearance.

| mm |  | in |
|---|---|---|
| 0.08 | MIN | 0.003 |
| 0.25 | MAX | 0.010 |

Rotate the pulley. Check for rough or damaged bearings.

The bearings **must** be replaced if any of the above conditions are found.

![[fa4idsb.png]]

Check the shock absorber for cracks and fluid leakage.

Push the shock absorber up and down. Check the vibration absorption.

The shock absorber **must** be replaced if any of the above conditions are found.

![[fa4idsc.png]]

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Some engines have a turnbuckle instead of a shock absorber. Check the parts of the turnbuckle for damage.

![[fa4idsd.png]]

Industrial System

Remove the cotter pin from the clevis pin.

![[08a00167.png]]

Remove the clevis pin from the control rod and belt tensioner housing and separate.

![[08a00166.png]]

Remove the control rod from the control rod retainer.

![[08a00165.png]]

Remove the first nut from the control rod threaded end.

![[08a00164.png]]

Remove the two flat washers from the control rod threaded end.

![[08a00163.png]]

Remove the remaining nut from the control rod threaded end.

![[08a00162.png]]

Remove the control rod from the capscrew threaded end.

![[08a00161.png]]

Remove the nut holding the dust seal to the capscrew.

![[08a00160.png]]

Cut the wire tie to remove the dust seal from the belt tensioner housing.

![[08a00159.png]]

Remove the dust seal from the threaded end of the capscrew and belt tensioner housing.

![[08a00158.png]]

Install complete unit onto the build fixture with the head of the capscrew and remove the belt tensioner housing from the belt tensioner assembled parts.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> It is **not** necessary to use a build fixture to disassemble or assemble as shown in the picture. This is an aid to clarify the steps in the procedures.

![[08a00157.png]]

Remove the compression spring from the spring retainer.

![[08a00155.png]]

Remove the spring retainer from the spring guide.

![[08a00154.png]]

Remove the spring guide from the flat washer.

![[08a00153.png]]

Remove the flat washer from the capscrew.

![[08a00152.png]]

Measure the free length of the spring

| mm |  | in |
|---|---|---|
| 147.3 | MIN | 5.80 |
| 155.4 | MAX | 6.12 |

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Replace with new springs if **not** within specifications.

![[kn8sptd.png]]

Install the flat washer onto the capscrew.

![[08a00152.png]]

Install the spring guide with the recessed/indented side next to the flat washer.

![[08a00153.png]]

Install the spring retainer with the large flat surface against the spring guide.

![[08a00154.png]]

Install the compression spring against the spring retainer.

![[08a00155.png]]

Set the assembled parts in the small build fixture with the head of the capscrew setting in the socket on the top of the build fixture.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> It is **not** necessary to use a build fixture to disassemble or assemble as shown in the picture. This is an aid to clarify the steps in the procedures.

![[08a00156.png]]

Set the belt tensioner housing over the assembled parts and allow the bottom of the housing to set on the build fixture with the threads of the capscrew protruding out of the hole in the top of the belt tensioner housing.

![[08a00157.png]]

Slip the dust seal over the end of the capscrew and the edge of the belt tensioner housing.

![[08a00158.png]]

Use a wire tie and fasten around the dust seal where it overlaps the belt tensioner housing.

Pull the tie until tight and cut off the excess of the tie.

![[08a00159.png]]

Push the dust seal down over threads of the capscrew and install the nut.

Use a 3/4-inch open-end wrench to tighten the nut.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Be sure just to tighten the nut far enough to get clearance for the following part to be installed. Do **not** completely tighten at this time.

![[08a00160.png]]

Install the control rod end on the threaded end of the capscrew.

Back the nut against the control rod and tighten.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 100 n•m [74 ft-lb]

![[08a00161.png]]

Install the nut and install on the control rod end.

![[08a00162.png]]

Install two flat washers onto the control rod end.

![[08a00163.png]]

Install a second nut onto the control rod end.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Do **not** tighten completely. Leave the washers loose.

![[08a00164.png]]

Install the assembled control rod end into the control rod retainer's slotted end.

![[08a00165.png]]

Install the assembled control rod parts into the end of the belt tensioner housing.

Align the holes and install the clevis pin to hold the parts together.

![[08a00166.png]]

Use a cotter pin to secure the clevis pin to the housings.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Bend the cotter pin around the clevis pin to keep from falling out during operation.

![[08a00167.png]]

### Install

Power Generation

The backside idler system uses a control rod with a spring (4) held by a retaining washers (2, 3) and jam nuts (1) or a shock absorber.

![[08a00030.png]]

Shock Absorber System

Install the fan hub assembly end of the shock absorber.

Tighten the upper capscrew (2).

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 52 n•m [38 ft-lb]

Install and tighten the lower capscrew for the idler pulley end of the shock absorber (1).

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 47 n•m [35 ft-lb]

![[08a00024.png]]

Turnbuckle Idler System

To install the control rod with spring, install the flat washer (3), lock washer (4), and capscrews (5) in the upper end of the control rod (2).

Install the control rod in the fan support.

Tighten the capscrew.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 60 n•m [44 ft-lb]

Install the spacer bushing (6), flat washer (7), lock washer (8), and capscrew (1) in the lower end of the control rod (2).

Install the lower end of the control rod on the fan idler arm.

Tighten the capscrew.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 60 n•m [44 ft-lb]

![[08a00023.png]]

Industrial System

Install the industrial tensioner.

Slide the threaded-rod end of the tensioner through the hole in the upper anchor bracket and secure with a washer and nut.

Do **not** tighten the nut. It will be tightened later to adjust the belt tension.

Attach the lower clevis end of the tensioner to the idler arm with capscrews and washers.

Tighten the capscrews.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 92 n•m [68 ft-lb]

![[08a00169.png]]

### Adjust

Power Generation

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> One of the nuts has left-handed threads.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> There is no adjustment required for engines equipped with a shock absorber.

The fan belt **must** be installed and under the tension of the fan idler arm spring to adjust the control rod.

The fan belt and a portion of the flat washer are **not** shown for clarity.

Turn the adjusting screw (2) until the end of the slot on the lower control rod end (4) is touching the spacer (3).

Hold the adjusting screw and tighten the two jam nuts (1).

![[08a00028.png]]

Install the fan drive belt. Refer to Procedure 008-002.

![[fa6blha.png]]

Industrial System

The fan belt is tensioned at the threaded rod end of the tensioner at the upper anchor bracket.

To adjust tension, loosen the jam nut (2) and adjust to specification with the tensioning nut (1).

Tighten the jam nut.

Use service tool belt tension gauge for industrial engines, Part Number 3164750, to adjust the belt tension.

The fan belt tension specification is 2669 N \[600 lbf\].
