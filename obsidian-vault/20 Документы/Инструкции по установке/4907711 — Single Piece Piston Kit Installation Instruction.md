## Single Piece Piston Kit Installation Instruction

### Purpose

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> If the engine has not been completely upgraded to Monotherm™ pistons, replacing any fewer than all six (6) articulated pistons with Monotherm™ pistons could result in significant engine damage.

This kit contains one (1) Monotherm™ piston kit. It is important to note that articulated pistons are **not** compatible with Monotherm® pistons. Therefore, the engine can **only** operate with either all articulated pistons or all Monotherm™ pistons.

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Failure to install connecting rods and rod bearings with an oil feed hole to Monotherm™ pistons could result in significant engine damage.

When upgrading to Monotherm™ pistons, connecting rods that are equipped with the oil drilling to the wrist pin bushing, along with drilled rod bearings **must** be used.
