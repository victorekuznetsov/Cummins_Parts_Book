**1. Introduction1.1 About These Assembly Instructions**

The purpose of this document is to provide assembly instructions for Cummins® Off-Highway Engines. These instructions should be followed during the assembly and installation of the engine into the OEM equipment/machine.

OEMs are responsible for complying with the requirements of Cummins® Advisor Installation Requirements (AIRs) which highlight interfaces, installation requirements, and operating limits in order to achieve "Approved Installation" status. An Approved Installation indicates all applicable Cummins® AIRs have been verified/documented jointly between the OEM Powertrain Engineer and the Cummins® Application Engineering Team. All pertinent AIR information necessary for engine installation will be contained in OEM Manuals.

The production engines Cummins Inc. provide vary in option content. Engine Serial Number (ESN) specific information should be evaluated to ensure that appropriate installation requirements and manuals are referenced during installation. Should further assistance be required, please contact your OEM Powertrain Engineer or authorized Cummins® distributor.

References can be made using Cummins® ESN specific literature such as the Operation and Maintenance Manual, Service Manual, Fault Code Troubleshooting Manual and Fluids for Cummins® Products Service Manual. Service literature is accessible using the following websites:

**1.1.1 Intended Use**

Cummins® Off-Highway Engines received by OEMs are considered Partly Completed Machinery (PCM).

The PCM in this case is an internal combustion diesel engine. A diesel engine is defined as an internal combustion engine in which ignition of diesel fuel is caused by the elevated temperature of the air in the cylinder due to mechanical compression.

The PCM will **not** function until the OEM integrates into equipment and all installation requirements are met and deemed an "Approved Installation" by Cummins®. The PCM will arrive at engine installer's facility secured to material handling hardware such as a skid or enclosure.

**1.3 Safety Precautions**

Read these instructions completely and become familiar with safety warnings, cautions, and procedures before starting the installation.

Only authorized and qualified maintenance technicians, who are familiar with the equipment and its operation, should carry out maintenance.

Before carrying out any maintenance work, become familiar with engine safety by reviewing all the documentation supplied with the engine.

Before carrying out any maintenance work, lock the machine and/or engine out of service for safe working.

To reduce the possibility of personal injury, personal protective equipment (PPE) is recommended to be utilized. Always meet on-site safety regulations for required PPE. Proper maintenance of safety equipment **must** be practiced. Integrity of safety equipment **must** be checked to verify equipment functionality is maintained.

**Incorrect Installation:**

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Incorrect installation, service, or parts replacement can result in severe personal injury, and/or equipment damage. Service personnel must be trained and experienced to perform electrical and mechanical component installations.

**Fall Hazard:**

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Falls can result in severe personal injury. Make sure that suitable equipment for performing tasks at height are used in accordance with local guidelines and legislation.

**Lifting:**

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> The engine lifting equipment must be designed to lift the engine and the OEM powertrain/upfitted components as an assembly without causing personal injury.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> For components that weigh 23 kg \[ 50 lb \] or more use a hoist or get assistance to lift to avoid possibility of personal injury.

**Sharp Edges and Sharp Points:**

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Projecting corners/parts may cause cuts, abrasions and other personal injury. Use PPE. Be aware of sharp edges and corners/sharp points. Cover/protect them.

**Fluid Exposure:**

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> For proper handling of various substances, refer to the manufacturer's safety data sheet. PPE must be utilized as required.

**Slip Hazard:**

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> Spilled coolant, fuels and lubricating fluids are a slip hazard and can cause severe personal injury. Clean up any spilled fluids before operating the engine.

**1.6 Protection From Hot Surfaces**

The engine may be supplied with heat shielding hardware to guard against hot surfaces. Heat shielding provided with the engine **must not** be modified.

Other surfaces that may get hot include but are **not** limited to cylinder block, cylinder head, exhaust manifold and turbocharger components.

The engine installer **must** make sure that protections from hot surfaces comply with local and national regulations.

**1.7 Protection From Moving Parts**

If guards provided with the engine for moving parts are disturbed or modified during installation, the engine installer **must** comply with local and national regulations.

**1.8 Protection From Noise**

High Noise Level:

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Engines in operation emit noise, which can cause hearing damage. Wear appropriate ear protection at all times.

After installation the engine can emit noise during operation. The engine installer is responsible for assessing any hazard that may result from noise emissions and for providing warnings and hearing protection advice.

The engine installer is responsible for making sure conditions comply with local and national regulations.

**1.9 Protection From Vibration**

The equipment produces vibration during operation. The engine installer is responsible for assessing the effects of vibration on the installation design and for including appropriate vibration considerations within the machinery design or installation.

**1.10 Control System**

The engine is supplied without a control system. The engine installer **must** make sure that a suitable control and wiring system is fitted to control and protect the equipment in accordance with local and national regulations.

**1.11 Stability**

The engine installer is responsible for making sure that the equipment installation complies with local and national regulations, and is stable under all foreseeable conditions, such as:

- Load
- Overload
- Fault Conditions
- Conditions during Transit
- Positioning

All installation, commissioning and operation **must** be completed in accordance with local, national and regional legislation.
