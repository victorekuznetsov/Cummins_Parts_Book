## Steel Fuel Supply Tube Installation Instructions

**Product Affected**

- ISX15 CM2350 X101
- X15 CM2350 114B

The purpose of this document is to provide installation instructions for two newly released fuel supply tube kits; plumbing fuel from the low-pressure pump to the secondary fuel filter. New steel fuel supply tube(s) with “flexible” section have been introduced for increased robustness against demanding operating conditions. Improved fuel supply tubes can be retrofitted to existing nylon fuel supply tube configuration(s). See Figure 1 and Figure 2 for visual representations.

It is **not** necessary to remove the fuel filter for this installation.

**Personal Protection Equipment (PPE)**

- Completing a Job Safety Assessment (JSA) prior to performing work helps identify job safety hazards and prevent incidents.
- To reduce the possibility of personal injury, personal protective equipment (PPE) must be utilized. Reference General Safety Instructions Procedure 204-006 in Section I of the corresponding Service Manual.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Batteries can emit explosive gases. To reduce the possibility of personal injury, always ventilate the compartment before servicing the batteries. To reduce the possibility of arcing, remove the negative (-) battery cable first and attach the negative (-) battery cable last.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Fuel is flammable. Keep all cigarettes, flames, pilot lights, arcing equipment, and switches out of the work area and areas sharing ventilation to reduce the possibility of severe personal injury or death when working on the fuel system.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Do not vent the fuel system on a hot engine; this can cause fuel to spill onto a hot exhaust manifold, which can cause a fire.

- Chock vehicle wheels or use an appropriate anti-roll device to stabilize vehicle.
- Open vehicle hood.
- Disconnect the batteries. See Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) service information.
- Do **not** remove the fuel filter during installation.

**Configuration ARemove**

Place an appropriate drip pan or absorbent mat directly under the low-pressure fuel supply tube plumbing to contain any fuel that may discharge during the repair process.

Clean area around the fuel pump gear pump and fuel filter supply tube connections.

- Clip zip ties securing fuel supply tube part number 3691498; plumbing fuel from the low-pressure pump to the fuel filter inlet.
- Disconnect and discard fuel supply tube.
- Remove Quick Disconnect fittings associated with detached fuel supply tube.

![[05r00154.png]]

Remove and discard zip tie clip from fuel filter head bolt boss (1).

![[05r00155.png]]

**Install**

- Route steel fuel supply tube (1) part number 5575157 under the fuel pump drain line (2) and filter to fuel pump (3) plumbing.
- Install banjo screw part number [[3924725]] with 2x sealing washer part number [[3963988]] hand tight, into fuel supply tube banjo(s):

![[05r00156.png]]

- Install P-clip part number [[3685612]] onto steel fuel supply tube.
- Mount fuel supply tube and P-clip assembly onto fuel filter using 10mm bolt.

**Torque Value:** 23 n•m \[ 17 ft-lb \]

![[05r00157.png]]

Torque banjo screws.

- At fuel filter inlet (1).
- At fuel pump gear pump outlet (2).

**Torque Value:** 34 n•m \[ 25 ft-lb \]

![[05r00158.png]]

- Using a zip tie, secure fuel supply tube plumbing fuel from the electric lift pump to the fuel pump gear pump inlet using existing engine block clip. Ensure there is no contact between steel supply tube and neighboring nylon fuel supply tubes or engine oil dip stick tube.
- If engine oil dip stick tube is contacting the steel supply tube, clip and reposition looped zip tie adjusting the dipstick tube to provide adequate clearance.

![[05r00159.png]]

If engine oil **nylon** dip stick tube is contacting the steel supply tube, to provide adequate clearance:

- Cut and reposition looped zip tie along nylon fuel supply tube.

![[06r00093.png]]

If engine oil **steel** dip stick tube is contacting the steel supply tube, to provide adequate clearance:

- Loosen dipstick tube base nut (1) adjusting its orientation to achieve adequate clearance.
- Tighten the dipstick tube base nut. See corresponding Service Manual. Referene Procedure 007-011 in Section 7.

![[06r00094.png]]

**Configuration BRemove**

Place an appropriate drip pan or absorbent mat directly under the low-pressure fuel supply tube plumbing to contain any fuel that may discharge during the repair process.

Clean area around the fuel pump gear pump and fuel filter supply tube connections.

- Disconnect and discard fuel supply tube part number 5440474; plumbing fuel from the low-pressure pump to the fuel filter inlet.
- Remove Quick Disconnect fittings associated with detached fuel supply tube.

![[05r00160.png]]

- Remove 10mm bolt (1) at filter head upper bracket boss and inspect for re-use. Replace bolt if visual rounding or thread damage is evident. Remove and discard, C-clamp located at the bottommost filter head bracket location (2).

![[05r00161.png]]

- Remove and discard, C-clamp located at the bottom of fuel supply tube mounting bracket.

![[05r00169.png]]

**Install**

- Route steel fuel supply tube (1) part number [[5558764]] between fuel pump drain line (2) and filter to fuel pump (3) plumbing.
- Install banjo screw part number [[3924725]] with 2x sealing washer part number [[3963988]] hand tight, into fuel supply tube banjo(s):

![[05r00162.png]]

- Install P-clip part number [[3685612]] onto steel fuel supply tube.
- Mount fuel supply tube and P-clip assembly onto fuel filter bracket using 10mm bolt.

**Torque Value:** 23 n•m \[ 17 ft-lb \]

![[05r00163.png]]

- Torque banjo screws.

**Torque Value:** 34 n•m \[ 25 ft-lb \]

![[05r00164.png]]

- Install tube clamp; swivel clip part number [[3975497]], onto steel fuel supply (1) and fuel pump drain (2), at predetermined plumbing location. Ensure there is no contact between fuel supply tubes or engine oil dip stick tube.
- If engine oil dip stick tube is contacting the steel supply tube, clip and reposition looped zip tie adjusting the dipstick tube to provide adequate clearance.

![[05r00165.png]]

**Finishing Steps**

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Batteries can emit explosive gases. To reduce the possibility of personal injury, always ventilate the compartment before servicing the batteries. To reduce the possibility of arcing, remove the negative (-) battery cable first and attach the negative (-) battery cable last.

- Connect the batteries. See original equipment manufacturer (OEM) service information.
- Start the engine and check for proper operation. Verify no leaks are present.
