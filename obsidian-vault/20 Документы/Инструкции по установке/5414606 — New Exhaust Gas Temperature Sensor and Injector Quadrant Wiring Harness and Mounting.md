## New Exhaust Gas Temperature Sensor and Injector Quadrant Wiring Harness and Mounting Bracket Installation Instructions

**Product Affected**

Engines:

- QSK60 CM850 MCRS
- QSK60 CM2150 MCRS

Applications:

- Industrial (main engine wiring harness with extruded rail design **only**).

**Introduction**

The purpose of this document is to outline the fitment procedure for the new exhaust gas temperature sensor and injector wiring harness and mounting brackets. For more information, see Technical Service Bulletin, New Exhaust Gas Temperature Sensor and Injector Quadrant Wiring Harness and Mounting Brackets, [[tsb160095 — New Exhaust Gas Temperature Sensor and Injector Quadrant Wiring Harness and Mountin\|TSB160095]].

**Preparatory Steps**

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Batteries can emit explosive gases. To reduce the possibility of personal injury, always ventilate the compartment before servicing the batteries. To reduce the possibility of arcing, remove the negative (-) battery cable first and attach the negative (-) battery cable last.

- Disconnect the batteries. See original equipment manufacturer (OEM) service information.

![[ck800wa.png]]

**Remove**

- Remove exhaust gas temperature sensor and injector wiring harnesses.
- Remove capscrews that secure exhaust gas temperature sensor and injector wiring harnesses to cylinder heads.
- Remove exhaust gas temperature sensor and injector wiring harness and mounting bracket assembly.
- Each exhaust gas temperature sensor and injector wiring harnesses quadrant has two mounting brackets and four capscrews.

![[19r99362.png]]

**Install**

- Install exhaust gas temperature sensor and injector wiring harness and mounting bracket assembly and capscrews.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 18 n•m [160 in-lb]

- Connect exhaust gas temperature sensor and injector connectors to exhaust gas temperature sensor and injector wiring harness connectors.

![[19r99363.png]]

Left and Right Engine Bank Quadrants (Front) **Only**

- Route main exhaust gas temperature sensor and injector wiring harness connections between cylinder four and cylinder five.
- Use low temperature aftercooler (LTA) coolant outlet flange capscrew to mount p-clip.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 45 n•m [33 ft-lb]

![[19r99364.png]]

Left and Right Engine Bank Quadrants (Rear) **Only**

- Route main exhaust gas temperature sensor and injector wiring harness connections over cylinder eight.
- Use aftercooler housing capscrew to mount p-clip adaptor.

Aluminum Aftercooler Cover Assembly

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 45 n•m [33 ft-lb]

Cast Iron Aftercooler Cover Assembly

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 65 n•m [48 ft-lb]

- Fit p-clip to mounting adaptor.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 45 n•m [33 ft-lb]

- Connect exhaust gas temperature sensor and injector wiring harness connector to main engine wiring harness connection.

![[19r99365.png]]

**Finishing Steps**

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Batteries can emit explosive gases. To reduce the possibility of personal injury, always ventilate the compartment before servicing the batteries. To reduce the possibility of arcing, remove the negative (-) battery cable first and attach the negative (-) battery cable last.

- Connect batteries. See OEM service information.

![[ck800wa.png]]
