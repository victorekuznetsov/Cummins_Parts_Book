## Water Pump Dust Shield (Excluder)

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> This is a product improvement and is not subject to campaign.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> This kit is to be installed when the water pump is replaced.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Use the following procedure in Service Manual, Signature™, ISX, and QSX15, Bulletin [[3666239 — Signature™, ISX, and QSX15 Service Manual\|3666239]]. [[10-008-062-tr — Water Pump|Refer to Procedure 008-062 in Section 8.]]

**Preparatory Steps**

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Coolant is toxic. Keep away from children and pets. If not reused, dispose of in accordance with local environmental regulations.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Do not remove the pressure cap from a hot engine. Wait until the coolant temperature is below 50°C \[120°F\] before removing the pressure cap. Heated coolant spray or steam can cause personal injury.

- Make sure the cooling system is drained with the radiator cap removed.
- Remove the belts (water pump belt, fan drive belt, and alternator belt).
- Remove the alternator.
- Remove the water pump (old dust shield is no longer required).

**Install**

Install the dust shield (excluder) onto the replacement water pump; two longer capscrews will need to be used, which are provided with the kit.

(Make sure the longer capscrews are installed in the dust shield and the water pump before installing the water pump on the engine). (Make sure the foam gasket on the dust shield is seated in place).

Install the two barbed fittings into the dust shield.

Tighten the fittings.

Torque Value: 20 N•m \[177 in-lb\]

Push the hoses onto the barbs of the fittings in the dust shield.

![[08c00339.png]]

Install the water pump on the engine (use new o-rings).

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The longer capscrews will need to be tightened with an open end wrench.

Route hoses as shown, securing them with the wire ties provided in the kit.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> The hoses are necessary to move the vent ports for the water pump away from the front of the engine where the vent ports are more susceptible to dirt intrusion.

**Finishing Steps**

- Install the alternator.
- Install the belts (water pump belt, fan drive belt, and alternator belt).
- Fill the cooling system.
- Install the radiator cap.
- Make sure the coolant drain hose is closed.
