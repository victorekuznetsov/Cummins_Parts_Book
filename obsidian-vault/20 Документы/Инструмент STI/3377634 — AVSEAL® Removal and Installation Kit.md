### Description

AVSEAL® Removal and Installation Kit

### Purpose

This document provides information for AVSEAL® Removal and Installation Kit, Part Number 3823154. The kit is used to install and remove the AVSEAL® II blind sealing plugs in the K38, K50, QSK38, QSK45, QSK50, QSK60, and QSK78 cylinder blocks and associated engine accessories.

[[28-001-026-tr — Cylinder Block|For additional information, refer to Procedure 001-026 (Cylinder Block) in Section 1 of the K38, K50, QSK38, QSK50 Service Manual, Bulletin 4021528.]]

For additional information, refer to Procedure 001-026 (Cylinder Block) in Section 1 of the QSK45 and QSK60 Service Manual, Bulletin [[4021530 — QSK45 and QSK60 Service Manual\|4021530]].

AVSEAL® II Blind Sealing Plug Removal

6 and 7 mm

- Install the remover screw (3) on the remover slide hammer kit (1).

8 and 10 mm

- Install the remover screw (2) on the remover slide hammer kit (1).

![[22600448.png]]

AVSEAL® II Blind Sealing Plug Installation

6 mm

- Assemble the installer guide 6 mm (9) to the installer adapter 6 mm (8). Install the guide and adapter assembly onto the seal installer (13).

7 mm

- Assemble the installer guide 7 mm (10) to the installer adapter 6 mm (8). Install the guide and adapter assembly onto the seal installer (13).

8 mm

- Assemble the extended installer guide 8 mm (11) to the installer adapter 10 mm (7). Install the guide and adapter assembly onto the seal installer (13).

10 mm

- Assemble the extended installer guide 10 mm (12) to the installer adapter 10 mm (7). Install the guide and adapter assembly onto the seal installer (13).
