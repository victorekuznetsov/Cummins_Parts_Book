### Description

Electrical Circuit Tester Kits

### Purpose

This service tool instruction provides procedures for using the electrical circuit tester kit, Part Numbers 3163594 or 3163878. The electrical circuit tester, Part Number 3163589, is portable and allows the technician to monitor voltage, current, and open circuits in the ECM harness at the ECM pins. The electrical circuit tester, Part Number 3163878, **only** allows the technician to monitor voltage in the harness and is **not** shown in the illustration below.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> Do **not** operate vehicle while tool is in use.

Both electrical circuit tester kits include an electrical circuit tester, harness adapter cable (10 feet), and ECM connector adapter cable (10 feet). The electrical circuit tester kits allow the technician to work on the following engines: ISB, ISC, ISL, ISM, ISX, Signature, QSB, QSC, QSL, and QSX. Optional adapter cables are available for power generation, Quantum™/CENSE™, and CELECT™/CELECT™ Plus.

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> To avoid severe ECM damage, refer to the appropriate wiring diagram to make certain the pin is located properly.

Use a multimeter and the electrical circuit tester, Part Number 3163589 or Part Number 3163879, to measure the voltage on a specific pin. Place the positive (+) meter lead in the desired pin. Find a ground (-) on the electrical circuit tester using the appropriate wiring diagram.

Set the toggle switch to the CLOSED position. This **only** applies to the electrical circuit tester, Part Number 3163589.

![[22a00090.png]]

> [!warning] CAUTION · Осторожно
> To avoid severe ECM damage, refer to the appropriate wiring diagram to make certain the pin is located properly.

Use a multimeter and the electrical circuit tester, Part Number 3163589, to measure the amperage on a specific pin.

Place the positive (+) meter lead in the desired pin's left test point; then place the negative (-) meter lead in the desired pin's right test point.

Set the toggle switch to the OPEN position.
