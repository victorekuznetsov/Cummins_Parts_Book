### Description

Injector Leak Test Kit

### Purpose

This document provides information for injector leak test kit, Part Number 3164001. The kit is used to identify faulty injectors on the Signature, ISX, ISX with CM870, and QSX15 engines. For additional information, refer to Procedure 006-026 in the Signature, ISX, and QSX15 Engines Service Manual, Bulletin [[3666239 — Signature™, ISX, and QSX15 Service Manual\|3666239]].
