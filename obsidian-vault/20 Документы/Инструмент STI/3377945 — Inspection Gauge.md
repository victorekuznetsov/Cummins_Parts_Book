### Description

Inspection Gauge

### Purpose

This service tool instruction provides information for the inspection gauge, Part Number 3165071. This inspection gauge is used to inspect the ISX cylinder head for reuse.

For the inspection procedures, refer to Procedure 002-004 in the ISX Troubleshooting and Repair Manual, Bulletin [[3666239 — Signature™, ISX, and QSX15 Service Manual|3666239]].
