### Description

Injector Sleeve Remover

### Purpose

The injector sleeve remover, Part Number 3377079, is used to remove the injector sleeve from the cylinder head with the use of an impact wrench. The remover can be used on the NH, V28, L10, V903, V504, and V55 series engines.

Lubricate the remover thrust bearing and threads with a heavy grease.

Place the remover, with the appropriate collet and end stop, into the injector bore.

![[22800546.png]]

The collet is properly seated when the end stop bottoms in the injector sleeve.

If the end stop does **not** bottom in the injector sleeve, a soft face hammer can be used to seat the collet.

![[22800547.png]]

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Wear appropriate eye and face protection when using compressed air. Flying debris and dirt can cause personal injury.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> With some engine applications, the remover collet can rotate. If the collet rotates, apply pressure to the top of the collet while operating the impact wrench. Once the collet grips the injector sleeve, the pressure can be removed.

Using an impact wrench and the appropriate socket, turn the remover nut in a **clockwise** direction until the injector sleeve is removed from the cylinder head.

![[22800548.png]]

Remove the injector sleeve from the injector sleeve remover.

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> If necessary, tap the injector sleeve on a workbench to release the collet from the sleeve.

![[22800549.png]]
