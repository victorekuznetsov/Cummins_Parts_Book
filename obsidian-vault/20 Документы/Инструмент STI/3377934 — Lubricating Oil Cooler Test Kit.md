### Description

Lubricating Oil Cooler Test Kit

### Purpose

This document provides information for lubricating oil cooler test kit, Part Number 4918200. The kit is used to test the oil cooler assembly for leakage. For additional information on the use of the kit, refer to Procedure 007-003 in the Signature, ISX, and QSX15 Base Engine Troubleshooting and Repair Manual, Bulletin Number [[3666239 — Signature™, ISX, and QSX15 Service Manual\|3666239]].
