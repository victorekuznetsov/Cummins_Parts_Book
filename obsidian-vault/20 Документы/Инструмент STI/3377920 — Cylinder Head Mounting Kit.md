### Description

Cylinder Head Mounting Kit

### Purpose

This document provides information for using cylinder head mounting kit, Part Number 3164913. The kit is used to mount the Signature, ISX, ISX with CM870, and QSX15 cylinder head to the engine stand, Part Number 3375194.

![[22c00242.png]]

| 2 | 3251767 | Hexagon head capscrew, M10 x 1.5 x 50-mm, class 10.9 | 8 |
| 3 | [[4001226]] | Hexagon head capscrew, M16 x 2.0 x 70-mm, class 10.9 | 4 |

> [!note] Note · Примечание
> \* The threaded insert is press fit into the mounting plate and is used for repair purposes **only**.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Do not use substitute capscrews without first verifying that they have the same specifications as the capscrews supplied in the kit. Failure to do so can result in equipment damage, severe personal injury or death.

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> The mounting plate must be used to mount only a Signature, ISX, ISX with CM870 or QSX15 cylinder head on the engine repair stand. Improper use can result in equipment damage, severe personal injury or death.

Inspect the mounting plate and all capscrews for any damage, rust, wear, or missing parts. Do **not** use the mounting plate or capscrews if damaged.

![[22c00249.png]]

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> All eight M10 capscrews must be used and tightened to specification. Failure to do so can result in equipment damage, severe personal injury or death.

Install the mounting plate (1) on the exhaust manifold mounting face the of cylinder head using the eight M10 capscrews (2).

Tighten the capscrews.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 60 n•m [44 ft-lb]

![[22c00243.png]]

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Rigging and lifting of the cylinder head must be done by trained and experienced personnel only. Do not attempt to lift the cylinder head while attached to the cylinder block. Improper use can result in equipment damage, severe personal injury or death.

Install one nylon lifting sling, Part Number 3375957 or Part Number 3375958, through the center valve camshaft bore. Install a second lifting sling, Part Number 3375957 or Part Number 3375958, through the center injector camshaft bore.

Connect the lifting slings to an appropriate hoist to lift the cylinder head.

![[22c00244.png]]

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> All four M16 capscrews must be used and tightened to specification. Failure to do so can result in equipment damage, severe personal injury or death.

Align the holes in the mounting plate with the holes in the engine repair stand. Install the four M16 capscrews through the engine repair stand plate and into the four mounting plate holes.

Tighten the capscrews.

> [!tip] Момент затяжки · Torque Value
> 150 n•m [110 ft-lb]

![[22c00245.png]]

> [!danger] WARNING · Опасно
> Rigging and lifting of the cylinder head must be done by trained and experienced personnel only. Do not attempt to lift the cylinder head while attached to the cylinder block. Improper use can result in equipment damage, severe personal injury or death.

Install one nylon lifting sling, Part Number 3375957 or Part Number 3375958, through the center valve camshaft bore. Install a second lifting sling, Part Number 3375957 or Part Number 3375958, through the center injector camshaft bore.

Connect the lifting slings to an appropriate hoist and raise the hoist to put the slings in tension.

Remove the four M16 capscrews from the engine repair stand.

![[22c00245.png]]

Remove the eight M10 capscrews (2) and mounting plate (1) from cylinder head.

![[22c00243.png]]
