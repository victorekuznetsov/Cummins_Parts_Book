### Description

Exhaust Lockplate Installer Kit

### Purpose

This document provides information for Exhaust Lockplate Installer Kit, Part Number 4919376. The kit is used to install exhaust manifold capscrew lockplates to the required specification on the QSK45 and QSK60 engines.

[[56-011-007-tr — Exhaust Manifold, Dry|For additional information, refer to Procedure 011-007 (Exhaust Manifold, Dry) in Section 11 of the Service Manual, QSK45 and QSK60, Bulletin 4021530.]]
