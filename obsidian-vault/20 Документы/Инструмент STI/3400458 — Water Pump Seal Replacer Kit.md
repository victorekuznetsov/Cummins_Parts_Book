### Description

Water Pump Seal Replacer Kit

### Purpose

This document provides information for Water Pump Seal Replacer Kit, Part Number 5298838. The kit is used to replace the oil and water seals on the QSK23 engine water pump. The kit components are assembled together when installing the engine water pump oil seal. For the engine water pump water seal, **only** the handle component is used.

- Use the following procedure for additional information on the seal installer in the QSK23 Troubleshooting and Repair Manual, Bulletin [[4021375 — QSK23 Troubleshooting and Repair Manual\|4021375]]. [[89-008-062-tr — Water Pump|Refer to Procedure 008-062 in section 8.]]
