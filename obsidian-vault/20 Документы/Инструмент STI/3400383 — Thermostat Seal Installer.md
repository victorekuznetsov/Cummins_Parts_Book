### Description

Thermostat Seal Installer

### Purpose

This document provides information for thermostat seal installer, Part Number 2892254. The installer is used to install the thermostat seal to the correct depth.

Use the following procedures for additional information on the thermostat seal installer.

- Refer to Procedure 008-016 in Section 8 in the K19 Service Manual, Bulletin [[4021499 — K19 Service Manual\|4021499]].
- [[56-008-016 — Coolant Thermostat Seal|Refer to Procedure 008-016 in Section 8 in the QSK45 and QSK60 Service Manual, Bulletin 4021530.]]
- [[28-008-013-tr — Coolant Thermostat|Refer to Procedure 008-013 in Section 8 in the K38, K50, QSK38, and QSK50 Service Manual, Bulletin 4021528.]]
- Refer to Procedure 008-016 in Section 8 in the QSK78 Troubleshooting and Repair Manual, Bulletin 3666727.
