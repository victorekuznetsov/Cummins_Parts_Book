### Description

Crankshaft Adapter and Gear Remover Kit

### Purpose

This document provides information for the Crankshaft Adapter and Gear Remover Kit, Part Number 4918530. The kit is used to remove the crankshaft adapter and gear from the crankshaft. For addition information, refer to Procedures [[10-001-017 — Manual Not Available|001-017]] and [[10-001-018 — Crankshaft Gear, Front (Crankshaft Installed)|001-018]] in the Signature, ISX, and QSX15 Engines, Service Manual, Bulletin [[3666239 — Signature™, ISX, and QSX15 Service Manual\|3666239]].
